**Five teddy bears** are hidden around the park, among about fifty possible spots. Shoot all five to start the song. You also earn 500 XP, and the song unlocks in "Lobby Music" (Barracks).

<Screenshot src={m + 'teddy_bear_example.webp'} alt="A hidden teddy bear" credit={by} source={src} />

<Tips>
  <Tip type="glitch" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
    Sometimes the fifth bear never spawns. Nothing makes it show up: you have to start a new game.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

Tick off each area once you have searched it.
