Two secrets need **Director's Cut mode**, which unlocks on The Beast from Beyond (guide coming soon).

## New lobby music

All of these steps are done **during round 1**.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Pick up a pair of sunglasses">
    A zombie drops them at random when it dies.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'weapons_of_rock/dischord/sunglasses.webp'} alt="The sunglasses" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the yeti's mask from the roller coaster">
    Ride the roller coaster with the sunglasses on. At the end of the first fast drop, a mechanical yeti blows icy air: shoot the **white mask** between its eyes. The new lobby music unlocks.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'lobby_music/yeti_example.webp'} alt="The roller coaster yeti" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'lobby_music/yeti_with_mask.webp'} alt="The white mask between its eyes" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## "Willard's Test" animated calling card

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Redo the first four Easter Eggs in Director's Cut">
    Zombies in Spaceland, Rave in the Redwoods, Shaolin Shuffle and Attack of the Radioactive Thing, all in Director's Cut mode.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish The Beast from Beyond's Easter Egg">
    At the end you fight Mephistopheles: beat him (guide coming soon).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish Zombies in Spaceland's Easter Egg as Willard Wyler">
    See the "Play as David Hasselhoff or Willard Wyler" guide for the code. At the end, the mode's final cutscene plays and the animated calling card unlocks.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
