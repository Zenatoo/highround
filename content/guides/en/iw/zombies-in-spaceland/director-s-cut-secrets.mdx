The "Willard's Test" calling card needs **Director's Cut mode** and a win against **Mephistopheles**, The Beast from Beyond's final boss. To unlock the mode, see [Unlock and use Director's Cut](../../the-beast-from-beyond/director-s-cut/).

This map's lobby music has its own guide: [Lobby music (Director's Cut)](../musique-salon/).

## "Willard's Test" animated calling card

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Redo the first four Easter Eggs in Director's Cut">
    Zombies in Spaceland, Rave in the Redwoods, Shaolin Shuffle and Attack of the Radioactive Thing, all in Director's Cut mode. At the end of each one, a **Talisman** appears: **pick it up before leaving the game**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Beat Mephistopheles on The Beast from Beyond">
    With the four Talismans, The Beast from Beyond's Easter Egg in Director's Cut ends with the fight against Mephistopheles. See [Final fight against Mephistopheles](../../the-beast-from-beyond/mephistopheles/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish Zombies in Spaceland's Easter Egg as Willard Wyler">
    See [Play as David Hasselhoff or Willard Wyler](../personnages-secrets/) for the code. At the end, the mode's final cutscene plays and the animated calling card unlocks.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
