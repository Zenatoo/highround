The Dischord makes the zombie it hits breakdance on its head: it creates a tornado that kills the zombies around it. It needs three parts: a **battery**, a **disco ball** and a **green crystal**.

**Before you start:** the crystal needs the **Laser elemental core** (see the elemental cores guide) and a Brute, which shows up around round 10.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Pick up the sunglasses">
    A zombie eventually drops them at random when it dies. Pick them up: the screen turns black and white and five targets appear around the park.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'weapons_of_rock/dischord/sunglasses.webp'} alt="The sunglasses" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>

  <Step title="Shoot the five targets">
    The five targets are picked at random from these spots:

    - **Spawn**: on the hedge, near the bridge.
    - **Cosmic Way**: above the deposit machine, under the bridge to Polar Peak, or above the Astrocade entrance (left of the big mouth in the mountain).
    - **Astrocade**: above the power switch, inside the claw machine, in the zombie window near Cryptid Attack, on the floor behind the prize counter, in the trash can near Black Hole, or behind the alien cutout across from the counter.
    - **Behind the Astrocade**: in the display case near the Blue Bolts machine, or behind the bumper cars piled up in the corner.
    - **Polar Peak**: left of the roller coaster yeti, visible from the walkway coming from the Astrocade.

  <Step title="Make the disco ball">
    Put one souvenir coin of **each color** (blue, green, red) in the souvenir machine in the alley behind the Astrocade. The ball comes out of the machine.

    Buy the **Golden Teeth** (300 tickets) at the Astrocade prize counter. Lure a Brute into the Kepler gator trap and trigger it: the Brute jams the jaw and some teeth break. Put the Golden Teeth in their place: the throat opens on a machine.

    Place the three parts on the alien cardboard cutout in the Astrocade, near the prize counter.
