Pack-a-Punch is in the projector room, which you reach through the big portal in Cosmic Way.

  <Step title="Use the four teleporters">
    Each one takes you back to Cosmic Way:

    - **Journey into Space**: near Star Mission, right of the Volk.
    - **Astrocade**: between the power switch and the entrance.
    - **Polar Peak**: inside the shop.
    - **Kepler**: at the bottom of the Moonlight Cafe stairs.

    The blue lights above the big portal show which ones are done, from left to right: Journey, Astrocade, Polar Peak, Kepler.

## Double Pack-a-Punch

It unlocks when you finish the main Easter Egg: the alien drops two fuses that go into the projector. After that, and **only for the current game**, you can upgrade a weapon once for 5,000 points, then a second time for 10,000 points. Weapons of rock can only be upgraded with this upgraded projector.
