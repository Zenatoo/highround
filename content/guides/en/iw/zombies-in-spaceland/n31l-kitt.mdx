N31L is a robot that gives out challenges for tickets. After each set of five challenges, he brings in **David Hasselhoff**, who follows you for about three minutes, shoots zombies and revives downed players.

**How the challenges work:** every player takes part in the same challenge. Interact with N31L to pause or restart the timer. A completed challenge pays out tickets; completed or failed, a new challenge follows.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Put N31L back together">
    Take his head from the ice cream stand at the bottom of the Hyper Slopes slides (Cosmic Way), and put it on his body, near the bridge that leads to spawn.

  <Step title="Complete the first set of five challenges">
    Possible challenges: kill while jumping, don't go down, kill from long range, melee kills, multi-kills.

    When N31L's icon disappears from the top left, interact with him to start the K.I.T.T. protocol: he flies off and comes back with David Hasselhoff.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the auxiliary battery">
    When he comes back, N31L stops moving. The battery is in one of these spots:

    - **Kepler**: on the ice cream cart in front of the Chromosphere, on the bench in front of Cosmic Tunes, or on the trash can across from the Ferris wheel (above the Bombstoppers machine).
    - **Polar Peak**: on a trash can left of the RPR Evo, in the cubbies near the Triton roller coaster entrance, or on the shop counter.

    Give it to N31L.

  <Step title="Complete the second set of five challenges">
    Possible challenges: blow off legs, repair windows, close-range kills, kill crawlers, kill without taking damage, protect a player (co-op). Then interact with N31L to call Hasselhoff back.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the floppy disk">
    It is underground, in one of these spots: on the workbench near the HVR wall weapon, on the bench at the end of the Racin' Stripes hallway, or on the edge of the table right of the Banshee. Give it to N31L.

  <Step title="Complete the last set of five challenges">
    Possible challenges: kill the zombies that hurt you, headshots, kill surrounded zombies first, kill inside the marked area, blow off arms, don't bleed out (co-op). Then interact with N31L to call Hasselhoff back.

    After this third call, you can call him back as often as you like for **5,000 points**.
