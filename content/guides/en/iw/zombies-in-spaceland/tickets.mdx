Tickets are the map's second currency. They buy special items, several of which you need for the weapons of rock and the Easter Egg.

## Earning tickets

- Play the arcade games (see the table below).
- Complete N31L's challenges.
- Kill zombies with traps: the more you kill at once, the more you earn.
- Pick up the backpack or the fanny pack some zombies drop.

## What you can buy

| Prize | Where | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grenade refill | Astrocade | 150 tickets |
| Ammo refill | Astrocade | 150 tickets |
| Golden Teeth | Astrocade | 300 tickets |
| Cryo grenade | Astrocade | 350 tickets |
| Forge Freeze | Astrocade | 500 tickets |
| Rewind, Transponder or Black Hole | "Weapons of the Future" stand | 25 tickets |
| Trip Mine, Armageddon, Portal Generator or Repulsor | "Weapons of the Future" stand | 50 tickets |
| Arcane Core | "Weapons of the Future" stand | 300 tickets |
