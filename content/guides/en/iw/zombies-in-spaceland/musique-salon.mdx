This track does not play during the match: it is added to the **lobby** music, the menu before you start a game. You need **Director's Cut mode**: to unlock it, see [Unlock and use Director's Cut](../../the-beast-from-beyond/director-s-cut/) on The Beast from Beyond.

All of these steps are done **during round 1**.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Pick up a pair of sunglasses">
    A zombie drops them at random when it dies.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'weapons_of_rock/dischord/sunglasses.webp'} alt="The sunglasses" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the yeti's mask from the roller coaster">
    Ride the roller coaster with the sunglasses on. At the end of the first fast drop, a mechanical yeti blows icy air: shoot the **white mask** between its eyes. The new lobby music unlocks.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'lobby_music/yeti_example.webp'} alt="The roller coaster yeti" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'lobby_music/yeti_with_mask.webp'} alt="The white mask between its eyes" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>
</Checklist>
