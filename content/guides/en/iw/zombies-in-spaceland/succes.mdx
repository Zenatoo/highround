Zombies in Spaceland has **10 trophies** (achievements on Xbox and PC). Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get Packed (bronze)">
    Upgrade a weapon with Pack-a-Punch. See the "Unlock Pack-a-Punch" guide.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Rock On! (bronze)">
    Build a weapon of rock. One is enough: Head-Cutter, Face-Melter, Dischord or Shredder.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Batteries Not Included (bronze)">
    Build the X-Quisite core. See the "Elemental cores and the X-Quisite core" guide.
  </Step>

  <Step title="I Love the 80's (bronze)">
    Unlock both secret songs, MW1 Theme and MW2 Theme. See the two "Music Easter Egg" guides.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Insert Coin (bronze)">
    Play each arcade game on the map at least once. The list is in the "Tickets and prizes" guide.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The Bigger They Are (bronze)">
    Kill 5 Brutes in a single game without dying. The first one shows up around round 10.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Brain Dead (silver)">
    Play 30 games and reach at least round 10 in each.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sticker Collector (silver)">
    Find every quest item on the map and fill the sticker book: N31L, X-Quisite, Head-Cutter, Face-Melter, Dischord, Shredder and SETI-COM.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="PowerPyx" source="https://www.powerpyx.com/call-of-duty-infinite-warfare-trophy-guide-roadmap">
        Everything has to be done in one and the same game, and the X-Quisite sticker needs the main Easter Egg.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sooooul Key (silver)">
    Get the soul key piece by finishing the main Easter Egg. See the "Main Easter Egg (Sooooul Key)" guide.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hoff The Charts! (bronze)">
    Unlock David Hasselhoff: finish the main Easter Egg, then enter his code in the lobby. See the "Play as David Hasselhoff or Willard Wyler" guide.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
