The Shredder disintegrates the zombie it hits and up to 20 zombies around it. It needs three parts: a **battery**, an **alien souvenir** and a **red crystal**.

**Before you start:** the crystal needs the **Fire elemental core** (see the elemental cores guide) and a Brute, which shows up around round 10.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Knock the battery off the boat">
    In Kepler, shoot the battery at the back, in the middle, of the river boat (Log Ride). It falls into the water. The boat comes back around about every 45 seconds.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'weapons_of_rock/shredder/battery_on_boat.webp'} alt="The battery at the back of the boat" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Catch the battery in a geyser">
    Go up to the geysers above the river. Every few seconds, a random geyser shoots the battery up: interact with it while it is in the air.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'weapons_of_rock/shredder/battery_in_fountain.webp'} alt="The battery shot up by a geyser" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        Stay next to one geyser and wait for it to come out there, instead of chasing it.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make the alien souvenir">
    Put three **red** souvenir coins in the Kepler souvenir machine (near the Moonlight Cafe stairs). The souvenir comes out of the machine.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'coins/coins_kepler.webp'} alt="The Kepler souvenir machine" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'weapons_of_rock/shredder/alien_souvenir.webp'} alt="The alien souvenir" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the gator's throat">
    Buy the **Golden Teeth** (300 tickets) at the Astrocade prize counter. Lure a Brute into the Kepler gator trap and trigger it: the Brute jams the jaw and some teeth break. Put the Golden Teeth in their place: the throat opens on a machine.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'golden_teeth.webp'} alt="The Golden Teeth" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'elemental_upgrades/xquisite_core/gator_trap.webp'} alt="The Kepler gator trap" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'elemental_upgrades/xquisite_core/gator_trap_no_teeth.webp'} alt="The broken teeth" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'elemental_upgrades/xquisite_core/gator_throat_machine.webp'} alt="The machine in the throat" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the right target with the Fire core">
    With the weapon that has the Fire core, shoot the **rightmost** target. Pick up the red crystal.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'elemental_upgrades/xquisite_core/crystal_correct_target.webp'} alt="The machine's targets" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build the Shredder">
    Place the three parts on the alien cardboard cutout in the underground passage, near the Kepler river.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'weapons_of_rock/shredder/alien_cardboard.webp'} alt="The underground passage cardboard cutout" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Weapons of rock can only be upgraded with the Pack-a-Punch upgraded by the main Easter Egg.
