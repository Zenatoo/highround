The Head-Cutter makes the head of the zombie it hits explode, and the blast hurts nearby zombies. It needs three parts: a **battery**, a **yeti plush** and a **yellow crystal**.

**Before you start:** the crystal needs the **Wind elemental core** (see the elemental cores guide) and a Brute, which shows up around round 10.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Buy cryo grenades">
    At the Astrocade prize counter, for 350 tickets.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'weapons_of_rock/head_cutter/cryo_grenades.webp'} alt="The cryo grenades at the prize counter" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Freeze the Polar Peak yeti">
    Throw a cryo grenade at the face of the yeti right in front of the Polar Peak entrance. It blows icy air for about 10 seconds.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'weapons_of_rock/head_cutter/yeti_polar_peak.webp'} alt="The yeti in front of Polar Peak" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill 10 frozen zombies with headshots">
    Lead zombies through the icy blast, then finish them with a headshot. After the tenth one, the yeti roars.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Grab the battery">
    Go into the Polar Peak shop: the battery is in the yeti's hand, next to the teleporter.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'weapons_of_rock/head_cutter/battery.webp'} alt="The battery in the yeti's hand" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make the yeti plush">
    Put three **green** souvenir coins in the Polar Peak souvenir machine (inside the shop). The plush comes out of the machine.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'coins/coins_polar_peak.webp'} alt="The Polar Peak souvenir machine" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'weapons_of_rock/head_cutter/yeti_plush.webp'} alt="The yeti plush" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the gator's throat">
    Buy the **Golden Teeth** (300 tickets) at the Astrocade prize counter. Lure a Brute into the Kepler gator trap and trigger it: the Brute jams the jaw and some teeth break. Put the Golden Teeth in their place: the throat opens on a machine.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'golden_teeth.webp'} alt="The Golden Teeth" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'elemental_upgrades/xquisite_core/gator_trap.webp'} alt="The Kepler gator trap" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'elemental_upgrades/xquisite_core/gator_trap_no_teeth.webp'} alt="The broken teeth" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'elemental_upgrades/xquisite_core/gator_throat_machine.webp'} alt="The machine in the throat" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the left target with the Wind core">
    With the weapon that has the Wind core, shoot the **leftmost** target on the machine. Pick up the yellow crystal.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'elemental_upgrades/xquisite_core/crystal_correct_target.webp'} alt="The machine's targets" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build the Head-Cutter">
    Place the three parts on the alien cardboard cutout in the Polar Peak shop.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'weapons_of_rock/head_cutter/cardboard_cutout.webp'} alt="The Polar Peak cardboard cutout" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Weapons of rock can only be upgraded with the Pack-a-Punch upgraded by the main Easter Egg.
