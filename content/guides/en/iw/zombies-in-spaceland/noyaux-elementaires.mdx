An elemental core gives a weapon an effect: **Electric, Laser, Wind or Fire**. They are also used to build the weapons of rock. All four together unlock the purple **X-Quisite** core.

**Before you start:** you need **Pack-a-Punch unlocked**. The steps are the same for all four cores, only the trap changes.

## One elemental core

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Buy an Arcane Core">
    For 300 tickets, at any "Weapons of the Future" stand in the park. It is an attachment that goes on any normal weapon and holds the cores.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'arcane_core.webp'} alt="The Arcane Core" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Release the UFOs in the projector room">
    In the Pack-a-Punch room, interact with the small UFOs on the table. Each one flies off to a different trap.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'ufo_projector_room.webp'} alt="The UFOs on the projector room table" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill 15 zombies with one use of the right trap">
    Pick the core you want and use the matching trap:

    - **Electric**: rocket trap in Journey into Space.
    - **Laser**: disco ball trap in the Astrocade.
    - **Wind**: Steel Dragon trap in Polar Peak.
    - **Fire**: Chromosphere trap in Kepler.

    When it works, the UFO stops circling the trap and wanders around the area.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={e + 'rocket_trap.webp'} alt="The rocket trap (Electric)" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={e + 'disco_ball_trap.webp'} alt="The disco ball trap (Laser)" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={e + 'steel_dragon_trap.webp'} alt="The Steel Dragon trap (Wind)" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={e + 'chromosphere_trap.webp'} alt="The Chromosphere trap (Fire)" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill the UFO with zombie souls">
    Kill about 25 zombies near the UFO with the weapon that has the Arcane Core: their souls go into the UFO.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the orb and equip it">
    Once full, the UFO drops an orb. Put it on a weapon that has an Arcane Core. The UFO then goes back to its trap: you can do it again to get another core of the same element.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'fire_orb.webp'} alt="A fire orb" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The X-Quisite core (purple)

You need to have gotten all four elemental cores at least once.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Buy the Golden Teeth">
    At the Astrocade prize counter, for 300 tickets.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'golden_teeth.webp'} alt="The Golden Teeth" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Break the gator's teeth with a Brute">
    The first Brute shows up around round 10. Lure it into the Kepler gator trap and trigger it: the Brute jams the jaw and some teeth break.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={e + 'xquisite_core/gator_trap.webp'} alt="The gator trap" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={e + 'xquisite_core/gator_trap_no_teeth.webp'} alt="The broken teeth" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put in the Golden Teeth">
    Place them where the broken teeth were: the throat opens on a machine with four targets.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'xquisite_core/gator_throat_machine.webp'} alt="The machine in the throat" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot each target with the right core">
    From left to right: **Wind, Electric, Laser, Fire**. Each correct shot drops a crystal to pick up.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'xquisite_core/crystal_correct_target.webp'} alt="The four targets" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Drop the cores off in the projector room">
    After each crystal, interact with the machine in the projector room to drop off the matching core. You need all four.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'xquisite_core/machine_projector_room.webp'} alt="The projector room machine" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Buy the X-Quisite core">
    It is now in the machine inside the gator's mouth. One per player per game.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'xquisite_core/xquisite_core_machine.webp'} alt="The X-Quisite core machine" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>
