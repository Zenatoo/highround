Ghosts 'N Skulls is a hidden arcade machine. You need **six skulls** to unlock it. Beating its mini-game gives you **every perk for the rest of the game** (except Up 'N Atoms in solo).

**Before you start:**

- The machine is upstairs in the Astrocade, between Black Hole and Cryptid Attack: it shows how many skulls you have.
- Skull 5 needs the **main Easter Egg** done.
- Each time you get a skull, a confirmation sound plays.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Skull 1: pop the spawn balloons">
    Pop all the balloons above the Hailstorm wall weapon, in the spawn area.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_1/balloons.webp'} alt="The balloons above the Hailstorm" credit={by} source={src} />

  <Step title="Skull 2: look at the five skulls hidden in the letters G-H-O-S-T">
    Skulls hide in letters on signs, one at a time, in the order G, H, O, S, T. Look at each skull until it bursts into yellow cubes. Each letter has two possible spots:

    - **G**: the G on the Galaxy Journey sign (near Escape Velocity), or the G on Roller Rampage (bumper cars).
    - **H**: the first H on the Chromosphere trap sign (Kepler), or the H on Hyper Slopes (Cosmic Way).
    - **O**: the O on Journey Into Space (Cosmic Way), or the O on Cosmic Tunes near the geysers (Kepler).
    - **S**: the S on Star Mission (rocket trap), or the S on the Astrocade sign in the Blue Bolts alley.
    - **T**: the second T on Octonian Hunter (Kepler), or above the T on the Moonlight Cafe sign closest to the power switch (upstairs, not the one by the teleporter).

  <Step title="Skull 3: get kills on the dance floor">
    On the Astrocade dance floor, get this many kills while standing on each tile color:

    - **Green**: 1
    - **Blue**: 9
    - **Pink**: 8
    - **Black (unlit)**: 4

    Every kill is kept: you can leave the floor and come back. Extra kills on a color don't count.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_3/dance_floor.webp'} alt="The Astrocade dance floor" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Skull 4: blow up the skull in each arcade game">
    In the Astrocade, beat the challenge on each machine:

    - **Rings of Saturn** (either of the two machines): a skull appears at random, score a basket before it disappears.
    - **Black Hole**: score 50 points.
    - **Cryptid Attack**: the skull appears above a row of teeth, hit the teeth below it.
    - **Zombie Zoom**: get past the skull before the gray zombie does.
    - **Bowling for Planets**: the skull appears in a hole, roll your ball into that hole before it disappears.

      <Tip type="faster" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        For Zombie Zoom, turn on the disco ball trap to keep zombies away, and tap fire repeatedly instead of holding it: the gun doesn't move back.
      </Tip>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        Bowling for Planets is by far the most annoying: the physics are fickle, expect several tries.
      </Tip>

  <Step title="Skull 5: shoot down the skull in the sky">
    After the main Easter Egg, pick up a pair of sunglasses (a zombie drops them at random). With the sunglasses on, a skull appears in the sky above the Pack-a-Punch portal. Shoot it down with the Spaceland ring's beam, like the UFO.

    <Shots>

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        Keep the sunglasses on until the skull is destroyed: if you take them off before, it doesn't count.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Skull 6: make a Brute shoot the machine">
    Lure a Brute to the Ghosts 'N Skulls machine and stand so it fires its laser at it. If it works, all six skulls are in and the screen crackles with electricity.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'glowing_machine.webp'} alt="The machine charged with electricity" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Win the mini-game">
    Every player interacts with the machine to enter the game. There are three rounds: grab a ghost with your weapon and throw it at the skulls before they reach the end of the arena.

    Sometimes a skull turns red and rushes to the vortex at the back: if **three escape in the same round**, you lose, and you have to make a Brute shoot the machine again.

    After the third round, you are back in the Astrocade with every perk.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
