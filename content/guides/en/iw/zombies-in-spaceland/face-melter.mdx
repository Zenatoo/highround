The Face-Melter launches the zombie it hits into the air like a rocket: it explodes and rains fire on the others. It needs three parts: a **battery**, a **toy rocket** and a **blue crystal**.

**Before you start:** you need **Pack-a-Punch unlocked**, the **Electric elemental core** (see the elemental cores guide) and a Brute, which shows up around round 10.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Throw a grenade into the Journey into Space teleporter">
    Activate the Journey into Space teleporter and throw a grenade into it (Frag, Cluster or Semtex). It sends back a glowing **red grenade**.

      <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/tele_journey.webp'} alt="The Journey into Space teleporter" credit={by} source={src} />

    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Carry the red grenade to the Pack-a-Punch portal">
    Pick it up and throw it again and again as you move toward the big portal in Cosmic Way: each time you pick it up, its timer resets. Throw it into the portal: the battery appears where you were standing.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/pap_tele_activated.webp'} alt="The Pack-a-Punch portal" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'weapons_of_rock/face_melter/battery.webp'} alt="The battery" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make the toy rocket">
    Put three **blue** souvenir coins in the Journey into Space souvenir machine (across from the way down to the underground). The rocket comes out of the machine.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'coins/coins_journey.webp'} alt="The Journey into Space souvenir machine" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'weapons_of_rock/face_melter/rocket_toy.webp'} alt="The toy rocket" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the gator's throat">
    Buy the **Golden Teeth** (300 tickets) at the Astrocade prize counter. Lure a Brute into the Kepler gator trap and trigger it: the Brute jams the jaw and some teeth break. Put the Golden Teeth in their place: the throat opens on a machine.

    <Shots>

  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the second target with the Electric core">
    With the weapon that has the Electric core, shoot the **second target from the left**. Pick up the blue crystal.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'elemental_upgrades/xquisite_core/crystal_correct_target.webp'} alt="The machine's targets" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build the Face-Melter">
    Place the three parts on the alien cardboard cutout in Journey into Space, near the Escape Velocity ride.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'weapons_of_rock/face_melter/alien_cardboard.webp'} alt="The Journey into Space cardboard cutout" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Weapons of rock can only be upgraded with the Pack-a-Punch upgraded by the main Easter Egg.
