Zombies sometimes drop red, blue or green **souvenir coins**. Put three of them in a souvenir machine, in any order, to make an item.

- **Cosmic Way**: near the Journey into Space door.
- **Journey into Space**: across from the way down to the underground.
- **Astrocade**: in the alley behind the Astrocade.
- **Polar Peak**: inside the shop.
- **Kepler**: near the Moonlight Cafe stairs.

## Recipes

| Item | Coins |
| --- | --- |
| Medusa Device | red, red, red |
| Fireworks trap | red, red, blue |
| Window laser trap | red, blue, blue |
| Turret | blue, blue, blue **or** red, green, green |
| Boombox | red, red, green **or** green, green, blue |
| Electric trap | green, blue, blue |
| Revocator | green, green, green |
| Kindle Pops | red, green, blue |

Some machines also give a weapon of rock part with a specific recipe: see the Head-Cutter, Face-Melter, Dischord and Shredder guides.
