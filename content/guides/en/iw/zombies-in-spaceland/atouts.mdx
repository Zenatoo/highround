In Zombies in Spaceland, perks are **candies** sold in vending machines. Each machine only works once the **power is on in its area** (see the "Turn on the power" guide).

| Perk | Effect | Where | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Up 'N Atoms** | Revive teammates faster; in solo, revives you on your own | Spawn, right side of the room, near the Hailstorm | 500 in solo, 1,500 in co-op |
| **Tuff 'Nuff** | Take more hits before going down | Journey into Space, near Star Mission and the Moon Shakes stand | 2,500 |
| **Mule Munchies** | Carry one more weapon | Journey into Space, near the Cosmic Tunes stand | 2,000 |
| **Blue Bolts** | Electric shock around you when you reload | Alley behind the Astrocade | 2,000 |
| **Trail Blazers** | Leaves a trail of fire when you slide | Behind the Astrocade, near the dance floor | 2,000 |
| **Quickies** | Faster reloads and window repairs | Polar Peak, upstairs, near the Ferris wheel | 3,000 |
| **Racin' Stripes** | Run faster | Underground | 2,000 |
| **Bombstoppers** | No more explosive damage | Kepler System, near the gator trap and the fountain | 1,500 |
| **Slappy Taffy** | Stronger melee | Kepler System, stairs between the Chromosphere and the Moonlight Cafe | 2,000 |
| **Bang Bangs** | More damage and a higher fire rate | Kepler System | 2,000 |
