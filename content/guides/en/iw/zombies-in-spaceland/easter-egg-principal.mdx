The goal: shoot down the UFO above the park to get a piece of the soul key. The "Sooooul Key" achievement unlocks at the end.

**Before you start:**

- Plan for **40 minutes to an hour**. Solo is the easiest: in co-op there is one alien per player and less time for the colors.
- Pack-a-Punch your **starting pistol** early: you use it for the whole quest.
- For the last step, you need a **weapon of rock** (Face Melter, Headcutter, Dischord or Shredder) upgraded with Pack-a-Punch.

## Power and Pack-a-Punch

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the five power switches">
    Each switch only powers its own area:

    - **Spawn**: employees-only room, near the M1 wall weapon.
    - **Journey into Space**: hallway between Star Mission and Rover Rampage.
    - **Astrocade**: left of the portal, near the NV4.
    - **Polar Peak**: right of the Ferris wheel, near Quickies.
    - **Kepler System**: upstairs in the Moonlight Cafe, near the portal.

## The Easter Egg

  <Step title="Get the SETI-COM from David Hasselhoff">
    One round after the third part, the screen shakes and the UFO hanging on Polar Peak flashes with four loud sounds. Hasselhoff then waits for you at a Cosmic Tunes stand: in Kepler (left of Octonian Hunter or near the geysers) or in Journey (near Mule Munchies). He gives you the SETI-COM.

  <Step title="Defend the SETI-COM three times">
    One defense per round. Walk around the park: **the screen shakes** at the right spot. Place the SETI-COM and hold out until the timer ends: **60, then 90, then 120 seconds**.

    - **Success**: take the SETI-COM back, a zombie drops a Max Ammo. Wait for the next round.
    - **Failure**: a Brute shows up and Hasselhoff moves to another stand. Get a SETI-COM from him again and retry in the same round.

    Possible spots: fountain near Bombstoppers (Kepler), between the purple and orange geysers (Kepler), middle of the Polar Peak shop, in front of the Polar Peak entrance, pool of blood in front of Escape Velocity (Journey), underground break room, in front of the bumper cars.

  <Step title="Bring the SETI-COM back to Hasselhoff">
    After the third defense, wait **one full round**, then bring him the SETI-COM. He gives you four speakers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place the four speakers">
    Put three speakers on the three golden symbols around the Pack-a-Punch portal, and the fourth right in front of the portal.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={goldenSymbols} alt="The golden symbols around the portal" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={lastSpeaker} alt="Where the fourth speaker goes" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="Zenato">
        If you can, have a weapon of rock in hand before you start the next part: it deals heavy damage to the alien.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Repeat the UFO's colors three times">
    Interact with a speaker (in co-op, all players together). You are locked in Cosmic Way and spawn. The speakers light up red, blue, yellow and green: remember which is which.

    The UFO flashes four colors, each with a sound. Note the order. When the UFO and the speakers turn white, activate the speakers in the same order. You need to get **three sequences** right.

    - In solo: 15 seconds for the first color, then 4 seconds between each. Less in co-op.
    - After a correct answer, the UFO floats for about 40 seconds and then starts again.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="Zenato">
        Not sure about the order? Don't answer: doing nothing costs nothing, while a mistake brings in a Brute. The UFO then shows the same sequence again.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill 50 clowns">
    They come after the third sequence and rush at you to explode: keep your distance and shoot from afar. Then one alien per player comes out of the portal.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Beat the alien: aim at the collar, hit the back">
    The glowing collar around its neck shows its health: green, yellow, then red. Shoot it until the alien lands, raises a bubble and **kneels down**.

    While it kneels, get behind it and melee the **fuse on its backpack**. A breaking sound confirms the hit. Do it **twice**: the third time it goes down, it dies on its own.

    - From afar, it fires a small, fast projectile. Up close, its blast takes about half your health and slows you down.
    - Keep moving in a loop: Cosmic Way portal, spawn, upper bridge, lower bridge, portal.
    - Killing lots of zombies makes more spawn at your feet.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={alienCollar} alt="The alien's collar to aim at" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={alienDowned} alt="The alien kneeling, ready to be hit" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="Zenato">
        This step feels endless if you only shoot: without the hits on its back, the alien doesn't die.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the fuses in the projector">
    The fuses appear in front of the portal and you can leave Cosmic Way again. Put them in the projector: for this game, your weapons can be upgraded a **second time for 10,000 points**, and weapons of rock once for 5,000.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={fuses} alt="The fuses in front of the portal" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={upgradedPap} alt="The upgraded projector" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Destroy the UFO">
    With your upgraded weapon of rock, shoot **four of the five nodes** on the Spaceland ring, around the spawn bridge: each one turns orange.

    **Wait until the UFO is right above the Pack-a-Punch portal**, then shoot the fifth. The ring charges for about 3 seconds and fires into the sky: if the UFO is in the path, it explodes.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={ringNodes} alt="The five nodes on the Spaceland ring" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={orangeNode} alt="A node turned orange" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the Soul Key">
    It appears in front of the portal. Pick it up: the "Sooooul Key" achievement pops. The ending cutscene only plays if you are **Willard Wyler**.

    <Screenshot src={soulKey} alt="The Soul Key in front of the portal" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>
