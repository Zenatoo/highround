The **Smiley** is the Slasher's circular saw. It has two modes: fire its blades as projectiles, or raise it in front of you so the blades spin and cut through anything that comes close. Each zombie killed with the blade raised costs one round of ammo. It can't go through Pack-a-Punch.

It is **the only weapon that kills the Slasher**.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Finish the main Easter Egg">
    You have to do it again in every game where you want the Smiley. See [Main Easter Egg (Locksmith)](../easter-egg-principal/).

    In Director's Cut mode you don't need to: the Smiley is available from the start of the game.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the Smiley in the Bear Lodge basement">
    It is on the rack left of the zombie strapped to the wheel (the knife-throwing game). If you buy another weapon in its place, it goes back on the rack.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'smiley/smiley_pickup.webp'} alt="The Smiley on its rack in the basement" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Kill the Slasher ("Tables Turned" achievement)

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Go into Rave mode from round 10">
    From round 10, the Slasher shows up when you are in Rave mode. See [Rave mode and the Slasher](../mode-rave/).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'rave_mode/slasher.webp'} alt="The Slasher in Rave mode" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill the Slasher with the Smiley">
    Other weapons do nothing to him. Killing him with his own saw unlocks the "Tables Turned" achievement.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
