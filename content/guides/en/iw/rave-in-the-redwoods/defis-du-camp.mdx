Five challenge stations are spread around the camp. Each one gives a challenge that earns a **badge** and a **special grenade**.

- You can only have **one challenge at a time**. You can switch whenever you want, but your progress starts over.
- Challenges and rewards are **not shared** between players.
- After you take a reward, the station waits a full round before giving it again.

Earning all five badges unlocks the "Top Camper" achievement.
