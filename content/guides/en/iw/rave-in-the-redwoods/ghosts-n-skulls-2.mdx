Ghosts 'N Skulls 2 is an arcade machine upstairs in the Bear Lodge. You need **six skulls** to unlock it: the machine shows how many you have. Finishing its mini-game gives you **every perk for the whole game** (except Up 'N Atoms in solo), plus **three more self-revives in solo** (six in all) or unlimited returns from the Afterlife Arcade in co-op.

**Before you start:**

- You need Pack-a-Punch (for the boat) and the **Ben Franklin**, the Wolf version of the Vlad (see [The Vlad: the crossbow and its four upgrades](../vlad/)).
- Several skulls are done in Rave mode: bring pouches (see [Rave mode and the Slasher](../mode-rave/)).
- Each time you complete a skull, a confirmation sound plays.

  <Step title="Skull 1: get the right number of kills with each trap">
    In this order, with **one activation** per trap:

    1. **Log Swing** (outside, in front of the Mess Hall, on the path down to the docks): **1** kill.
    2. **Woodchipper** (archery range, on the small hill above the Recreation Area): **9** kills.
    3. **Rave Stereo** (Recreation Area stage, started at the DJ decks): **9** kills.
    4. **Waterfall** (just outside the Bear Lodge, towards the Mess Hall, behind the waterfall): **2** kills.

    Count the round's zombies to know when to start each trap: in solo, round 4 has 18, so kill 9 with the trap to leave 9. If a trap gets the wrong number, redo only that one. The confirmation sound plays once the Waterfall turns off.

  <Step title="Skull 2: match the machine's green symbol">
    Wait one round after skull 1. In Rave mode, aim at the **green symbol** on the machine until it disappears, then leave Rave mode.

    Wait one more round. Go into Rave mode at the **Turtle Island campfire**: from there you can see every spot. Five green symbols have appeared on the map; aim at the one that is **identical** to the machine's. Aiming at a wrong symbol fails the step: wait a round, take a symbol from the machine again, then wait another round.

    The nine possible spots:

    - **Turtle Island**: in the attic window.
    - **Docks**: in the boathouse rafters.
    - **Beach**: in the life ring, in the Slappy Taffy building.
    - **Mess Hall swamp**: right of the Sewer Tunnel entrance, behind the generator.
    - **Mess Hall**: in the restroom urinal.
    - **Camp Owl**: in the first cabin on the left coming from the mines.
    - **Bear Lodge basement**: in the window the zombies come through.
    - **Recreation Area**: on the big tree in the middle (visible from the stage).
    - **Archery**: on one of the targets.

  <Step title="Skull 3: hit the skull in the knife game">
    In the Bear Lodge basement, play the knife-throwing game. A skull appears somewhere on the wheel: land a knife in it. You earn 50 points and the confirmation sound plays.

    The game can only be started once per round: if you miss, wait for the next one.

  <Step title="Skull 4: spell SKULL with the symbols">
    Wait one round after skull 3 (if it's a special round, wait for the next one). In Rave mode, starting from the Turtle Island campfire, aim at the green symbols that match the letters **S, K, U, L, L**, in that order. They use the same nine spots as skull 2.

    Your progress is kept if you leave Rave mode and come back. If you aim at a wrong symbol, start over the next round.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'iw_symbols.webp'} alt="The chart of symbols and their letters" credit="RayPoopertonIII (image hosted by mmmrkennedy)" source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Skull 5: shoot the skulls from the boat">
    Take a weapon with plenty of ammo (an assault rifle like the NV4, or a machine gun; upgrading it helps). Get on the boat: skulls appear in front of you, shoot them. A skull that turns red is about to escape. After about 10 seconds, they disappear and the sound plays.

    If a skull escapes, try again once the boat is back at the boathouse.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Skull 6: shoot the machine with the Ben Franklin">
    The machine shows all six skulls and electricity comes out of the screen: it is ready.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_6/glowing_machine.webp'} alt="The machine charged with electricity" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Win the mini-game">
    Every player interacts with the machine to enter the game. There are three rounds: catch a coloured ghost with your weapon and throw it at a skull **of the same colour**, before the skulls reach the end of the arena.

    - A skull hit by the wrong colour turns red and rushes to the vortex at the back: a red ghost can still stop it.
    - If **three skulls escape in the same round**, you lose: shoot the machine with the Ben Franklin again.
    - In rounds 2 and 3, the skulls flip over and hide their colour. In round 3 they don't flip back: note the colours beforehand (a photo helps).

    After the third round, you're back in the Bear Lodge with every perk.
  </Step>
