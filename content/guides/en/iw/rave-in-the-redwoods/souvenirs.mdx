Zombies sometimes drop blue, purple or orange **souvenir coins**. Put three of them in a **totem**, in any order, to build an item.

- **Bear Lodge**: at the top of the path down to the amphitheater.
- **Mess Hall**: near the fire.
- **Beach**: near the burning barrel.
- **Recreation Area**: left of the climbing wall door.
- **Mines**: left of the Bang Bangs machine.
