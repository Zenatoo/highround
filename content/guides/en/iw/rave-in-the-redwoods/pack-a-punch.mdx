Pack-a-Punch is in the **Projector Room**. To get there, you repair the boat to **Turtle Island**, then the old projector in the island's cabin.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power">
    See [Turn on the power](../courant/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the handle in the Recreation Area">
    It leans against the stand near the Quickies machine.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'handle.webp'} alt="The handle near Quickies" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the engine in the Mess Hall">
    It sits against the pillar in the middle of the room.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'engine.webp'} alt="The engine against the Mess Hall pillar" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the propeller in the Tuff 'Nuff room">
    Upstairs in the Bear Lodge, in the small room with the Tuff 'Nuff machine, above spawn.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'propeller.webp'} alt="The propeller in the Tuff 'Nuff room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Grab the first film reel on the docks">
    It lies on the docks, in front of the boathouse. No prompt shows from a distance: walk over it to pick it up.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'film_reel_docks.webp'} alt="The film reel on the docks" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        Grab this reel before getting on the boat: you'll have both reels in a single trip.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Repair the boat and leave for Turtle Island">
    The boat is in the boathouse, on the docks. Interact with it to repair it, then get on: after a slow ride, it drops you on the island.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'boathouse.webp'} alt="The boathouse" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Grab the second reel behind the cabin">
    It is right in front of the back door of the Turtle Island cabin, near the campfire.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'film_reel_cabin.webp'} alt="The reel behind the cabin" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Repair the projector">
    In the cabin, put both reels in the broken projector. It projects a portal onto the wall.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'broken_proj.webp'} alt="The broken projector in the cabin" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go through the portal">
    It takes you to the Projector Room. You have **30 seconds** to use Pack-a-Punch (**5,000 points**), then you are sent back to the map. The portal then recharges for about a minute.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Double Pack-a-Punch

It unlocks **once the main Easter Egg is done** (see [Main Easter Egg (Locksmith)](../easter-egg-principal/)). You can then put an already upgraded weapon through Pack-a-Punch again for **10,000 points**.

The Vlad and the Smiley never go through Pack-a-Punch, even with the double upgrade.
