The Rave in the Redwoods Talisman needs **Director's Cut mode**. To unlock it and see what it changes, see [Unlock and use Director's Cut](../../the-beast-from-beyond/director-s-cut/) on The Beast from Beyond. This map's lobby music has its own guide: [Lobby music (Director's Cut)](../musique-salon/).

In Director's Cut, on this map, the **Smiley** is available from the start of the game (see [The Smiley: the Slasher's saw](../smiley/)), and the main Easter Egg is much easier: count 25 to 35 minutes.

## The Rave in the Redwoods Talisman

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Finish the main Easter Egg in Director's Cut">
    The steps are the same as in a normal game. See [Main Easter Egg (Locksmith)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the Talisman">
    After the Super Slasher, the Talisman appears where the Soul Key usually is. Pick it up before leaving the game: you also earn the "Director's Cut II" animated calling card.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'main_ee/talisman.webp'} alt="The Talisman after the Super Slasher" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

The Talismans from Zombies in Spaceland, Rave in the Redwoods, Shaolin Shuffle and Attack of the Radioactive Thing are then used for the fight against Mephistopheles, at the end of The Beast from Beyond's Easter Egg in Director's Cut.
