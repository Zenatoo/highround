This track does not play during the match: it is added to the **lobby** music, the menu before you start a game. You need **Director's Cut mode**: to unlock it, see [Unlock and use Director's Cut](../../the-beast-from-beyond/director-s-cut/) on The Beast from Beyond.

Everything happens **during round 1**. One try per game: if you miss, start a new game.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Start the knife game during round 1">
    In Director's Cut, go down to the Bear Lodge basement during round 1 and start the knife-throwing game, on the zombie strapped to the wheel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hit the white talisman on the zombie's head">
    A white mask (the Talisman) appears on the zombie's head: land a knife in it before it disappears. The new lobby music unlocks.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'lobby_music/white_tailsman.webp'} alt="The white talisman on the zombie's head" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>
