In Rave mode, **ten purple dots** appear in the Bear Lodge. Aim at all of them to start the song "Puppet Strings": it plays right away in the Recreation Area, you earn XP and the "Rave On" achievement, and the song unlocks under "Lobby music" (Barracks).

**Before you start:** turn on the power, take a weapon you can aim down sights with (the starting M1 is enough) and a Rave mode pouch (see [Rave mode and the Slasher](../mode-rave/)).
