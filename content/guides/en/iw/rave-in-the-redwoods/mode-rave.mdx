**Rave mode** changes the look of the map: neon colours on the zombies and the scenery, hidden doors, and quest steps that only work in this mode. You need it for the main Easter Egg, the Vlad, the hidden song, several charms and Ghosts 'N Skulls 2.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get a Rave mode pouch">
    From about round 3, zombies sometimes drop one when they die.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'rave_mode_pouch.webp'} alt="A Rave mode pouch" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use the pouch on a fire">
    Any fire on the map works: the Bear Lodge fireplace, the amphitheater fire, the one in the Recreation Area, the one in front of the Mess Hall, the Turtle Island campfire… Each pouch only works once.

    Rave mode is **per player**. To share it, the others must stand next to you when you use the pouch.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make it last with the fairies">
    The mode lasts **one minute**. Each zombie you kill shortens it by a few seconds; taking hits, buying, using a Fate & Fortune card or reviving someone changes nothing.

    Groups of fairies float around the map: each group you touch adds about **30 seconds**. A used group comes back the next round.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'fairies.webp'} alt="A group of fairies" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

In Rave mode, **your points are doubled**: a good way to earn money early on.

## The hidden wooden doors

Three doors made of branches can only be seen and bought in Rave mode. The rest of the time, they are wooden barriers or walls.

- **Camp Owl**: left of the Volk wall weapon.
- **Mess Hall**: on the raised wooden path to Camp Wolf.
- **Beach**: near the Sewer Tunnel, above the Trail Blazers building.

## The Slasher

From **round 10**, the Slasher shows up when you are in Rave mode. No normal weapon can kill him: only the Smiley can. See [The Smiley: the Slasher's saw](../smiley/).

<Screenshot src={m + 'slasher.webp'} alt="The Slasher" credit={by} source={src} />

For the "Hallucination Nation" achievement, stay **in Rave mode for 5 minutes after round 10**: touch the fairies to last that long, and keep an eye on the Slasher.
