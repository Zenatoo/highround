Rave in the Redwoods has **10 perk machines**. A single switch powers all of them: see [Turn on the power](../courant/).

| Perk | Effect | Where | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Up 'N Atoms** | Revive teammates faster; in solo, revives you on its own | In front of the Bear Lodge, under the upstairs balcony, next to the door to the amphitheater fire | 500 in solo, 1,500 in co-op |
| **Tuff 'Nuff** | You take more hits before going down | Upstairs in the Bear Lodge, small room with bunk beds, through the balcony | 2,500 |
| **Mule Munchies** | Carry one more weapon | Bear Lodge, behind a door to the right of the golf club lying on the couch | 2,000 |
| **Blue Bolts** | Electric blast around you when you reload | On the steps from the Bear Lodge up to the Mess Hall | 2,000 |
| **Bombstoppers** | No more explosive damage | Adventure Course, down in the swamp | 1,500 |
| **Quickies** | Faster reloads and window repairs | Recreation Area, near the speakers and the central structure | 3,000 |
| **Bang Bangs** | More damage and a higher fire rate | In the mines, near the wooden stairs, before the mine cart | 2,000 |
| **Racin' Stripes** | You run faster | Camp Owl, on a cabin balcony | 2,000 |
| **Trail Blazers** | Leaves a trail of fire when you slide | Small building by the beach, behind Slappy Taffy, near the Sewer Tunnel exit | 2,000 |
| **Slappy Taffy** | Stronger melee | In the Bait & Tackle shop, on the lake beach | 2,000 |

Rave in the Redwoods has neither Deadeye Dewdrops nor Change Chews.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="mmmrkennedy" source="https://mmmrkennedyzombiesguides.pages.dev/games/IW/rave_in_the_redwoods/rave_in_the_redwoods_guide">
    Finishing the Ghosts 'N Skulls 2 mini-game gives you every perk until the end of the game (except Up 'N Atoms in solo). See [Ghosts 'N Skulls 2 (every perk for good)](../ghosts-n-skulls-2/).
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source="https://mmmrkennedyzombiesguides.pages.dev/games/IW/rave_in_the_redwoods/rave_in_the_redwoods_guide">
    Failing a challenge of the main Easter Egg takes away a random perk: buy it back before trying again if you need it.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

Weapon charms add ten more effects: see [The 10 weapon charms](../charmes/).
