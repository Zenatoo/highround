There is **only one switch**, and it powers every perk machine on the map. It is in the **Bear Lodge basement**, the starting cabin. You can't get down there from spawn: you have to go around the camp and through the mines.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Head out to the Thunderbird Amphitheater">
    Open the Bear Lodge door that leads to the big campfire, on the Up 'N Atoms machine side.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Cross the Adventure Course">
    Open the door to the obstacle course (the one with the climbing wall), then jump over the broken bridge.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Recreation Area">
    This is the rave area, with the big glowing statue in the middle and the Quickies machine.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go down into the mines">
    Open the mine door (**1,500 points**), go down the stairs and follow the rails: they lead to the Bear Lodge basement.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Flip the switch">
    It is in the basement, in the same room as the zombie strapped to a wooden wheel (the knife-throwing game).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'power/power_switch.webp'} alt="The switch in the Bear Lodge basement" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Once the power is on, you can build the boat that leads to Pack-a-Punch: see [Unlock Pack-a-Punch (and double Pack-a-Punch)](../pack-a-punch/).
