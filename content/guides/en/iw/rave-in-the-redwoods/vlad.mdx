The **Vlad** is a crossbow that fires explosive bolts. It turns into **four different versions**, one per animal statue. Neither the Vlad nor its upgraded versions go through Pack-a-Punch, even with the double upgrade.

**Before you start:** you need Pack-a-Punch, to reach Turtle Island (see [Unlock Pack-a-Punch (and double Pack-a-Punch)](../pack-a-punch/)), and a Rave mode pouch (see [Rave mode and the Slasher](../mode-rave/)).

## Get the Vlad

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Take a hot dog in the Turtle Island cabin">
    Interact with the bucket in the cabin bathroom. The hot dog replaces your tactical equipment: you can throw it, and it comes back after a second.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'hot_dog_bucket.webp'} alt="The bucket of hot dogs in the bathroom" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go into Rave mode">
    Use a pouch on a fire. The Bear Lodge fireplace is the most convenient.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'bear_lodge_fireplace.webp'} alt="The Bear Lodge fireplace" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Knock down the three deer heads">
    Throw a hot dog at each deer head hanging on the wall: it flies off. Shoot it until it breaks, then pick up the **glowing symbol** it drops.

    - **Bear Lodge**: above the couch with the golf club on it.
    - **Mess Hall**: above the fireplace.
    - **Camp Owl**: in one of the cabins you can't enter.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'deer_bear_lodge.webp'} alt="Bear Lodge deer head" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'deer_mess_hall.webp'} alt="Mess Hall deer head" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'deer_owl_camp.webp'} alt="Camp Owl deer head" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'glowing_symbol.webp'} alt="The glowing symbol to pick up" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the crossbow cabinet">
    Leave Rave mode (wait for the timer to run out, kill zombies to shorten it, or get downed). Then interact with the cabinet locked with chains, in the Bear Lodge: the padlock disappears and you can take the Vlad.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'crossbow_cabinet.webp'} alt="The Bear Lodge crossbow cabinet" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

You can carry several Vlads at once. If you buy another weapon in its place, the Vlad goes back to the cabinet.

## The four upgrades

| Statue | Weapon | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deer | **Whirlwind EF-5** | Fires a blast that pushes zombies back, like the Thundergun |
| Eagle | **Trap-o-Matic** | A crossbow in each hand; two bolts stuck close together link up with a deadly laser |
| Owl | **Acid Rain** | Makes acid rain fall around the point of impact |
| Wolf | **Ben Franklin** | Drops a small lightning bolt on the point of impact |

The steps are the same for all four. Only pick up the statue for the version you want.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Find the small animal statue">
    The four statues share **nine spots**, picked at random each game:

    - **Adventure Course**: left of the NV4 wall weapon.
    - **Climbing wall**: on the side opposite the Reaver wall weapon.
    - **Recreation Area**: in the trailer, left of the Type-2 wall weapon.
    - **Mines**: halfway up the stairs near Bang Bangs.
    - **Camp Owl**: on the balcony of the Racin' Stripes cabin.
    - **Camp Wolf**: on the picnic table, left of the RPR Evo wall weapon.
    - **Between Camp Wolf and the Mess Hall**: next to the bench, near the Volk wall weapon.
    - **Docks**: on a table near the Reaver wall weapon (coming from the Mess Hall).
    - **Beach**: behind the canoe leaning on the rack, behind Slappy Taffy.

  <Step title="Charge the statue on the speakers">
    Place it on the speakers left of Quickies, in the Recreation Area (two spots on each side of the middle stack). Damage the zombies around it: those that burst into **red mist** count, those you kill normally don't. You need about **eight**. A sound plays: take the statue back.

    If you place several statues, they charge at the same time.

    <Screenshot src={u + 'speakers.webp'} alt="The speakers left of Quickies" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place the statue in front of its big statue">
    You need an **unupgraded** Vlad in hand for the next part. Place the small statue in front of the big statue of the same animal:

    - **Deer**: on the beach, left of the Fate & Fortune machine, or on the docks, where the walkway leaves towards the Mess Hall.
    - **Eagle**: at the Thunderbird Amphitheater (two spots on the same wall).
    - **Owl**: at Camp Owl, near the mine entrance, or in the middle of the camp, behind the lit "Camp Owl" sign.
    - **Wolf**: at Camp Wolf, near the mine entrance, or at the camp entrance coming from the Mess Hall.

  <Step title="Get about 20 kills with the Vlad">
    Kill zombies near the statue with the Vlad (direct hit or explosion). When it's full, pick up the statue: your Vlad becomes that animal's version.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        An already upgraded Vlad does not charge the statue: take a plain Vlad from the cabinet.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Getting 100 kills with an upgraded Vlad unlocks the "Stick 'em" achievement.
