Rave in the Redwoods has **10 trophies** (achievements on Xbox and PC): 8 bronze and 2 silver. Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Pump It Up (bronze)">
    Upgrade a weapon with Pack-a-Punch. See [Unlock Pack-a-Punch (and double Pack-a-Punch)](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Locksmith (bronze)">
    Get the soul key piece by finishing the main Easter Egg. See [Main Easter Egg (Locksmith)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Super Slacker (bronze)">
    Unlock Kevin Smith: finish the main Easter Egg, then enter his code in the lobby. See [Play as Kevin Smith](../personnages-secrets/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Stick 'em (bronze)">
    Get 100 kills with an upgraded Vlad. See [The Vlad: the crossbow and its four upgrades](../vlad/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tables Turned (silver)">
    Kill the Slasher with his own weapon, the Smiley. See [The Smiley: the Slasher's saw](../smiley/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hallucination Nation (bronze)">
    Stay in Rave mode for 5 minutes after round 10. Touch the groups of fairies to make the mode last, and watch out for the Slasher, who shows up from that round. See [Rave mode and the Slasher](../mode-rave/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Rave On (bronze)">
    Find the hidden song. See [Hidden song: Puppet Strings](../musique-puppet-strings/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Top Camper (bronze)">
    Earn the five camp challenge badges. See [Camp challenges (badges)](../defis-du-camp/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ride For Your Life (bronze)">
    Ride the map's **four ziplines**: the one by the camp cabins, the one in the Recreation Area, the one on the beach, and the one back from Turtle Island (so you need the boat). A zipline only works if the areas at both ends are open.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Scrapbooking (silver)">
    Fill the whole album in the inventory (the screen that shows your quest items): the **photos** row and the **charms** row.

    - **Photos**: build the boat, repair the projector (Pack-a-Punch), get and upgrade the Vlad, and finish Kevin Smith's three challenges to complete his photo.
    - **Charms**: all ten. See [The 10 weapon charms](../charmes/).

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="COD LOUL" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TSWg6K2_5JY">
        Everything has to be done in one and the same game.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>
</Checklist>
