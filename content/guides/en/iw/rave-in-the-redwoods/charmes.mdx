**Charms** are small items that give an extra effect, a bit like a perk. You attach one to any normal weapon, **only one per weapon**. The first time you equip one, a recording tells a character's story.

Each charm spawns in **one of two** possible spots. You then place it somewhere specific, pass a small trial, and pick it back up in the same spot to equip it. All ten charms also count for the "Scrapbooking" achievement (see [The map's 10 achievements and trophies](../succes/)).

| Charm | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Binoculars** | Co-op only: when you reload near a teammate, an electric arc runs between you |
| **Shovel** | Co-op only: reviving a teammate sets off an explosion that kills the zombies around |
| **Arrowhead** | When you aim, your sights lock onto the head |
| **Ring** | You regain health with melee hits |
| **Bird Mask** | Weapons you are not holding slowly refill their magazine |
| **8 Ball** | Sliding into a zombie hurts it |
| **Fish** | The longer you run without stopping, the faster you go |
| **Frog** | You can sprint in water |
| **Pacifier** | Zombies that hit you take damage |
| **Boots** | Heads explode on a headshot and hurt the zombies around |

  <Step title="Arrowhead: place it at the archery range">
    On the counter of the small building next to the archery range.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'arrowhead/countertop.webp'} alt="The archery range counter" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Arrowhead: hit the eight targets in Rave mode">
    Go into Rave mode (the closest fires are in the Recreation Area and at the amphitheater), then come back to the range. Eight targets pop up one by one on the hay bales: you have **2 seconds** to hit each one in solo, less in co-op (0.75 seconds with four players). Miss one and it all starts over. Then equip the arrowhead at the counter.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

    On the beach, next to the bin on the dock with the four canoe racks, or at the Thunderbird Amphitheater, in a dark corner near a pile of beer crates.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'boots/boots_beach.webp'} alt="Boots on the beach dock" credit={by} source={src} />
