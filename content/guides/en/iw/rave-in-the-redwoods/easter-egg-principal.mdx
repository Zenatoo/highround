The goal: help **Kevin Smith** put an old photo back together, then beat the **Super Slasher** on Turtle Island to get a piece of the soul key. The "Locksmith" achievement unlocks at the end.

**Before you start:**

- Count **40 minutes to an hour**.
- You need the power and Pack-a-Punch: see [Turn on the power](../courant/) and [Unlock Pack-a-Punch (and double Pack-a-Punch)](../pack-a-punch/).
- Take the **Banshee** (wall weapon just outside spawn) and keep it for the first two challenges: a shotgun easily tears off arms and legs.
- Each failed challenge takes away **a random perk**, but you can try again right away.

## Kevin Smith's three challenges

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Talk to Kevin Smith">
    In the Turtle Island cabin, use the radio: Kevin appears in the attic, on the beams above you.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'kevin_in_attic.webp'} alt="Kevin Smith in the cabin attic" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Challenge 1: pick up the photo piece behind the statue">
    In the Recreation Area, on the small walkway behind the big glowing statue (the Burning Man).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'photo_1.webp'} alt="The first photo piece behind the statue" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Challenge 1: tear off 10 arms in front of the amphitheater fire">
    Place the photo on the ground in front of the big fire of the Thunderbird Amphitheater, just outside the Bear Lodge. Rave mode starts: tear off the **arms of 10 zombies in 60 seconds**.

    In co-op, everyone stays near the photo when it is placed, and nobody should already be in Rave mode.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'photo_1_amphitheater.webp'} alt="The spot in front of the amphitheater fire" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Challenge 1: kill the Slasher and bring the photo back">
    The photo starts glowing: you have **30 seconds** to interact with it. A Slasher then appears: kill him in under **60 seconds**, or the whole challenge has to be redone. He only takes damage once Rave mode is over.

    When he dies, you get a Max Ammo and a nuke kills every zombie. Take the photo back and bring it to Kevin.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'photo_1_glowing.webp'} alt="The glowing photo" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Challenge 2: pick up the photo piece in the Tuff 'Nuff room">
    Upstairs in the Bear Lodge, on the floor near the door of the Tuff 'Nuff room.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'photo_2.webp'} alt="The second photo piece in the Tuff 'Nuff room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Challenge 2: tear off 10 legs in front of Quickies">
    Place the photo on the ground in front of the Quickies machine, in the Recreation Area. Tear off the **legs of 10 zombies in 60 seconds**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'photo_2_ground_quickies.webp'} alt="The spot in front of Quickies" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="CODZombiesGuides" source="https://codzombiesguides.com/main-quests/infinite-warfare/rave-in-the-redwoods/">
        Gas grenades or the balloon trap (see [Totems and souvenir coins](../souvenirs/)) help a lot with this challenge.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Challenge 2: kill the Slasher and bring the photo back">
    Same as challenge 1: interact with the glowing photo, kill the Slasher in under 60 seconds, then bring the photo back to Kevin.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'photo_2_glowing.webp'} alt="The second glowing photo" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Challenge 3: pick up the skull in the basement">
    In the Bear Lodge basement, on the floor left of the Magic Wheel, on a pile of bones.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_pickup.webp'} alt="The skull left of the Magic Wheel" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Challenge 3: get 10 headshots in front of Slappy Taffy">
    Place the skull on the beach, in front of the Slappy Taffy machine. Get **10 headshots in 60 seconds**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_place_down.webp'} alt="The spot on the beach in front of Slappy Taffy" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Challenge 3: kill the Slasher and take the skull back">
    Same as the first two challenges: interact with the glowing skull, kill the Slasher in under 60 seconds, then take the skull back.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_glowing.webp'} alt="The glowing skull" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The Super Slasher

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Press the buttons in the basement">
    In the Bear Lodge basement, press the buttons on the wall **whose light above is off**: three are right of the stairs, the fourth left of the Magic Wheel. In co-op, each player presses a button at the same time. An alarm confirms it.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'button_no_light.webp'} alt="A button with no light above it" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'three_buttons.webp'} alt="The three buttons right of the stairs" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'one_button.webp'} alt="The button left of the Magic Wheel" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get on the boat with the whole team">
    All players interact with the boat at the same time. This time it doesn't go to Pack-a-Punch: it stops near Turtle Island and the Super Slasher appears.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        Recommended weapons for the fight: FHR-40, Mauler, RPR-Evo, NV4, and the Vlad upgraded into the Acid Rain or the Whirlwind EF-5 (see [The Vlad: the crossbow and its four upgrades](../vlad/)).
      </Tip>
      <Tip type="tech" author="ZombieSlaya Mr." source="https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2589580023">
        Useful Fate & Fortune cards for the fight: Scoped Dollars, Irish Luck and Nade Party.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Charge the skulls with zombie souls">
    You go into Rave mode. Find the skulls (two in solo, more in co-op) and kill zombies next to them: you need about **30 souls per skull**. The skull slowly rises, then a big beam of light shines on it when it's full.

    It then slowly sinks again: recharge it before it drops too far, or you have to fill it again.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'boss_fight_skull.webp'} alt="A skull to charge" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'charged_skull.webp'} alt="A charged skull in its beam of light" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Lure the Super Slasher into the circle">
    The charged skulls form a circle on the ground. Get the boss inside it, walking or with his slam jump (a mark appears on the ground where he'll land). In the circle, the lower part of his torso glows: shoot it. Rave mode stops.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'boss_circle.webp'} alt="The circle formed by the skulls" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'slam_circle.webp'} alt="The mark on the ground before his jump" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'glowing_part_on_slasher.webp'} alt="The glowing lower torso" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the symbols on his body">
    A symbol appears somewhere on the boss: shoot it until it disappears. Another one appears elsewhere. Repeat **about eight times**, until he steps back and beats his chest.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'symbol.webp'} alt="A symbol on the boss's body" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take cover from the storm">
    The boss throws two saw blades that follow you along the ground, jumps onto the cabin roof and starts a storm. Get behind a rock, **inside one of the green circles**, for about 30 seconds. Skeletons arrive: they are much tougher than zombies.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'green_circle.webp'} alt="The green circles behind the rocks" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Repeat the cycle twice">
    Rave mode comes back: skulls, circle, symbols, storm. The dreamcatchers above the cabin show how many cycles are left. From the second cycle, walls block some paths during Rave mode.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'dreamcatcher.webp'} alt="The dreamcatchers above the cabin" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish off the Super Slasher">
    In the third cycle, after the symbols, there is no storm: shoot him until he dies.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the Soul Key">
    The soul key piece appears. Pick it up: the ending cutscene plays and the "Locksmith" achievement pops.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'soul_key.webp'} alt="The Soul Key piece" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Finishing the quest also unlocks:

- **double Pack-a-Punch**: see [Unlock Pack-a-Punch (and double Pack-a-Punch)](../pack-a-punch/);
- the **Smiley**, the Slasher's saw: see [The Smiley: the Slasher's saw](../smiley/);
- **Kevin Smith** as a playable character: see [Play as Kevin Smith](../personnages-secrets/).

In Director's Cut mode, a Talisman appears instead: see [Director's Cut: the Talisman](../director-s-cut-secrets/).
