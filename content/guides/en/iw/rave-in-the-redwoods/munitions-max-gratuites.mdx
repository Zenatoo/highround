The knife-throwing game in the basement can refill your ammo once per round.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Start the knife game in the basement">
    In the Bear Lodge basement (the switch room), interact with the zombie strapped to the wooden wheel. The game can only be started **once per round**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'ghost_and_skulls_2/skull_3/knife_throwing_game.webp'} alt="The knife-throwing game" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Land all six knives in the zombie's head">
    If all six hit the head, you get a Max Ammo. No power-up shows on screen, and only the thrower gets it.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
