The Beast from Beyond has **10 trophies** (achievements on Xbox and PC): 7 bronze and 3 silver. Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Double Feature (bronze)">
    Go through the Cargo Bay portal to the theatre for the first time. See [Unlock Pack-a-Punch (and double Pack-a-Punch)](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Friends Forever (bronze)">
    Insert the four floppy disks into N31L in the right order. See [Main Easter Egg (The End?)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Egg Slayer (bronze)">
    Equip the Venom-X. See [Get the Venom-X](../venom-x/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Encryption Decryption (silver)">
    Upgrade the Venom-X twice with Pack-a-Punch, up to the Venom-Z. See [Upgrade the Venom-X into the Venom-Y, then the Venom-Z](../venom-y-et-venom-z/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Failed Maintenance (bronze)">
    Kill at least one zombie with **each of the five traps** on the map. You need the power and the theatre portal open.

    - The **electric water** trap, between the spawn area and the Medical Bay.
    - The **Freezer** trap, in the cafeteria.
    - A **laser** trap, in a hallway of the base.
    - The **acid** trap, outside.
    - The **Movie Wormhole** trap, on the theatre screen.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Message Sent (bronze)">
    Send the theatre turnstile back in time. See [Send the turnstile back in time (Message Sent)](../message-sent/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Broken Record (bronze)">
    Unlock the hidden song by shooting the fifteen astronauts. See [Hidden song: Scattered Lies](../musique-scattered-lies/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Cracking Skulls (silver)">
    Finish Skull Breaker. See [Skull Breaker (every perk for good)](../skull-breaker/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Super Duper Combo (bronze)">
    In the Skull Breaker mini-game, destroy a group of at least 10 skulls at once.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The End? (silver)">
    Get the last piece of the soul key by finishing the main Easter Egg. See [Main Easter Egg (The End?)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>
