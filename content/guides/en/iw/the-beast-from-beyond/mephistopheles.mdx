Mephistopheles is the **last boss** of Infinite Warfare Zombies, and the hardest. You face him at the end of The Beast from Beyond's Easter Egg, in Director's Cut, with the four Talismans. Beating him unlocks the "Demon Slayer" animated calling card, a Director's Cut camo and the **Willard Wyler** character.

**Before you start:**

- Director's Cut mode must be on and you must have the **four Talismans** (see [Unlock and use Director's Cut](../director-s-cut/)).
- Finish The Beast from Beyond's main Easter Egg in Director's Cut (see [Main Easter Egg (The End?)](../easter-egg-principal/)): after the Mammoths, the fight goes on.
- Keep the **Entangler**: it's used throughout the fight.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
    Practise in the lobby's **Boss Battles** mode: no reward, but you restart the fight right away instead of redoing the whole quest.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Before the fight

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Gear up between the two fights">
    After the Cryptid fight, you can get ready before going in: refill your Fate & Fortune cards, buy back lost perks (especially Up 'N Atoms) and spin the Magic Wheel.

    Recommended weapons: the **Mauler** (Sentinel variant) or the **Venom-Z**; otherwise a VPR or an FHR-40 in an epic variant.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## His attacks

Each attack has its own animation: **watch him all the time**. He mostly throws **fireballs**, but also a black hole, a fire breath, a big ground slam, meteors, a **wall of lava**, a **fire tornado**, and he summons enemies.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
    The wall of lava and the enemy summon start with the same animation: both arms raised. Always assume it's the wall and move aside.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
    Play it safe. Taking your time costs nothing, while dying means redoing the whole quest. Don't stay in a circle to finish filling it if you're about to go down.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## The fight

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Fill a Talisman circle">
    There are **five circles** on the ground, one per Talisman symbol. Stay inside to fill it. Start with the **farthest one**: it gets harder with each circle.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Throw four souls at him">
    The filled circle releases **blue souls**. Grab them with the Entangler and throw **four** at Mephistopheles: he teleports to the middle of the arena.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the glowing symbol">
    The symbol glows on his **chest** (once, it's a blue symbol on his **shoulder**). Shoot it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Survive the horde, then the meteors">
    Enemies arrive in large numbers: clowns the first time, the Slasher the second, **two Mammoths** the fourth. Then comes a shower of meteors: **hide behind a pillar**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill all five circles">
    Repeat for each circle. The fifth always starts with the **fire tornado**: stay in a dip to avoid it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Raise the five Talismans">
    In the last phase, he uses every attack and every type of enemy shows up. Shoot him until **all the Talismans rise**, then use each one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish off Mephistopheles">
    Keep shooting until he dies. The ending cutscene plays.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'directors_cut/meph_dc.webp'} alt="The “Demon Slayer” calling card" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

To play as Willard Wyler afterwards, see [Unlock and use Director's Cut](../director-s-cut/).
