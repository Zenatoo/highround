**Director's Cut** is a bonus mode of Infinite Warfare Zombies: you start with lots of points, every perk and weapons already upgraded. It's also the way to the game's last boss, **Mephistopheles**, and to the **Willard Wyler** character.

## Unlock the mode

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Finish the main Easter Egg on all five maps">
    You need all five, in any game:

    - Zombies in Spaceland ("Sooooul Key" achievement);
    - Rave in the Redwoods ("Locksmith");
    - Shaolin Shuffle ("Pest Control");
    - Attack of the Radioactive Thing ("Soul-Less");
    - The Beast from Beyond ("The End?"), which gives the last piece of the soul key.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use the Soul Jar in a new game">
    Start a **new game** (required), on any map. In the **Projector Room**, use the **Soul Jar**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'directors_cut/soul_jar.webp'} alt="The Soul Jar in the Projector Room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn on Director's Cut in the lobby">
    In the pre-game lobby, an option now lets you turn the mode on or off, in the bottom left. A Director's Cut logo shows when it's on.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## What the mode changes

- **25,000 points** at the start.
- **Every perk** on the map for the whole game, even after going down (except Up 'N Atoms in solo).
- In solo, **6 uses** of Up 'N Atoms; in co-op, instant return from the Afterlife Arcade.
- Magic Wheel weapons come out **already upgraded** once with Pack-a-Punch, and the special weapons are in it too, upgraded when possible.
- The **double Pack-a-Punch** fuses are already in the projector.
- Unlimited, faster Fate & Fortune card refills.
- On Rave in the Redwoods and Shaolin Shuffle, everyone can buy the Smiley and the Katana, even without the quest.

## The Talismans

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Redo the first four Easter Eggs in Director's Cut">
    Spaceland, Rave in the Redwoods, Shaolin Shuffle and Attack of the Radioactive Thing. At the end of each quest, a **Talisman** appears: **pick it up before leaving the game**. Each one also unlocks an animated calling card ("Director's Cut I" to "IV").
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish The Beast from Beyond in Director's Cut">
    With the four Talismans, The Beast from Beyond's quest doesn't stop at the Mammoths: it goes on with the fight against **Mephistopheles**. See [Final fight against Mephistopheles](../mephistopheles/).

    The Beast from Beyond doesn't give a Talisman. Finished in Director's Cut, its quest gives the "Director's Cut V" animated calling card.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'directors_cut/beast_dc.webp'} alt="The “Director's Cut V” calling card" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Lobby music

In Director's Cut, each map hides a white mask that unlocks lobby music during round 1. On The Beast from Beyond, it's the Extinction theme. It's all in [Lobby music: the Extinction theme (Director's Cut)](../musique-salon/).

## Play as Willard Wyler

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Beat Mephistopheles">
    Director's Cut must be on. Beating Mephistopheles also unlocks the "Demon Slayer" animated calling card and a Director's Cut camo.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Willard Wyler's code on Spaceland">
    In the lobby, select Zombies in Spaceland, with Director's Cut on, and enter: **left, left, down, up, up, right**. Willard Wyler's icon appears. If you open another menu (Barracks, loadout…) after the code, enter it again.

    <Screenshot src="iw/zombies-in-spaceland/wyler_code/wyler_icon.webp" alt="Willard Wyler's icon in the lobby" credit={by} source={zisSrc} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish the Spaceland Easter Egg as Willard">
    The real ending cutscene plays and the "Willard's Test" animated calling card unlocks. See the Zombies in Spaceland guide "Director's Cut: the "Willard's Test" calling card".
  </Step>
</Checklist>
