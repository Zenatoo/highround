These tracks don't play during the match: they're added to the **lobby** music, the menu before you start a game. On The Beast from Beyond, a single shot unlocks three: the **Extinction theme** (the alien mode of Call of Duty: Ghosts), the **Mephistopheles theme** and the **Afterlife Arcade** music.

You need **Director's Cut mode**: see [Unlock and use Director's Cut](../director-s-cut/).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power and open the theatre during round 1">
    Start a game in Director's Cut. Before the end of round 1, put N31L's head back in its terminal (see [Turn on the power (N31L's head)](../courant/)), then go through the Cargo Bay portal to the theatre.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the mask at the Arcade shooting gallery">
    In the Arcade, use the **Octonian Hunter** shooting gallery: the white mask comes down from the ceiling and moves across the top of the gallery. Shoot it. One try per game: if you miss, start a new game.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'lobby_music/shooting_gallery.webp'} alt="The Octonian Hunter shooting gallery in the Arcade" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## On the other maps

Each map hides its own white mask, always in Director's Cut and during round 1:

- **Zombies in Spaceland**: with the sunglasses, the mask of the yeti on the Polar Peak roller coaster. See [Lobby music (Director's Cut)](../../zombies-in-spaceland/musique-salon/).
- **Rave in the Redwoods**: the mask on a zombie's head, at the knife-throwing game in the basement. See [Lobby music (Director's Cut)](../../rave-in-the-redwoods/musique-salon/).
- **Shaolin Shuffle**: the mask on the painting near the Rat King's throne. See [Lobby music (Director's Cut)](../../shaolin-shuffle/musique-salon/).
- **Attack of the Radioactive Thing**: the mask in the viewfinder of the TV Studio camera, right of the MacTav-45. See [Lobby music (Director's Cut)](../../attack-of-the-radioactive-thing/musique-salon/).
