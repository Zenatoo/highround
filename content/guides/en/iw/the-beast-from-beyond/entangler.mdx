The Entangler is an item gun: it grabs things and throws them back. It has its own weapon slot. It's used everywhere: two floppy disks and several steps of the main Easter Egg, double Pack-a-Punch, the "Message Sent" achievement, Skull Breaker and the Mephistopheles fight.

You get it with the **first two skulls** of Skull Breaker.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Skull 1: blow open the electrical box">
    A box appears in **one** of these three spots:

    - **Flooded room**: above the stairs down to the Medical Bay.
    - **Ops Center**: above Bombstoppers.
    - **Cargo Bay**: above the stairs, next to the P-LAW wall weapon.

    Open it with a grenade (or the OSA's underbarrel grenade launcher), then shoot the skull that appears inside. A confirmation sound plays.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_1/box_medical.webp'} alt="Box above the Medical Bay stairs" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_1/box_ops.webp'} alt="Box above Bombstoppers" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_1/box_cargo.webp'} alt="Box above the Cargo Bay stairs" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_1/skull.webp'} alt="The skull in the open box" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="tech" author="Gameranx" source="https://gameranx.com/features/id/120160/article/cod-zombies-the-beast-from-beyond-the-complete-easter-egg-guide/">
        Cook the grenade for a moment before throwing it: it explodes in the air, at the box's height.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Skull 2: stand on the red crosses in the Cargo Bay">
    There are **four red crosses** on the Cargo Bay floor:

    - on the upper walkway, near the P-LAW wall weapon;
    - at the bottom of the P-LAW stairs;
    - under the walkway to the theatre portal;
    - near the X-Eon wall weapon.

    Each player stands on a **different** cross (in solo, one is enough). It turns into a green circle and a lockdown starts.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_2/red_x_upper.webp'} alt="Red cross on the upper walkway" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_2/red_x_stairs.webp'} alt="Red cross at the bottom of the stairs" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_2/red_x_portal.webp'} alt="Red cross under the portal walkway" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_2/red_x_xeon.webp'} alt="Red cross near the X-Eon" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hold your spot until the top of the beam">
    Stay in your circle while the skull, in the middle of the room, rises to the top of its beam. Zombies keep coming, but only in the Cargo Bay. If a player leaves their circle, it fails: try again next round.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_2/skull_middle_room.webp'} alt="The skull in the middle of the Cargo Bay" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Jump into the skull">
    Once at the top, the skull moves somewhere else in the Cargo Bay. Run and **jump into it**: it disappears after a few seconds. Missed it? Start the lockdown again next round.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_2/skull_moved.webp'} alt="The skull after it moved" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the Entangler">
    It appears in the middle of the Cargo Bay.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_2/entangler.webp'} alt="The Entangler in the middle of the Cargo Bay" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

For the rest of the skulls, see [Skull Breaker (every perk for good)](../skull-breaker/).
