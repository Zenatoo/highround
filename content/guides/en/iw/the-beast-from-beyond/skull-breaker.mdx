Skull Breaker is an arcade machine hidden in the **theatre's Arcade**. You need **six skulls** to unlock it. Finishing its mini-game gives you **every perk for the whole game** (except Up 'N Atoms in solo), and your Fate & Fortune cards recharge faster. The "Cracking Skulls" achievement unlocks.

**Before you start:** the power and access to the theatre (see [Unlock Pack-a-Punch (and double Pack-a-Punch)](../pack-a-punch/)). The machine shows how many skulls you have, and each completed skull plays a confirmation sound.

  <Step title="Skull 4: follow the skull's path">
    From the Bang Bangs walkway, in the Ops Center, jump down into the spawn area: a small **red cross** is where you land. Stand on it: a **pink** or **green** skull appears in front of you. Go and get it, then follow the symbols of that colour on the ground to the next skull.

    **Walk, don't run.** The game sometimes swaps the colours of the next symbols: look carefully. A wrong symbol or running out of time (the blue bar on screen) fails the step: try again next round.

  <Step title="Skull 5: place the eight queens">
    On a cafeteria table, a chessboard holds **one queen**. Find the other seven and place them so no queen threatens another (the eight queens puzzle). **mmmrkennedy's solver** gives the solution based on your first queen's square. You can carry several queens at once.

    The queens are among these **twelve spots**:

    - **Cafeteria**: right of a couch; on a counter right of the Freezer trap.
    - **Cargo Bay**: on the right going down the stairs to the lowest level.
    - **Proteus hallway**: on the way outside, left of the zombie entrance; upstairs, near the open railing closest to the Ops Center.
    - **Ops Center**: at the foot of the big pillar, on the EBR-800 wall weapon side; in a corner left of Bombstoppers; in the room above Bombstoppers, in the corner closest to Bombstoppers.
    - **Medical Bay**: on the floor under the dissected Cryptid's table, at the head end; on a scale, on the counter under the broken screen in the force field room (you need to have opened that room, see the main Easter Egg).
    - **Spawn**: under the zombie entrance on the flooded room stairs; at the top of the ladder, across from Up 'N Atoms.

    When all eight queens are placed correctly, confetti bursts out of the chessboard.

  <Step title="Skull 6: enter the cheat code at the machine">
    Three sheets with arrows are hidden among these **six spots**:

    - **Cafeteria**: on a counter left of the Freezer trap, next to a tray.
    - **Medical Bay**: on the counter, under the six green screens on the wall.
    - **Medical Bay**: in the force field room, right of the floppy disk.
    - **Projector Room**: on the red toolbox, left of the portal.
    - **Theatre restrooms**: in the sink, right of a blood stain on the wall.
    - **Theatre lobby**: on the candy counter, in front of the popcorn machine.

    Each sheet matches one of the nine pieces in the chart below: group 1 is the **start** of the code, group 2 the **middle**, group 3 the **end**. Stand right up against the machine and enter the full code on the D-pad, **less than a second** between each arrow. A sound and a line from your character confirm it.

  <Step title="Win the mini-game's three rounds">
    You have to destroy every skull before they reach the end, over **three rounds**. With the Entangler:

    - grab a **coloured cube** and fire it at a skull to change its colour. Two skulls of the same colour side by side (or diagonally) form a group;
    - grab a **bomb** of the same colour and fire it at the group to destroy it.

    A bomb of the wrong colour turns the group red and it escapes: a red bomb destroys it. A group of **10 skulls or more** unlocks the "Super Duper Combo" achievement. If you fail, you can play again right away with the same code.

    At the end, you're back in the Arcade with every perk.
  </Step>
