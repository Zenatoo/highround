**Fifteen plush astronauts** are hidden: eleven in the base and four in the theatre. They're always in the same spots. Shoot all fifteen: your character raises a fist, you earn **2,000 XP** and **Scattered Lies** starts playing, in the base and the theatre (not outside). The "Broken Record" achievement unlocks.

**Looking for the Extinction theme?** That's another hidden track, for the lobby. It unlocks by shooting a white mask during round 1, but only in Director's Cut. See [Lobby music: the Extinction theme (Director's Cut)](../musique-salon/).

**Before you start:** you need Pack-a-Punch and access to the theatre (see [Unlock Pack-a-Punch (and double Pack-a-Punch)](../pack-a-punch/)).
