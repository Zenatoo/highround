A small secret that unlocks the "Message Sent" achievement: send the theatre's **turnstile** back in time. It answers Shaolin Shuffle's "Message Received" achievement, where you repair a subway turnstile.

**Before you start:** you need the Entangler (see [Get the Entangler](../entangler/)) and access to the theatre.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Open the vortex with the 197# console">
    Outside, in the snow, use the console near the Magic Wheel until it shows **197#**. A vortex appears on its left.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'console.webp'} alt="The console in the snow" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'vortex.webp'} alt="The vortex left of the console" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Grab the turnstile in the theatre street">
    In the street in front of the theatre, the turnstile is behind the dumpster with the **KBS Longbow** wall weapon, on the left side. Grab it with the Entangler.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'turnstile.webp'} alt="The turnstile behind the dumpster" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fire the turnstile into the vortex">
    Carry it back outside, on foot, and fire it into the vortex. If the vortex is gone, enter 197# on the console again. A few seconds later, the vortex disappears and the achievement unlocks.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
