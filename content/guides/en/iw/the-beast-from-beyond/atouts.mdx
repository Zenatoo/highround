On The Beast from Beyond, there are **11 perk machines**. They all work as soon as the **power** is back on (see [Turn on the power (N31L's head)](../courant/)). There is **no Slappy Taffy** on this map. Six machines are in the base, one outside and four in the theatre: you need to open the Cargo Bay portal for those (see [Unlock Pack-a-Punch (and double Pack-a-Punch)](../pack-a-punch/)).

| Perk | Effect | Where | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Up 'N Atoms** | Revive teammates faster; in solo, revives you on its own | Spawn area, across from the ladder | 500 in solo, 1,500 in co-op |
| **Tuff 'Nuff** | You take more hits before going down | Ops Center, on the ledge at the bottom of the hole (green light) | 2,500 |
| **Bang Bangs** | More damage and a higher fire rate | Ops Center, on the upper walkway | 2,000 |
| **Bombstoppers** | No more explosive damage | Ops Center, under the control room | 1,500 |
| **Blue Bolts** | Electric blast around you when you reload | Medical Bay, near the stairs | 2,000 |
| **Trail Blazers** | Leaves a trail of fire when you slide | Cargo Bay, at the very bottom, between the containers | 2,000 |
| **Change Chews** | A random elemental effect after a number of headshots | Outside, in the snow, on the right as you leave the cafeteria | 1,500 |
| **Quickies** | Faster reloads and window repairs | Theatre, right as you come out of the Cargo Bay portal | 3,000 |
| **Deadeye Dewdrops** | Aim locks onto the head | Theatre, in the street, on the left | 1,500 |
| **Racin' Stripes** | You run faster | Theatre, side alley (at the top of the stairs, outside) | 2,000 |
| **Mule Munchies** | Carry one more weapon | Theatre, dark alley with red lights | 2,000 |
