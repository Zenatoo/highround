The Venom-X fires a **Cryptid egg** that bursts after two seconds (or when you aim) into a cloud of gas that hurts enemies. Killed Cryptids can drop an egg that refills a little ammo. Equipping the Venom-X unlocks the "Egg Slayer" achievement.

**Before you start:** the power and Pack-a-Punch. Your first trip out of Pack-a-Punch spawns a Phantom, useful for the first step.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Lure a Phantom into the electric water trap">
    The trap is between the spawn area and the Medical Bay. Bring a **Phantom** (big blue Cryptid) into the water and set off the trap while it's in there. It just has to take damage, not necessarily die.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'water_trap.webp'} alt="The electric water trap" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Press the right colour button">
    In the Ops Center, the box on the wall at the bottom of the stairs near **Bang Bangs** is now open. It holds **3 to 6 colour buttons** (red, green, blue, black, yellow, white).

    The right button depends on the number of buttons, their colours and your round. The easiest is **mmmrkennedy's solver**: pick the number of buttons, your round, then the colours from top to bottom. The chart below gives the same rules.

    - **Right button**: success sound, the box closes.
    - **Wrong button**: error sound, a Phantom appears. The box opens again with new buttons at the end of the round.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'buttons.webp'} alt="The open box with its colour buttons" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'venom_x_button_info.webp'} alt="The chart of button rules" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="tech" author="mmmrkennedy" source={solver}>
        The game tracks one of its own variables badly: the solver can sometimes be wrong. It's not you, it's a game bug.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Lock a zombie in the containment chamber">
    Still in the Ops Center: when a zombie is in the **circle on the floor** of the chamber, use the panel left of the Magic Wheel. The zombie is locked in and a timer shows at the top of the chamber: **5 minutes in solo**, 3 minutes in co-op.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'containment_chamber.webp'} alt="The containment chamber" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'containment_circle.webp'} alt="The circle on the floor" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'containment_activate.webp'} alt="The panel left of the Magic Wheel" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'containment_time.webp'} alt="The timer at the top of the chamber" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn on the consoles above Bombstoppers">
    While the zombie is locked in, go up to the room above Bombstoppers and use the **middle console**. The two consoles on each side light up.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'middle_console.webp'} alt="The middle console" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Solve the four mazes">
    On a console's top screen, there's a red square and a blue diamond. Aim at the middle of the screen and move the red square with the **D-pad** to the diamond, without touching the invisible walls. The square only moves about once a second: wait between presses.

    You need **four mazes** in all: three on one console, one on the other. mmmrkennedy's solver draws the path.

    A failure kills the zombie in the chamber and spawns a Phantom: try again next round with a new zombie.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'maze_screen.webp'} alt="A maze screen" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="tech" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        On PC, under Controls then Actions, bind "Use Souvenir" to left arrow, "Toggle Kung-Fu Mode" to right arrow, "Next Card" to up arrow and "Previous Card" to down arrow: you can then use the arrow keys like a D-pad.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the Venom-X">
    A door opens left of the button box: the Venom-X is behind it. If you plan to upgrade it, **keep the zombie in the chamber alive** for the start of the next part.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'venom_x_pickup.webp'} alt="The Venom-X behind the door" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

To upgrade it, see [Upgrade the Venom-X into the Venom-Y, then the Venom-Z](../venom-y-et-venom-z/).
