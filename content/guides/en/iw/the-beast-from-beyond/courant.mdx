At the start of the game, it isn't zombies attacking you but **Cryptids**, the aliens from Extinction. To turn the power back on, you have to bring **N31L's head** back to its terminal. The next round, the zombies return.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Open the way to the Ops Center">
    It's the big main room, with a hole in the middle of the floor.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'hole.webp'} alt="The hole in the middle of the Ops Center" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="tech" author="Activision" source="https://blog.activision.com/call-of-duty/archives/how-to-survive-the-beast-from-beyond">
        The Cryptids in the first rounds are fast and hit hard. Buy a second weapon quickly and hold a hallway or a high point.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Drop into the hole and aim for the ledge">
    Let yourself fall and land on the **ledge at the very bottom**, marked by a green light. If you miss it, you die. The **Tuff 'Nuff** machine is on this ledge.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up N31L's head">
    Follow the hallway to the end: the head is on the floor.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'head.webp'} alt="N31L's head on the floor at the end of the hallway" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the head back in its terminal">
    Go back to the spawn area and put the head in the slot on the wall, near the door. The power comes back: perk machines, Magic Wheel… From the **next round**, zombies replace the Cryptids.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'slot.webp'} alt="The head slot near the spawn door" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>
