The goal: repair N31L, install him in the Projector Room, then survive a final fight against the Cryptids. That's how you get the **last piece of the soul key** and the "The End?" achievement.

**Before you start:**

- Count **45 minutes to an hour** in solo. The quest is shorter than on the other maps, but it needs the Entangler first.
- You need the **power**, **Pack-a-Punch** (see [Unlock Pack-a-Punch (and double Pack-a-Punch)](../pack-a-punch/)) and the **Entangler** (see [Get the Entangler](../entangler/)).
- Before the final fight, get your perks and upgrade your weapons, with double Pack-a-Punch if you can. Doable in solo, but much easier as a team.

## The four floppy disks

You can pick them up in any order, but **only one at a time**. Each one has a symbol, shown in the bottom left of the screen above your points while you hold it.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="The Phantom's floppy disk">
    The first **Phantom** (big blue Cryptid) you kill drops it. One always appears the first time you come out of Pack-a-Punch. The floppy disk stays on the ground.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        Kill this Phantom near N31L, in the spawn area: you won't have to cross the map with the floppy disk.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="The snow floppy disk">
    Outside, in the snow, at the foot of a pillar **left of the Pack-a-Punch portal**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'floppy_pap.webp'} alt="The floppy disk at the foot of the pillar" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="The red room floppy disk">
    In the hallway with the **Proteus** wall weapon, a grate closes off a **red room**, under the stairs. The floppy disk is on the floor, in front of a vent that smokes now and then.

    Grab it with the Entangler and fire it into the vent **while it's smoking**. If you miss, the floppy disk reappears on the floor: try again. If it works, it gets sucked in.

## Hack N31L

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Insert the floppy disks in the right order">
    In front of N31L, in the spawn area, there are **four slots**. The order depends on the symbols: in the chart below, find the row that contains your four symbols and insert the floppy disks in that row's order, from left to right. The game also hides these lists on sheets around the map, but the chart is faster.

    If it's right, the scoreboard flashes **yellow then red** and the "Friends Forever" achievement unlocks. N31L turns **evil**: he closes doors at random (you reopen them for free) and sets off traps.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'floppy_cheat.webp'} alt="The floppy disk symbol chart" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="codzombiesguides.com" source="https://codzombiesguides.com/main-quests/infinite-warfare/the-beast-from-beyond/">
        A wrong order spawns Cryptids and makes you lose the floppy disks: you have to get them again, with new symbols. Check twice before inserting.
      </Tip>
      <Tip type="tech" author="Player.One" source="https://www.player.one/call-duty-infinite-warfare-zombies-guide-beast-beyond-easter-egg-120086">
        In co-op, give one floppy disk to each player: everyone calls out their symbol.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fire the theatre button into a poster">
    In the theatre, grab the **button on the wall** behind the Brute cardboard cutout with the Entangler. Fire it into a **Beast from Beyond** poster in the lobby, between the Arcade and the candy counter. If you drop it, it reappears on the wall right away.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'button_theatre.webp'} alt="The button behind the Brute cutout" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'beast_poster.webp'} alt="A Beast from Beyond poster" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Press the button under the Medical Bay desk">
    In the force field room, **go prone** in front of the table where the floppy disk was and use the button under the desk. A panel of **16 handles** (4 × 4) opens in the dissected Cryptid's room.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'button_med_bay.webp'} alt="The button under the desk" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn all 16 handles the same way">
    The goal: every handle pointing the same way, all vertical or all horizontal. Turning one handle also turns others. The method that works:

    1. Note the **horizontal** handles at the start.
    2. Turn each of them once, even if it moved in the meantime.
    3. If some are still horizontal, repeat with those, until everything is vertical.

    N31L is then **hacked** for about **5 minutes**, then turns evil again. You can redo the handles right away.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'handles.webp'} alt="The panel of 16 handles" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Install N31L

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Grab N31L with the Entangler">
    While he's hacked, grab N31L with the Entangler. **Every door closes** and the zombies come. Doors only open if N31L looks at them: turn around in front of a closed door until it opens.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="glitch" author="mmmrkennedy" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ed0xAyO3giA">
        Before grabbing N31L, fire the Entangler at an N31L TV, switch weapons while firing, then take the Entangler back. You can then open doors normally while carrying him.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Install N31L in the Projector Room">
    Fire N31L at the **computer right of the projector**. When everyone is ready, use it to start the final fight (in co-op, all players together).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'neil_computer_pap.webp'} alt="The computer right of the projector" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The final fight

You arrive in another Cargo Bay.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Find the ammo crate">
    In an open crate, a **glowing yellow box** refills the ammo of the weapon in your hands, free and without limit.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'ammo_crate.webp'} alt="The yellow ammo box" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill the four Rhinos">
    A laser cannon opens the crates one by one: **four Rhinos** come out, a few seconds apart.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Survive the portals">
    The portals on one side of the room send Cryptids and Phantoms for about a minute, then those on the other side, then all of them at once. The laser then targets you too.

    Set up in the **sloped crate with a roof**: the laser doesn't go through, and the Cryptids only come from below.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'sloped_crate.webp'} alt="The sloped crate with a roof" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn off the three screens">
    After about 45 seconds, **three screens** light up. Turn all three off quickly, or a screen (and its portals) comes back on. From the sloped crate, in this order:

    1. in the corner, behind and left of the ammo crate;
    2. in the corner, behind and right of the ammo crate;
    3. at the bottom of the sloped crate.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'console_1.webp'} alt="First screen" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'console_2.webp'} alt="Second screen" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'console_3.webp'} alt="Third screen" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for the countdown to end">
    The laser opens a crate on a computer showing **99**. The countdown lasts about **2 minutes 30** (not 99 seconds) and enemies keep coming. At zero, the computer sparks: use it, the Cryptids disappear.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill the two Mammoths">
    The lights dim and **two Mammoths** (blue Rhinos) come out of the crate above the ammo. They soak up a huge amount of damage. Every 30 to 60 seconds, the portals drop 3 to 5 Cryptids.

    When they take damage, they leave a **blue fire** on the ground that hurts a lot and doesn't go out: don't kill them in a walkway. Aim for the mouth.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'mammoth_crate.webp'} alt="The Mammoth crate above the ammo" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="Decezy" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o9IU7jnKD9s">
        With the Ego Trip Fate & Fortune card and a Mauler (Sentinel variant) or an EBR-800 (Bomber variant), the Mammoths die in one hit.
      </Tip>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        Their charge always puts you on a red screen, even with Tuff 'Nuff. Keep your distance.
      </Tip>
      <Tip type="tech" author="Player.One" source="https://www.player.one/call-duty-infinite-warfare-zombies-guide-beast-beyond-easter-egg-120086">
        Recommended weapons for the fight: FHR-40, VPR, Kendall 44.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Watch the ending cutscene">
    With both Mammoths dead, the cutscene plays and the "The End?" achievement unlocks. You then return to the map.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

After the quest, the map goes haywire: rounds ending too early, Cryptids not really spawning, Pack-a-Punch out of reach. Better to end the game there.

In Director's Cut mode, the quest gives the "Director's Cut V" animated calling card. With the four Talismans, it leads to the fight against Mephistopheles: see [Unlock and use Director's Cut](../director-s-cut/) and [Final fight against Mephistopheles](../mephistopheles/).
