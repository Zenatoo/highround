Both upgrades rely on **Morse code**: a key, a message encrypted with that key, then a number of enemies to kill. Upgrading the Venom-X twice unlocks the "Encryption Decryption" achievement.

- **Venom-Y**: its gas freezes and kills zombies (about 5 seconds). Big Cryptids (Phantoms, Rhinos…) only take the explosion.
- **Venom-Z**: the gas lasts longer and the weapon has more ammo.

**Before you start:** you need the Venom-X (see [Get the Venom-X](../venom-x/)). In the options, set the **dialogue volume to 0**: your character won't talk over the beeps any more.

## Venom-Y

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Listen to your key at the terminal right of Bang Bangs">
    In the Ops Center, the terminal right of Bang Bangs plays a word in Morse code: **ARCHER** or **CROSS**. That's your key.

    - ARCHER: `.- .-. -.-. .... . .-.`
    - CROSS: `-.-. .-. --- ... ...`

    <Screenshot src={m + 'venom_x/venom_y/key_terminal.webp'} alt="The key terminal right of Bang Bangs" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find your number at the terminal at the bottom of the stairs">
    The terminal at the bottom of the stairs near Bang Bangs plays a long message encrypted with your key. After the start of the message, listen to the **first two letters** of the number:

    | Number | With the ARCHER key | With the CROSS key |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | 15 | `.... .--.` | `-..- .-` |
    | 18 | `--. .--.` | `.-- .-` |
    | 20 or 25 | `...- -..` | `.-.. ---` |
    | 30 | `...- ---` | `.-.. --..` |

    The easiest is still **mmmrkennedy's solver**: you type the beeps as you go and it gives you the number.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'venom_x/venom_y/code_terminal.webp'} alt="The message terminal at the bottom of the stairs" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the number in Morse in the control room">
    Go up to the control room above Bombstoppers. On the panel at the top of the stairs, the left button makes a short beep, the right one a long beep. Spell the number **in English**: FIFTEEN, EIGHTEEN, TWENTY or THIRTY. For 20 or 25: enter TWENTY, and if no success sound follows, enter FIVE.

    - **Right**: a pickup sound.
    - **Wrong**: an error sound at the end of the round. Start again.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'venom_x/venom_y/morse_input_panel.webp'} alt="The Morse panel at the top of the stairs" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill that many Cryptids with the Venom-X">
    Your progress shows in the bottom right of the scoreboard.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the Venom-X through Pack-a-Punch">
    It becomes the **Venom-Y** for 5,000 points.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Venom-Z

**Before you start:** you need double Pack-a-Punch (see [Unlock Pack-a-Punch (and double Pack-a-Punch)](../pack-a-punch/)) and the Venom-Y.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Swap your key">
    Your key becomes the other word: ARCHER becomes CROSS, and the other way round. The terminal right of Bang Bangs gives it to you again if you're not sure.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Redo the message and the Morse code">
    Listen to the terminal at the bottom of the stairs with your new key, then enter the new number on the control room panel, as for the Venom-Y. The number changes.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill special enemies with the Venom-Y">
    This time, they have to be **special** enemies: any Cryptid, ninjas or clowns. The progress stops at **50%**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make the ghosts appear with the 195# console">
    Outside, use the console near the Magic Wheel until it shows **195#**. Ghosts start floating around you.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'message_sent/console.webp'} alt="The console in the snow near the Magic Wheel" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the ghosts near the Pack-a-Punch portal">
    Go near the Pack-a-Punch portal and shoot as many ghosts as your number, with any weapon, **in the same round**. The progress goes up to 100%.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the Venom-Y through double Pack-a-Punch">
    It becomes the **Venom-Z** for 10,000 points.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
