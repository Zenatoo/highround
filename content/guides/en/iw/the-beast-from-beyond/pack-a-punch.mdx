Pack-a-Punch is in the Projector Room. You get there through a portal outside, once the bridge is repaired with **three pieces of metal**. One of them is in the theatre: so you need to open the theatre portal first.

**Before you start:** turn the power back on (see [Turn on the power (N31L's head)](../courant/)).

## Open the theatre

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Press the button on the Cargo Bay walkway">
    In the Cargo Bay, the button is on the wall of the **upper walkway**. A turret comes into the room and fires at the opposite wall: a **portal to the theatre** opens. Going through it the first time unlocks the "Double Feature" achievement.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'theatre/button.webp'} alt="The button on the Cargo Bay walkway" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'theatre/portal_cargo.webp'} alt="The Cargo Bay portal to the theatre" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the theatre's return portals">
    Two more portals link the theatre to the base. To activate one, use the symbol on the ground in front of it.

    - **Street in front of the theatre**: leads to the hallway near the Medical Bay.
    - **Alley left of the theatre**: leads to the spawn area.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'theatre/portal_street.webp'} alt="The street portal" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'theatre/portal_hallway.webp'} alt="The exit in the hallway near the Medical Bay" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'theatre/portal_alley.webp'} alt="The alley portal" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'theatre/portal_spawn.webp'} alt="The exit in the spawn area" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Pack-a-Punch

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Pick up the three pieces of metal">
    In any order. Each piece you pick up spawns **three Cryptids**.

    - **Cargo Bay**: at the very bottom, left of the stairs, in the corner near a container.
    - **Medical Bay**: right of the **Blue Bolts** machine, in the dark corner at the bottom of the stairs.
    - **Theatre**: on a **burning car** in the street, right of the portal.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/metal_cargo_bay.webp'} alt="Piece of metal in the Cargo Bay" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/metal_med_bay.webp'} alt="Piece of metal near Blue Bolts" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/metal_theatre.webp'} alt="Piece of metal on the burning car" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Repair the broken bridge">
    Outside, in the snow, place the three pieces on the **broken bridge**. Cross it and use the big portal on the other side.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/broken_bridge.webp'} alt="The broken bridge outside" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upgrade your weapon in the Projector Room">
    Pack-a-Punch costs **5,000 points**. You have **30 seconds** there, then you're sent back to the base. The portal then recharges for about **a minute**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get ready for the return">
    The first time you're sent back in a round, a **Phantom** (big blue Cryptid) or **three Cryptids** appear. The first Phantom you kill drops a **floppy disk** for the main Easter Egg: remember where it dies.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        The Phantom is tough: keep a good weapon on you when you leave Pack-a-Punch.
      </Tip>
      <Tip type="faster" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        Kill this first Phantom near N31L, in the spawn area: the floppy disk stays on the ground and you won't have to come back for it.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Double Pack-a-Punch

It lets you upgrade an already upgraded weapon **a second time**, for **10,000 points**, until the end of the current game. It needs the **Entangler** (see [Get the Entangler](../entangler/)). In Director's Cut mode, the fuses are already in the projector.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get the alien fuses">
    In the hallway with the **Proteus** wall weapon, between the cafeteria and the Cargo Bay, shoot the electrical box on the wall to open it. Take the fuses inside.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'double_pap/fuse_box.webp'} alt="The closed electrical box" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'double_pap/fuse_box_open.webp'} alt="The open box with the fuses" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the fuses in the Sasquatch's hand">
    In the theatre, in the hallway between the big screening room and the lobby, put the fuses in the hand of the **Sasquatch cardboard cutout**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'double_pap/sasquatch.webp'} alt="The Sasquatch cutout" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Swap the projector reel">
    Take the **film reel** on the right side of the candy counter, in the theatre lobby. In the Projector Room, use the reels at the front of the projector to put it in place: it now projects footage from Zombies in Spaceland.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'double_pap/film_reel.webp'} alt="The reel on the counter" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'double_pap/projector.webp'} alt="The reels at the front of the projector" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pull a Brute helmet out of the screen">
    Start the theatre's **Movie Wormhole trap**. While it runs, fire the Entangler at the vortex on the screen: a **Brute helmet** comes out. Drop it on the ground, then pick it up.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'double_pap/wormhole_trap.webp'} alt="The Movie Wormhole trap" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'double_pap/vortex.webp'} alt="The vortex on the screen" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the head on the Brute cutout">
    It's next to the Sasquatch. If the fuses are already in its hand, the Brute head fires a laser at them and **charges** them.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'double_pap/brute.webp'} alt="The Brute cutout near the Sasquatch" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the charged fuses in the projector">
    Bring them back to the Projector Room. Double Pack-a-Punch is ready.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
