Shaolin Shuffle has **10 trophies** (achievements on Xbox and PC): 7 bronze and 3 silver. Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Pest Control (silver)">
    Get the soul key piece by finishing the main Easter Egg. See [Main Easter Egg (Pest Control)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Message Received (bronze, hidden)">
    During the third part of the main Easter Egg, repair the turnstile in the spawn subway with the coin earned in the burning street.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Soul Brother (bronze)">
    Collect all the newspaper clippings. Your inventory on this map is a newspaper page whose pictures fill in with the quest items: the achievement comes with the end of the main Easter Egg.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Exterminator (bronze, hidden)">
    Beat the final boss with the special guest: finish the Rat King fight playing as Pam Grier. See [Play as Pam Grier](../personnages-secrets/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sliced and Diced (bronze)">
    Put the katana through Pack-a-Punch. See [The Kirisute Gomen katana](../katana/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shaolin Skills (bronze)">
    Max out all four Chi styles in the same game. See [Chi powers, fast travel doors and Torii portals](../pouvoirs-chi/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Book Worm (silver)">
    Collect all the biographies of AJ, Andre, Poindexter and Sally. See [Nunchucks and the characters' biographies](../nunchakus/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Coin-Op (silver)">
    Finish Skullbuster. See [Skullbuster (every perk for the whole game)](../skullbuster/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Beat of the Drum (bronze, hidden)">
    Find the hidden song. See [Hidden song: Cats on the Boulevard](../musique-cats-on-the-boulevard/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Some Assembly Required (bronze)">
    Build every craftable on the map in the same game. See [The 5 Mahjong craftables](../equipements-mahjong/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>
