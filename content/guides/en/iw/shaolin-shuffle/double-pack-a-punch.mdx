The alien fuses let you upgrade a weapon that already went through Pack-a-Punch **a second time**, for **10,000 points**. It only lasts for the current game.

**Before you start:**

- Open Pack-a-Punch (see [Unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/)).
- With a Chi power, open the pair of fast travel doors **Racin' Stripes roof ↔ Main Street** (next to Blue Bolts). See [Chi powers, fast travel doors and Torii portals](../pouvoirs-chi/).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Press the button in the Pack-a-Punch room">
    In the Pack-a-Punch room, press the button on the wall. A hidden timer starts.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/double_pap/proj_room_button.webp'} alt="The button in the Pack-a-Punch room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Run to grab the fuses on the roof">
    As soon as you're back on the map, take the Main Street fast travel door, next to Blue Bolts. On the other side, on the Racin' Stripes roof, take the **uncharged alien fuses** from the box on the wall, next to the door.

    You have less than half a minute before the box closes: don't take any detour. Too late? Start again as soon as the Pack-a-Punch portal is available again.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/double_pap/fuse_box.webp'} alt="The fuse box on the roof" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Let the train charge the fuses">
    Place the fuses on the electrified rails of the Main Street subway (either side works). Wait for the train to run over them, then pick them up: they're charged.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/double_pap/electric_rails.webp'} alt="The electrified rails of the Main Street subway" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the fuses in the projector">
    Insert them in the Pack-a-Punch projector. Any weapon that is already upgraded can now be upgraded a second time for **10,000 points**, until the end of the game.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/projector.webp'} alt="The Pink Cat projector" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>
