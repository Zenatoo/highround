Three **alarm clocks** send you back in time to hit items linked to your character. At the end, a fire sale puts the **nunchucks** in the Magic Wheel. Once the quest is done with any character, the nunchucks stay in the Magic Wheel **in all your later games**. They can be upgraded with Pack-a-Punch.

**Before you start:**

- Open **both pairs of fast travel doors** and **every Torii portal** (see [Chi powers, fast travel doors and Torii portals](../pouvoirs-chi/)).
- The quest can't be done playing as Pam Grier.
- Failed a clock? Finish the round to try again.

  <Step title="First clock: inside the disco">
    Find your character's item on a wooden pallet left of Blue Bolts: a trophy for AJ, a microphone for Andre, a newspaper for Sally, a comic book for Poindexter.

    Start the disco clock: a wind effect appears at the edges of the screen, the clock is ticking. Melee the item, go through the portal on the right, turn left straight away and melee the same item lying on a pile of wood. If it worked, the effect stops without sending you back to the clock.

  <Step title="Second clock: Bang Bangs subway">
    Start the clock in the Bang Bangs subway station and go through the portal right next to it. Melee the **script** lying on a dumpster next to Bombstoppers. You come back to the subway: quickly cross the tracks and melee the other script on the ground, next to Bang Bangs.

  <Step title="Third clock: disco roof">
    Start the roof clock. Drop through the open skylight near Quickies and melee the **book** next to the Rack-9 wall weapon. Back at the clock, melee the book lying on the roof ledge, on the street side.

  <Step title="Bring your character up on their TV">
    There is one TV per character. Use it several times, until your character's drawing shows up:

    - **Sally**: in the spawn subway car.
    - **Andre**: in the Heebie Geebies.
    - **Poindexter**: on the roof near the RPR Evo wall weapon.
    - **AJ**: next to Blue Bolts.

    A horde of ninjas and zombies arrives: kill everything. The TV then shows colour bars.

  <Step title="Hit the three clocks in a row">
    Quickly, in this order: the **disco roof** clock, the one **inside the disco**, then the **subway** one. Too slow? You're sent back to the first clock, but you can try again right away.

    A **fire sale** appears: the first spin of the Magic Wheel gives the nunchucks.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

According to Gameranx, the first clock done with a character unlocks their **biography**. For the "Book Worm" achievement, you need those of AJ, Andre, Poindexter and Sally. In Director's Cut mode, the nunchucks are already in the Magic Wheel.
