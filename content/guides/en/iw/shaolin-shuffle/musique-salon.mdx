This track does not play during the match: it is added to the **lobby** music, the menu before you start a game. You need **Director's Cut mode**: to unlock it, see [Unlock and use Director's Cut](../../the-beast-from-beyond/director-s-cut/) on The Beast from Beyond.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Start a game in Director's Cut">
    The whole next step happens during **round 1** ("scene 1").
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the white mask near the throne">
    Go to the Rat King's lair, in the sewers, and shoot the **white Talisman mask** on the painting next to his throne. The new lobby music unlocks.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'lobby_music/white_talisman.webp'} alt="The white mask on the painting near the throne" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="ZombieSlaya Mr." source="https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2998577393">
    The lair is in the sewers: you need to have turned on all four switches before the end of round 1. See [Turn on the power](../courant/).
  </Tip>
</Tips>
