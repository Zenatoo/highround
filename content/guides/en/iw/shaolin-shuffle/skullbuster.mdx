Skullbuster is a hidden arcade machine. You need **six skulls** to unlock it, in any order. Winning its mini-game gives you **every perk until the end of the game** (except Up 'N Atoms in solo), and every weapon from the Magic Wheel comes out already Pack-a-Punched. The "Coin-Op" achievement unlocks.

**Before you start:**

- The machine is upstairs in the Disco Inferno: it shows how many skulls you have.
- Each time you complete a skull, a confirmation sound plays.

  <Step title="Skull 2: complete the Mahjong hand">
    On the disco roof, right of the KBS Longbow wall weapon, Mahjong tiles are lined up and some are missing. Find them on the map and arrange the **14 tiles** into a winning hand: **four sets of three** (a run like 3-4-5, or three of a kind like 3-3-3) and **one pair**. Never more than four identical tiles.

    You can only carry one tile at a time: picking up another one swaps them. Some tiles on the map are decoys. When the hand is right, fireworks go off behind the tiles.

    Where to look for tiles: red table on the left of the disco roof; pillar on the disco's 2nd floor, on the KBAR-32 side; bar on the disco's 2nd floor, left of the machine; wooden table in the disco's private lounge; Heebie Geebies bar; red crate in the Heebie Geebies back hallway; car right of the Dojo; red crate near Racin' Stripes; table near the R-VN wall weapon; pallet left of Blue Bolts; bench next to the Magic Wheel in the Bang Bangs subway; box (the farther of the two) in the Tuff 'Nuff room.

  <Step title="Skull 3: draw 1972 on the windows">
    Candles mark four windows. On each one, shoot the right panes to "draw" a digit:

    - **1**: window right of the Inferno Disco sign, on the street side.
    - **9**: across the alley, from the Racin' Stripes roof.
    - **7**: Trail Blazers hallway, left of the stairs.
    - **2**: outside, left of the Dojo.

    A wrong pane or being too slow plays an error sound: try that digit again next round.

  <Step title="Skull 4: jump from circle to circle">
    At the start of a round, go to the RPR Evo wall weapon near Deadeye Dewdrops: a white circle floats in front of the old Torii portal. Jump from circle to circle up to the skull, then jump into it. The circles slowly fade: don't hang around. If you fall, try again next round.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_4/white_circle.webp'} alt="The first white circle" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        Don't sprint: you easily overshoot the next circle. Only the first jump may need it.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Skull 5: jump in front of the train">
    A skull is painted on the front of the train that runs through the Bang Bangs subway. **Jump** in front of the train as it arrives: standing on the tracks is not enough.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_5/skull_on_train.webp'} alt="The skull on the front of the train" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        The train downs you. In solo without Up 'N Atoms, that's game over.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Skull 6: call 555-0152">
    Dial **555-0152** on a payphone in either subway (the number is also written on the machine). A man answers: stay on the line until the confirmation sound. If you take a hit, the call drops and you have to call back.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_6/payphone.webp'} alt="A subway payphone" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Win the Skullbuster mini-game">
    There are three rounds. Skulls move towards the end of the board: make groups of **three skulls of the same colour** (in a line or an L) to destroy them. With your weapon, the **Entangler**, grab a coloured cube and fire it at a "?" tile to give it that colour.

    Each wrong colour is a strike. At **three strikes**, you lose and have to redo skull 6 (the call). After the third round, you're back in the disco with every perk.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="tech" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        Before firing, check the possible groups of the other colours: a "?" tile you use for blue may be the only one that can finish a red group.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>
