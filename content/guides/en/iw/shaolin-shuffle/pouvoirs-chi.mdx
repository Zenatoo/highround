Pam Grier teaches kung fu at the **Black Cat Dojo**. Each player can learn one of four fighting styles there: **Dragon**, **Tiger**, **Crane** or **Snake**. The Chi power is for fighting, but also for opening the **fast travel doors** and the **Torii portals**. The main Easter Egg needs its shurikens.

## Get and level up a style

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Talk to Pam Grier from round 5">
    From round 5, talk to Pam in the Dojo, then take one of the **four gourds** around the Dojo: each gives a style.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={'iw/shaolin-shuffle/pam.webp'} alt="Pam Grier in the Dojo" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'all_gourds_dojo.webp'} alt="The four gourds around the Dojo" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Level up your style">
    Each tier is earned with a number of kills:

| Tier | What you get | To reach the next one |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Melee only | 15 melee kills |
| 2 | The **shuriken**: fully charged, it splits into three | 25 shuriken kills |
| 3 | The style's own power | 50 kills with that power |
| 4 | The style's ultimate attack | — |

    The Chi power can be used once per round. Picking up any **gourd** on the map recharges it; a gourd comes back about five minutes later.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

To switch styles, take that style's gourd back at the Dojo. Your progress is **kept for each style**, but it isn't shared: maxing out Dragon doesn't level up Crane.

<Screenshot src={m + 'gourd.webp'} alt="A gourd on the map" credit={by} source={src} />

## The four styles

| Style | Tier 3 | Tier 4 (ultimate) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dragon** | Pillars of yellow energy burst out of the ground and kill the zombies caught in them | A dragon circles around where you threw it and kills zombies |
| **Tiger** | A thrown ball of energy becomes a vortex that sucks in and kills zombies | Ground pound that sends out a shockwave |
| **Crane** | Freezes zombies | Flying kick |
| **Snake** | Summons a skeleton that fights alongside you | Flurry of very fast punches |

Maxing out **all four styles in the same game** unlocks the "Shaolin Skills" achievement.

## Fast travel doors and Torii portals

Break them with melee or the shuriken. Fast travel doors work **in pairs**: you have to open both ends.
