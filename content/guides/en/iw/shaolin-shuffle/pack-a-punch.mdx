Pack-a-Punch is in the Projector Room. You get there through a portal projected onto a wall of the **Pink Cat**, on Main Street. You need three items, in any order: a **flyer**, a **coin** and a **film reel**.

**Before you start:** the coin, and one of the reels, are in the sewers: you need all **four switches** (see [Turn on the power](../courant/)).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Find the flyer">
    It opens the Pink Cat's front door. It is in **one** of these four spots:

    - On a pile of trash, left of the Dojo.
    - On a chair on the roof near the Dojo, in front of Racin' Stripes.
    - On a stool on the disco roof, past the corner with the KBS Longbow wall weapon.
    - On the ground, in front of the Pink Cat.

    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the coin under Tuff 'Nuff">
    Only one spot: in the sewers, **under the Tuff 'Nuff machine**. Go prone to grab it. It opens the door of the room on the left as you come in from the street.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'coin_jug.webp'} alt="The coin under Tuff 'Nuff" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the film reel">
    It repairs the projector. It is in **one** of these five spots:

    - Disco Inferno, on a couch on the 2nd floor, right of the Rack-9 wall weapon.
    - Pink Cat, on a couch near the rightmost entrance (you need the flyer).
    - Main Street subway, on boxes between the ticket booth and the lockers.
    - Sewers, on a crate at the top of the stairs near Tuff 'Nuff.
    - Rat King's lair, in the sewer holes in the wall, right of his throne.

    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open both Pink Cat doors">
    The flyer opens the front door, the coin opens the door of the room on the left.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pink_cat_door1.webp'} alt="The Pink Cat front door" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pink_cat_door2.webp'} alt="The door of the room on the left" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Repair the projector and take the portal">
    Put the reel on the projector: it projects a portal onto the wall. Use it to reach the Pack-a-Punch room. An upgrade costs **5,000 points**.

    You have **30 seconds** there before being sent back to the map. The portal then recharges for about a minute.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'projector.webp'} alt="The Pink Cat projector" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

To upgrade a weapon a second time, see [Double Pack-a-Punch (alien fuses)](../double-pack-a-punch/).
