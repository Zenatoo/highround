Billionaire David Savage hid a treasure on the map. You make a series of coded messages (in Willard Wyler's alphabet) appear by looking at specific spots. The reward is the **"Trolltastic" animated calling card**. Your progress is kept from one game to the next.

**Before you start:** one step only works between **midnight and 1 a.m., New York time** (US Eastern time). Changing the clock on your console or PC does nothing: the game takes the time from its servers.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Find the radio that talks about David Savage">
    Use the hidden radios on the map (the same ones as for the hidden song, see [Hidden song: Cats on the Boulevard](../musique-cats-on-the-boulevard/)) until you hear the one about "eccentric billionaire David Savage".
  </Step>

  <Step title="Look at the Savage Industries clock">
    On the disco roof, look straight at the **Savage Industries** clock: a coded message appears on it.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'savage_ind_clock.webp'} alt="The Savage Industries clock" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search the 12 bins in both subways">
    Hold the action button on **12 standing bins**, in any order: each one makes a noise.

    **Bang Bangs subway (5)**: between the lockers; left of the lockers, against the gate; on the other side of the gate, next to the bench; in front of the Magic Wheel; at the bottom of the stairs, on the Bang Bangs side.

    **Spawn subway (7)**: right of the Hornet wall weapon, in the dark corner; next to Up 'N Atoms; upstairs, right of the Magic Wheel; left of the Magic Wheel; opposite, right of the bank; left of the bank; opposite, at the bottom of the stairs.
