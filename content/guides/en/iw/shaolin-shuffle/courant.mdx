There are **four switches**. Each one powers its own area. All four are needed to go down into the sewers, where you'll find Tuff 'Nuff, the Pack-a-Punch coin, a film reel and the Rat King's lair.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Spawn subway">
    On one of the station pillars, across from the Up 'N Atoms machine.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'power_spawn.webp'} alt="Spawn subway switch" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Disco Inferno roof">
    Left of the Quickies machine.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'power_roof.webp'} alt="Disco roof switch" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="The park">
    On the wall, left of the Deadeye Dewdrops machine.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'power_deadeye.webp'} alt="Park switch" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Main Street subway">
    Left of Bang Bangs, across the tracks.

  </Step>
