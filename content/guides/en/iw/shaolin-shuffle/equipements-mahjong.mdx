Five tables on the map hold Mahjong tiles and a piece of equipment. Each table is missing **three tiles**: bring them back and the equipment appears. It comes back **two rounds** after you take it. The tables are always in the same spots.

You can only carry **one tile at a time**. Building all five in the same game unlocks the "Some Assembly Required" achievement.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Toy Robot">
    Once placed, it fires explosives at zombies and electrocutes the ones that come close. It explodes at the end and kills the zombies around it.

    **Table**: near the bus, in the street near the Dojo.

    **Where to look for the tiles**: at the top, on the front of the bus; halfway up the stairs between the spawn subway and the Dojo, near some trash; in a crate left of Tuff 'Nuff.
