The "Kirisute Gomen" katana is bought from a wall of the Black Cat Dojo, but only after finishing the main Easter Egg **once**. It goes through Pack-a-Punch like a normal weapon, with no extra quest.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Finish the main Easter Egg once">
    In any game. See [Main Easter Egg (Pest Control)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the katana from the Dojo wall">
    It hangs on the Dojo wall, right of where Pam stands. It costs **10,000 points**, but it is **free** in the game where you just finished the quest.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'katana/katana_on_wall.webp'} alt="The katana on the Dojo wall" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put it through Pack-a-Punch">
    One upgrade is enough for the "Sliced and Diced" achievement. With the alien fuses, you can upgrade it a second time (see [Double Pack-a-Punch (alien fuses)](../double-pack-a-punch/)).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

In Director's Cut mode, every player can buy it, even without finishing the quest.
