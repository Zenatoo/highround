Small radios are hidden around the map. Use **seven** of them, any seven: on the seventh, the song "Cats on the Boulevard" starts and the "Beat of the Drum" achievement unlocks. **17 spots** are known.

These radios are also used for David Savage's treasure hunt (see [David Savage's treasure hunt (Trolltastic card)](../chasse-au-tresor-savage/)).

    - In the red crate near the Heebie Geebies restrooms.
