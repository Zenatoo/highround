Shaolin Shuffle has **10 perk machines**. Deadeye Dewdrops is new on this map and replaces Slappy Taffy. The machines need their area's power (see [Turn on the power](../courant/)).

| Perk | Effect | Where | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Up 'N Atoms** | Revive teammates faster; in solo, revives you on its own | Spawn subway, across from the switch | 500 in solo, 1,500 in co-op |
| **Tuff 'Nuff** | You take more hits before going down | Sewers (all four switches needed); the Pack-a-Punch coin is underneath | 2,500 |
| **Blue Bolts** | Electric blast around you when you reload | Main Street, in front of the Pink Cat, next to a fast travel door | 2,000 |
| **Bang Bangs** | More damage and a higher fire rate | Main Street subway, across the tracks from the switch | 2,000 |
| **Trail Blazers** | Leaves a trail of fire when you slide | Inside the Disco Inferno, in the hallway of the blue room (cloakroom) | 2,000 |
| **Quickies** | Faster reloads and window repairs | Disco roof, next to the switch | 3,000 |
| **Racin' Stripes** | You run faster | Roof near the Dojo, at the top of the stairs from the park | 2,000 |
| **Deadeye Dewdrops** | More damage when you aim at zombies | The park, next to the switch, the Volk wall weapon and the lion statue | 1,500 |
| **Mule Munchies** | Carry one more weapon | Alley with the wooden ladder, next to the Disco Inferno | 2,000 |
| **Bombstoppers** | No more explosive damage | Same alley as Mule Munchies, with a police car behind it | 1,500 |
