The Shaolin Shuffle Talisman needs **Director's Cut mode**. To unlock it and see what it changes, see [Unlock and use Director's Cut](../../the-beast-from-beyond/director-s-cut/) on The Beast from Beyond. This map's lobby music has its own guide: [Lobby music (Director's Cut)](../musique-salon/).

## The Shaolin Shuffle Talisman

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Redo the main Easter Egg in Director's Cut">
    Same steps as usual: see [Main Easter Egg (Pest Control)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the Talisman in the lair">
    When the Rat King dies, the **Talisman** appears in the middle of the lair, where the Soul Key usually drops. Pick it up before leaving the game. You also get the **Director's Cut III** animated calling card.

      <Screenshot src={m + 'main_ee/talisman.webp'} alt="The Talisman in the middle of the lair" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'main_ee/shaolin_dc.webp'} alt="The Director's Cut III calling card" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>
</Checklist>

This Talisman is one of the four (Spaceland, Rave, Shaolin, Radioactive) that lead to the fight against Mephistopheles on The Beast from Beyond.

## What changes on Shaolin in Director's Cut

- Every player can buy the Dojo **katana**, even without finishing the quest.
- The **nunchucks** are already in the Magic Wheel.
