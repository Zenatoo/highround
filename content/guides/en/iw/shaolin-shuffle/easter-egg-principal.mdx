The goal: defeat the Rat King, ruler of the sewers, to get a piece of the soul key. The quest has **three parts** (the Eye, the Brain and the Heart), each ending with a fight against him, then a final fight in his lair. The "Pest Control" achievement unlocks at the end.

**Before you start:**

- Count **1 to 1.5 hours** in solo.
- Turn on all **four switches** and open **Pack-a-Punch**: see [Turn on the power](../courant/) and [Unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
- You need a **Chi power at tier 2**: it's what gives you the shurikens. See [Chi powers, fast travel doors and Torii portals](../pouvoirs-chi/).
- Keep some **explosives** for the second part, and a **bullet weapon with lots of ammo** for the final fight (for example an NV4 upgraded with Pack-a-Punch).

## Part one: the Eye

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get Pam talking about the terrorised streets">
    Talk to Pam Grier in the Dojo until she says the line that starts with "The one who terrorizes these streets…". Rat cages appear around the map.

    Wait until she's done talking before talking to her again: while she's speaking, you can't interact with her.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Follow the rat from cage to cage">
    Throw a shuriken at any cage: the rat escapes and runs to another cage. Follow it and break that cage with a shuriken, and so on.

    You break **six cages** in all: the rat escapes from the first five, and the sixth disappears with no rat. A big yellow circle then appears next to it.

    Two cages near the Dojo to start with: at the top of the ramp from the street in front of the Dojo, and in the side hallway of the Heebie Geebies.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="Zenato">
        Open most doors before freeing the rat: you won't lose it on the way.
      </Tip>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="Zenato">
        Only break the cage the rat just went into: hitting another one restarts the chase.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill 15 zombies in the yellow circle">
    The zombies have to be in the circle, not you. After the 15th, the circle disappears and ninjas arrive (eight). Kill them all: a key appears where the circle was. Pick it up.

  <Step title="Shoot the four symbols in order">

    2. Ground floor restrooms of the Disco Inferno: the **second** from the left.

<Checklist>

  <Step title="Find six orange symbols with the Eye">

    - Above the train, in the spawn subway.
    - Barbershop window, left of the Dojo.
    - Inside the Dojo, above the main door.
    - Behind a speaker, left of the Heebie Geebies stage.
    - Police car door, behind Bombstoppers.
    - At the top of the Heebie Geebies ramp, first zombie spawn on the right.
    - Building in the distance, right of the RPR Evo wall weapon (near Deadeye Dewdrops).
    - Clothesline near the Deadeye Dewdrops Magic Wheel.
    - Floor of the blue room, in the Trail Blazers hallway (jump to see it).
    - Water tower left of the KBS Longbow, on the disco roof.
    - Air duct, seen through the disco roof skylight.
    - Disco Inferno sign above the Main Street Magic Wheel.
    - Main Street subway, behind the stairs on the Bang Bangs side.

  <Step title="Listen to the Morse message on the phone">
    After the sixth symbol, a payphone rings in one of the two subway stations: it's the one **not lit red**. Pick it up and listen **to the end**: if you hang up too early or a zombie hits you, you have to listen again.

    The message is a **three-digit number in Morse code**: 15 beeps, five per digit. Note the short (·) and long (−) beeps. There are only **six possible numbers**:

| 1st digit | 2nd digit | 3rd digit | Number |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| · · − − − | − − − · · | · − − − − | **281** |
| · · · · − | − − − − − | − − · · · | **407** |
| · · · · − | · · − − − | − − − − − | **420** |
| · · · · · | − − − − · | − · · · · | **596** |
| − − · · · | · − − − − | · · · − − | **713** |
| − − − · · | · − − − − | − − − · · | **818** |

    The first five beeps are almost always enough: look them up in the first column. Only **407 and 420** start the same way. Then listen to the **sixth beep**: long for 407, short for 420. Another clue: 420 ends with five long beeps.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Spell the hidden word with Wyler's alphabet">
    A letter appears on the wall, right of the way down to the street, and **six symbols** appear around the roof. These symbols are Willard Wyler's made-up alphabet: each one stands for a letter. You need a chart: mmmrkennedy's page (linked in the sources) has one, with a tool that filters the words.

    The letter on the wall is the **first letter of a hidden word**, taken from one of the lists below. Shoot the symbol that gives the next letter:

    - **Right shot**: the letter is added on the wall.
    - **Wrong shot**: the word is wiped and another one is drawn from the same list. You can start again right away.
    - After **ten failed words** in the round, wait for the next round: another list is then picked.
    - A **gong** marks the end of the word.

  <Step title="Survive the burning street">
    At the start of the next three rounds, a **cigarette burn** shows in the top right of the screen. Then the game skips three rounds at once and locks you in the street in front of the Dojo, behind flames. Kill everything that comes: a **turnstile coin** drops and the fire disappears.

## Final fight: the Rat King's lair

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Go down into the lair">
    When you're ready, talk to Pam in the Dojo, then enter the Rat King's lair, in the sewers. Take your weapon with the big ammo reserve.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        During the Eye challenge, there is no way to get ammo back. If you run dry, the game is stuck and you have to quit.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hurt him until he climbs up to the beams">
    His attacks: he throws his shield, sends a shockwave along the ground towards you and summons ninja rats. Sometimes he hides behind his shield (a yellow armour icon appears when you shoot): the more you shoot it, the longer he keeps it up.

    A shuriken on his **staff** breaks it; it comes back after a minute or two.

    Once he has taken enough damage, he climbs into the beams and the **Eye**, the **Brain** and the **Heart** appear on the ground.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pass the three challenges">
    Take one of the three organs, then use the big symbol in the middle of the room: its challenge starts. The order is up to you. After each challenge, a gong sounds: hurt him again until he goes back up to the beams.

    - **Eye**: about every 10 seconds, the Eye scans the room and shows pink symbols on the walls and ceiling (you can also scan with your own Eye). Shoot them all. If you take too long, he throws his shield and each impact creates more symbols.
    - **Heart**: acid falls all around the arena. Kill a zombie in a puddle to make it disappear, and clear the whole ring before the next wave. Standing in acid hurts you.
    - **Brain**: the Brain is in the middle. Zombies with blue eyes attack it without going after you. **Don't kill them** and protect them from the Rat King: breaking his staff helps a lot. If one dies, another takes its place.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        Each challenge leaves a Max Ammo in the middle, which doesn't disappear. Only take it when you really need it.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish off the Rat King and take the Soul Key">
    After the third challenge, you can kill him. The ending cutscene plays and the **Soul Key** appears in the middle of the room: pick it up. The "Pest Control" achievement pops.

    In Director's Cut mode, a **Talisman** appears in its place. See [Director's Cut: the Talisman](../director-s-cut-secrets/).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'main_ee/soul_key.webp'} alt="The Soul Key in the middle of the lair" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Finishing the quest once also unlocks the Dojo **katana** and **Pam Grier's code**. See [The Kirisute Gomen katana](../katana/) and [Play as Pam Grier](../personnages-secrets/).
