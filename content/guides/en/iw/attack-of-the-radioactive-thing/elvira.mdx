Elvira, Mistress of the Dark, doesn't just open Pack-a-Punch: she can also fight by your side, and get stronger with pages from her book.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Make Elvira an ally">
    Give her book back, fill the vial with the cleaver and hand it to her: she gets up and fights with you for a while. The details are in [Unlock Pack-a-Punch with Elvira (and double Pack-a-Punch)](../pack-a-punch/). Each new full vial brings her back.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'elvira.webp'} alt="Elvira on her couch, in the TV Studio" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the book pages in the garage">
    Once the gas station garage is open (see [Main Easter Egg (Soul-Less)](../easter-egg-principal/)), take the pages from the shelf, next to the tyres, right of the chemistry table.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'elvira_upgrade/pages.webp'} alt="The book pages in the garage" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give the pages to Elvira">
    Bring them to her at the TV Station. From now on, when you summon her, she can make **six jets of lava** burst out that hurt zombies.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
