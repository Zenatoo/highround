Find and use the **five pink vinyl records** hidden around the map: the song "Brachyura Boogie" then plays on every jukebox on the map. No need to turn on a jukebox. On this map, no achievement is tied to the song.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Power Station record">
    On the right side of the switch building, hidden behind a metal sheet.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'power.webp'} alt="Record behind the metal sheet, at the Power Station" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Market record">
    Under the cash register, right of the Fate & Fortune machine.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'market.webp'} alt="Record under the Market cash register" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Snack Shack record">
    Under a blue tarp, under the scaffolding, left of the ERAD wall weapon.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'snack_shack.webp'} alt="Record under the blue tarp at the Snack Shack" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Motel record">
    In a bin, left of Bombstoppers.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'motel.webp'} alt="Record in the bin left of Bombstoppers" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="RV Park record">
    Under the table of the open RV, near the big propane tank.
