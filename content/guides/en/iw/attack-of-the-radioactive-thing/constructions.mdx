Four devices can be built on the map. Each one needs **a blueprint and three parts**, always in the same spots. You can only carry **one part and one blueprint at a time**. Once you've taken a device from the workbench, you have to wait two rounds to take another one.

| Device | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Seismic Wave Generator** | Pounds the ground every few seconds and kills the zombies around it (it shakes players a bit) |
| **Violet Ray Device** | A cone of radiation that destroys zombies passing in front of it (not Crogs or players) |
| **Hypnosis Device** | Draws the zombies around it, then they explode |
| **Mind Control Device** | Turns up to 4 nearby zombies into allies |
