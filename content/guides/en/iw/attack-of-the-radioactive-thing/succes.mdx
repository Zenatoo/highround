Attack of the Radioactive Thing has **8 trophies** (achievements on Xbox and PC): 3 bronze and 5 silver. None is tied to the hidden song or the calling cards. Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="MAD Proto (silver)">
    Assemble the M.A.D.: get it from the Magic Wheel, then attach its three parts. See [The M.A.D.: get it and upgrade it](../mad/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bait and Switch (bronze)">
    Repair and use all five traps on the map. See [Repair and use the 5 traps](../pieges/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Quarter Muncher (bronze)">
    Finish Skull Hop: the six skulls, then the mini-game. See [Skull Hop (every perk for good)](../skull-hop/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Soul-Less (silver)">
    Get the Soul Key piece by finishing the main Easter Egg. The achievement pops during the ending cutscene. See [Main Easter Egg (Soul-Less)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Belly of the Beast (silver)">
    In the monster's belly, at the end of the main Easter Egg, enter the safe code without a mistake.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="PowerPyx" source="https://www.powerpyx.com/call-of-duty-infinite-warfare-absolution-dlc-trophy-guide-roadmap/">
        You have to get it right on your first trip into the belly: write down the safe code before going any further.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Mistress of the Dark (silver)">
    Unlock Elvira: finish the main Easter Egg once, then enter her code in the lobby. The achievement pops at the start of the next game. See [Play as Elvira](../personnages-secrets/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Unpleasant Dreams (silver)">
    Get the Soul Key piece playing as Elvira: redo the main Easter Egg with her.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Dear Diary (bronze)">
    Listen to all of Willard Wyler's recordings, after finishing the main Easter Egg, in the same game, and not as Elvira. See [Willard Wyler's diary (Dear Diary)](../journal-de-wyler/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>
