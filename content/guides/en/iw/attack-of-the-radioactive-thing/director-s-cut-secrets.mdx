This map's Talisman needs **Director's Cut mode**. To unlock it and see what it changes, see [Unlock and use Director's Cut](../../the-beast-from-beyond/director-s-cut/) on The Beast from Beyond. This map's lobby music has its own guide: [Lobby music (Director's Cut)](../musique-salon/).

## Talisman and "Director's Cut IV" calling card

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Finish the main Easter Egg in Director's Cut">
    The steps are the same. See [Main Easter Egg (Soul-Less)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the Talisman on the beach">
    After the cutscene, the Talisman appears on the beach. Pick it up **before leaving the game**, or it's lost. It comes in addition to the Soul Key piece, and the "Director's Cut IV" animated calling card unlocks.

    This Talisman is one of the four you need to face Mephistopheles on The Beast from Beyond.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
