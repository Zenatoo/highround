To finish the bomb in the main Easter Egg, you have to make **a final chemical** and place it on it. There are only **four** possible ones, and the game picks one each game.

<ChemistryShortcut />

Each reaction is started with a **number to enter** on a keypad. That number depends on the game: you need to find the number **O**, the map's **colour**, then read digits on **boards**. Everything is explained below, in order: first the recipes and the ingredients, then the digits to find.

**Before you start:** open the gas station garage and set up the chemistry equipment on its table (see [Main Easter Egg (Soul-Less)](../easter-egg-principal/)). Take the crowbar and the cleaver. Keep paper at hand.

## The recipes

Pick a final chemical: the list shows only its recipe, with the reactions in order, what to place on the table, where to find each ingredient and which board to read its diamond on.

<ChemistryRecipes />

- The garage holds **four finished chemicals**. A fifth one pushes out the oldest.
- You can only carry **one item at a time**. Ingredients stay where they are: you can come back for them as many times as you want.

## Knowing which one to make

Two ways to do it.

### Without the radio: try them one by one

The simplest. Make the final chemicals in the order of the list, from fastest to longest: **1,3,5 tera-nitro-phenol**, then **3-methyl-2,4-di-nitrobenzene**, then **Octa-hydro-2,5-nitro-3,4,7-para-zokine**, then **3,4-di-nitroxy-methyl-propane**. Bring each one to the bomb:

- if it's the right one, your character reacts when picking it up and the bomb accepts it;
- if it isn't, the bomb refuses it, with no penalty at all: move on to the next one.

### With the radio: the right one first time

  <Step title="Repair a radio and listen to it">

  <Step title="Only listen to Dr. Bright">
    A man explains how to make the chemicals: ignore him. Only **Dr. Bright** matters. Only one of his recordings gives the answer; the others each rule out one chemical.

    | What Dr. Bright says | What it means |
    | --- | --- |
    | He announces he has found the perfect solution to the mutant crab problem, then gives a name | **That's the one** to make |
    | He doesn't think a given chemical would be useful in the situation | Not that one |
    | He finds a given chemical would be a rather crude solution | Not that one either, even if it sounds good |
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The digits to find

Once the recipe is chosen, each reaction asks for a number to enter. It depends on three things to note down in the game, in this order.

### 1. The number O

The TV Station console changes the colour of the whole map: **full colour** (normal), **red**, **green** or **blue**.

Four places show "O = a number":

- the gas station front door;
- the Market back room, above the couch;
- the beach, under the big concrete bridge;
- the fridge in the closed RV (also visible by jumping in front of its front window).

In full colour, all four show "=". Switch the map to red, then green, then blue: each time, one of them turns to "≠" and is therefore wrong. The one that keeps "=" in all three colours gives **O**. O is always 2, 4, 8, 9, 11 or 15.

### 2. The map's colour

There is only **one colour for the whole game**, the same for every ingredient.

1. In the motel office, read the number **M** on the wall, right of the window.
2. Work out **O × M**.
3. The TV next to Elvira, at the TV Station, shows ranges of numbers with a colour each. Take the colour of the range your result falls in.

Example: O × M = 106, and the TV shows "less than 107: green, 107 to 109: blue, more than 109: red". The colour is **green**. The ranges change every game. The calculator below also does this calculation.

### 3. The diamonds on the boards

**Set the console to the map's colour first**, before reading anything: every diamond changes with the colour, and only the right one shows the right digits. In that colour, they don't move for the rest of the game: you only need to write them down once.

Each ingredient, and each intermediate product, has its diamond on one of the six boards. A diamond holds four digits, always laid out the same way:

- **red at the top**: it counts;
- **blue on the left**: it counts;
- green on the right and white at the bottom: they're there to mislead you, ignore them.

| Board | Where | Diamonds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Spawn** | Tent hallway | Racing Fuel, Vodka, Baking Soda, Detergent, Insect Repellent |
| **Power Station** | Dirt path behind the Market, going up from the Power Station | Quarters, Glass Cleaner, Pennies, Nail Polish Remover, Drain Opener |
| **RV Park** | Next to the RV near Mule Munchies | Ice, Vinegar, Plant Food, Paint |
| **Beach** | Below the RV Park path, near the water | Motor Oil, Wheel Cleaner, Fat |
| **Gas station** | In the garage, near the bomb | Hexamine, Phenolsulfonic Acid, Phenol, Aldehyde Sludge, Formaldehyde, Dinitro |
| **TV Studio** | Right of Bang Bangs | Acetaldehyde, Glycerol, Methylbenzene, Mixed Acid, Nitrated Glycerol |

Glycerol, Mixed Acid and Nitrated Glycerol don't lead to any final chemical: you don't need them.

## The number to enter on the keypad

For each reaction:

1. For each item you place on the table, add its **red** digit and its **blue** digit.
2. Add up the results of all the items placed.
3. Subtract **O**, only once.

Example, with made-up digits: O = 4. Formaldehyde needs Racing Fuel (red 3, blue 6) and Quarters (red 8, blue 2). Racing Fuel gives 9, Quarters gives 10, so 19 in all. 19 − 4 = **15**: that's the number to enter.

<ChemistryCalculator />

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="Zenato">
    O is subtracted only once per reaction, not once per ingredient. And you enter a single number per reaction.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Run a reaction

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Place the items on the squares">
    On the garage table, place each item of the reaction on a square, in front of the tubes.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'squares_chem.webp'} alt="The squares in front of the tubes" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the number on the keypad">
    The number has one or two digits. To enter a digit, look at the key and use it.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'keypard_number.webp'} alt="The table's keypad" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Press the big button">
    - **Success**: gas appears at the edges of the screen, and the product arrives on the right side of the garage.
    - **Failure**: after a few seconds, you lose almost all your health and the table is cleared. The number or an item was wrong: check them before trying again.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'big_button.webp'} alt="The big button" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bring the final chemical to the bomb">
    Repeat for each reaction of your final chemical, then bring it to the bomb and use it. If it refuses it, move on to the next final chemical.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
