Two free tools are used almost everywhere on this map. Get them early: without them, no Pack-a-Punch and no Easter Egg.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Take the cleaver at the Market">
    It's stuck in a dead shark, behind the Market counter, near Deadeye Dewdrops. It replaces your knife and one-shots until around round 10.

    It's used to:

    - fill Elvira's vial (Pack-a-Punch);
    - get the left leg, from a radioactive soldier (main Easter Egg);
    - take the fat from the Market freezer (chemistry and the "Make 'Em Squeal" calling card).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'cleaver.webp'} alt="The cleaver stuck in the shark" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the crowbar at the motel">
    It's on the floor, in the motel's maintenance room. You get down there by the steps in the corner of the pool.

    It's used to:

    - open the M.A.D.'s three fuse boxes;
    - open the fuse cabinet for double Pack-a-Punch;
    - knock down the torso (the hanging pig), break the restroom mirror and the rear-view mirror (main Easter Egg);
    - set the safe's four gauges (main Easter Egg);
    - take the Quarters, the ice and the Pennies (chemistry).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'crowbar.webp'} alt="The crowbar in the motel maintenance room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>
