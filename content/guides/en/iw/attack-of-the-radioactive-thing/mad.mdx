The **M.A.D.** (Modular Atomic Disintegrator) is the map's special weapon. You only get it from the **Magic Wheel**, then upgrade it with three parts hidden in fuse boxes.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get the M.A.D. from the Magic Wheel">
    With the power on, spin the Magic Wheel until you land on it. It's down to luck.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the crowbar at the motel">
    It leans against a wall of the motel's maintenance room. You get down there by the steps in the corner of the pool.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'crowbar.webp'} alt="The crowbar in the motel maintenance room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the RV Park fuse box (grip)">
    It's on the outside wall of the men's restroom, in the RV Park. Hit it with the crowbar while carrying the M.A.D.: the part attaches to the weapon on its own.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'mad_upgrade/grip.webp'} alt="The fuse box on the RV Park restroom" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the TV Station fuse box (crank)">
    It's on the outside wall of the TV Station, near the Pack-a-Punch portal, across from the Magic Wheel.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'mad_upgrade/crank.webp'} alt="The TV Station fuse box" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the gas station fuse box (plunger)">
    It's on the brick wall behind the gas station, next to a portrait of Willard Wyler. With all three parts, the "MAD Proto" achievement unlocks.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'mad_upgrade/plunger.webp'} alt="The fuse box behind the gas station" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="tech" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        If you lose the M.A.D. and get it back from the Magic Wheel, you can take the parts again from the same boxes.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## What the parts change

According to mmmrkennedy:

- **Grip**: less recoil and more range.
- **Crank**: hold aim to charge a stronger shot, which uses more ammo. A molecule shows on the weapon's screen when it's charged. Switching weapons loses the charge.
- **Plunger**: the weapon recharges on the **green radioactive eggs** placed around the map. A used egg comes back 5 rounds later.
