Two calling cards unlock in secret on this map. Neither gives an achievement.

## Message Rerouted (animated card)

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Shoot the turnstile stuck in the rock">
    Take a sniper rifle. On the hill, along the path from the gas station to the beach, a turnstile sticks out of a rock, left of the "Mason's" sign. Shoot it: it disappears into a vortex and the card unlocks.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'message_rerouted/turnstile.webp'} alt="The turnstile in the rock" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'message_rerouted/turnstile_zoomed_out.webp'} alt="The rock seen from farther away" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Make 'Em Squeal

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Take the fat from the Market freezer">
    With the cleaver, hit the leftmost hanging meat in the Market freezer: you get the fat. Don't pick up any other ingredient afterwards, or it replaces the fat.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'ingredients/fat.webp'} alt="The leftmost hanging meat" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Crawl up to the toilet">
    Go to the RV Park restrooms. Back against the wall, facing the middle stall (the closed one), go prone **before** getting close, then crawl up to the toilet.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'make_em_squeal/stall.webp'} alt="The closed middle stall" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Let the head take the fat">
    A zombie head comes out of the toilet. Once it's fully out, the fat disappears, and the card unlocks about 5 seconds later.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'make_em_squeal/head.webp'} alt="The head coming out of the toilet" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'make_em_squeal/make_em_squeal_calling_card.webp'} alt="The Make 'Em Squeal calling card" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        One try per game: the head comes out the first time someone gets close. If you arrive standing or without the fat, it's a fail until the next game.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>
</Checklist>
