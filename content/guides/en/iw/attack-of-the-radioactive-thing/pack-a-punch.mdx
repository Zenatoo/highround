**Elvira** is the one who opens the portal to the Projector Room, where Pack-a-Punch is. First you give her book back, then you fill a vial with the cleaver.

**Before you start:** turn on the power (see [Turn on the power (and the teleporters)](../courant/)).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Take Elvira's book">
    It's in a wall safe, at the back of the Ice Cream Parlour, left of the Skull Hop machine.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/elviras_book.webp'} alt="Elvira's book in the Ice Cream Parlour safe" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bring the book to Elvira">
    She sits on a couch, in the TV Studio. She gives you an **empty vial**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'elvira.webp'} alt="Elvira on her couch, in the TV Studio" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the cleaver at the Market">
    It's stuck in a dead shark, behind the Market counter, near Deadeye Dewdrops. It's free and replaces your knife.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'cleaver.webp'} alt="The cleaver stuck in the shark" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill the vial with the cleaver">
    Kill **about 20 zombies** with the cleaver. The vial's level shows on the scoreboard. In co-op, the progress is shared by the whole team.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="COD Zombified" source="https://codzombified.blogspot.com/2017/09/getting-cleaver-in-attack-of.html">
        The cleaver only one-shots until around round 10. Fill the vial early in the game.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give the full vial back to Elvira">
    She gets up, follows you and fights by your side for a while.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Let Elvira open the portal">
    Stand near the closed portal, in front of the TV Studio. Elvira walks up to it and opens it.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/closed_portal.webp'} alt="The closed portal in front of the TV Studio" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/open_portal.webp'} alt="The open portal" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go through the portal">
    You have **30 seconds** in the Projector Room to use Pack-a-Punch, then you're sent back to the map. A first upgrade costs **5,000 points**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the next portals">
    The portal never comes back in front of the TV Studio. About 45 seconds after you return, a new one opens either **behind the Ice Cream Parlour** or **in the middle of the RV Park**. Look for the lightning storm in the sky: it's above the portal.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/electric_storm.webp'} alt="The lightning storm that marks the portal" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/pap_ice_cream.webp'} alt="Portal behind the Ice Cream Parlour" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/pap_rv.webp'} alt="Portal in the middle of the RV Park" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Double Pack-a-Punch

It lets you upgrade an already upgraded weapon **a second time**, for the current game. You need fuses hidden in an RV, charged with the electric trap.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Take the crowbar at the motel">
    It leans against a wall of the motel's maintenance room. You get down there by the steps in the corner of the pool.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'crowbar.webp'} alt="The crowbar in the motel maintenance room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Press the button in the Projector Room">
    During a Pack-a-Punch visit, press the button on the wall. When you come out, you don't land in the same place: you're locked inside an RV in the RV Park.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/double_pap/button.webp'} alt="The button on the Projector Room wall" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the RV cabinet">
    Hit the cabinet above the window with the crowbar and take the **alien fuses**, still uncharged. Be quick: you only have a few seconds before you're pulled out of the RV. If you miss, try again at the next portal.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/double_pap/closed_cabinet.webp'} alt="The closed cabinet above the window" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/double_pap/fuses_rv.webp'} alt="The fuses in the cabinet" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill the vial again">
    Get about 20 more cleaver kills. This time, **don't give the vial to Elvira**: it's for the fuses.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place the fuses at the Power Station">
    With the fuses and the full vial, go to the Power Station electric trap. The screen shakes at the right spot: place the fuses on the ground. The vial is poured over them.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/double_pap/fuses_on_ground.webp'} alt="The fuses on the ground, at the Power Station" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Repair and start the electric trap">
    The missing part is in the bin next to the bus, near the Power Station. Repair the trap, then start it: it charges the fuses.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'traps/electric_part.webp'} alt="The part in the bin near the bus" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'traps/electric_repair.webp'} alt="Where to repair the electric trap" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the fuses in the projector">
    Take the charged fuses back and put them in the Pack-a-Punch projector. Until the end of the game, an already upgraded weapon can be upgraded a second time for **10,000 points**.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
