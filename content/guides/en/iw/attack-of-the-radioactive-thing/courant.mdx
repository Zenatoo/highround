Here, there is **only one switch** for the whole map, but its handle is missing at the start: you have to fetch it on the beach. The power runs the perks, the traps and the teleporters.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Take the handle on the beach">
    On the beach, near the OSA wall weapon, a zombie hand sticks out of the sand holding the switch handle. Pick it up.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'power/power_switch.webp'} alt="The zombie hand holding the handle, on the beach" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        Remember where this arm is: it's the right arm of the body you rebuild in the main Easter Egg.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put it in the Power Station box">
    The box is in the Power Station, near the drive-in. Put the handle in and pull it: the power comes on across the whole map.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'power/power_box.webp'} alt="The switch box in the Power Station" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the four teleporters">
    Once the power is on, activate the four pads. Each one then always sends you to the same place:

    - **Power Station** (on the roof of the building next door) → TV Studio
    - **TV Studio** (in front of the building, near Bang Bangs) → RV Park
    - **RV Park** (near the path down to the beach) → Main Street
    - **Main Street** (right of the Market) → Power Station

    A used pad recharges for about a minute.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'teleporters/tele_power.webp'} alt="Power Station teleporter" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'teleporters/tele_tv_station.webp'} alt="TV Studio teleporter" credit={by} source={src} />

      <Screenshot src={m + 'teleporters/tele_street.webp'} alt="Main Street teleporter" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>
</Checklist>
