The map has **five traps** at **750 points** per use. They need the power (see [Turn on the power (and the teleporters)](../courant/)). Four of them are broken at the start: find the missing part and place it in the right spot.

Repairing and using all five traps unlocks the "Bait and Switch" achievement.

  <Step title="Repair the Electric Trap (Power Station)">
    The part is in the bin next to the bus, near the Power Station. Repair the trap on the Power Station fence.

      </Tip>

  <Step title="Repair the Deep Freezer (Market)">
    The part sits on crates, on the left side of the gas station. Repair the trap on the Market freezer wall, between the shop and the back room.

      <Screenshot src={m + 'fuel_part.webp'} alt="The part on the picnic table" credit={by} source={src} />
