Elvira is playable on Attack of the Radioactive Thing with a code you enter in the pre-game lobby, using the arrows of the D-pad or the keyboard.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
    If you open the Barracks or your loadout after the code, the icon disappears: enter the code again.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Finish the main Easter Egg once">
    Once is enough. See [Main Easter Egg (Soul-Less)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Elvira's code">
    Select Attack of the Radioactive Thing, then enter: **down, right, down, left, up, right**. Elvira's icon appears: you play as her when the game starts. The "Mistress of the Dark" achievement pops at the start of that game.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'elvira_code/elvira_icon.webp'} alt="Elvira's icon in the lobby" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Finish the main Easter Egg as Elvira to get the "Unpleasant Dreams" achievement. However, Willard Wyler's diary doesn't work with her (see [Willard Wyler's diary (Dear Diary)](../journal-de-wyler/)).
