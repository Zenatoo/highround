This track does not play during the match: it is added to the **lobby** music, the menu before you start a game. You need **Director's Cut mode**: to unlock it, see [Unlock and use Director's Cut](../../the-beast-from-beyond/director-s-cut/) on The Beast from Beyond.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Shoot the mask in the TV Studio camera">
    In Director's Cut, **during round 1**, go to the TV Studio. The camera right of the MacTav-45 wall weapon points at Elvira: in its viewfinder, a small **white mask** (the Talisman) appears. Shoot it: the new lobby music unlocks.
