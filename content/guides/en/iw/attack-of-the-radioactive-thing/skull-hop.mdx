Skull Hop is the game on the arcade machine hidden in the back room of the Ice Cream Parlour, next to Elvira's safe. You need **six skulls** to unlock it. Winning the mini-game gives you:

- **every perk on the map, plus Trail Blazers**, until the end of the game, even when you go down (except Up 'N Atoms in solo);
- unlimited Fate & Fortune card refills;
- the "Quarter Muncher" achievement.

**Before you start:**

- The machine shows how many skulls you already have.
- Skull 3 needs the pool trap repaired (see [Repair and use the 5 traps](../pieges/)).
- Skull 6 needs the **M.A.D. with its three parts** (see [The M.A.D.: get it and upgrade it](../mad/)).
- Count more than an hour.

  <Step title="Skull 2: fill the canister in the radioactive puddle">
    Take the small yellow canister in front of the gas station and place it in the radioactive puddle near the Ice Cream Parlour. Stay next to it while it fills, **about 4 to 5 minutes**: if you move away, it stops. When it makes a louder sound, take it back quickly (within 30 seconds), or you have to start over.

  <Step title="Skull 3: kill exactly 16 zombies with the pool trap">
    Place the canister in the motel pool, then start the Electric Pool Trap. You have to kill **exactly 16 zombies** with a single use of the trap. Example: if 33 zombies are left in the round, kill 17 elsewhere first, then lure the last 16 into the pool.

  <Step title="Skull 4: spell the word on the drive-in screen">
    Note the word written on the outside wall of the Ice Cream Parlour, above the bench near the UDM wall weapon. It changes every game.

    Four symbols are near the swings. They add letters at the bottom of the drive-in screen; shoot the symbol in the top right of the screen to confirm a letter. There's only room for 4 letters: a fifth one wipes the line. How the symbols turn into letters isn't obvious: watch the DDB and Ray Pooperton III videos in the Videos tab.

  <Step title="Skull 5: free the ghosts from Willard Wyler's portraits">
    Melee the "Don't Blink" sheet on a picnic table, near the propane tank and the open RV in the RV Park. Then take the canister back from the pool.

    Go to the four portraits of Willard Wyler: behind the gas station, in the Market back room (the one with the safe), in the Ice Cream Parlour and in the motel office. At each one:

    1. use the portrait: a bubble that slows everything down appears;
    2. place the canister in front and hit it: ghosts come out;
    3. kill them **inside the bubble**, at least 7, and keep shooting until the bubble ends. A shot outside makes every ghost disappear.

    Each portrait only works once.

  <Step title="Skull 6: shoot the machine with the charged M.A.D.">
    With all three parts on the M.A.D., hold aim to charge a shot, then fire at the Skull Hop machine. The mini-game opens.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="tech" author="PowerPyx" source="https://www.powerpyx.com/call-of-duty-infinite-warfare-absolution-dlc-trophy-guide-roadmap/">
        Each attempt uses 5 M.A.D. shots: keep some ammo.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Win the mini-game">
    In co-op, all players use the machine together. The game has **3 rounds**, and strikes reset each round.

    Coloured lines next to the skulls show which platform to stand on. Get on the platform of that colour, grab a cube and fire it at the skulls. When the colours flash, switch platforms. A wrong colour or a fall turns a skull red: hit it with a red cube before it reaches the vortex. Three strikes and the game is lost: then redo skull 6.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'coloured_lines.webp'} alt="The coloured lines next to the skulls" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="glitch" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        Losing the first game can turn the machine off for good. To avoid it: after skull 5, use the "Skull Hacker" Fate & Fortune card to start the game, lose on purpose (fall three times), then do skull 6 normally. After that, you can try as many times as you want.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>
