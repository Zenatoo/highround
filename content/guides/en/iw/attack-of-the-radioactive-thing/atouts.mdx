The power must be on for the machines to work (see [Turn on the power (and the teleporters)](../courant/)). Prices are the same as on the other Infinite Warfare maps. **Change Chews** makes its debut on this map.

| Perk | Effect | Where | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Up 'N Atoms** | In solo, revives you on its own; in co-op, revive teammates faster | Spawn room, right hallway | 500 in solo, 1,500 in co-op |
| **Tuff 'Nuff** | You take more hits before going down | In front of the Market, near the "M. Mason" sign | 2,500 |
| **Bang Bangs** | More damage and a higher fire rate | TV Studio, outside, near the teleporter | 2,000 |
| **Quickies** | Faster reloads and window repairs | Snack Shack | 3,000 |
| **Racin' Stripes** | You run faster and longer | Next to a shack, on the way to the TV Station | 2,000 |
| **Bombstoppers** | No more explosive or fall damage | Around the motel pool | 1,500 |
| **Deadeye Dewdrops** | Your aim locks onto the head | Inside the Market, near the cleaver | 1,500 |
| **Change Chews** | After a number of headshots, a random elemental effect | Behind the gas station, near a dumpster, at the beach entrance | 1,500 |
| **Mule Munchies** | Carry a third weapon | RV Park | 2,000 |
| **Blue Bolts** | Electric blast around you when you reload | Power Station | 2,000 |
| **Slappy Taffy** | Stronger melee | Playground, near the swings in front of the drive-in screen, on the Ice Cream Parlour side | 2,000 |
