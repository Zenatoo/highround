The goal: defeat **Crog-Zilla**, the giant crab on the beach, to get a piece of the Soul Key. The "Soul-Less" achievement unlocks at the end.

**Before you start:**

- It's the longest quest in Infinite Warfare, because of the chemistry: count **50 minutes to 1 hour 20**.
- Keep **paper** at hand: several numbers change every game, and some can't be read again.
- You need the power, Pack-a-Punch, the **crowbar** and the **cleaver**. See [Unlock Pack-a-Punch with Elvira (and double Pack-a-Punch)](../pack-a-punch/) and [The cleaver and the crowbar](../hachoir-et-pied-de-biche/).
- The body parts and the glass can be picked up in any order.

## The body and the key

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get the head in the RV">
    During a Pack-a-Punch visit, press the button on the Projector Room wall. You come out locked inside an RV in the RV Park: the head is on the ledge, in front of the big window.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'head.webp'} alt="The head on the RV ledge" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Knock the torso down in the freezer">
    Repair the Market's Deep Freezer (see [Repair and use the 5 traps](../pieges/)) and start it. During the trap or right after, hit the pig hanging right of the torso with the crowbar. Then pick up the torso.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={e + 'torso.webp'} alt="The torso in the freezer" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={e + 'torso_meat.webp'} alt="The pig to hit" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get the left arm">
    In the RV Park fire pit, right of the Volk wall weapon.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'left_arm.webp'} alt="The left arm in the fire pit" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get the right arm out of the beach">
    It's the arm stuck in the sand that held the power handle. Build a **Seismic Wave Generator** (see [The 4 devices to build](../constructions/)) and place it **near** the arm, not on it. With each shake, the arm comes out a bit more: take it when it stops moving.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'right_arm.webp'} alt="The right arm on the beach" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get the left leg">
    From round 5, **radioactive soldiers** appear. Kill one with the cleaver: the first one killed that way always drops the leg.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'radioactive_zombie.webp'} alt="A radioactive soldier" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        The leg doesn't seem to disappear. If you didn't see it drop, look on the ground where you killed the soldier.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Knock the right leg out of the tree">
    It hangs in the last tree on the left of the path from the Snack Shack to the TV Station, near the fence. Pull the pin on a frag or cluster grenade, cook it for a moment, then throw it into the tree. Bio-Spikes and C4 don't work well.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'right_leg_tree.webp'} alt="The right leg in the tree" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take Elvira's mirror">
    Give Elvira a full vial, wait until she's done fighting and leaves again. Then take the mirror from the TV Station couch.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'elviras_mirror.webp'} alt="Elvira's mirror on the couch" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="tech" author="Heckin Poss" source="https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2551179005">
        You can take the mirror when the prompt says Elvira isn't there. If it won't come, step away a bit and come back.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Break the restroom mirror">
    Hit the mirror in the RV Park restrooms with the crowbar. A shard falls into the sink: pick it up.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'unbroken_mirror.webp'} alt="The RV Park restroom mirror" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hit the white car's side mirror">
    On Main Street, hit the right side mirror of the white car with a roof using the crowbar.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'car_mirror.webp'} alt="The white car's side mirror" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up the body and the mirrors at spawn">
    In the spawn room:

    - **The six body parts**: on the upright bed, right of the tunnel out of the Up 'N Atoms room.
    - **The broken mirror shard**: on the table with the pill bottles, left of the body.
    - **Elvira's mirror**: on the crates right of where the zombies come out.
    - **The side mirror**: on the big crate across from Up 'N Atoms.

    <Shots>

    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the punch card in the machine">
    Take the card from the desk at the back of the Ice Cream Parlour, next to the safe with Elvira's book. Put it in the machine in the spawn room, right of the side mirror.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={e + 'punch_card.webp'} alt="The punch card on the desk" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={e + 'machine_in_spawn.webp'} alt="The machine in the spawn room" credit={by} source={src} />

  <Step title="Read the order of the digits on the reels">
    The code uses the digits **3, 4, 5, 6 and 8**, once each, in an order that changes every game. To find it, go to the TV Station, in front of the colour console: four film reels are on the shelf and one on the floor. Read them from left to right, counting the one on the floor as the **third**.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={e + 'reels.webp'} alt="The reels in front of the console" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={e + 'number_on_reel.webp'} alt="A digit on a reel" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the code in the machine">
    On the machine, a light moves from one digit to the next; the leftmost one is 0. Press "use" when it lights up the digit you want. Three mistakes or a zombie hit kick you out of the machine, but what you already entered stays. A laser then forms a zombie out of the parts.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="Zenato">
        A zombie hit kicks you out of the machine: clear the round before you start.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the same code backwards">
    Use the machine again and enter the same code, **in reverse order**. The zombie turns into a **key**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the gas station garage">
    The key opens the gas station garage door, on Main Street.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'garage_door.webp'} alt="The garage door" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The fight against Crog-Zilla

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Charge the bomb and start the fight">
    Each player uses the bomb to charge it, then one player uses it again to start the fight. You're taken to the beach.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Push the cart to the water">
    Stay near the bomb cart to move it towards the water. At each stop, the monster throws eggs and fireballs, and zombies arrive: shoot the projectiles down so the cart moves again. Max Ammo waits for you near the OSA and comes back after a few minutes.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={e + 'nuke_cart_spawn.webp'} alt="The bomb cart" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={e + 'max_ammo_boss_fight.webp'} alt="The Max Ammo near the OSA" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Aim at his chest with the rays">
    The bomb goes into his mouth. He now has a **deadly ground slam** when he raises one or two claws: run, for example onto the bridge towards the RV Park. His laser, when his antenna glows, does little damage.

    A spot on his chest glows: aim at it with one of the four **death rays** (next to the OSA, on the path to the RV Park, on the path up to Main Street, on the big concrete bridge). After the first hit, marked with a skull, the chest keeps glowing and the **Spartan SA3**, a wall weapon near the cart's starting point, can hit it too. After 3 or 4 hits, he stops attacking: everyone uses the cart.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={e + 'glowing_spot.webp'} alt="The glowing spot on his chest" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={e + 'ray_osa.webp'} alt="Ray next to the OSA" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={e + 'ray_rv.webp'} alt="Ray on the RV Park path" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={e + 'ray_gas.webp'} alt="Ray on the Main Street path" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={e + 'ray_bridge.webp'} alt="Ray on the concrete bridge" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={e + 'spartan_wallbuy.webp'} alt="The Spartan SA3 wall weapon" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={e + 'nuke_cart_empty.webp'} alt="The cart to use" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get through the lasers">
    He spits radiation over the front of the beach, sends two waves of eggs and fireballs, then spits again, until he pushes you back against the wall under the bridge. The radiation then disappears and **lasers** appear. Each player has to jump and slide through them to the cart before the timer runs out.

    If someone fails, the lasers disappear: use the cart again and start over. The next spit is followed by only one wave of eggs.

    <Screenshot src={e + 'raditation_spit.webp'} alt="The radiation on the beach" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="tech" author="mmmrkennedy" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WyuYjiJlzaI">
        His video shows every possible path through the lasers.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the safe code in his belly">
    You're in his belly. **Each player** enters the code written down at the safe, on a machine that works like the one in the spawn room. You get 3 mistakes per try and 3 tries.

    If the code fails, you're sent back outside: you have to do the laser run again. Survive the waves until the lasers come back, get through them, then try the code again.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="PowerPyx" source="https://www.powerpyx.com/call-of-duty-infinite-warfare-absolution-dlc-trophy-guide-roadmap/">
        Getting the code right on your first trip into his belly unlocks the "Belly of the Beast" achievement.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Watch the ending cutscene">
    With the right code, the cutscene starts. There's no Soul Key to pick up: you get it directly, and the "Soul-Less" achievement pops during the cutscene.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
