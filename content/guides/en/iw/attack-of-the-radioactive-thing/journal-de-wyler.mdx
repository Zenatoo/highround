Willard Wyler left his diary by the motel pool. Listening to all of his recordings unlocks the "Dear Diary" achievement.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Find the diary at the motel">
    It's a book on a table, by the motel pool. Use it to hear a recording. Before finishing the main Easter Egg, you only hear one per game.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'wyler_diary/wyler_book.webp'} alt="The diary on a table by the pool" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish the main Easter Egg">
    See [Main Easter Egg (Soul-Less)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Listen to the whole diary in the same game">
    After the cutscene, without leaving the game, go back to the diary: you can now use it as many times as you want. Listen to it to the end to unlock "Dear Diary".

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="PowerPyx" source="https://www.powerpyx.com/call-of-duty-infinite-warfare-absolution-dlc-trophy-guide-roadmap/">
        Don't play as Elvira: Willard has no lines for her, and the diary doesn't work.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>
</Checklist>
