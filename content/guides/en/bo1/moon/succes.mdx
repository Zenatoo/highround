Moon has **7 achievements** (trophies on PlayStation). No French names were found: the names are the game's English ones. The achievements shared by all maps (like **The Collector** or **Sacrificial Lamb**) are in [The base game zombies achievements](../../kino-der-toten/succes/). Check them off as you go: your progress stays on your device.

{/* TO CHECK: Platinum Pursuit's PS3 list counts 6 trophies for Rezurrection while IGN and PowerUp Gaming list 7 (shared note). Grades/Gamerscore: IGN (Xbox 360) and PowerUp Gaming (PS5). */}

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Cryogenic Slumber Party (silver, 75G)">
    Finish part 1 of the Easter Egg: the pyramid opens. Doable in solo. See [Main Easter Egg: Richtofen's Grand Scheme](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Big Bang Theory (gold, 100G)">
    Finish part 2: the rockets hit the Earth. You need at least 2 players. See [Main Easter Egg: Richtofen's Grand Scheme](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="One Small Hack for a Man... (bronze, 5G)">
    Hack something. The easiest way: take the **Hacker** in the Laboratories and hack a **barrier** that the zombies have broken. See [The Hacker and the space suit](../hacker-et-combinaison/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="One Giant Leap (bronze, 5G)">
    Be **trapped in the arrival room** and freed by a **revive**, in co-op. The Excavators must drill into Tunnel 6 and Tunnel 11.

    **To check:** the following method comes from a single source.

    Place the player(s) who want the achievement on the **switch side** (Laboratories, Bio-Dome, teleporter) and at least one player near the arrival room (the Quick Revive one). Lure a crawler near that room, then the other players **go down with a grenade** (it does not work with PhD Flopper). Let them bleed, then kill the crawler to end the round. In the next round, they are revived in the arrival room by the player who stayed there: achievement.

    {/* TO CHECK: method = hXcHector only. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Perks in Spaaaaace! (bronze, 30G)">
    Buy **all 8 perks** in the same game. You only keep 4 at a time: with the **Hacker**, hack a machine you already bought to get the perk and your points back, then buy the next one. The perks are in [The Perk-a-Colas](../atouts/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fully Armed and Operational (bronze, 25G)">
    Have **3 Pack-a-Punched weapons at the same time**. You need **Mule Kick** (4,000 points, third weapon) and **15,000 points** to upgrade everything at Area 51. Do not get put down or grabbed by an Astronaut Zombie: losing Mule Kick makes you lose the third weapon. See [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ground Control (bronze, 10G)">
    Stop **every Excavator** from drilling into the base, **in the same game**. Listen for the voice announcing the attacks, take the **Hacker**, go back to the arrival room and hack the console blinking green. Do it for all three, over several rounds. Remember: the Hacker replaces the suit.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="hXcHector" source="https://www.hxchector.com/moon">
    The Hacker saves time for three achievements: One Small Hack for a Man..., Perks in Spaaaaace! and Ground Control. Go get it early, but remember you lose the suit.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
