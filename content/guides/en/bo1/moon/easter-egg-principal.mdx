This is the biggest Easter Egg of Black Ops 1: the end of the Zombies maps' story. It is done in **two parts**, each with its own achievement:

- **Cryogenic Slumber Party** (part 1): the pyramid opens and frees Samantha. Possible in **solo**.
- **Big Bang Theory** (part 2): Richtofen swaps his soul with Samantha's, then the rockets hit the Earth. You need **at least 2 players**.

The quest is called "Richtofen's Grand Scheme": this is the wording from the description of the Cryogenic Slumber Party achievement.

**Before you start:**

- Count on a long game: the steps mostly take patience (Excavator Pi can take **several rounds** to arrive).
- **Prerequisites in Black Ops 1:** you must have done the Call of the Dead Easter Egg ([Co-op Easter Egg: Ensemble Cast](../../call-of-the-dead/easter-egg-coop/)) and the Shangri-La one ([Main Easter Egg: Time Travel Will Tell](../../shangri-la/easter-egg-principal/)) beforehand. They give the player who plays **Richtofen** the Focusing Stone and the **Golden Rod** (also called Vril Device or Vril Generator depending on the source).

  **To check:** the sources do not agree on who must have done these two quests. One source says "everyone", others say "the Richtofen player is enough".

  {/* TO CHECK: prerequisites. callofdutyzombies.com + IGN guide text: all players (IGN) / the player who is Richtofen (CZ note, hXcHector: "Richtofen should have the Vril Generator"); IGN's own comment says two players finished it with only one having both. */}

- From the **Mystery Box**, you need a **Wave Gun**, a **QED** and a **Gersch Device** (you can draw them during the quest). See [Wave Gun, QED and Gersch Device](../armes-speciales/).
- You also need the **Hacker**: [The Hacker and the space suit](../hacker-et-combinaison/).
- **Let Excavator Pi drill into Tunnel 6.** Do not hack it before step 3.
- Order tip: at first, only open the **Tunnel 6** doors.

## Part 1: Cryogenic Slumber Party

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power and play Samantha Says">
    Turn on the power (Tunnel 6 is recommended, it will be used again). See [Turn on the power](../courant/). Then go back down to the bottom of the Receiving Bay, toward the Tunnel 6 entrance, outside: there are **4 terminals** (from left to right: **red, green, blue, yellow**).

    Each terminal lights up in turn in its color, then all four flash together. Press the terminals **in the same order** (action button: X on Xbox, Square on PlayStation, F on PC). All four flash twice, then the sequence starts again, **longer**. Keep going until all four screens flash quickly in green then turn off.

    {/* TO CHECK: ZT says up to five colours; CZ and IGN do not give the length. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hack the buttons in the Laboratories">
    Take the **Hacker** in the Laboratories. Hack **one of the 4 red buttons** on the wall (it costs **500 points**). Four terminals then light up in green across the three floors of the Laboratories.

    The player holding the Hacker has **60 seconds** to **hack all four**. Then, activate **all four red buttons at the same time**: there is no more time limit, but it must be simultaneous, so run or jump next to them (with two or more players, or very fast alone). A high-pitched hum and an automated voice confirm it.

    If you fail, start again from the first button (500 points again).

    {/* TO CHECK: the 500-point retry cost = Techcall blog (vague) only. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Let Excavator Pi drill into Tunnel 6">
    You have to wait for **Pi** to drill into **Tunnel 6**. This can happen **at any moment**, sometimes after many rounds. When the voice announces it, **ignore the alarm** until the tunnel is drilled. Then go to the arrival room and **hack Pi's console** with the Hacker: the Excavator leaves and Tunnel 6 is open again.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Follow the black egg to the pyramid">
    A **floating sphere** (the "Black Egg") appears in Tunnel 6, near the **M16** wall weapon. You have to **knife it or shoot it** to make it move. It bounces through the tunnel (sometimes it sticks to the wall or ceiling: hit it again) and stops above two barrels, near the **MPL**, in front of the exit door.

    Outside, it rises toward the **dish** (satellite antenna) above the Receiving Bay. Shoot it with the **Wave Gun**: the egg comes back down into **Tunnel 11**. It bounces again and gets stuck in a **hard-to-see corner** near the **Semtex** wall weapon. Free it with a **Semtex** or an explosive weapon (the Ray Gun works). It then bounces, goes to the pyramid door and enters the **Mythos Disc**.

    If the egg seems to disappear, shoot where you last saw it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill the tube and open the pyramid">
    A **tube** rises to the right of the pyramid. Kill **25 zombies** near it. Then activate the **switch** on the wall to the right of the tube (to the left if you are looking at the power switch). The pyramid opens and shows **Samantha frozen in ice**. Everyone gets a **90-second Death Machine** and you earn **Cryogenic Slumber Party**.

    {/* TO CHECK: Techcall says 25 to 30 kills; CZ, ZT, IGN say 25. */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Part 2: Big Bang Theory (2 players or more)

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Bring back the Casimir plates from Area 51">
    Teleport to **Area 51**. To the right of the teleporter, **outside the playable area**, a shelf holds **hexagonal plates**. Knock them down with a **grenade**. Then throw a **Gersch Device** onto them **or right next to them**: they get pulled toward the teleporter. Go back to the Moon.

    Use the Death Machine at Area 51 for points.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Charge the Golden Rod">
    The plates are on the ground in front of **Quick Revive**. Throw a **QED** at them to send them to the **computer**, in the opposite corner. Then find a **big S-shaped cable**: it most often appears in the **Laboratories** (on the stairs going up to Deadshot, under the desks), but also in Tunnel 6 or outside near the Mystery Box spot. Go back to the computer and **hold the action button** to plug it in.

    The **Richtofen** player puts the **Golden Rod** between the plates, then presses the action button in front of the computer until the dialogue ends: the screen stays green. Take the charged Golden Rod.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Swap the souls">
    At the pyramid, **four tubes** rise in the corners. Fill each one with **25 zombies** (100 in total). Samantha screams and the map shakes. When all are full, Richtofen uses the **Mythos Disc** to insert the Golden Rod. Richtofen and Samantha swap souls: the game's voice becomes Richtofen's, and the Richtofen player gets **all the perks for good**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Arm the rockets">
    Throw a **QED** at the pyramid's sphere: it comes loose and goes near the four **Samantha Says** terminals. Play Samantha Says again **three times**, harder each time. Each win raises a **rocket** out of the trench. When all three are up, throw a **Gersch Device** at the sphere.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The launch">
    Dialogue and a countdown follow, then the **three rockets** head for the Earth. **All players** get all the perks for good, and you earn **Big Bang Theory**. The story: Maxis, now an AI, asks the team to destroy the Earth to cut Richtofen's link to the Aether.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## What can go wrong

- The black egg gets stuck outside the pyramid if you do the steps out of order.
- A bad Gersch Device throw at the plates: Samantha takes the device.
- You have no explosive to free the egg in Tunnel 11.
- The Hacker: only 60 seconds to hack the four terminals. And the Hacker holder has no suit: they cannot leave the base.
- Pi takes a long time to arrive: you can keep playing normally.

## Solo or co-op

**Part 1** can be done alone. **Part 2** needs at least 2 players, one of whom plays Richtofen. According to the sources, the full quest is not possible in solo. Some videos show "solo": be careful, they may show another version of the game.

## Black Ops III version

Moon also exists in the **Zombies Chronicles** of Black Ops III. In that version, the quest has **no prerequisites**, and the map has other perks and other prices. The videos you will see may come from there: do not mix the two.
