On the Moon, two pieces of equipment change everything: the **P.E.S. suit** (to breathe) and the **Hacker** (to hack almost anything). But **you cannot have both**: taking the Hacker makes you lose the suit.

## The P.E.S. suit and the air

- Without a suit and a helmet, you **die** outside: there is **no air and no gravity** in unprotected places. Jump: if you land slowly, there is no gravity.
- A suit is waiting for you in the **Receiving Bay**, when you arrive. Others are in the **airlocks** between the open areas and Tunnel 6, Tunnel 11, the Laboratories and the Bio-Dome.
- After the power (see [Turn on the power](../courant/)), you can **take off your helmet indoors**, as long as the windows are not broken. The switch room is outside: keep your helmet on.
- **Do not use explosives indoors**: broken windows drain the room of its air and gravity. The explosive bullets of Mustang & Sally do the same. The **Bio-Dome** looks like it is outside, but its windows do not break.
- The yellow **launch pads** throw you into the air. A big fall can put you down without Jugger-Nog or PhD Flopper.

## Where to find the Hacker

The Hacker is a small object placed in the **Laboratories**, on a randomly chosen desk or shelf, between Double Tap Root Beer and Deadshot Daiquiri. Only one player can hold it at a time.

**To check:** the precise list of the 6 spots comes from a single site (Game Rant). The other sources only say "on a random desk in the Laboratories".

{/* TO CHECK: 6 spots, 2 per floor, marked by yellow paper = Game Rant only. GamesRadar and hXcHector say "a random desk in the labs". */}

1. Across from Double Tap, to the left of the entrance door.
2. To the left of the stairs to the 2nd floor.
3. On a shelf to the right, just after the 2nd floor stairs.
4. On a shelf to the right of the stairs to the 3rd floor.
5. On a shelf behind a chair, to the right of Deadshot Daiquiri (3rd floor).
6. On a shelf to the left of the Bio-Dome doors (3rd floor).

When you take it, you **give up the suit**: you can no longer go outside. To get back outside, there are three ways: go back to Area 51 then return, get grabbed by an **Astronaut Zombie**, or jump into a **Gersch Device** black hole. See [Wave Gun, QED and Gersch Device](../armes-speciales/).

## The Excavators

After the power, three **Excavators** can drill into the base: **Pi** (Tunnel 6), **Omicron** (Tunnel 11) and **Epsilon** (Bio-Dome). A loudspeaker announces each attack. If one is very close, you go down almost immediately, even with Jugger-Nog. When it drills through, the area loses its air and gravity.

To stop one: go to the **arrival room** (the Quick Revive one) with the Hacker and hack the console whose light is blinking green. This gives you the **Ground Control** achievement (see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/)).

The goal can be the opposite. For the Easter Egg, let **Pi drill into Tunnel 6**, then hack its console to reopen the tunnel: see [Main Easter Egg: Richtofen's Grand Scheme](../easter-egg-principal/). At high rounds, letting the Excavator drill into the **Bio-Dome** is a strategy: the pads and low gravity make it a good place to fight the horde, according to GamesRadar.

## The enemies of the Moon

- **Astronaut Zombies**: their name is displayed above their head, like a player. If they grab you, they headbutt you, **steal your first perk** and teleport you a bit further away. They take a lot of damage, **ignore the Nuke and Insta-Kill**, and explode when they die: the zombies around are killed, and players are pushed back without damage. A shot to the visor does more damage. They always come back (60 points per kill).
- **Nova 6 crawlers**: they arrive when you open the Double Tap door and can **teleport** forward. Their gas blurs your vision and slows you down without a suit. A knife hit does not make them explode.
- **Hellhounds**: only at Area 51, once the siren has sounded.
