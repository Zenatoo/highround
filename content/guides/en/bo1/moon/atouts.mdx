Moon has **8 Perk-a-Cola machines**: Quick Revive, Jugger-Nog, Speed Cola, Double Tap Root Beer, Stamin-Up, PhD Flopper, Deadshot Daiquiri and **Mule Kick**, a new perk that gives a third weapon. You can have **4 at the same time**. The effects and general rules are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/).

Two machines are on **Earth** (Area 51): Jugger-Nog or Speed Cola, only one at a time. The other six are on the Moon base and work after the power is on (see [Turn on the power](../courant/)).

## The machines

**To check:** the prices and places come from hXcHector's guide (the same author as the IGN wiki), except Mule Kick, also confirmed by GamesRadar. No second opinion was found for the other prices.

| Perk | Price | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quick Revive** | 500 in solo, 1,500 in co-op | **Receiving Bay**, the arrival room on the Moon |
| **Jugger-Nog** | 2,500 | **Area 51**, next to the teleporter. Alternates with Speed Cola every time you come back |
| **Speed Cola** | 3,000 | **Area 51**, same spot, alternating with Jugger-Nog |
| **Stamin-Up** | 2,000 | **Tunnel 11**, second room, near the Semtex |
| **Double Tap Root Beer** | 2,000 | **Laboratories**, after the switch. Opening its door wakes the Nova 6 crawlers |
| **Deadshot Daiquiri** | 1,500 | **Laboratories**, upstairs, near the Bio-Dome and the MP5K wall weapon |
| **Mule Kick** | 4,000 | **Outside, on the surface**, near the teleporter to Area 51, on the Laboratories side |
| **PhD Flopper** | 2,000 | **Bio-Dome**, at ground level |

In solo, **Quick Revive** lets you get back up on your own, **3 times**: then the machine disappears.

## Mule Kick

**Mule Kick** lets you carry **a third weapon**. If you lose the perk (by going down, for example), you also lose the third weapon. It was born on Moon, then an update added it to all the other Black Ops maps (PC patch 1.15 from 2011 says so: "available on all Zombies maps").

## PhD Flopper and falls

**PhD Flopper** removes fall damage and explosive damage, and makes a small explosion when you dive (prone) from a height. It is useful on Moon because of the **low gravity** and the Bio-Dome's **launch pads**: without PhD Flopper or Jugger-Nog, a big fall can put you down. Avoid the first Bio-Dome pad if you have neither.

## All the perks in one game

Buying all 8 perks in the same game is an achievement, "Perks in Spaaaaace!". Since you only keep 4, the **Hacker** is used to give some back: hack a machine you already bought to get the perk and its points back. See [The Hacker and the space suit](../hacker-et-combinaison/) and [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
