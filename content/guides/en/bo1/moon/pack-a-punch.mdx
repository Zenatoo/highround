On Moon, the **Pack-a-Punch** is not on the Moon: it is on **Earth**, at **Area 51** (Hangar 18), next to the starting teleporter. You can use it **right from the start**, before the first round. To come back later, you have to go through the teleporter on the Moon's surface. The price and general workings are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/): **5,000 points** per upgrade.

## Before round 1: upgrade your pistol

At the start, there is no round. The Area 51 zombies die in **one knife hit**, until the **siren** sounds: they then become stronger (no more one-hit kills) and all run. The zombies in this area **do not count** toward the rounds.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Earn points with your knife">
    Kill zombies with your knife: each killing blow gives **130 points**. Aim for about **2,000 points** before the siren. Run in circles in the middle and avoid the water, which slows you down.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Group them and cook a grenade">
    When all the zombies have spawned, they follow you in a line. Cook a grenade (up to 4 ticks), throw it at your feet so it explodes behind you, and knife the ones that remain. Repeat as long as you have grenades: you reach about **4,000 points**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot into the group with the M1911">
    Shoot the M1911 at tightly grouped zombies to earn points on several at once. Aim for about **5,000 points**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to the Pack-a-Punch">
    Upgrade your pistol: it becomes **Mustang & Sally**. Wait for the area to be calm to pick up the weapon, then step on the teleporter (all players) to go to the Moon.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="Game Rant" source="https://gamerant.com/black-ops-moon-pack-a-punch-guide/">
    Do not fire Mustang & Sally inside the base: its explosive bullets break the windows and the room loses its air.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="faster" author="Matt Hughes (GamesRadar)" source="https://www.gamesradar.com/cod-black-ops-rezurrection-dlc-guide">
    If you prefer **Jugger-Nog** to a Pack-a-Punch, the same method is enough to buy it before leaving. But the machine next to the teleporter changes every time you come back (Jugger-Nog, red, or Speed Cola, green): according to GamesRadar, restart the game until you see the red one. See [The Perk-a-Colas](../atouts/).
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Coming back to the Pack-a-Punch later

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Put on the suit">
    Cross the base with the P.E.S. suit. If you are holding the **Hacker**, you have no suit: do not linger outside. See [The Hacker and the space suit](../hacker-et-combinaison/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go up to the surface">
    Take **Tunnel 6** or **Tunnel 11** (they lead to the same place), then go up the **Laboratories** stairs. On the **third floor**, take the room on the left to go out to the surface.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step on the teleporter">
    All players stand on the teleporter, to the right of the **AK74u** wall weapon. It sends you to **Groom Lake**: the Pack-a-Punch is in the center.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upgrade fast and leave">
    The Area 51 zombies are **all runners** and more keep spawning: do not stay long. **Hellhounds** also appear here, and only here on the map. Once your weapons are upgraded, take the teleporter again.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

- You cannot go back **twice in the same round**. Survive the next round, then wait **about 2 minutes**: green lights turn on above the teleporter and the cage opens.
- Later in the game, with a good weapon, Area 51 is used to **earn "free" points**: these zombies do not change the round.

{/* TO CHECK: the 2-minute wait and "once per round" = Game Rant PaP guide only. */}

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="Game Rant" source="https://gamerant.com/black-ops-moon-pack-a-punch-guide/">
    With the **Hacker**, hack the Pack-a-Punch machine: grates close around you for about **30 seconds** and zombies cannot get through. Also throw a **Gersch Device** to pull them in. The article advises against going back without these two items.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Several upgraded weapons

With **Mule Kick** (4,000 points, see [The Perk-a-Colas](../atouts/)), you carry three weapons. To have **three upgraded at the same time**, you need 15,000 points at the Pack-a-Punch. It is an achievement: see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
