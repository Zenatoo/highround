Moon hides **one real song** and **three short tunes** to activate. The song, "**Coming Home**", is composed by **Kevin Sherwood** and sung by **Elena Siegman**. There is no fight: you just need to find the right places.

## The song: "Coming Home"

Find **3 teddy bears wearing helmets** and press the action button on each one (X on Xbox, Square on PlayStation, F on PC).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Under the starting area">
    The first bear is **under the starting area** of the Moon base.
  </Step>

  <Step title="In the airlock between the M16 and the Power Room">
    The second is in the **airlock** between the M16 wall weapon and the switch room.
  </Step>

  <Step title="In a broken wall near Stamin-Up">
    The third is in a **broken wall**, near the Stamin-Up machine (Tunnel 11).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The three short tunes

These are not real songs: short electronic tunes. Press the action button on:

- **the computer next to the MP5K wall weapon**: an electronic version of "Damned" (called **Re-Damned** by Kevin Sherwood);
- **the equipment near Stamin-Up**: "Coming Home" in an **8-bit** version;
- **the equipment across from Double Tap Root Beer**: "Pareidolia" in an **8-bit** version.

"Pareidolia" is also Shangri-La's hidden song: see [Hidden song: Pareidolia](../../shangri-la/musique-pareidolia/).
