Moon has three special weapons, all found in the **Mystery Box** (950 points): the **Wave Gun**, the **Quantum Entanglement Device (QED)** and the **Gersch Device**. The Ray Gun is there too. The first three are used in the Easter Egg: see [Main Easter Egg: Richtofen's Grand Scheme](../easter-egg-principal/). The Mystery Box rules (moving, Fire Sale, etc.) are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/).

## Wave Gun

The **Wave Gun** is actually **two energy pistols** held one in each hand (the "Zap Guns"). Each one fires on its own and can kill in one hit. On console, **press left on the directional pad** to merge them into **a single weapon**, a rifle. Its energy disables zombies in a small radius: they **swell up**, rise, then **explode**.

{/* TO CHECK: PC key to merge the two guns not found. Pack-a-Punched name and ammo of the Wave Gun: not found in any readable source (a video exists: 3dP4goCEkdU). */}

- It comes **only from Moon's Mystery Box**.
- It is needed for the Easter Egg: a shot at the satellite sends the black egg to Tunnel 11.
- Its upgraded name and upgraded ammo: no readable source. Watch MrDalekJD's video above.

## Quantum Entanglement Device (QED)

The **QED** is a **special grenade** with a **random effect**. When you throw it, a shotgun turret, a black hole, etc. can appear. **Some effects hurt you** or your teammates: be careful where you throw it.

The description of Kenshin9977's video "Les effets des Q.E.D." mentions, for example, an effect that **revives** one or several teammates and an effect that **upgrades the weapon you are holding**. The video details them all.

{/* TO CHECK: the two effects come from links in the video description only; full list not found in text. */}

In the Easter Egg, the QED is used to **push the plates** toward the computer and to **detach the black egg** from the pyramid.

## Gersch Device

The **Gersch Device** is a special grenade that creates a **black hole**: it pulls zombies in. You have **3** when you take it out of the box, and it takes the place of your special grenade. It only exists on **Ascension** and **Moon**.

- **To teleport**: by jumping into the black hole, you are sent elsewhere on the map. It is also a way to **get back outside without a suit** (for example when you are holding the Hacker). See [The Hacker and the space suit](../hacker-et-combinaison/).
- **To protect yourself**: throw it at Area 51 to keep zombies away while you use the Pack-a-Punch. See [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
- **In the Easter Egg**: to bring the Casimir plates onto the Area 51 teleporter, then to finish with the black egg.
