Like on the earlier maps, Moon hides **radios** with story messages, and adds **data tapes** ("Data Tapes"). All of this is done without any special fight. For the songs, see [The hidden songs](../musiques/). For the big quest, see [Main Easter Egg: Richtofen's Grand Scheme](../easter-egg-principal/).

## The 5 radios

Press the action button on each radio (X on Xbox, Square on PlayStation, F on PC). You can find them **in any order**: the messages always play in story order. They are long: get out of the zombies' reach. The crawlers will probably come anyway.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Near the Olympia">
    Outside, **near rocks in the dirt**, next to the Olympia wall weapon (Tunnel 6).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Above the MPL room">
    On a **pipe**, above the room after the MPL. The **power must still be off**, or an **Excavator must have drilled through** the room, to reach it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Near Double Tap">
    **Outside, behind a window**, next to Double Tap Root Beer. **Break the glass with a grenade.**
  </Step>

  <Step title="At the crane, near Mule Kick">
    **Hanging from a crane**, near Mule Kick, just after the MP5K room.
  </Step>

  <Step title="In the Bio-Dome">
    On a **piece of metal**, in the Bio-Dome, across from PhD Flopper. Use the **launch pads** to reach it.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The 6 data tapes

Six data tapes are hidden in **random places** on the map. When you have one, bring it to the **reel-to-reel tape recorder** in the **Laboratories**, between Double Tap Root Beer and Deadshot Daiquiri. Each tape contains a message from the Zombies story. The sources say you get them one after the other.

Kenshin9977's video shows each radio (from 17:14) and each reel (from 26:27).

## Other secrets

- **No hidden boss** on Moon.
- **Samantha Says**, the color game, is part of the main quest.
- No Jolts in Black Ops 1: only the radios, the tapes and the songs.
