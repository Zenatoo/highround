On Moon, there is **only one switch**. It is in the big room at the center of the base, the **MPD / Power Room**, next to the **pyramid**. Before you turn it on, the Moon base has no power, no air and no gravity. Once the power is on, air and gravity come back in some areas, the teleporter to Earth and the base perks work, and the three **Excavators** wake up.

The general rules (points, Mystery Box, perks, Pack-a-Punch) are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/).

## The way to the switch

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Start from Earth">
    You start at **Area 51** (Hangar 18), with no round: the zombies in this area do not count. All players step on the teleporter to leave for the Moon. Round 1 starts when you arrive. The tip for earning points before leaving is in [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put on the suit">
    In the **Receiving Bay**, the arrival room, take the **P.E.S.** suit: without it, you suffocate outside. Everything about it is in [The Hacker and the space suit](../hacker-et-combinaison/). Quick Revive is in this room.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open a door in the arrival room">
    Two doors (**A** and **B**) lead out of the room. They cost **750 each** according to the map. Opening one automatically unlocks the other, and they lead to the same place, outside.

    {/* TO CHECK: door price 750 = hXcHector plan only (read by eye). GamesRadar says opening one unlocks the other. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose Tunnel 6 or Tunnel 11">
    Outside, two paths lead to the center: **Tunnel 6** and **Tunnel 11**. They have as many doors as each other and each has a good weapon, so no real advantage according to GamesRadar. Count **about 3,000 points** in all, including the starting door.

    - **Tunnel 6**: the Olympia at the entrance, the MPL in the next room, the M16 near the end.
    - **Tunnel 11**: the M14, the PM63 and the Semtex. **Stamin-Up** is in the second room.

    **To check:** the door prices come from a single annotated map, read by eye. For Tunnel 6: 750, then 1,000, then 1,250. For Tunnel 11, the map shows more labels (750, 750, 750 and 1,250), which does not match GamesRadar's "3,000". Check in game.

    {/* TO CHECK: Tunnel 6 = 750 + 1,000 + 1,250 (plan) = 3,000 (GamesRadar, consistent). Tunnel 11 = more labels on the plan than 3,000 allows; GamesRadar says equal number of doors. */}

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="tech" author="hXcHector" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-black-ops/Moon_Easter_Egg_-_Cryogenic_Slumber_Party_/_Big_Bang_Theory">
        If you want to do the Easter Egg, only open the Tunnel 6 doors at first: it is used for several steps. See [Main Easter Egg: Richtofen's Grand Scheme](../easter-egg-principal/).
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reach the central room and flip the switch">
    The central room contains the **pyramid**, the **Stakeout** shotgun and often the **Mystery Box**. The switch is right next to the pyramid, in front of the fuel tanks. Activate it: the power is on.

    If the Mystery Box is not there, it may appear later on the Moon's surface.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## What the power changes

- **Air and gravity** come back in some areas. You can then take off your helmet indoors, as long as the windows are not broken. The switch room is outside: keep your helmet on.
- **The teleporter** from the surface to Area 51 works.
- **The base perks** can be bought. The Area 51 ones work from the start.
- **The Excavators** become active: at any moment, a loudspeaker announces that one of them is going to drill into Tunnel 6, Tunnel 11 or the Bio-Dome.

**Watch the windows.** Explosives break the windows of closed rooms and let the air and gravity escape. Avoid explosives indoors. The Bio-Dome looks like it is outside, but its windows do not break.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="hXcHector" source="https://www.hxchector.com/moon">
    Do not go near an Excavator: it puts you down almost immediately. To stop them with the Hacker, see [The Hacker and the space suit](../hacker-et-combinaison/).
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## After the power

From the central room, go up the stairs to the right of the pyramid and keep going through the doors until you see the surface through a window. From there, one door leads to the surface, another to the **Bio-Dome**. The two paths meet further on. On the surface, the big circle is the **teleporter to Area 51**. The **Laboratories** (three floors) have **Double Tap Root Beer** and **Deadshot Daiquiri**. See [The Perk-a-Colas](../atouts/).

On the Bio-Dome, the yellow launch pads throw you into the air: avoid the first one without PhD Flopper or Jugger-Nog, a bad fall can put you down.

The **Nova 6 crawlers** arrive as soon as you open the Double Tap door: the power is not needed. They can teleport short distances. Their gas does not bother you if you wear the suit.
