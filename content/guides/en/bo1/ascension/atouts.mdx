Ascension has **five base Perk-a-Colas**. **PhD Flopper** and **Stamin-Up** appear here for the first time. **Double Tap** does not exist on this map. You can only have **four perks** at a time. The general rules (prices, maximum number) are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/).

**To check:** the prices and locations come from a single source (hXcHector, whose text IGN reuses). Check them in game.

{/* TO CHECK: prices and spots of the 5 machines: hXcHector Ascension page only (IGN wiki is the same author). Price rule: two independent sources needed. Double Tap absence: hXcHector + IGN (same author) + the perk buttons in mmmrkennedy list only 4 machines besides Quick Revive; no independent text says "no Double Tap" besides IGN. */}

| Perk | Price | Effect | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Quick Revive** | 1,500 points (500 in solo) | Revives twice as fast. In solo: you get back up on your own, 3 purchases maximum, and you come back with the Mustang and Sally | Starting room |
| **Jugger-Nog** | 2,500 points | A lot more health | Tower, second floor, near the starting room |
| **Speed Cola** | 3,000 points | Reloads twice as fast | Building behind the starting area, toward the "Speed Cola" lander station |
| **PhD Flopper** | 2,000 points | No more fall damage or damage from your own explosives; a jump from high up causes a small explosion that kills | Near the "PhD Flopper" lander station and a broken-down truck |
| **Stamin-Up** | 2,000 points | You run faster and for longer | Hallway near the "Stamin-Up" lander station |

For the main Easter Egg, you need one player in front of **Jugger-Nog, PhD Flopper, Speed Cola and Stamin-Up**: see [Main Easter Egg: the Kassimir Mechanism](../easter-egg-principal/).

## Mule Kick

According to the IGN wiki, **Mule Kick** (third primary weapon) was added to **all** Zombies maps by an update. On Ascension, it is in the room **between Speed Cola and the power room**, for **4,000 points**. According to MP1st, PC update 1.15 (October 10, 2011) made it available on all maps.

{/* TO CHECK: Mule Kick spot and price 4,000 = IGN (hXcHector author) only. MP1st confirms "available on all maps" on PC patch 1.15 but gives no spot or price. Console update date unknown. */}

## The space monkeys

The **Space Monkeys** attack your machines: see [Space monkeys, dolls and radios](../secrets/).
