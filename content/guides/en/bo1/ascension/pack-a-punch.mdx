On Ascension, the **Pack-a-Punch** is not in a hidden room: it is under the launch pad. To open it, you have to **launch the rocket**. To launch it, you have to use the **three Lunar Landers**. The Pack-a-Punch rules (price, upgrade) are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/).

## The three landers

A lander is the capsule you arrived in at the start of the game. There are three stations around the map, in the "far corners":

- **PhD Flopper**: on your right when you leave the starting room, on the way to the power.
- **Stamin-Up**: on your left when you leave the start, at the end of that path.
- **Speed Cola**: in the buildings after the switch, toward the rocket.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power">
    Without power, the landers don't work. See [Turn on the power](../courant/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to each station and bring the lander back from it">
    Call the lander with the station's control panel, get in and go back to the starting room. A ride costs **250 points**. You have to do it **from each of the three stations**, once each.

    A rocket icon shows on screen and fills in green with each return. A lander takes about 30 seconds to recharge after a ride.

    {/* TO CHECK: refuel time (about 30 s) and "250 points" per ride: hXcHector only (IGN is the same author). The one-way-from-spawn rule is also hXcHector only. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch the rocket">
    When the icon is fully green, go back to the power room and press the **launch button**, next to the switch. The rocket takes off from the pad.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/rocket_launch_button.webp'} alt="The control panel with the rocket launch button" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go down to the Pack-a-Punch">
    The machine is in a **side room, under where the rocket was**. You may need to open a door or a grate to go down. It stays open until the end of the game.

    {/* TO CHECK: gate/door prices to reach the PaP room are unknown (only a blocked-wiki snippet), none given. */}

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/pap_room.webp'} alt="The area under the launch pad, where the Pack-a-Punch is" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

When the upgrade is done, **go and get the weapon yourself** from the machine.

## A free Double Points

When the rocket takes off, shoot it until it explodes: a **Double Points** power-up appears under where it was. {/* TO CHECK: single source (mmmrkennedy), not in hXcHector/Altered Gamer. */}

## The "Space Race" achievement

You have to upgrade a weapon **before round 8**. See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="hXcHector" source="https://hxchector.com/call-of-duty-black-ops-walkthrough/ascension">
    For Space Race: only use the M1911, the M14 and the knife, buy no perks and only open the doors that lead to the switch and the landers. Once the rocket is launched, wait in the Pack-a-Punch room until you have **5,000 points**, then upgrade your weapon.
  </Tip>

  <Tip type="mistake" author="Altered Gamer" source="https://www.alteredgamer.com/cod-black-ops/115603-getting-tactical-with-zombies">
    Don't forget to take your weapon back from the machine: it isn't handed back on its own.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
