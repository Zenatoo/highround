Three **teddy bears** are hidden on the map. Activate all three: the song "**Abracadavre**", by **Elena Siegman** and **Kevin Sherwood**, starts.
The action button is **X** on Xbox 360, **Square** on PS3 and **F** on PC.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Starting room">
    On the upper floor of the starting room, **to the left** of the **Olympia** weapon wall.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Speed Cola station">
    **On top of a wall**, to the left of the hallway between the lander station and the **Speed Cola** machine.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Stamin-Up station">
    **Behind a grate**, to the left of the lander when you face the station.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

To find the lander stations, see [Launch the rocket and unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).

Another hidden song exists in the Black Ops III version ("Samantha's Sorrow"). It is **not** in Black Ops 1.
