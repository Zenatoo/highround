From the start of the game, a voice asks you for help: it is **Dr. Gersch**, a prisoner of **Samantha**. He asks you to **repair the mechanism**, a sort of metal capsule near the **Stamin-Up** lander. The sources call it the **Kassimir Mechanism** (also written "Casimir").

This quest has no official name in the game and gives **no achievement**. The reward: Gersch's release, then a **90-second Death Machine** for each player.

**Before you start:**

- You need **4 players**, one per perk in step 2. In solo, the quest is impossible in the original game.
- Count **1 h 20 to 1 h 40** in co-op. The difficulty mostly depends on your luck with the Mystery Box.
- The power must be on and the Pack-a-Punch open: see [Turn on the power](../courant/) and [Launch the rocket and unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
- You also need **several Gersch Devices**, **Matryoshka Dolls**, an upgraded **Ray Gun** (Porter's X2) and an upgraded **Thundergun** (Zeus Cannon). See [Thundergun, Gersch Device and Matryoshka Dolls](../armes-speciales/). Ideally, each player carries a different item.
- Most recent videos show the **Black Ops III** version (Zombies Chronicles): some details are different there. This guide describes the Black Ops 1 version.

## Tracking your progress

Near the mechanism there is a crashed rocket module. It has **one light on for each step completed**. Go back and look at it to know where you stand. Each finished step is announced by a voice.

## The steps

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Step 1: the generator and the TV (node 1)">
    1. Get a **Gersch Device** from the Mystery Box.
    2. Go near the **PhD Flopper** lander station. At the bottom of the stairs, facing the landers, look to the right: a **glowing generator** is **outside the playable area**. You can hear a high-pitched noise there.
    3. Throw the Gersch Device **onto the generator or very close to it** so that it gets sucked in. Samantha laughs. If you throw it too far, the Gersch disappears.
    4. Go to the **Stamin-Up** station. Looking at the lander, turn left: under a staircase, a **TV** shows static. Hold the action button on it. A symbol appears: node 1 is done, the first light on the module turns on.

    {/* TO CHECK: the symbol on the TV: mmmrkennedy says an eye, Accelerated Ideas a triangle, CODZ an Illuminati logo. The generator and TV are both needed (CODZ lists them as two steps, mmmrkennedy as node 1). */}

    Depending on the sources, the symbol is an eye, a triangle or an "Illuminati" logo. Don't worry about it: only the change of image matters.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 2: the perk buttons (node 2)">
    During the **next space monkey round**, a red button appears near four machines: **Jugger-Nog, PhD Flopper, Speed Cola and Stamin-Up** (not Quick Revive). The **4 players** must each press a button **at the same time**. A beep signals success. If you fail, you have to wait for the next monkey round.

    - **Jugger-Nog**: in front of the machine (according to one source, behind you when you face it).
    - **PhD Flopper**: on the wall to the left of the machine.
    - **Speed Cola**: on the other side of the door frame, to the right of the machine.
    - **Stamin-Up**: on the wall just to the left of the machine.

    {/* TO CHECK: Speed Cola button: mmmrkennedy + CODZ (right side of the door frame) vs Accelerated Ideas (wall opposite the stairs, left). Jugger-Nog: in front (mmmrkennedy, AI) / behind you facing the machine (CODZ). */}

    The monkeys only appear if a player has a perk: see [Space monkeys, dolls and radios](../secrets/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 3: the clock (node 3)">
    The Pack-a-Punch must be open. Go down to the spot where the rocket took off: a **wall clock** is right below, with a **circle** on the floor. The **4 players** must stay in this circle for **2 minutes straight**.

    1. First, make a **crawler zombie** spawn and take it far away, near the Stamin-Up area, so it doesn't get in the way.
    2. Everyone go back to the circle. You can shoot, move and use your equipment.
    3. When the hand has made a full turn, a **Nuke** goes off and the round ends.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'clock.webp'} alt="The wall clock under the spot where the rocket took off" credit={by} source={src} />

    {/* TO CHECK: Ballistic Knife: Accelerated Ideas says that in BO1 one player must equip it and everyone stand still; hXcHector is unsure; mmmrkennedy and CODZ say nothing and allow moving/shooting. Not required in the text. */}

    **To check:** one source says you have to equip a **Ballistic Knife** for the clock to start. Two others don't ask for it. If in doubt, bring one to the team: it costs nothing to try.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 4: spell L-U-N-A (node 4)">
    **Letters float in the sky** when a lander is running. You have to hit them with the lander, in the order **L, U, N, A**. Avoid the other letters. The landers must be **called by a teammate**: buying them doesn't work.

    Put one player in the starting room, one at **Speed Cola**, one at **Stamin-Up**, and the last one **in the lander**. Then:

    1. Lander called **from the Stamin-Up station**: letter **L**.
    2. Called **from the start**: letter **U**.
    3. Called **from the Speed Cola station**: letter **N**.
    4. Called **from the Stamin-Up station**: letter **A**.

    {/* TO CHECK: order confirmed by hXcHector, mmmrkennedy, Accelerated Ideas, CODZ (all agree). */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step 5: destroy the white orb">
    Near the crashed rocket module, in front of the **Stamin-Up** station, a **white orb** glows on the ground. The module's four lights are then all on.

    1. Throw a **Gersch Device** so that it lands on the orb.
    2. While the black hole is there, shoot it with the upgraded **Ray Gun** and the upgraded **Thundergun**, and throw **Matryoshka Dolls**.
    3. Keep going until the orb rises into the air. Gersch thanks you.

    According to CODZ, you need at least **2 Zeus Cannon shots, 2 Ray Gun shots and 1 or 2 dolls**. Too much power causes an overload; not enough, and Gersch asks for more power. You can only damage the orb while the black hole is above it.

    {/* TO CHECK: "2 Zeus + 2 Ray Gun shots + 1-2 dolls" = CODZ only. Upgraded Crossbow: hXcHector, Accelerated Ideas list it as "possibly"; mmmrkennedy and CODZ do not. */}

    **To check:** some sources also mention an upgraded **Crossbow**. The others don't.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

When everything is done, Gersch is freed, the screen briefly turns black and white while Samantha gets angry, then **each player gets a Death Machine for 90 seconds**.

## What can go wrong

- A button pressed too late in step 2: wait for the next monkey round.
- A Gersch thrown too far from the generator: it disappears. Get another one from the Mystery Box.
- Not enough special weapons for the orb: the box is a lottery, prepare your items beforehand.
