On Ascension, the special weapons come **only from the Mystery Box** (950 points). The general rules for the box are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/). Four items are specific to this map or very useful: the **Ray Gun**, the **Thundergun**, the **Gersch Device** and the **Matryoshka Dolls**.

You need them for the Easter Egg: see [Main Easter Egg: the Kassimir Mechanism](../easter-egg-principal/).

**To check:** the ammo comes from a single source (hXcHector, reused by the IGN wiki).

{/* TO CHECK: ammo and details below = hXcHector/IGN (same author). Box spots: the first box is next to the power switch (IGN, Altered Gamer); other spots unknown. */}

## Ray Gun

An automatic pistol, 20 bullets per magazine and 160 in reserve. Upgraded, it becomes the **Porter's X2 Ray Gun**.

## Thundergun

A semi-automatic special weapon, 2 bullets per magazine and 12 in reserve. Long reload (about 3 seconds). Upgraded, it becomes the **Zeus Cannon**.

{/* TO CHECK: "only one player can hold it at a time" is said by mmmrkennedy/Gameranx about the Black Ops III remake: not stated here. */}

## Gersch Device

A special grenade, also called a "black hole bomb". You get **3**. It creates a black hole that **sucks in zombies**. It takes the special grenade slot, like the Matryoshka Dolls.

The black hole can also **carry you away**: according to Altered Gamer, it is a way to escape or to save a downed teammate. The **They are going THROUGH!** achievement requires killing at least 5 zombies with a single Gersch: see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).

## Matryoshka Dolls

Another special grenade, **3** of them. Each doll causes **4 explosions**. It is not used to distract zombies.
