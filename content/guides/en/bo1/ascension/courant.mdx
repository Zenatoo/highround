Ascension is an abandoned Soviet space base. The map is in **black and white** until the power is on. There is **only one switch**, and it is **free**.

Once the power is on, the colors come back and everything starts working: the **Perk-a-Colas**, the **traps** (fire traps and automatic turrets) and the **Lunar Landers**. Without power, no Pack-a-Punch: see [Launch the rocket and unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).

The general rules (points, Mystery Box, power-ups) are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/).

## Where the switch is

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Start from the starting room">
    You start on the ground floor of the main building, the control tower. **Quick Revive** is there too.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go all the way up the tower">
    The switch is at the top of the building that sits "in the middle" of the map. You have to open doors to get there: count on several doors, the prices are not known for sure.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate it">
    Hold the action button on the switch. The colors come back.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'power_switch.webp'} alt="The power switch panel on Ascension" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

The control panel also has a **rocket launch button**, right next to it. You will use it later for the Pack-a-Punch.

## Good to know

- The first **Mystery Box** is right next to the switch. After a few uses, it changes location: the TV screen near its spots shows where it is.
- In solo, **Quick Revive** can be bought **without power** (3 purchases maximum).
- The Lunar Landers cost **250 points** per ride and need power.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="Altered Gamer" source="https://www.alteredgamer.com/cod-black-ops/115603-getting-tactical-with-zombies">
    Open doors early to learn the map. Don't go outside at random: the outdoor areas are dangerous.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
