Ascension has no boss. Here are its special enemies and its small secrets: the **Space Monkeys**, the **Matryoshka Dolls** and the **radios**. The hidden song has its own guide: [Hidden song: Abracadavre](../musique-abracadavre/).

## The Space Monkeys

Instead of **Hellhound** rounds, landers fall from the sky and release **space monkeys**. They are small, fast and hard to hit.

- A monkey round **only starts if a player has bought a perk**.
- They can attack you, but they first run to **the machines whose perk you own**. If they "attack" them long enough, everyone **loses that perk** and has to buy it again. A sound plays and the perk icon flashes on your screen.
- They throw grenades back: only throw a grenade if you cooked it so that it explodes on impact.

**The reward:**

- You kill **all the monkeys before they touch a machine**: the last one drops a **Perk-a-Cola** power-up (a random perk, even if you already have four).
- You kill all the monkeys **despite a theft**: you get a **Max Ammo**.

{/* TO CHECK: "even if you already have four" = mmmrkennedy only. hXcHector says the perk is random for each player and that you get a Max Ammo too when no perk is stolen. */}

    Stay near the machines you want to defend. With **Stamin-Up**, you can run more easily from one to the other.
  </Tip>

## The four Matryoshka Dolls

Four dolls, one per character, are hidden on the map. Press the action button: you hear a short funny line from your character.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Takeo">
    Next to the **PhD Flopper** machine.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Richtofen">
    On a **desk**, on the floor above **Jugger-Nog**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Nikolai">
    On the **machine to the left of Speed Cola**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Dempsey">
    Next to the **Claymore** wall buy, in the **Stamin-Up** station area.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

{/* TO CHECK: dolls: mmmrkennedy gives who is where; hXcHector only lists the 4 areas (near PhD Flopper, Claymore, Speed Cola, above Jugger-Nog), consistent. */}

These dolls are a separate secret and are not used for the main Easter Egg.

## The six radios

Six **radios** tell a bit of the base's story from before the zombies arrived. Each one only appears **after you have found the previous one**.

**To check:** this list comes from a single source.

{/* TO CHECK: radios list = hXcHector only (IGN same author). Order of the list is not confirmed as the order of appearance. */}

- Between the steps, near the **PhD Flopper** lander station.
- In a **cage**, near the **Claymore** station.
- In the **starting area**, near crates on the ground floor.
- In the **Pack-a-Punch room**, on pipes at the bottom.
- Next to **Speed Cola** and the Matryoshka Doll.
- Next to **Stamin-Up**.

The action button is **X** (Xbox 360), **Square** (PS3) or **F** (PC).
