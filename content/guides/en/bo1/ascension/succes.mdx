<Checklist>
  <Step title="The Eagle has Landers (35G, bronze)">
    Escape using **all three Lunar Landers**. You have to use each of the three, not the same one three times. See [Launch the rocket and unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="They are going THROUGH! (35G, bronze)">
    Kill **at least 5 zombies with a single Gersch Device**. The Gersch comes from the Mystery Box: see [Thundergun, Gersch Device and Matryoshka Dolls](../armes-speciales/). Wait for many zombies to come close, then throw it.

    On PC, this achievement didn't always unlock: update 1.15 fixed it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Space Race (45G, silver)">
    Upgrade a weapon at the Pack-a-Punch **before round 8**. See [Launch the rocket and unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/): the tip to get there quickly is there.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Chimp on the Barbie (35G, bronze)">
    Kill a **space monkey** with a **fire trap**. Buy a perk (for example Stamin-Up) to attract the monkeys, then activate the fire trap next to that machine (1,000 points, active for 30 seconds). See [Space monkeys, dolls and radios](../secrets/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

The main Easter Egg gives no achievement: see [Main Easter Egg: the Kassimir Mechanism](../easter-egg-principal/).
