The **31-79 JGb215** is Shangri-La's special weapon. Players often call it the "Baby Gun". It **shrinks** zombies. You find it **only in the Mystery Box** (950 points, see [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/)).

## How it works

- It is a **semi-automatic** weapon: **5 bullets** in the magazine, **25** in reserve.
- A zombie that gets hit becomes tiny for **a few seconds**.
- A shrunken zombie deals **little damage** and dies in **one hit**.
- You can **kick it like a ball**: just walk into it.
- The effect is short: kill them quickly.

## Pack-a-Punch: The Fractalizer

At the Pack-a-Punch (see [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/)), the weapon becomes **The Fractalizer**: **8 bullets** in the magazine, **40** in reserve, and the shrinking effect lasts **longer**.

## Why keep it

The 31-79 is used in the main Easter Egg: you shrink the crystal ball, then use the **Fractalizer** (the Pack-a-Punch version) for the last part. The normal version is not enough. Keep it: the Mystery Box is the only source. See [Main Easter Egg: Time Travel Will Tell](../easter-egg-principal/).

## The Small Consolation achievement

"Small Consolation" requires using the 31-79 JGb215 on **every type of zombie** on the map: normal zombie, **Shrieker** and **Napalm**. See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
