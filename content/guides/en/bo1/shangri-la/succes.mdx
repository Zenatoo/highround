Shangri-La has **5 achievements** (trophies on PlayStation), for a total of **150G** on Xbox 360. The names are the game's English ones. Check them off as you go: your progress stays on your device.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Time Travel Will Tell (75G, silver)">
    Take the **Focusing Stone**. You need **4 players**. Everyone gets the achievement, but only the player who takes the stone keeps all the perks. See [Main Easter Egg: Time Travel Will Tell](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Blinded By the Fright (30G, bronze)">
    Kill a **Shrieker** while it blinds you. The Shrieker is fast, its scream blurs your vision, and it kills the zombies around it when it dies. Listen for its sound to spot it. Let it get close enough to blur your vision, then kill it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Zomb Disposal (30G, bronze)">
    Kill a **Napalm** zombie without it hurting any player. It is slow, leaves fire on the ground and **explodes** when it dies. Kill it **from a distance**: the explosion can down you even with Jugger-Nog.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Monkey See, Monkey Don't (5G, bronze)">
    Get back a power-up stolen by a monkey by killing it. See [The monkeys' free perk and other secrets](../secrets/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Small Consolation (10G, bronze)">
    Use the **31-79 JGb215** on **every type of zombie** on the map. See [The 31-79 JGb215](../31-79-jgb215/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>
