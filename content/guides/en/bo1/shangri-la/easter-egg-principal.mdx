Shangri-La's Easter Egg makes you travel through time: the **Eclipse**. You help the explorers **Brock** and **Gary**, trapped in a time loop, open a passage behind the Pack-a-Punch. Reward: the **Focusing Stone**, which gives you **all the perks**, kept until the end of the game, even if you go down. It is also the **Time Travel Will Tell** achievement: see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).

**Before you start:**

- You need **4 players**, no more, no less: entering the Eclipse takes 4 presses at the same time. There is **no solo version** in the original game (PC mods let you do it alone, but not on console).
- Count **1 h 20 to 1 h 40** in co-op.
- Open **all the doors** and turn on the power: [Turn on the power](../courant/).
- Find the **31-79 JGb215** in the Mystery Box, then Pack-a-Punch it: [The 31-79 JGb215](../31-79-jgb215/) and [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
- Keep some **explosives** (grenades, Ray Gun, Monkey Bomb) and buy **Spikemores** from the Power Room wall weapon.

## The Eclipse

Almost all the steps are done **inside the Eclipse**. The 4 players press **at the same time** the four stone buttons in the corners around **Quick Revive** (action button). The Eclipse lasts **5 minutes**: a beam above Quick Revive slowly sinks into the wall to show the time left. Failing a step ends the Eclipse, but does not cost you a round: you restart the Eclipse and try again.

<Screenshot src={m + 'buttons_to_enter_eclipse.webp'} alt="The stone buttons around the Quick Revive machine" credit={by} source={src} />

From here on, **every step is done inside the Eclipse**, unless you are told otherwise. After each successful step, a **crystal ball** appears in the sky and a yellow radio gives a hint about what comes next.

## The steps

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Crystal 1: the symbol tiles">
    On the mine cart side, press the button on the brick structure near the **MPL** wall weapon. **24 tiles** come out of the ground: 12 near the mine cart, 12 near the rope bridge. A symbol only shows when you **stand on it**, and each one has a twin tile on the other side.

    Two players stand **at the same time** on two identical tiles to make them disappear. One player finds a symbol, **moves away**, then the other looks for the same one. A **wrong pair** resets all the tiles. When all 12 pairs are done, the Eclipse stops and ball 1 appears above the button structure.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tiles_minecart.webp'} alt="The symbol tiles on the mine cart side" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Crystal 2: the slide lever">
    **Three** players stand on the **metal grate** at the foot of the slide, in the AK-74u room. The **fourth** goes down the slide and touches the **lever** on the right wall as they pass. Press the action button several times to be sure. Ball 2 appears above the **geyser** near the start of the mine cart.

    {/* TO CHECK: callofdutyzombies.com says instead: "all 4 players slide down the water slide and stand still on the pressure plate for 15-30 seconds" (no lever). mmmrkennedy (3 on the grate + 1 rides) and hXcHector/IGN "slide lever" agree with each other. */}

    <Screenshot src={m + 'slide_grate.webp'} alt="The metal grate at the foot of the slide" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Crystal 3: shrink the ball">
    Knock the crystal ball above the slide down with an **explosive**. Shoot it with the **31-79** to shrink it. Then push it **with your knife** into the slide: it ends up in the **geyser** at the bottom. Stand on the geyser to **launch it into the air**. Ball 3 appears above the spikes between the mine cart area and the start. **Keep the 31-79**: you will need it at the end.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'ball_above_slide.webp'} alt="The crystal ball above the slide entrance" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Crystal 4: light the gas">
    In the tunnel at the start of the mine cart, turn the **valve** **4 times**: three pipes start to leak. Wait for a **Napalm zombie**, then lead it past each leak to light it. Do not get too close: if it explodes, you have to wait for another one. The leaks are: to the right of the valve; in the AK-74u room, above the Mystery Box spots; underground, above the **M16** wall weapon. When everything is burning, pull the **lever** next to the valve. Ball 4 appears above the zombie spawn point, near the start of the mine cart.

    {/* TO CHECK: leak spots: mmmrkennedy. callofdutyzombies.com: next to the valve, next to the geyser, before the generator room. hXcHector: kill the Napalm in the Power Switch Room, then pull the lever. */}

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'valve.webp'} alt="The valve tunnel, at the start of the mine cart" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pipe_leaking_gas.webp'} alt="A leaking pipe" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Crystal 5: plug the holes">
    Buy **Spikemores** in the Power Room. In the tunnel that leads to the waterfall, **four holes** line up on the **left** wall (going down). Blow up a Spikemore in front of each one: an object comes to plug it. When all four are plugged, press the **brick** on the wall, in the pool at the foot of the waterfall. Ball 5 appears above the **Mud Room**, on the rope bridge side.

    {/* TO CHECK: callofdutyzombies.com: "place the mines next to the holes and lure a zombie to blow it up". hXcHector: zombies must run past the mine, shooting does not work. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Crystal 6: wall tiles, teepee and dials">
    The symbols from the start are also on the **walls**: **knife** each tile to make it glow. There are **12**:

    - mine cart area: to the left of the mine cart start; at the next corner toward the start; on the spike wall between the start and the mine cart;
    - starting room: on each side of Quick Revive, behind the wall; on the spike wall toward the Mud Room; to the left of the Pack-a-Punch stairs;
    - Mud Room: to the left of the dial closest to the slide; to the left of the **PM63** wall weapon;
    - waterfall: to the left of the **Stakeout**; at the bottom of the stairs to the Power Room;
    - Power Room: to the left of the left lever.

    Then destroy the **teepee** outside the map, near the start of the mine cart, with an **explosive**. This ends the Eclipse without a ball: restart it. Finally, set the **four dials** in the Mud Room to **4, 3, 16 and 1**. The number matches the symbol **at the top** of the dial: a hook is worth 10, a bar 5, a dot 1. So: 4 = four dots, 3 = three dots, 16 = hook + bar + dot, 1 = one dot. The order does not matter; for example, coming from the start: 4 on the left dial, then 3, 16, then 1 on the right dial. Ball 6 appears above the Mud Room, behind the **Olympia** wall weapon.

      <Screenshot src={m + 'first_dial.webp'} alt="One of the Mud Room dials" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="The gongs and the dynamite">
    Take the 31-79 to the **Pack-a-Punch**: you need the **Fractalizer**. There are **eight gongs** on the map, of which **four are the right ones** (they change from game to game, but not if you fail). Hit them with your knife: a right gong rings and your character says it sounds good. A wrong one makes the **crystals flash red** and shuts off the gongs already activated. When all four ring, the crystals glow **yellow**.

    The eight gongs: at the bottom of the rope bridge near the Pack-a-Punch plate; at the top of the bridge, to the left then to the right of the **PM63**; right at the exit of the Mud Room toward the start; in the starting room, to the left of the **M14**; to the left of the mine cart start; at the bottom of the slope, to the right of the zombie window; to the right of the tunnel.

    Stand **under the crystal above the geyser**: it holds a stick of **dynamite**. Shoot it with the **Fractalizer** and **catch** the dynamite before it hits the ground (you do not need to press the action button). If you miss it, redo the gongs.

    While the crystals glow, shoot the crystal above the **Mud Room** with the Fractalizer: the shot bounces off the six balls and **shrinks the big meteor** above the Pack-a-Punch.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'glowing_crystals.webp'} alt="The crystals glowing after the four right gongs" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'crystal_with_dynamite.webp'} alt="The crystal above the geyser holding the dynamite" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="The Pack-a-Punch room">
    Open the Pack-a-Punch stairs (see [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/)). At the top, the machine is gone: a **brick wall with holes** replaces it. Brock and Gary talk about dynamite. When they are done, give the **dynamite** to Brock through the hole, then **all run down**: this ends the Eclipse.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'wall_with_hole.webp'} alt="The brick wall with a hole, at the top of the stairs" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the Focusing Stone">
    This step is done **outside the Eclipse**. Open the stairs again: the wall is gone and the **Focusing Stone** floats above an altar. Press the action button to take it. The player who takes it gets **all the perks**, for good. Everyone gets the **achievement**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'stone_on_altar.webp'} alt="The Focusing Stone on its altar" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Redoing the quest

A radio appears at the foot of the Pack-a-Punch stairs: listen to it and the Eclipse buttons are active again. You can redo the whole quest **up to 4 times** in the same game, so that **each player** gets their Focusing Stone.

## What can go wrong

- A **wrong pair of tiles** resets the tiles.
- A **wrong gong** shuts off the gongs already activated.
- A **missed dynamite** forces you to redo the gongs.
- Losing the **31-79** before the end: the Mystery Box is the only source.
- **Time**: the Eclipse lasts 5 minutes. If you run out of time, you can restart it in the next round.
- Being pushed out of the Pack-a-Punch stairs when the water rises.
- A **Napalm zombie** that explodes in the wrong place (crystal 4).

A wrong dial setting is not punished.
