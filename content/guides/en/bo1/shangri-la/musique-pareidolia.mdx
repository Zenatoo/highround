Three **red meteors** are hidden on the map. Activate all three: the song "**Pareidolia**" starts playing. It is by **Kevin Sherwood** and **Elena Siegman**.

To activate a meteor, press the action button: **X** on Xbox 360, **Square** on PS3, **F** on PC.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Starting room">
    To the **right of the spikes** that lead toward the mine cart area.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'meteor_spawn.webp'} alt="The red meteor in the starting room, to the right of the spikes" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Underground, mine cart side">
    In the room where the mine cart rails run, near the **barbed wire**, to the left of the window the zombies come out of.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'meteor_underground.webp'} alt="The red meteor in the rail room, underground" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Top of the rope bridge">
    At the very top of the **rope bridge**, **on the left** when you face the waterfall.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'meteor_rope_bridge.webp'} alt="The red meteor at the top of the rope bridge" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>
