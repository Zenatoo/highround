On Shangri-La, the power comes from **water**: two levers release water onto two water wheels, under the temple. Once the power is on, **all the perk machines** work, and you can use the **mine cart** and the **slide**. You also need the power for the Pack-a-Punch: see [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).

The general rules (points, Mystery Box, power-ups) are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/).

## The way to the levers

At the start, you have two paths. **Both lead to the power**: choose according to the weapons you want to pass. Facing the Quick Revive machine:

- the **right** path goes past the **Stakeout**;
- the **left** path goes past the **AK-74u**, the **M16** and the **Mystery Box** (it is often there, but not always).

Here is the left path:

    Turn right, following the arrow. The red-tinted room has the **M16** on the wall (**1,200 points**) and one last pile of rubble to clear. Its price is not given either.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pull the two levers">
    The two levers are side by side, along the wall of the **Power Room**, a room under the start. Both must be pulled.

## What the power turns on

- The **perk machines**: see [The Perk-a-Colas](../atouts/).
- The **mine cart** (250 points per ride) and the **slide** (free).
- The **Pack-a-Punch**, which you still have to unlock afterwards with the stone plates.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="GamesRadar" source="https://www.gamesradar.com/call-of-duty-black-ops-annihilation-dlc-guide/3">
    After 2 or 3 rounds, leave **a single zombie** alive to explore in peace: the next round does not start while it is there. You then have enough points to open a few passages.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="GamesRadar" source="https://www.gamesradar.com/call-of-duty-black-ops-annihilation-dlc-guide/3">
    Keep moving: zombies come out of the ground, the water, the ledges and the ceiling of the caves.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
