Shangri-La's Pack-a-Punch is at the top of a **stone staircase** in front of the Quick Revive machine, in the starting room. At the start, some steps are missing. To raise them, you have to **stand on stone plates** at the same time. The upgrade price is **5,000 points** (see [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/)).

**Before you start:** turn on the power, see [Turn on the power](../courant/).

## How to do it

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Look for the raised plates">
    There are **four statues** on the map. Each one has a stone plate in front of it. The number of raised plates depends on the number of players: **one per player**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap_statue.webp'} alt="A stone statue with rotating sections" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put one player on each plate">
    Each player stands on a **different plate**, at the same time. When a plate sinks, a rotating section of the statue stops. When everything has stopped, the missing steps rise.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pressure_plate.webp'} alt="A stone plate on the ground, near a gong" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Rush up to the Pack-a-Punch">
    Go to the top of the staircase in front of Quick Revive and upgrade your weapon.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap_machine.webp'} alt="The Pack-a-Punch machine at the top of the steps" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Where are the plates?

According to a recent guide, there are four:

| Area | Location |
| --- | --- |
| Starting room | In front of the Quick Revive machine |
| Rope Bridge | At the foot of the rope bridge |
| Power Room | Between the two power levers |
| Mine cart tunnel | Halfway down the tunnel that goes underground |

Only the **raised** plates count. They change from game to game.

{/* TO CHECK: "they change each game": zfrr.me only ("which slab goes with which part changes each time"). */}

## How long?

The steps stay raised for **a little under a minute**. Stones on each side of the staircase slowly rise: when they reach the top, it is over.

{/* TO CHECK: window length. hXcHector/IGN "about 45 seconds"; mmmrkennedy "60 seconds regardless of player count"; zfrr.me "about a minute". Maybe the final patch changed it. */}

**To check:** depending on the sources, the window lasts **45 seconds** or **60 seconds**. Count on 45 to be safe.

At the end, the water pushes you to the bottom of the stairs. To start again, press the plates again.
