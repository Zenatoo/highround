Shangri-La has **8 Perk-a-Cola machines**. They all work once the power is on: see [Turn on the power](../courant/). The effects of each perk are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/). You can have **4 at a time**.

| Perk | Price | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quick Revive** | 500 points in solo, 1,500 in co-op | Starting room, against the middle wall |
| **Jugger-Nog** | 2,500 points | Outside, on one of the two surface spots |
| **Speed Cola** | 3,000 points | Outside, on the other surface spot |
| **Double Tap Root Beer** | 2,000 points | Caves |
| **PhD Flopper** | 2,000 points | Caves |
| **Stamin-Up** | 2,000 points | Caves |
| **Deadshot Daiquiri** | 1,500 points | Caves |
| **Mule Kick** | 4,000 points | Waterfall area |

{/* TO CHECK: prices: hXcHector/IGN (one source) and zfrr.me agree, except zfrr.me says Deadshot is 1,000 on PC. Quick Revive spawn room: hXcHector, IGN, zfrr. */}

## What changes from game to game

Quick Revive does not move. **Jugger-Nog and Speed Cola swap places** every game. The two surface spots are, depending on the source, near a pillar close to the MPL and on the bridge next to the Mud Room.

{/* TO CHECK: surface spots: hXcHector "left or right of the starting area"; zfrr.me "by a pillar near the MPL, or on the bridge beside the Mud-Pit Maze". */}

The four other cave machines (Double Tap, PhD Flopper, Stamin-Up, Deadshot) are underground. According to one guide, they share **four spots**: near the water levers, in the Semtex room, in the M16 room and in the geyser room.

{/* TO CHECK: cave spots: zfrr.me says 4 underground spots shared by the 4 perks (random per game). hXcHector says Double Tap near the power switch, Stamin-Up near the M16, Deadshot near the Semtex, all fixed; its PhD Flopper line ("house near the lighthouse") looks copied from Call of the Dead and is not used. */}

**To check:** our sources do not agree on the exact location of each cave machine. Go around the caves before spending your points.

## Mule Kick

Mule Kick was not there when the map came out. An update added it **to all** Black Ops maps. On Shangri-La, it is in the **waterfall area**, **at the bottom of the waterfall**, near a Mystery Box spot. It costs **4,000 points** and gives you a **third weapon** (which you lose if you lose the perk).

{/* TO CHECK: Mule Kick: IGN Mule_Kick ("added to every map as of September 27th", year not printed) and MP1st PC patch 1.15 (10 Oct 2011: "Mule Kick perk machine is now available on all Zombie maps"). Location: IGN and zfrr.me. Price 4,000: IGN and zfrr.me. */}
