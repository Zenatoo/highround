Shangri-La hides a few small secrets. The hidden song is in [Hidden song: Pareidolia](../musique-pareidolia/), the big Easter Egg in [Main Easter Egg: Time Travel Will Tell](../easter-egg-principal/).

## The monkeys and the power-ups

The **zombie monkeys** are there from the start of the game. When a power-up drops and nobody takes it after a few seconds, a monkey grabs it and carries it toward the Pack-a-Punch stairs. Kill it before it leaves the area: you get the power-up back. If you manage it, you earn the **Monkey See, Monkey Don't** achievement (see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/)). If a monkey bites your feet, one knife hit is enough.

## The free perk

While it carries the power-up, the monkey changes power-up **every second**: Carpenter, Fire Sale (if available), Nuke, Double Points, Insta-Kill and Max Ammo. If you kill it, you get the power-up shown **at that moment**, even if it differs from the stolen power-up. Another monkey then comes to take it.

The trick: **if the power-up stolen at the start is a Max Ammo**, a **Perk Bottle** (a free perk) is added to the list. It is only shown for **0.25 seconds**. Kill the monkey at that moment and pick up the power-up: you get a **free perk**, even if you already have **4**.

## Blowing up all the monkeys

A controller code makes **all the monkeys** in the temple **explode**.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Stand on the tile">
    In the starting room, step on the tile that is slightly **higher** than the others and look at the Pack-a-Punch stairs.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tile_to_stand_on.webp'} alt="The slightly raised tile in the starting room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the code">
    Press the directional pad: **Up, Up, Down**, then **A** (Xbox) or **Cross** (PS3). Leave **less than 0.6 seconds** between presses.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The waterfall trap

At the foot of the waterfall, a **displaced brick** in the rocks triggers a free trap: water is pushed out and sweeps enemies away. It can be reused about every **30 seconds**.
