The base game has **4 achievements** (trophies on PlayStation) for Zombies. Three work on **any map** (Kino der Toten and Five); only one is tied to Five. The names are the in-game English ones. The downloadable maps have their own achievements, in their own guides: [The map's achievements and trophies](../../ascension/succes/) (Ascension), [The map's achievements and trophies](../../call-of-the-dead/succes/) (Call of the Dead), [The map's achievements and trophies](../../shangri-la/succes/) (Shangri-La) and [The map's achievements and trophies](../../moon/succes/) (Moon).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="The Collector (20 G, silver)">
    Buy **all the wall weapons** on a map in **the same game**. Ammo and upgrades don't count. Kino der Toten is considered the easiest map for this. See the table below.
  </Step>

  <Step title="See Me, Stab Me, Heal Me (15 G, bronze)">
    Revive a downed teammate **from a distance** with a **Ballistic Knife** upgraded with Pack-a-Punch. See [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/). Works on all maps.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sacrificial Lamb (15 G, bronze)">
    Hit a teammate (or let them hit you) with a bolt from a **Crossbow** upgraded with Pack-a-Punch, and kill **6 zombies** with the explosion. Works on all maps. PowerPyx advises spending nothing other than on the weapon you want, never buying ammo, and looking for it in the Mystery Box: it often takes several tries.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hands Off the Merchandise (20 G, bronze)">
    Kill the **Pentagon Thief** before it steals your equipment. **Five only.** It only appears after the power is on. See [The Pentagon Thief](../../five/voleur-du-pentagone/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The Collector on Kino der Toten

Purchase prices of the map's wall weapons (hXcHector and COD Zombified agree):

| Weapon | Price |
| --- | --- |
| M14 | 500 points |
| Olympia | 500 points |
| PM63 | 1,000 points |
| MPL | 1,000 points |
| MP5K | 1,000 points |
| MP40 | 1,000 points |
| AK74u | 1,200 points |
| M16 | 1,200 points |
| Stakeout | 1,500 points |
| Bowie Knife | 3,000 points |
| Claymores | 1,000 points |
| Grenades (4) | 250 points |

Where to find them: M14 and Olympia in the **Lobby**; PM63 in the **Upper Hall**; MPL in the **Lower Hall**; AK74u in the **Alley**; M16 on the **stage / in the theater**; Stakeout and MP40 in the **Foyer**; MP5K in the **Dressing Room**; Bowie Knife on the **theater seats**, near Jugger-Nog; Claymores on the stage / in the theater; the **grenades** in the **projector room**, next to the Pack-a-Punch (the only place you can buy them).

These prices add up to **13,150 points**, matching PowerPyx's figure (weapons). With the doors (about **7,500 points** according to PowerPyx), count roughly **20,650 points**, **21,150** with Quick Revive.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="PowerPyx" source="https://www.powerpyx.com/guides/call_of_duty_black_ops.html">
    Keep moving and watch all around you. Play the first 3 rounds with the **knife** and the pistol only to keep your points, and throw grenades at big hordes: you get 2 back (and 2 Claymores) at the end of every round.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="faster" author="PowerPyx" source="https://www.powerpyx.com/guides/call_of_duty_black_ops.html">
    In multiplayer, **each player** has to buy every weapon. One player can keep their points while the other opens the doors.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
