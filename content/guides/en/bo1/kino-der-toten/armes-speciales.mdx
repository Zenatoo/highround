Kino der Toten has three special weapons. **None can be bought off the wall**: they all come out of the **Mystery Box** (950 points, see [Black Ops Zombies basics](../systemes-bo1/)). The **Thundergun** was born on this map. The magazines and reserves come from the old IGN wiki.

## Ray Gun

A green **automatic** plasma pistol, with a rather slow rate of fire. Up close, it can down you. Magazine **20**, reserve **160**, reload of about 2.3 seconds. If you are downed, it replaces your M1911 with a single magazine.

With **Pack-a-Punch**, it becomes the **Porter's X2 Ray Gun**: magazine **40**, reserve **200**.

## Thundergun

A **semi-automatic** compressed-air gun: it pushes back the zombies in front of you and kills them. Magazine **2**, reserve **12**, reload of about 3 seconds. On Kino it only exists in the Mystery Box (and on Ascension).

With Pack-a-Punch, it becomes the **Zeus Cannon**: magazine **4**, reserve **24**, with no manual reload.

None of our written sources give a reliable trick to be sure of getting it from the box.

## Monkey Bomb (Cymbal Monkey)

A special grenade: **3** monkeys per pull. The monkey makes noise and attracts zombies, then explodes in a radius. A **Max Ammo** gives some back. The sources don't mention a Pack-a-Punch version.
