On Kino der Toten, there is **a single** power switch, on the theater **stage**. The power turns on the perk machines and the traps, opens the third door of the entrance hall and starts the **Nova 6 crawlers**. There is no point going further without it: the Pack-a-Punch requires linking the **teleporter**, and the teleporter needs the power. The general rules are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../systemes-bo1/).

## Getting to the stage

You start in the **Lobby** (the spawn room). Two routes lead to the stage:

- **Lower route (left)**: Lower Hall (fire trap, MPL wall weapon), then the Alley (AK74u, Double Tap), then the Back Room, then a staircase that goes down to the stage.
- **Upper route (right)**: Upper Hall (portrait room, PM63), then the Foyer (Speed Cola, MP40, Stakeout), then the Dressing Room (MP5K), then the stage.

Both routes are cross-checked by several sources. Fan sources recommend the **upper route** for high rounds, and find the Alley and the Back Room dangerous (zombies drop from the roof).

### The doors

- In the Lobby, two doors cost **750 points** each: one at the top right of the staircase, one on the left under the staircase.
- The third door, facing you under the staircase, **can't be bought**: it opens with the power.
- In the Lower Hall, a **steel door** costs **1,000 points**.

**To check:** these prices come from a single source (COD Zombified, a copy of the old wiki). The other doors on the map have no reliable price, so we don't give them.

## Turning on the power

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Go to the stage">
    Take one of the two routes above. The switch is at the **back of the stage**, near the curtains.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use the switch">
    Use the switch with the action button. The power turns on for the whole map.

The power also starts the **Nova 6 crawlers**: it's best to finish them with the knife, because they release a cloud of gas when a normal weapon kills them.

## Linking the teleporter

The teleporter **only goes to the projector room**, where the Pack-a-Punch is. You have to link it for every trip.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Touch the stage mainframe">
    On the stage, use the teleporter **mainframe**: the link starts.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/mainframe.webp'} alt="The teleporter mainframe on the stage" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish the link in the Lobby">
    Go to the Lobby and use the **teleporter pad** (the disc on the floor): the link is complete.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/teleporter_pad.webp'} alt="The teleporter pad in the Lobby" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go back to the stage and step into the teleporter">
    All players in the teleporter leave together. What comes next is in [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
    After a trip, the teleporter takes about **one minute** to recharge, and you have to **link it again** (mainframe, then the Lobby pad) every time.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
