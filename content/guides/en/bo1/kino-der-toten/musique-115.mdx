Three **meteorite fragments** (stones) are hidden on the map. Use all three, in **any order**, and the song "**115**" starts. It is sung by **Elena Siegman** (music by Kevin Sherwood). It only plays **once per game**. Above all you need to be able to cross the Lobby, the Dressing Room and the Alley's upper floor: so open the doors you need.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Lobby">
    In a **glass jar**, near the low barrier, "under" the staircase closest to **Quick Revive**. Hold the action button.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Dressing Room">
    On a **stand** near the mannequins, **behind the MP5K wall**, near a window. Hold the action button.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Alley upper floor (the room upstairs)">
    On a **shelf** with scattered black letters, next to a zombie window. Hold the action button.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'rock_alley.webp'} alt="The shelf with the black letters, on the Alley's upper floor" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>
