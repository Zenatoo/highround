Kino der Toten has **no main Easter Egg**: only small secrets. For the hidden song, see [Hidden song: 115](../musique-115/). The reels and the rocket require the **Pack-a-Punch**: see [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).

## The three film reels

Three reels are played in the projector room. Each one starts an audio recording and changes the image on the theater screen.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Go to the Pack-a-Punch">
    Link the teleporter and head to the projector room.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Look for the reel on the way back">
    On your way out, the teleporter drops you in **one of four rooms** at random, for a few seconds. In one of them, a **film reel** is lying around (on a table, a bed, a window ledge or a projector cart): pick it up.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put it in the projector">
    Back in the projector room, use the **projector** with the reel. The screen image changes and an **audio recording** starts. There are **three reels** in total: make more trips.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'projector.webp'} alt="The projector in the Pack-a-Punch room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

**To check:** the name and look of each room (dentist's office, conference room, Samantha's room, and its destroyed version) come from a single source, COD Zombified.

## The miniature rocket

In the **conference room** (the set that looks like the Pentagon), a small **rocket** on the table can take off through the ceiling.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power">
    Nothing works without power.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Jump 5 times in front of the left mannequin">
    In the **Dressing Room**, near the door of the Speed Cola room, there are two mannequins. Jump **5 times** in front of the one on the **left**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get to the conference room">
    Make trips to the Pack-a-Punch until the return drops you in the conference room.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use the rocket">
    Use the rocket on the table: it takes off.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'rocket.webp'} alt="The miniature rocket on the conference room table" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The radios

Two hidden radios each play a message from **Dr. Ludvig Maxis**.

- A radio on the **big chandelier** above the theater: shoot it.
- A radio on the **roof of the guard tower**, outside: you can see it from the Alley barrier, near Double Tap. You need a weapon with a scope.

**To check:** these two radios only come from one site (allthings.how); COD Zombified also mentions them.
