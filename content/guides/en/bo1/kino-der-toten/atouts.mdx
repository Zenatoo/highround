Kino der Toten has **five machines**: Quick Revive, Jugger-Nog, Speed Cola, Double Tap Root Beer and **Mule Kick** (added after release). The machines work with the **power** (see [Turn on the power and link the teleporter](../courant/)), except Quick Revive in solo. Stamin-Up, PhD Flopper and Deadshot Daiquiri are **not on this map**. You can keep 4 perks at a time. The general rules are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../systemes-bo1/).

| Perk | Price | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quick Revive** | 1,500 points (500 in solo) | Lobby, the spawn room |
| **Jugger-Nog** | 2,500 points | Theater, at the bottom of the stage, near the back seats |
| **Speed Cola** | 3,000 points | Foyer, between the two staircases, near the MP40 |
| **Double Tap Root Beer** | 2,000 points | Alley, behind the iron door, near the AK74u |
| **Mule Kick** | 4,000 points | Upper Hall, in the portrait room, near the PM63 |

## Quick Revive in solo

In solo, Quick Revive only costs **500 points** and revives you yourself, **up to 3 times**. You can buy it **before the power**.

## Mule Kick

Mule Kick gives you a **third weapon**: the next one you buy or take from the box goes into that third slot. You lose it with the perk. The machine did not exist at launch: a 2011 update added it to all maps (see [Black Ops Zombies basics](../systemes-bo1/)). Older videos and sites therefore don't show it on Kino.
