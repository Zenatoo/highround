Black Ops Zombies (2010) keeps the rules of World at War, with new weapons, new enemies and a series of maps that are easy to pick up at first. This guide explains **once and for all** the rules shared by the six maps (Kino der Toten, Five, Ascension, Call of the Dead, Shangri-La and Moon). The guide for each map links back here.

It describes the **original versions** of the game (PC, PS3, Xbox 360), after the latest updates. Many videos show the Black Ops III remakes (Zombies Chronicles), mods or the 2026 PS4/PS5 port instead: their rules may differ.

**To check:** most of the numbers in this guide come from the old IGN wiki and the hXcHector site, which have **the same author**: that counts as a single source. Numbers that have not been cross-checked are flagged.

## Players and start

- 1 to 4 players online, 2 in split screen.
- You start with **500 points** and an **M1911**.

{/* TO CHECK: 500 points and M1911 at start: hXcHector only. Players: Wikipedia. */}

## Points

You earn points by hitting and killing zombies, and you spend them on doors, wall weapons, the Mystery Box, Perk-a-Colas, traps and Pack-a-Punch.

- **+10** points for every bullet that hits.
- A kill gives **60** points for a body shot, **100** for a headshot, **130** with the knife, **50** with an explosive or a special weapon. These values go up a little with the round.
- Repairing a board on a barrier gives **10** points, but it stops after a few barriers.
- Traps give **no points** for their victims.

{/* TO CHECK: all point values: hXcHector (zombies page) only, "values rise with the round" is vague. Trap kills give no points: IGN Traps (same author). */}

    During rounds 1 to 3, shoot a zombie 4 or 5 times in the torso with the pistol, then finish it with the **knife**: that earns the most points.

## Base prices

- **Doors**: the cheapest cost **750** to **1,000 points**. Exact prices change from map to map: see each map's guide.
- **Mystery Box**: **950 points**.
- **Pack-a-Punch**: **5,000 points** per upgrade.
- **Traps**: 750 to 1,500 points per use, depending on the trap.

{/* TO CHECK: door "750 to 1,000" and trap price range: hXcHector/IGN only. Mystery Box 950: hXcHector + IGN (one source), no independent source reachable. Pack-a-Punch 5,000: IGN + hXcHector, CZ also advises 5,000 on Kino. */}

## Health

A zombie downs you in **2 hits**, a Hellhound in **3**. With **Jugger-Nog**, it takes many more. If you go down, your teammates have about **30 seconds** to revive you. If you die, you come back the next round.

{/* TO CHECK: Jugger-Nog = 8 hits per hXcHector/IGN (same author); a fan site says up to 5. Revive window 30 s: hXcHector only. Hellhounds 3 hits: IGN. */}

## The Mystery Box

For **950 points**, the box gives you a random weapon. A beam of light in the sky shows where it is. After a few pulls, a **teddy bear** appears: the box moves somewhere else and your price is refunded. On Nacht der Untoten, the only map where it never moves, it stays in place.

The base list is the same on all maps: Commando, FN FAL, Galil, FAMAS, AUG, G11, Spectre, HK21, RPK, SPAS-12, HS10, Dragunov, L96A1, Python, CZ75, dual CZ75 (dual wield), China Lake, M72 LAW, Crossbow and Ballistic Knife. Each map adds its own special weapons: see below.

## Wall weapons

Each map has weapons to buy off the wall. Buying ammo for a wall weapon costs half of its purchase price (see the Kino weapons in [The base game zombies achievements](../succes/)). The lists and prices for the other maps are in their guides. For a **Pack-a-Punch** weapon taken from the wall, the ammo refill costs **4,500 points**.

## Power-ups

| Power-up | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Max Ammo** | Refills the ammo of all your weapons (when downed, only the M1911 is refilled) |
| **Double Points** | Points doubled for 30 seconds |
| **Insta-Kill** | One hit kills anything for 30 seconds (not on George Romero) |
| **Nuke** | Kills all zombies and gives **400 points** to everyone |
| **Carpenter** | Repairs all barriers |
| **Fire Sale** | The box costs **10 points** at every location for 30 seconds (Kino der Toten and Five) |
| **Bonfire Sale** | Pack-a-Punch at **1,000 points** for 30 seconds (Five) |
| **Death Machine** | Infinite machine gun for 30 seconds, with no reload and no perk to buy (Five, Call of the Dead, Ascension) |
| **Perk-a-Cola** | A random perk (Ascension, Call of the Dead) |
| **Wunderwaffe** | The Wunderwaffe DG-2 for free (Call of the Dead) |

## The Perk-a-Colas

You can have **4 perks** at the same time. The machines only work once the **power** is on, except Quick Revive in solo.

| Perk | Price |
| --- | --- |
| **Quick Revive** | 1,500 points (500 in solo) |
| **Jugger-Nog** | 2,500 points |
| **Speed Cola** | 3,000 points |
| **Double Tap Root Beer** | 2,000 points |
| **Stamin-Up** | 2,000 points |
| **PhD Flopper** | 2,000 points |
| **Deadshot Daiquiri** | 1,500 points |
| **Mule Kick** | 4,000 points |

- **Quick Revive**: in solo, it revives you by itself, up to 3 times. You can buy it even without power.
- **Jugger-Nog**: a lot more health.
- **Mule Kick**: a third weapon.

## Pack-a-Punch

**5,000 points** per upgrade. The weapon keeps its name, but it changes and gets more powerful. Put the weapon in the machine, wait, take it back. You can't take a teammate's upgraded weapon, even if the game offers it. Access changes on each map:

| Map | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Kino der Toten | Teleporter to the projector room (30 seconds) |
| Five | Four DEFCON switches |
| Ascension | Three lunar landers, then the rocket launch |
| Call of the Dead | The machine appears on one of four rocks lit by the lighthouse |
| Shangri-La | Pressure plates (1 to 4 depending on the number of players), about 45 seconds |
| Moon | On Earth from the start, then on the base behind a closed room |

## Special grenades

- **Monkey Bomb** (Cymbal Monkey): 3 per purchase. Attracts zombies, then explodes. In the box on Kino, Five and Shangri-La.
- **Gersch Device**: a black hole that sucks in zombies. In the box on Ascension and Moon.
- **Matryoshka Dolls**: cluster explosions, with no decoy effect. In the box on Ascension and Call of the Dead.
- **Quantum Entanglement Device (QED)**: in the box on Moon.

Gersch Device and Matryoshka Dolls share the special grenade slot.

## Special enemies

- **Hellhounds** (dogs): Kino der Toten, Moon (and the older maps). One round every 5 to 7 rounds or so. Killing them all gives a **Max Ammo**.
- **Nova 6** (crawlers, "Creepy Crawlers"): Kino der Toten, Five, Moon. Crawlers that release gas when you kill them with a normal weapon.
- **Pentagon Thief**: Five.
- **Space monkeys**: Ascension. They attack the perk machines.
- **George A. Romero**: Call of the Dead. Electric hammer, calmed by ice-cold water.
- **Zombie monkeys**, **Shrieker** and **Napalm**: Shangri-La. The monkeys steal power-ups (500-point bonus), the Napalm gives 300 points and can down you even with Jugger-Nog.
- **Zombie astronauts**: Moon. They steal your first perk and ignore the Nuke and Insta-Kill.
