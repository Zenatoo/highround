On Kino der Toten, the **Pack-a-Punch** is in the **projector room**, above the theater. You get there through the **teleporter** on the stage, for only **30 seconds**. There is no quest: you just turn on the power and link the teleporter. Count **5,000 points** per upgrade. The general rules are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../systemes-bo1/).

## Before you leave

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power">
    The switch is on the stage. See [Turn on the power and link the teleporter](../courant/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Link the teleporter">
    Use the stage mainframe, then the Lobby pad. Same guide as above.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get 5,000 points ready">
    An upgrade costs **5,000 points**. Keep them before you leave: you only have 30 seconds.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The trip

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Step into the teleporter">
    On the stage, walk into the teleporter. **All players** inside leave together.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upgrade your weapon in 30 seconds">
    You arrive in the projector room. A **clock** above the machine shows the time: at 12 o'clock, you are sent back. Put your weapon in the machine, pay, then **take it back** before the end.

    **A weapon left in the machine when the time runs out is lost for good.**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Come back through one of the four rooms">
    The teleporter sends you back to one of **four rooms** at random, then to the Lobby. You only visit for a few seconds: this is where the film reels are hidden (see [The film reels, the rocket and the radios](../secrets/)).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## What to know

- The teleporter takes about **1 minute** to recharge, and you have to **link it again** (mainframe then Lobby pad) on every trip.
- The projector room is the **only Pack-a-Punch room** in Zombies that zombies can't enter: you are safe there, but only for 30 seconds.
- You can't take a teammate's upgraded weapon, even if the game offers it.
- Buying ammo for an upgraded weapon taken from the wall costs **4,500 points**.
- After the trip, the zombies that were in front of the theater run toward the **Lobby**: don't rush into the theater on foot.

**To check:** the refill price (4,500) only comes from IGN and hXcHector, which have the same author. "The zombies run toward the Lobby" only comes from COD Zombified.

{/* TO CHECK: 4,500 refill: IGN + hXcHector (launch value). Zombies run to the Lobby after a teleport: COD Zombified only. Zombies cannot enter the PaP room: COD Zombified + hXcHector. */}

In the Black Ops III remake, the room and the link are almost the same, but the weapons and the rules change: videos that show it don't apply to the original.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source="https://mmmrkennedyzombiesguides.pages.dev/games/BO1/kino_der_toten/kino_der_toten_guide">
    Never leave your weapon in the machine while waiting for the countdown to end: if the teleporter sends you back before you have taken it, it is lost.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
