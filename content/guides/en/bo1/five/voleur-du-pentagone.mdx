Instead of **Hellhound** rounds, "Five" has the **Pentagon Thief**. It comes out of a **teleporter**, steals **the weapon you are holding** and runs away. If you kill it **before it has stolen anything from you**, you get a **Bonfire Sale**: the Pack-a-Punch at 1,000 points. It's also what you need to do for the **Hands Off the Merchandise** achievement (see [The base game zombies achievements](../../kino-der-toten/succes/)).

It only appears **once the power is on**: see [Turn on the power](../courant/).

## How it behaves

- It comes out of a teleporter. Only **the player it targets** can see it. The others only see a **faint red cloud**.
- It is **faster than you**.
- When it catches you, it takes **the weapon you are holding** and teleports you both. Then it runs between the teleporters or chases another player.
- After several teleports, it disappears. **The stolen weapons are then lost for good.** A **Max Ammo** appears at the last teleporter it used.
- The lights turn blue or flicker before it arrives (according to two sources).

## Killing the Thief before it steals from you

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Keep a backup weapon in hand">
    It steals **the weapon in your hand**: hold a cheap pistol or a weapon you don't care about, and keep your best weapon **on your back**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get a hard-hitting weapon ready">
    The sources recommend the **Ray Gun**, the **Winter's Howl** (it slows the Thief), the **Mustang and Sally** (in solo, after a Quick Revive), **Claymores** placed near the elevator, grenades, and a light machine gun or a fast weapon aimed at the head. It has **a lot of health**, but it can be killed. See [Winter's Howl and the special weapons](../armes-speciales/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill it before it touches you">
    If it dies before it has stolen, it drops a **Max Ammo** and a **Bonfire Sale**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to the Pack-a-Punch">
    During the Bonfire Sale, **all teleporters lead to the Pack-a-Punch**, which only costs **1,000 points** instead of 5,000. See [DEFCON 5 and the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## If you already let it steal

You can still get everything back if you kill it **before it disappears**. You get your weapons back and it drops a **Max Ammo** and a **Fire Sale**. If you bought a new weapon in the meantime, it is lost when you get the old ones back.

  <Tip type="mistake" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-black-ops/Zombies">
    Don't enter a teleporter as a group: the zombies follow you through.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## The "Hands Off the Merchandise" achievement

**Hands Off the Merchandise**: kill the Pentagon Thief **before it steals your equipment**. This achievement only exists on "Five". The Thief only appears after the power, and at the end of a round. PowerPyx notes that it sometimes seems to never come.
