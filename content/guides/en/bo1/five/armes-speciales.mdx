"Five" has **no weapons to build** and no upgrade quest. The special weapons all come out of the **Mystery Box** (950 points, general rules in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/)). There is no Thundergun here: it is exclusive to Kino der Toten.

## Winter's Howl

The map's own weapon, along with **Verrückt**. It can only be found in the **Mystery Box**, on "Five" and on Verrückt.

- It fires **one shot at a time** and **freezes** zombies into blocks of ice, which then shatter.
- **Pack-a-Punched**, it becomes the **Winter's Fury**.
- Against the **Pentagon Thief**, it **slows** it down: very useful, see [The Pentagon Thief](../voleur-du-pentagone/).

**To check:** the ammo differs depending on the sources: **6 in the magazine and 24 in reserve** (9 and 36 once upgraded) according to IGN, but 6 and 30 (9 and 45) according to a forum from the launch era.

## Ray Gun

A **fully automatic** pistol (20 bullets in the magazine, 160 in reserve). It also comes from the Mystery Box. Once upgraded, it becomes the **Porter's X2 Ray Gun** (40 / 200). How it works is explained in [Ray Gun, Thundergun and Monkey Bomb](../../kino-der-toten/armes-speciales/).

{/* TO CHECK: Ray Gun ammo 20/160: hXcHector and NextGenUpdate agree; upgraded 40/200: NextGenUpdate only. */}

## Monkey Bomb

**Monkey Bombs** (Cymbal Monkey, 3 at a time) also come out of the Mystery Box. How they work is explained in [Ray Gun, Thundergun and Monkey Bomb](../../kino-der-toten/armes-speciales/).

## Mustang and Sally

In **solo**, when **Quick Revive** gets you back up, you come back with this special pistol. It is one of the recommended weapons against the Pentagon Thief. See [The Perk-a-Colas](../atouts/).
