"Five" has **5 Perk-a-Cola machines**: Quick Revive, Speed Cola, Jugger-Nog, Double Tap Root Beer and Mule Kick. There is no Stamin-Up, PhD Flopper or Deadshot Daiquiri. **The machines work once the power is on**: see [Turn on the power](../courant/). The general rules are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/).

## The machines

| Perk | Price | Effect | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Quick Revive** | 1,500 points (500 in solo) | You revive allies faster. In solo: you can revive yourself | Starting room, at the back, next to a red phone |
| **Speed Cola** | 3,000 points | Faster reloading | First floor, in the hallway near the elevator, behind the first door |
| **Jugger-Nog** | 2,500 points | More health | War Room, ground floor, under the catwalk |
| **Double Tap Root Beer** | 2,000 points | More firepower | War Room, near the elevator and the balcony |
| **Mule Kick** | 4,000 points | A third weapon | War Room, near Jugger-Nog |

## Mule Kick and the map version

Mule Kick was **not** in "Five" when the game came out (November 2010). An update added it to all the Black Ops maps. In the game's final state, the machine is in the War Room, near Jugger-Nog. Old guides don't mention it.

## Quick Revive in solo

In a solo game, Quick Revive costs **500 points** and can be bought **3 times at most**. When you go down, you get back up on your own. You then come back with the **Mustang and Sally** pistol: see [Winter's Howl and the special weapons](../armes-speciales/).
