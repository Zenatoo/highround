"Five" hides a song: "**Won't Back Down**", by **Eminem** with **P!nk**. To hear it, you have to use **three red phones** that ring and flash. The power must be on (see [Turn on the power](../courant/)), and the last phone is in the **Panic Room**: so you will need the Pack-a-Punch (see [DEFCON 5 and the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/)).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Starting room">
    On the **table to the left of the Quick Revive machine**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'phone_quick_revive.webp'} alt="The red phone on the table to the left of Quick Revive, in the starting room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Power room, in the Labs">
    On a **desk**, opposite the power switch.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'phone_power.webp'} alt="The red phone on the desk in the power room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Panic Room">
    On a **small table**, next to a barrier, near a window. This phone is in the Pack-a-Punch room: you first have to raise the DEFCON to 5, then enter a teleporter.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'phone_panic_room.webp'} alt="The red phone on the small table in the Panic Room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

When the three phones have been used, the song plays through the speakers. **It only plays once per game.**
