"Five" takes place in the **Pentagon**, in November 1963. You play **Kennedy, McNamara, Nixon or Castro**. The power is all the way down in the **Labs**: you have to go down floor by floor, opening doors and taking two elevators. The general rules (points, Mystery Box, power-ups) are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/).

This page covers the original Black Ops 1 version. The map's remake in Black Ops 4 ("Classified") works differently.

## The way to the power

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Stay in the starting room at first">
    Save your first points for the first rounds: the starting room has a few wall weapons and **Quick Revive**. Count about **4,000 points** of doors and elevators before reaching the power, depending on the sources.

    {/* TO CHECK: route cost: IGN "about $4,000"; NextGenUpdate (launch era, Nov 2010): 4 doors 3,750 + elevator rides about 250 each (PowerPyx also says an elevator ride costs 250). Single door prices (first door 750, bottom-floor rooms 1,250) rest on NextGenUpdate only: not published. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the doors and take the first elevator">
    Buy the doors that lead to the **first-floor elevator**, clear the rubble, then go down to the **War Room** (the second floor, the big pentagon-shaped room). **Riding an elevator costs points** (about 250 each according to two sources).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the second elevator">
    In the War Room, clear the way again to the **second elevator**, which goes down to the **Labs**, the bottom level.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the power room">
    From the Labs elevator, the power room is **the second room on the left**. Look for the **big "3"** painted on the wall. The switch is inside.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the switch">
    The power turns on.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## What the power turns on

- The **perks**: see [The Perk-a-Colas](../atouts/).
- The **teleporters**, single-player pads. They send you to a **random** spot on the map.
- The **two electric traps**, built into the metal detectors in the hallways next to the starting room. Each one needs a part to find before it works.
- The **Pentagon Thief**, which only appears once the power is on: see [The Pentagon Thief](../voleur-du-pentagone/).
- The path to the Pack-a-Punch: see [DEFCON 5 and the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-black-ops/Zombies">
    Don't enter a teleporter as a group: the zombies follow you through. One player at a time.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Solo or with others

Everything can be done solo. In that case, **Quick Revive** only costs 500 points and can be bought 3 times. See [The Perk-a-Colas](../atouts/).

**Unlocking the map.** If "Five" doesn't appear in the menu, finish the campaign, or use the main menu terminal with the code `3ARC UNLOCK`.
