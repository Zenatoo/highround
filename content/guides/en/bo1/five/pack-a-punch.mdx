On "Five", the **Pack-a-Punch** is in a hidden room: the **Panic Room**. To get in, you have to raise the **DEFCON** level to 5 in the War Room, then step into a teleporter. You then have **a short window** to upgrade your weapon. The power must be on: see [Turn on the power](../courant/).

The Pack-a-Punch costs **5,000 points**, and **4,500 points** to refill a weapon that is already upgraded. The general mechanics are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/).

## Raising the DEFCON

In the **War Room** (the second floor), there are **DEFCON switches** on the walls. The level starts at **1**, and each switch adds **1**. Activate **all four** to reach **DEFCON 5**.

## Getting into the Panic Room

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Reach DEFCON 5">
    Once the fourth switch is activated, **all teleporters** send you to the **Panic Room**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step into a teleporter">
    Take any teleporter on the map, one player at a time. According to one site, a portal with the Pack-a-Punch image also appears in the middle of the War Room.

    {/* TO CHECK: PaP portal in the middle of the War Room: Cod Zombie Guides only. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upgrade your weapon in the window of about 30 seconds">
    In the Panic Room, the back wall opens and the machine appears. The room's doors stay closed and **no zombies spawn for about 30 seconds**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'machine.webp'} alt="The Pack-a-Punch machine in the Panic Room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get out before it closes">
    When the doors open, **the Pack-a-Punch stops working** and zombies can come in. Once the **last player** is out, the doors lock: you have to **redo the four switches** to come back.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
    During a **Bonfire Sale**, all teleporters lead straight to the Pack-a-Punch and it only costs **1,000 points**: see [The Pentagon Thief](../voleur-du-pentagone/).
  </Tip>
</Tips>
