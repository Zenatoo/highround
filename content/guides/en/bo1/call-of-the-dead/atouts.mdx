Call of the Dead has **7 Perk-a-Colas** from the start, three of which are new compared to the first maps: **PhD Flopper**, **Stamin-Up** and **Deadshot Daiquiri**. All the machines need the **power** (see [Turn on the power](../courant/)), except Quick Revive in solo, which you can buy without power. You can have **4 perks** at the same time. The general rules are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/).

## The machines

| Perk | Price | Effect | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Quick Revive** | 500 points (solo) / 1,500 points (co-op) | Revive a teammate faster, or revive yourself in solo | Starting area |
| **PhD Flopper** | 2,000 points | No more explosion or fall damage; explosion when you dive from a height | In the house near the lighthouse |
| **Stamin-Up** | 2,000 points | Run faster and longer | Behind the lighthouse |
| **Deadshot Daiquiri** | 1,500 points | More accurate aim, no sway with the sniper rifle, aim assist | At the very top of the lighthouse |
| **Jugger-Nog** | 2,500 points | More health | At the back of the ship, at the bottom |
| **Double Tap Root Beer** | 2,000 points | Doubled fire rate | At the front of the ship |
| **Speed Cola** | 3,000 points | Faster reload, faster barrier repair | In the ice tunnel, near the lighthouse |

## Mule Kick

According to IGN, an update added **Mule Kick** (a third weapon, lost with the perk) to **all** Black Ops maps. On Call of the Dead, the machine would be at the **back of the ship**, for **4,000 points**. PC patch 1.15 from October 2011 also says that Mule Kick is available on all Zombies maps.

{/* TO CHECK: Mule Kick on Call of the Dead: IGN Mule_Kick page (place back of the ship, 4,000) + MP1st PC patch 1.15 (all maps, no place or price). HX and MK (launch-era / 2026 guides) do not list it. */}

**To check:** the location and price of Mule Kick on this map are only given by IGN. The guides for the map do not mention it.

## George's free perks

When you beat **George Romero**, he drops a power-up that gives you a random perk. See [George Romero](../george-romero/).
