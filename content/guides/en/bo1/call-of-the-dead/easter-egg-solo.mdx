In this Easter Egg, you help the **old team** (Dempsey, Nikolai, Takeo and Richtofen), locked in a room at the foot of the lighthouse, reach another place. They talk to you through a **metal door**. You need a **fuse**, some explosives, the ship's wheel and a **V-R11**. At the end, you earn the **Stand-In** achievement and a **Wunderwaffe DG-2**.

**Before you start:**

- Count **25 to 45 minutes**. It is an easy quest (3 out of 10 according to mmmrkennedy).
- Doable in **solo or co-op**. In co-op, there is a longer version: [Co-op Easter Egg: Ensemble Cast](../easter-egg-coop/).
- Bring **Semtex grenades** or frag grenades for the generators, and the **V-R11** from the Mystery Box (see [V-R11, Scavenger and Wunderwaffe DG-2](../armes-speciales/)).
- The dialogues pause between steps: wait for them to finish.

## The steps

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power">
    See [Turn on the power](../courant/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Knife the metal door">
    Go to the room **under PhD Flopper**, at the bottom of the lighthouse, and knife the **metal door**. You hear the old team: they say they need a fuse.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'metal_door.webp'} alt="The metal door at the foot of the lighthouse" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the fuse and put it in the electrical box">
    The **fuse** is in the PhD Flopper room, upstairs. It spawns in **one of three places** at random:

    - on the **desk**, to the left of the PhD Flopper machine;
    - to the left of the **locker**, opposite the machine;
    - on the **table with the car batteries**.

    Put it in the **electrical box to the right of the metal door**. More dialogue follows.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'fuse_desk.webp'} alt="The fuse on the desk" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'fuse_locker.webp'} alt="The fuse to the left of the locker" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'fuse_table.webp'} alt="The fuse on the table with the batteries" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'electrical_box.webp'} alt="The electrical box to the right of the door" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Destroy the 4 generators">
    About **20 seconds** after the fuse, **four generators with red lights** turn on. Destroy them with **explosives**: Semtex is recommended (sold at the bow of the ship, above Jugger-Nog). A frag grenade works too. Each light goes out when its generator is destroyed.

    1. **Lighthouse**: opposite the metal door.
    2. **Back of the ship**: in the water, outside the map, to the right of the MPL wall weapon.
    3. **Shortcut between the ship and the starting area.**
    4. **Behind the lighthouse**: through the zombie spawn window, to the left of Stamin-Up.

    When all four lights are out, **knife the door again**.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the wheel and the levers">
    Go up to the ship's **control room** (the power room). **Turn the wheel twice to the right**: the brown peg must be at the bottom right ("5 o'clock" position). Then, on the panel to the **right of the wheel**, pull:

    - the leftmost lever, **once**;
    - the rightmost lever, **three times**.

    If it is right, a **foghorn** sounds and a **submarine** surfaces in the distance, near Jugger-Nog. In solo, you do not use it afterwards. A green beam goes through the lighthouse.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'wheel_post.webp'} alt="The wheel turned two notches" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'levers_post.webp'} alt="The levers pulled" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn a zombie with the V-R11">
    Take the **V-R11** from the Mystery Box. On the **ground floor of the lighthouse**, shoot a zombie: it turns back into a **human**, runs into the **beam in the middle of the stairs** and floats upward. **Keep shooting it until it falls to the ground** before it reaches the top. If you miss, start again right away with another zombie.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'beam_lighthouse_stairs.webp'} alt="The beam in the middle of the lighthouse stairs" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'downed_human.webp'} alt="The downed human in the beam" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get the Golden Rod">
    Once the downed human reaches the top, the **Golden Rod** (also called the **Vril Device**) appears and comes down to the bottom. Pick it up and put it in the **tube to the left of the metal door**.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'golden_rod.webp'} alt="The Golden Rod at the foot of the lighthouse" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'tube.webp'} alt="The tube to the left of the metal door" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish the Easter Egg">
    Wait for the dialogue to end: the **fuse box** next to the door starts sparking. **Knife it**. A **Wunderwaffe DG-2** drops as a power-up.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Rewards

- The **Stand-In** achievement (see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/)).
- A **Wunderwaffe DG-2** as a power-up. Afterwards, George Romero drops one instead of the Death Machine: see [George Romero](../george-romero/).
