Call of the Dead has **two special weapons in the Mystery Box**, the **V-R11** and the **Scavenger**, and a third one you cannot buy, the **Wunderwaffe DG-2**. The Ray Gun and the regular Mystery Box weapons are explained in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/). Videos of the Black Ops III version of the map show weapons that do not always work like they do here.

## V-R11

A semi-automatic pistol that fires green energy, like the Ray Gun. It exists **only in the Mystery Box**.

- **3 bullets in the magazine, 9 in reserve.**
- One shot **turns a zombie into a human**. The human runs away and the zombies chase it, like with a Monkey Bomb.
- A second shot on the human makes it **explode**. This is the **Stuntman** achievement.
- It is used for the Easter Egg: see [Solo Easter Egg: Stand-In](../easter-egg-solo/).
- On **George Romero**, one shot calms him for a few seconds. See [George Romero](../george-romero/).

Upgraded at the Pack-a-Punch: **V-R11 Lazarus**, **6 bullets in the magazine, 18 in reserve**.

## Scavenger

A bolt-action sniper rifle that fires **explosive bullets**. They stick to a surface and explode after a short delay, like the crossbow bolt. It exists **only in the Mystery Box**.

- **3 bullets in the magazine, 15 in reserve.**
- **Shooting on Location** achievement: kill 10 zombies with a single shot, from more than 100 feet (about 30 meters). Wait for a higher round, when the zombies are more bunched up.

Upgraded at the Pack-a-Punch: **Hyena Infra-Dead**, **6 bullets in the magazine, 30 in reserve**, with an infrared scope.

## Wunderwaffe DG-2

You cannot buy it or draw it from the box. It comes as a **power-up** (a lightning bolt icon, similar to the Death Machine one) when you finish the map's Easter Egg.

- There is **only one** and any player can pick it up. If nobody takes it, it disappears like a normal power-up.
- It has **3 bullets in the magazine and 15 in reserve**.
- Once the Easter Egg is done, **George Romero drops a Wunderwaffe DG-2 instead of the Death Machine** when you beat him.

To get it: [Solo Easter Egg: Stand-In](../easter-egg-solo/) or [Co-op Easter Egg: Ensemble Cast](../easter-egg-coop/).
