Call of the Dead takes place on a **shipwreck** near a **lighthouse**, on a frozen island in Siberia. You do not play the usual team but four actors: Sarah Michelle Gellar, Robert Englund, Danny Trejo and Michael Rooker. The general rules of the game (points, Mystery Box, power-ups) are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/).

The power needs **only one switch**. It is at the very top of the ship.

## What the power turns on

- The **Perk-a-Colas**: see [The Perk-a-Colas](../atouts/).
- The **lighthouse**, which marks where the Pack-a-Punch appears: see [Find the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
- The **catapult**, one floor below the switch.

## Getting around

The map has **two ziplines** and a **catapult**. The start and arrival areas must be open to use them. Zombies and George Romero can use them too.

- One zipline goes from the **top of the lighthouse** to the **back of the ship**.
- Another goes from the **top of the ship** to the **starting area**. Jump toward the rope to grab it.
- The **catapult** sends players (and zombies) from the ship to the lighthouse. It needs the power.

## The areas to know

- **Start**: a pool of frozen water, with Quick Revive.
- **Ship**: cut in two halves linked by a rail. Jugger-Nog is at the back, at the bottom, and Double Tap at the front.
- **Lighthouse**: Deadshot is at the very top. Next to it, the **PhD Flopper** house also holds the metal door for the Easter Egg.
- **Behind the lighthouse**: the Stamin-Up house.
- **Ice tunnel**: a slide leads to Speed Cola.

{/* TO CHECK: no buyable door, no trap and no special round is described by any opened source (HX, MK, IGN 2014, Kenshin9977 chapter "Pas de manches spéciales"). Not stated as fact in the text. */}

The guides consulted mention **no door to buy** and no trap. There are no Hellhounds or special monsters: only zombies and **George Romero** (see [George Romero](../george-romero/)).

## The frozen water

Do not stay in the frozen water: after about **20 seconds**, your screen and your character freeze and you cannot move until someone touches you. Zombies freeze too.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="hXcHector" source="https://www.hxchector.com/call-of-the-dead">
    Once frozen, you are an easy target for the zombies and for George. Get out of the water before the 20 seconds are up.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## The Mystery Box

The Mystery Box moves around: a blue beam in the sky shows where it is, and it is easier to see when the weather is dark. Two spots are mentioned: under the control room, inside the ship, and in front of the lighthouse, near the stairs of the PhD Flopper house.

It contains two weapons specific to the map, the **V-R11** and the **Scavenger**: see [V-R11, Scavenger and Wunderwaffe DG-2](../armes-speciales/).
