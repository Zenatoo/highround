Call of the Dead (Escalation pack) has **5 achievements** (trophies on PlayStation). The names are the game's English ones. Check them off as you go: your progress stays on your device.

{/* TO CHECK: no French names found for the 4 DLC packs. Values (Xbox Gamerscore) from HX/IGN; PowerUp Gaming (PS5 list, 10 Jul 2026) lists the same five names. */}

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Stand-In (35G, bronze)">
    Finish the Easter Egg in solo or co-op: "send the team to Heaven". See [Solo Easter Egg: Stand-In](../easter-egg-solo/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ensemble Cast (45G, silver)">
    Finish the Easter Egg **in co-op** (at least 2 players). See [Co-op Easter Egg: Ensemble Cast](../easter-egg-coop/). It also unlocks Stand-In.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Stuntman (20G, bronze)">
    Make a zombie explode with the **V-R11**: a first shot turns it into a human, a second makes it explode. See [V-R11, Scavenger and Wunderwaffe DG-2](../armes-speciales/).

    {/* TO CHECK: HX's achievement text says the V-R11 must be Pack-a-Punched, while its weapon text and MK describe the second shot on the human with the normal gun. */}

    **To check:** one guide says you need a Pack-a-Punched V-R11. The same guide describes the second shot on the human elsewhere without mentioning an upgrade. If the explosion does not happen, upgrade the weapon.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shooting on Location (20G, bronze)">
    Kill **10 zombies with a single Scavenger shot**, from more than **100 feet** (about 30 meters). Wait for a higher round, when the zombies are bunched up. See [V-R11, Scavenger and Wunderwaffe DG-2](../armes-speciales/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Quiet on the Set (30G, bronze)">
    "Cut the director's lights": **beat George Romero**. According to hXcHector, do it **in the frozen water**: he sinks and the achievement unlocks. See [George Romero](../george-romero/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Ensemble Cast is the only one that requires co-op. The base game achievements are in [The base game zombies achievements](../../kino-der-toten/succes/).
