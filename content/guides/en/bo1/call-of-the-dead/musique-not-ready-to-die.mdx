Three **red meteorites** are hidden on the map. Use all three: the song **"Not Ready to Die"** by **Avenged Sevenfold** starts playing. Use each meteorite with the action button (X on Xbox 360, Square on PS3, F on PC). You need to have turned on the power to reach some areas: see [Turn on the power](../courant/).

## The story radios

Five radios tell the story of the map. Use them with the action button. They are (according to hXcHector):

- next to **Stamin-Up**;
- at the top of the **lighthouse**;
- under the metal walkways, near **PM63** and the frozen water;
- near **Double Tap**;
- on a rock in the frozen water, after the slide to **Speed Cola**.
