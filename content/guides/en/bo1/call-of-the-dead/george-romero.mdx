**George A. Romero**, the zombie movie director, is the **boss** of Call of the Dead: he has become a giant zombie. He comes out of the **pool of water** in the starting area right at the start of the game and follows you. There is no other special monster on the map.

He has two states: **calm** and **angry**.

## Calm

- He slowly follows the nearest player. Calm, he moves at about the speed of a crawling zombie.
- Now and then, he screams. The zombies around him then become **sprinters**.

## Angry

He gets angry as soon as he takes damage or hits a player with his projector. A screen shake and a scream announce it. He then runs toward you.

- He hits the ground with his projector: the electricity **electrifies nearby zombies**. They run fast and **shock** you if they touch you.
- A few hits from his hammer put you down. His electricity and screams slow you down, which leaves you vulnerable to the zombies.

## Calming him for good

Lead him into **any body of water**: he calms down and stays calm. Shooting him in the water does not make him angry again. Out of the water, a V-R11 shot only calms him for a few seconds.

## Beating him

He is **very tough**. His health depends on the number of players: according to mmmrkennedy, **250,000 per player**, so 1,000,000 with four. Use all your upgraded weapons.

**To check:** the figure of 250,000 per player comes from a single guide. The others only say that his health increases with the number of players.

- **Insta-Kill and Nuke** have no effect on him.
- A glowing halo around his projector is the only health gauge: it flashes when he takes damage and turns **orange** when he is almost beaten.
- For the **Quiet on the Set** achievement, shoot him with everything you have **in the frozen water**: he sinks under the water and the achievement unlocks.

## What he drops

When he is beaten, he goes back into the water and drops:

- a **Death Machine** (a machine gun with infinite ammo, 30 seconds);
- a **Perk-a-Cola power-up**: a random perk.

If you have already finished the Easter Egg in a game, he drops a **Wunderwaffe DG-2** instead of the Death Machine: see [V-R11, Scavenger and Wunderwaffe DG-2](../armes-speciales/).

He **never dies for good**: he comes back **two rounds** later. Videos show that you can win several free perks this way ("7 perks" or "all perks", depending on the title).

## Making him flee without beating him

Shoot him **two or three times** with an **upgraded V-R11 (Lazarus)**, preferably in the frozen water of the main area. He leaves, either for the rest of the round or for one or two rounds. He drops **nothing** and you do not get the achievement.
