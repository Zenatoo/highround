**Ensemble Cast** is the **co-op** version of the Easter Egg. It repeats the steps of [Solo Easter Egg: Stand-In](../easter-egg-solo/) and adds four: the **vodka bottle**, the **radios**, the **foghorns** and the **lighthouse dials**. It unlocks the **Ensemble Cast** achievement (and **Stand-In** too) and gives a **Wunderwaffe DG-2**.

**Before you start:**

- You need **at least 2 players** (up to 4).
- Read the solo version first: the shared steps are detailed there with screenshots.
- The V-R11 comes from the Mystery Box: see [V-R11, Scavenger and Wunderwaffe DG-2](../armes-speciales/).

The guides do not all give the same **order**. The only sure constraint: the wheel and levers come **before** the foghorns. Here is mmmrkennedy's order.

## The steps

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power, hit the door, insert the fuse, destroy the generators">
    Same as solo: [Solo Easter Egg: Stand-In](../easter-egg-solo/), from the "Turn on the power" step to "Destroy the 4 generators". Then hit the metal door again.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Catch the vodka bottle">
    A frozen **vodka bottle** hangs from a **rail**. One player stands **right underneath**, another **knifes the ice** to make it fall. It can be in four places:

    - the stair rail in front of the PhD Flopper house;
    - the rail just above it, near the Mystery Box spot;
    - on the ship, the rail at the top right of the MPL wall weapon;
    - on the ship, the rail that links the two halves.

    If nobody catches it, it breaks and reappears somewhere else. Put it in the **tube to the left of the metal door**, then hit the door for new dialogue.

    {/* TO CHECK: catch: MK says no need to press anything, just stand under it; HX says the catcher presses the use button. HX lists three spots (not the one by the Mystery Box). */}

    **To check:** mmmrkennedy says you just need to stand under the bottle; hXcHector says to press the action button to catch it. Try without pressing, then with.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the 4 radios in order">
    Use the radios **in this order**:

    1. **Ship**: on a computer, in front of the Mystery Box spot under the control room.
    2. **Stamin-Up house**: on a barrel, to the right of a zombie spawn window.
    3. **Bow of the ship**: in a shipping crate, at the bottom of the lighthouse zipline.
    4. **Metal door room**: on a cabinet under the stairs, to the right of a spawn window.

    If you get the order wrong, nothing happens until **four radios** have been used in total: they then all crackle and stop responding for a few seconds. Then start again from the first one.

  <Step title="Activate the 4 foghorns in order">
    All players can use them, with no time limit. Order:

    1. At the foot of the lighthouse, next to the pool.
    2. After the slide to Speed Cola, in the right corner, in the water.
    3. At the foot of the lighthouse, left side, next to the building.
    4. After the slide to Speed Cola, behind the big rock on the island.

    If you get it wrong, nothing happens until the **fourth** press: the submarine **dives** and only comes back at the end of the round. **Stop pressing while it dives**: those presses count toward the next attempt and ruin it. It comes back up after the round ends: then start again from the first horn.

  <Step title="Set the 4 lighthouse dials to 2-7-4-6">
    Each of the four floors of the lighthouse has a **colored dial**. Each press adds **1** to the dial **and to its neighbors** (the one above and the one below). From top to bottom, you need:

    - **Yellow: 2**
    - **Orange: 7**
    - **Blue: 4**
    - **Purple: 6**

    mmmrkennedy's method:

    1. Turn the **purple** until the **purple** is right.
    2. Turn the **orange** until the **blue** is right.
    3. Turn the **yellow** until the **orange** is right.
    4. Turn the **yellow** until the **yellow** is right, **also turning the purple the same number of times**.
    5. Turn the **blue** until the **blue** is right.

    mmmrkennedy also offers a small calculator: [Call of the Dead: dial tool](https://mmmrkennedyzombiesguides.pages.dev/games/BO1/call_of_the_dead/call_of_the_dead_dial_solver).

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="Kenshin9977" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8hSR_j1rxp4">
        Another method: set the yellow to 2, turn the blue until the orange is at 7, set the purple to 6, turn the orange until the blue is at 4, then set the yellow back to 2.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>

  </Step>

  <Step title="V-R11, Golden Rod and finish">
    Same as solo: turn a zombie with the **V-R11** at the foot of the lighthouse, shoot down the **human** in the beam before it reaches the top, pick up the **Golden Rod**, put it in the **tube to the left of the door**, wait for the dialogue, then **knife the fuse box** that is sparking. A **Wunderwaffe DG-2** drops.

  </Step>
</Checklist>

## What can go wrong

- The bottle falls and breaks.
- Radios in the wrong order.
- A foghorn pressed while the submarine is diving.
- The V-R11 human is not shot down before it reaches the top.
- The fuse is not always in the same place.
