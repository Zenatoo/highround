Here, the **Pack-a-Punch** has no fixed place: it **appears on a rock in the water** and leaves after a while. The **lighthouse** shows you where it will appear. You must turn on the power first: see [Turn on the power](../courant/). The price and general workings are in [Black Ops Zombies basics](../../kino-der-toten/systemes-bo1/).

## How it works

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power">
    When the power is on, the lighthouse activates.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Watch for the lightning">
    Every **1 min 40 to 2 min**, you see a flash of lightning and the lighthouse spins very fast. After about **15 seconds**, it lights up a spot.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to the lit spot">
    The Pack-a-Punch appears there. It stays for about **2 minutes**, then it leaves for 1 min 40 to 2 min. **It cannot leave while someone is using it.**
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The three spots

Two sources mention **three** spots. An older source mentions four rocks: see below.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Behind the lighthouse">
    In the water, between the lighthouse and the Stamin-Up machine.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap_behind_lighthouse.webp'} alt="The Pack-a-Punch behind the lighthouse" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

hXcHector mentions **four rocks** and IGN (same author, updated later) **three**. We follow three. If the lighthouse lights up another spot, let us know.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-black-ops/Call_of_the_Dead">
    A thunderclap announces that the lighthouse is about to light up a rock. Upgrade your weapon before the machine turns back into a rock.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
    The Pack-a-Punch does not leave while a player is using it: if you are upgrading a weapon, you are safe.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
