Forsaken has all **10 perks** of Cold War, each with its own machine. The power is on from the start: each machine works as soon as you reach its area. Only **PhD Slider** comes late, since it's at the end of the route, in the Observation Tower.

The price doesn't depend on the perk but on **how many you already have**: 2,500 Essence for the first one, then 500 more each time. Perk upgrades (tiers I to V) are unlocked between games with Aetherium crystals. It's all explained in [Cold War Zombies basics](../../die-maschine/systemes-cold-war/).

## The machines

In the order you come across them on the way to Pack-a-Punch:

| Perk | Effect | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Death Perception** | You see enemies through walls | Staging Area, in the top right corner as you leave the spawn |
| **Jugger-Nog** | More health | Checkpoint, on the ground floor, on your left as you enter, next to the stairs |
| **Tombstone Soda** | When downed, you become a shadow and can revive yourself | Main Street, at the end of the street, against the building on the right |
| **Quick Revive** | Faster health regen and revives | Storage Zone 5, on the ground floor |
| **Stamin-Up** | You run faster | Storage Zone 10, on the Beach Pizza side (left door on Main Street) |
| **Mule Kick** | A third primary weapon | Video Store (the Arcade building), Anytown West |
| **Speed Cola** | Faster reload | Anytown Rooftops: through the rooftop passage from the Video Store upstairs, or up the Bar stairs |
| **Elemental Pop** | Each bullet can trigger a random ammo mod | Burger Town, Anytown East |
| **Deadshot Daiquiri** | Your aim snaps to critical spots | Bar, Anytown East |
| **PhD Slider** | Sliding into enemies makes them explode | Observation Tower, in the lockdown button room |

## PhD Slider

It's the **tenth and last perk** of Cold War, added with Forsaken (Season Six). Sliding into enemies causes an explosion, bigger if the slide is long. Its tiers:

- **I**: no environmental damage while sliding.
- **II**: longer slide.
- **III**: you no longer take damage from your own explosives.
- **IV**: faster slide.
- **V**: when you fall from a great height, the landing makes an explosion.

Tier V is used for [Music Easter Egg: Samantha's Ballad](../musique-samanthas-ballad/). Any tier is enough to turn on the Bar's racing machine, see [The arcade and its mini-games](../salle-d-arcade/).

<Tips>

    With PhD Slider at tier V and **Stamin-Up at tier II** (more fall damage), jump off roofs and walkways into the horde: the landing explosion deals a lot of damage.

## Der Wunderfizz

The **Der Wunderfizz** machine sells every perk. It's in the **Board Room**, the bunker office: from the Bunker Entrance, go past the reception, take the corridor, then the double door on the left. This passage only opens **after the lockdown**.

During the main quest, a second Der Wunderfizz appears in front of the boss door, at the end of the escort. See [Main Easter Egg (Pyrrhic Victory)](../easter-egg-principal/).

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="Call of Duty" source="https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23733292/surviving-forsakena-guide-to-the-final-chapter-of-call-of-duty-black-ops-cold-war-zombies">
    If you lose your perks after going down, stop by the Board Room to buy them back in one go, without running around the whole map again.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
