The **Chrysalax** is Forsaken's special weapon, forged with Dark Aether crystals. It has two forms, and you switch between them whenever you want:

- **Chrysalax Savager**: an axe. Each hit kills several zombies, and it can throw a spinning energy blade.
- **Chrysalax Storm**: when you move the grip forward, it becomes a rapid-fire weapon. Its shots can turn zombies into Aetherium crystals that explode.

You need it for the [Main Easter Egg (Pyrrhic Victory)](../easter-egg-principal/).

## Ways to get it

- **The build quest** below: the only sure method.
- The **Mystery Box**, with some luck.
- The **Trials** in the Observation Tower, as a Legendary reward. According to one guide, it's a common reward on this map.
- The big chest from the **Der Eisendrache** machine in the Arcade, with no guarantee. See [The arcade and its mini-games](../salle-d-arcade/).

## The build quest

You need three crystals, **in any order you like**, then you put them together at the Particle Accelerator.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Lift the lockdown and press the tower button">
    First unlock Pack-a-Punch: see [Lift the lockdown and unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).

    In the Observation Tower, go into the room across from the lockdown console. To the right of a slanted window with metal shutters, there is a button **with no on-screen prompt**: hold the action button on it. A cutscene starts. This is also the start of the main quest.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'button.webp'} alt="The button next to the window with metal shutters, in the Observation Tower" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tempered Crystal Heart: burn a Tormentor">
    Kill a **Tormentor** with fire: it drops the Tempered Crystal Heart. The safest way is the **Flamethrower**, in the Support tab of the crafting table, for **100 High-Grade Salvage**. The Ring of Fire may also work.

    {/* TO CHECK: Flamethrower price: tracker.gg "100 High-Grade Salvage", COD Zombified "100 credits". Ring of Fire "may also work" = mmmrkennedy. */}

    <Screenshot src={c + 'heart.webp'} alt="The Tempered Crystal Heart dropped by a Tormentor" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Energetic Geode: make an Abomination shoot a small crystal">
    **Small purple** Dark Aether **crystals** are scattered all over the map. Wait for an **Abomination** to spawn, then stand right next to one of these crystals: its ranged electric attack has to **hit the crystal**. A Tormentor exploding on it works too. The crystal becomes the Energetic Geode.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={c + 'dark_aether_crystal.webp'} alt="A small purple Dark Aether crystal" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={c + 'geode.webp'} alt="The Energetic Geode ready to be picked up" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Polymorphic Crystal Core: defend the Jim's Donuts crystal">
    **Two rounds** after the tower button, an announcement talks about an anomaly and an orange object falls from the sky onto **Jim's Donuts**, the Main Street shop next to the rappel down.

    Go inside and stand on the **orange crystal** behind the counter. Stay in its small circle and kill the waves of enemies: **5 Plaguehounds**, then **3 Tempests**, then **2 Mimics**, along with normal zombies. Once they're all dead, pick up the crystal.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={c + 'jims_donuts.webp'} alt="The Jim's Donuts shop on Main Street" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={c + 'jimsdonuts_crystal.webp'} alt="The orange crystal behind the Jim's Donuts counter" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the Chrysalax together at the Particle Accelerator">
    Go to the **Particle Accelerator**, in the bunker. Behind the big central cylinder, there is a **pool of purple liquid**. Drop the three crystals in it, then get **about 10 melee kills** right next to it. The Chrysalax comes out of the pool: take it.

    {/* Melee kills: 10 (mmmrkennedy, tracker.gg) vs "10 to 15" (COD Zombified). */}

    </Shots>
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Upgrading it

The Chrysalax goes through **Pack-a-Punch** like a normal weapon, no quest needed.
