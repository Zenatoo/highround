This guide tells you **where** to find each piece of equipment on Forsaken. How they work (crafting table prices, Arsenal rarities, Mystery Box, Trials, exfil) is the same on every map: it's explained in [Cold War Zombies basics](../../die-maschine/systemes-cold-war/).

## Crafting tables

The crafting table makes grenades, equipment and scorestreaks with **Salvage** (the materials enemies drop).

- **Staging Area** (starting area)
- **Main Street**
- **Anytown Rooftops**, next to Speed Cola
- **The Amplifier / Particle Accelerator**, in the bunker
- During the main quest, one last crafting table appears in front of the boss door.

Forsaken came with two new support items, available on every map: the **Hand Cannon**, a big cannon that works very well against special and elite enemies, and the **ARC-XD**, a small explosive remote-controlled car that fits through tight spots.

## Arsenals

The Arsenal upgrades your weapon's rarity and sells or repairs armor. There are four:

- **Staging Area**
- **Main Street**
- **Bar**, upstairs (Anytown East)
- **The Amplifier / Particle Accelerator**

A fifth one appears in front of the boss door during the main quest.

## Mystery Box

It costs **950 Essence** and moves after a few uses. On Forsaken, it can give you the **Chrysalax**, the map's special weapon (see [The Chrysalax](../chrysalax/)).

Known spots:

- Staging Area
- Storage Zone 5, on the roofs
- Storage Zone 10, on the roofs
- Anytown West, in the street
- Anytown East, in the street
- The Amplifier, in the bunker
- Observation Tower, in the lockdown button room

## Trials

The **Trials** computer is in the **Observation Tower**, reachable once the lockdown is lifted. Chain the challenges without claiming the reward to push it up to Legendary. On Forsaken, the Chrysalax often comes out as a Legendary reward.

{/* "Very common Legendary reward" = COD Zombified only. Trials computer in the tower: official guide + COD Zombified. */}

## The Suspended Hind trap

A helicopter hangs above Anytown. For **2,000 Essence**, it fires at the zombies around it for a long time. There are **four switches** to start it:

- On the **Bar's top balcony**.
- In the **Anytown West** street, at the foot of the stairs coming down from the Video Store roof.
- In the **Anytown East** street.
- On the **Anytown Rooftops**.

{/* Price and "four switches" + Bar balcony: official guide. The 3 other spots: COD Zombified layouts only (Anytown West switch next to the pistol blueprint planter, per its pistol post). */}

## Teleporters and ziplines

Forsaken is a teleporter map. A **map on the wall**, near the teleporter on the Video Store roof, shows where each one leads.

| From | To | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Staging Area | Checkpoint | From the start (after a 30-second charge) |
| Main Street Bridge (Storage Zone 5 and 10 roofs) | Video Store roof (Anytown West) | From the start |
| Fuel Processing (Anytown East) | Bunker Entrance | Once repaired with 4 parts |
| The Amplifier (bunker) | Observation Tower | After you kill the Abomination |
| Observation Tower | Staging Area | After the lockdown |

After the lockdown, **ziplines** also leave from the Observation Tower (to the Burger Town roof and over Main Street), and the Main Street one goes back up to the Checkpoint. The whole route is detailed in [Lift the lockdown and unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).

## Rampage Inducer

In the Staging Area, an orange canister lets you **vote to speed up** zombie spawns and speed until round 20. Only useful if you want to climb rounds fast.

## Arcade

The Arcade tokens and machines (Anytown West) are detailed in [The arcade and its mini-games](../salle-d-arcade/).

## Exfil

Like on the other maps, **exfil** can be started from round 10, then every 5 rounds. Then follow the marker to the helicopter landing zone and clear it of zombies.
