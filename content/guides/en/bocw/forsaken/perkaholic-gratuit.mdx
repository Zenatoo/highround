The **Perkaholic** gives you **every perk at once**, for free. You need a videotape, a memory game with TVs and a ghost to hunt.

These perks aren't permanent: you lose them as usual if you go down.

{/* TO CHECK: every step comes from mmmrkennedy and tracker.gg, which word the quest the same way (one source family). No independent text guide found; the Flapix, Mallow and I1-Smokey- videos confirm the quest exists (titles only). */}

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Press the hidden button in the Observation Tower">
    Lift the lockdown, then press the button with no prompt next to the window with metal shutters. See [Main Easter Egg (Pyrrhic Victory)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Knock down the Video Store tape">
    In the **Video Store**, on the ground floor, throw an **explosive** (grenade, Semtex…) to the left of a shelf. A **VHS tape** falls from behind it. Go **prone** to pick it up.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={p + 'video_store.webp'} alt="The Video Store entrance" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={p + 'vhs_location.webp'} alt="The shelf where you throw the explosive" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={p + 'vhs_on_floor.webp'} alt="The VHS tape on the floor" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Play the TV Store memory game">
    In the **TV Store**, put the tape in the **VCR**. Four TVs turn on: **blue, green, red and orange**. They flash in a sequence: shoot them **in the same order**. There are three rounds:

    1. **4** flashes.
    2. **8** flashes.
    3. **12** flashes.

    If you get all three right, only one TV stays on, in **green**.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={p + 'vhs_player.webp'} alt="The TV Store VCR" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={p + 'vhs_four_tvs.webp'} alt="The four colored TVs" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={p + 'single_tv.webp'} alt="The TV that stayed green" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wake the ghost">
    Buy **Tombstone Soda** (Main Street). Then use the green TV: it turns red. A **spectral entity** appears: it follows you slowly, sometimes scrambles your screen and tries to kill you if it gets too close.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hunt it in Tombstone shadow form">
    Go to **The Amplifier**, in the bunker, and go down. Choose the **Tombstone shadow** (the mode that lets you revive yourself). The entity floats above the **Particle Accelerator**: shoot it. It splits into several copies: shoot the one with **glowing eyes**. Keep going until it disappears.

    {/* Who goes down: mmmrkennedy says down yourself in The Amplifier; tracker.gg says any player can be downed by an enemy, and the player in Tombstone shadow gets the perks. */}

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={p + 'the_amplifier.webp'} alt="The Amplifier, in the bunker" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={p + 'entity.webp'} alt="The entity above the Particle Accelerator" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Revive yourself">
    Once the entity is gone, revive yourself: you get **every perk**.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
