A dancing zombie, **Ronald Raygun**, is locked in the back room of **Beach Pizza**, on Main Street. Deliver his four pizzas in time: after each delivery, he gives you a random reward. It's **often a Ray Gun**, but not every time.

**You need Aether Shroud at tier III**, the one that lets you teleport a short distance. If your loadout doesn't have it, you can switch loadouts mid-game from the pause menu.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get into the Beach Pizza back room">
    Open Beach Pizza (the left door on Main Street). Charge your Aether Shroud, stand in front of the **wooden door to the left of the counter** and activate it: you teleport to the other side.

    Ronald Raygun is dancing next to a **stack of pizza boxes**. **Don't kill him**, or the quest fails.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={r + 'door.webp'} alt="The wooden door to the left of the Beach Pizza counter" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={r + 'stack_of_boxes.webp'} alt="Ronald Raygun and the stack of pizza boxes" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deliver a pizza in under 45 seconds">
    Take a box from the stack: a timer starts and the screen shows the customer. Put the pizza down next to the mannequins before the timer runs out. The four addresses, in any order you like:

    - **Video Store**, ground floor: the boxes near a mannequin, in the corner on your right as you enter.
    - **Cinema**: the bench with seated mannequins, on your left in the lobby as you come in from Anytown West.
    - **Bar**, ground floor: the booth with mannequins, to the right of the bar.
    - **Burger Town**: the booth with seated mannequins, to the right of the counter.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="COD Zombified" source="https://codzombified.blogspot.com/2021/11/forsaken-free-ray-gun-in-cold-war.html">
        A single missed delivery is enough to lose the quest until your next game.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Collect your reward">
    Go back to Beach Pizza and interact with **Ronald** himself (not the stack of boxes): he gives you a random reward (Ray Gun, another weapon, points, Salvage, ammo, armor…).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Do it again for all four pizzas">
    There are **four orders** in total, so four chances to get the Ray Gun.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
