Forsaken's music Easter Egg is **"Samantha's Ballad"**, a new version of the famous "Samantha's Lullaby". To hear it, you have to find a Dark Aether bunny and hand it over to **Bubby**, the Burger Town mascot. As a bonus, you get a big loot chest.

**You need PhD Slider at tier V.** This tier makes your landing explode when you fall from high up. Tiers are unlocked between games with Aetherium crystals: see [Cold War Zombies basics](../../die-maschine/systemes-cold-war/). The PhD Slider machine is in the Observation Tower: see [Perks and Der Wunderfizz](../atouts/).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Buy PhD Slider">
    First lift the lockdown to reach the Observation Tower: see [Lift the lockdown and unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).

  <Step title="Land on the scorch marks">
    Three **scorch marks** are on the ground in Anytown. Jump off a roof so that **the landing explosion** hits the mark. Each time, it drops a random item (ammo, bomb, points…). Keep going until an **Aether Bunny**, a little rabbit, comes out. Grab it fast before it runs off.

    **Bubby** is the big mascot in front of **Burger Town**, in Anytown East. Open the panel on **its back** and put the Aether Bunny inside.

  <Step title="Defend Bubby for 2 minutes">
    The area locks down and enemies come: zombies, **Tormentors** and **Krasny Soldats**. They go after Bubby: its health bar must not hit zero before the end. Bubby sometimes spits out **cheeseburgers**: pick them up, they heal you.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the chest and take the cassette">
    A **big chest** appears in front of Bubby. It holds loot and a **cassette tape**: pick it up and the music starts.
