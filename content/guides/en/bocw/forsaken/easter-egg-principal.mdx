Forsaken is the last Cold War map and the end of the first chapter of the **Dark Aether** story. The **Requiem** team teleports into the heart of a secret **Omega Group** site, a fake American town in Ukraine. They are looking for Sergeant **Kazimir Zykov**, the only one who knows how to defeat **the Forsaken**, a Dark Aether creature that Colonel Kravchenko wants as an ally.

The goal of the quest: build the **Aetherium Neutralizer**, escort it to the Forsaken and defeat it with help from Samantha Maxis.

**Before you start:**

- Count on **20 to 40 minutes** solo.
- There is **no achievement or trophy**: Cold War didn't add any for the maps released after launch. The reward is the **"Pyrrhic Victory" Dark Ops challenge** (XP and a calling card) and the ending cutscene. Like any completed main quest, it also gives Aetherium crystals: as many as the last exfil available at that point.
- Doable solo or in co-op.
- You need the **Chrysalax**. Start its quest early and work on the other steps at the same time.
- The three Neutralizer parts can be done **in any order you like**.
- Starting the Neutralizer is a **point of no return**: get ready before.

## Start the quest

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Lift the lockdown">
    Follow the route to the Observation Tower and press the red button. It's all in [Lift the lockdown and unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Press the hidden button in the tower">
    In the Observation Tower, go into the room across from the lockdown console. To the right of a slanted window with **metal shutters**, a button shows **no prompt**: hold the action button on it. A cutscene shows the Forsaken arriving.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'button.webp'} alt="The button next to the window with metal shutters" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get the Chrysalax">
    You need it to break the crystals later on. The full quest is in [The Chrysalax](../chrysalax/). While you do it, work on the Fuel Tank, the Monitoring Device and the Housing Unit.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The Neutralizer parts

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Fuel Tank: fill the Fuel Processing tanks">
    At **Fuel Processing** (Anytown East), there are **four tanks** with a control panel. Solo, activate **one**. In co-op, each player stands at a tank and you activate them **at the same time**.

    {/* Tank count: COD Zombified (4 tanks, solo 1, each player one at the same time), tracker.gg ("four"), GamesRadar and Charlie Intel (one per player). mmmrkennedy: "depends on player count". */}

    The area locks down and zombies come: kill them near the tanks to fill them with a red liquid. Once they're full, the **Fuel Tank** drops in the middle. If you fail, try again the next round.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'processing_tanks.webp'} alt="The Fuel Processing tanks" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'fuel_tank.webp'} alt="The Fuel Tank in the middle of the tanks" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Monitoring Device: the Grand Prix machine and the ARC-XD">
    1. Pack-a-Punch a weapon with the **Dead Wire** ammo mod (electric, 2,000 Essence).
    2. Get an **Arcade Token**: knife the token changer in the Arcade, it's free once per game.
    3. In the **Arcade** (Video Store building), electrocute a zombie right next to the **Grand Prix** machine. Its energy turns the machine on.
    4. Use the machine (**2,000 Essence**): you drive a small car, the **ARC-XD**.
    5. Drive it to the **TV Store**, across the street, behind the counter. Its pulses knock out a loose **vent grate**: go inside.
    6. Stop between the shelf and the wall, to the left of the shelves, and blow up the car.
    7. Walk into the TV Store: the wall is broken open. Pick up the **Monitoring Device** on the shelf.

    {/* TO CHECK: Arcade Token: mmmrkennedy and tracker.gg say it is needed; COD Zombified, Charlie Intel, GamesRadar, Gameranx, Dexerto only mention the 2,000 price. */}

    Depending on the guide, the machine asks for a token on top of the 2,000 Essence, or only the 2,000: grab the free token to be safe. See [The arcade and its mini-games](../salle-d-arcade/).

  </Step>

  <Step title="Housing Unit: make an Abomination charge in the Board Room">
    The **Housing Unit** is stuck high up in a corner of the **Board Room**, the Der Wunderfizz room in the bunker. Wait for an **Abomination** and lure it here. Stand against the wall, under the item, and **dodge at the last moment** when it charges: it has to hit the wall, not you. The item falls. If it doesn't work, try again.

## The 3 Catalyzed Crystal Shards

**Two rounds** after the tower button, three **big purple crystals** fall on the map. You need the Chrysalax.

- **Staging Area**, in the middle.
- **Storage Zone 5**, on the roofs.
- **The Amplifier**, behind the Mystery Box.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Break a big crystal">
    Three orbs spin around it. Shoot them until they're all destroyed: they speed up with each orb you hit. The Chrysalax's gun form is the handiest. Then hit the crystal with the **axe**.

    It drops a **Crystal Shard**: pick it up, it replaces your tactical grenade. An **Abomination** spawns close by.

    <Shots>

      <Screenshot src={m + 'crystal_shard.webp'} alt="The Crystal Shard to pick up" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make the Abomination swallow the shard">
    Throw the Crystal Shard **into its open mouth**. If you miss, go back to the crystal: another shard is waiting for you. Once it has swallowed it, its tail changes color. Kill it and pick up the **Catalyzed Crystal Shard**.

## The Neutralizer and the escort

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get ready">
    Buy your perks, armor and scorestreaks and upgrade your weapons **before** what comes next. During the escort and the boss, you won't be able to roam the map or earn Essence anymore.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build the Aetherium Neutralizer">
    At the end of **Main Street**, under the bridge, near Tombstone, use the crafting table once you have the Fuel Tank, the Monitoring Device, the Housing Unit and the three Catalyzed Crystal Shards: it builds the Neutralizer.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'workbench.webp'} alt="The Neutralizer crafting table at the end of Main Street" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Escort the Neutralizer">
    Activate it: it rises, clears the debris behind the crafting table and moves down the street. Stay inside its **protective bubble**.

    Every so often, it says it's running out of fuel. Then break an **orange** Dark Aether **crystal** with the Chrysalax (some are up high, on ladders and fire escapes), pick up the shard and give it to the Neutralizer.

    Since the October 13, 2021 update, these crystals respawn: you can no longer get stuck without fuel.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'orange_dark_aether_crystal.webp'} alt="An orange Dark Aether crystal during the escort" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'orangeshard_escort_alt.webp'} alt="Another orange crystal along the route" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Last preparation in front of the Containment Door">
    The escort stops in front of a big door, the **Containment Door**. Then these appear: a **Pack-a-Punch**, an **Arsenal**, a **crafting table**, a **Der Wunderfizz** and an ammo crate. This is your **last chance** to gear up: you can't come back.

    When you're ready, use the door: the fight begins.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The fight against the Forsaken

The fight takes place in **Town Square**. The Forsaken stays in place, and **only two laser cannons** can hurt it.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Charge the cannons with Samantha">
    **Samantha Maxis** floats above a **purple circle**. Kill zombies **inside this circle**: the "Maxis Essence" meter (on the left of the screen) goes up. When it's full, Sam goes to charge one of the two cannons, then moves somewhere else.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Break a piece of armor, then fire the cannon">
    The Forsaken has **four pieces of armor** to break, one by one:

    1. A **shoulder**, whichever you want.
    2. The **other shoulder**.
    3. The **lower belly**.
    4. The **head**.

    {/* TO CHECK: shoulder order: free (mmmrkennedy) vs left then right (tracker.gg); COD Zombified describes breaking both shoulder pads before the cannon shot. */}

    Shoot the armor with your weapons until it breaks: a weak spot appears. Get on a **charged cannon** and shoot it. Each good shot takes off about **a quarter of its health**. If your shot doesn't deal enough damage, its health goes back up: recharge a cannon with Sam and try again.

    At half health, you are pulled into the void for a moment with a vision of Sam, then the fight resumes with new attacks.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Dodge its attacks">
    - **Lightning Slam**: it raises an arm and slams the ground, with an electric shockwave. Right under the hand, you go down in one hit. It stops once both its arms are broken.
    - **Blizzard**: two cold zones that slow you down.
    - **Energy Ball**: energy balls that follow you.
    - **Laser Eyes**: once both arms are broken, a laser comes out of its eyes and follows a player.

    {/* TO CHECK: mmmrkennedy only: breaking the right arm first gives Blizzard, the left arm first gives Energy Ball, and both keep coming once both arms are broken. Gameranx and The Nerd Stash only mention an electric homing ball and an ice storm. */}

    According to one guide, the first arm you break decides the attack: the right one gives Blizzard, the left one Energy Ball.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish the fight">
    After the shot to the head, a scene plays out: the Forsaken drains every player's health until everyone is down. That's normal. You can go down earlier (explosives, zombies) to skip some dialogue. The ending cutscenes follow.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="The Nerd Stash" source="https://thenerdstash.com/black-ops-cold-war-how-to-complete-the-forsaken-easter-egg-guide/">
    Save **Kazimir** and **Semtex** grenades for the boss, and a powerful scorestreak like the **Death Machine** or the **War Machine**.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## The end of the story

The Forsaken is defeated, but at a price: **Samantha sacrifices herself** and locks herself in the Dark Aether with it. The final cutscene also reveals that the mysterious **Director of Requiem** is **Edward Richtofen**. This is the end of the Cold War story; the Dark Aether story continues in Vanguard, then in the following games.
