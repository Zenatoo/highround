On Forsaken, there's no Aether Tool like on the previous maps. Instead, three **shooting challenges** upgrade your weapon's **rarity** for free: one for **pistols**, one for **sniper rifles** and one for **SMGs**.

It always works the same way:

1. Hold a weapon of the right class.
2. Pick up the **blueprint** for that class: targets appear.
3. Hit **every target** before they disappear. During the challenge, zombies ignore you.
4. If you succeed, the weapon in your hands goes up one rarity level right away. A sound tells you if you fail.

Each challenge can only be done **once per game**: if you fail, you'll have to wait for the next one.

    There's an SMG wall buy on Main Street. Open **Beach Pizza**: the blueprint then appears **on cinder blocks**, to the right of the door. The targets move through three areas, one after the other:

    1. **Beach Pizza**: 6 targets.
    2. **Storage Zone 10**, on the ground floor: 5 targets.
    3. **Storage Zone 10**, on the roofs: 5 targets.

    Listen carefully: a sound tells you when the targets arrive and when they move to the next area.
