On Forsaken, **the power is already on** from the start: perks and Arsenals work right away. But a **security lockdown** blocks some doors, ziplines and machines. To lift it, you cross the map from teleporter to teleporter up to the **Observation Tower** and press a button. Pack-a-Punch is right next to it.

Don't worry about the route: an objective marker on screen guides you at every step.

## The route to the Observation Tower

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Activate the Staging Area teleporter">
    You spawn outside, in the **Staging Area**, a fenced-in pen. Here, **rounds follow each other with no break** as long as nobody has taken the first teleporter: the zombies get stronger fast.

    Go straight to the teleporter and activate it. You have to hold out for **about 30 seconds** while it charges, then step inside. Even if you run, you usually get there around round 3.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="Call of Duty" source="https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/23733292/surviving-forsakena-guide-to-the-final-chapter-of-call-of-duty-black-ops-cold-war-zombies">
        You can stay a bit in the Staging Area to earn Essence before you leave, but leave as soon as the horde gets too strong.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Checkpoint: go down to Main Street">
    You arrive at the **Checkpoint**. Jugger-Nog is on the ground floor. Go upstairs and buy the door that blocks the rappel down: **500 Essence** (750 according to one source). Go down to **Main Street**.

    {/* TO CHECK: Checkpoint gate price: 500 (Charlie Intel power guide, Gameranx) vs 750 (Turtle Beach). */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Main Street: climb up to the bridge teleporter">
    The next teleporter is on the **Main Street Bridge**, a walkway between the roofs of the two warehouses. Two routes lead there:

    - On your right as you arrive: the **Storage Zone 5** door (Quick Revive), then the zigzag stairs up to the roofs.
    - On your left: the **Beach Pizza** side door, which leads to **Storage Zone 10** (Stamin-Up), then the roofs.

    Take the teleporter: it sends you to the roof of the **Video Store**, in Anytown West.

    {/* TO CHECK: prices from a single source each: Storage Zone 5 door 1,000 and a 1,000 fence before the teleporter (Turtle Beach; Gameranx says the teleporter is already open); Beach Pizza side door 750 (COD Zombified). */}

    According to the sources, the Storage Zone 5 door costs **1,000**, the Beach Pizza one **750**, and a 1,000 fence may still block the way to the teleporter.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Anytown West: open the three shops">
    On the roof, look at the **map on the wall**: it shows where each teleporter leads. Go down to the street. Three doors open here: **Video Store and Arcade**, **Cinema** and **TV Store**. You will need to open all three to find the parts for the next step.

    {/* TO CHECK: Turtle Beach's PaP guide says 1,250 each; Turtle Beach's perk page and a GameSpot search snippet give 1,500 for the Video Store. */}

    They cost **1,250** each according to one source, the Video Store maybe 1,500.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the 4 teleporter parts">
    Follow the marker to **Fuel Processing**, in Anytown East (go through the Cinema or the TV Store). The teleporter is broken: Major Carver asks you to find **4 parts**. Each one has several possible spots, and an icon on the minimap shows the area.

    | Part | Where | Possible spots |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Circuit Board** | Video Store, ground floor, the storeroom near the stairs | Under the stairs, on the shelf next to the stairs, on the crates opposite |
    | **Focusing Lens** | Cinema | In the hands of a seated mannequin, near the candy display on the counter, on a bench near the posters |
    | **Cathode-Ray Tube** | TV Store | On the shelf to the right of the wall buy (between two TVs), on the small metal cart to the left of the counter, on the shelves behind the counter |
    | **Fryer Cage** | Burger Town | Under the American flag, to the left of the soda machine behind the counter, in the counter display case |

    {/* Spots: COD Zombified (3 per part) and tracker.gg (up to 4 per part) describe the same places in different words. Burger Town door price: not found in any readable source. */}

    Burger Town also has a door to open (price not confirmed).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Repair the Fuel Processing teleporter">
    With the 4 parts, go back to the teleporter and **hold the action button** until the bar fills up. Step inside: you arrive at the **Bunker Entrance**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bunker: open the blue door and kill the Abomination">
    Go straight ahead to the big **blue door** (1,500 according to one source) that leads to **The Amplifier** and the **Particle Accelerator**. Cross over to the teleporter at the back.

    {/* TO CHECK: blue door 1,500 = Turtle Beach only (Charlie Intel only says "buy the blue door"). */}

    When you get close, it turns red and an **Abomination** comes out: a big three-headed enemy. It is **invincible for a few seconds** at first, so don't waste your bullets. Then aim for its **glowing mouths**. Once it's dead, take the teleporter.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="Turtle Beach" source="https://eu.turtlebeach.com/blog/black-ops-cold-war-zombies-forsaken-unlock-pack-a-punch-guide-ps4-xbox-one-series-x-pc-ps5">
        It soaks up a lot of damage but it's slow. A path on the right makes a loop: run around it while you shoot.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Lift the lockdown in the Observation Tower">
    Follow the corridor to the console with the **big red button** and press it. The lockdown is lifted. **Pack-a-Punch** is right across from the button.

    Don't stay too long in the tower: the **radiation** makes you lose health after a while. In exchange, no zombies spawn here.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## After the lockdown

- Pack-a-Punch costs **5,000**, **15,000** then **30,000 Essence** per tier, and an ammo mod (like Dead Wire) **2,000**. How it all works is explained in [Cold War Zombies basics](../../die-maschine/systemes-cold-war/).
- The Observation Tower also has **PhD Slider**, the **Trials** computer and a marksman rifle wall buy.
- New routes open up: a teleporter from the tower to the Staging Area, ziplines from the tower (to the Burger Town roof and over Main Street), the Main Street zipline to the Checkpoint, and the bunker corridor to the **Board Room** and Der Wunderfizz. See [Crafting tables, Mystery Box, Arsenal, traps and exfil](../equipements/).
- In the room across, another button starts the main quest. See [Main Easter Egg (Pyrrhic Victory)](../easter-egg-principal/).
- During the main quest, a **second Pack-a-Punch** appears in front of the boss door.
