The **Arcade** is in the **Video Store** building, in Anytown West. Its machines are playable: each one sends you into a mini-game, and you win a loot chest or Essence. You need one **Arcade Token** per game on a machine.

## Tokens

The **token changer** is in the Arcade, next to the door to the Video Store storeroom (the one with the stairs).

- **Free**: knife the changer. It drops a token, **once per game**.
- **Bought**: **10,000 Essence** per token at the changer. That's expensive.
- **Random**: a zombie you kill can drop a token.

<Screenshot src={m + 'arcadetoken_buy.webp'} alt="The Arcade token changer" credit={by} source={src} />

## The three machines

| Machine | The mini-game | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **World at War** | You are teleported into **Nacht der Untoten**, the very first Zombies map (2008), with set weapons. Survive a few waves. | A **big chest** of loot at the end |
| **Der Eisendrache** | An arena that looks like the Black Ops III map. Stay in the **ring of fire** and kill the zombies coming out of the portals to feed the **dragon**, before time runs out. | A **big chest** (often gold): perks, Legendary weapons, Salvage, sometimes the **Chrysalax** |
| **Enduro** | You drive an **ARC-XD** on a course of moving obstacles and pick up Essence. | The **Essence** you picked up |

## The hidden machines

### Grand Prix (Arcade)

A fourth Arcade machine, **Grand Prix**, is used for the main quest: it turns on when you electrocute a zombie next to it with the Dead Wire ammo mod, then has you drive an ARC-XD to the TV Store. It's all in [Main Easter Egg (Pyrrhic Victory)](../easter-egg-principal/).

### Anytown Grand Prix (Bar)

A hidden ARC-XD race **upstairs in the Bar**, in Anytown East.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the machine with PhD Slider">
    Buy **PhD Slider** (Observation Tower, any tier). Go upstairs in the Bar: the machine is under a **dusty tarp**, to the right of the wall buy. Kill a zombie right next to it with a **slide explosion**: its soul goes into the machine and turns it on.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={g + 'bar.webp'} alt="The Bar in Anytown East" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={g + 'grandprix_arcade_machine_bar.webp'} alt="The Anytown Grand Prix machine upstairs in the Bar" credit={by} source={src} />

  </Step>

  <Step title="Put in one token per player">
    **Every player** in the game puts an Arcade Token into the machine, then starts the race.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Do the 3 laps">
    Drive the ARC-XD for **3 laps**, following the **purple arrows** on the ground. There are a few shortcuts. You can pick up Essence along the way. The reward depends on your time:

    | Time | Reward |
    | --- | --- |
    | Under 1:40 | Legendary: 5,000 points |
    | From 1:40 to 2:10 | Epic: 4,000 points |
    | Over 2:10 | Rare: 3,000 points |
