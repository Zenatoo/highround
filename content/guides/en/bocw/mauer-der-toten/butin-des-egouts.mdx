A secret added with Season Five, in August 2021. Treyarch slipped it into the map without telling anyone: the player SASTAC posted his find on video on August 18, 2021. Throw explosives into a sewer hole and it spits loot back out, including a free **LT53 Kazimir**.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Find the sewer hole">
    In the **East Berlin Streets**, look for an open manhole, surrounded by barbed wire, next to a military truck with its headlights on.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'hole.webp'} alt="The open manhole in the East Berlin Streets" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Throw explosives into it">
    Throw grenades into the hole: frag grenades, stun grenades, or shots from explosive weapons. A **scream** comes up from the hole when it works. Keep going until the loot comes out. According to one source, it takes about 13 explosives.

    {/* TO CHECK: "13 explosive equipment" = codzombieguides only; the others say "until loot comes out". */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the loot">
    The hole spits out Salvage, armor, and sometimes a **perk can**. The **LT53 Kazimir** is in the batch every time.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

You can do it again **up to three times** per game. Do it at the end of a round, with a single zombie alive: the hole is in the middle of the street.
