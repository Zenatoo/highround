Dr. **Aleksandra Valentina** is the final boss of the [Main Easter Egg (Tin Man Heart)](../easter-egg-principal/). The fight starts **as soon as you put the second Cleansed Rock** on the warhead in the lab: gear up before that.

## Get ready

- Every useful perk, especially Jugger-Nog, Quick Revive and Stamin-Up.
- Pack-a-Punched, high-rarity weapons, and an upgraded **CRBR-S**.
- Full armor, **Self-Revives** and a **Death Machine** scorestreak, made at the crafting table.

There is no time limit: take your time.

## Her attacks

| Attack | The sign | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Fire** | Three orange crystals appear above her head and shoot at you | Run in a zigzag or break her line of sight. You can't dodge them by walking |
| **Wipe** | A blue aura around the edges of the screen, which goes blurry and shakes | Get into cover **right away**, out of her sight. Otherwise you go down in one hit, even with full armor |
| **Heal** | Zombies float around her | Kill them fast: she heals with them |

## How it goes

1. She has a **blue bar** (shield) and a **red bar** (health). Break the shield, then shoot her. If you stop hitting her, the shield comes back.
2. After losing part of her health, she leaves the lab. She changes **arenas four times**: **West Berlin Street**, the roof of the **Destroyed Penthouse** and the **East Berlin Streets**, before coming back to the **lab** for the end.
3. Between two arenas, you have a short moment to restock (ammo, armor, perks). If you take too long, she teleports you to the new arena.

  </Tip>

## The end

When her health hits zero in the lab, interact with her: a cutscene plays. All that's left is to **protect Klaus** while he carries the warhead to the portal. When he goes in, the game ends on the ending cutscene.
