The power comes back on in the **Power Room**, under the streets. The first try fails: you first have to replace two blown **fuses**, carried by two **Tempests**. The game shows you the way with purple arrows on the walls and markers on screen.

Without power, the perks, the Arsenal, the Pack-a-Punch and the rappel lines don't work. Expect round 5 or 6 if you don't spend on anything else.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Take one of the two starting ziplines">
    Both paths cost the same and lead to the same place, the Korber Rooftop:

    - The **rooftop** zipline (Apartment Rooftop) leads to the **Destroyed Penthouse**, with Quick Revive. A good pick in solo.
    - The zipline in **apartment 503**, on the floor below, leads to the hotel's **Room 301**.

    Then open the Destroyed Penthouse door or the Hotel Hallway door to reach the **Korber Rooftop**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go down to the street through the Garment Factory">
    Under the Korber Rooftop, open the door to the **Garment Factory** workshop. The stairs lead out into the **Alley**, then into the **East Berlin Streets**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill the first Tempest in the street">
    A Tempest spawns in the East Berlin Streets, often in or in front of the Electronics Store, sometimes over by the Bar. Kill it and pick up the **Electrical Fuse** it drops.

    A Tempest can also spawn earlier, at random: it drops a fuse too.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go underground">
    Two entrances, same price:

    - the **manhole** near the Alley, to the **Service Passage** (Deadshot Daiquiri);
    - the **subway entrance**, next to the Military Tent, to the **Ghost Station**.

    In the Ghost Station, cross the tracks **without getting run over** by the ghost trains: a bell rings before they come through.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reach the Power Room">
    On the other side of the tracks, two paid paths:

    - to the north, the **Switch Control Room** then the **Maintenance Tunnel**, and the Power Room door;
    - to the south, the **Sewer Access**, and its door to the Power Room.

    Pull the switch in the middle of the Power Room: it blows and ejects the old fuses.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill the second Tempest">
    The second Tempest is underground, usually in the **Sewer Access** (sometimes in the Switch Control Room). Follow the marker, kill it and take the fuse.

    If you came in from the north, the door between the Power Room and the Sewer Access still costs Essence. To save money, go back through the tracks instead of opening every door.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put in the fuses and turn it back on">
    Put both fuses in the box next to the switch, then pull it again. The power comes back on across the whole map.

    Elemental Pop and a crafting table are in the same room.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The doors

**To check:** the solo prices are confirmed by two sources, but the co-op prices come from only one. They may be wrong.

| Door | Solo | Co-op |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rooftop or apartment 503 zipline | 500 | 750 |
| Destroyed Penthouse or Hotel Hallway, to the Korber Rooftop | 750 | 1,000 |
| Garment Factory, to the stairs and the street | 1,000 | 1,250 |
| Manhole or subway entrance | 1,250 | 1,500 |
| Ghost Station, to the Switch Control Room or the Sewer Access | 1,500 | 1,750 |
| Maintenance Tunnel or Sewer Access, to the Power Room | 1,500 | 1,750 |

Total to the Power Room: **6,500 Essence in solo**, **8,000 in co-op**.

{/* Solo prices: GameSpot (solo column) and COD Zombified's annotated layouts give the same figures. TO CHECK: co-op prices and the fact that solo is cheaper come from GameSpot only. The official guide says "at minimum 5,000 Essence" to reach the Power Room, which matches neither total. */}

## After the power

- All the **rappel lines** work. The ones in the **Safe House** and the **Sewer Access** go up to **West Berlin Street**, from where you reach Checkpoint Charlie. See [Unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
- The perks and the Arsenal work. See [Perks and Der Wunderfizz](../atouts/) and [Crafting tables, Mystery Box, Arsenal and exfil](../equipements/).

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="DizzyOP" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ATQKSdW0dKI">
    To get the power fast, save your points during the first rounds by killing with your knife, then open the doors all in one go.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="Turtle Beach" source="https://www.turtlebeach.com/blog/black-ops-cold-war-zombies-mauer-der-toten-tips-and-tricks-guide-ps4-xbox-one-series-x-pc-ps5">
    Try to kill both Tempests around round 4: later on, with your starting weapon, they get hard.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
