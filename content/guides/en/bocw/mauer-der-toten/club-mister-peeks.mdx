**Mister Peeks**, the Mystery Box's stuffed bunny, has been torn into **six pieces**. Pick them all up: you get teleported into his **nightclub**, a dance floor full of zombies. Survive three waves and pick a door to win a random reward. It's the equivalent of Die Maschine's Coffin Dance: see [The Coffin Dance and its rewards](../../die-maschine/coffin-dance/).

One of the pieces is in the Department Store, after the power. According to GameSpot, you'd better have a Pack-a-Punched weapon before starting the waves, especially in solo.

## The nightclub

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Survive three waves">
    The last piece teleports every player into the nightclub. The zombies dance, then attack when the music starts again. There are **three waves**, each harder than the last, with Manglers and Mimics.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a door">
    Mister Peeks invites you on stage, in front of **three doors**. You don't have much time: pick fast, open the door and take what's inside. You are then sent back to the East Berlin Streets.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

The rewards are **random**: a golden chest with a perk and items, a perk such as Jugger-Nog, Salvage, and sometimes the **CRBR-S**. If you were too slow during the waves, you only leave with power-ups such as a Max Ammo.

## Going back

You can go back to the nightclub **every 15 rounds**. Go to the East Berlin Streets and look at the **Hasenbau** building: Mister Peeks is sitting on the ledge of a lit window. Shoot him to get teleported.

<Screenshot src={m + 'bunny_window.webp'} alt="Mister Peeks on the Hasenbau window ledge" credit={by} source={src} />
