**Mauer der Toten** ("the wall of the dead") is the third round-based map of Cold War. It came out on **July 15, 2021**, with the Season Four Reloaded update, free for all players.

The story: on February 2, 1985, Berlin is overrun by zombies after a breach into the Dark Aether. Colonel **Kravchenko** (Omega Group) holds the Requiem team prisoner. He offers them a deal: clean up the city and capture the traitor responsible, **Dr. Aleksandra Valentina**, in exchange for the life of their pilot, Raptor-1.

The systems shared by every map (Essence, Salvage, Arsenal, armor, perks, Pack-a-Punch, exfil...) are explained in [Cold War Zombies basics](../../die-maschine/systemes-cold-war/).

## The areas

The name of the area you're in shows up next to the minimap. In the order you find them:

| Area | What you find there |
| --- | --- |
| **Apartment Rooftop** and **5th Floor Apartments** | The start: a rooftop, then the floor below (apartments 501 and 503). Crafting table, Tombstone Soda, two paid ziplines |
| **Destroyed Penthouse** | Ruined building across from the start, with Quick Revive on the roof |
| **Hotel** (the Königshaus) | Room 301, Hallway, Lounge, Room 304 and Room 305. Stamin-Up in the Lounge, the CRBR-S safe in Room 305 |
| **Korber Rooftop** and **Garment Factory** | The roof of the textile factory (big KORBER sign) with Speed Cola, then the workshop below |
| **East Berlin Streets** | The main street: Alley, Bar (Jugger-Nog), Electronics Store, Grocery Store and a Military Tent |
| **Service Passage**, **Ghost Station**, **Switch Control Room**, **Maintenance Tunnel**, **Safe House**, **Sewer Access**, **Power Room** | The underground: the Weinmeisterstraße ghost subway station, the CIA safe house where Klaus waits, the power room |
| **West Berlin Street**, **Department Store**, **Blasted Suite** | The other side of the wall, after the power: Trials computer, Der Wunderfizz |
| **Checkpoint Charlie** and the **Death Strip** | The no man's land between the two walls, with the Pack-a-Punch and two watchtowers |

There are also hidden areas: the **secret lab** of the main quest and Mister Peeks' **nightclub**.

## Ziplines and rappel lines

- At the start, two **paid ziplines**: the rooftop one leads to the Destroyed Penthouse, the one in apartment 503 leads to the hotel's Room 301. See [Turn on the power](../courant/).
- A zipline links the Destroyed Penthouse and the Korber Rooftop, going through the "O" of the KORBER sign.
- The **rappel lines** only work **after the power**. They are free and act as shortcuts: from the Safe House or the Sewer Access up to West Berlin Street, from West Berlin Street to the Department Store, from the Grocery Store to the Destroyed Penthouse.

## The map's hazards

- **Ghost trains**: burning trains run through the Ghost Station. A train downs or kills you. A bell rings before they come, and often two trains pass one after the other. Since August 12, 2021, a "Train Inbound" subtitle also warns you.
- **The Checkpoint Charlie watchtowers**: they shoot at players caught in their searchlight. Pay at their base and they shoot at the zombies for a while.

## The enemies

On top of the normal zombies, the map has:

- **The Disciple**: new, unique to Mauer der Toten. It floats above the fight, makes zombies stronger (their eyes turn purple) and summons more of them. Up close, it drains your health to heal itself. The first one guards the Pack-a-Punch, and more come back later in the game.
- **Tormentor**: new. A very fast sprinter that explodes next to you. One explosion takes about half your health: two are enough to down you without armor or Jugger-Nog.
- **Tempest**: floats and throws lightning. Two of them carry the power fuses.
- **Krasny Soldat**: the flamethrower soldier, around round 10. The first one drops Klaus's battery.
- **Mangler**, **Mimic** and **Megaton**, like on the other maps. Since Season Five (August 2021), an **HVT Mimic** (tougher, red) can also spawn.

## What's new on this map

- **Klaus**, a robot ally you rebuild. See [Klaus, the robot ally](../klaus/).
- **The CRBR-S**, the map's wonder weapon. See [The CRBR-S and its 3 kits](../crbr-s/).
- **Mule Kick**, which lets you carry a third weapon. See [Perks and Der Wunderfizz](../atouts/).
- **The LT53 Kazimir**, a tactical grenade that opens a small hole into the Dark Aether and pulls zombies into it. If you walk into it, you get teleported to a random spot. See [Crafting tables, Mystery Box, Arsenal and exfil](../equipements/).

Since September 2021, the **Rampage Inducer** (an orange canister in the starting area) makes zombies more numerous and faster if every player votes for it. Since November 2, 2021, its effect lasts until round 55 (round 20 before).
