**Klaus** is a robot sent by Requiem to a CIA safe house under Berlin. You find him chained to his chair, with no hands and no battery, in the **Safe House**. Once repaired, he shoots zombies with a rifle, and he **revives a downed player** if he is nearby.

Klaus is also needed for the free CRBR-S, for the main Easter Egg and for several secrets. You first need the power and the Pack-a-Punch: see [Unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).

## Build Klaus

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Put Brain Rot on a weapon">
    At the Pack-a-Punch, buy the **Brain Rot** ammo mod (2,000 Essence) for one of your weapons. A zombie hit by Brain Rot fights on your side for a while.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get the hands in Room 305">
    Go to the hotel, in front of **Room 305**: its door is boarded up. Turn a zombie with Brain Rot right next to the door: it tears off the boards. Go in and take the **robot hands** from the body lying on the bed.

    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill a Krasny Soldat for the battery">
    The first **Krasny Soldat** (the flamethrower soldier) shows up around **round 10**. Kill it and pick up the **battery** it drops.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'battery.webp'} alt="The battery dropped by a Krasny Soldat" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Repair Klaus in the Safe House">

## Using Klaus

- While he is out, your tactical equipment is replaced by **Klaus's remote**: aim at a spot to send him there. That's how he opens lockers, boxes and walls.
- After a while, he goes back to recharge. To call him back, pay **2,000 Essence** at one of his **yellow radios**: on the **Korber Rooftop**, on **West Berlin Street** and in the **East Berlin Streets** (Military Tent).

## The blacklight

Send Klaus in front of the tall, narrow locker in the **Switch Control Room**: he smashes it open. Take the **Blacklight** inside: it attaches to your flashlight and reveals hidden marks. You need it for the free CRBR-S and for Klaus's upgrades.

## Upgrade Klaus

Klaus has **three upgrades**. Each one makes his weapon stronger, and the last two change his look. Only the first one is needed for the main Easter Egg.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Find the satellite dish">
    In the **Death Strip**, on both sides of the Pack-a-Punch, search the **rubble piles** on the ground until you find the **Microwave Dish**. It comes out of a random pile.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'deathstrip.webp'} alt="The Death Strip near the Pack-a-Punch" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'dish.webp'} alt="The satellite dish out of a rubble pile" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put it on the upgrade station">
    Klaus's upgrade station is in the **Garment Factory**. Put the dish on it.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'upgradestation.webp'} alt="The upgrade station in the Garment Factory" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Let Klaus kill zombies">
    Bring Klaus out and let him kill **40 to 50 zombies**, depending on the source. When it's done, a sound plays and the small screen at the top right of the station goes from **red to green**.

    {/* TO CHECK: kill count, about 40 (codzombieguides), 45 (mmmrkennedy, tracker.gg), 50 (DizzyOP, GamesRadar). */}

    <Screenshot src={m + 'monitor.webp'} alt="The station screen turned green" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Defend Klaus for one minute">
    Send Klaus into the station with the remote. A big wave of zombies comes: defend him for **about one minute**. He can't take many hits, so keep an eye on his health bar. At the end, he comes back out with an upgraded weapon.

    If you fail, try again later: his kill gauge has to turn green again.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the two floppy disks">
    With the blacklight, light up the **six electrical boxes** below. Two of them show a small **Klaus head**. Send Klaus in front of each one: he opens it and you take a **floppy disk**.

    1. 5th Floor Apartments, across from the zipline.
    2. Hotel Lounge, across from Stamin-Up.
    3. Ghost Station, near the way to the Service Passage.
    4. Maintenance Tunnel, near the Safe House.
    5. West Berlin Street, near the "Café Müller" sign.
    6. Blasted Suite, near the barrier.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'klaussymbol.webp'} alt="The Klaus head seen under the blacklight" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'apartments.webp'} alt="5th Floor Apartments box" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'hotel.webp'} alt="Hotel Lounge box" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'ghoststation.webp'} alt="Ghost Station box" credit={by} source={src} />

      <Screenshot src={m + 'westberlinstreet.webp'} alt="West Berlin Street box, under the Café Müller sign" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'blastedsuite.webp'} alt="Blasted Suite box" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Insert the floppy disks">
    Go back to the Garment Factory, send Klaus into the station and insert a floppy disk. This time, **no defense**: the upgrade happens on its own. Do it again with the second disk. Klaus gets a new outfit each time.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
