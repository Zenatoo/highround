The goal of **Tin Man Heart**: prepare a warhead in the Endstation secret lab, capture the traitor **Valentina**, then help Klaus carry the warhead into the portal.

**Before you start:**

- Expect **40 minutes to 1 hour** in solo, once you are well equipped.
- Doable in solo or in co-op. Cold War has **no guided mode**: the quest is the same for everyone.
- **No achievement or trophy**: like every map released after Cold War's launch, Mauer der Toten has none. The quest unlocks the **"Tin Man Heart"** Dark Ops challenge (XP and calling card), the ending cutscene and a bonus of Aetherium crystals.
- The uranium step has a **5-minute timer**: if you miss it, the game ends. Read the whole step before you start it.
- To take a breather between two steps, leave a single zombie alive at the end of a round (a crawler, for example).

## Get ready for the quest

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power and unlock the Pack-a-Punch">
    See [Turn on the power](../courant/) and [Unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build Klaus and get the CRBR-S">
    Klaus does several steps for you, and the CRBR-S is used to knock down parts and to open the lab. See [Klaus, the robot ally](../klaus/) and [The CRBR-S and its 3 kits](../crbr-s/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Do Klaus's first upgrade">
    The satellite dish and the one-minute defense in the Garment Factory are enough: **you don't need the floppy disks**. Klaus has to be upgraded before the train step. Do it early: the defense gets hard in high rounds.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Open the secret lab

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Build the Hacking Helm">
    The **Hacking Helm** has three parts. Shoot each object with the CRBR-S to knock the part down, then pick it up.

    - **Antenna**: the radio tower on the Apartment Rooftop, the starting rooftop.
    - **Electronic Boards**: the small box with a green light above the Ghost Station ticket booth, near the M16 wall buy and the "Weinmeisterstraße" sign.
    - **Transistor**: one of the three radios in the Electronics Store (near the TV, near the office chair or on a shelf). The right one changes every game.

    Then go to the Safe House and interact with Klaus in his chair to build him the helm.

  <Step title="Have Klaus break the brick wall">
    In the **Sewer Access**, go up the small staircase to the bare brick wall. Send Klaus in front of it with the remote: he smashes it and reveals a **metal wall**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'brickwall.webp'} alt="The brick wall at the top of the small staircase in the Sewer Access" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Melt the metal wall with the Blazer">
    You need the CRBR-S **Blazer** kit, which drops at random from zombies killed with the weapon. Shoot the metal wall with the beam until it melts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Survive the lab lockdown">
    As soon as a player goes in, the round ends and a **Tormentor round** starts. Two machines are protected by red shields: they drop the next round.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Fill the three canisters

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Take the canisters and a lure">
    Take the **three empty canisters** from the central machine, the **Conversion Unit**.

    Shoot the other machine (green screens, on the lower floor) with the CRBR-S: it gives you an **Endstation Lure**, a lure that takes the place of your tactical equipment. You can only carry one at a time. If there are none left, wait for the next round. In co-op, each player should take one.

    {/* TO CHECK: lure availability. tracker.gg: next round if none is available; GamesRadar: one per round; GameSpot: each player can take one. */}

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'conversionunit.webp'} alt="The Conversion Unit in the middle of the lab" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'luremachine.webp'} alt="The machine that gives the lures" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the canisters in three harvesters">
    The **Essence Harvesters** are big green boxes. There are seven spots, but **only three** show up in each game. They are easy to see: find them ahead of time.

    - West Berlin Street, against a small shack near the Death Strip.
    - Destroyed Penthouse, in the ruined room under Quick Revive.
    - East Berlin Streets, under the arch between the Bar and the Electronics Store.
    - Korber Rooftop, next to the pillar in front of Speed Cola.
    - Alley, next to the dumpster, at the foot of the rope.
    - Sewer Access, left of the XM4 wall buy, near the lab.
    - Ghost Station, on a platform near the M16 wall buy.

    Interact with each harvester to put a canister in it.

  <Step title="Fill the canisters with Tempests">
    Throw a lure next to a harvester: **Tempests** spawn. Kill them near the harvester. When the canister glows **purple**, it's full: take it. If not, throw another lure.

    Bring each full canister back to the Conversion Unit. With a full canister, you can **neither sprint nor climb**, and enemies come to stop you: get covered in co-op. Do it again for all three canisters.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="PC Gamer" source="https://www.pcgamer.com/cod-cold-war-mauer-der-toten-easter-egg-wonder-weapon/">
    Make sure the canister is in the harvester before you throw the lure: otherwise, the Tempests you kill fill nothing.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Stop the train

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Pull the railway switch">
    Bring Klaus out. In the **Switch Control Room**, pull the Railway Switch on the big console.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'railwayswitch.webp'} alt="The railway switch in the Switch Control Room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put Klaus on the track">
    In the Ghost Station, send Klaus onto the track **farthest from Mule Kick**, on the ticket booth and M16 wall buy side. A train comes and Klaus stops it.

    If it fails, wait for the next round and pull the switch again: only one try per round.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'klaus_on_tracks.webp'} alt="Klaus on the right track" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the keycard and the bomb from the train">
    Get on board quickly, before the train leaves again. Take **Rico's Keycard** from the seated body, and the **bomb** (the warhead) from the crate.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'train_keycard.webp'} alt="Rico's Keycard on a seated body" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'train_bomb.webp'} alt="The bomb in the train" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the bomb in the lab">
    Go back to the lab and put the bomb on the Conversion Unit, with the three canisters.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The uranium (twice)

You do this part **twice**, over two rounds. Get ready beforehand: the 5-minute timer starts as soon as the Megaton dies, and failing **ends the game**.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Activate the satellite">
    In the **Safe House**, use the computer on the central table with Rico's keycard. Pick "Browse Files" until the "Activate the Disruptor Satellite" option shows up, then follow the marker on screen.

    According to most guides, you then have to use the computer in a tent: the one on **West Berlin Street** (next to the Trials) the first time, the **Military Tent** in the East Berlin Streets the second time.

    {/* TO CHECK: GameSpot, PC Gamer, EarlyGame and KeenGamer use the West Berlin tent computer, then the East Berlin one the 2nd time; DizzyOP's description uses only the Safe House computer twice; mmmrkennedy says "follow the objective marker". */}

    <Screenshot src={m + 'safehouse_computer.webp'} alt="The Safe House computer" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill the Megaton">
    A beam comes down from the sky and elite enemies spawn, including an **HVT Megaton** and Mimics. Kill the others first and save the Megaton for last. As always, it splits in two (Blaster and Bomber): each half drops a **Uranium Rock**. The 5-minute timer starts.

    Since July 29, 2021, the rocks drop even if a ghost train kills the Megaton.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build the two uranium devices">
    Take a rock and carry it to the closer of the two quest workbenches: in the tent on **West Berlin Street** or in the **Military Tent** in the East Berlin Streets. Build a **Uranium Device**. Do the same with the second rock.

    Carrying uranium slowly drains your health. Healing Aura helps a lot.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'west_bench.webp'} alt="The West Berlin Street quest workbench" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'east_bench.webp'} alt="The Military Tent quest workbench" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Crash them into each other on the zipline">
    Hook one device to the end of the **Destroyed Penthouse** zipline, and the other to the end of the **Korber Rooftop** one (the zipline that goes through the "O" of KORBER). Both slide, crash into each other in the middle and a **Cleansed Rock** falls into the East Berlin Streets. The timer stops.

    In solo, do the two devices one after the other. In co-op, split the two rocks.

    {/* Penthouse and Korber ziplines: mmmrkennedy / tracker.gg, PC Gamer, KeenGamer, EarlyGame, DizzyOP, COD Zombified. GameSpot alone says the Apartment Rooftop. */}

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'penthouse_zipline.webp'} alt="The Destroyed Penthouse zipline" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'korber_zipline.webp'} alt="The Korber Rooftop zipline" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the Cleansed Rock in the warhead">
    Pick up the rock in the street and put it on the warhead, in the lab.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Do it again the next round">
    Move on to the next round and do the whole step again for a **second Cleansed Rock**.

    **Warning:** putting in the second rock starts the boss fight **right away**. Drop it near the warhead, then gear up: perks, upgraded weapons, armor, Self-Revives, scorestreaks.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="GameSpot" source="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/how-to-complete-mauer-der-toten-main-easter-egg-in-cod-black-ops-cold-wars-zombies/1100-6494140/">
    Take Healing Aura as your field upgrade for this step: you heal the uranium damage without dropping the device and without losing time.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="PC Gamer" source="https://www.pcgamer.com/cod-cold-war-mauer-der-toten-easter-egg-wonder-weapon/">

  </Tip>

## The fight against Valentina

Valentina shows up in the lab. She changes arenas four times before coming back to the lab. Her attacks and the strategy are covered in [Beat Valentina](../boss-valentina/).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Beat Valentina">
    Break her shield (blue bar), then empty her health (red bar). Kill the zombies floating around her right away: they heal her. When the screen turns blue and shakes, hide out of her sight.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Protect Klaus to the portal">
    Interact with Valentina once she is down: a cutscene plays. Then **defend Klaus** while he carries the warhead to the portal. Once Klaus is in the portal, the game ends on the ending cutscene.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
