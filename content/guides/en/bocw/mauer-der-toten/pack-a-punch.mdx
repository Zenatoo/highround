The Pack-a-Punch is at **Checkpoint Charlie**, in the no man's land (the **Death Strip**) between the two walls, between two watchtowers. From the very start, you can see it from the starting rooftop: it glows red, under a circle of floating zombies. To unlock it, you need the power, then you have to beat **a Disciple**.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power">
    See [Turn on the power](../courant/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go back up to the surface by a rappel line">
    After the power, take the rappel line in the **Safe House** or the one at the back of the **Sewer Access**. They are free and lead to **West Berlin Street**. Then climb over the rubble toward Checkpoint Charlie.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start the trial">
    Walk up to the machine: it is locked ("Locked Pack-a-Punch") and asks you to face the Dark Aether to prove your worth. Interact with it.

    The floating zombies drop to the ground and attack, led by a **Disciple**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill the Disciple">
    Focus your fire on the Disciple, especially its face: it buffs the zombies and summons more. When it dies, the machine sends out a shockwave that kills the remaining zombies. The Pack-a-Punch is unlocked for the rest of the game.

    The first Disciple goes down fast. More will come back later in the game, like normal enemies.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="Call of Duty" source="https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2021/07/black-ops-cold-war-season-four-mauer-der-toten-zombies-tips">
    During the trial, you can pay at the base of a watchtower so it shoots at the zombies. To escape, you can also break a pile of rubble that takes you back to the East Berlin Streets.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="faster" author="GameSpot" source="https://www.gamespot.com/articles/mauer-der-toten-guide-how-to-pack-a-punch-and-turn-on-power-in-cod-black-ops-cold-war/1100-6493986/">
    The trial is easy: you can beat it around round 6, even without Jugger-Nog.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Prices

Like on every Cold War map:

- 3 upgrade tiers: **5,000**, **15,000** then **30,000 Essence**;
- one ammo mod: **2,000 Essence**.

The rest is explained in [Cold War Zombies basics](../../die-maschine/systemes-cold-war/).

Buy the **Brain Rot** ammo mod early if you want to build Klaus: you need it to open the Room 305 door. See [Klaus, the robot ally](../klaus/).
