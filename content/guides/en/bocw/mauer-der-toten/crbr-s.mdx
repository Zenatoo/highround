The **CRBR-S** (pronounced "Cerberus") is the wonder weapon of Mauer der Toten. It is an energy pistol surrounded by a small orb, the **Copy-Cat**: every shot fires twice. You can Pack-a-Punch it, and it transforms with three **Mod Kits**. You need it for the main Easter Egg.

## How to get it

- From the **Mystery Box**. See [Crafting tables, Mystery Box, Arsenal and exfil](../equipements/).
- As a top-tier **Trials** reward.
- Sometimes behind a door in the nightclub. See [Mister Peeks' club](../club-mister-peeks/).
- **For free**, from the safe in Room 305 (below). It's the only sure way.

## The three kits

When you kill zombies with the CRBR-S, they sometimes drop a **kit**. You don't pick which one. When you pick it up, your pistol transforms:

| Kit | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Blazer** | A continuous laser beam. It shreds enemy armor, and it's the one that melts the secret lab wall |
| **Diffuser** | A spread energy blast, like a shotgun. Great against groups |
| **Swarm** | Homing explosive projectiles: when you aim, they lock on to zombies |

When the kit runs out of ammo, you get the base pistol back. Picking up a kit also refills some of the base pistol's ammo.
