Three **golden cassette tapes** are hidden on the map. Pick up all three: the secret song starts, **"Amoeba"** by the punk band **Adolescents**.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Garment Factory">
    Sitting on a mannequin's neck, just right of the door that goes down to the factory stairs and the Alley.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tape1.webp'} alt="The cassette on a mannequin in the Garment Factory" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Electronics Store">
    On a shelf, on the middle row, near the exit that leads to the Bar and Jugger-Nog.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tape2.webp'} alt="The cassette on a shelf in the Electronics Store" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Blasted Suite">
    Leaning against a flower vase, on a table next to the crafting table. The Blasted Suite is on the West Berlin side, which you reach after the power.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tape3.webp'} alt="The cassette against the vase in the Blasted Suite" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

The song only plays once per game.
