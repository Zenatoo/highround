This guide lists where to find the useful machines on Mauer der Toten. How they work (Salvage, item prices, Arsenal, armor, Trials, exfil) is the same on every map: see [Cold War Zombies basics](../../die-maschine/systemes-cold-war/).

## Crafting tables

The crafting table makes equipment with Salvage (the scrap enemies drop).

- **5th Floor Apartments**, in the hallway under the starting rooftop.
- **Power Room**, next to the power switch.
- **Grocery Store**, in the East Berlin Streets.

{/* TO CHECK: the Grocery Store crafting table is only on COD Zombified's annotated layout (with a screenshot); the official guide names only the first two. */}

### The LT53 Kazimir

A tactical grenade that came with this map, and has since been available on every round-based map. It opens a small hole into the Dark Aether that pulls in and kills nearby zombies. If **you** walk into it, you get teleported to a random spot on the map.

- At the crafting table for **2,000 Salvage**.
- More rarely: from an enemy, in the Mystery Box or as a Trials reward.
- For free with the sewer hole: see [The sewer loot](../butin-des-egouts/).

## Quest workbenches

Two small workbenches are only used for the main quest (crafting the uranium device): in the tent on **West Berlin Street**, next to the Trials computer, and in the **Military Tent** in the East Berlin Streets. See [Main Easter Egg (Tin Man Heart)](../easter-egg-principal/).

## Mystery Box

A spin costs 950 Essence, like everywhere. A beam of light in the sky shows where it is. It can give you the CRBR-S: see [The CRBR-S and its 3 kits](../crbr-s/).

Possible spots:

- **Apartment Rooftop**, next to the starting zipline;
- on the roof of the **Destroyed Penthouse**;
- **Hotel Hallway**;
- **Korber Rooftop**;
- **Sewer Access**, in the small dead-end room;
- **Maintenance Tunnel**;
- **Ghost Station**, between the two tracks (watch out for the trains).

## Arsenal

The Arsenal upgrades weapon rarity and sells armor. It works after the power.

- **Hotel Hallway**, next to the wall buy;
- **Electronics Store**, in the back room;
- **Sewer Access**.

{/* TO CHECK: Arsenal spots come from the official guide only ("Armory"); COD Zombified's hotel layout marks an upgrade station in the Hallway too. Others may exist past the power. */}

## Trials

The **Trials** computer is on **West Berlin Street**, after the power. The top reward can be the CRBR-S.

## Exfil

Exfil works like on the other maps: you can call it from round 10, then every 5 rounds. See [Cold War Zombies basics](../../die-maschine/systemes-cold-war/).
