Mauer der Toten has **8 perk machines** and a **Der Wunderfizz**. The machines only work after the power: see [Turn on the power](../courant/).

The price doesn't depend on the machine but on how many perks you already have: **2,500 Essence** for the first one, then 500 more for each one after that. This price, the perk tiers and Der Wunderfizz are explained in [Cold War Zombies basics](../../die-maschine/systemes-cold-war/).

| Perk | Where |
| --- | --- |
| **Tombstone Soda** | 5th Floor Apartments, in apartment 503, next to the zipline to the hotel |
| **Quick Revive** | On the roof of the Destroyed Penthouse, across from the start |
| **Speed Cola** | Korber Rooftop, where you land from the zipline that goes through the "O" of KORBER |
| **Stamin-Up** | Hotel Lounge, next to the bar |
| **Jugger-Nog** | In the Bar in the East Berlin Streets |
| **Deadshot Daiquiri** | Service Passage, on the left wall as you come down through the manhole |
| **Mule Kick** | Ghost Station, on the platform across the tracks |
| **Elemental Pop** | Power Room, next to the power switch |
| **Der Wunderfizz** | Department Store, near the Pack-a-Punch: go up by the West Berlin Street zipline |

**Death Perception** and **PhD Slider**, added to the game later, have no machine here: you can only get them from Der Wunderfizz.

## Mule Kick

Mule Kick arrived with this map: it is its first machine in Cold War. It lets you carry **a third primary weapon**.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="Treyarch" source="https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2021/07/black-ops-cold-war-season-four-mauer-der-toten-zombies-tips">
    The Mule Kick machine fires real bullets. If zombies are chasing you while you buy the perk, use it.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

Since September 8, 2021, the machine is also there in local (offline) games.

## Free perks

- Mister Peeks' nightclub can give you a perk. See [Mister Peeks' club](../club-mister-peeks/).
- The sewer hole in the East Berlin Streets can spit out a perk can. See [The sewer loot](../butin-des-egouts/).
- Trials rewards can give you one too.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="Cod Zombie Guides" source="https://www.codzombieguides.com/mauer-der-toten">
    Go prone in front of each perk machine: the first time, you get 100 Essence. In co-op, only the first player to do it gets them.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
