A little shooting gallery on the starting rooftop. Hit the five targets and you win an **Aether Tool**: it upgrades the weapon in your hands by **one rarity tier**, for free. The best value is to save it to take a weapon from Epic to Legendary, the most expensive upgrade at the Arsenal.

**Only one try per game**: if you miss a target, it's over until the next game.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power">
    The folder that starts the challenge only shows up after the power. See [Turn on the power](../courant/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bring a scoped weapon">
    The targets are far away and move fast. Take a sniper rifle or a tactical rifle with a scope (3x or more). Clear out the zombies first, or keep a single crawler alive.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the folder">
    On the **Apartment Rooftop**, in the corner closest to the street, next to the sandbags, there is a folder on the ground. Interact with it: the first target appears.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'folder.webp'} alt="The folder on the ground on the starting rooftop" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hit the five targets">
    Silhouettes appear **one at a time**, always in the same order, and slide from left to right before disappearing. Each target you hit makes the next one appear.

    1. Behind the yellow **Königshaus** sign, on the building across. Aim through the "O".
    2. In the lit windows of the **Königshaus**, a few floors lower.
    3. Behind the **KORBER** sign, in the "O".
    4. In the distance, right of Korber: the **three yellow windows** of the **Der Dozent** building, under the roof.
    5. Behind the **Der Dozent** sign.

  <Step title="Pick up the Aether Tool with the right weapon">
    After the fifth target, the Aether Tool appears right next to you. It upgrades **the weapon you're holding** when you pick it up: switch back to the weapon you want to upgrade first.

  <Tip type="mistake" author="Game Rant" source="https://gamerant.com/call-of-duty-black-ops-cold-war-zombies-free-aether-tool-upgrade-mauer-der-toten/">
