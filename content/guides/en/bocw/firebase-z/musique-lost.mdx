Three **cassette tapes** are hidden on the map. Pick up all three, in any order: the song "**Lost**" plays for the whole team. It is composed by Kevin Sherwood, written by Craig Houston and sung by Julie Nathanson, the voice of Samantha Maxis.

The tapes show **no prompt**: aim at them and **hold the interact button**.

The third one is in the Scientist Quarters, which only open with the power: see [Turn on the power (the 3 reactors)](../courant/).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Equipment Storage (Village)">
    On the ground floor, on the metal shelves on the right as you come in from the courtyard. Look in the unit next to the one with a keypad: the tape is in the cubby with a red box.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tape2.webp'} alt="The tape on a shelf in the Equipment Storage" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Motor Pool Office (base)">
    At the Motor Pool, go into the office across from the garage. The tape is on the floor, against a green filing cabinet, to the right of a desk with a cassette player.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tape3.webp'} alt="The tape against the filing cabinet in the Motor Pool Office" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Scientist Quarters (Village, with the power)">
    Above the Briefing Room, go up to the balcony. Between the Scientist Quarters doors, there are two small units: the tape is on the middle shelf of the one on the right. It is above the Pack-a-Punch.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tape1.webp'} alt="The tape on the Scientist Quarters shelf" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Since Season Two (February 2021), once you have found the song in-game, you can also listen to "Lost" in the menu's music player.
