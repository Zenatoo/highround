Firebase Z has **five perk machines** and **one Der Wunderfizz machine**, which sells the others. With the updates, all **10 perks** of Cold War are available on the map.

The price doesn't depend on the machine: your first perk costs **2,500 Essence**, then each next perk costs 500 more. There is no limit. The effects and upgrade tiers of each perk are in [Cold War Zombies basics](../../die-maschine/systemes-cold-war/).

## The machines

| Perk | Where | Condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quick Revive** | Village, Equipment Storage: on the right, under the arch, on the way to the teleporter | None |
| **Tombstone Soda** | Helipad, on the other side of the crashed helicopter, in the corner near the blue gate | None |
| **Stamin-Up** | Mission Control, at the top of the stairs, on the left | The Mission Control reactor must be activated |
| **Jugger-Nog** | Military Command, in the operations room after the reactor, near the stairs to the Sky Bridge | None |
| **Speed Cola** | Data Center, upstairs, to the left of the door, near the Memory Transference Station | None |
| **Der Wunderfizz** | Village, in the room to the left of the Atrium (Ravenov and Pack-a-Punch) | The power |

The other five perks are sold at **Der Wunderfizz**: you pick the perk on the machine.

- **Deadshot Daiquiri** and **Elemental Pop**, since the map came out;
- **Mule Kick**, since Season Four Reloaded (July 2021);
- **Death Perception**, since Season Five (August 2021);
- **PhD Slider**, since Season Six (October 2021).

## Tombstone Soda, the map's new perk

Tombstone Soda came out with Firebase Z, which has its own machine for it. When you go down, you become a **shadow** in the Dark Aether, with your weapons. Kill enough zombies and you get back up with all your gear. If the shadow loses all its health, you die. A teammate can also revive you normally.

Its upgrades, bought between matches with Aetherium crystals:

1. The shadow regenerates its health.
2. You stay down for **60 seconds**.
3. If you die, you leave a **stash** with your weapons, equipment and resources: you pick it up when you respawn.

Tiers 4 and 5, added later, are in [Cold War Zombies basics](../../die-maschine/systemes-cold-war/).
