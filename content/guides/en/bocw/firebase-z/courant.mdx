On Firebase Z, there is **no power switch**. The power comes from three **Aether Reactors** hidden in the base, on the other side of the teleporter. You have to activate and defend all three. After that, the Pack-a-Punch and the Village's electric doors work.

The map has two halves:

- the **Village**, where you start: the Courtyard, the **Atrium** with Ravenov and the Pack-a-Punch, the Radio Room and the Equipment Storage;
- the **base** (Outpost 25), which you reach through a teleporter: the **Helipad** in the middle, and three gates (north, east, south) to the rest.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Open the Equipment Storage door">
    Earn a few points in the Village, then open the door to the **Equipment Storage**, next to Quick Revive: **1,250 Essence**. Go up the stairs to the walkway on the roof. The **teleporter** is at the end: it takes you to the Helipad, at the foot of a crashed helicopter.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reach a reactor">
    Three icons mark the reactors on your screen, and purple Dark Aether arrows show the way. You have to buy doors to get there.

    | Reactor | Where | From the Helipad |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Mission Control** | To the north, in the Stamin-Up building | North gate, then Open Lot and walkway |
    | **Data Center** | To the east, at the bottom of the spiral staircase | East gate, then Barracks and stairs |
    | **Military Command** | To the south, in a small outdoor courtyard, next to the ways into the Jungle Defense | South gate, then Motor Pool |

    Mission Control has two entrances: buying one also opens the other. You can also reach Military Command through the **Sky Bridge**, from the Planning Offices.

    {/* TO CHECK: PC Gamer says the blue door into the Motor Pool costs 1,250 (single source). No source lists the other door prices. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate it and defend it">
    Activating a reactor costs **500 Essence**. A wave of zombies comes: kill them **near the reactor** to charge it. On each side, a **Collection Unit** must stay standing: protect them. Two bars show up, the charge and the damage. If the reactor takes too much damage, you have to pay again.

    Stock up on ammo and equipment before you activate it: the wave is long.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Do the same with the other two">
    When all three reactors are charged, everyone gets a message: the power is on. The three reactors cost **1,500 Essence** in total.

    The Pack-a-Punch in the Atrium works, along with Der Wunderfizz, an Arsenal and new rooms in the Village. Go back to Ravenov in the Atrium: it is also the start of the [Main Easter Egg (Maxis Potential)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## After the power: the Assault Rounds

Once the power is on, some rounds become **Assault Rounds**. Dark Aether rifts open in one of the three fortified areas, and a big wave comes out:

- **Scorched Defense**, to the north, behind Mission Control;
- **Rocky Defense**, to the east, through the lower doors of the Data Center;
- **Jungle Defense**, to the south, behind Military Command.

Each area has a firing terminal with a **Support** (Napalm Strike, Cruise Missile or Artillery): that is what you must protect. Automatic turrets help, but they aren't enough. During the round, you can buy more of that Support at the terminal. If you hold out, power-ups drop at the terminal: Double Points, Max Ammo, Salvage…

{/* TO CHECK: which Support sits at which Defense. Only Rocky Defense = Artillery is sourced (Dexerto box guide, reworded by GGRecon). */}

To get there fast, take the **jump pads**: 500 Essence per jump. See [Free jump pads](../tremplins-gratuits/) to stop paying for them.

From round 30, the **Orda**, the map's giant boss, can show up during an Assault Round. See [Beat the Orda](../boss-orda/).

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="Call of Duty" source="https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2021/02/Guide-to-Surviving-Firebase-Z">
    Save your Supports, your strongest weapon and your Field Upgrade for the Assault Rounds. The zombies come out of the rifts in groups: a sniper rifle or a rocket launcher is finally useful here.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="faster" author="Call of Duty" source="https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2021/02/Guide-to-Surviving-Firebase-Z">
    Before the power, run around the crashed helicopter on the Helipad: it is a big open space, handy for training zombies.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
