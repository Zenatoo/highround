A **stuffed bunny** sends you into the Dark Aether. There, a golden chest is waiting for you, with **free Jugger-Nog** and other rewards. It takes a few minutes.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power">
    You need the power and the Pack-a-Punch active. See [Turn on the power (the 3 reactors)](../courant/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the bunny in the Atrium">
    Go upstairs in the **Atrium**, above Der Wunderfizz. Go through the hole in the wall and stop near the junk, in front of the Scientist Quarters door. At the end of a narrow passage, a **blue stuffed bunny** sits on a cabinet, outside the playable area.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'bunny.webp'} alt="The stuffed bunny, out of the map, upstairs in the Atrium" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Stare at it until it stares back">
    **Stare at the bunny** without moving the camera. After a while, it turns its head toward you: keep looking at it. You hear a scream and you are taken to the **Dark Aether**. Shooting it also works, but you don't need to.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the floating bunny three times">
    In the Dark Aether, look for a **bright light** and a creepy sound: the bunny floats there, glowing pink. Shoot it: it teleports somewhere else. Do it again. On the **third** shot, a **golden chest** appears near it.

    There are zombies there too: don't hang around.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the chest">
    Open it and take what's inside. You then go back to the map.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## What's in the chest

**Jugger-Nog** is guaranteed. The rest is random, for example:

- the **RAI K-84** or an M82;
- a Cymbal Monkey;
- Salvage and High-Grade Salvage;
- Stun Grenades;
- the "Full Tour" calling card.

Since Season Two, the chest stays longer before it disappears.
