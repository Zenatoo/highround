The **Cymbal Monkey** (the monkey bomb) pulls zombies in, then explodes. On Firebase Z, you can upgrade it: it turns **red**, plays dance music, the zombies **dance** around it, and it does **more damage**. A nod to the monkey bombs of Gorod Krovi (Black Ops III).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power">
    All three Aether Reactors must be activated. See [Turn on the power (the 3 reactors)](../courant/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get some Cymbal Monkeys">
    Craft them at the crafting table for **1,000 Salvage** each. Zombies sometimes drop them too.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get 50 kills near a reactor">
    Go near one of the three Aether Reactors: **Military Command**, **Mission Control** or **Data Center**. Wait for a big group of zombies to come, throw the monkey bomb in the middle. Repeat until **50 zombies are killed** by monkey bombs near the reactors.
