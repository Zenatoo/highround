The Firebase Z Pack-a-Punch is in the **Atrium**, the Village building where Ravenov is, right next to his office. You can see it from the start, but it says "You need to turn the power on first": you need the power first.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power">
    Activate and defend the three Aether Reactors in the base. It is all explained in [Turn on the power (the 3 reactors)](../courant/). There is **no other step**: as soon as the third reactor is charged, the machine works.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go back to the Atrium">
    Take the teleporter back to the Village and go into the Atrium. Talk to Ravenov on the way: it is the first step of the [Main Easter Egg (Maxis Potential)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upgrade your weapon">
    Three tiers, like on every Cold War map:

    | Tier | Price |
    | --- | --- |
    | 1 | 5,000 Essence |
    | 2 | 15,000 Essence |
    | 3 | 30,000 Essence |

    The machine also applies your **ammo mod**, for 2,000 Essence. How it all works is in [Cold War Zombies basics](../../die-maschine/systemes-cold-war/).

    {/* TO CHECK: ammo mod price 2,000 = Inverse only (also flagged in the game-wide notes). */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="mmmrkennedy" source="https://mmmrkennedyzombiesguides.pages.dev/games/BO_CW/firebase_z/firebase_z_guide">
    The RAI K-84, the map's wonder weapon, goes through the Pack-a-Punch like a normal weapon. Upgrade it before the final fight against the Orda. See [Build the RAI K-84](../rai-k-84/).
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="faster" author="mmmrkennedy" source="https://mmmrkennedyzombiesguides.pages.dev/games/BO_CW/firebase_z/firebase_z_guide">
    During the fight against the Orda, at the end of the Easter Egg, you can still use the Pack-a-Punch: it is right next to the arena.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
