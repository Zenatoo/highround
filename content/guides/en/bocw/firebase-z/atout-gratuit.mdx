**Sergei's severed head** opens the **Colonel's safe**. Inside: a **free perk**, picked at random among the ones you don't have, and an Intel.

You need an **Essence Trap**: it unlocks during the [Main Easter Egg (Maxis Potential)](../easter-egg-principal/), after Ravenov's badge, the serum and Dr. Peck's interrogation. Do those steps first, up to the Memory Transference Station in the Data Center.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Take an Essence Trap">
    Upstairs in the **Data Center**, take an Essence Trap at the Memory Transference Station.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find Sergei's head">
    A **glowing severed head** appears in one of these **six places**, at the edge of the Assault Round areas, not in the trenches:

    - **Scorched Defense**: under the bunker stairs;
    - **Scorched Defense**: near a tank;
    - **Rocky Defense**: on the sandbags;
    - **Rocky Defense**: to the left of the stairs to the Weapon Lab;
    - **Jungle Defense**: near the barrels;
    - **Jungle Defense**: between the left path and the middle one.

    Pick it up.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'scorched_loc1.webp'} alt="Scorched Defense, under the bunker stairs" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'scorched_loc2.webp'} alt="Scorched Defense, near a tank" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'rocky_loc1.webp'} alt="Rocky Defense, on the sandbags" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'rocky_loc2.webp'} alt="Rocky Defense, to the left of the stairs to the Weapon Lab" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'jungle_loc1.webp'} alt="Jungle Defense, near the barrels" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'jungle_loc2.webp'} alt="Jungle Defense, between the left path and the middle one" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the head at the Field Hospital">
    In the **Field Hospital**, put the head on the **Charging Plate**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'charging_plate.webp'} alt="The Field Hospital Charging Plate" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Catch a zombie in the trap">
    Damage a **zombie** until it has very little health left, throw the trap in front of it and press the interact button twice when it is on it. Any zombie works. Bring the full trap back and put it next to the head, on the plate.

    The head starts talking and shouts numbers. Then it rushes at you and **explodes**. You don't need to remember the numbers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the safe">
    Go up to the **Colonel's Office**, above Jugger-Nog in Military Command. The safe is **on the floor**: use it, then take the perk and the Intel.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'safe.webp'} alt="The Colonel's Office safe" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="Game Rant" source="https://gamerant.com/call-of-duty-black-ops-cold-war-zombies-firebase-z-safe-perk/">
    If you have trouble trapping a normal zombie, try a Mangler or a Mimic that you've weakened a lot.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="Game Rant" source="https://gamerant.com/call-of-duty-black-ops-cold-war-zombies-firebase-z-safe-perk/">
    If you already have every perk, the safe can't give you anything. Come back after you've gone down and lost some.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
