The goal: make Dr. Peck talk, get three Aether crystals, repair the breach to the Dark Aether and beat the **Orda** to free Samantha Maxis.

**Before you start:**

- Plan for **40 minutes to 1 hour** in solo. Most of the work can be done by round 20.
- Doable in solo or in co-op: the steps are the same.
- You need the **power**: see [Turn on the power (the 3 reactors)](../courant/).
- You need the **RAI K-84** for one of the three crystals. Start working on it early: see [Build the RAI K-84](../rai-k-84/).
- Firebase Z has **no achievements or trophies**, not even for this quest. The reward is a Dark Ops challenge, "Maxis Potential" (XP and a calling card), the ending cutscene, and since May 2021 bonus Aetherium crystals.

## Ravenov's badge

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Talk to Ravenov, then to Dr. Peck">
    In the **Atrium** (Village), talk to Ravenov. Then go to **Mission Control**, in the base: Dr. Peck is locked up on the ground floor, in a blue room behind a window. Talk to him through the window.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pecksroom.webp'} alt="Dr. Peck's room, behind the Mission Control window" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get the badge">
    Go back to Ravenov in the Atrium. He puts his **ID Badge** in the box under his window: take it.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The truth serum

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Open three lockers with the badge">
    Each keypad locker holds a compound. Any order.

    - **Equipment Storage** (Village): in the room to the right of Quick Revive. Compound S16.
    - **Engineering** (base): coming out of the teleporter, the area on the left, on the ground floor under the Planning Offices. Compound 36.
    - **Colonel's Office**: the office above Jugger-Nog, in Military Command. Compound P65.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'chem_spawn.webp'} alt="The Equipment Storage locker" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'chem_eng.webp'} alt="The Engineering locker" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'chem_office.webp'} alt="The Colonel's Office locker" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Mix the serum at the Field Hospital">
    At the **Field Hospital** (near the Barracks), use the **Chemical Mixer**. **Hellhounds** show up: kill them all. Use the mixer a second time, then take the **Agent Delivery System**, to the left of the mixer.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'chem_mixer.webp'} alt="The Field Hospital Chemical Mixer" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'agent_delivery_system.webp'} alt="The Agent Delivery System to the left of the mixer" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Gas Dr. Peck">
    Go up to the top of Mission Control, on the **OPC** side (Omega Portal Chamber, the big sphere). Put the Agent Delivery System on the **Air Conditioner Unit**: the gas fills Peck's room.

    Go back and talk to Peck, several times, until Weaver says "Alright, Peck. You've had your fun…". Peck sends you to the Data Center.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'chem_acunit.webp'} alt="The Air Conditioner Unit near the OPC" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The Mimics' memories

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Take an Essence Trap at the Data Center">
    Upstairs in the **Data Center**, near Speed Cola, use the **Memory Transference Station**. It gives **Essence Traps**: take one. It replaces your tactical equipment.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'mimic_trapstation.webp'} alt="The Data Center Memory Transference Station" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find a disguised Mimic">
    The **Mimic** is an enemy that hides as an item on the ground. You need a hidden Mimic, not one that comes with the wave. They hide in **three places**:

    - **Motor Pool**: in front of the garage, coming out of the teleporter, the area on the right;
    - **Sky Bridge and Colonel's Office**: the Jugger-Nog room and the walkway above it;
    - **Peck's Quarters**: above the Pack-a-Punch, in the room on the right.

    Look for a **pile of several items** on the ground (armor, ammo, Supports…) that you can't pick up. A single item on its own doesn't count. Walk up to it: one of the items turns into a Mimic. Only one place has a Mimic each round, and each of the three places gives a memory.

    {/* Three fixed areas: mmmrkennedy and NoahJ456's "UPDATED STEP" (video description). GameSpot: Motor Pool and the Village. GamesRadar and Charlie Intel describe random clusters anywhere: launch-day state, before the Feb 5, 2021 patch cut the number of Mimic trap spawns. */}

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'mimic_motorpool.webp'} alt="The Motor Pool road" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'mimic_jugroom.webp'} alt="The Jugger-Nog room and the Sky Bridge" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'mimic_spawnlocation.webp'} alt="Peck's Quarters, above the Pack-a-Punch" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Trap it">
    Damage the Mimic down to **about 10% health**, without killing it. Throw the trap on the ground. When the Mimic steps on it, **press the interact button twice quickly**. If it is weak enough, it stays trapped: pick up the trap. If it has too much health, it escapes and you need a new trap.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bring the memory back">
    You have **3 minutes** to put the trap back in the Memory Transference Station. If it is the right Mimic, you hear the memory of **Brahms**, **Sokolov** or **Zhabin**. If not, you hear other bits of story, then "memory corrupted".

    Repeat for all three names. After the third, the machine ejects a **floppy disk**.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source="https://mmmrkennedyzombiesguides.pages.dev/games/BO_CW/firebase_z/firebase_z_guide">
    The hidden Mimic only appears if nobody is nearby at the start of the round. If you killed it by mistake or can't find anything, finish the round away from these places and come back on the next one.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="NoahJ456" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HgN6lRcp-rA">
    The items reset every round. Once the Mimic is out, don't leave the area while you weaken it.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## The breach and the three crystals

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Put the floppy disk in the Quantum Main Frame">
    Upstairs in the **Planning Offices**, against the wall closest to the sphere, put the floppy disk in the **Quantum Main Frame** computer. The OPC door opens. Go in: a dimensional **breach** appears.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'breach_floppyinsert.webp'} alt="The Quantum Main Frame computer in the Planning Offices" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get the Aethermeter">
    When the speakers announce "Attention: membrane instability detected. Stabilization required", go talk to Dr. Peck. Then Ravenov gives a code. Open the **locker to the left of Peck's window** and take the **Aethermeter**, a detector that beeps near buried crystals.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'locker_peck.webp'} alt="The locker to the left of Peck's room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the shovel in the Scorched Defense">
    The **shovel** is in a small bunker on the left of the **Scorched Defense**, just behind Mission Control.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'shovel_room.webp'} alt="The bunker on the left in the Scorched Defense" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'shovel.webp'} alt="The shovel" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open Lot crystal: the right container">
    In the **Open Lot**, dig near the window that looks onto Engineering. Lots of containers appear around you. Almost all of them have **black particles** in their smoke. Find the only one with just **white smoke** and take it.

    If you take the wrong one, Mimics come out. Kill them and wait for the next round to try again.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'container_lot.webp'} alt="The spot to dig in the Open Lot" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'container_noblack.webp'} alt="The right container, with no black particles" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'container_black.webp'} alt="A wrong container, with black particles" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Jungle Defense crystal: survive in the dome">
    In the **Jungle Defense**, dig in the middle lane, where the zombies come from during Assault Rounds. A **dome** locks you in and enemies come: zombies, Mimics, Manglers, Hellhounds. Hold out until the dome disappears, then take the container.

    Come with your perks, armor, a Self-Revive and the RAI K-84: it is the hardest step.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'container_jungle.webp'} alt="The spot to dig in the Jungle Defense" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Barracks crystal: catch it with the RAI K-84">
    Dig in front of the **Field Hospital** door closest to the Data Center stairs. The container teleports somewhere in the Barracks or the Field Hospital. **Don't get close**, or it moves again.

    Spot it from a distance and shoot it with the **RAI K-84's secondary fire** (the energy mine). While the vortex holds it, run and pick it up. Too slow or too far: it teleports again, try again.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'container_hospital.webp'} alt="The spot to dig in front of the Field Hospital" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put a crystal in each reactor">
    Place a crystal in each of the three Aether Reactors: **Military Command**, **Mission Control** and **Data Center**. Any order.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'container_militarycommand.webp'} alt="The Military Command reactor" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'container_missioncontrol.webp'} alt="The Mission Control reactor" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'container_datacenter.webp'} alt="The Data Center reactor" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go back to the OPC, then to Peck">
    In the OPC, a scene plays and the breach closes. Then go talk to Dr. Peck one last time: the blinds are down, but you can talk to him.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The satellite and the final fight

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Realign the satellite">
    Upstairs in the Planning Offices, use the satellite computer. Move the cursor over the yellow dots: each one shows a flag (USA or USSR). Pick the only one that shows a **question mark** and confirm.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'sat.webp'} alt="The satellite screen, with the yellow dots" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get ready, then start the OPC terminal">
    **Warning: there is no going back.** The terminal on the right in the OPC starts a cutscene, then you are sent to the Village courtyard to face the Orda. Before you press it: perks, armor, Pack-a-Punch, Supports, upgraded RAI K-84.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'terminal.webp'} alt="The OPC terminal" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Beat the Orda">
    Shoot the hole where its head should be when it glows orange, and stay away from it. The full strategy is in [Beat the Orda](../boss-orda/).

    When the Orda falls, the ending cutscene plays: Samantha comes out of the Dark Aether. The quest is done.

    {/* TO CHECK: whether the match ends or goes on after the final cutscene: no source says. */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## If you get stuck

- **The Mimic escapes the trap**: it had too much health. Get another trap at the Memory Transference Station.
- **Wrong container in the Open Lot**: wait for the next round.
- **The Barracks container runs away**: you got too close, or the vortex was too far from it.
- A bug that stopped you from catching a Mimic in solo was fixed in February 2021, with Season Two.
