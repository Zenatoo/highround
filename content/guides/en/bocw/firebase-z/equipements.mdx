This guide tells you **where** to find each machine on Firebase Z. How they work (Salvage, item prices, rarities, Trials, exfil) is explained once and for all in [Cold War Zombies basics](../../die-maschine/systemes-cold-war/).

## Crafting tables

The **crafting table** makes equipment (grenades, Supports…) with **Salvage**, the scrap that enemies drop.

- **Village**: in front of the Atrium.
- **Helipad**: where the teleporter drops you.

Firebase Z added two **Supports** to the game, craftable on every map: the **Napalm Strike** (a line of fire that blocks a path) and the **Artillery** (a rain of shells on a group). You call them in by throwing a marker.

## Mystery Box

A spin costs **950 Essence**. A beam of light in the sky shows where it is. It has **six spots**:

| Spot | Where |
| --- | --- |
| **Courtyard** | In the Village courtyard, near the Pack-a-Punch. Never there at the start of a match |
| **Planning Offices** | Coming out of the teleporter, left door, then upstairs |
| **Rocky Defense** | Outside, against the wall, near the Artillery terminal |
| **Motor Pool** | Facing a wrecked truck, on the way back from the Weapon Lab |
| **Barracks** | Through the door next to Tombstone Soda, following the main path |
| **Jungle Defense** | In the area that an explosion opens when you activate the Military Command reactor, the only one outdoors |

{/* Six spots: Charlie Intel and Dexerto (GGRecon rewords Dexerto). The dexerto.fr image caption says "at least 7" but lists the same six. */}

The RAI K-84, the map's wonder weapon, can come out of the box. You can also build it for free: see [Build the RAI K-84](../rai-k-84/).

## Arsenal

The **Arsenal** upgrades the rarity of your weapons and sells armor. There are four:

- **Village**, in the rooms that open with the power;
- **Planning Offices**, with the power;
- **Barracks 1**;
- **Weapon Lab**, at the back of the Motor Pool.

## Trials

The **Trials** computer (paid challenges that give rewards) is on the **Helipad**. It is easier to reach and cheaper than the one on Die Maschine. Some Trials give an Intel.

| Area | Weapon |

| Village, before the Atrium | Magnum (Uncommon) |

| Sky Bridge | Hauer 77 (Rare) |

| Mission Control, outside stairs | M16 (Uncommon); another Uncommon weapon inside |
| Field Hospital, outside | Type 63 (Uncommon) |
| Data Center, stair landing | AK-47 (Uncommon) |
| Data Center, downstairs | An SMG (Rare) |

These are the starting rarities. Since Season Four Reloaded, a wall buy can go up one rarity every 5 rounds.

## Ammo crates

Village road, Helipad (next to the helicopter), Open Lot, Barracks 1, Data Center entrance, Motor Pool garage and Military Command.

## Exfil

Exfil works like on the other maps: from the end of round 10, then every 5 rounds, you can call it at the radio in the starting area. You then run to the exfil zone and hold out until the helicopter arrives. The details are in [Cold War Zombies basics](../../die-maschine/systemes-cold-war/).
