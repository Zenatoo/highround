The base's **jump pads** send you from one defense area to another for **500 Essence** per jump. With this little "Simon says" game, they become **free** for the rest of the match.

## The six jump pads

| Jump pad | It sends you to |
| --- | --- |
| **Jungle Defense** (1) | Scorched Defense |
| **Jungle Defense** (2) | Rocky Defense |
| **Mission Control**, inside | Jungle Defense |
| **Mission Control**, right next to it | Rocky Defense |
| **Data Center** | Scorched Defense |
| **Rocky Defense**, upstairs | Jungle Defense |

## Make them free

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power">
    The jump pads work with the power, and the Pack-a-Punch must be active. See [Turn on the power (the 3 reactors)](../courant/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use each jump pad once">
    Jump at least once with **each of the six** jump pads. It costs 3,000 Essence in total.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'jungledefense1.webp'} alt="First Jungle Defense jump pad" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'jungledefense2.webp'} alt="Second Jungle Defense jump pad" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'missioncontrol1.webp'} alt="First Mission Control jump pad" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'missioncontrol2.webp'} alt="Second Mission Control jump pad" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'datacenter.webp'} alt="The Data Center jump pad" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'rockydefence.webp'} alt="The Rocky Defense jump pad" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the green light">
    One of the jump pads has a **green light** on its left panel. Shoot it: **arrows** show up on its screen.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'greenlight.webp'} alt="The green light on a jump pad's panel" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Move in the direction of the arrows">
    Move your character in the direction of each arrow, fast and without mistakes: **five moves** per jump pad. If you get it right, you are sent to the next jump pad, which now has the green light. Do it again.

    The sequences are **always the same**:

    | Jump pad | Sequence |
    | --- | --- |
    | Jungle Defense | Left, right, left, right, left |
    | Jungle Defense | Up, right, left, up, left |
    | Mission Control | Up, down, up, down, up |
    | Mission Control | Right, down, up, left, right |
    | Data Center | Down, left, right, left, up |
    | Rocky Defense | Up, right, left, right, down |

    {/* TO CHECK: sequences from mmmrkennedy and tracker.gg, which share the same text (one source). MysteryHQ's description (z-kXPM7KDzc) links an image of them, not readable as text. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish all six">
    After the sixth, all jump pads are **free** until the end of the match.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
