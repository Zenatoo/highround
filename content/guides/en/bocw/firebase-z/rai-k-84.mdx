The **RAI K-84** is the wonder weapon of Firebase Z: an energy rifle shaped like an AK.

- **Primary fire**: powerful energy shots, a bit like the Ray Gun.
- **Secondary fire**: it launches an **energy mine**. Shoot it several times with the primary fire: it explodes into a **vortex** that holds enemies in place.

It goes through the Pack-a-Punch. It is also used in the [Main Easter Egg (Maxis Potential)](../easter-egg-principal/), to catch one of the crystals: build it early.

You can also get it from the **Mystery Box** (950 Essence), as a **Trials** reward or in [The bunny chest](../coffre-du-lapin/), but that's luck. Building it is free and certain.

## The parts

You need the blueprint first, then the eye. After that, the three parts (Barrel Assembly, Aetherium Converter, Power Cell) can be done **in any order**, at the same time if you want.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Take the blueprint in the Weapon Lab">
    The **Weapon Lab** is a small room at the back of the **Motor Pool**, south of the Helipad. The blueprint hangs on the wall, to the left of the computer. The weapon's crafting table is right below it.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'blueprint.webp'} alt="The RAI K-84 blueprint in the Weapon Lab" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get the eye in the Scorched Defense">
    Go to the far end of the **Scorched Defense**, the trench area to the north. A tank is burning at the back, with a body leaning against it: it is Dr. Dimitri Kuhlklay. Interact with it to take his **eye**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tank_body.webp'} alt="The body leaning against the burning tank" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the drawer with the Retina Scanner">
    Go back to the Weapon Lab and use the eye on the computer's **Retina Scanner**. A drawer opens under the computer: take the **locker key**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'scanner.webp'} alt="The Weapon Lab Retina Scanner" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Barrel Assembly: open the Barracks lockers">
    In **Barracks 1** and **Barracks 2**, open the lockers with the key. **Mimics** come out (up to three). Kill them: one of them drops the **Barrel Assembly**. If it doesn't drop, keep opening lockers.

    One of the Barracks 2 lockers also holds an Intel, Kuhlklay's journal.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'mimic_locker.webp'} alt="A Barracks locker" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Aetherium Converter: the dartboard">
    In the Weapon Lab, use the computer. The screen shows a **circle cut into slices**: it is a dartboard. A light spins and **stops three times**. Write down the three slices where it stops. They change every match.

    Go back to the Village, go into the Atrium and turn left toward the **Mess Hall**. A **dartboard** hangs on the wall, just to the left of Der Wunderfizz. Shoot **only once** in each of the three slices you wrote down (what counts is the numbers printed on the board, not the rules of the real game), then **in the center**. The board opens and the **Aetherium Converter** falls to the floor.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'dartboard_ref_new_numbered.webp'} alt="The slices of the computer's circle and their numbers on the dartboard" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'dartboard.webp'} alt="The dartboard in the Village" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'aetherium_converter.webp'} alt="The Aetherium Converter fallen from the dartboard" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="GamesRadar" source="https://www.gamesradar.com/cold-war-zombies-firebase-z-wonder-weapon/">
        The sequence lasts 10 to 15 seconds. Take a screenshot at each stop, and stun nearby zombies with a Stun Grenade so you can watch it in peace.
      </Tip>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="GamesRadar" source="https://www.gamesradar.com/cold-war-zombies-firebase-z-wonder-weapon/">
        Hit each slice only once, and fire the four shots quickly one after the other: otherwise it doesn't count.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Power Cell: kill a Mangler">
    **Manglers** (armored zombies with a cannon on their arm) show up around round 15. Kill them: one of them ends up dropping an **Uncharged Power Cell**. If it doesn't drop, wait for the next one.

    {/* TO CHECK: GamesRadar says to shoot the Mangler's arm cannon while it charges, without killing it. mmmrkennedy, tracker.gg, PC Gamer, KontrolFreek and Gfinity all say to kill it. */}

    Bring it back to the Weapon Lab and put it on the charger, on the **right** side of the room, on the table with the big shell. Wait **one full round**, then come back to take it.

    {/* TO CHECK: charge time: one full round (mmmrkennedy, GamesRadar) vs two rounds (PC Gamer) vs "1-2 rounds" (Gfinity). */}

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'uncharged_power_cell.webp'} alt="The empty Power Cell dropped by a Mangler" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'power_cell.webp'} alt="The Weapon Lab charger" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build the weapon">
    With the three parts, go to the crafting table under the blueprint, in the Weapon Lab, and build the RAI K-84.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
