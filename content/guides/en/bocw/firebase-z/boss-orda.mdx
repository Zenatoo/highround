The **Orda** is an "Elder God", a giant monster from the Dark Aether. It is the boss of Firebase Z. You run into it in two ways:

- at the end of the [Main Easter Egg (Maxis Potential)](../easter-egg-principal/), in the Village courtyard;
- during **Assault Rounds**, from **round 30**.

## Where to hit it

It has no head: instead, there is a **hole**. From time to time, this hole **glows orange**: that is its weak spot. Shoot into it at that moment. You can hit it the rest of the time, but it takes less damage. During the quest fight, the characters warn you when it is vulnerable.

## Its attacks

- **Ground slam** when you are too close. It can down you in one hit.
- **Plague clouds** (parasites) thrown from afar. You can shoot them down in the air. If they hit you, they drain your health for a while.
- It **sends Hellhounds** at you.
- According to some sources, it also spits a beam or fireballs.

Meanwhile, normal zombies keep coming.

## The quest fight

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get ready before the OPC terminal">
    Once you press the terminal, there is no going back. Get your perks, armor, a Self-Revive, Supports, and upgrade the **RAI K-84** at the Pack-a-Punch, ideally to tier 3. See [Build the RAI K-84](../rai-k-84/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Stay far away and run in circles">
    The Orda stays in one corner of the courtyard. Never get close. Run laps around the Village: run ahead, stop now and then to kill what's following you and shoot the weak spot, then move on. Don't stay in the same place too long.

    A route that works: go along the teleporter path, then jump onto the upper floor of the Pack-a-Punch building. You can hit the Orda once or twice per lap.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use the Village machines">
    The **Pack-a-Punch**, **Der Wunderfizz**, the **crafting table** and the **Arsenal** stay usable during the fight. It's harder with zombies on your back, but it can save you.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish it off">
    When its health bar is empty, the ending cutscene plays.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## During Assault Rounds

From round 30, the Orda can come with the wave of an Assault Round. The same rules apply: stay far away and aim for the weak spot. Assault Rounds are explained in [Turn on the power (the 3 reactors)](../courant/).

Since November 2021, the Orda is no longer nearly immortal in these rounds with 3 or 4 players: a bug was fixed. Its health also depends on the round: the further you go, the tougher it is.
