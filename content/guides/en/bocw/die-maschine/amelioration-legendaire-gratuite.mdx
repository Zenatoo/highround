In the Dark Aether, a **giant hand** comes out of a door in the Medical Bay and grabs the zombies walking by. After **15 kills**, the weapon you are holding goes straight to **Legendary** rarity (orange), without spending any High-Grade Salvage.

**Before you start:**

- Turn on the power and build Pack-a-Punch: see [Unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
- Pack-a-Punch **the weapon you want to upgrade**: you need a Pack-a-Punched weapon to shoot the buttons, and it is the weapon **in your hands** at the end that becomes Legendary.
- Everything happens **in the Dark Aether**. If you get sent back, go in again and pick up where you left off.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Go to the Medical Bay hallway">
    Go into the Dark Aether through an Anomaly and head to the bottom of the **Medical Bay**. Behind Speed Cola, a **hallway** closed off by a barricade is lined with doors.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'hallway.webp'} alt="The barricaded hallway in the Medical Bay" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the four red buttons">
    Next to four of the hallway doors (two on the left, two on the right), a small **red button** is fixed to the wall. Shoot each one with your Pack-a-Punched weapon: it turns **green**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'buttonneardoor.webp'} alt="A red button next to a hallway door" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bring out the hand">
    When all four are green, the closest door **on the left** lets out a green light. Shoot the **button to the right of that door**: a giant hand comes out and sweeps the zombies walking by for a few seconds.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'glowingdoor.webp'} alt="The door glowing green" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'buttontorightofglowingdoor.webp'} alt="The button to the right of the glowing door" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Let the hand kill 15 zombies">
    After a few grabs, the door closes again. Wait for the green light to come back (about a minute), lure more zombies into the hallway and shoot the button again. Once the hand has killed **15 zombies**, an orb flies to you: the weapon you are holding becomes **Legendary**.

    {/* 15 kills: mmmrkennedy, tracker.gg, COD Zombified, FIRUIN (codzombieguides says 20). Cooldown about one minute: FIRUIN. */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="FIRUIN" source="https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2951105088">
    The hand struggles to catch zombies walking along the right wall, and it rarely takes more than two at a time. Get the zombies to walk down the middle of the hallway, as close to the door as possible.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="COD Zombified" source="https://codzombified.blogspot.com/2020/12/monster-hand-easter-egg-and-legendary.html">
    The hallway is narrow and zombies come from both sides. It is much easier in co-op, or early in the game with a good weapon.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
