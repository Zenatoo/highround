Shoot **five blue orbs** hidden in the Particle Accelerator: zombies perform the **Coffin Dance** for you, the meme of the dancing pallbearers (2020), then a **chest** full of rewards appears.

**Before you start:** turn on the power and build Pack-a-Punch. See [Unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/). The orbs are always in the same spots.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'orb2.webp'} alt="Second orb, in the barricade under the stairs" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Orb 3: on an air vent">

    <Screenshot src={m + 'orb3.webp'} alt="Third orb, on an air vent near the Weapons Lab" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Orb 4: behind the grate in the Main Power wall">
    <Screenshot src={m + 'orb4.webp'} alt="Fourth orb, behind the grate in the Main Power wall" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Orb 5: behind the machines, to the right of the stairs">
    Under the Armory.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'orb5.webp'} alt="Fifth orb, behind the machines to the right of the stairs" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Watch the dance and open the chest">
    After the fifth orb, you are taken into the Dark Aether, in the dark version of the Particle Accelerator: the zombies dance. At the end of the scene, a **chest** appears near the Pack-a-Punch. It holds:

    - **Salvage** and **High-Grade Salvage**;
    - a **free Jugger-Nog** bottle, for **one player only**;
    - a **random weapon**. If you are lucky, it is a **Ray Gun** or the **D.I.E. Shockwave**.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="FIRUIN" source="https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2951105088">
    Do it as soon as Pack-a-Punch is built: you get Jugger-Nog early in the game, without paying for it.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="FIRUIN" source="https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2951105088">
    During the dance, you can walk around the Dark Aether and break Aetherium crystals to pick up items.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
