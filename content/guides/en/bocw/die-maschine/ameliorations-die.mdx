The D.I.E. Shockwave has **four elemental versions**. Each one is picked up from a **Weapon Crate** after a short quest. Once a crate is open, you can swap your D.I.E. for its version there.

| Version | Element | Crate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nova-5** | Toxic gas | Crash Site |
| **Cryo Emitter** | Ice | Medical Bay |
| **Electrobolt** | Electricity | Particle Accelerator |
| **Thermophasic** | Fire | Pond |

You need all **four** for the main Easter Egg. See [Main Easter Egg (Seal the Deal)](../easter-egg-principal/).

**Before you start:**

- You need the D.I.E. See [Get the D.I.E. Shockwave](../die-shockwave/).
- **Nova-5** and **Cryo Emitter** can be done right away.
- **Electrobolt** and **Thermophasic** only open up after the first three steps of the main Easter Egg: the Aetherscope, the journal and the reflections, then the Medical Bay computer. Before that, their Anomalies are not there.

## Nova-5 (toxic)

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Pull in the empty canister">
    Upstairs in the Nacht der Untoten building, in the **Mezzanine**, an **Empty Canister** sits in a corner, out of reach. Aim at it with the D.I.E.'s **secondary fire**: it flies over to you. Pick it up.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'canister.webp'} alt="The empty canister out of reach, upstairs in the building" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill it with Plaguehound gas">
    In the **Weapons Lab**, downstairs, near Deadshot Daiquiri, place the canister in the **Cleaning Unit**, the machine on the wall. Kill a **Plaguehound** (toxic dog) right next to it: the machine sucks in its gas. When the canister is full, take it back.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'canisterunit.webp'} alt="The Cleaning Unit in the Weapons Lab" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Free the Crash Site crate">
    At the **Crash Site**, place the canister on the vine-covered **Weapon Crate**, near the plane's cockpit. Knife the canister: the gas destroys the vines. Take the **Nova-5**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'nova_crate.webp'} alt="The vine-covered Weapon Crate at the Crash Site" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Cryo Emitter (ice)

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Knock down the flask">
    Go up to the **Penthouse**, at the top of the Nacht building. On the Pond side, a **wooden crate** is balanced on the ledge. Shoot it with the D.I.E. (main fire): it falls and breaks below, at the Pond. Go down and pick up the **Empty Flask**.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'flaskbox_unbroken.webp'} alt="The wooden crate on the Penthouse ledge" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'flaskbox_broken.webp'} alt="The broken crate and the flask" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Charge the mushroom with a Megaton">
    At the **Pond**, a **mushroom** grows on a tree. Stand near the tree when a **Megaton** is around: its ranged attack (a green liquid) has to hit the mushroom. It turns **purple**.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'fungus.webp'} alt="The mushroom on the Pond tree" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'activated_fungus.webp'} alt="The mushroom after it turned purple" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill the flask">
    Place the flask under the mushroom and wait about **one minute**, then take it back, full.

    {/* 60 s: mmmrkennedy; "a minute or two": FIRUIN. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Medical Bay crate">
    In the **Medical Bay**, use the flask on the chained **Weapon Crate**: the cold breaks the chains. Take the **Cryo Emitter**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'cryo_crate.webp'} alt="The chained Weapon Crate in the Medical Bay" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Electrobolt (electric)

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Enter the Anomaly at the bottom of the Particle Accelerator">
    After the Easter Egg computer, an **Anomaly** appears at the very bottom of the **Particle Accelerator**. Go in: you are in the Dark Aether, for a limited time.

    Switch back to the base version of the D.I.E. (Shockwave): the other versions struggle with this step.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'elec_portal.webp'} alt="The Anomaly at the very bottom of the Particle Accelerator" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Suck the energy out of the three crystals, one at a time">
    Three **glowing crystals** can only be seen in the Dark Aether. Aim at one with the D.I.E.'s **secondary fire** to suck in its energy. You can only carry **one at a time**.

    - **Crash Site**, to the left of Jugger-Nog;
    - **Penthouse**, to the right of Der Wunderfizz;
    - **Pond**, to the right of the Mystery Box.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'elec_jug_rock.webp'} alt="Crash Site crystal, to the left of Jugger-Nog" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'elec_wunder_rock.webp'} alt="Penthouse crystal, to the right of Der Wunderfizz" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'elec_box_rock.webp'} alt="Pond crystal, to the right of the Mystery Box" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fire each charge into the crate">
    After each crystal, go back to the **Particle Accelerator** and shoot the **Weapon Crate** with the D.I.E. (main fire). One of the crate's crystals lights up. Do the same with the other two, then take the **Electrobolt**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'elec_crate.webp'} alt="The Weapon Crate in the Particle Accelerator" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="FIRUIN" source="https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2951105088">
        You probably won't have time to make all three round trips in a single visit. No problem: a charge you sucked in can be fired into the crate from the normal world. Go back into the Dark Aether for the next crystal.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Thermophasic (fire)

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Enter the Pond Anomaly">
    After the Easter Egg computer, an **Anomaly** appears at the **Pond**. Go into the Dark Aether.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pond_anomaly.webp'} alt="The Pond Anomaly" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the fuse at the Crash Site">
    At the **Crash Site**, under the nose of the plane, near the Aether Tunnel, a gray **fuse box** sits on the ground. Shoot it or hit it, then take the **fuse**.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'fuse_crashsite.webp'} alt="Where the box is at the Crash Site" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'fuse.webp'} alt="The fuse box" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put it in the Plasma Cutter">
    Go to the **Weapons Lab** and place the fuse in the **Plasma Cutter**. The game sends you back to the normal world.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'plasmacutter.webp'} alt="The Plasma Cutter in the Weapons Lab" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Pond crate">
    Go back to the **Pond**: the **Weapon Crate** on the old truck opens. Take the **Thermophasic**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'firebox.webp'} alt="The Weapon Crate on the Pond truck" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="FIRUIN" source="https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2951105088">
    Prepare everything early: take the fuse during your first trip into the Dark Aether, and get the first Megaton to hit the mushroom. All that's left then is to go to the crates.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="PC Gamer" source="https://www.pcgamer.com/cod-cold-war-zombies-easter-egg-steps-die-maschine/">
    For the final fight of the Easter Egg, the Electrobolt is the most effective version.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
