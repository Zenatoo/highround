In the Dark Aether, some **zombies float in the air**. Shoot them all down: a last one appears above the Pond. Shoot it and it drops a free **scorestreak** (a support item). Stand under it without shooting, and you get a **jumpscare**.

**Before you start:** turn on the power and build Pack-a-Punch. See [Unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/). Everything happens **in the Dark Aether**, through any Anomaly. If you get sent back, go in again and carry on.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Shoot down the five floating zombies">
    A zombie floats at **one of these five spots**. Shoot it: it disappears, and the next one appears somewhere else. The order changes every game.

    - **Particle Accelerator**: outside the barrier, on the Weapons Lab side;
    - **Control Room**: in the water, under the walkway to the Medical Bay (light up the water with your flashlight);
    - **Pond**: near the trees, to the left of the Tunnel door;
    - **Crash Site**: in the trees, above the plane's tail;
    - **Yard**: near the trees, to the left of the Living Room door.

  <Step title="Choose: reward or scare">
    Go to the **Pond**: a last zombie floats in the middle.

    - **Shoot it**: a random scorestreak drops to the ground below it (anything from the Combat Bow to the Chopper Gunner).
    - **Stand under it without shooting**: a scary face pops up on screen. It is just a scare.

  </Step>
