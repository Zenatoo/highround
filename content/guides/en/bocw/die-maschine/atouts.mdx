Die Maschine has **six perk machines** and a **Der Wunderfizz** machine. They all work once the **power** is on: see [Turn on the power](../courant/).

The price doesn't depend on the perk: **2,500 Essence** for your first, then **500 more** for each one after that. There is no limit. The effects and tiers of each perk are in [Cold War Zombies basics](../systemes-cold-war/).

| Perk | Where |
| --- | --- |
| **Jugger-Nog** | Crash Site, to the right of the plane wing stuck in the ground |
| **Quick Revive** | Pond |
| **Stamin-Up** | Bedroom, upstairs in the Nacht der Untoten building, next to the wall that opens onto the Crash Site |
| **Speed Cola** | Medical Bay, downstairs |
| **Deadshot Daiquiri** | Weapons Lab, downstairs, near the Cleaning Unit |
| **Elemental Pop** | Main Power room, the one with the power button |
| **Der Wunderfizz** | Penthouse, on the roof of the Nacht der Untoten building |

## Der Wunderfizz

Der Wunderfizz sells **every perk**: you choose the one you want, no randomness. Perks you can't afford appear grayed out. It is the only way to get, on Die Maschine, the four perks added after the map came out:

| Perk | Available since |
| --- | --- |
| **Tombstone Soda** | 4 February 2021 |
| **Mule Kick** | 15 July 2021 |
| **Death Perception** | 12 August 2021 |
| **PhD Slider** | 7 October 2021 |

None of our sources confirms its price.

The machine is in the **Penthouse**, at the top of the Nacht der Untoten building. When the map launched, you could only use it **from the Dark Aether**: go in through an Anomaly, climb to the Penthouse and buy before you get sent back. See [The Dark Aether and its anomalies](../ether-sombre/).
