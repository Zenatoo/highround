Three **satellite dishes** sit on top of pylons around the map. Turn them all toward the **Penthouse antenna**, on the roof of the Nacht der Untoten building: three Essence canisters appear, **1,500 Essence** in total.

**Before you start:**

- Turn on the power and build Pack-a-Punch: see [Unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
- Put the **Dead Wire** ammo mod (electric) on a weapon at the Pack-a-Punch (2,000 Essence). The dishes only turn with it. A **sniper rifle** is ideal: they are far away.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Spot the antenna">
    Every dish has to aim at the **antenna** on the roof, in the Penthouse. Look at it once so you know where to aim.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'penthouse.webp'} alt="The antenna on the Penthouse roof" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn the three dishes">
    Shoot each dish with your Dead Wire weapon: it turns a little each time. When it faces the right way, a **blue spark** shoots out of it.

    - **Yard**: stand in front of the tank, look in the direction of its barrel, then turn about 45° to the right. The pylon is in the distance.
    - **Crash Site**: behind Jugger-Nog, in the forest. Stand between the rock and the plane wing stuck in the ground.
    - **Pond**: behind Quick Revive, in the forest. Facing the machine, turn about 45° to the right.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'crashsite_behind_jug.webp'} alt="The dish behind Jugger-Nog, at the Crash Site" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pond_behind_quick_revive.webp'} alt="The dish behind Quick Revive, at the Pond" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the Essence">
    When all three dishes aim at the antenna, lightning links them to the roof. Go up to the **Penthouse**: **three Essence canisters** have appeared near Der Wunderfizz. Pick them up: 500 each.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'essence_canisters.webp'} alt="The three Essence canisters near Der Wunderfizz" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Right after, a voice reads out a series of numbers. According to tracker.gg, it is the key to read a coded piece of intel ("G Cipher") dropped by Megatons.
