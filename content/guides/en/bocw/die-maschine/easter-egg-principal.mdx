Projekt Endstation, Poland, 1983. The facility's particle accelerator has torn a rift into the **Dark Aether**. The goal of the Easter Egg: capture and purify a Megaton to give **Orlov** his memory back, so he can shut down the accelerator and close the rift. At the end, you escape by helicopter and the "Seal the Deal" achievement unlocks.

**Before you start:**

- Count on **40 minutes to 1 hour** solo. It is one of the simplest Easter Eggs in the series.
- The steps are the same solo and in co-op.
- Turn on the **power** and build **Pack-a-Punch**: see [Turn on the power](../courant/) and [Unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
- Many steps take place in the Dark Aether, where your time is limited: see [The Dark Aether and its anomalies](../ether-sombre/).
- Rewards: the "Seal the Deal" achievement, the "Evil Unleashed" Dark Ops challenge, and since May 2021 as many Aetherium crystals as the last possible exfil.

## The Aetherscope and Vogel's journal

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Pick up the three Aetherscope parts">
    Go into the Dark Aether through any Anomaly. The three **Aetherscope** parts appear **one at a time**, in a random order:

    - **Particle Accelerator**, near the Aether Tunnel;
    - **Yard**, under the concrete stairs, near the Rampage Inducer;
    - **Crash Site**, on the plane wreckage, near the cockpit.

    <Shots>

      <Screenshot src={m + 'scope_spawn.webp'} alt="Aetherscope part under the Yard stairs" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'scope_crashsite.webp'} alt="Aetherscope part on the plane wreckage" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build the Aetherscope">
    Back in the normal world, go to the **crafting table behind the Pack-a-Punch**, in the Particle Accelerator, and build the Aetherscope. Keep it: without it, you can't see the journal.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'scope_bench.webp'} alt="The crafting table behind the Pack-a-Punch" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take Vogel's journal">
    Go into the Dark Aether through the **Medical Bay Anomaly**. Upstairs in the Medical Bay, in the computer room, take **Dr. Vogel's journal** from the desk.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'reflect_anomaly.webp'} alt="The Medical Bay Anomaly" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'reflect_book.webp'} alt="Vogel's journal on the desk" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give the journal to the three reflections">
    Still in the Dark Aether, three **blue orbs** bring up **Spectral Reflections**, ghosts from the past. For each one: use the orb, then use the ghost to give it the journal. Do them **in this order**:

    1. **Medical Bay**, downstairs, behind Speed Cola;
    2. **Control Room**, between two Trials podiums;
    3. **Particle Accelerator**, in front of the Aetherscope crafting table.

    No need to listen to each scene until the end: move on to the next one. If the Dark Aether sends you back before you are done, go in again and carry on.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'reflect_1.webp'} alt="First reflection, behind Speed Cola" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'reflect_2.webp'} alt="Second reflection, between two Control Room podiums" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'reflect_3.webp'} alt="Third reflection, in front of the Aetherscope crafting table" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the password on the computer">
    In the normal world, go back to the Medical Bay **computer**, where the journal was. Hold the action button: it enters the password on its own. The big machine on the ceiling, behind the glass, wakes up.

    Do this step **before** the D.I.E. upgrades: it makes the Electrobolt and Thermophasic Anomalies appear.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'reflect_comp.webp'} alt="The Medical Bay computer" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Getting Orlov ready

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get the four D.I.E. versions">
    You need the D.I.E. Shockwave and its four versions: Nova-5, Cryo Emitter, Electrobolt and Thermophasic. See [Get the D.I.E. Shockwave](../die-shockwave/) and [The 4 D.I.E. upgrades](../ameliorations-die/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the machine's four ports">
    Downstairs in the Medical Bay, look up: the ceiling machine has **four colored ports**. Shoot each one with the right D.I.E. version. A port you got right goes back up to the ceiling and stays there.

    | Port | Version |
    | --- | --- |
    | Green | Nova-5 |
    | Blue | Cryo Emitter |
    | Yellow | Electrobolt |
    | Orange | Thermophasic |

    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get the Dark Aether Wrench">
    A new **Anomaly** appears in the Medical Bay, on the Particle Accelerator side. Go in and watch the reflection on the walkway above Speed Cola. When the scene ends, you return to the normal world: pick up the **Dark Aether Wrench** from the ground, where the reflection was.

  <Step title="Repair the Yard tank">
    Go to the **tank** in the Yard and use the wrench **three times**. A zombie climbs out of the hatch: kill it, then throw an explosive into the open hatch (a Semtex stuck inside, a grenade or C4). The tank fires at a tree at the Crash Site.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tank.webp'} alt="The Yard tank" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bring back the decontamination agent">
    At the **Crash Site**, at the foot of the fallen tree, pick up the **decontamination agent**, a golden ball. Carry it to the Medical Bay's **Containment Unit**, where you found the wrench, and place it in the machine that glows red.

    While you carry it, you can't shoot, sprint, climb or take an Aether Tunnel. Switching weapons drops it: you can clear the zombies and then pick it back up. If it stays on the ground, it goes back to the tree.

    {/* Drop rules: mmmrkennedy (respawns at the tree if dropped), COD Zombified (swap weapons to drop it and fight). */}

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'agent_floor.webp'} alt="The decontamination agent at the foot of the tree" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'containment_unit.webp'} alt="The Medical Bay Containment Unit" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get a split Megaton sucked in">
    Plaguehounds show up when you place the agent. Then, wait for a **Megaton**. Bring its health down so it splits in two, then lure the **Megaton Blaster** and the **Megaton Bomber** under the ceiling machine: it sucks in each half, one after the other. They have to walk into the small circle on the ground, under the machine.

    Both halves have to be **alive** under the machine. Careful with the D.I.E.: it can kill both of them in a single shot.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'machine.webp'} alt="The Medical Bay ceiling machine" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start the purification">
    Go back to the Medical Bay **computer** and use it (in co-op, **every player** has to use it). A cutscene starts, then a short **lockdown**: stay in the room until the purification is done. After that, lots of Megatons, Plaguehounds and zombies arrive: head out to an open area.

    {/* All players must interact: COD Zombified, Gameranx. Lockdown: COD Zombified ("mini lockdown"), MrDalekJD chapter "Mini Lockdown". */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The last reflection and the final fight

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get ready">
    The final fight locks you in the Particle Accelerator. Before you start the next part: buy your perks, upgrade your weapons, craft Cymbal Monkeys, turrets, a War Machine and Stimshots. The Electrobolt is the most effective D.I.E. version for this fight.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Talk to Orlov and take the photo">
    An **Anomaly** appears in the **Living Room** of the Nacht der Untoten building. Go in: in the Dark Aether, the room is called **Omega Outpost**. Use the reflection in the corner, near the **M1911** wall buy. When the scene ends, you return to the normal world: the **Orlov family photo** is on the table nearby.

    **Picking it up starts the final fight.**

    <Screenshot src={m + 'final_orlov_portal.webp'} alt="The Living Room Anomaly" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Protect Orlov">
    Everyone is teleported to the **Particle Accelerator**. Orlov, as a ghost, shuts down the accelerator in **three stages**: Magnetic Containment, Primary Coolant, then Fail-Safe (the last one under the Pack-a-Punch, the tightest spot). A bar at the top left shows his progress.

    Orlov **can't die**, but he stops as long as zombies are hitting him. Your only job: survive and keep enemies away from him. Armored zombies, Megatons and Plaguehounds keep coming.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Escape to the Pond in 90 seconds">
    After a short dialogue, a **90-second** timer starts. **Deadly lightning** bursts out of the ground: don't touch it. There is only one way:

    1. Weapons Lab;
    2. Control Room;
    3. Tunnel;
    4. Crash Site;
    5. **Pond**: get in the helicopter.

    You are done: "Seal the Deal" unlocks.

    {/* Escape to the Pond: mmmrkennedy, tracker.gg, COD Zombified, FIRUIN (Gameranx says the Yard: wrong). */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## What can go wrong

- **The decontamination agent** drops and stays on the ground: it goes back to the foot of the Crash Site tree.
- **The Dark Aether sends you back** before a step is done: go in again and pick up where you left off.
- **The Anomalies are not in the right place**: you did the D.I.E. upgrades before the computer. Do the password first.
- **Both Megaton halves die** before they are under the machine: wait for the next Megaton.
- **Too slow on the escape**, or hit by lightning: the escape fails.
