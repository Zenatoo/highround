Three **cassette tapes** are hidden on the map. Use all three: the third one starts "**Alone**", composed by **Kevin Sherwood** and sung by **Clark S. Nova**. You don't need the power for the first one, but the other two are in the facility: you need to have opened the way to the Particle Accelerator (see [Turn on the power](../courant/)).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Living Room">
    On the ground floor of the Nacht der Untoten building, in the **overturned fridge**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tape1.webp'} alt="The tape in the overturned fridge in the Living Room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Medical Bay">
    On a **shelf**, near a wooden crate.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tape2.webp'} alt="The tape on a shelf in the Medical Bay" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Particle Accelerator">
    Sitting **on some machines**, under the accelerator ring.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tape3.webp'} alt="The tape on some machines in the Particle Accelerator" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Once unlocked, you can listen to the song again in the game's music player.
