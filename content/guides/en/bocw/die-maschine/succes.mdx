Die Maschine is the **only map** in Cold War with its own trophies (achievements on Xbox and PC): **7**, plus **2** Zombies trophies you can earn on any map. Firebase Z, Mauer der Toten and Forsaken have none. Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device. The names are the ones from the English version of the game.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Heavy Metal (bronze)">
    Rebuild Pack-a-Punch. See [Unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Carpe D.I.E.M. (bronze)">
    Free the D.I.E. Shockwave: the Living Room door blows open and you take the weapon. See [Get the D.I.E. Shockwave](../die-shockwave/). Do it early: the D.I.E. makes several other trophies easy.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Seal the Deal (silver)">
    Close the rift: this is the main Easter Egg. See [Main Easter Egg (Seal the Deal)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Two Birds, One Stone (bronze)">
    Kill both halves of a **Megaton** with a single shot. The first Megaton arrives between rounds 6 and 10, the next one between 11 and 15. While it splits in two, the halves are invincible: wait until it is done, then fire once with the **D.I.E.** when they are close to each other.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Nightmare at 20 Feet (silver)">
    Kill **100 enemies** without stepping off the **plane wing** stuck in the ground at the Crash Site. Go all the way to the end. Zombies can climb up the sides, but not from behind. In co-op, your teammates' kills count if they are on the wing too. With the D.I.E., it is quick.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Entamaphobia (silver)">
    Survive **15 rounds** in a row in the **Yard**, the starting area: don't open any door and don't leave. The Yard has an ammo crate and a crafting table. Take a shotgun or a machine gun at the start, and run in circles around the courtyard. In co-op, tell everyone not to open anything.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Craftwork (silver)">
    Craft **14 different items** at the crafting table in a single game. At launch, the crafting table offered exactly 14: the 4 tactical, the 5 lethal and 5 support items (Combat Bow, Sentry Turret, War Machine, Chopper Gunner and Self-Revive). The Self-Revive requires player **level 54**. You need a lot of Salvage: aim for high rounds, ideally in co-op. The prices are in [Cold War Zombies basics](../systemes-cold-war/).

    {/* TO CHECK: whether Support items added later (Cruise Missile, Napalm Strike, Artillery, Death Machine...) count towards the 14 types. No source says. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Socket-to-me (bronze)">
    Upgrade a skill once, in the Skills menu, with Aetherium crystals. Works on every map.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Six Pack (bronze)">
    Raise **six perks** to tier III in the Skills. Works on every map. This is the longest one: you need a lot of crystals. A game up to round 15 gives you two.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
