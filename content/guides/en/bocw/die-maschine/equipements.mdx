Where to find, on Die Maschine, everything you need to gear up: crafting tables, Mystery Box, Arsenal, ammo, Trials and the exfil zone. The prices and rules, shared by every map, are in [Cold War Zombies basics](../systemes-cold-war/).

## Crafting tables

There are **three**, one on the surface and two underground:

- **Yard**, the starting area, against the Nacht der Untoten building;
- **Control Room**, in the facility;
- **Particle Accelerator**, behind the Pack-a-Punch. This is also where you build the Aetherscope for the main Easter Egg.

{/* Count: tracker.gg map guide (1 surface + 2 underground). Yard: COD Zombified, Gamer Journalist, PowerPyx. Control Room: COD Zombified. Particle Accelerator: mmmrkennedy ("workbench behind Pack-a-Punch"), PC Gamer and FIRUIN (Aetherscope built at the crafting table there). */}

## The Mystery Box

A pull costs **950 Essence**. A beam of light in the sky shows where it is.

{/* TO CHECK: tracker.gg counts 6 spawns (3 surface + 3 underground), but the sources name 7 places: Yard, Nacht building, Weapons Lab, Particle Accelerator (COD Zombified), Crash Site (COD Zombified, DiamondLobby, GINX), Pond (mmmrkennedy and FIRUIN: Electrobolt crystal next to it), Medical Bay (tracker.gg main quest: port "nearest to the Mystery Box"). */}

According to tracker.gg, it has **six spots**, three on the surface and three underground. Only two are confirmed by several sources:

- **Crash Site**: between the tail and the body of the big plane, on the left, next to Jugger-Nog;
- **Pond**.

The others are each given by a single source only: we will add them once they are checked.

## The Arsenal

There is **only one**, in the **Particle Accelerator**. It turns on with the power. You use it to upgrade your weapons' rarity and to buy armor. See the prices in [Cold War Zombies basics](../systemes-cold-war/).

## Ammo and wall buys

There are **six ammo crates**: four on the surface, two underground. According to DiamondLobby, refilling both your weapons costs **250 Essence**.

**To check:** only the M1911, the MP5 and the Gallo SA12 are confirmed by two sources. The other rows only come from COD Zombified's maps.

Since July 2021, a wall buy can gain a rarity every 5 rounds: its color and price can therefore change during the game.

## The Trials

The **Trials** computer is at the back of the **Control Room**. Each challenge costs **500 Essence**. The rewards appear on **four podiums** in the same room. Some Trials also give Intel about the map's story.

## Exfil

From the end of round 10, then every 5 rounds, the **Yard radio** lets you call an exfil. The helicopter lands at the **Pond**, near Quick Revive. This is also where the main Easter Egg ends.

{/* Exfil at the Pond by Quick Revive: GamesRadar, FIRUIN ("the pond, where the evacuation usually takes place"); main quest ends there per mmmrkennedy and tracker.gg. */}

## The Rampage Inducer

Since September 2021, the orange canister that speeds up the rounds (up to round 55 since November 2021) is in the **Yard**, near the concrete stairs. See [Cold War Zombies basics](../systemes-cold-war/).
