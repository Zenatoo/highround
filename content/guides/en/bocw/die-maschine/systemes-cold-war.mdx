Cold War changes a lot compared to older Zombies: weapons with a rarity, armor, materials to pick up, no perk limit, and an exfil you can call mid-game. These rules are **the same on all four** round-based maps (Die Maschine, Firebase Z, Mauer der Toten and Forsaken). This guide explains them once and for all.

It describes the game **in its final state**, after Season Six (October 2021). Videos from 2020 sometimes show prices or rules that have changed since. There is **no guided mode** in Cold War.

## Before the game: loadout and skills

You start with **one weapon from your class** (Common rarity, with its attachments) and a **Field Upgrade**, a special ability that recharges as you kill zombies (see below).

Between games, the **Skills** menu lets you permanently upgrade your perks, Field Upgrades, ammo mods and weapon classes. You pay with **Aetherium crystals**:

| Tiers | Crystal |
| --- | --- |
| I to III | **Raw** Aetherium |
| IV | **Refined** Aetherium |
| V | **Flawless** Aetherium |

{/* TO CHECK: tier I = 1, II = 2, III = 3 Raw crystals: PowerPyx only (launch). Refined at round 20 and Flawless at round 35: GamesHedge only. */}

According to PowerPyx, tiers I, II and III cost 1, 2 and then 3 Raw crystals. You earn crystals by finishing certain rounds (one after round 10, another after round 15, then more as you go higher) and by completing an **exfil**. Since May 2021, finishing a map's main quest gives as many crystals as the last possible exfil.

## Essence

The currency is called **Essence**. You earn it by killing zombies and completing objectives, and you spend it on doors, wall buys, the Mystery Box, perks and Pack-a-Punch. You start every game with **500 Essence**.

## Salvage and the crafting table

Enemies drop two kinds of materials:

- **Salvage** (green), 50 per pickup;
- **High-Grade Salvage** (blue), 10 per pickup, rarer. Stronger enemies and higher rounds give more.

You also get some at certain rounds: at least 450 High-Grade Salvage in total before the end of round 20.

Salvage is used at the **Arsenal** (below) and at the **crafting table**, where you make your equipment. There is always a crafting table in the starting area.

| Category | Item | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tactical | Stimshot (heal) | 100 Salvage |
| Tactical | Stun Grenade | 250 Salvage |
| Tactical | Decoy | 500 Salvage |
| Tactical | Cymbal Monkey | 1,000 Salvage |
| Lethal | Tomahawk | 100 Salvage |
| Lethal | Frag, Semtex | 250 Salvage |
| Lethal | Molotov, C4 | 500 Salvage |
| Support | Combat Bow | 50 High-Grade |
| Support | Sentry Turret, War Machine | 100 High-Grade |
| Support | Chopper Gunner | 250 High-Grade |
| Support | Self-Revive | 250 High-Grade, +50 for each new purchase |

{/* Prices: GINX + Gamer Journalist (+ Dexerto for Bow and Chopper Gunner). Support paid in High-Grade: GINX + Steam guide 3067282668. Self-Revive +50 per craft: Treyarch 20 May 2021. */}

- The Self-Revive requires player **level 54**.
- Other support items came with the seasons: Cruise Missile, Napalm Strike, Artillery, Death Machine, Flamethrower, Hand Cannon and ARC-XD. Their prices are not confirmed by our sources. The **LT53 Kazimir** tactical grenade (it opens a black hole that sucks zombies in) can be crafted on every map since July 2021.
- While you control a support item that takes over your view (the Chopper Gunner, for example), zombies ignore you.

## The Arsenal: weapon rarity and armor

The **Arsenal** turns on with the power. It upgrades your weapon's **rarity**, sells and repairs **armor**, and can change attachments. Each rarity raises damage and the number of attachments, and it stacks with Pack-a-Punch.

| Rarity | Color | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Common | White | (starting weapon) |
| Uncommon | Green | 500 Salvage |
| Rare | Blue | 1,000 Salvage |
| Epic | Purple | 500 High-Grade |
| Legendary | Orange | 1,000 High-Grade |

Armor replaces the old shield: it absorbs part of the hits. **Armor Shards** dropped by enemies refill it a little, and the Carpenter power-up refills it completely.

| Armor | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | 500 Salvage |
| Tier 2 | 1,000 Salvage |
| Tier 3 | 250 High-Grade |
| Repair | 250 Salvage |

{/* Arsenal and armor prices: Steam guide 3067282668 + Sportskeeda (+ GameSpot for Epic and Legendary). */}

Upgrading your armor by one tier also refills it: no need to repair it just before. If nobody has armor, the first Armor Shard of the game is replaced by a full tier 1 armor.

Since July 2021, **wall buys** can gain a rarity at the end of every round that is a multiple of 5, up to Legendary.

## Pack-a-Punch and ammo mods

Each map has its own quest to unlock **Pack-a-Punch**. Once it is set up, it works the same everywhere:

| Upgrade | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Level 1 | 5,000 Essence |
| Level 2 | 15,000 Essence |
| Level 3 | 30,000 Essence |
| Ammo mod | 2,000 Essence |

Each level adds damage and a camo. The **ammo mod** gives your bullets an elemental effect. You buy it in a separate tab of the machine. Its tiers are upgraded in the Skills.

{/* TO CHECK: whether every ammo mod is offered at the machine or only the one in the loadout (GamesRadar: "upgrade your Ammo Mods from the Loadout menu"; Steam guide 3067282668: an "ammo mod" tab at the machine). */}

| Ammo mod | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Napalm Burst** | Sets enemies on fire |
| **Dead Wire** | Electrocutes and stuns |
| **Cryofreeze** | Slows enemies down |
| **Brain Rot** | Turns a zombie into an ally for a while |
| **Shatter Blast** (since February 2021) | Explosion that breaks enemy armor |

At tier IV, the first four also work on special enemies. At tier V, the effect spreads to several enemies around.

## Perks

There are **10 perks** in the final game. Their price doesn't depend on the perk, but on **how many you already have**: **2,500 Essence** for the first, then **500 more** each time (3,000, 3,500…). There is **no limit**: you can have them all.

{/* Price rule: TV Tropes, TheGamer, Charlie Intel (2,500 on most machines), COD Zombified (2,500 then 3,000 on Die Maschine). No limit: Treyarch Season Six notes, Charlie Intel. */}

When you go down, you lose your perks one by one.

| Perk | Base effect | Added |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Jugger-Nog** | +50 max health | Launch |
| **Quick Revive** | Faster health regen, revive allies faster | Launch |
| **Speed Cola** | Reload faster | Launch |
| **Stamin-Up** | Run and sprint faster | Launch |
| **Deadshot Daiquiri** | Aim snaps to weak spots, no scope sway | Launch |
| **Elemental Pop** | Each bullet can trigger a random ammo mod | Launch |
| **Tombstone Soda** | When downed, you become an armed shadow in the Dark Aether and can get back up | February 2021 |
| **Mule Kick** | A third weapon | July 2021 |
| **Death Perception** | Enemies visible through walls | August 2021 |
| **PhD Slider** | Sliding into enemies causes an explosion | October 2021 |

Each map has its own machines. Perks added after a map came out are bought on it at **Der Wunderfizz** (below).

### What the tiers give

Tiers I to V are bought in the Skills. A few notable effects:

- **Jugger-Nog**: III raises the bonus to +100 health; V leaves you at 1 HP with your armor emptied, instead of downing you.
- **Quick Revive**: III fully heals you when you revive an ally; V revives you if you kill an enemy while you are down (you then lose the perk).
- **Speed Cola**: II recharges Field Upgrades 20% faster; V lets you reload and fire while sprinting.
- **Stamin-Up**: II removes fall damage; V removes the sprint slowdown.
- **Deadshot Daiquiri**: II adds 50% damage against armor; V raises damage with each hit on the same enemy.
- **Elemental Pop**: II reduces the ammo mod cooldown; V releases an electric blast when you reload.
- **Tombstone Soda**: III leaves a stash with your weapons and resources when you die; V keeps your other perks when you go down.
- **Mule Kick**: II sometimes drops ammo; V gives back the third weapon when you buy the perk again.
- **Death Perception**: III adds 20% to the Salvage you pick up; V shows chests and power-ups through walls.
- **PhD Slider**: III makes you immune to your own explosives; V creates an explosion when you fall from high up.

### Der Wunderfizz

**Der Wunderfizz** is a machine that sells several perks: you **choose** the one you want (no randomness). Perks you can't afford appear grayed out. It is how Tombstone Soda, Mule Kick, Death Perception and PhD Slider came to the older maps. None of our sources confirms its price.

## Field Upgrades

Only one in your loadout. It recharges as you kill zombies; the more defensive ones need more kills.

| Field Upgrade | Effect | Added |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Frost Blast** | Frozen blast: slowed enemies take more damage | Launch |
| **Healing Aura** | Heals you and your allies; upgraded, revives from a distance | Launch |
| **Energy Mine** | Explosive mine | Launch |
| **Ring of Fire** | Circle that raises your damage and burns zombies | Launch |
| **Aether Shroud** | Makes you invisible for a few seconds | Launch |
| **Frenzied Guard** | Draws every enemy to you, your armor takes the hits | February 2021 |
| **Toxic Growth** | Plants that slow and poison | April 2021 |
| **Tesla Storm** | Lightning between you and your allies that stuns zombies | August 2021 |

## Mystery Box, Trials and power-ups

- **Mystery Box**: **950 Essence**. A beam of light marks it; it moves after a few pulls. The weapon you swap stays on the ground in front of the box: you can take it back. The higher the round, the rarer the weapons. It can also give a support item.
- **Trials**: a computer offers challenges at **500 Essence** each. Chain them without collecting the reward to raise it from Common to Legendary (weapons, perks, power-ups, intel, often a special weapon at Legendary).
- **Power-ups**: Max Ammo, Insta-Kill, Double Points, Nuke, Carpenter (also repairs armor), Fire Sale, Death Machine and **Full Power** (recharges your Field Upgrade). Most last about 30 seconds.

## Exfil

At the end of round 10, then every 5 rounds (15, 20…), you can call an **exfil** at the radio in the starting area. In co-op, the majority must vote yes. You then have to reach the exfil zone before the timer runs out while zombies pour in, then clear the zone so the helicopter can land. Success gives experience and Aetherium crystals, more the higher the round.

## The Rampage Inducer

Since September 2021, an orange canister in the starting area of every map lets you **vote to speed up** the rounds: more zombies, faster ones. You can turn it on or off once per round. It also works offline since Season Six.

Since the 2 November 2021 update, its effects last until **round 55** (instead of 20). Super Sprinters, very fast zombies, then have a **20%** chance to spawn at first, then 5% more every 5 rounds.

## Enemies

- Normal **zombies**, explosive barrel zombies, zombies that come out of the ground, and **armored** zombies (helmet, chest plate) that you have to strip of their armor.
- Tougher **special and elite enemies**: Plaguehound (toxic dog), Megaton (it splits in two, Megaton Bomber and Megaton Blaster, when its health hits zero), Mimic, Mangler, Tempest, Disciple, Tormentor, Abomination…
- **Bosses** specific to some maps, like the Orda on Firebase Z.

Since July 2021, enemy health grows much less at high rounds than at launch.

Each map only has some of these enemies: see each map's guides.
