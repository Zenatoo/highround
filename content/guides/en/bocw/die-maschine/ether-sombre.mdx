The **Dark Aether** is the dimension the zombies come from in Cold War. On Die Maschine, the facility's particle accelerator tore a rift into it. During the game, you can cross over: it is the same map, darker, with extra paths and items. Almost every quest on the map requires you to go there.

## How to get in

You get in through **Anomalies**, purple portals that appear after the **power** is on. Hold the action button in front of an Anomaly to cross over.

- Your stay is **time-limited**: after a while, the game sends you back to the normal world. Don't hang around.
- Which Anomalies are open depends on your progress in the quests. For example, the ones for the Electrobolt and Thermophasic upgrades only appear after the main Easter Egg computer.
- A door opened in the normal world is not necessarily open in the Dark Aether.

## The map's Anomalies

| Where | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| Particle Accelerator, in the middle | Building Pack-a-Punch (after the two terminals) |
| Medical Bay | Vogel's journal and the Easter Egg reflections, then the wrench |
| Particle Accelerator, at the very bottom | The D.I.E.'s Electrobolt upgrade |
| Pond | The D.I.E.'s Thermophasic upgrade |
| Living Room (Nacht der Untoten building) | The last reflection of the main Easter Egg |

For the side quests (the giant hand, the floating zombies), any Anomaly will do.

## What changes on the other side

- **Aether Tunnels**: passages that take you to a closed-off spot on the map, for **500 Essence**. This is how you get the Pack-a-Punch part.
- **Purple crystals**: outside, Aetherium crystals grow all over. Shoot them: they drop items (Essence, Salvage, armor plates).
- **Spectral Reflections**: ghosts replay scenes from the map's past. Several steps of the main Easter Egg go through them.
- **Der Wunderfizz**: the perk machine is on the roof of the Nacht building, in the **Penthouse**. At launch, you could only use it from the Dark Aether. See [Perks and Der Wunderfizz](../atouts/).

## The round 42 giant

From **round 42** on, go into the Dark Aether and head to the **Pond**. Look at the forest, outside the map: a **giant** walks by. It does nothing else: it is just a scene to watch.

Most guides call it the **Orda**, like the Firebase Z boss. Tracker.gg names it "Hulking Summoner": a piece of intel on the map talks about giants that the Dark Aether survivors call "elder gods".
