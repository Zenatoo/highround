Die Maschine's power is at the very back of the underground facility, in the **Main Power** room. You have to cross the whole map to reach it: count on **8,250 Essence** of doors and debris. The game helps you: **purple arrows** painted on the walls and a marker show you the way.

The name of each area (Yard, Living Room, Crash Site…) shows at the top left of the screen when you enter it.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Yard: open the building door">
    You spawn in the **Yard**, the courtyard in front of the Nacht der Untoten building. Open the building door, the one with a bunny painted on it: **500**.

    You arrive in the **Living Room**, on the ground floor.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Living Room: clear the stairs">
    The stairs to the upper floor are blocked by rubble marked with a red cross. Blow it up: **750**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bedroom: break the wall to the Crash Site">
    Upstairs, go to the end of the **Bedroom**, opposite the Stamin-Up machine. The wall marked with a red cross opens with a charge: **1,000**. Jump outside: you are at the **Crash Site**, the area with the crashed plane.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Crash Site: clear the Tunnel entrance">
    Cross the Crash Site to the far end, to the rock marked with a purple arrow. Blow up the obstruction: **1,250**. You enter the **Tunnel**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tunnel: enter the facility">
    In the Tunnel, walk up to the facility doors (**Facility**): they open on their own, for free.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Facility: go left to the Medical Bay">
    Right after the doors, debris blocks the left path. Blow it up: **1,500**. Go down the stairs and open the **Medical Bay** door: **1,500**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Particle Accelerator: open the power room">
    From the Medical Bay, you enter the big **Particle Accelerator** room. The **Main Power** room has a door on each side: open either one, **1,750**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Press the red button">
    In Main Power, press the big **red button** near the window. It is free. The power is on.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The doors on the way

| Passage | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Yard to the Living Room | 500 |
| Living Room stairs | 750 |
| Bedroom to the Crash Site | 1,000 |
| Crash Site to the Tunnel | 1,250 |
| Tunnel to the Facility | Free |
| Left debris in the Facility | 1,500 |
| Medical Bay door to the Particle Accelerator | 1,500 |
| Particle Accelerator to Main Power | 1,750 |
| **Total** | **8,250** |

In the Facility, another path goes to the right. The left path is the one our sources describe, with its prices.

## What the power turns on

- The **perk machines** and the **Arsenal**. See [Perks and Der Wunderfizz](../atouts/) and [Crafting tables, Mystery Box, Arsenal and exfil](../equipements/).
- Two doors that only open with the power: the one in the Yard, which leads straight to the Main Power room, and a door upstairs in the Nacht building.
- The two Particle Accelerator terminals, the first step of Pack-a-Punch. See [Unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).

Once the Yard door is open, you can go back and forth between the surface and the power without going through the Crash Site again.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="GamesRadar" source="https://www.gamesradar.com/black-ops-cold-war-zombies-guide/">
    Most players turn on the power around round 6 or 7. Save up by staying in the Yard and the Crash Site early on.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="faster" author="DiamondLobby" source="https://diamondlobby.com/call-of-duty/black-ops-cold-war/how-to-find-mystery-box-in-die-maschine-cod-zombies/">
    Don't linger in the Living Room or the Bedroom: they are closed rooms where zombies surround you fast. The Crash Site is much wider.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
