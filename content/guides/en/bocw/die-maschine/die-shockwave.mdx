The **D.I.E. Shockwave** (Decompressive Isotopic Estrangement Machine, "D.C.D" in the French version) is Die Maschine's special weapon. Its main fire sends out an energy wave that kills several zombies at once. Its secondary fire **sucks things in**: energy, but also items, which is what its four upgrades rely on.

You can get it in four ways:

- from the **Mystery Box** (rare);
- as the Legendary reward of the **Trials**;
- at random from the Coffin Dance chest: see [The Coffin Dance and its rewards](../coffin-dance/);
- **guaranteed** with the short quest below. It unlocks the "Carpe D.I.E.M." achievement.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Kill a Megaton and take its keycard">
    The **Megaton** is the big toxic zombie. The first one arrives between rounds 6 and 10. When its health hits zero, it splits in two: the Megaton Bomber and the Megaton Blaster. Kill both: a **Keycard** drops. Pick it up.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'keycard.webp'} alt="The keycard dropped by the Megaton" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Weapons Lab: take the remote">
    Go to the **Weapons Lab**, in the facility. Use the keycard on the **Weapon Locker**: a drawer slides out with the **D.I.E. Remote Control**. Take it.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'weapon_locker.webp'} alt="The Weapon Locker in the Weapons Lab" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Living Room: turn on the D.I.E.">
    Head back up to the **Living Room**, on the ground floor of the Nacht der Untoten building. Opposite the stairs, a **hole in the wall** glows blue: behind it, a closed metal door. Bring a group of zombies first, then use the remote on the hole.

    The D.I.E., on the other side of the door, **sucks in the zombies** that die around it, through the door's window. It needs **20 to 30**. If it stops before it is full, it needs about **30 seconds** before it starts again.

    {/* Zombie count: about 20 (FIRUIN), 25 to 30 (COD Zombified), about 30 (tracker.gg). Cooldown about 30 s: mmmrkennedy; "about a minute" for the rest (FIRUIN). */}

    <Screenshot src={m + 'brokenwall.webp'} alt="The hole in the Living Room wall" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Blow the door open and take the weapon">
    When the D.I.E. is full, a sound lets you know. Use the hole in the wall again: the D.I.E. blows the door open. Go in and take the **D.I.E. Shockwave** from the skeleton's hands.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pickup.webp'} alt="The D.I.E. Shockwave in the skeleton's hands" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## What next

- The D.I.E. has **four** elemental **upgrades**, required for the main Easter Egg: see [The 4 D.I.E. upgrades](../ameliorations-die/).
- Since Season Six (October 2021), it can also be **Pack-a-Punched**: see [Unlock Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="PC Gamer" source="https://www.pcgamer.com/cod-cold-war-zombies-easter-egg-steps-die-maschine/">
    Fight your first Megaton at the **Pond**, near the mushroom tree. Let it throw its green liquid at you from a distance so it hits the mushroom: it turns purple. You save time on the Cryo Emitter upgrade.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="PowerPyx" source="https://powerpyx.com/call-of-duty-black-ops-cold-war-trophy-guide-roadmap/">
    With the D.I.E., two achievements become easy: "Two Birds, One Stone" (one shot kills both halves of a Megaton) and "Nightmare at 20 Feet" (100 kills on the plane wing). See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="PC Gamer" source="https://www.pcgamer.com/cod-cold-war-zombies-easter-egg-steps-die-maschine/">
    Don't turn on the D.I.E. with no zombies around: if it doesn't suck in enough in time, it stops and you have to wait.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
