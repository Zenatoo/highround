Die Maschine's Pack-a-Punch is broken at the start of the game. You have to **rebuild** it in the middle of the **Particle Accelerator**, with a part hidden in the **Dark Aether**. Everything is guided by on-screen markers. The "Heavy Metal" achievement unlocks at the end.

**Before you start:** turn on the power. See [Turn on the power](../courant/). Keep at least **500 Essence** for the Aether Tunnel, and a bit more to be safe.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Activate the two terminals">
    In the Particle Accelerator, there is a **terminal on each side** of the room, shown by a marker. Activate both. The screens around them turn green.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the Anomaly">
    An **Anomaly** (a purple portal) appears in the middle of the room, at the bottom of a staircase. Kill the zombies around it, then hold the action button in front of it: you cross into the **Dark Aether**. Your time there is limited: see [The Dark Aether and its anomalies](../ether-sombre/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the Aether Tunnel">
    Follow the marker to the **Aether Tunnel**, a passage that takes you to a closed room. It appears at random in one of these spots:

    - at the **Crash Site**, under the nose of the plane, which floats in the air;
    - at the **Pond**, under a tank floating in the air.

    The passage costs **500 Essence**.

    {/* Tunnel spots: codzombieguides (plane nose at the Crash Site, floating tank at the Pond) and COD Zombified (1 of 3 spots: under the cockpit, or one of two side by side at the Pond). Gameranx also says one of three tunnels at random. */}

    {/* TO CHECK: PC Gamer (and an IGN copy) say an obstruction costing 1,250 must be cleared in the Dark Aether on the way to the tunnel. */}

    According to PC Gamer, an obstruction at **1,250** can block the way in the Dark Aether: keep enough to pay for it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the machine part">
    The tunnel drops you in a small closed room in the facility:

    - from the Crash Site, a **Medical Bay storage room**: the part is in the corner;
    - from the Pond, a **room next to the Weapons Lab**: the part sits on a crate.

    Pick up the **Machine Part**. The room's door opens.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Rebuild Pack-a-Punch">
    Go back to the middle of the Particle Accelerator. The blue outline of the Pack-a-Punch appears: interact with it to place the part. The game sends you back to the normal world, and Pack-a-Punch works.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Prices

| Upgrade | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Level 1 | 5,000 Essence |
| Level 2 | 15,000 Essence |
| Level 3 | 30,000 Essence |
| Ammo mod | 2,000 Essence |

Details on ammo mods and weapon rarity are in [Cold War Zombies basics](../systemes-cold-war/). Since Season Six (October 2021), the **D.I.E. Shockwave** can also be Pack-a-Punched.

Pack-a-Punch is required by almost every quest on the map: the free special weapon, the main Easter Egg and all the side quests.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="FIRUIN" source="https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2951105088">
    While you are in the Dark Aether, go through the Crash Site: under the nose of the plane, shoot the gray box and take the **fuse**. You will need it later for the Thermophasic upgrade. See [The 4 D.I.E. upgrades](../ameliorations-die/).
  </Tip>
</Tips>
