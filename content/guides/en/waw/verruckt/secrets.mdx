Verrückt has **no main Easter Egg**: no quest, no boss, no hidden reward. The map's secrets are small. The general rules are in [World at War Zombies basics](../../nacht-der-untoten/systemes-waw/).

## The dentist chair

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Find the dentist chair">
    According to a community guide, a dentist chair is in the Juggernog spawn room.

    {/* TO CHECK: room. Only mmmrkennedy (BO3 doll list, "Juggernog spawn room, on the dentist chair") places it; callofdutyzombies.com gives no room; codzombified calls it an "operating table" with a valve under it. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Crouch near the wheel">
    **Crouch** next to the **wheel** on the side of the chair.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use it three times">
    Hold the use button **three times**. You hear a **drill** and a **scream** in the distance. There is **no reward**.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The radios

There are radios in the asylum, but they are useless in World at War: they are **not interactive**. In the Black Ops remake, shooting a radio on the American side plays a music track from the campaign: this does not exist in the original.

## The hidden song

A real song unlocks in the toilets: see [Hidden song: Lullaby for a Dead Man](../musique-lullaby-for-a-dead-man/).

## What does not exist here

- No **Pack-a-Punch** (it arrives on Der Riese).
- No special weapon specific to the map.
- No **achievements** or trophies: see [World at War Zombies basics](../../nacht-der-untoten/systemes-waw/).
- The doll quest of "Samantha's Sorrow" and the **Mule Kick** machine come from the Black Ops remakes, not from World at War.
