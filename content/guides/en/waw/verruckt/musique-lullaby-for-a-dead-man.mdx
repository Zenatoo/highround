Verrückt hides a **song**: "**Lullaby for a Dead Man**" (sometimes written "Lullaby of a Deadman"), composed by **Kevin Sherwood** and sung by **Elena Siegman**. There is **nothing else** to gain but the music. The toilets are upstairs, on the Double Tap side.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Go up to the toilet room">
    This is the toilet room, upstairs, **near the power room**, on the Double Tap side. One video also places it as the room with the **STG-44** wall weapon.

    {/* TO CHECK: room named "near the power room on the Double Tap side" (mmmrkennedy) and "room with the STG-44" (Alexvire video description bryRllMTpkg). Probably the same room. */}

    <Screenshot src={m + 'toilet.webp'} alt="The Verrückt toilet room, with the toilet bowls at the back" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use the left toilet">
    Pick the **left-most toilet**. Hold the use button until you hear the flush.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Do it three times">
    Flush **three times in a row**. After the third one, the song starts.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

The trick works on **PC, Xbox 360 and PS3**, but not on the Wii version of the game (according to a French video). It also exists in the Verrückt remakes (Black Ops and Black Ops III), with the same steps.
