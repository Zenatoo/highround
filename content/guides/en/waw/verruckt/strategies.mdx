Verrückt is the hardest map of World at War: one site rates it "by far" the hardest, with no easy corner to hold out in. A 2009 guide also says the zombies are **much faster** than on Nacht der Untoten, and that they can hit you **through the windows**. Still, most guides agree on one method: **camping**.

The basic rules (points, barriers, box) are in [World at War Zombies basics](../../nacht-der-untoten/systemes-waw/). To open the map, see [Turn on the power](../courant/).

## As a team

In a four-player game, two teams of two each hold one wing. **Both teams can shoot all the zombies**, so kills get stolen. The goal: turn on the power, then meet up in the middle of the building.

## Starting plan

- **German side**: start with the Gewehr and aim for the head. At around **4,000 points** or **round 5**, open the debris, go upstairs, buy the **MP40** and **Double Tap**, turn on the power, then camp in the box room, leaving **only one side open**.
- **American side**: start with the M1 Garand. At the same threshold, open the door and buy the **Thompson**. After the power, take the **Speed Cola** (called Sleight of Hand in this guide), then camp in the box room, with only one side open. Your teammates arrive through the door that links the two halves.

## Holding out in the high rounds

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="Smity" source="https://steamcommunity.com/app/10090/discussions/0/4693405423939731203/">
    Starting on the German side: take **Juggernog** first and open the doors up to the Speed Cola room, but **keep closed** the door to the Trench Gun and the Bouncing Betties. Camp at the **Speed Cola window**: no zombie spawns there in World at War. You can hold out there until about round 20.
  </Tip>

  <Tip type="tech" author="Smity" source="https://steamcommunity.com/app/10090/discussions/0/4693405423939731203/">
    Overwhelmed? Fall back to the **Double Tap balcony**. The flamethrower burns the zombies at the windows and the electric trap handles the rest.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

### A solo player at round 100

One player reached **round 100 solo**, in about **3 hours**. Here is his method.

- **Rounds 1 to 15**: start on the **allied** side, camp in the **kitchen** and place **Bouncing Betties** in front of you.
- **Rounds 15 and 16**: the kitchen gets too hot. You can still dodge in the **corridor** that separates the two sides, with a Thompson with Double Tap.
- **From round 17**: use the **wall trap** in the corridor on the German side. Rounds 17 to 25 are the hardest: up to **3 zombies** can spawn late through a broken wall, and the stairs can get blocked.
- **Around round 25**: zombies come out faster, so it is easier. Repair as many windows as you can between rounds.
- **Around round 30**: take the **flamethrower** for the last 1 to 5 zombies. Its damage goes up with the zombies' resistance.
