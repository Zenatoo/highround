On Verrückt, the power is the first real step of the game. At the start, the map is cut into **two halves**: an American side and a German side, separated by an electric door. While the power is off, this door stays closed, the Perk-a-Cola machines do not work and neither do the electric traps. The general rules of the game are in [World at War Zombies basics](../../nacht-der-untoten/systemes-waw/).

The **switch** is in the **Mystery Box room** (guides call it the "random box room"). It is free: it is the doors on your way that you have to pay for.

**To check:** the sources still online give neither the door prices nor the exact path to the switch. This guide only describes what is cross-checked.

## The steps

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Start with your half of the map">
    Depending on the number of players, you spawn on the **German** side or the **American** side. A 2009 guide advises starting with the **Gewehr** (headshots) on the German side, and with the **M1 Garand** on the American side.

    {/* TO CHECK: which side starts for solo / 2 / 3 players. Kotaku 2009 preview: 4 players = two pairs on opposite sides. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the first door">
    Each side has a door blocked by **debris**. It costs about **4,000 points**: the 2009 guide says "about 4,000 points, or round 5", and a tutorial video also says "4,000 points and more" to turn on the power.

    {/* TO CHECK: the 4,000 figure comes from cheatcodes.com (2009) and the description of video NTc5uXaioWM; it is a rough number, not an exact door price. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to the Mystery Box room">
    On the German side, the 2009 guide goes through the **upper floor** after the debris. On the American side, it opens the door and then buys the **Thompson** on the way.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the switch">
    Use the **switch** in the box room. The **power turns on**: the door that separated the two halves opens, and the **Perk-a-Colas** and the **electric traps** work. Your teammates on the other side can finally join you.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

After that, the box room is a good place to hold out: the 2009 guide advises leaving **only one side open**. See [Survive as long as possible](../strategies/). The machines are described in [The Perk-a-Colas](../atouts/).

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="alternatereality8" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TA02A6MlNvo">
    For a long solo game, start on the **American** side: you can turn on the power without opening the doors of the trap corridor on the German side.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
