Verrückt is the map that **invented the Perk-a-Colas**: machines that give you a bonus, paid for with your points. There are **four**: Juggernog, Double Tap Root Beer, Speed Cola and Quick Revive. They only work once the **power** is on (see [Turn on the power](../courant/)). You **lose all your perks** when you go down.

The shared rules (buying, losing perks) are in [World at War Zombies basics](../../nacht-der-untoten/systemes-waw/). There is no Pack-a-Punch on this map.

## The four machines

**To check:** the prices come from a single fan site (a copy of a wiki). The locations come from a few player posts and guides: not all the exact rooms are confirmed.

| Perk | Effect | Price | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Juggernog** | More health | 2,500 points | Spawn room on the German side |
| **Double Tap Root Beer** | You shoot faster | 2,000 points | German side, upstairs (the Double Tap "balcony") |
| **Speed Cola** | Faster reloading | 3,000 points | A room with a window; exact room to be confirmed |
| **Quick Revive** | Faster revive | 1,500 points | American side, near the spawn room |

{/* TO CHECK: Speed Cola is called "Sleight of Hand" in the 2009 cheatcodes guide, "Speed Cola" in the Steam thread and codzombified. Official in-game name not confirmed. Quick Revive spot: mmmrkennedy names a "Quick Revive spawn room" (BO3 layout, same map), not a WaW-specific source. */}

According to the sources, **Speed Cola** is also called **Sleight of Hand**: it is the same machine. The exact in-game name remains to be confirmed.

If you play the Black Ops version of Verrückt (Rezurrection or Hardened), a fifth machine, **Mule Kick**, exists. It is **not** in World at War.

## In which order should you buy them?

- On the German side, a high-level player takes **Juggernog** first, then opens the doors up to the Speed Cola room.
- After the power, the 2009 guide buys **Double Tap** on the German side and **Sleight of Hand** (Speed Cola) on the American side.
