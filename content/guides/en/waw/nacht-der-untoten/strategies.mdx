Nacht der Untoten is known as one of the hardest maps to get a high round on: no perks, no Pack-a-Punch, only wall weapons and the box. There is no quest to finish: the goal is to last as many rounds as possible.

## The basics

- **Repair the barriers.** Every repair gives you points, and zombies tear off the boards faster and faster as the rounds go up. See [World at War Zombies basics](../systemes-waw/).
- **Spend with a purpose.** You have no perks to buy: your points go to doors, wall weapons, the cabinet and the box. The list is in [Wall weapons and the Mystery Box](../armes-et-boite/).
- **The scoped Kar98k cabinet** (1,500 points, on the second floor) gives you a precision weapon. Its ammo costs 380 points.
- **The Ray Gun** can come out of the box, very rarely.
- **The explosive barrels** around the map can be used to kill zombies: according to the sources, they can be shot to deal damage. We don't know whether they respawn.

## Solo or co-op

The map can be played with 1 to 4 players. The videos in Kenshin9977's guide cover solo and duo separately. Nothing more precise is confirmed by our written sources.
