Nacht der Untoten is the smallest map and the only one without perks, power or Pack-a-Punch. Your gear therefore comes from **three things**: the wall weapons, the sniper rifle cabinet and the Mystery Box. The general rules (points, barriers, power-ups) are in [World at War Zombies basics](../systemes-waw/).

## The scoped rifle cabinet

On the **second floor**, above the Mystery Box room, a **cabinet** opens for **1,500 points** and gives you a **scoped Kar98k**. After that, its ammo costs 380 points.

## The Mystery Box

- It is in the "**Help**" room (named after the word written on the wall), on the ground floor, behind a door you have to open.
- It **never changes place** on this map.
- The price per roll is not confirmed by our sources.
- The **radio** on the table to the right of the box plays music: see [The secret radio](../radio/).

{/* TO CHECK: fixed location in the Help room: codzombified + mmmrkennedy (room name and radio position). Price (950 in snippets of blocked pages): not written. Whether the box ever moves in the WaW original: only a search snippet says it does not. */}

The **Ray Gun** can come out of the box: it is a very rare weapon, introduced with World at War. The official Call of Duty blog confirms it for Nacht der Untoten. There is no Pack-a-Punch here to upgrade it.

The full content of the box is not confirmed by our sources.

{/* TO CHECK: Mystery Box weapon list unknown (Fandom page blocked). Springfield, Gewehr 43, Arisaka seen in a search summary only. */}

The Black Ops (and Black Ops III) remakes change the map: perks, power, other wall weapons. Don't take their lists for World at War ones.
