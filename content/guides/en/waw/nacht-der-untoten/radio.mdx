Nacht der Untoten has no secret quest, but it hides a **radio** that plays music. It needs neither power nor points.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Go to the Mystery Box room">
    This is the "Help" room, on the ground floor (see [Wall weapons and the Mystery Box](../armes-et-boite/)). The radio sits on the **table to the right of the box**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'radio.webp'} alt="The table at the back of the room, with the radio and a lamp" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot or knife it">
    One shot or one knife hit on the radio starts a song.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Skip to the next one">
    Shoot the radio again to change the song.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

The exact songs are not confirmed by our sources. A video of the radio on PC is in the list above.

## The song "Undone": not in World at War

In Black Ops and Black Ops III, shooting the **explosive barrels** outside, behind the windows, unlocks the song "Undone". According to mmmrkennedy, this is **Black Ops only**. For World at War, it is not confirmed: don't waste ammo on it.
