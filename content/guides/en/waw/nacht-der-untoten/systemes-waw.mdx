Call of Duty: World at War (Treyarch, 2008) created the **Nazi Zombies** mode. This guide explains the rules **shared by all four maps**: Nacht der Untoten, Verrückt, Shi No Numa and Der Riese. The guide of each map links back to it.

It covers the **original versions** (PC, PS3, Xbox 360) in their final state. Many videos actually show the Black Ops remakes ("Rezurrection", "Hardened") or the Black Ops III one ("The Giant"): some rules are different there, so don't rely on them.

**To check:** almost all the numbers in this guide come from a single source. When a number has only one source, we say so. Prices that our sources do not confirm are not given.

## The four maps

| Map | Release | Date (consoles) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nacht der Untoten** | Base game: bonus mode, unlocked after the campaign | November 11, 2008 |
| **Verrückt** | Map Pack 1 (DLC) | March 19, 2009 |
| **Shi No Numa** | Map Pack 2 (DLC) | June 11, 2009 |
| **Der Riese** | Map Pack 3 (DLC) | August 6, 2009 |

{/* TO CHECK: dates are console release dates (Wikipedia "Call of Duty: World at War - Zombies" map table); PC dates not found. */}

You play with **1 to 4** players in co-op, or solo. When all the players are down, the game is over. A downed player can be revived by an ally. The four named characters (Dempsey, Nikolai, Takeo, Richtofen) only arrive in Shi No Numa: before that, you play four nameless American soldiers.

Wii, Nintendo DS and iOS versions exist, with their own rules: this guide does not cover them.

## Points and barriers

You start with an **M1911** pistol. You earn **points** by killing zombies and by **repairing barriers** (the boards on the windows). You spend them to open **doors**, buy **wall weapons**, roll the **Mystery Box** and, depending on the map, buy perks.

Zombies tear off the boards to get in. The higher the round, the faster they tear them off. Always keep one window to repair: it is easy money and it buys you time.

The point values per enemy and per board are not confirmed by our sources.

## Doors and wall weapons

Closed areas open with points (prices vary by map). Wall weapons are bought once; buying the same weapon again only refills its ammo, for a lower price. The lists for each map are in the guide of that map (for the first one, see [Wall weapons and the Mystery Box](../armes-et-boite/)).

## The Mystery Box

The **Mystery Box** gives you a random weapon for points. It first appears in Nacht der Untoten, where it never moves. The **Ray Gun**, the rarest weapon, can come out of it on Nacht der Untoten and Verrückt: this is confirmed by the official Call of Duty blog.

{/* TO CHECK: Mystery Box price 950 points appeared only in search snippets of blocked pages: not written. Teddy-bear relocation: exists on Der Riese per codzombified (cage with a teddy bear), single source. Not on Nacht. */}

The price of the box is not confirmed by our sources. On Der Riese, according to a single source, the box can change location.

## Power-ups

Killed zombies sometimes drop a power-up to pick up:

- **Max Ammo**: refills the ammo of all your weapons;
- **Nuke**: kills all the zombies on the map;
- **Insta-Kill**: every hit kills, for about 30 seconds;
- **Double Points**: doubled points, for about 30 seconds;
- **Carpenter**: repairs all the barriers at once.

## The Perk-a-Colas

Perks appear in Verrückt. There are **none** on Nacht der Untoten.

| Perk | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Juggernog** | You take more hits before going down |
| **Speed Cola** | Faster reloading, and faster barrier repair |
| **Double Tap Root Beer** | Higher rate of fire |
| **Quick Revive** | You revive your allies faster |

## Pack-a-Punch

The **Pack-a-Punch** only exists on **Der Riese**. It needs the power and linked teleporters: see [Link the teleporters and unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../../der-riese/pack-a-punch/). On the other three maps, your weapons cannot be upgraded.

## Traps and special grenades

- **Electric traps**: from Verrückt on, you pay each time you use them. Zombies killed by a trap give no points.
- **Bouncing Betty**: a mine you can buy, on Verrückt, Shi No Numa and Der Riese.
- **Monkey Bomb**: a monkey grenade that attracts zombies, on Der Riese only.

## The prestige weapons

- **Ray Gun**: Mystery Box only; first appearance on Nacht der Untoten.
- **Wunderwaffe DG-2**: lightning that jumps from one zombie to the next, on Shi No Numa (Mystery Box). See [The Wunderwaffe DG-2](../../shi-no-numa/wunderwaffe-dg-2/).
- **Monkey Bomb** and **Bowie Knife**: Der Riese. See [DG-2, Monkey Bomb, Ray Gun and Bowie Knife](../../der-riese/armes-speciales/).

## Special enemies

**Hellhounds** appear from Shi No Numa on, and on Der Riese. They come in special waves, with fog. According to a single source, a round of dogs comes back every 4 to 7 rounds on Der Riese, and the last dog drops a **Max Ammo**. The Monkey Bomb does not work against them. There are none on Nacht der Untoten or Verrückt.

## What changes from one map to the next

| Map | What's new |
| --- | --- |
| Nacht der Untoten | Mystery Box, wall weapons, explosive barrels. No power, no perks, no traps. |
| Verrückt | Perks, electric traps, power. |
| Shi No Numa | The four named characters, Wunderwaffe DG-2, Hellhounds, Bouncing Betty. |
| Der Riese | Pack-a-Punch, teleporters, Monkey Bomb, Bowie Knife, Carpenter. |

Verrückt: see [Turn on the power](../../verruckt/courant/) for how to start the power.

## No main quest, no achievements on the first two maps

There is **no main quest** in World at War Zombies. There are no achievements or trophies for Nacht der Untoten and Verrückt either. The 20 Zombies achievements come from Map Packs 2 and 3.
