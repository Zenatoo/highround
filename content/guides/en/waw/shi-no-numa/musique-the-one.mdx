Shi No Numa hides a song: "**The One**", written by **Kevin Sherwood** and sung by **Elena Siegman**. Activating it also unlocks the **Dead Air** achievement (see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/)). There is no power to turn on: you just need to have opened the way to the **Comm Room**.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Go to the Comm Room">
    This is the communications room, one of the four huts. Enter the **back room, on the right**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the phone">
    A phone is **off the hook** on the **desk**. You hear a busy tone when you get close.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'phone.webp'} alt="The Comm Room desk, with the phone on it" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Press the use button">
    Aim at the receiver and press the use button. At first, you hear a tone and then silence. Keep pressing: a voice whispers "I can hear you!" and then the song starts.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

The sources disagree on the **number of presses**: **2**, **3** or **4** depending on the pages and videos. Keep pressing until the song starts.

## Good to know

- Treyarch wrote and recorded this music in-house: lyrics and music by Kevin Sherwood (sound designer), vocals by Elena Siegman. The Shi No Numa **end-of-game song** is a preview of "Beauty of Annihilation", the hidden song of Der Riese.
- This song also exists in the Black Ops and Black Ops III remakes. A second song, "Samantha's Sorrow" (fishing hut), only exists in Black Ops III: it is **not** in World at War.
