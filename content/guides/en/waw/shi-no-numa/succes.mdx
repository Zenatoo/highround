Shi No Numa has **10 achievements** (Xbox 360) or trophies (PS3), which came with Map Pack 2. Check them off as you go: your progress stays on your device. The names are the in-game English ones. Nacht der Untoten and Verrückt have none, as [World at War Zombies basics](../../nacht-der-untoten/systemes-waw/) says.

{/* Names, conditions, grades: codzombified essentials (Gamerscore + trophy grade) and PlayStation LifeStyle 2009 (conditions, tips): they agree except Soul Survivor. */}

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Dead Air (bronze)">
    Answer the Comm Room phone: the song "The One" starts. See [Hidden song: The One](../musique-the-one/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hammer Time (bronze)">
    Repair **200 boards** in a single game. You don't need to earn points with them. 2009 tip: keep a wounded zombie outside, trying to get in, and hold the repair button on the window for about 10 minutes. Stay far enough away: it can hit you through the window.
  </Step>

  <Step title="It's a Trap! (bronze)">
    Kill at least one zombie with **three different traps** during the same round. 2009 tip: at the Fishing Hut, keep a zombie, activate the hut's power, run to the **Flogger** (the flail door), then to the Doctor's Quarters for its electric trap, with the zombies on your heels. You need points for all three traps.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Weapon of Minor Destruction (bronze)">
    Don't kill more than **1 zombie** with a Nuke. 2009 tip: keep a single zombie alive, let a Nuke drop and pick it up.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Perk-A-Holic (bronze)">
    Have all **four perks** at the same time. Count on rounds 12 to 15 depending on your weapons. See [The hut Perk-a-Colas](../atouts/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fertilizer Man (bronze)">
    Get **200 kills** in a game.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Big Brawler (bronze)">
    Get **10 knife kills in a row** during an Insta-Kill, before it ends.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Dead-Head (silver)">
    Get **150 headshots** in a game.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Big Baller (silver)">
    Earn **75,000 points** in a single game. This is the hardest: you will get most of the others along the way. The 2009 guide puts it around round 30 with the loop around the Comm Room, see [Survive as long as possible](../strategies/). All the trophies can be done solo, according to the 2009 guide.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Soul Survivor (silver)">
    Reach **round 15** without being downed.

    {/* TO CHECK: wording. codzombified: "without getting downed"; PSL2: "without getting revived". */}

    **To check:** according to one source, you must not be "downed"; according to another, not "revived". Avoid both.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
