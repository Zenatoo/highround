The **Wunderwaffe DG-2** is the special weapon that arrived with Shi No Numa. One shot sends a bolt of lightning that **jumps from zombie to zombie**. You do **not build** it and you cannot buy it from the wall: it comes **only from the Mystery Box**. There is no Pack-a-Punch on this map: it cannot be upgraded.

## What it does

- The chain lightning can hit up to **10 zombies**.
- It has **3 shots per magazine** and **15 in reserve**.
- It fires one shot at a time: its rate of fire is slow.
- Only one person uses it at a time: if the holder puts it away or switches, someone else can pull it from the box.

## Getting it

1. Open the Mystery Box in the hut where it is (a pillar of light in the sky marks it).
2. Try your luck: the Wunderwaffe is one of the possible rolls, like the Ray Gun.
3. When you get it, **tell your team**.

The official 2009 guide advises looking for the Wunderwaffe, a Ray Gun or a heavy machine gun from rounds 10 to 16, because the team struggles to hold out without them late in the game.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="Call of Duty Zombies" source="https://www.callofdutyzombies.com/zombie-library/zombies-library/worldatwar/shinonuma/wunderwaffe-dg-2-r44/">
    Back up and shoot at the line following you: zombies line up like a "snake" and the lightning jumps from one to the next.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="Call of Duty Zombies" source="https://www.callofdutyzombies.com/zombie-library/zombies-library/worldatwar/shinonuma/wunderwaffe-dg-2-r44/">
    Its rate of fire is slow and it has little ammo. One or two players at most carry it, and the others keep machine guns or assault rifles to cover. Plan a backup weapon for when it is empty.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="DocBooch" source="https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/?p=38517">
    A high-round pattern: walk (without running) around the Comm Room with the Wunderwaffe, then turn around at a hut to shoot the line of zombies. See [Survive as long as possible](../strategies/).
  </Tip>
</Tips>

In the story, Richtofen created it. In Black Ops, the map's version changes weapons; don't rely on videos of that version for the box list.
