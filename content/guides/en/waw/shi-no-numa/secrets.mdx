**There is no main Easter Egg** in Shi No Numa on World at War: no quest, no boss, no final objective. Videos that talk about a "quest" on this map show a remake. Here are the small secrets of the original version.

## The phone and the song

In the Comm Room, a phone off the hook starts the hidden song "The One". Everything is in [Hidden song: The One](../musique-the-one/).

## The meteorite

Near the **Storage Hut**, a big **shiny rock** sits outside. It is the **Element 115** meteorite.

- Shoot it: each of the four characters says a line.
- Richtofen says, in short, that this is probably where Element 115 comes from.
- Dempsey wonders whether this rock brought the zombies. Nikolai thinks the Red Army would be interested in it. Takeo says it is not of this world.

## The radios

Several radios tell a bit of the story of Group 935. They give neither points nor rewards.

- **In the starting room**: three radios. Press each one (in any order) to hear the message.
- **Storage Hut**: a radio that replays the start of the Verrückt trailer.
- **Doctor's Quarters**: a radio of which you only understand a few words: "Element 115", "Find Doctor Richtofen", "at Verrückt".

## What does not exist in World at War

The song "Samantha's Sorrow", Samantha's dolls and the fishing hut plates are **Black Ops III** secrets only. The Vanguard remake (Shi No Numa Reborn) has a real quest, which does not exist in the original version.
