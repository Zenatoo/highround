Shi No Numa is the **first World at War map with Perk-a-Colas**. There are **four**: Jugger-Nog, Speed Cola, Double Tap Root Beer and Quick Revive. There is no power to turn on and no Pack-a-Punch on this map. The general rules (points, box, power-ups) are in [World at War Zombies basics](../../nacht-der-untoten/systemes-waw/).

## The four machines

| Perk | Effect | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Jugger-Nog** | More health | 2,500 points |
| **Speed Cola** | Faster reloading | 3,000 points |
| **Double Tap Root Beer** | Higher rate of fire | 2,000 points |
| **Quick Revive** | You revive your teammates faster | 1,500 points |

Double Tap mostly works with **automatic weapons**. A comment under the official 2009 guide says shotguns benefit from it too: we could not confirm it.

## Where are they?

The machines are in the **four huts** around the big central hut: the **Storage Hut**, the **Doctor's Quarters**, the **Fishing Hut** and the **Comm Room**. Each hut has its own gate, which costs points to open.

The perks are placed **at random**: from one game to the next, the same perk is not in the same hut. So look in every hut you open. The machines show their price in "$".

The official 2009 guide also advises heading for the hut where the Mystery Box is: a **pillar of light** in the sky marks it.

  <Tip type="tech" author="CoD:Hub" source="https://totalgamingnetwork.com/forum/tgn-official/gaming-news/169066-cod-waw-shi-no-numa-field-guide">
    In a hut, two players guard the back windows and two guard the front door. Place Bouncing Betties around, then buy the perks. If it gets hot, one player activates the hut's electric trap to block the door.
  </Tip>

## In which order?

No need to buy everything fast: the official guide puts the tour of the huts **between rounds 10 and 16**, with weapons already better than the starting ones. Each gate you open spawns **three zombies**.

- Open the Storage Hut, Doctor's Quarters and Fishing Hut first.
- Keep the **Comm Room for last**: the dead-end corridor behind the box is a refuge when the dogs arrive.
- Quick Revive is mostly useful in co-op: the official guide wants a team "medic" who has taken it.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="DocBooch" source="https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/?p=38517">
    To get all four perks at once (Perk-A-Holic achievement), count on rounds 12 to 15 depending on your weapons. See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
  </Tip>
</Tips>

In the Black Ops version of Shi No Numa (Rezurrection), the prices and weapons are not the same: don't rely on videos of that version for prices.
