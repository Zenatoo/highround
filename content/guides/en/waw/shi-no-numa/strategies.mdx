Shi No Numa is big: a **big hut** in the middle, **four huts** around it, and **Hellhounds** from round 5 to 7. This guide combines the official 2009 guide and a trophy guide from the same year. The general rules are in [World at War Zombies basics](../../nacht-der-untoten/systemes-waw/).

**To check:** this advice dates from 2009 (first version of the map). Later patches may have changed details, for example the dog schedule.

## The phases

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Rounds 1 to 4: earn points">
    Weaken a zombie with a few bullets, let it tear boards off at the window, then finish it with the **knife** before it gets in. Each body shot earns 10 points, the final knife hit 130, and each repaired board 10.

    Tell your team you are doing this, otherwise they think you are in trouble and come to help.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Rounds 5 to 9: open the Downstairs">
    To reach the lower area ("Downstairs"), you can open **the stairs** or the **Warning Room**. Both work. The stairs give more room to run; the Warning Room leaves you a small corner at your back, but more zombies come from the yard.

    First weapons: the **Thompson** (1,200 points) or the **M1897 shotgun** (1,500 points). In the official guide, it is better to buy them than to throw your points into the box right away.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Rounds 10 to 16: the perks">
    Head to one of the four huts, two players in front and two behind. Buy the perks, plant Bouncing Betties around the hut, try the box for a Wunderwaffe, a Ray Gun or a machine gun. See [The hut Perk-a-Colas](../atouts/) and [The Wunderwaffe DG-2](../wunderwaffe-dg-2/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Round 16 and up: roles">
    At least two players with heavy weapons, one with the Ray Gun or the Wunderwaffe, and a "medic" with **Quick Revive**. Go back and hide in a corner of the lower ground floor, with Betties, and only go out to the huts when needed.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The dogs

- The first dogs arrive **between rounds 5 and 7**. After that, there are **three rounds of pause**, then each round can be a dog round or a zombie round.
- Signs: the fog thickens, the music changes and a character says it. A ball of lightning and smoke announces a dog's arrival.
- Go to a **narrow corner** with the whole team: two standing behind, two kneeling in front. Automatic weapons are the best. Grenades and Bouncing Betties help (Betties do not hurt you, grenades do).
- 2009 tip: go to the **dead-end corridor behind the starting box**, crouched, and shoot at the corners. Drop Betties outside. Reload **before** taking the Max Ammo left by the last dog.

## The "crawler"

Keep alive a wounded zombie that can no longer grab you, so you can **take your time**: you can buy, roll the box and repair. The 2009 trophy guide recommends it, for example by crippling a zombie with a grenade just before the end of the round. Each hut gate you open spawns 3 zombies, so count on them.

## High rounds

The 2009 guide describes a loop: the Wunderwaffe holder **walks** (without running) counter-clockwise around the Comm Room, turns around at a hut and headshots the line of zombies with a machine gun. For the dogs, he stands on the dry area to the left of the Comm Room, facing the dock, away from the wall.

According to this guide, **75,000 points** come around round 30. For other high-round methods (phases, first trap, dog rounds), watch the videos by CrafTot7 (in French), Doughnuts and Cleystar.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="DocBooch" source="https://www.playstationlifestyle.net/?p=38517">
    Your first steps: once the first gate is open (around round 3), count about 2,000 points to roll the box several times and aim for the Wunderwaffe and an MG42.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="CoD:Hub" source="https://totalgamingnetwork.com/forum/tgn-official/gaming-news/169066-cod-waw-shi-no-numa-field-guide">
    Traps are often activated too late, because nobody wants to spend their points. Decide together who pays before it turns into chaos, especially in front of a hut or in the yard.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
