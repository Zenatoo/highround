Three **glowing green jars**, with a brain and a spinal cord inside, are hidden on the map. Use all three: the song "**Beauty of Annihilation**" starts. It is by **Kevin Sherwood** and sung by **Elena Siegman**.

There is no sound when you use a jar: it **simply stops humming**. The order does not matter.

    In the **small room next to** teleporter B, to the right of the teleporter, on a **table** near a blackboard.

  </Step>
