The **Fly Trap** is a game of hide-and-seek with **Samantha**. There is **no main quest** on Der Riese, and this one gives **no reward**. But it unlocks the **Elevate Your Senses!** achievement (see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/)). Count 5 to 20 minutes alone. It is easy.

In Black Ops III, this step starts a real quest that leads to the Annihilator weapon: that does not exist in the original version.

## What you need

A bullet weapon **upgraded with the Pack-a-Punch**. See [Link the teleporters and unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/). Without an upgrade, shooting the panel does nothing.

## The steps

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Shoot the control panel">
    Go near the **M1A1 Carbine** wall weapon (through the left door of the starting room). From the zombie barrier, look to the end of the aisle: a **control panel** is on a distant pillar. Shoot it with your upgraded weapon.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'panel.webp'} alt="View toward the distant pillar where the control panel is" credit={by} source={src} />

    Samantha says she wants to play hide-and-seek. **Objects fly** into the air.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the three objects">
    You must **hit** three objects, with any damage (bullet, knife, grenade).

    - A **teddy bear with a Bowie Knife**, in a broken window up high, above the M1A1 Carbine.
    - A **Monkey Bomb**, in the furnace of the Thompson room, under the stairs.
    - A **teddy bear with a bottle of Juggernog**, in a cage to the right of the M1897 Trench Gun wall weapon, bottom left.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'window.webp'} alt="The broken window up high, above the M1A1 Carbine" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'furnace.webp'} alt="The furnace in the Thompson room, with the Monkey Bomb" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'cage.webp'} alt="The cage corridor, near the M1897 Trench Gun" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Listen to Samantha">
    She comments on each object. After the last one, she yells that you won and that the game is over. There is nothing to pick up.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## A note on the objects

The objects in the furnace and the window only exist **if the trap has been activated**. Once the game is over, the furnace Monkey Bomb cannot be picked up.
