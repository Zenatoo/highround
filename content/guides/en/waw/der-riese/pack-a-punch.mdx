Der Riese is the map where the **Pack-a-Punch** first appeared. It exists nowhere else in World at War (see [World at War Zombies basics](../../nacht-der-untoten/systemes-waw/)). To get to it, you must **turn on the power**, then **link the three teleporters** to the **mainframe** in the starting room.

One upgrade costs **5,000 points**.

## Before you start

Turn on the power: see [Turn on the power](../courant/). Without power, the teleporters do not work.

## Link the three teleporters

There is one at the end of each wing of the map:

- **Teleporter A**: left of the starting room (through the Animal Testing Lab);
- **Teleporter B**: right of the starting room;
- **Teleporter C**: toward the power switch.

## Using the Pack-a-Punch

The Pack-a-Punch is behind the mainframe door, in the starting room. One upgrade costs **5,000 points** and makes the weapon more powerful.

The names of the upgraded weapons are not confirmed by our sources, except for the Wunderwaffe: see [DG-2, Monkey Bomb, Ray Gun and Bowie Knife](../armes-speciales/).

## The teleporter back

Once linked, a teleporter sends you to the starting room.

**To check:** only one source gives a price for this return trip (1,500 points).

## Don't mix it up with the remakes

In **Black Ops** (Rezurrection) and **Black Ops III** ("The Giant"), the map has additions (Mule Kick perk, a new machine, a quest). They do not exist in the original version.
