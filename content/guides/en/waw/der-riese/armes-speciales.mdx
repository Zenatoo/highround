Der Riese brings together the strongest weapons of World at War: the **Wunderwaffe DG-2**, the **Ray Gun**, the **Monkey Bomb** and the **Bowie Knife**. Three come from the **Mystery Box** (see [World at War Zombies basics](../../nacht-der-untoten/systemes-waw/)), the knife is bought from the wall. The Pack-a-Punch can upgrade bullet weapons and the DG-2: see [Link the teleporters and unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).

## Wunderwaffe DG-2

An energy weapon that hits several zombies. It comes from the **Mystery Box**. It first appeared on Shi No Numa: see [The Wunderwaffe DG-2](../../shi-no-numa/wunderwaffe-dg-2/).

Pack-a-Punched, it becomes the **DG-3 JZ**. According to one source, the magazine doubles.

## Ray Gun

A rare weapon from the **Mystery Box**. The official Call of Duty blog confirms its presence on Nacht der Untoten and Verrückt; on Der Riese, it also comes out of the box according to the map's achievements.

{/* TO CHECK: Ray Gun in the Der Riese box: only the generic game-wide note + achievement "Acquire Waffle Weapons!" (Kotaku). */}

## Monkey Bomb

A toy monkey with cymbals and dynamite on its back. You use it with the **special grenade** button. It takes **longer to prime** than a normal grenade. It is not for sale: it comes from the **Mystery Box**.

**Hellhounds** don't fall for it, even though the explosion can hurt them.

{/* TO CHECK: priming time and Hellhounds ignoring it: COD Zombified essentials only. Monkey Bomb only from the box: a search snippet (not a source) and the game-wide notes. */}

- **The furnace in the Thompson room.** Throw a Monkey Bomb into the furnace in the Thompson room: Samantha scolds you ("why are you so cruel to Mister Monkey?"). One source says the power-up chances then go up a lot; this is not confirmed.

{/* TO CHECK: furnace line: MK, ZT, CZ agree. "Power-up odds rise dramatically": CZ only. */}

## Bowie Knife

The **Bowie Knife** first appears on this map. You buy it for **3,000 points** at the entrance of the teleporter C building, near the power switch. It is mostly used in the first rounds, where it kills in one hit.

{/* TO CHECK: price 3,000: ZT and CZ agree; location: ZT "entrance of the Teleporter C building", CC "near the power switch". One-hit kills early: ZT only. */}

For the **40 Knives!** achievement, kill 40 zombies with it: see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).

## The "Acquire Waffle Weapons!" achievement

It asks you to have the **Ray Gun**, the **DG-2** and the **Monkey Bomb** at the same time in a game.
