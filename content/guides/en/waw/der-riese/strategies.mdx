Here are some pointers for lasting a long time on Der Riese. **No reliable written guide** describes the best strategy for this map: this guide stays short and points to player videos. The general rules are in [World at War Zombies basics](../../nacht-der-untoten/systemes-waw/).

**To check:** everything below comes from single sources or video titles, not from in-game testing.

## The basic plan

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the power and get the perks">
    The perks, traps and teleporters depend on it. See [Turn on the power](../courant/) and [The Perk-a-Colas](../atouts/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Link the teleporters and upgrade your weapon">
    The Pack-a-Punch makes your main weapon much stronger. See [Link the teleporters and unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick your spot to hold out">
    The best-known strategy is the **catwalk** strategy: the teleporter C balcony. Zombies reach it from a single direction and the height lets you see how many there are.

## Tips

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="COD Zombified" source="https://codzombified.blogspot.com/p/der-riese.html">
    The teleporter C balcony is a good spot to hold out: you shoot down at the approaching zombies.
  </Tip>

  <Tip type="tech" author="COD Zombified" source="https://codzombified.blogspot.com/p/blog-page_17.html">
    Hellhounds come every **4 to 7 rounds**, with fog. The last dog drops a **Max Ammo**: save your bullets for it. The Monkey Bomb does not attract them.
  </Tip>

  <Tip type="tech" author="CODZombie" source="https://codzombie.com/maps/der-riese">
    A linked teleporter sends you back to the starting room: it is an emergency exit. According to one source, the return trip costs 1,500 points.
  </Tip>

  <Tip type="faster" author="ZombieTron" source="https://www.zombietron.it/map/der-riese">
    The Bowie Knife kills in one hit in the early rounds. It is sold near the entrance of teleporter C.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
