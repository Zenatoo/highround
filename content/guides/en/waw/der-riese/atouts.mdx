Der Riese has **four perks**: Juggernog, Speed Cola, Double Tap Root Beer and Quick Revive. They only work with the **power**: see [Turn on the power](../courant/). Their effects are explained in [World at War Zombies basics](../../nacht-der-untoten/systemes-waw/).

**Mule Kick** does not exist here: it is a perk of the Black Ops version of the map.

**To check:** the prices come from a single family of sources (COD Zombified and CODZombie, which copy the same wiki). The locations do not cross-check well: the sources contradict each other. Check in game.

| Perk | Price | Where to find it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Juggernog** | 2,500 points | Upstairs, at the top of the Animal Testing Lab stairs |
| **Double Tap Root Beer** | 2,000 points | At the end of the bridge, on the walkway, toward the teleporter B room |
| **Speed Cola** | 3,000 points | Behind shutters near the garage |
| **Quick Revive** | 1,500 points | On the teleporter C side, near Maxis's office |

## Why these conflicts?

- **Juggernog**: the Animal Testing Lab stairs are confirmed by one more source (the music guide calls it "the Jug stairs"). Another source puts it in the courtyard.
- **Quick Revive**: one of the price lists forgets it, but three sources and a video description mention it. It is on the map.
- **Speed Cola** and **Double Tap**: one source puts them in the courtyard, the other at precise spots. Look in the courtyard and around the bridge first.
