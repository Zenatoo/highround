Der Riese has **10 achievements and trophies**, added with Map Pack 3. Check them off as you go: your progress stays on your device. The names are the in-game English ones; we found no official French name. The general rules are in [World at War Zombies basics](../../nacht-der-untoten/systemes-waw/).

**To check:** the conditions come from a single article (Kotaku, July 2009, before release), which writes "Der electrican!" with a typo. The tenth achievement has no description there.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Wacker Packer!">
    Upgrade **one weapon** with the Pack-a-Punch. See [Link the teleporters and unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pack Addict!">
    Upgrade **5 weapons** with the Pack-a-Punch.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Der Electrician!">
    Link **all the teleporters before round 7**. Be quick: each link takes less than 30 seconds. See [Link the teleporters and unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Frequent Flyer!">
    Use the **teleporter 8 times**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Locksmith!">
    Open **all the doors** of the map.
  </Step>

  <Step title="40 Knives!">
    Kill **40 zombies** with the Bowie Knife. See [DG-2, Monkey Bomb, Ray Gun and Bowie Knife](../armes-speciales/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="The Might of the Monkey!">
    Use a **Monkey Bomb**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Acquire Waffle Weapons!">
    Have the **Ray Gun**, the **DG-2** and the **Monkey Bomb** at the same time in a game. They all come from the Mystery Box: you need luck.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Perkaholics Anonymous!">
    Reach **round 20** without buying **any perk**. See [The Perk-a-Colas](../atouts/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Elevate Your Senses!">
    Activate the **Fly Trap**. See [The Fly Trap](../piege-a-mouches/).

    {/* TO CHECK: description of Elevate Your Senses is "???" on Kotaku; "Activate the Fly Trap" comes from ZombieTron only. */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Nacht der Untoten and Verrückt have no achievements. Shi No Numa has its own achievements in Map Pack 2.
