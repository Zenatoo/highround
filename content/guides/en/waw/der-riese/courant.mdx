On Der Riese, the power wakes up the **perks**, the **electric traps** and the **teleporters**, and it lowers a **bridge** in the courtyard. You have to turn it on before thinking about the Pack-a-Punch. The general rules are in [World at War Zombies basics](../../nacht-der-untoten/systemes-waw/).

The switch is **behind the generator**, at the back in the middle of the map, in the courtyard (the room where the Mystery Box appears).

**To check:** our sources give no door prices for this map. The path below comes from a single detailed source (COD Zombified, a copy of a wiki) and from a map description.

## The map layout

The three teleporters are marked **A**, **B** and **C**. The red dot at the bottom of the map is the starting room, with the mainframe.

## The path to the switch

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Leave the starting room">
    You start in the **mainframe** room, with two wall weapons (Kar98k and Gewehr 43). Two doors lead out: the left one to the left factory room, the right one to the right factory room. Open either one.

    {/* TO CHECK: door prices not found in any readable source (a search snippet said 750 / 1,250; not published). */}

    The door prices are not confirmed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go through the corridor and up the stairs">
    Behind the factory room, a corridor leads to a **staircase**. It ends at a Mystery Box barrier, which opens onto a **balcony**. The left corridor is the **Animal Testing Lab**, the right one the **garage**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Jump into the courtyard">
    From the balcony, jump down: you land in the **courtyard**. The Mystery Box is there, near the generator.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Press the switch">
    Go around the **generator**: the switch is behind it. Press it. The power turns on for the whole map.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'power_switch.webp'} alt="The power switch box, near the generator" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## What the power turns on

- **The perks**: the machines only work with power. See [The Perk-a-Colas](../atouts/).
- **The teleporters**: you can then link them to the mainframe. See [Link the teleporters and unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
- **The electric traps**.
- **The bridge**: it lowers into the courtyard and links the two balconies. Doors also open between the courtyard and the left and right factory rooms.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="COD Zombified" source="https://codzombified.blogspot.com/p/blog-page_17.html">
    When you link a teleporter or teleport, a power-up appears in the starting room. After several uses, there are none left.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
