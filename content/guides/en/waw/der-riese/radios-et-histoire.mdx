Der Riese hides **recordings**: radios and phonographs that you activate by **holding the use button**. They tell the beginning of the Zombies story. There is no reward and no achievement: it is for the story. The map's rules are in [World at War Zombies basics](../../nacht-der-untoten/systemes-waw/).

## Where to find them

There are **7 recordings**: four radios and three phonographs. Three of them change depending on when you listen to them, the other four are fixed.

**To check:** the list of locations comes from a single source (COD Zombified, a copy of a wiki). The numbers from the other sources match, but the locations do not.

## The story in short

Nacht der Untoten and Verrückt only have four nameless American soldiers. At **Shi No Numa** the four named heroes arrive: the German doctor **Edward Richtofen**, the American marine **Tank Dempsey**, the Soviet soldier **Nikolai Belinski** and the Japanese soldier **Takeo Masaki**. There you learn that the dead are awakened by **Element 115**.

In **Der Riese**, Richtofen leads the others into the **Group 935** factory, near Breslau, where he worked. Group 935 was a secret research group, led by the German scientist **Ludvig Maxis**, created after the discovery of Element 115 in the region. According to the story, they want to use the teleporter and face **Samantha Maxis**, the little girl who seems to command the zombies.

The radios tell the **original crime**: Maxis's teleportation tests, with Richtofen as assistant, test subjects who survive but cannot be controlled, Samantha's dog turned into the first Hellhound, and Richtofen **locking Maxis and Samantha** in the teleporter. Samantha is also the voice of the hide-and-seek game: see [The Fly Trap](../piege-a-mouches/).

World at War has no goal and no ending: the story only lives in these recordings, in the Fly Trap and in the following games. Der Riese is the **fourth and last Zombies map** of World at War.
