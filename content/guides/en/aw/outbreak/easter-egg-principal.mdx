The goal: collect the four characters' cards, raise their access level to 50, then call for rescue. The Warbird arrives… and gets shot down. The "Game Over, Man!" achievement unlocks, and the **Mk 25** upgrade station opens.

**Before you start:**

- Expect **20 to 30 minutes** in solo. The quest works with 1 to 4 players.
- Each player needs the **Exo suit** (see [Get the Exo suit and turn on the power](../courant/)) and **Exo Slam** (see [Exo upgrades and where to find them](../atouts/)).
- **Who picks up the cards?** If the character is in the game, only that character's player can take the card. If nobody plays that character, anyone can take it.
- Going for the **gold trophy**? Read [The real ending and the gold trophy](../fin-alternative/) before the level 49 step.

## The black box

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Pick up the Black Box">
    It is on the ground in the Courtyard, left of the Warbird wreck, wedged between the wreck and the wall, behind the bus. It then shows on your screen.

    {/* TO CHECK: COD Zombified (single source) says that in co-op it can instead be in the Morgue, ground floor, under a console near the power station. */}
    Not there? One source says that in co-op it can be on the Morgue ground floor, under a console near the power station.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'black_box.webp'} alt="The Black Box near the Warbird wreck" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put it in the Morgue computer">
    The terminal is on the right side of the Morgue, near the Exo Reload power station. The computer replies that it needs security clearance: time for the cards.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'black_box_computer.webp'} alt="The Morgue computer" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The four cards (in any order)

Each card you pick up adds 1 to the **access level**, the number on the map at the bottom left of the screen.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Lilith's card: the 3D printer">
    Get the **EM1** from the 3D printer (see [3D printer and special weapons](../imprimante-3d/)). Then **shoot the printer with the EM1 while buying from it again** (1,000 credits).

    {/* TO CHECK: Millenium only: in co-op one player can shoot while another one buys. */}
    In co-op, one player can shoot while another one pays.

    Instead of a weapon, the printer gives out Lilith's card. Take it.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'lilith_card.webp'} alt="Lilith's card in the 3D printer" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Decker's card: the incinerator">
    You get into the incinerator through a trash chute (Administration or Exo Testing Room):

    - **Solo**: buy a trash chute and **step back** so you don't fall in. Wait for it to close, buy it again right away and jump in.
    - **Co-op**: two players buy and jump into **both** trash chutes **at the same time**.

    In the incinerator, **shoot the ash all over the floor**. When you hit the right spot, the card pops up into the air: pick it up.

    {/* TO CHECK: the closing wall can crush you (The Escapist, Se7enSins, COD Zombified) or only teleports you out (MargwaNetwork). */}
    A wall slowly moves in: depending on the source, it sends you back out or crushes you. Don't hang around.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'decker_card.webp'} alt="Decker's card in the incinerator" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="mmmrkennedy" source="https://mmmrkennedyzombiesguides.pages.dev/games/AW/outbreak_AW/outbreak_AW_guide">
        Keep the EM1: it fires without reloading, so you can spray the floor without stopping.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Oz's card: the Holding cells">
    In Holding, the white cells go up and down. Crouch and look **under the raised cell**: a metal bar moves when you shoot it. Keep shooting until it disappears (it takes a lot of bullets).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'oz_metal_bar.webp'} alt="The metal bar under the cell" credit={by} source={src} />

    Then **every player** buys Exo Slam and does an **Exo Slam at the same time** on the cell's roof. It sinks and another cell rises, with Oz's card behind its glass door.

    {/* TO CHECK: which cell. Bar under the middle cell (mmmrkennedy, COD Zombified, Millenium), the last cell in line (The Escapist), the farthest one (Cod Zombie Info) or the nearest one (MargwaNetwork). Slam target: the same cell (The Escapist, Se7enSins, Millenium, Cod Zombie Info, MargwaNetwork) or the outermost cell (mmmrkennedy, second photo). */}
    Most sources say to slam **the cell you shot under**. mmmrkennedy shows the outermost cell, the one in the photo.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'outermost_holding_cell.webp'} alt="The cell to Exo Slam, according to mmmrkennedy" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'oz_card.webp'} alt="Oz's card in the raised cell" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source="https://mmmrkennedyzombiesguides.pages.dev/games/AW/outbreak_AW/outbreak_AW_guide">
        The cell goes back down after a few seconds. If nobody takes the card in time, do the Exo Slam again.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kahn's card: infected in the Administration">
    Wait for an **infected round** and let a green zombie infect you. While you are infected, press the **four red keypads** in the Administration: they turn green. The order doesn't matter.

    - All four must be green **during the same infection**. If you get cured before that, you have to start over.
    - In co-op, everyone must be infected and each player presses some: 1 keypad each with 4 players, 2 + 1 + 1 with 3 players, 2 each with 2 players. In solo, you do all four.
    - The infection only gives you 60 seconds: be quick.

    The keypads:

    1. On the security office door, across from Exo Medic.
    2. Across from the entrance door, next to the decontamination shower.
    3. At the end of the red hallway, around the corner on the right.
    4. On an upstairs balcony, left of the MP11 wall weapon.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'kahn_keypad_1.webp'} alt="Keypad 1: security office door" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'kahn_keypad_2.webp'} alt="Keypad 2: near the decontamination shower" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'kahn_keypad_3.webp'} alt="Keypad 3: at the end of the red hallway" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'kahn_keypad_4.webp'} alt="Keypad 4: balcony near the MP11" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>

    The security office window, across from Exo Medic, opens: grab Kahn's card behind it, fast. Then run to decontamination (250 credits).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'kahn_card.webp'} alt="Kahn's card behind the security office window" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Level 49, then 50

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Raise your access level to 49">
    Once all four cards are taken, killed zombies randomly drop **small spinning black cards**. Walk over them: each one adds 1 to your access level. They disappear quickly.

    **Each player** must reach level **49**.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'keycards_on_ground.webp'} alt="Cards dropped by zombies" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'hud_number_light_grey.webp'} alt="The access level at the bottom left of the screen" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go back to the Morgue computer">
    Use it: it says the user has not identified themselves.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use your character's hand scanner">
    Each player puts their hand on **their own character's scanner**, and only that one. They then go up to level **50**. In solo, you only have yours to do.

    - **Lilith**: Morgue, upstairs, in the room between Exo Reload and the Exo Testing Room.
    - **Decker**: Exo Testing Room, halfway up the stairs.
    - **Oz**: Holding, left of Exo Health.
    - **Kahn**: Administration, left of the Morgue door, across from the MP11.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'lilith_keypad.webp'} alt="Lilith's scanner in the Morgue" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'decker_keypad.webp'} alt="Decker's scanner on the Exo Testing Room stairs" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'oz_keypad.webp'} alt="Oz's scanner near Exo Health" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'kahn_keypad.webp'} alt="Kahn's scanner across from the MP11" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use the computer one last time">
    The clearance is accepted and the computer announces that power is back in the Exo Testing Room.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Calling for rescue

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Use the suit stations all together">
    In the Exo Testing Room, each player stands at an Exo suit station and uses it **at the same time**. In co-op, it sometimes takes several tries.

    "Ride of the Valkyries" starts playing and the computer announces that the experimental upgrade station is online.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Watch the rescue from the Courtyard">
    Go back to spawn. The Warbird arrives… and gets hit by a missile before it lands. The "Game Over, Man!" achievement unlocks.

    The game goes on. You can now take a weapon to **Mk 25**: see [Upgrade your weapons (Mk 20 and Mk 25)](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>
