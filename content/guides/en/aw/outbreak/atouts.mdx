In Exo Zombies, perks are called **Exo upgrades**. Outbreak has five of them, sold in wall lockers. To buy one, you need:

- the **Exo suit** (see [Get the Exo suit and turn on the power](../courant/));
- the area's **power station** turned on.

Only exception: in solo, you can buy Exo Medic even without a suit.

{/* TO CHECK: Exo Health price. 2,500 per Accelerated Ideas, Se7enSins, GameCraves and IGN; 2,000 per Millenium and Jugga-Nog. */}

| Upgrade | Effect | Where | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Exo Medic** | Revive teammates faster; in solo, revives you on your own (3 purchases max per game) | Administration, at the Courtyard entrance, near decontamination | 500 in solo, 1,500 in co-op |
| **Exo Health** | You take more hits before you go down | Holding, northwest side | 2,500 (2,000 per some sources) |
| **Exo Reload** | You reload much faster | Morgue, upstairs | 2,000 |
| **Exo Soldier** | You shoot and reload while sprinting or sliding, switch weapons faster and shoot better without aiming | Holding, east side | 1,750 |
| **Exo Slam** | Jump, then hit the ground: kills at the point of impact and stuns around it | Main Hub, east wall | 2,000 |

When you are down, you lose your upgrades **one by one** until someone revives you.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="Game Craves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-havoc-dlc-trophy-guide/">
    For Exo Slam: jump, then press the right stick (R3). Exo Slam is required for Oz's card in the [Main Easter Egg (Game Over, Man!)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Tip>
</Tips>
