The main Easter Egg has a **second ending**, found in August 2015: this time, the Warbird really lands and takes you away. As a reward, a **gold Outbreak trophy** shows up in the reception room of Descent, the fourth map.

The quest is the same as the [Main Easter Egg (Game Over, Man!)](../easter-egg-principal/). Only one step changes: getting to level 49.

**Before you start:**

- Expect **25 to 35 minutes** in solo.
- The whole climb to level 49 is done **with melee only**: it is the longest step.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Do the quest up to the four cards">
    Black Box, then Lilith's, Decker's, Oz's and Kahn's cards, as usual.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get to level 49 with melee only">
    Only pick up **cards dropped by zombies killed with melee**. As long as you get it right, the access level number at the bottom left stays **light grey**.

    If you pick up a single card from a zombie killed any other way, the number turns **dark grey**: the real ending is lost, and you have to start a new game.

        During this step, a zombie killed with melee always drops a card.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Never go down">
    {/* TO CHECK: "nobody may go down" comes from mmmrkennedy only. */}
    According to mmmrkennedy, **no player may go down** during the whole game. Otherwise, you get the normal ending.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish the quest as usual">
    Level 50 at the hand scanners, the Morgue computer, the suit stations, then head to the Courtyard. This time, the Warbird picks you up and **the game ends**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go see your trophy in Descent">
    The gold Outbreak statue shows up in the Descent reception room. See [The Reception trophies](../../descent/trophees-de-la-reception/).

  </Step>
</Checklist>
