Nothing to build on Outbreak: the map has **security systems** that work as traps, two **trash chutes** to escape, and **orbital care packages** that drop from the sky.

## Security systems (traps)

Look for the **blue screens with a keypad** on the walls. Once the area's power is on, a screen turns on the security system nearby:

- price: **500 credits**;
- duration: **30 seconds**;
- credits for the zombies killed go to the player who paid.

Depending on the spot, the security system slams the **doors you already opened** like a guillotine on the zombies passing through, or turns on **lasers** in the Exo Testing Room that disintegrate anything they touch.

{/* TO CHECK: only one switch location is sourced (Morgue, COD Zombified). IGN gives the rule (blue keypad screens, 500, 30 s) but no list. The Exo Testing lasers come from GameCraves only. */}
One of these screens is in the **Morgue**. The other locations are not checked yet.

The **Security** power-up turns on every trap on the map at once, for 30 seconds.

## Trash chutes

There are two: one in the **Administration**, one in the **Exo Testing Room**. Pay, jump in, and you come out somewhere else on the map: handy to get away from a horde. The one in the Exo Testing Room drops you in the small Exo Medic room, in the Courtyard.

TO CHECK: the Administration chute goes to the Exo Testing Room (Jugga-Nog) or back to the Administration entrance (COD Zombified). First use costs 100 (COD Zombified, Jugga-Nog). */}
{/* TO CHECK: prices 100, then 500, then 2,000, reset each round (Jugga-Nog only). */}
The price goes up with each use in the same round, then goes back to the starting price the next round.

The trash chutes also hide the **incinerator**, a secret room in the [Main Easter Egg (Game Over, Man!)](../easter-egg-principal/).

## Orbital care packages

{/* TO CHECK: camouflage lasts 30 s per IGN only. */}

From time to time, Atlas drops a package on the map. Any player can pick it up. You can carry **three** at once.

| Package | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Turret** | An automatic turret |
| **Assault drone** | A drone that attacks zombies |
| **Camouflage** | Zombies ignore you for 30 seconds |
| **Credits** | Extra credits |

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="Game Craves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-havoc-dlc-trophy-guide/">
    Packages don't seem to disappear: pick them up at the end of a round. Turrets are not very useful; keep the assault drones and the camouflage for infected rounds instead.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Power-ups

{/* TO CHECK: durations are single-source: Hyper Damage 30 s (GameCraves), Power Surge and Security 30 s (IGN). */}

| Power-up | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Hyper Damage** | Every zombie dies in one hit for 30 seconds |
| **DNA Bomb** | Kills every zombie on the map |
| **Multiplier** | Doubles the credits you earn |
| **Full Reload** | Refills all ammo. A dog round always ends with this one |
| **Power Surge** | Every 3D printer at 10 credits for 30 seconds |
| **Security** | Every trap turns on for 30 seconds |
