Outbreak has **no main power switch**. Each area has its own **power station** (a generator). Turning it on is free, and it stays on for the whole game. It only powers the machines around it: Exo upgrades and the suit station. Doors and wall weapons work without power.

You start **without an Exo suit**. It is free, but it is in the Exo Testing Room: you need to open three doors to get there.

## Door prices

| Door | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Courtyard (spawn) to the Main Hub | 750 |
| Courtyard to the Administration | 750 |
| Main Hub to the Morgue | 1,000 |
| Main Hub to Holding | 1,000 |
| Administration to the Morgue | 1,000 |
| Morgue to the Exo Testing Room | 1,250 |
| Holding to the Exo Testing Room | 1,250 |

Whichever way you go, the suit costs you **3,000 credits** in doors.

## Go get the suit

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Open a Courtyard door (750)">
    The Courtyard is the spawn yard, with the bus and the Warbird wreck. With the bus behind you, the door on your left leads to the **Main Hub**, the big round room. The other door, at the end of the short hallway, leads to the **Administration**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Morgue door (1,000)">
    From the Main Hub, take the door on your right as you come in: a blue tunnel leads to the Morgue. From the Administration, it is the door that leads to the Morgue. Both ways cost the same.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Exo Testing Room door (1,250)">
    In the Morgue, go up the stairs and follow the "Exo Room" signs on the walls. The last door, at the top, leads to the Exo Testing Room.

    Other way: from the Main Hub, open the Holding door (1,000), then the Exo Testing Room door (1,250).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn on the generator and take your suit">
    Go down into the room and turn around: the generator is against the wall, on the ground floor. Turn it on, then take a suit at the station.

    The suit gives you the double jump, the boost, the boost slide and the Exo punch. It also lets you buy Exo upgrades: see [Exo upgrades and where to find them](../atouts/).

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare/Outbreak">
        If you die (not just go down), you come back the next round with your starting pistol and **no suit**: you have to go get one again.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The five power stations

To find a station, follow the **glowing red cables** that run from an Exo upgrade.

{/* All five stations cross-checked: IGN + COD Zombified (Courtyard map: "turns on Exo Medic, also restores power to the Administration Centre") + GameCraves + Cheat Code Central/Millenium for the Exo Testing generator. */}

| Area | Where | What it powers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Courtyard** | West side of the yard | The Administration and Exo Medic |
| **Main Hub** | In the small booth in the middle of the room | The room and Exo Slam |
| **Morgue** | North side, ground floor | Exo Reload, upstairs |
| **Exo Testing Room** | Against the east wall, ground floor | The Exo suit station |
| **Holding** | Small room on the east side, with the Weapon Upgrade Station | Exo Health and Exo Soldier |

## Good to know

- **Decontamination**: during infected rounds, a green zombie can infect you. You then have **60 seconds** to get to the decontamination station in the Administration and pay **250 credits**. Each hit you take removes 5 seconds. Several players can get cured at the same time.
- **Credit terminals**: the wall boxes with a green light give free credits, then recharge.
