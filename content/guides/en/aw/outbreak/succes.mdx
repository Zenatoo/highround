Outbreak has **10 trophies** (achievements on Xbox and PC): 8 bronze and 2 silver, worth 250 Gamerscore. Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="I'm Alive! (bronze)">
    Survive to round 10.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Survivor (bronze)">
    Survive to round 25.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="Game Craves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-havoc-dlc-trophy-guide/">
        Train the zombies in circles around the bus in the Courtyard, or on the catwalks of the Exo Testing Room. The Courtyard is close to Exo Medic and decontamination. An upgraded CEL-3 Cauterizer kills in one shot even late.
      </Tip>
      <Tip type="glitch" author="Game Craves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-havoc-dlc-trophy-guide/">
        The trophy sometimes doesn't pop at the start of round 25: finish the round, it should pop then.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="20/20 (silver)">
    Take **two weapons to Mk 20** in the same game. Expect about 100,000 credits in total. See [Upgrade your weapons (Mk 20 and Mk 25)](../pack-a-punch/).

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="Game Craves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-havoc-dlc-trophy-guide/">
        To earn credits, use SMGs or assault rifles. The CEL-3 Cauterizer kills too fast to earn much.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Moneybags (bronze)">
    Hold **15,000 credits** at once. There is no bank: the "bank" in the description just means your credits.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Cheapskate (bronze)">
    Get the Exo suit **without spending a single credit**. You need at least one teammate: they open the three doors (750, 1,000 and 1,250 credits) and turn on the Exo Testing Room generator. You buy nothing and just take your suit. See [Get the Exo suit and turn on the power](../courant/).

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="Game Craves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-havoc-dlc-trophy-guide/">
        Don't go down on the way: going down costs credits, and the trophy won't unlock anymore. Don't turn on a generator yourself.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Burgle Burgle Burgle (bronze)">
    "Steal" 10 kills from a teammate: they throw a **Distraction Drone** (3D printer), and you kill 10 zombies drawn to their drone while it is active. So you need at least two players.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Game Over, Man! (silver)">
    Call for rescue by finishing the main Easter Egg. See [Main Easter Egg (Game Over, Man!)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Come On And ... (bronze)">
    Hit **10 zombies with a single Exo Slam**. Buy Exo Slam in the Main Hub, gather a big group, jump over it and hit the ground.
  </Step>

  <Step title="PC Load Letter (bronze)">
    Use the 3D printer **15 times in the same game**. See [3D printer and special weapons](../imprimante-3d/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Do you even Exo? (bronze)">
    Get **10 Exo melee kills in 30 seconds**. You need the suit: the starting knife doesn't count.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="Game Craves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-havoc-dlc-trophy-guide/">
        Wait for a Hyper Damage power-up: every zombie dies in one hit for 30 seconds. Do it early in the game, while zombies are still weak.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>
</Checklist>
