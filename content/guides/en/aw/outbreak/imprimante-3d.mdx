The **3D printer** replaces the Mystery Box: for **1,000 credits**, it gives you a random weapon. If you already have two weapons, the one in your hands is replaced.

Only one printer works at a time. After several uses, it breaks down and another one turns on somewhere else on the map.

{/* TO CHECK: "the printer needs its area's power" comes from Digital Trends only. */}
It needs its area's power: see [Get the Exo suit and turn on the power](../courant/).

## The 5 locations

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Morgue, the short south hallway (at the start of the game)">
    The short hallway right after the Morgue door, on the Administration side. This is usually where the printer is at the start.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Morgue, ground floor">
    In the middle of the Morgue, under the stairs that lead up to Exo Reload.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Main Hub">
    North of the small central booth, next to the power station.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Exo Testing Room">
    Under the stairs, on the south side of the room.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Holding">
    In one of the small rooms on the south side.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

The **Power Surge** power-up turns on every printer for 30 seconds, at **10 credits** per use. Use it to look for a good weapon or for Distraction Drones.

## The special weapons

Some weapons only come out of the printer:

- **EM1**: a laser rifle. You need it for Lilith's card in the [Main Easter Egg (Game Over, Man!)](../easter-egg-principal/).
- **CEL-3 Cauterizer**: an energy shotgun. Once upgraded, it kills in one shot even in high rounds. It is the favorite pick for the Mk 25 station: see [Upgrade your weapons (Mk 20 and Mk 25)](../pack-a-punch/).
- **Distraction Drone**: a drone that pulls the zombies around it for a few seconds, like the monkey in other Zombies modes.
- **Nano Swarm**: a grenade that creates a bubble that hurts zombies.

Using the printer 15 times in the same game unlocks the **PC Load Letter** achievement.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="Game Craves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-havoc-dlc-trophy-guide/">
    The CEL-3 Cauterizer kills very well but earns few credits. To make money, keep an SMG or an assault rifle.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
