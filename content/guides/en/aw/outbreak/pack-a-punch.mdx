There is no Pack-a-Punch in Exo Zombies: you upgrade your weapons **one level at a time** at a **Weapon Upgrade Station**. The level shows next to the weapon's name: Mk 1, Mk 2…

- Each use costs **2,500 credits** and adds **one level**, up to **Mk 20**.
- The upgrade also refills the weapon's ammo.
- With each level, the weapon deals more damage. Its attachments and camo change, and the magazine grows over time.

Expect about **50,000 credits** to take a weapon up to Mk 20.

## The two Mk 20 stations

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Administration">
    In the middle of the area, at the corner of the decontamination station.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Holding">
    In the small room on the east side, the one with the power station.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Taking two weapons to Mk 20 in the same game unlocks the **20/20** achievement: see [All 10 achievements and trophies](../succes/).

## The Mk 25 station

A third station, the **Experimental Weapon Upgrade**, opens at the end of the main Easter Egg. It only works **for the current game**.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Finish the main Easter Egg">
    See [Main Easter Egg (Game Over, Man!)](../easter-egg-principal/). When all players use the suit stations, the computer announces that the experimental station is online.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take a weapon to Mk 20">
    The Mk 25 station only accepts a weapon that is already **Mk 20**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use the station in the Exo Testing Room">
    It is at the back of the Exo Testing Room. For **2,500 credits**, your weapon goes straight from Mk 20 to **Mk 25**, with a diamond camo.

    **Only once per game**: the station breaks down right after. Pick your weapon carefully.
