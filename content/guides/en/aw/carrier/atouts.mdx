Exo upgrades are the perks of Exo Zombies. To buy one, you need the **Exo suit** and the **power in its area** (see [Get the Exo suit and turn on the power](../courant/)). On Carrier, you can have all of them at once. When you go down, you lose them one by one.

| Upgrade | Effect | Where | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Exo Stockpile** (new) | 20% more reserve ammo; 5 grenades and 3 special grenades instead of 4 and 2; two grenades back each round instead of one | Med Bay | 2,000 |
| **Exo Medic** | Revive teammates faster; in solo, revive yourself | Upstairs on the Gun Deck, and upstairs in the Hangar near the lockers | 1,500 in co-op, 500 in solo |
| **Exo Health** | You can take more hits | Moon Pool, against the right wall | 2,500 (2,000 according to some sources) |
| **Exo Reload** | Faster reload | Lift | 2,000 |
| **Exo Slam** | Crouch in mid-jump to slam the ground: damage all around you | Bio Lab, upstairs, on the roof of the glass cells | 2,000 |
| **Exo Soldier** | You switch weapons faster, and you can shoot while sprinting and sliding | Armory, upstairs | 1,750 |

Each starting area has its own Exo Medic: in co-op, each half of the team can get theirs.
