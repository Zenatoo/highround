Three machines unique to Carrier give you credits or items: the **Grenade Disposal** and the **Weapon Disposal**, both in the **Armory**, and the **fishing rod** on the Gun Deck. All three are also used in the Easter Egg.

## Grenade Disposal

It's a big "Plinko"-style board on the lower wall of the Armory, with a slot at the top. It works from the start of the game.

- Throw a grenade into the slot: it falls into a box that pays **100 to 1,000 credits**, and sometimes a power-up.
- At the start of each round, the slot doesn't move. After each grenade, it changes size and moves from left to right.
- You get a free grenade every round: throw the ones you have left at the end of the round to earn credits.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="GameCraves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-supremacy-dlc-trophy-guide/">
    For the **Slot Jockey** achievement (30 grenades in one game), buy contact grenades: you can hold them in your hand with no risk of blowing yourself up. Teleport Grenades count too.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

After the first step of the Easter Egg, it gets a special mode with three red lights: that's the grenade puzzle from [Main Easter Egg (Flotsam & Jetsam)](../easter-egg-principal/).

## Weapon Disposal

A zombie arm sticks out of a slot in the Armory and asks for weapons. It only turns on after the **first step of the Easter Egg** (the generator hidden behind Chompy).

Give it a weapon (wall weapon or from the 3D printer): it gives you credits (200) or an Easter Egg item. In order: a **bottle**, then the three fishing rod parts, and later some **C4**.

- An item drops about every **two weapons**.
- A weapon you've already given it doesn't count.

## The fishing rod

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get the three parts">
    The Weapon Disposal gives you, in order, the **reel**, the **line** and the **hook**. Place each part as soon as you have it: if you get a new part before placing the old one, the new one replaces it, and you have to ask for the old one again.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Build the rod on the Gun Deck">
    It hangs on the railing of the **Gun Deck**, upstairs, across from the Vault Door (the big round door).

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'fishing_rod.webp'} alt="The empty rod on the Gun Deck" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'fishing_rod_finished.webp'} alt="The finished rod" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fish">
    Each try costs **100 credits**. The result is random: credits, junk (old boots…), a **fish**, a **shovel** or a **red fish**. The fish and the shovel are used in the Easter Egg, the red fish for Carrier's gold trophy.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
