Three **stuffed sharks** are hidden on the ship. Activate all three to start "Mars, the Bringer of War" by Gustav Holst (*The Planets*). You can activate them in any order. There is **no prompt** on screen: walk up to one and press the action button. A sound confirms it, and each shark plays a short bit of the music.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Hangar: above Captain DJ's locker">
    In the Hangar, go up the stairs and turn right into the locker room. The shark is on the shelf, above Captain DJ's locker (near Exo Medic).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'shark_hangar.webp'} alt="The shark above Captain DJ's locker" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Gun Deck: in the dark corner up top">
    Go from the Hangar to the Gun Deck. Right after the door marked "HANGAR", turn left straight away: in a dark corner with metal braces, the shark sits on the lowest brace. You reach it through the upper passage between the Hangar and the Gun Deck, with an Exo jump.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'shark_gun_deck.webp'} alt="The shark in the dark corner between the Hangar and the Gun Deck" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Lift: on the crane platform">
    In the Lift, jump onto the shark tanks. The stuffed shark is in the right corner, on the crane platform, at eye level, next to the cables that power Exo Reload.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'shark_lift.webp'} alt="The shark on the Lift crane platform" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>
