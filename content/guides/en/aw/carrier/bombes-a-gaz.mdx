On Carrier, in some rounds, an **Atlas strike team** boards the ship and plants **gas bombs** in several areas. It's a side objective, a bit like the survivor rescues on Infection.

## How it works

- Every few rounds, Atlas soldiers show up and plant bombs in **three areas** of the ship.
- **Defuse them in time.** If a bomb goes off, its area is **contaminated with gas until the end of the round**, like the infected areas on Infection.
- The further you get, the more often the bombs come back and the faster they go off. In later rounds, Atlas also sends **Goliaths** (the soldiers in heavy armor).
- The Atlas soldiers shoot at you while you defuse: the Explosive Touch power-up doesn't protect you from their bullets.

## Tips

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="GameCraves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-supremacy-dlc-trophy-guide/">
    Kill every Atlas soldier in a wave: you get a **camouflage** that makes defusing easy, since the zombies ignore you for a while.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="GameCraves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-supremacy-dlc-trophy-guide/">
    No need to cross the whole map for a bomb planted in an area you never go to (Gun Deck, Hangar, Lift): the gas clears after one round.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## The Right of First Defusal achievement

Defuse **5 gas bombs** in the same game. If you defuse one each time the Atlas soldiers arrive, you usually get it around round 20.
