On Carrier, the game starts with the team split in two: in co-op, two players spawn at the bottom of the **Gun Deck** and two in the **Hangar**. They can see each other through a grate, but they have to open doors to meet up. The Exo suit is in the **Cargo**, the ship's big hold: that's your first goal.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get to the Cargo">
    From the **Gun Deck**: open the lower door to the **Bio Lab** (750), then the **Cargo** door (2,000). Total: 2,750 credits.

    From the **Hangar**: open the **Moon Pool** (750), then the **Lift** (1,250), then the **Cargo** (2,000). Total: 4,000 credits.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn on the Cargo power">
    As on every Exo Zombies map, there is no main switch: each area has its own free **power station** that powers the machines around it. To find the machine it powers, follow the **glowing red cables**.

    In the Cargo, the station is **next to the Lift door**. It turns on the Exo suit station.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the Exo suit">
    It's free. It gives you the double jump, boost, slide and Exo punch. Without it, you can't buy Exo upgrades: see [Exo upgrades and where to find them](../atouts/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn on the power in the other areas">
    Turn on each station before you use the Exo upgrade in its area (table below).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The power stations

| Area | Where | What it turns on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Gun Deck** | Downstairs, against the crates at the edge of the deck | Exo Medic, upstairs |
| **Hangar** | Downstairs, in the corner, under the shortcut corridor | Exo Medic, upstairs in the Hangar |
| **Bio Lab** | Downstairs, at the foot of the glass cells, near the teleporter | Exo Slam, on the roof of the cells |
| **Armory** | Upstairs | Exo Soldier |
| **Cargo** | Next to the Lift door | The Exo suit |
| **Lift** | At the back, next to the teleporter | Exo Reload |
| **Moon Pool** | Against the right wall, near the shark cage | Exo Health |

## The doors

| Door | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Gun Deck (downstairs) to the Bio Lab | 750 |
| Gun Deck to the Med Bay | 750 |
| Hangar to the Moon Pool | 750 |
| Gun Deck (upstairs) to the Armory | 1,250 |
| Med Bay to the Bio Lab | 1,250 |
| Moon Pool to the Lift | 1,250 |
| Armory (downstairs) to the Bio Lab | 1,750 |
| Armory (downstairs) to the Lift | 1,750 |
| Bio Lab to the Cargo | 2,000 |
| Lift to the Cargo | 2,000 |

With the suit, a **corridor above the Hangar station** leads straight to the Gun Deck: jump into it with the double jump.

## Good to know

- The in-game map (Back button, or View on Xbox) shows the icons of the Exo upgrades, the 3D printer and the Weapon Upgrade Station.
- For the Easter Egg, you need to open the whole **Cargo, Lift, Moon Pool, Hangar** path. See [Main Easter Egg (Flotsam & Jetsam)](../easter-egg-principal/).
