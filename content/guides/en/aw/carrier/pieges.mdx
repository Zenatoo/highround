Carrier's traps are set off at **security switches**: Chompy in the Cargo, the Moon Pool water jets, the Armory steam, and more switches on the Gun Deck, in the Hangar and in the Bio Lab. As everywhere in Exo Zombies, a trap only works if the **power in its area** is on, and the credits for the zombies it kills go to the player who paid for it.

## Chompy

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Start Chompy in the Cargo">
    Chompy is a robot lawnmower with a grinder at the front. It lives in a small room in the **Cargo**. Press the safety switch on the wall: **500 credits**. The wall opens and Chompy does several laps of the Cargo, grinding up the zombies in its path.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'chompy.webp'} alt="Chompy in the Cargo" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ride it">
    Players (and zombies) can climb onto Chompy. It stays out for about **one minute**, then goes back in and rests for about **30 seconds**.

    {/* TO CHECK: about 1 min out and 30 s cooldown = GameCraves only. */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Chompy can also come out on its own during a round, or with the **Security** power-up, which turns on every trap on the map.

Its room also hides the first step of the Easter Egg. See [Main Easter Egg (Flotsam & Jetsam)](../easter-egg-principal/).

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="GameCraves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-supremacy-dlc-trophy-guide/">
    For the **Take Off, Hoser!** achievement (ride Chompy for 5 minutes in total in one game), do several laps from the early rounds, when there are few zombies.
  </Tip>

## The other traps

Each trap is started at a blue **safety switch** on the wall, for **500 credits**.

- **Moon Pool water jets**: they throw zombies into the air. They don't kill them, but they slow them down a lot: enough to finish them off or run away.
- **Armory steam**: upstairs, two switches fill the corridor with blue steam that kills zombies. It hurts you too if you walk through it.
- More switches are on the **Gun Deck** (upstairs, near the Armory door), in the **Hangar** (upstairs and in the shortcut corridor) and in the **Bio Lab** (downstairs, near the cells).

{/* Switch spots and the 500 price: COD Zombified's annotated maps (in-game "COST: 500" visible). What the Gun Deck, Hangar and Bio Lab switches do is not described; a se7ensins post calls the Bio Lab one a laser trap. */}

## The decontamination zones

There are two: in the **Med Bay** and in the **Moon Pool**. They cure the infection from green zombies for **250 credits**.
