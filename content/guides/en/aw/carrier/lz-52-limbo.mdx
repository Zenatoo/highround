The **LZ-52 Limbo** is Carrier's special weapon: an energy weapon that cuts zombies in half.

## Getting it

It only comes out of the **3D printer**, at random, for 1,000 credits per try. See [3D printer and special weapons](../imprimante-3d/).

## How it works

It fires a small round that bursts and creates a **big laser beam**. The beam is roughly **perpendicular** to your shot (across, horizontal or diagonal) and goes through the whole room. Any zombie the beam touches is sliced.

- Magazine: **8 shots**, with **16** in reserve.
- It's heavy to handle, but very powerful. Aim at a group: a single shot can hit a whole line of zombies.
- It can be upgraded at the Weapon Upgrade station, up to **Mk 25**. See [Upgrade your weapons (Mk 20 and Mk 25)](../pack-a-punch/).

## The How low can you go? achievement

Hit **18 enemies with a single shot**. You only need to hit them, not kill them.
