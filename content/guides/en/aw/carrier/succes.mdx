Carrier has **10 trophies** (achievements on Xbox and PC): 8 bronze and 2 silver. Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Ensign (bronze)">
    Reach round 10.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Admiral (silver)">
    Reach round 30. It's easier in solo or with two players. The **Cargo** is the best room for running zombies around in a train.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Flotsam & Jetsam (silver)">
    Sink the ship by finishing the main Easter Egg. See [Main Easter Egg (Flotsam & Jetsam)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Slot Jockey (bronze)">
    Use the Grenade Disposal (the grenade board in the Armory) **30 times** in the same game. Get contact grenades: you can hold them in your hand safely. Teleport Grenades count too. See [Grenade and Weapon Disposal, fishing rod](../machines-de-recyclage/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Right of First Defusal (bronze)">
    Defuse **5 gas bombs** in the same game. See [Gas bomb rounds](../bombes-a-gaz/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take Off, Hoser! (bronze)">
    Ride Chompy for **5 minutes in total** in the same game. One run lasts about a minute: do it several times, starting from the early rounds. See [Chompy and the other traps](../pieges/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Telefragathon (bronze)">
    Kill **50 enemies** with Teleport Grenades in the same game. Buy them in the Armory (750 credits) and throw them into the middle of a zombie train. Doable before round 10.
  </Step>

  <Step title="How low can you go? (bronze)">
    Hit **18 enemies** with a single LZ-52 Limbo shot. Hitting them is enough. See [The LZ-52 Limbo](../lz-52-limbo/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hugs! (bronze)">
    Kill **20 enemies** during a single **Explosive Touch** power-up (the green star: for about 15 seconds, zombies that touch you explode). Build a big train first, then grab the power-up and charge into it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Mad Hops (bronze)">
    Jump over **4 live sharks in 10 seconds**. Three sharks always swim in the **Lift** tanks. You have to lure the fourth one into the **Moon Pool**.

    1. In the Moon Pool, kill zombies on the floor grates: the blood draws a shark under the floor.
    2. Wait until it passes under the grate **closest to the Lift door**, then jump over it with an Exo jump.
    3. Rush straight into the Lift and jump over each tank, one jump per shark.

    Missed it? Try again while the Moon Pool shark is still there; otherwise, kill more zombies on the grates to bring it back.

      <Tip type="glitch" author="GameCraves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-supremacy-dlc-trophy-guide/">
        This trophy is finicky: some players say it didn't unlock even though they jumped over every shark. It sometimes takes several tries.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>
</Checklist>
