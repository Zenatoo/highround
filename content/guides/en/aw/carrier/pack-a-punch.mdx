In Exo Zombies, Pack-a-Punch is called the **Weapon Upgrade** station. On Carrier, there are two: the normal station, up to **Mk 20**, and a special station that takes a weapon to **Mk 25**.

## The normal station (up to Mk 20)

- **Where**: downstairs in the **Armory**, in the corner near the Lift door, on the same wall as the Grenade Disposal (the big grenade board). On the in-game map, it's the up-arrow icon.
- **Price**: **2,500 credits per level**. Each use adds one level (Mk 1, Mk 2…), refills your ammo and makes the weapon stronger.
- **Maximum**: Mk 20.

## The Mk 25 station

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Unlock it with the Easter Egg">
    It turns on when you place the **third tablet** in the Vault Door during the Easter Egg. See [Main Easter Egg (Flotsam & Jetsam)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bring a Mk 20 weapon">
    The station is on the **Gun Deck**, next to the fishing rod. It takes a **Mk 20** weapon to **Mk 25** for **2,500 credits**.

    On Outbreak, the Mk 25 station only works **once**, for a single weapon. This isn't confirmed for Carrier, but choose your weapon carefully.
