The goal: bring **four tablets** to the big round door on the Gun Deck (the **Vault Door**), then blow it open with C4 to sink the ship. The game ends with a cutscene and the "Flotsam & Jetsam" achievement unlocks.

**Before you start:**

- Plan on **one hour to one hour twenty** in solo. The steps are the same in solo and co-op, but co-op helps a lot with the grenades and the teleporter.
- Lots of luck involved: the fishing rod gives its items at random.
- The Vault Door has **five lights**: the first turns green with the generator step, then one per tablet.
- It's at the top of the Gun Deck, on your right just before the Armory door.

## Preparation

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the generator hidden behind Chompy">
    Buy **Teleport Grenades** in the Armory (750 credits). Go to the **Cargo** and start Chompy (500 credits, see [Chompy and the other traps](../pieges/)). When the wall opens to let Chompy out or back in, throw a Teleport Grenade into the small room behind it, before it closes.

    You are teleported inside: turn on the **generator** (on your left, in the corner). A few seconds later, you are sent back out. The Weapon Disposal turns on, and the Grenade Disposal gets three red lights on its right side.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'teleport_grenade.webp'} alt="The Teleport Grenades wall buy" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'chompy.webp'} alt="Chompy in the Cargo" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'chompy_room.webp'} alt="Chompy's room when the wall opens" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'chompy_power.webp'} alt="The generator in Chompy's room" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'weapon_disposal.webp'} alt="The Weapon Disposal turned on" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get the bottle and build the fishing rod">
    Give weapons (wall weapons or from the 3D printer) to the **Weapon Disposal**, the zombie arm in the Armory. About every two weapons, it gives an item, in this order: a **bottle** (it drops on the floor next to it), then the **reel**, the **line** and the **hook**. A weapon you've already given doesn't count.

    Place each part on the rod on the **Gun Deck**, across from the Vault Door, as soon as you have it: a new part replaces the one you were carrying. After that, each cast costs **100 credits**. Leave the bottle on the floor for now.

## Tablet 1: the grenade puzzle

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Throw a Teleport Grenade into the Grenade Disposal">
    The Grenade Disposal is the big grenade board on the lower floor of the Armory. Throw (or shoot) a Teleport Grenade into the top slot: the dispenser shifts and an icon cycles in a loop: **distraction drone**, **frag grenade**, **explosive drone**, **Nano Swarm**.

    If the dispenser goes back to normal during the puzzle, throw another Teleport Grenade.

    {/* TO CHECK: mmmrkennedy, COD Zombified, The Escapist and Cod Zombie Info use one Teleport Grenade at the start; Game Rant throws another after the first row; GameCraves throws one before each row. */}

    <Screenshot src={m + 'activated_grenade_disposal.webp'} alt="The Grenade Disposal in puzzle mode" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill the three rows">
    Throw a **frag** or **contact grenade** into the slot when the right icon is showing: your grenade turns into that type. Each completed row turns a red light green.

    - **Row 1**: distraction drone, frag grenade.
    - **Row 2**: explosive drone, distraction drone, explosive drone, frag grenade.
    - **Row 3**: explosive drone, Nano Swarm, explosive drone, distraction drone, explosive drone, frag grenade.

    For the last icon of each row, a Nano Swarm works too.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'distraction_drone.webp'} alt="Distraction drone icon" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'frag_grenade.webp'} alt="Frag grenade icon" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'explosive_drone.webp'} alt="Explosive drone icon" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'nano_swarm.webp'} alt="Nano Swarm icon" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="Game Rant" source="https://gamerant.com/call-duty-advanced-warfare-carrier-floatsam-jetsam-guide/">
        Don't kill the last zombie of the round with a grenade: the round ends and the puzzle resets. Keep one zombie alive, far from the machine.
      </Tip>
      <Tip type="faster" author="Game Rant" source="https://gamerant.com/call-duty-advanced-warfare-carrier-floatsam-jetsam-guide/">
        Frag grenades go in more easily than contact grenades. With a contact grenade, aim just a little below the slot. Each player carries 4 grenades (5 with Exo Stockpile): in co-op, you have more.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place the tablet in the Vault Door">
    The tablet comes out of a slot on the right of the board. Bring it to the Vault Door and interact. If it won't take it, wait until Oz has finished talking, then try again.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tablet_1.webp'} alt="The first tablet on the right of the Grenade Disposal" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Tablet 2: the laser maze

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Open the whole path">
    The **Cargo → Lift → Moon Pool → Hangar** doors must be open.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Drink the bottle and start the timer">
    Interact with the bottle, next to the Weapon Disposal: you're drunk. Then go to the **Bio Lab** side, in front of the Cargo door, and press the **green keypad**. A timer starts and lasers appear all along the route.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'bottle.webp'} alt="The bottle" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'drunk_button.webp'} alt="The keypad that starts the maze" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make it to the Hangar teleporter">
    Go through the **Cargo**, the **Lift** and the **Moon Pool** to the **Hangar** teleporter without touching a laser. While drunk, you can only Exo jump and slide. There are several paths in each room: the longest one is often the easiest.

    If you touch a laser, the timer stops and the lasers disappear: press the keypad again to restart right away.

    {/* TO CHECK: in co-op, mmmrkennedy says all players must reach the teleporter; GameCraves says only the drunk player runs the maze. Timer: 2 min per COD Zombified and VGB; GameCraves says it depends on the number of players. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the tablet">
    It's on the floor in front of the Hangar teleporter. Bring it to the Vault Door.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'hangar_teleporter.webp'} alt="The Hangar teleporter, end of the maze" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'tablet_2.webp'} alt="The second tablet in front of the teleporter" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="se7ensins" source="https://www.se7ensins.com/forums/threads/exo-zombies-carrier-full-easter-egg-video-and-text-tutorial.1334183/">
        Keep a camouflage (scorestreak reward) in reserve: if a new round starts while you're in the maze, use it so the zombies leave you alone.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Tablet 3: the teleporter and the island

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Collect 20 teleporter parts">
    From now on, **teleporting zombies and dogs** drop a part when they die. Walk over it to pick it up: you can only carry one at a time, and it disappears quickly. They show up around round 10, and regularly around round 15.

    Bring each part to the **broken teleporter in the Bio Lab**, next to the generator and the glass cells. After **20 parts**, it works.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'telefrag_piece.webp'} alt="A teleporter part on the floor" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'inactive_teleporter.webp'} alt="The broken teleporter in the Bio Lab" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fish up a shovel">
    Fish until you get a **shovel**. In co-op, give a shovel to several players: you'll dig faster.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Dig on the island">
    With the shovel, take the Bio Lab teleporter: the first trip costs **100 credits**. You land on an **island**. Hold the action button on the sand to dig, until you find a **box** with the tablet. Its spot changes every game.

    After about **30 seconds**, you are sent back to the ship. Didn't find it? Wait for the teleporter to recharge and go back.

    {/* TO CHECK: GameCraves alone says the teleporter price rises up to 1,000 with each use and resets each round. */}

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tablet_3.webp'} alt="The box with the third tablet on the island" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place the tablet">
    Bring it to the Vault Door. This also unlocks the **Mk 25 station** next to the fishing rod: see [Upgrade your weapons (Mk 20 and Mk 25)](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Tablet 4: Captain DJ's locker

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Find Captain DJ's locker">
    In the **Hangar**, go up to the locker room (near Exo Medic). Interact with **Captain DJ**'s locker: the characters say it has an **eye scanner**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'captain_djs_locker.webp'} alt="Captain DJ's locker" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot down the empty drone">
    A few rounds later (between 2 and 5), **two treasure drones** fly together somewhere on the map. One carries a power-up, the other looks empty. Shoot down the **empty drone** before it leaves: it takes a lot of bullets. It drops a **blue lever**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'empty_drone.webp'} alt="The empty drone" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place the lever in the Moon Pool">
    Place the lever on the Moon Pool **electrical box**, next to the 3D printer. **Don't activate it yet.** Activating it will cost **1,000 credits**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'electrical_box.webp'} alt="The Moon Pool electrical box" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Catch a fish">
    Go back to the fishing rod until you get a **fish**. The shark will trade it for the eye.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Lure the shark">
    Kill zombies on the **three grates** around the Moon Pool tank: their blood flows into the water and draws a **shark** under the floor.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'three_squares.webp'} alt="The three Moon Pool grates" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go down in the cage">
    When the shark passes under the **middle circle**, activate the box (1,000 credits): the floor opens and the cage goes down. The player with the fish jumps onto the cage to get in. Underwater, the shark bites the cage: hold the action button on its mouth. Your fish turns into **Captain DJ's eye**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'shark.webp'} alt="The shark biting the cage" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the locker">
    Use the eye on Captain DJ's locker: it opens to reveal the last tablet. Bring it to the Vault Door.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tablet_4.webp'} alt="The fourth tablet in the locker" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The ending

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get the C4">
    Keep giving weapons to the Weapon Disposal until you get **C4**. It comes quickly.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'c4.webp'} alt="The C4" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Blow up the Vault Door">
    Plant the C4 on the Vault Door. The game ends, the ending cutscene plays and the "Flotsam & Jetsam" achievement unlocks.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Carrier's gold trophy

During this Easter Egg, you can also earn **Carrier's gold trophy**, displayed in the reception room of the Descent map. Fish up a **red fish** and keep it, then finish the laser maze (tablet 2) in **under one minute**. If you feel you won't make it, touch a laser on purpose and start over. All the details: [The Reception trophies](../../descent/trophees-de-la-reception/).
