The **3D printer** is the Mystery Box of Exo Zombies: it gives you a random weapon.

## The 3D printer

- **Price**: **1,000 credits** per try.
- **Only one is active at a time**: after several uses, it breaks down and shows up somewhere else.
- On the in-game map, its possible spots are marked with a **question mark in a square**.
- The **Power Surge** power-up turns on every printer at once, at 10 credits per try, for a short time.

There are **five spots**, each one next to a ship layout screen:

- **Gun Deck**: downstairs, at the back, next to the layout screen.
- **Hangar**: upstairs, next to the layout screen.
- **Bio Lab**: downstairs, at the entrance, next to the layout screen.
- **Armory**: downstairs, against the back wall.
- **Moon Pool**: next to the shark cage's electrical box.

## Carrier's new weapons

<Checklist>
  <Step title="LZ-52 Limbo">
    The map's special weapon, only from the 3D printer. See [The LZ-52 Limbo](../lz-52-limbo/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="M1 Irons">
    Two old revolvers, one in each hand. Very powerful from the start, but slow to reload and short on ammo: Exo Reload makes them much nicer to use.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Teleport Grenades">
    Wall buy in the **Armory**, next to the Cargo door, across from the Grenade Disposal: **750 credits**. Throw one: you are teleported to where it lands, and the zombies around it (about 4 meters) die.

    They help you escape when you're cornered, and you can't do the Easter Egg without them. See [Main Easter Egg (Flotsam & Jetsam)](../easter-egg-principal/).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'teleport_grenade.webp'} alt="The Teleport Grenades wall buy in the Armory" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Other useful grenades: **contact grenades** (750 credits) are in the Armory, next to the Teleport Grenades, and **explosive drones** (1,000 credits) are in the Moon Pool.
