In Descent's Reception, next to the Self Check-In counter, a display case has **seven slots**. Each one gets a trophy (a cup, not an achievement) when you pull off a specific feat on one of the four Exo Zombies maps. Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device.

Trophies earned on the other maps show up in your next game of Descent.

{/* TO CHECK: the list of the 7 trophies (bus, burger, shark, trident; silver and gold) comes from one jeuxvideo.com forum post (alexidu62, 12 Aug 2015); a blocked-wiki snippet gave the same list. The three gold ones are confirmed by mmmrkennedy and NGTZombies. */}

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Silver bus (Outbreak)">
    Finish the Outbreak main Easter Egg. See [Main Easter Egg (Game Over, Man!)](../../outbreak/easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Gold bus (Outbreak)">
    Finish the Outbreak Easter Egg with the **alternate ending**: once you have the four access cards, get to level 49 **only with cards dropped by zombies killed in melee**, and without anyone going down. The Warbird then really comes to pick you up and the game ends. See [The real ending and the gold trophy](../../outbreak/fin-alternative/).

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="NGTZombies" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8SmEKIISLUA">
        It's much easier in the early rounds. Restart the game if you don't get the EM1 early or if Decker's card takes too long.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Gold burger (Infection)">
    Finish the Infection main Easter Egg. It's the only trophy from this map. See [Main Easter Egg (Meat Is Murder)](../../infection/easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Silver shark (Carrier)">
    Finish the Carrier main Easter Egg. See [Main Easter Egg (Flotsam & Jetsam)](../../carrier/easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Gold shark (Carrier)">
    During the Carrier Easter Egg, catch the **red fish** with the fishing rod, then finish the **laser maze** (tablet 2) in **under one minute**. A message announces the gold reward. If you see you won't make it in time, touch a laser on purpose: you can try again in the same game.

    {/* TO CHECK: mmmrkennedy and the jeuxvideo.com forum both say Red Fish + under 1 minute; NGTZombies (wEuKdq7yv7w) says under 54 s ("54 is a silver") and does not mention the fish. */}

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="NGTZombies" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wEuKdq7yv7w">
        Learn the path by heart and do it without drinking the bottle by the Weapon Disposal: you can't see the lasers anymore, but you go much faster.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Silver trident (Descent)">
    Finish the Descent main Easter Egg, all the way to Oz's memory. See [Main Easter Egg (Reunion)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Gold trident (Descent)">
    Kill Oz in round 20 in Double Feature mode ("Double Tap" achievement). See [Double Feature mode](../seance-double/).

    <Screenshot src={'aw/descent/gold_trophy/gold_trophy.webp'} alt="The gold trident in the Reception" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## What about all seven?

Nothing known. Flapix and other players who got them all found **no reward**.
