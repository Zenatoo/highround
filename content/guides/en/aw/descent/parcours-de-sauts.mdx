This is a step of the [Main Easter Egg (Reunion)](../easter-egg-principal/). It shows up after the grenade challenge. Many players find it the hardest part of the quest: watch one of the videos, it's much clearer than text.

<Screenshot src={m + 'jumping_plates.webp'} alt="The blue platforms around the starting area" credit={by} source={src} />

**How it works:**

- **Glowing blue platforms** appear in the void, around the starting platform (Landing Area). You need the Exo suit.
- Reach the last platform, the biggest one: you are sent back to the map and a new course appears. There are **9** of them.
- You can only stay on a platform for a short time: it disappears, then comes back. Don't stop.
- **Falling costs nothing**: you respawn in the starting area and can try again as many times as you want.

## The 9 courses

{/* TO CHECK: the one-line hints below come from GameCraves only; check them against Spiderrichard's video. GameCraves also says a finished pattern never has to be redone. */}

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Course 1">
    A simple Exo jump over a gap.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Course 2">
    Jump onto the starting platform, then onto a tiny platform in the middle, then onto the last one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Course 3">
    The hardest one. Get a run-up from the back of the platform, slide, double jump, then do two Exo boosts in a row.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Course 4">
    Exo jump, then double jump or boost to get around the wall.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Course 5">
    A fairly simple climb, with Exo jumps across a few small platforms.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Course 6">
    More climbing, with small backward dodges to line up each jump.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Course 7">
    From the starting platform, a running Exo jump, then boosts between the vertical platforms up to the last one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Course 8">
    Take your time: the ledges are very narrow.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Course 9">
    One last climb, like courses 5 and 6.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

After the ninth, Oz's next challenge ("don't move") starts the round after.
