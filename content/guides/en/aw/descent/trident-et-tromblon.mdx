Descent adds two special weapons: the **KL03-Trident** and the **Blunderbuss** (called "Tromblon" in the French version). Both only come out of the **3D printer**, the Exo Zombies version of the mystery box.

## The 3D printer

It gives you a random weapon for **1,000 credits**. There are several on the map, but **only one works at a time**: it moves after a few uses.

- Reception
- Spa
- Galleria
- Lounge, next to the airlock door

Oz's arena also has one during the round 13 fight. With the Power Surge bonus, every printer works at the same time and only costs 10 credits.

## The KL03-Trident

It fires energy balls that **bounce off walls** and home in on zombies. Against a group, just shoot at a wall and let the shots do the work: you can focus on moving around.

- According to GameCraves, it has 5 shots per magazine and 45 in reserve.
- Very good for the Easter Egg and for the round 13 Oz fight. Less useful against mutated Oz in round 20.
- Make it your top priority at the Weapon Upgrade Station. See [Upgrade your weapons (Mk 20 and Mk 25)](../pack-a-punch/).

    "Trickshot" achievement (100 zombies killed behind you, in one game): lead a group of zombies into the Spa, then run toward a wall and fire your five shots into it. The shots bounce back and kill the zombies following you.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## The Blunderbuss

An old wide-barreled gun: **huge damage** and a spread shot that hits several zombies, but **only one shot** before a long reload.

- Get **Exo Reload** to reload faster. See [Exo upgrades and where to find them](../atouts/).
- Move in, shoot, back off while you reload, repeat.
- It is the best weapon against mutated Oz: about **8 to 10 shots** are enough to kill him in round 20.
- A point-blank shot destroys a drone in one hit during the Easter Egg drone challenge.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="GameCraves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-reckoning-dlc-trophy-guide/">
    "Big Game" achievement: land the **final blow** on mutated Oz with the Blunderbuss. When he is almost out of health, the other players stop shooting and let the one with the Blunderbuss finish him.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="GameCraves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-reckoning-dlc-trophy-guide/">
    On PS3, this achievement needs the **Lynx**, a sniper rifle from the 3D printer, instead of the Blunderbuss.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
