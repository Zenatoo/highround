In Exo Zombies, perks are **Exo upgrades** you buy from wall machines. You need the **Exo suit** first, and a machine only works once the **switch of its area** is on. See [Get the Exo suit and turn on the power](../courant/).

| Upgrade | Effect | Where | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Exo Medic** | Revives teammates faster; in solo, revives you on your own | Landing Area (starting area) | 1,500 |
| **Exo Stockpile** | More reserve ammo and grenades | Concierge | 2,000 |
| **Exo Slam** | Slams the ground from a jump: damage around you | Galleria, upstairs, at the top of the stairs | 2,000 |
| **Exo Reload** | Reloads faster | Annex | 2,000 |
| **Exo Health** | More health | Tidal Generator, on the top floor, near the teleporter | 2,500 (2,000 according to some sources) |
| **Exo Soldier** | Switches weapons faster, better hip fire, shoots and reloads while sprinting or sliding | Lounge, opposite the Weapon Upgrade Station | 1,750 |

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="Call of Duty" source="https://www.callofduty.com/blog/archives/pro-tips--exo-zombies-part-four-descent">
    Get **Exo Reload** if you use the Blunderbuss: it only has one shot before a long reload.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="GameCraves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-reckoning-dlc-trophy-guide/">
    For the round 20 Oz fight, every upgrade helps except Exo Slam. In Double Feature, the GameCraves team only bought Exo Health and Exo Reload.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="GameCraves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-reckoning-dlc-trophy-guide/">
    In Double Feature mode, Exo Medic does not revive you in solo.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
