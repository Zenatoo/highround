**Double Feature** ("Séance Double" in the French version) is Descent's hard mode, in black and white like an old movie. The goal: kill Oz a third and final time, in round 20. Video creators call it Descent's second ending: you earn the "Double Tap" achievement and the Reception's **gold trident**.

## Start the mode

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Finish the main Easter Egg">
    The mode unlocks with "Reunion". At the end of the Easter Egg, the map restarts straight into Double Feature. See [Main Easter Egg (Reunion)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use the keypad in the starting area">
    In a new game, **as soon as you spawn**, turn around: the Double Feature keypad is right behind you, in the starting area. Use it.

    In co-op, **every player** has to use it to vote. It also works if the host has already finished the Easter Egg, or in a local game.

## What changes

- **Black and white** screen and **no more HUD**: only your credit count stays on screen. You can no longer see your weapons, ammo or upgrades.
- Zombies have about **twice as much health**.
- You only earn credits **by killing**, not by hitting.
- **Nobody can be revived.** A downed player comes back the next round if the others finish it. In solo, Exo Medic doesn't revive you: it's almost impossible alone.
- No infection timer, no health bar for Oz.
- Only upside: a weapon upgrade costs only **1,500 credits** instead of 2,500.

## The GameCraves strategy

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Rounds 1 and 2: knife in the starting area">
    Only get melee kills, then open the Reception door and go to the 3D printer. If nobody got the **Blunderbuss** before the printer moves, restart the game.
  </Step>

  <Step title="One player with the Blunderbuss in the Spa">
    The Blunderbuss player circles the **Spa** until the end. They get Exo Health and Exo Reload, then put all their credits into upgrading their weapon. The others open the doors for them, only get Exo Health and Exo Reload, and use the 3D printer. Each player should have at least a Cauterizer, a Blunderbuss, a Trident or an S12 shotgun. Distraction drones help a lot against Oz.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The fixed rounds">
    Rounds **4 and 12** are dog rounds: easier, with a free Full Reload. Round **8** and a later round are infection rounds: group up in the Spa, because you can't see who is infected anymore.

    {/* TO CHECK: fixed dog rounds 4 and 12 and infected round 8 = GameCraves only. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Don't touch the orbital care packages">
    Don't even pick them up before the first Oz fight: if you die with a care package in reserve, it is lost.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Round 13: the first fight">
    The Trident is the most useful here. Lead the zombies into the arena, avoid the traps, destroy the turrets and heal on the mat. See [The Oz fights](../combats-contre-oz/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Rounds 14 to 19: prepare for the end">
    Everyone gets at least Exo Health. At the end of round 19, keep a few zombies alive and pick up every care package: Sentinel reinforcements, assault drones, Goliath. Players without a Blunderbuss get into a Goliath as soon as the round ends.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Round 20: Oz, quarter by quarter">
    - **1st quarter**: turn on the generators right away and deal the first quarter of damage.
    - **2nd quarter**: same thing. Stay in the Goliaths as long as you can.
    - **3rd quarter**: the wait is longer: throw all the assault drones.
    - **4th quarter**: call in the Sentinel reinforcements, one after the other if you have several. Use everything you have left, then lure Oz to the middle and finish him with the Blunderbuss.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The reward

Killing Oz in Double Feature unlocks the "**Double Tap**" achievement. In your next game of Descent, the **gold trident** is on display in the Reception. See [The Reception trophies](../trophees-de-la-reception/).
