The **Goliath suit** is a new orbital care package on Descent: a big robot armor that you pilot. You use it for the Easter Egg, for the final Oz fight and for two achievements.

<Screenshot src={m + 'goliath_suit.webp'} alt="A Goliath suit" credit={by} source={src} />

## Where and when to find it

- It drops **at random**, from **round 8**. Usually, two or three drop before round 20.
- When the Goliath icon shows up on screen, check the **roof above Exo Health**, at the top of the Tidal Generator (go through the Galleria stairs). If it isn't there, take the nearby teleporter and look in the **Landing Area**.

{/* TO CHECK: "from round 8" and "two or three before round 20" = GameCraves; mmmrkennedy says one normally shows up by round 8-12; FOC gaming says about 3 before round 20. */}

## The controls

- A **machine gun** that never overheats.
- A single **rocket**.
- A lock-on **rocket barrage**.
- Melee **punches**.

While you are inside, zombies can't kill you, but the Goliath's health bar, at the top of the screen, **goes down over time** and faster when you take hits. You can still use the teleporters with it.

## What it's for

<Checklist>
  <Step title="The Easter Egg">
    You need a Goliath to go out through the Lounge airlock and hit the switch at the bottom of the sea. See [Main Easter Egg (Reunion)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="K.O. achievement">
    Kill **20 enemies with punches** in a Goliath in one game. You can use several Goliaths. Go for dogs and normal or infected zombies: EMP, armored and teleporting zombies take several hits. Do it at the start of a round, when there are lots of zombies.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The final fight and the Size DOES Matter achievement">
    Keep a Goliath for round 20. As soon as round 19 ends, get in: you arrive in the arena already suited up. When Oz loses his shield on the central mat, **punch him** to unlock "Size DOES Matter". See [The Oz fights](../combats-contre-oz/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="GameCraves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-reckoning-dlc-trophy-guide/">
    In Double Feature mode, don't pick up orbital care packages before the first Oz fight: if you die with a care package in reserve, it is lost.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
