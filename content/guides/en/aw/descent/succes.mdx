Descent (Reckoning pack) has **10 trophies** (achievements on Xbox and PC): 8 bronze and 2 silver. Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device. The names are the ones from the English version of the game.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Smooth Operator (bronze)">
    Beat Oz a first time, in the round 13 fight. See [The Oz fights](../combats-contre-oz/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="How the mighty have fallen! (bronze)">
    Beat Oz a second time, in his mutated form, in round 20. See [The Oz fights](../combats-contre-oz/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reunion (silver)">
    Beat Oz's challenges: this is the main Easter Egg. See [Main Easter Egg (Reunion)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Double Tap (silver)">
    Beat Oz one last time: in round 20 of Double Feature mode, which unlocks with "Reunion". It's the hardest trophy of the pack. See [Double Feature mode](../seance-double/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Big Game (bronze)">
    Kill mutated Oz, in round 20, with a Blunderbuss: when he is almost out of health, let the Blunderbuss player land the final blow. See [The Trident and the Blunderbuss](../trident-et-tromblon/).

    {/* TO CHECK: GameCraves says the PS3 version asks for the Lynx sniper rifle instead (single source). */}

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="tech" author="GameCraves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-reckoning-dlc-trophy-guide/">
        On PS3, this trophy needs the Lynx (a sniper rifle from the 3D printer) instead of the Blunderbuss.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Coast 2 Coast (bronze)">
    Cross the water with a **teleport grenade** (Telefrag), sold in the Lounge (750 credits). Throw it from the starting platform to the top of the Tidal Generator, or the other way around. A missed throw costs nothing: try again as many times as you want. Orbital care packages dropped over there make good markers.

    {/* Telefrag price 750: GameCraves (4 grenades for 750) and COD Zombified's Lounge map ("Teleport Grenades costs 750"); Video Games Blogger says 500. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Size DOES Matter (bronze)">
    Punch mutated Oz with a Goliath suit, when he has lost his shield on the central mat. See [Pilot the Goliath](../goliath/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="K.O. (bronze)">
    Kill 20 enemies with punches in a Goliath, in one game (you can use several Goliaths). See [Pilot the Goliath](../goliath/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Nah, I'm Good. (bronze)">
    Reach round 7 without anyone opening a door. Both doors of the starting area stay closed. You can buy the wall weapons (MK14 and Bulldog) and Exo Medic, and going down or dying doesn't count. It's easy in solo.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Trickshot (bronze)">
    Kill 100 enemies behind you with the KL03-Trident, in one game. Lead the zombies into the Spa, run toward a wall and shoot it: the shots bounce back onto the ones following you. See [The Trident and the Blunderbuss](../trident-et-tromblon/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

The gold trophy in the Reception is not an achievement: see [The Reception trophies](../trophees-de-la-reception/).
