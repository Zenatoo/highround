On Descent, like on every Exo Zombies map, there is **no single power switch**: each area has its own, which powers the machines around it (Exo upgrades, 3D printer). There are **six** on the map. Doors and wall weapons work without power.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Start in the Landing Area">
    You spawn on a small platform, the **Landing Area**. You find a switch there, the Exo Medic upgrade, the MK14 and Bulldog wall weapons, a credit machine and **two doors** at 750 credits. Turn on the switch right away.

    Once you go down into the complex, you can't walk back up: you have to take the **transport tubes** (two exits in the Landing Area, one in the Reception, one in the Concierge).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Reach the Galleria and get the Exo suit">
    The shortest way: the Landing Area door to the **Concierge** (750), then the Great Hall door to the **Galleria** (2,000). Total: 2,750 credits.

    The Exo suit machine is in the Galleria, upstairs, on the round platform. The suit is **free**: double jump, boosts, slide and Exo punch. The Galleria switch is at the top of the stairs, near the Tidal Generator door: turn it on.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'exo_suit.webp'} alt="The Exo suit machine in the Galleria" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn on the other switches">
    Each one powers the machines in its area (table below). Turn them on as you open up the map.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The six switches

| Area | Where |
| --- | --- |
| **Landing Area** | In the middle of the platform, under the Exo Medic |
| **Great Hall** | At the back of the Great Hall, between the Spa and Galleria doors |
| **Galleria** | Upstairs, at the top of the stairs, near the Tidal Generator door |
| **Annex** | Against the back wall, between the Galleria and Lounge doors |
| **Lounge** | In the middle, at the foot of the small staircase, under the Exo upgrade |
| **Tidal Generator** | On the ground floor, in the corner opposite the entrance |

## The doors

| Door | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Landing Area to the Reception | 750 |
| Landing Area to the Concierge | 750 |
| Reception to the Concierge | 750 |
| Reception to the Promenade | 750 |
| Promenade to the Annex | 750 |
| Galleria (upstairs) to the Tidal Generator | 1,000 |
| Great Hall to the Spa (two doors) | 1,250 each |
| Annex to the Lounge (two doors) | 1,250 each |
| Great Hall to the Galleria | 2,000 |
| Annex to the Galleria | 2,000 |

{/* TO CHECK: FOC gaming says turning on a switch gives 100 credits (single source). */}

According to one guide, each switch you turn on also gives you **100 credits**: in co-op, leave it to the player saving up for a door.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="COD Zombified" source="https://codzombified.blogspot.com/2015/08/spa-map-layout-for-descent-exo-zombies.html">
    Open the **Spa** early, around round 3 or 4. It is the only area of the map with a decontamination mat: an infected player who can't afford the door dies in front of it.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="GameCraves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-reckoning-dlc-trophy-guide/">
    The map has a lot of doors, and several are expensive. For the "Nah, I'm Good." achievement, don't open either of the two Landing Area doors before round 7.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
