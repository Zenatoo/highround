Oz, now a zombie, is the boss of Descent. In **every game**, he teleports you into his arena at fixed rounds. You don't need to do the Easter Egg for this.

<Screenshot src={m + 'arena.webp'} alt="Oz's arena" credit={by} source={src} />

## Round 5: the warm-up

You are teleported into the arena. Oz talks to you from behind a window for 30 to 60 seconds, then you are sent back to the map. There is nothing to do.

<Screenshot src={m + 'oz_behind_window.webp'} alt="Oz behind his window in round 5" credit={by} source={src} />

## Round 13: the first fight

{/* TO CHECK: round 13 (official Pro Tips blog, GameCraves, jeuxvideo.com, Flapix title) vs "round 12, or 13 if 12 was a dog round" (mmmrkennedy). GameCraves says round 12 is always a dog round, so both point to round 13 in practice. */}

**Get ready during round 12**: save credits, keep your orbital care packages, and get good weapons and your Exo upgrades.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Shoot Oz when his window opens">
    Oz is behind one of the **four windows** up high. After his speech, a loud noise tells you the window is opening: shoot until his booth explodes. He then moves to another window. You have to destroy **all four booths**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'open_window.webp'} alt="Oz's window open" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Avoid his traps">
    After each booth, Oz adds one more trap. At the last one, he uses them all:

    - **Lasers**: yellow at the top of the arena, they turn red when active. Touching them does little damage but **shuts down your Exo suit**: don't double jump.
    - **Gas**: around the pillars, it infects you. Cure yourself on the **decontamination mat** in the middle of the arena, usable as often as you like.
    - **Electric floor**: part of the floor deals damage, wait for it to turn off.
    - **Turrets** on the wall, on the ceiling and in the doors under the booths: destroy them fast.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'lasers.webp'} alt="The lasers at the top of the arena" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'gas.webp'} alt="The gas around the pillars" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'decon_pad.webp'} alt="The decontamination mat in the middle" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'electric_floor.webp'} alt="The electric floor" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'sentry_gun.webp'} alt="A floor turret" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'wall_turret.webp'} alt="A wall turret" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Collect the reward">
    When the fourth booth explodes, a DNA Bomb clears the arena and you get a Full Reload. Credit crates drop: pick them up before the 10-second countdown ends and sends you back to the map. The "Smooth Operator" achievement unlocks.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'credits_drop.webp'} alt="A credit crate" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="GameCraves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-reckoning-dlc-trophy-guide/">
    In the arena, killing zombies gives you **no credits**. There is a 3D printer and a Weapon Upgrade Station there, and care packages with a Full Reload drop from time to time.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="faster" author="GameCraves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-reckoning-dlc-trophy-guide/">
    If someone has distraction drones, throw them every time a window opens. The Trident is very useful here, more than the Blunderbuss, whose range is too short to hit Oz well in his booth.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Round 20: mutated Oz

Oz comes back as a big mutated creature, on the ground, with a health bar at the top of the screen. He is protected by a **shield**.

<Screenshot src={m + 'mutated_oz.webp'} alt="Mutated Oz" credit={by} source={src} />

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on the four generators">
    They are in the **four corners** of the arena, against the wall. They power the decontamination mat in the middle.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Trigger the mat when Oz is on it">
    Lure Oz to the middle and activate the mat when he walks onto it. He stops and loses his shield for a few seconds: unload your best weapons on him.

    The game caps the damage: **a quarter of his health at most** each time you use the mat.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Repeat three times">
    After each use, turn the four generators back on and wait for Oz to come back. With each quarter, the wait is longer and the traps from the first fight come back. His punches infect you and he fires EMP rockets.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Collect the reward">
    Same as the first fight: Full Reload, DNA Bomb and credit crates. The "How the mighty have fallen!" achievement unlocks. The game then goes on.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="GameCraves" source="https://gamecraves.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-reckoning-dlc-trophy-guide/">
    The best weapons against mutated Oz: the Blunderbuss (8 to 10 shots to kill him), the CEL-3 Cauterizer, the S12 shotgun or a machine gun. Keep a Goliath and Sentinel reinforcements for this fight: get into the Goliath right at the end of round 19.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

Three achievements happen here: "Big Game" (the final blow with the Blunderbuss), "Size DOES Matter" (a punch in a Goliath) and, in Double Feature mode, "Double Tap". See [The Trident and the Blunderbuss](../trident-et-tromblon/), [Pilot the Goliath](../goliath/) and [Double Feature mode](../seance-double/).
