In Exo Zombies, Pack-a-Punch is called the **Weapon Upgrade Station**. It upgrades your weapon **one level per use**, up to Mk 20. On Descent, a second, hidden station goes up to **Mk 25**.

## Up to Mk 20

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Find the Weapon Upgrade Station">
    There are two: in the **Galleria** (downstairs, opposite the fountain) and in the **Lounge** (at the back, in front of the big window). A third one appears in Oz's arena, during the round 13 fight.

    {/* Galleria and Lounge: COD Zombified's annotated maps, both "costs 2500" (a blocked-wiki snippet also named the Lounge). TO CHECK: the arena station = GameCraves only. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upgrade your weapon level by level">
    Each level costs **2,500 credits** and refills your ammo. In Double Feature mode, a level only costs **1,500 credits**.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="Call of Duty" source="https://www.callofduty.com/blog/archives/pro-tips--exo-zombies-part-four-descent">
    If you have the Trident, make it your top upgrade priority: the whole team benefits. See [The Trident and the Blunderbuss](../trident-et-tromblon/).
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## The Mk 25

The Mk 25 station is in the **small secret room of the Tidal Generator**, the one the Easter Egg opens at the start. It only works from a specific step of the quest.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Open the Tidal Generator secret room">
    Turn the three valves on the spiral staircase: a door opens halfway up. This is the first step of the [Main Easter Egg (Reunion)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Progress in the Easter Egg up to the number panel">
    When the bottom row of the number panel matches the top row, the Mk 25 station in the secret room gets power.

    {/* TO CHECK: mmmrkennedy only says the number panel powers the Mk 25 station. COD Zombified and Video Games Blogger only say the station is in the room that opens with the valves. */}

    <Screenshot src={m + 'number_display.webp'} alt="The Tidal Generator number panel" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take a Mk 20 weapon to Mk 25">
    Put a weapon that is already **Mk 20** in the station.

    {/* TO CHECK: on the other maps the Mk 25 station costs 2,500, works once, on one Mk 20 weapon, then breaks (Escapist, PrestigeIsKey, GamingBolt, GameCraves on Outbreak). No source states these rules for Descent. */}

    On the other maps, this station only works **once**, for a single weapon: choose carefully. Our sources don't confirm this for Descent yet.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'mk_25_upgrade_station.webp'} alt="The Mk 25 station in the secret room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>
