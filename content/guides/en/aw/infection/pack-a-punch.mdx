In Exo Zombies, the Pack-a-Punch is called the **Weapon Upgrade Station**. You don't need to unlock it: you just open the room it is in.

## Normal upgrades (up to Mk 20)

- Each use costs **2,500 credits** and raises your weapon by **one level** (Mk 1, Mk 2…). It also refills your ammo.
- Each level adds damage, sometimes an attachment, and changes the camo.
- The maximum is **Mk 20**.

There are **two stations** on Infection:

| Area | Where | To get in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Value Voltage** | Inside the gas station | 1,000 door |
| **Burger Town** | In the storage room, at the back of the restaurant (Burger Town Storage) | 1,000 door |

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare/Infection">
    Each survivor you save gives **one free level** to the weapon in your hands when the countdown ends. See [Survivor rescue](../sauvetage-des-survivants/).
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Going past Mk 20

A third, more powerful station is hidden **inside the giant burger** on the roof of Burger Town. It raises a weapon **beyond Mk 20**, up to Mk 25 and its diamond camo.

You don't need to finish the Easter Egg: you only need to get as far as the **key** that Bubby, the mascot, gives you.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Play the Easter Egg up to Bubby's key">
    Prepare the pan, cook the burger, give it to Bubby, then charge his battery. In exchange for the battery, he gives you a key. Every step is in [Main Easter Egg (Meat Is Murder)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the giant burger">
    Climb the ladder up to the giant burger, on the roof of Burger Town, and open the hatch with the key. The station is inside.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'main_ee/hatch.webp'} alt="The giant burger's hatch" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upgrade a Mk 20 weapon">
    First raise your weapon to Mk 20 at the normal stations, then use the one in the burger.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
