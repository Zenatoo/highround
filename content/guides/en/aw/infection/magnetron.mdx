The **Magnetron** is Infection's special weapon: an automatic microwave gun. It cooks zombies from the inside until they burst. It looks a bit like the EM1, Outbreak's energy weapon.

## Getting it

You can't buy the Magnetron off a wall: it comes **only from the 3D printer**, at random, for **1,000 credits** a roll. So it can take several tries.

- The active printer moves after a few rolls. Its five locations are in [3D printer and special weapons](../imprimante-3d/).
- The **Power Surge** bonus turns on every printer on the map for 30 seconds, at **10 credits** a roll: that's the best time to look for the Magnetron.

## What it's for

- **The main Easter Egg**: you need it to cook the meat in the pan. See [Main Easter Egg (Meat Is Murder)](../easter-egg-principal/).
- **The Popcorn achievement**: cook 50 zombies with the Magnetron. See [All 10 achievements and trophies](../succes/).
- **Close combat**: it is very strong up close, ideal in the narrow hallways of the Sewers.

## Upgrading it

You upgrade the Magnetron like the other weapons, level by level, at the Weapon Upgrade Stations. It can even go past Mk 20 thanks to the station hidden in the giant burger. See [Upgrade your weapons (Mk 20 and Mk 25)](../pack-a-punch/).
