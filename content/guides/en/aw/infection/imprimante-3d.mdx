In Exo Zombies, the mystery box is called the **3D Printer**. It makes a random weapon for **1,000 credits**. The weapon shown on the screen doesn't count: the roll is random. If you already have two weapons, the one in your hands gets replaced.

## The five locations

Only one printer is active at a time. After a few rolls, it breaks down and shows up somewhere else. On the minimap, its locations are marked with a **question mark in a square**.

{/* Value Voltage, Atlas Command and the Pit: IGN + COD Zombified (the weebly fan site wrongly says there is none in the Sewers). TO CHECK: Hoard More and Burger Town = IGN only (COD Zombified's Burger Town map shows no printer). */}

| Area | Where |
| --- | --- |
| **Atlas Command** | In the Command Center (1,000 door). This is where it is at the start of the game. |
| **Hoard More** | At the top of the stairs, west side of the warehouse |
| **Value Voltage** | Inside the gas station (1,000 door) |
| **The Pit** | Near the decontamination, in the Sewers |
| **Burger Town** | Inside the restaurant |

## The special weapons

The printer mostly gives regular weapons, but also two special weapons:

- the **Magnetron**, Infection's microwave gun, which you can only get here. You need it for the Easter Egg. See [The Magnetron](../magnetron/).
- the **CEL-3 Cauterizer**, Outbreak's special weapon, which comes back on this map.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare/Exo_Zombies">
    The **Power Surge** bonus turns on every printer for 30 seconds, at **10 credits** a roll. Rush to the nearest one.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare/Infection">
    If a survivor dies, the printers stop working for 60 seconds. See [Survivor rescue](../sauvetage-des-survivants/).
  </Tip>
</Tips>
