In Exo Zombies, perks are called **Exo upgrades**. There are five on Infection, one per area (except The Pit), sold in wall lockers. To buy one, you need:

- the **Exo suit** (see [Get the Exo suit and turn on the power](../courant/));
- the area's **power station** turned on.

{/* TO CHECK: Exo Health price. 2,500 per Accelerated Ideas, Se7enSins, GameCraves and IGN; 2,000 per Millenium, Video Games Blogger and Jugga-Nog. Exo Medic solo price (500) is only sourced for Outbreak, so it is left out here. */}

| Upgrade | Effect | Where | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Exo Medic** | Revive your teammates faster; in solo, revives you on your own | Hoard More (the start), north side of the warehouse | 1,500 |
| **Exo Soldier** | You shoot and reload while sprinting or sliding, switch weapons faster and shoot better without aiming | Value Voltage, outside, west side | 1,750 |
| **Exo Slam** | Jump then hit the ground: kills at the point of impact and stuns around it | Atlas Command, east side, near a container | 2,000 |
| **Exo Reload** | You reload much faster | North Sewers, in its small room (you get in by dropping down) | 2,000 |
| **Exo Health** | You take twice as many hits before going down | Burger Town, inside the restaurant, near the Carousel | 2,500 (2,000 according to some sources) |

When you are down, you lose your upgrades **one by one**, as long as nobody revives you.

There is no Exo Stockpile on Infection: that upgrade only arrives with Carrier.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare/MEAT_IS_MURDER">
    For the main Easter Egg, get Exo Health first: you can take more hits during all the back and forth. See [Main Easter Egg (Meat Is Murder)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare/Infection">
    If a survivor dies, the power stations turn off: the lockers stop working until you turn their station back on. See [Survivor rescue](../sauvetage-des-survivants/).
  </Tip>
</Tips>
