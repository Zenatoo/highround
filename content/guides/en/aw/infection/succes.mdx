Infection has **10 trophies** (achievements on Xbox and PC): 8 bronze and 2 silver. They are part of the Ascendance DLC. Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Alright, Alright (bronze)">
    Survive until round 10.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Climbing the Corporate Ladder (bronze)">
    Survive until round 25. Open up the map, get the Exo upgrades and upgrade your weapons: see [Exo upgrades and where to find them](../atouts/) and [Upgrade your weapons (Mk 20 and Mk 25)](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="One Man's Poison... (bronze)">
    Kill 5 host zombies at once with the decontamination station: every zombie on the platform dies when it goes off. See [Special zombies and the Goliath](../ennemis/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Indirect Fire (bronze)">
    Kill 100 zombies with traps. See [The map's traps](../pieges/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Burgertown Escort Service (bronze)">
    Save 1 survivor. See [Survivor rescue](../sauvetage-des-survivants/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Abandon All Hope (silver)">
    Save 4 survivors in the same game.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Blood-Spattered Savior (bronze)">
    Save 20 survivors in total. The counter carries over from one game to the next.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Love Tap (bronze)">
    Kill a Goliath with a melee attack. The first one shows up around round 10. See [Special zombies and the Goliath](../ennemis/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Popcorn (bronze)">
    Cook 50 zombies with the Magnetron. See [The Magnetron](../magnetron/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="MEAT IS MURDER (silver)">
    Activate Bubby: it's the end of the main Easter Egg. This achievement is secret. See [Main Easter Egg (Meat Is Murder)](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>
