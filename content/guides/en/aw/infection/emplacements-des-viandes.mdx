This guide goes with the [Main Easter Egg (Meat Is Murder)](../easter-egg-principal/): it lists where to look for the valves and the pieces of meat.

## The four valves

The valves are in the **Sewers**. They are **orange wheels** on **yellow pipes**. Four appear at random among **32 spots**. A valve you have already turned lets out steam.

<Screenshot src={m + 'valve_example.webp'} alt="An orange valve on a yellow pipe" credit={by} source={src} />

The 32 spots are shown one by one in mmmrkennedy's video (Videos tab). Turn up your screen brightness: the sewers are very dark.

## The pieces of meat

Only one piece appears in each of the four areas below (none in Hoard More). There are **36 possible spots**. You can only carry one piece at a time: bring it back to the pan before looking for the next one.

<Screenshot src={m + 'meat_example.webp'} alt="A piece of meat" credit={by} source={src} />
