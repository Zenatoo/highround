The goal: cook a burger for **Bubby**, the Burger Town mascot, charge his battery, then launch the rocket hidden in the giant burger on the roof. The "MEAT IS MURDER" achievement (silver) unlocks at the end.

**Before you start:**

- Plan for **30 to 45 minutes**. You can do it solo or with up to four players.
- Open **every door** on the map: you will cross every area several times (prices in [Get the Exo suit and turn on the power](../courant/)).
- You need the **Magnetron**, which comes at random from the 3D printer. That's the only luck involved. See [The Magnetron](../magnetron/).
- Get **Exo Health** to take more hits, and keep **250 credits** for the decontamination.
- At the end of a round, leave one or two **legless** zombies alive: you can work in peace without the next round starting. Keep an eye on them so they don't die on their own.

## Part 1: the golden pan

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get the Exo suit">
    It is in the south Sewers. You need to open a Hoard More door (1,500), then a Sewers gate (2,000). Details in [Get the Exo suit and turn on the power](../courant/).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'exo_suit.webp'} alt="The Exo suit station in the Sewers" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get the golden pan in Hoard More">
    Go back to the starting warehouse. Climb onto the highest crate, then boost jump to the cracked roof, on the north side: the **golden pan** sits on a beam. Pick it up.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'golden_pan.webp'} alt="The golden pan on the cracked roof of Hoard More" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn the four valves in the Sewers">
    In the sewers, look for **four orange valves** on yellow pipes. They appear at random among 32 spots. Turn each one: steam comes out once it's done. Turn up the brightness if you can't see anything.

    {/* TO CHECK: order. IGN, mmmrkennedy and COD Zombified give no order; Millenium says each valve makes the next one appear, VCPost says "in the correct order". */}

    <Screenshot src={m + 'valve_example.webp'} alt="An orange valve on a pipe" credit={by} source={src} />

    The 32 spots are in [Meat and valve locations](../emplacements-des-viandes/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go behind the waterfall">
    After the fourth valve, a secret passage opens **behind the small waterfall**, in the south of the Sewers, between the Insta-Gator trap and the power station. It leads to an **altar**.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'secret_passage.webp'} alt="The secret passage behind the waterfall" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'altar.webp'} alt="The altar" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill the pan with blood">
    Place the pan on the altar: it sinks. Kill zombies **in the lower part** of the room until the pan is full of blood. The altar then rises again: take the pan back.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'lower_area.webp'} alt="The lower part of the room, where kills count" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place the pan on the Burger Town stoves">
    Go to the restaurant kitchen and place the pan on the stoves.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'stoves.webp'} alt="The stoves in the Burger Town kitchen" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Part 2: the burger

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Pick up the four pieces of meat">
    One piece appears in each of these areas: **Burger Town, Sewers, Value Voltage and Atlas Command** (none in Hoard More). You can only carry **one at a time**: bring each piece back to the pan before going for the next one.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'meat_example.webp'} alt="A piece of meat" credit={by} source={src} />

    The 36 possible spots, with pictures: [Meat and valve locations](../emplacements-des-viandes/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Cook the meat with the Magnetron">
    When the four pieces are in the pan, shoot it with the **Magnetron** until you hear a **"ding"**. The meat turns into a burger patty.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get the bun">
    Kill the zombies in **Burger Town uniform** (with the cap), mostly around the restaurant. One of them eventually drops a **burger bun**: walk over it to pick it up. Not all of them drop it: keep at it.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'bun.webp'} alt="The burger bun dropped by a zombie employee" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put it together and take the burger">
    Use the bun on the patty in the pan, then pick up the finished burger.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare/Infection">
        As soon as you take the burger, **you are infected**. You have 60 seconds to get cured, and each hit you take removes 5.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Decontaminate yourself (and the burger)">
    Run to the **decontamination** in The Pit, in the Sewers, and pay 250 credits. The fastest way: the **chute** on the side of Burger Town, which drops into the sewers. The burger stays in your inventory, cleaned too.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give the burger to Bubby">
    Bubby is the mascot in front of Burger Town, at the drive-through speaker. Give him the burger: he eats it, then complains that his **batteries are dead**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'bubby.webp'} alt="Bubby, the Burger Town mascot" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Part 3: Bubby's battery

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get the battery out in Atlas Command">
    Go into the Atlas Command **Command Center** (the prefab building). A screen now shows Bubby's blueprint: use it. Right away, run to the **other building**, the one with the power station, near the Sewers entrance. A small wall box opens with the **battery**: take it.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'command_unit_1.webp'} alt="The Atlas Command Command Center" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'bubby_screen.webp'} alt="The screen with Bubby's blueprint" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'command_unit_2.webp'} alt="The power station building" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'battery_box.webp'} alt="The battery box" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        The box closes again if you take too long. If it does, use the screen again and start over.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Charge the battery in Value Voltage">
    Place the battery on an electric car **charging station** in Value Voltage. Zombies are drawn to the station and can knock the battery off: put it back. When the light goes from **blue to red**, it's full: take it back.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'charging_station.webp'} alt="The Value Voltage charging station" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="COD Zombified" source="https://codzombified.blogspot.com/2015/04/exo-zombies-infection-easter-egg-guide_9.html">
        Charge it between two rounds, when there are almost no zombies left.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give the battery to Bubby">
    He gives you a **key**: pick it up.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Part 4: the rocket

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Go inside the giant burger">
    Climb the ladder up to the **giant burger**, on the roof of Burger Town, and open the hatch with the key. Inside, use Bubby's screen: it turns red, **"Access Denied"**. That's normal.

    The burger also holds a station that upgrades weapons beyond Mk 20: see [Upgrade your weapons (Mk 20 and Mk 25)](../pack-a-punch/).

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'hatch.webp'} alt="The giant burger's hatch" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'bubby_screen_burger.webp'} alt="Bubby's screen inside the burger" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get a zombie arm with the Insta-Gator">
    Go down to the south Sewers and turn on the **Insta-Gator** trap (500 credits), near The Pit. When an alligator comes out of the water to grab a zombie, **shoot it** quickly: it explodes and leaves a floating **zombie arm**. Pick it up before it sinks.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'insta_gator_trap.webp'} alt="The Insta-Gator trap panel" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'arm.webp'} alt="The floating zombie arm" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>

    {/* TO CHECK: toilet fallback, COD Zombified only. */}

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="tech" author="COD Zombified" source="https://codzombified.blogspot.com/2015/04/exo-zombies-infection-easter-egg-guide_3.html">
        If the arm doesn't appear, flush the toilet with the green ring, upstairs at the back of Burger Town, then start the trap again.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Hoard More safe">
    Climb the stairs of the starting warehouse up to the **fingerprint reader**. Use the arm on it: the safe next to it opens. Take the **red tablet**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'fingerprint_reader.webp'} alt="The fingerprint reader upstairs in Hoard More" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch the rocket">
    Go back into the giant burger and use the red tablet on the "Access Denied" screen. You get thrown outside. The burger spits out **bombs**, then a **rocket** lifts off after a short countdown.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        You can pick up the bombs by walking over them and throw them at zombies. But they hurt you if they explode near you, and the ones nobody picks up end up exploding.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give the blue tablet to Bubby">
    Go back up into the burger: a **blue tablet** waits for you under Bubby's screen. Take it and give it to Bubby. The achievement unlocks.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'blue_tablet.webp'} alt="The blue tablet under Bubby's screen" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The reward

Bubby wakes up. Depending on the game, he either explodes right away and leaves **power-ups**, or he grabs a sign and fights by your side for a while (about one round), then explodes and leaves **four power-ups**.
