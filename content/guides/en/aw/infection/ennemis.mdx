Infection keeps the Outbreak zombies and adds two new ones: the **Goliath** and the **acid zombies**. The map also has its own special rounds.

## The Goliath

It's a zombie in an **XS-1 Goliath** armor suit, the map's mini-boss. The first one shows up around **round 10**.

- It hits with a huge **hammer** and can down you **in two hits**. Stay out of its reach.
- It carries a **shield** that blocks every bullet.
- It uses **EMP** attacks: a shockwave on the ground around it, or a missile from a distance. They shut down your Exo suit for a few seconds.
- It takes a huge amount of damage. In co-op, all shoot it together. Aim for **the head or the back**.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare/Infection">
    **Love Tap** achievement: kill a Goliath with a **melee** attack. Only the last hit counts.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Acid zombies

These zombies drip **drops of acid** as they walk. When they die, they leave a **pool of acid**. Walking in it hurts you: kill them from a distance and watch where you step.

## Infected zombies and decontamination

During **infected rounds**, host zombies (surrounded by green gas) infect you if they hit you. You then have **60 seconds** to reach the **decontamination** in The Pit, in the Sewers, and pay **250 credits**. Each hit you take removes 5 seconds. If the timer hits zero, you turn into a zombie.

Several players can get cured at the same time, and **every zombie on the platform dies** when it goes off. You are also camouflaged for a few seconds.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare/Infection">
    **One Man's Poison...** achievement: kill 5 host zombies at once with the decontamination. Lure a group of host zombies onto the platform in The Pit, then turn it on.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Toxic areas

From time to time, a whole area fills with **green gas** for about one round. If you stay in it, you get infected. Leave the area and wait for the gas to clear.

## The other zombies

- **Dogs**: they come **at the same time as the zombies**, not on their own like on Outbreak. Don't assume the round only has dogs.
- **EMP zombies** (blue sparks): one hit shuts down your Exo suit for about 5 seconds.
- **Exploding zombies**: they blow up near you. Kill them before they reach you, or boost away.
- **Exo zombies**: with their suits, they jump high and rush at you, even without legs.
