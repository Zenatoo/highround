Infection has **six traps**, one per area (two in the Sewers). Each one costs **500 credits** and only works if its area's **power station** is on (see [Get the Exo suit and turn on the power](../courant/)).

{/* 500 cross-checked for five traps: turrets, ambulance, Insta-Gator, camouflage spray and orbital strike (IGN + COD Zombified, + Video Games Blogger for the Insta-Gator). TO CHECK: the Hoard More electric floor = IGN only. */}

| Trap | Area | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Electric floor** | Hoard More, near the MK14 wall weapon | Electrifies the whole warehouse floor |
| **Ambulance** | Value Voltage, panel on the ambulance | Its siren draws zombies and dogs around it |
| **Turrets** | Atlas Command, panel in the Command Center | Two automatic turrets shoot zombies, one at each end of the area |
| **Insta-Gator** | South Sewers, near the pool and The Pit | Alligators burst out of the water and eat the zombies |
| **Camouflage spray** | North Sewers, in a corner near Exo Reload | You are invisible to **every** zombie on the map for 30 seconds |
| **Orbital strike** | Burger Town, on the roof | A series of orbital strikes hits around the restaurant |

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare/Infection">
    The electric floor hurts you too. Stay upstairs or on the warehouse shelves while it runs.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="COD Zombified" source="https://codzombified.blogspot.com/2015/04/infection-exo-zombies-call-of-duty.html">
    The Insta-Gator doesn't pick sides: if you get too close to the water, it eats you too.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare/Infection">
    The ambulance and the electric floor help a lot to protect a survivor waiting for the helicopter in Hoard More. See [Survivor rescue](../sauvetage-des-survivants/).
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Traps and quests

- **Easter Egg**: the Insta-Gator is used to get the zombie arm. See [Main Easter Egg (Meat Is Murder)](../easter-egg-principal/).
- **Indirect Fire achievement**: kill 100 zombies with traps. The **Security** bonus turns on every trap at once: make the most of it.
