Infection's secret song is Bach's **Toccata and Fugue in D minor**, played on the organ. To start it, find **three small golden rockets** and interact with each one. Each rocket plays a few notes: on the third one, the music starts.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Value Voltage">
    Inside the gas station, on a shelf across from the Weapon Upgrade Station. This is the easiest one: it's in plain sight.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'voltage.webp'} alt="The Value Voltage golden rocket" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Atlas Command">
    On the ground, right of the Exo Slam locker, behind a bit of fence, under the scrolling "Atlas Camp Overrun" sign.

    {/* TO CHECK: mmmrkennedy says "on the ground, right of Exo Slam"; COD Zombified says "under the scrolling Atlas Camp Overrun sign, behind a bit of fence". Probably the same spot, merged here. */}

    <Screenshot src={m + 'atlas.webp'} alt="The Atlas Command golden rocket" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Burger Town roof">
    Climb onto the restaurant roof. The rocket is in a gap in the fence, near the big burger sign, behind the satellite dish. It's hard to aim at: press the action button several times around the spot until you hear the organ.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'burger_town.webp'} alt="The golden rocket on the Burger Town roof" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>
