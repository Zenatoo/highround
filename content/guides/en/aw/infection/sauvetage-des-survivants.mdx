Infection adds an optional mission: **Civilian Extraction**. From time to time, a survivor shows up somewhere on the map. You have to escort them to a Warbird (the Atlas helicopter) that comes to pick them up.

## How it works

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Find the survivor">
    After a few rounds, a survivor shows up. Follow the **icon on your screen** to find them. Hurry: they are alone against the zombies.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Escort them to Hoard More">
    You have to bring them to the extraction point: the **top of the crates**, on the Hoard More shelves (the starting warehouse). Stay close to them and kill the zombies that attack them.

    The survivor can take many more hits than a player, but they can die. Watch their **outline**: if it turns **red**, they are about to die.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hold out until the Warbird arrives">
    A countdown starts. The Warbird arrives above the warehouse and shoots the zombies with its machine gun. Keep the survivor alive until the end.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Collect your reward">
    Each player gets **one free upgrade level** on the weapon in their hands **when the countdown ends**. Pull out your best weapon before that. See [Upgrade your weapons (Mk 20 and Mk 25)](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## If the survivor dies

Every player gets the **Power Drain** penalty for **60 seconds**:

- wall weapons, 3D printers and Weapon Upgrade Stations stop working;
- every **power station** turns off, so the Exo upgrades and traps do too.

When the penalty ends, you have to **turn each station back on by hand**. They are listed in [Get the Exo suit and turn on the power](../courant/).

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare/Infection">
    Once the survivor is on the shelves, set off the Hoard More **electric floor**, or the **ambulance siren** in Value Voltage to draw the zombies away from them. See [The map's traps](../pieges/).
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="TheRelaxingEnd" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=juf7bhEZsSc">
    Distraction Drones also help keep the zombies away from the survivor.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="faster" author="GamingBolt" source="https://gamingbolt.com/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare-ascendance-dlc-glitches-meat-is-murder-traps-achievements">
    For Abandon All Hope (4 survivors in the same game), open every door on the map first, and keep grenades and drones to protect the survivors.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## The three achievements

- **Burgertown Escort Service** (bronze): save 1 survivor.
- **Abandon All Hope** (silver): save 4 survivors in the same game.
- **Blood-Spattered Savior** (bronze): save 20 survivors in total, across several games.
