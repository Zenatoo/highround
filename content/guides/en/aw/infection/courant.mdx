Infection takes place in a small town around a **Burger Town** fast-food restaurant. The map has six areas:

- **Hoard More**: the starting warehouse.
- **Value Voltage**: the gas station and its charging stations, to the north.
- **Atlas Command**: the Atlas military camp, to the south.
- **Sewers**: the sewers, which connect everything. A gate splits them in two: the south and the north.
- **The Pit**: a small corner of the sewers, with the decontamination.
- **Burger Town**: the restaurant, which you can only reach through the sewers.

Like on Outbreak, there is **no main power switch**: each area has its own **power station**. Turning it on is free. It powers the Exo upgrades and the traps of its area. Doors and wall weapons work without power.

| Door | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Hoard More to Value Voltage | 1,500 |
| Hoard More to Atlas Command | 1,500 |
| Value Voltage to the Sewers | 2,000 |

| Inside the gas station (Value Voltage) | 1,000 |

The Pit is open, with no door to pay for.

## Get the suit

The Exo suit is **free**. It is in the Exo Suit room, in the **south Sewers**. You need to open two doors: **3,500 credits** in total.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Open a Hoard More door (1,500)">
    Take the north door to Value Voltage, or the south door to Atlas Command. Both lead to the sewers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Sewers gate (2,000)">
    In Value Voltage, it is in the south-east. In Atlas Command, it is in the south-west. Both lead to the south Sewers.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take your suit">
    Follow the hallway north to the Exo suit station. Take one.

    It gives you the double jump, the boost, the boosted slide and the Exo punch. It also lets you buy the Exo upgrades: see [Exo upgrades and where to find them](../atouts/).

  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The six power stations

To find a station, follow the **cables** that run from an Exo upgrade or a trap.

{/* Value Voltage, Atlas Command, Sewers south (Insta-Gator), Sewers north (Exo Reload + camouflage spray) and Burger Town: IGN + COD Zombified (+ mmmrkennedy for Atlas). TO CHECK: Hoard More = IGN only (COD Zombified has no Hoard More map). */}

| Area | Where | What it powers |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hoard More** | South side of the warehouse | Exo Medic and the electric floor |
| **Value Voltage** | Inside the gas station (1,000 door) | Exo Soldier and the ambulance |
| **Atlas Command** | In the building next to the Command Center, near the Sewers entrance | Exo Slam and the turrets |
| **South Sewers** | From the Tac-19 wall weapon, go west then drop down one level | The Insta-Gator |
| **North Sewers** | Around the corner, north of Exo Reload | Exo Reload and the camouflage spray |
| **Burger Town** | In the storage room, at the back of the restaurant (1,000 door) | Exo Health and the orbital strike |

The traps are covered in [The map's traps](../pieges/).

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-advanced-warfare/Infection">
    If a survivor dies, every station turns off for 60 seconds. After that, you have to **turn them back on one by one**. See [Survivor rescue](../sauvetage-des-survivants/).
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Good to know

- **Decontamination**: it is in The Pit. If you are infected, you have **60 seconds** to get there and pay **250 credits**. Each hit you take removes 5 seconds.
- **Toxic areas**: from time to time, a whole area fills with green gas for about one round. Get out, or you get infected.
- **Trash chute**: on the side of Burger Town, a chute drops straight down into the Sewers.
- **Credit terminals**: the wall boxes with a green light give free credits, then recharge.
