There is **no Pack-a-Punch machine** in Gröesten Haus. Instead, a small secret transforms the **Mystery Box**: **every weapon it gives is already upgraded**, and each pull still costs **1,000 Jolts**.

- The secret works **solo and in co-op**.
- It works **only in Gröesten Haus**, **not in the Prologue**. See [The Prologue](../prologue/).
- Allow until about **round 8** to finish it, according to PowerPyx.
- Max Ammo refills all your weapons, including upgraded ones.

## The steps

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Open the Mystery Box room">
    You need the box first: shoot the 10 lanterns. Everything is in [Open the Mystery Box room](../boite-mystere/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get a Jack-in-the-Box">
    Use the Mystery Box until it gives you a **Jack-in-the-Box**. It often comes out in the **first 5 pulls**, and at the latest around the **10th** according to PowerPyx.

    {/* TO CHECK: "within 5 draws, guaranteed within 10" = PowerPyx only (single source). */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Throw it on the beam">
    In front of the box room, upstairs, throw the Jack-in-the-Box **onto a wooden beam**, near the start of the stairs. It has to **stay on the beam**.

    **To check:** the sources don't describe the spot with the same words. They all talk about the same area, upstairs, near the stairs and the Blitz machine.

    {/* TO CHECK: landing spot described four ways. PowerPyx: the wooden beam outside the box room, at the end where the staircase starts. mmmrkennedy: the "metal square" on top of a beam above the staircase near the Blitz machine; the square disappears when the Jack-in-the-Box explodes. Gameranx: the wooden beam where it meets the left wall, below the central beam with the hanging lantern (standing at the top of the stairs, looking at the window). A PowerPyx comment says the spot must be exact. Settle with a capture in game. */}

    - **Metal square** (mmmrkennedy): it is on top of a beam, above the stairs near the Blitz machine. When the Jack-in-the-Box explodes, **the square disappears**: that's your sign that it worked.
    - **Left beam** (Gameranx): stand at the top of the stairs, look at the window, then look up at the big central beam where a lantern hangs. Aim at the spot where the beam meets the **left wall**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'poutre-jack-in-the-box.webp'} alt="The upstairs beamed ceiling, crosshair on the beam where to throw the Jack-in-the-Box" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="mistake" author="PowerPyx (comment reply)" source="https://www.powerpyx.com/cod-ww2-groesten-haus-pack-a-punch-guide/">
        If the piano accepts nothing afterwards, throw **another Jack-in-the-Box** at the same spot. Players say the Jack-in-the-Box has to land in the **exact right place**: throwing it anywhere on the beam isn't always enough.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Earn 10,000 Jolts">
    Kill zombies until you have **10,000 Jolts** (some posts say 9,999). Don't spend anything else until the end.

    {/* TO CHECK: 10,000 = PowerPyx, mmmrkennedy, Gameranx, fan hub; 9,999 = one forum post. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pay the piano">
    The **piano** is upstairs, next to the box room. Hold the **action button** on it (Square on PS4, X on Xbox One). **No message appears.** If it works, your Jolts drop by 10,000.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'piano.webp'} alt="The upstairs piano, next to the Mystery Box room door" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enjoy the box">
    Every weapon pulled from the box is **upgraded automatically**. Keep some Jolts to pull the box as much as you want.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Good to know

- WWII Zombies' normal Pack-a-Punch (**Ubersprengen**) exists on other maps: everything is explained in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/). Here, there is no machine: only the piano changes the Mystery Box.
- To avoid running out of heavy weapons, see [Surviving in the house](../strategies/).
