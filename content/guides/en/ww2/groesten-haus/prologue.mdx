The **Prologue** is the very first part of Nazi Zombies: a small tutorial in a **two-storey farmhouse**. This farmhouse is the same house as the bonus map **Gröesten Haus**. You learn the basics (weapons, armour, Blitz machine), then you can keep surviving there for as long as you stay in the house.

The basics of the mode (Jolts, Special Abilities, Mystery Box…) are explained in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).

## Prologue or Gröesten Haus?

Both names refer to **the same house**. The game offers it in two ways:

| | Prologue | Gröesten Haus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How to get there | It's the tutorial, you can replay it | Bonus map to unlock |
| Machine | A Lebenblitz (according to PowerPyx) | A random Blitz machine upstairs |
| Mods and consumables | Not allowed, Freefire required | Not specified in the sources |
| Hidden piano Pack-a-Punch | Doesn't work | Works |

## Unlock Gröesten Haus

To play the bonus map online, you have to **finish an Easter Egg of The Final Reich**. According to the sources, the simple version is enough: see [Main Easter Egg: Fireworks](../../the-final-reich/easter-egg-principal/).

Offline, it would be available without finishing the quest (single source, to check).

## What's in the house

- One wall weapon, the **MP-40**, on the ground floor.
- An **armour station**: its price goes up with each purchase.
- A Blitz machine (the **Lebenblitz** in the Prologue according to PowerPyx).
- A **shovel** for melee hits.
- A **hidden Mystery Box**: see [Open the Mystery Box room](../boite-mystere/).

In the Prologue, **you can't use mods or consumables**, and you are forced to take the **Freefire** Special Ability with no mods (PowerPyx, for the Mountaineer challenges).

The Prologue **has no real ending**: you can survive until you go down. Don't go too far from the house. If you leave through the front door, you **end the Prologue** (Kotaku, PowerPyx).

## The achievement: Pressure Cooker

**Pressure Cooker**: *In Prologue, survive until wave 20.* It is a bronze achievement, very rare (0.1% of players on PS4 on PlatPursuit, in November 2026).

{/* TO CHECK: timing of Pressure Cooker. Gamepressure: you do not have to fight wave 20's zombies; a PowerPyx commenter: pops at the start of round 20; PowerPyx roadmap text: on reaching round 19. */}

According to the sources, it pops at the **start of wave 20** or when you **reach** wave 20: you don't need to finish it (one source even says wave 19). To do it, open the Mystery Box and follow the route in [Surviving in the house](../strategies/).

The full list of the game's achievements is in [The map's achievements and trophies](../../the-final-reich/succes/).

## The Mountaineer challenge

The **Mountaineer** is a secret character. It is unlocked with **three challenges in the Prologue**:

- Survive **25 waves**.
- Survive **25 waves without going down**.
- Survive **25 waves without opening the secret room**.

The last challenge is the trap: it **forbids the Mystery Box**. So be careful not to shoot the 10 lanterns by accident. You then only have the **MP-40**, the Lebenblitz and armour.
