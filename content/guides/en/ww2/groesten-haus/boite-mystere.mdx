In the house, there is almost nothing at the start: a wall weapon, armour and a machine. To get a **Mystery Box**, you have to find it: it is hidden behind a locked door upstairs. The door opens once you have shot **10 lanterns** scattered around the house and outside it.

- Each successful shot makes a **raven caw** and lights a **purple glow** in the lantern.
- Once all 10 lanterns are hit, the door opens upstairs.
- The box costs **1,000 Jolts** per use, it never changes place and you can use it as many times as you want.
- You can do this **right from the start of the game**.
- It works in **the Prologue** as well as in **Gröesten Haus**: see [The Prologue](../prologue/).

**Warning:** if you are going for the Mountaineer character challenge in the Prologue (25 waves without opening the secret room), **don't shoot all the lanterns**. See [The Prologue](../prologue/).

## The 10 lanterns

The house has two levels: the ground floor (kitchen, main room with the MP-40 wall weapon) and the upper floor (armour, Blitz machine, wall shotgun, room door). The lanterns are **6 downstairs** and **4 upstairs**. Two of the ones downstairs are **outside**, seen through a window: they are the hardest to find.

## After it opens

A door opens upstairs: behind it, the Mystery Box. The general rules of the box (price, possible weapons) are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).

- It can give a weapon, but also a **Jack-in-the-Box**: a spring box that draws zombies in for a few seconds, then explodes and kills them.
- Heavy weapons (machine guns) help the most in this small house.
- A secret lets you receive **all weapons already upgraded**: [The hidden piano Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
- To last longer, see [Surviving in the house](../strategies/).
