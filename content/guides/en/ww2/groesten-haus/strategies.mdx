Gröesten Haus is a **tiny survival map**: a two-storey house, **with no power, no Pack-a-Punch, no main Easter Egg and no traps**. There is nothing to do except hold out as long as possible. Zombies spawn **always in the same places, at the same time**, every round: you can learn the map by heart.

The general rules (Jolts, Special Abilities, mods) are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/). The same house is used as the tutorial: see [The Prologue](../prologue/).

- The random Blitz machine gives you **a random Blitz**: you can't refuse it to get another one.
- Armour gets more and more expensive: only buy it when you really need it.
- Reminder: no Jugger-Nog in WWII. It is the **Lebenblitz** that gets you back up on your own: see [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).
- The Mystery Box is hidden: see [Open the Mystery Box room](../boite-mystere/).

## The starting plan

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Open the Mystery Box from round 1">
    Shoot the 10 lanterns right away. See [Open the Mystery Box room](../boite-mystere/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Look for a heavy weapon and Jack-in-the-Boxes">
    A **machine gun** from the box is the best choice according to Gameranx. **Jack-in-the-Boxes** serve as an emergency exit when you're stuck.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick Freefire">
    Take the **Freefire** Special Ability: it gives infinite ammo for a while. Keep the machine gun in your hands when you activate it, and aim for the **head**. The sources also say it makes the MP-40 useful later.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Aim for the piano Pack-a-Punch">
    When you have 10,000 Jolts set aside, see [The hidden piano Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/). All the weapons from the box are then upgraded.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Making the zombies circle

The basic method is to **loop around the house** so that the zombies follow you in a line. It is described by Gameranx and PowerPyx for the Prologue, the house is the same.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Start downstairs">
    Shoot the zombie that comes in through the **kitchen window**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go up to the armour station">
    Shoot the zombies climbing the stairs and in the corridor.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Jump off the ledge">
    Drop off the ledge near the Blitz machine, keep shooting, then **climb the stairs opposite** up to the armour station. The zombies line up behind you.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start again">
    When they get too close, go back down and do the loop again.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

{/* TO CHECK: the route is Gameranx's (Prologue/"Glausten Haus" article, mentions the Lebenblitz near the ledge). In Gröesten Haus the machine near the ledge is the Random Blitz per other sources. */}

## The Bloodraven Mountaineer challenge

Gröesten Haus has a secret character specific to the map: the **Bloodraven Mountaineer**. It requires **three challenges** (mmmrkennedy says they were confirmed by reading the game's code):

- **Wave 30 without going down** (hint: "And stay out!").
- **Wave 20 without getting hit** (hint: "Tag world champion."). Co-op is recommended. Stay near the **wall Combat Shotgun** from the start. If another player is hit, you can still succeed: you have to reach wave 20 without being hit, not survive 20 clean waves.
- **Kill the Brenner that arrives at wave 50** (hint: "If you can't take the heat...").

{/* TO CHECK: challenge wording and conditions: mmmrkennedy (3 hints + conditions), Accelerated Ideas. Both say the same, but they are not independent reads of the game. Brenner at wave 50 = mmmrkennedy (single source). */}

The **Brenner** is a flamethrower, a mini-boss. According to mmmrkennedy, it arrives at **wave 50**.

For wave 50, joshfloat's video advises upgrading a **Ribeyrolles** and a **De Lisle**, making the **Wüstling** charge at you to trap them, and using the upgraded Ribeyrolles to stun zombies. xNighTMaRez's video shows a solo game with no hits taken until the Brenner dies.

{/* TO CHECK: Wüstling and weapons advice comes only from the description of joshfloat's video YYWzy3-OBcs; wave of the first Wüstling in this map is unknown. */}

The other secret characters are in [Unlock the secret characters](../../the-final-reich/personnages-secrets/).
