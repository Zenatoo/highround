Chapter 2 is played on a **cargo ship** tossed by the sea. You find the **Pommel** of the Sword of Barbarossa here. Do [Easter Egg, chapter 1: Into the Storm](../easter-egg-chapitre-1/) first: the general rules of the quest (fixed rounds, maps to finish once beforehand) are explained there.

**Before you start:**

- In co-op, **all players** must interact with the first battery.
- The quest batteries cost **3,000 Jolts** each.
- The Pack-a-Punch must be unlocked before round 8: see [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).

## Round 1: the vision

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Hit the battery in the Barracks">
    In the **back left corner of the Barracks**, a battery sits on a crate. Hit it with your knife: you enter the **Easter Egg vision**. The screen is covered in electricity, your field of view widens and some zombies sometimes become invisible.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Rounds 3 to 5: escort the fish

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Follow the Red Herring">
    At the end of the round, a **Red Herring** (a red fish) appears in mid-air. Follow it to the **red-floored room**: it stops and creates a battery **charging station**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'red_herring.webp'} alt="The Red Herring floating in mid-air" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Buy a battery">
    A **battery buy** wall appears somewhere on the map: **3,000 Jolts**. It can be:

    - under the boat you spawn under, to the left of Lebenblitz;
    - in the metal bin, to the left of the starting pistol wall weapon;
    - floating above the ship's side, to the left of **Schildblitz**;
    - in the metal bin on the lower deck;
    - under a "boat" to the right of the SMG wall weapon;
    - to the left of **Lebenblitz**.

    Place the battery in the station and kill zombies next to it (about **8 to 10 kills**). The fish then appears above the station and heads to the **Barracks**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'battery_charging_1.webp'} alt="The battery charging in the station of the red-floored room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Do it again">
    Buy another battery and defend the new station. When it is full, the fish goes **to the foot of the cafeteria stairs**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Don't place the third battery yet">
    Unlock the **Pack-a-Punch** first. When you fill the battery, you are **teleported** into an arena. Make sure it is charged **before the end of round 8**.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Round 8: the arena and the cup game

The arena is **not a boss fight**. It is a series of islands that look like other maps, with Straub speaking when you arrive:

1. **The Final Reich**: a Wüstling and zombies.
2. **The Darkest Shore**: a Meuchler and zombies.
3. **The Shadowed Throne**: a Sizzler and zombies.

When the zombies are dead, small islands appear for you to jump to the next one. If you fall, you go back to the previous island **and you are downed**.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Play on the last island">
    A fish appears above the circle on the ground, goes into one of the **cups**, then they shuffle. **Shoot the right cup once they have stopped.** Right answer: the next round comes, with more cups. Wrong answer: a Meuchler and/or a Wüstling appears.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'fish_cup_game.webp'} alt="The shuffled cups on the last island" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the Pommel">
    After **three rounds**, you are back on the cargo ship. The **Pommel** is in a crate on the table in front of you (and the sword, if you have already finished the quest). It gives you **all the perks** and becomes your **tactical grenade**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pommel.webp'} alt="The Pommel in the crate on the table" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The boss: the Stadtjäger

The **Stadtjäger** is the boss of The Shadowed Throne. It is **invulnerable** until it has fired a volley of **three grenades** with its mechanical arm. After they explode, it becomes vulnerable. There is no clear hint: keep circling it and shooting. Bring speed and several upgraded weapons.

Then defend the extraction and move on to [Easter Egg, chapter 3: Beneath the Ice](../easter-egg-chapitre-3/).
