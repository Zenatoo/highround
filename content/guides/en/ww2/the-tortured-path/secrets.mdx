The secrets of The Tortured Path, apart from the main Easter Egg. The Bodega Cervantes duck game and the Pack-a-Punch Upgrade are in [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/). The sword on the survival maps is in [The Sword of Barbarossa](../epee-de-barberousse/).

## The Classic (special PPSh-41)

**The Classic** is a special **PPSh-41**: a **70-round** magazine, more reserve ammo and a faster fire rate.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Look for the flare on the ground">
    On **any map**, at the end of a **round 3, 6 or 9**, a crate is dropped. A **flare** burns on the ground where it lands.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'classic_flare.webp'} alt="The flare on the ground at the drop spot" credit={by} source={srcA} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Drop 1,750 Jolts on it">
    Place **1,750 Jolts** on the flare. The Classic then joins the **list of SMG wall weapons**: after that you have to land on it at random.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The Darkened Path and the Sanguine Skull

**The Darkened Path** is a harder version of the maps: faster zombies, more health, harder objectives.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Reach Shattered rank 50">
    This is The Tortured Path's own rank (see [How The Tortured Path works](../chapitres/)).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the red skull">
    When you load a map, a **red skull** appears in front of you. Activating it starts The Darkened Path.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'red_skull.webp'} alt="The red skull in front of the player when the map loads" credit={by} source={srcA} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish the main Easter Egg in this mode">
    You unlock the **Sanguine Skull**, a weapon charm.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'skull_charm.webp'} alt="The Sanguine Skull weapon charm" credit={by} source={srcA} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The Kontrollgranates (Altar of Blood)

These grenades create a **gas** that makes zombies **friendly**: they attack other zombies. You get them on **Altar of Blood** after the main quest.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Blow up the five Klaus symbols">
    Throw a **Jack-in-the-Box** at each of the five metal Klaus symbols:

    - on a rock behind **Lebenblitz**;
    - on the back wall, between barrels, behind **Schnellblitz**;
    - above the door to the left of **Kugelblitz**, between two pots;
    - above the M1 Garand wall weapon, behind vases (the symbol can't be seen);
    - on the scaffolding across from **Faustblitz**.

## The secret recordings

**To check:** these locations come from a single article, written just after release.

Each chapter hides a recording that unlocks texts in the lobby menus. According to Gameranx:

- **Chapter 1**: on the ceiling of the house with the gramophone. Shoot it, then pick it up from the gramophone.
- **Chapter 2**: in the starting room, one of the hanging zombies holds the recording. Shoot it to make it fall.
- **Chapter 3**: on the scaffolding outside the big ritual room, too high to reach: shoot it.
