On The Tortured Path, the Pack-a-Punch is called the **Ubersprengen**. There is no power to turn on: you activate it by **charging three batteries**. You kill zombies **next to each battery** until it is full. The principle is the same in all three chapters.

The Pack-a-Punch rules (price, tiers) are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).

{/* TO CHECK: Pack-a-Punch price on this map. Game-wide price is in the systems guide (Activision support page); no source confirms it for The Tortured Path. */}

**In the chapters**, the batteries appear at rounds **1, 4 and 7**. In survival mode (after the quest), they appear at rounds **1, 5 and 10**. The first battery is there from the start; you need to kill **about 10** next to it. When a battery is full, its green light goes out or it stops taking energy.

## The Pack-a-Punch Upgrade (survival maps)

After the main Easter Egg, each survival map has a **secret step** that lets you upgrade the **Tesla**, the **Ripsaw** and the **melee weapons** at the Pack-a-Punch into "Shadowed Throne" variants. It gives you a **fuse** that the machine takes by itself. See [Build the Tesla Gun](../../the-final-reich/tesla/), [The Ripsaw and the R.I.P. Saw](../../the-darkest-shore/scie-ripsaw/) and [The three special melee weapons](../../the-shadowed-throne/armes-de-melee/).

### Bodega Cervantes: the duck game

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Take a scoped weapon">
    The **M1 Garand** with a 4x scope is recommended: there is a wall weapon near the start.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the plastic duck">
    On the bridge behind the windmill, a duck sits on the back wall. Shoot it: a shooting gallery starts.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'bodega_duck.webp'} alt="The plastic duck on the bridge wall, behind the windmill" credit={by} source={srcB + 'bodega_cervantes_guide'} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Knock down the 16 ducks">
    The ducks pop up behind the wall: shoot them before they go back down. If you get them all, an animal in a dress with balloons rises to the left of the bridge. If you fail, you can try again next round.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the fuse">
    It is on the ground behind you. It goes into the machine by itself.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'bodega_fuse.webp'} alt="The fuse on the ground behind the player" credit={by} source={srcB + 'bodega_cervantes_guide'} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>
