Last chapter: the underground excavation site ("Beneath the Ice"). You find the **Blade** here, **assemble the Sword of Barbarossa** at the forge and beat the **Guardian**. You need the **Hilt** (chapter 1) and the **Pommel** (chapter 2): see [Easter Egg, chapter 1: Into the Storm](../easter-egg-chapitre-1/) and [Easter Egg, chapter 2: Across the Depths](../easter-egg-chapitre-2/).

The quest's **achievement** is I Have the Power: see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).

**Before you start:**

- The **runes** are picked at random each game, but you can guess the code.
- Remember to charge the Pack-a-Punch at the same time: see [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).

## Round 1: the forge battery

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Break the battery">
    In the **Forge Room**, hit the battery **three times** with your knife to break it. After that, you can interact with it **every other round**: broken at round 1, you use it on even rounds.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'battery.webp'} alt="The Forge Room battery" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Round 2: the first rune code

    - left side: on the ceiling, right at the top of the stairs of the Schnellblitz room;
    - rune room: at the top of the Schnellblitz stairs, to the right of the assault rifle wall weapon, next to a head;
    - rune room: on the left wall, at the top of the **Schildblitz** stairs;
    - right side: halfway up the Schildblitz stairs, on the right wall;
    - right side: on the wall behind Schildblitz;
    - right side: on the ceiling of the door to the Forge Room;
    - right side: on the wall above the SMG wall weapon;

## Round 4: the torches and the second rune

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Take the torches">
    A box of **flares** appears on the stairs between the Forge Room and the start. They replace your tactical grenade, and you only carry **two** at a time.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'box_of_flares.webp'} alt="The box of flares on the Forge Room stairs" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Light the forge bowls">
    Throw a flare into each of the **four stone bowls**, under the head statues of the Forge Room. Take more flares when you need them.

    {/* TO CHECK: number of bowls: mmmrkennedy says four in the Forge Room plus one on the ground; Accelerated Ideas (June 2018) says three ember bowls. */}

    <Screenshot src={m + 'stone_bowls.webp'} alt="The stone bowls under the Forge Room statues" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Light the bowl on the ground">
    A **fire bowl on the ground** appears, to the left of where the crates drop near **Laufenblitz**. Throw a flare into it: a wall opens to the left of Laufenblitz and reveals a **rune**. Pick it up.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'final_stone_bowl.webp'} alt="The fire bowl on the ground, near Laufenblitz" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'open_wall.webp'} alt="The open wall to the left of Laufenblitz with the rune" credit={by} source={src} />

  </Step>

  <Step title="Place the rune and enter a new code">
    Place the rune on the rune wall, then find a **new code** as before. The old runes stay visible: don't reuse them.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Round 5: the Pommel and the sacrificial stones

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Place the Pommel">
    Between rounds, another crate drops in the rune room with the **Pommel**. Place it in the Forge Room's glowing bowl.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'glowing_bowl_pommel.webp'} alt="The Forge Room glowing bowl for the Pommel" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill a sacrificial stone">
    Four **Sacrificial Stones** are on the ground around the map. **All players** stand on a stone at the same time and get kills. When it is enough, a **sound** rings out. There is **no visual indicator**: listen carefully.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'sacrifice_stone.webp'} alt="A sacrificial stone on the ground" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the third rune">
    A rune is in the Forge Room's **blood pool**: it falls from one of the statues. Place it on the wall and enter the third code.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'rune_3.webp'} alt="The rune in the Forge Room's blood pool" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Round 6: the blade and the sword

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Place the Blade">
    A last crate drops with the **Blade**. Place it in the glowing bowl.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'glowing_bowl_blade.webp'} alt="The Forge Room glowing bowl for the Blade" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assemble the sword">

  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The boss: the Guardian

The **Guardian** is a **melee** boss, very fast, that soaks up a lot of damage. Unlike the others, it is not invulnerable: you can hurt it while it attacks you.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Shoot until it retreats">
    Keep shooting and running. It eventually retreats to the **blood pool room** (the sacrifice chamber).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the four statues">
    Blood flows from **four statues**. **Shoot all four** to stop the ritual: otherwise, the boss heals. When you interrupt it, it is **stunned** for a short moment: shoot it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Do it again">
    Repeat until it dies. An ending cutscene then plays. The sword becomes available on the two other maps, and all three maps can also be played in survival.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## If it doesn't work

- You missed a fixed round.
- The chapter 1 Wüstlings didn't appear, or the zombie died too early.
- You didn't finish chapters 1 and 2 before 3.
- The Pack-a-Punch wasn't charged in time.
