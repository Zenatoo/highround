The **Sword of Barbarossa** is the special weapon of The Tortured Path. It is made of three pieces: the **Hilt**, the **Pommel** and the **Blade**. In the main map, you assemble it at the end of chapter 3: see [Easter Egg, chapter 3: Beneath the Ice](../easter-egg-chapitre-3/). The sources I read don't give its damage.

Once the main Easter Egg has been **finished at least once**, you can get it in two ways.

## In the chapters

Replay the quest: the **Hilt** (chapter 1) and the **Pommel** (chapter 2) are then delivered **with the sword**. See [Easter Egg, chapter 1: Into the Storm](../easter-egg-chapitre-1/) and [Easter Egg, chapter 2: Across the Depths](../easter-egg-chapitre-2/).

## In the survival maps

The principle is the same everywhere:

1. **Finish the main Easter Egg** at least once.
2. Give **Jolts** to the stones on the map (**1,750 Jolts** each), by dropping them on the ground in front of them. The stone glows purple when it is right.
3. A **glowing ring** appears. Stand in it and let a zombie **down you**: you are revived **with the sword**, and you keep all your perks, except Lebenblitz (Quick Revive) in solo.
