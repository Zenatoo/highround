The Tortured Path ("The twisting path") is the Zombies map of **DLC 3, United Front**. It came out on **26 June 2018** on PS4; according to Kotaku, Xbox One and PC got it within the following month.

{/* TO CHECK: exact Xbox One / PC release dates of DLC 3 (Kotaku only says "within a month"). */}

This is **not an open map**. It is **three mini-maps**, the "chapters", that you play one after the other. Each chapter has timed objectives, a boss and an extraction. It is nothing like a classic game: read this guide again before you start. The rules shared by all maps are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).

This guide describes the game **in its final state**, after the summer 2018 patches. The June 2018 videos sometimes show steps that have been fixed since.

## The three chapters

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Chapter 1: Into the Storm">
    In **Malaga**, a destroyed Allied port town. The boss is the **Raketenbrenner**. You earn the **Hilt** of the Sword of Barbarossa here. See [Easter Egg, chapter 1: Into the Storm](../easter-egg-chapitre-1/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Chapter 2: Across the Depths">
    On a **cargo ship** tossed by the sea. The boss is the **Stadtjäger**, already seen on The Shadowed Throne. You earn the **Pommel** here. See [Easter Egg, chapter 2: Across the Depths](../easter-egg-chapitre-2/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Chapter 3: Beneath the Ice">
    In an underground, circular **Nazi excavation site** filled with Geistkraft energy. The boss is the **Guardian**, new on this map. You earn the **Blade** here and assemble the sword. See [Easter Egg, chapter 3: Beneath the Ice](../easter-egg-chapitre-3/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Each chapter takes about **15 minutes** if you only do the objectives. To play chapter 2, then 3, you must have **finished the previous one**. You pick the next map yourself in the lobby, and you can change your gear and your character there.

## How a chapter plays out

- You must **survive until round 10**.
- At rounds **3, 6 and 9**, a random **objective** must be completed before time runs out. **Fail a single objective and the game is lost.**
- Completing an objective gives you a **B.A.T. Ordinance Drop**, a dropped crate that contains random weapons, Jolts and bonuses.
- At **round 10**, a boss appears. You must beat it before time runs out.
- After the boss, you defend an **extraction point** for about **one minute**, then you escape.

Zombies come fast from round 1, even in solo. The first objective is often a wave of special zombies to kill in time.

## What is different from the other maps

- **No power to turn on, no doors to open**: everything is accessible from the start.
- **Random wall weapons**: you pay for a weapon **of a given type** (SMG, assault rifle, sniper rifle, shotgun, LMG…), but the game picks which one. You can't refuse it. The weapons are not the same from one game to the next.
- **No armour machine** (Geistschild) in the chapters. You can still equip the **Armored Up** mod in your loadout, and **armour bonuses** drop from the B.A.T. crates.
- **Blitz machines are there**: Lebenblitz, Laufenblitz, Schnellblitz, Kugelblitz, Schildblitz and Faustblitz. I couldn't find a reliable source for **their prices on this map**. The general prices are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).
- **Pack-a-Punch with batteries**: see [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
- **Mystery Box**: I haven't seen one in the chapters, but the **U.S.S. Mount Olympus** in survival mode has one.

## Your progression: the Shattered rank

The map has its **own rank system**, the **Shattered** rank. It gives a **damage bonus** that grows with the time you spend on it. Kotaku also notes that a defeated boss gives a **boss attachment** for a random weapon, with **10% more damage**.

At **Shattered rank 50**, a red skull appears when you load a map: it starts the hard mode "The Darkened Path". See [The map's secrets](../secrets/).

## After the quest: three survival maps

Once you have finished the main Easter Egg one time, each mini-map can also be played in **classic survival**, round after round:

- **Bodega Cervantes** (a Spanish bodega: the chapter 1 map);
- **U.S.S. Mount Olympus** (a U.S. Navy ship: the chapter 2 map);
- **Altar of Blood** (the chapter 3 map).

There you find the Pack-a-Punch (with the batteries at **rounds 5 and 10** instead of 4 and 7), the Sword of Barbarossa and secrets specific to each map. See [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/), [The Sword of Barbarossa](../epee-de-barberousse/) and [The map's secrets](../secrets/).

The map's secret characters are in [Unlock the secret characters](../personnages-secrets/), and the achievements in [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
