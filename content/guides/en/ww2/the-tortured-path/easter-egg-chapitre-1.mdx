The main Easter Egg of The Tortured Path, "**I Have the Power**", is split into **three chapters**. Chapter 1 is played in the port of **Malaga** (the windmill map) and gives you the **Hilt** of the Sword of Barbarossa. To understand the format, first read [How The Tortured Path works](../chapitres/).

**Before you start:**

- Allow **1 h 20 to 1 h 35** for the three chapters in solo. Difficulty 7/10 according to mmmrkennedy, and very "annoying".
- The steps have **fixed rounds**: a failed step can leave you without enough rounds.
- **Finish each map once in a normal game** before you attempt the quest. Otherwise the chapter 1 quest may not progress.
- If you leave the lobby between two chapters, you have to start again at chapter 1.
- Doable solo or in co-op.

## Round 1: the rope and the zombie parts

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Shoot the rope">
    Shoot the **rope** hanging from the tree, to the left of the battery behind the windmill.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'rope_in_tree.webp'} alt="The rope hanging from the tree behind the windmill" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find six zombie parts">
    You need **one head, one torso, two arms and two legs**. They lie around the map, at spots picked at random from this list.

    **Heads:**

    - on the fence behind the windmill, to the left of the battery;
    - in a cupboard near the broken wall, in the SMG wall weapon house;
    - in a pot in the sink of the intact house;
    - in the back of the truck, next to **Lebenblitz**;
    - on a shelf in the destroyed house.

    **Torsos:**

    - on the overturned cart in front of the windmill;
    - in a wheelbarrow, in the garden of the destroyed house;
    - in a crate to the left of the sniper rifle wall weapon, in front of the destroyed house;
    - at the window above the Pack-a-Punch;
    - in the fireplace of the intact house's living room.

    **Arms:**

    - on a wooden pallet under the cellar stairs;
    - in the planter in front of the destroyed house;
    - on a crate of canned food behind the windmill;
    - holding a basket, in the living room of the intact house;
    - to the left of the radio table, in front of the windmill;
    - to the right of **Laufenblitz**, in the windmill;
    - in the rubble to the right of the destroyed house's fireplace;
    - in the destroyed house's fireplace;
    - on the logs behind the destroyed house;
    - in the fireplace of the SMG wall weapon house.

    **Legs:**

    - under the wooden "leaf" to the left of the Pack-a-Punch, in the windmill;
    - hanging from the ceiling in front of the destroyed house's front door;
    - on the fence to the right of the battery behind the windmill;
    - on the cellar's wine rack;
    - in the hanging plant in front of the destroyed house;
    - on the driver's side wing of the blue truck;
    - on the ground next to the fence, to the left of **Kugelblitz**;
    - in the hanging plant to the right of the destroyed house's back door;
    - in the hole in the wall to the left of **Schildblitz**;
    - on the ground in front of the painting, to the right of **Schildblitz**.

    {/* TO CHECK: Accelerated Ideas (June 2018) gives a shorter, differently worded list (e.g. 3 heads, 4 bodies, 9 legs). The list above is mmmrkennedy's (2025). */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Round 2: the branch in the river

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Raise the branch">
    Hold the action button on the **branch** in the river, to the left of **Kugelblitz**. It slowly rises out of the water. When it stops, pick up the **rope** on it.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'branch_in_river.webp'} alt="The branch in the river, to the left of Kugelblitz" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Round 3: the rods and the two Wüstlings

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Pick up the two rods">
    A **pod** drops near the dead cow, to the left of **Lebenblitz**. Two rods fall next to it: one **behind the cow**, one **in front of the truck**. Pick them up.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'drop_pod.webp'} alt="The pod that fell near the dead cow" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for two Wüstlings">
    Keep playing until **two Wüstlings** appear. If you don't have them by **round 5**, start over.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Round 5: the club

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Kill the front Wüstling">
    Line up the two Wüstlings. Shoot the one **behind** so that it **charges** the one in front. If done right, the front Wüstling dies and drops its **club** (its arm). Pick it up.

    {/* TO CHECK: Wüstling spelling: mmmrkennedy "Wustling", DIG_DUG7 "Wurstling"; the game's shared notes use "Wüstling". */}

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        If the two Wüstlings don't appear at round 5, first finish each map once in a normal game. The author says that fixed his problem, without knowing why.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Jam the windmill with the club">
    Place the club in the windmill's **gears** when the **longest blade points straight down**. If it is right, you can place the zombie parts. If not, take the club back and try again. The position has to be very precise, especially in solo: the blade passes in front of the door just before the right moment.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'windmill_gears.webp'} alt="The windmill gears where the club goes" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place the zombie, then stop the windmill at the top">
    Place the zombie parts, the rope and the rods. Take the club back from the gears. Then stop the windmill when the **zombie's blade is at its highest point**: about **20 seconds** after you took the club back.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'club.webp'} alt="The Wüstling's club to pick up" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'windmill_blade.webp'} alt="The windmill's long blade pointing down" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'zombie_highest_point.webp'} alt="The zombie's blade at its highest point" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="tech" author="CienkaRentaFX" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BxkrTs1wjbI">

      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Rounds 6 to 8: the living zombie and the battery

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Round 6: charge the zombie">
    Between rounds 5 and 6, lightning strikes the zombie, who falls to the ground. During round 6, **kill zombies near him** to charge him: he will be stronger for the defence step. Round 6 is an objective round: if it is a kill objective, it can be finished while you charge. Otherwise, charge the zombie before doing the objective.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Round 7: defend him">
    At the start of the round, the zombie gets up and walks through the windmill to the destroyed house. **Defend him** until he crawls into the rubble next to the fireplace. The Wüstlings target him even if **Frontline** is active: deal with them.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'debris.webp'} alt="The rubble next to the destroyed house's fireplace" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Round 8: charge the battery">
    The zombie comes out of the rubble with a **battery**. Kill zombies near him until it glows green, then **let the zombies kill him**. If he dies before it is fully charged, you have to restart the map.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the battery in the generator">
    Take the dropped battery and put it in the **wine cellar generator**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'generator.webp'} alt="The wine cellar generator" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the Hilt">
    The door opens: take the **Hilt**. It gives you **all the Blitz machines** for the rest of the game. If you have already finished the quest once, the sword is there too.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'hilt.webp'} alt="The Hilt behind the cellar door" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

You can then finish the map: survive until the boss and the extraction.

## The boss: the Raketenbrenner

At the end of the chapter, a **Raketenbrenner** appears: a fast Brenner with **two flamethrowers** and a **shield**. Take **Laufenblitz** to run faster.

- Look for the **large red zones** on the ground: they are **airstrikes**. Lure the boss to the centre.
- The blast **breaks its shield** and makes it flinch. Shoot while the shield is down, until it comes back.
- The zones appear at random and the zombies keep coming. Listen for the bombs falling to time your run.
- Stay in the **light** part of the red zone to take little damage.

{/* TO CHECK: Gameranx (June 2018) is the only strategy text; mmmrkennedy only adds that the airstrike breaks the shield. Boss round: see "Comment fonctionne The Tortured Path". */}

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
    To kill the Raketenbrenner before the fifth airstrike (challenge of the Cavalier character), play with **Frontline**, the **Vicious** mod and the **9mm Sap** pistol. The airstrike that breaks the shield counts even if it doesn't hit the boss.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

After the boss, defend the extraction point for about a minute, then the next map awaits: [Easter Egg, chapter 2: Across the Depths](../easter-egg-chapitre-2/).
