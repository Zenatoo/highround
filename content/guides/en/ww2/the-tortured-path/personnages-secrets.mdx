The Tortured Path adds playable characters that you unlock by completing **challenges**. Track your progress in the lobby: **Soldier, Dossier, Challenges** (Lobby Menu, Soldier, Dossier, Challenges).

mmmrkennedy says he **confirmed the conditions by reading the game's code**. They may differ from what other sites write.

The character names are the ones used in the English sources. The official names of the challenges are not known: the challenges are described by their rules.

## Cavalier (Storm Chaser): chapter 1

<Screenshot src={m + 'cavalier.webp'} alt="The Cavalier character" credit={by} source={src} />

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Evacuate without anyone using Blitz">
    In **Into the Storm**, finish the map without any player having taken a Blitz machine.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Evacuate without picking up armour from the B.A.T. crates">
    No **armour** bonus from the **B.A.T. Ordinance Drops**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete a “Defend” objective without shooting or using an explosive">
    Melee, tactical equipment and special abilities are allowed. Use the **Jack-in-the-Box** and **Frontline** so the zombies don't target the objective.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get the Hilt without opening a B.A.T. crate">
    See [Easter Egg, chapter 1: Into the Storm](../easter-egg-chapitre-1/). Interacting with a crate to **see its contents** counts as opening it and fails the challenge. If you shoot the parachute and the crate breaks on landing, that is a fail too.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill the Raketenbrenner before the fifth airstrike">
    The airstrike that breaks its shield counts even if it doesn't hit the boss. Tip: **Frontline**, the **Vicious** mod and the **9mm Sap** pistol.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

</Checklist>

## Iron Tiger (Archaeological Dig): chapter 3

<Screenshot src={m + 'iron_tiger.webp'} alt="The Iron Tiger character" credit={by} source={src} />

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Evacuate without armour from the B.A.T. crates">
    In **Beneath the Ice**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Evacuate without being downed">
    No player must go down.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the “Defend” objective using only lethal or tactical equipment">

  </Step>

    Same chapter.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
