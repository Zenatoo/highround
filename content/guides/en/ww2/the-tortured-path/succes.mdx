The Tortured Path has **10 trophies** (achievements on Xbox and PC), 7 bronze and 3 silver. They are part of the **United Front pack (DLC 3)**. Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device. The names are the game's English ones.

**To check:** the official **French names** of these 10 trophies were not found in an authorised source. The grades (bronze and silver) come from PlatPursuit's PS4 list.

{/* TO CHECK: French names of the 10 United Front trophies (ActuGaming's French list has no United Front section). A FR rule for "I Have the Power" was only seen in a search snippet of a blocked site: not used. Xbox Gamerscore values and Steam names were not checked. */}

The percentages of players who earned them (PS4, November 2026) are very low: all are under 1%.
