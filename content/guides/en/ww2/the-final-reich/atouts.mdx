The Final Reich has **six Blitz machines** and **armour stations**. They only work with the **power**: see [Turn on the power](../courant/). The rules (four Blitz at most, a new one replaces the oldest) are in [WWII Zombies basics](../systemes-ww2/).

## The six machines

| Blitz | Effect | Price | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lebenblitz** | Solo: 3 automatic revives. Group: revives twice as fast | 500 Jolts solo, 1,500 Jolts in a group | Village Entrance, where you spawn |
| **Laufenblitz** | 7% faster, infinite sprint | 2,000 Jolts | Sewers, in the generator tunnel, near the Sewers door |
| **Faustblitz** | More melee damage | 2,000 Jolts | Command Room, on the upper platform, near the weapons workbench |
| **Schildblitz** | Electric shock on every reload | 2,000 Jolts | Laboratory, after the electric trap tunnel, near the stairs |
| **Kugelblitz** | Double damage | 2,000 Jolts | Pub, right at the entrance, in front of the bar tables |
| **Schnellblitz** | Twice as fast reload | 3,000 Jolts | Laboratory, in a corner at the back |

- **No Jugger-Nog** in the game: your health regenerates on its own.
- The price of **Lebenblitz** depends on the number of players: 500 solo, 1,500 in a group.
- **Schnellblitz** is the most expensive (3,000 Jolts). It helps a lot with submachine guns that have a big magazine.

## Armour (Geistschild)

Each **armour point** cancels **one** hit that would have killed you. You can have **3**. The first point costs **500 Jolts** and the price goes up with every purchase: according to PC Games, it doubles each time.

The known stations:

- **Village Square**, next to the valve "generator" (next to the gas valves).
- **Morgue**, a station with the "white room" on its right.
- **Pub**, in the floor hole (with the Combat Shotgun).
