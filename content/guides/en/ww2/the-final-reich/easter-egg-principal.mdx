The **Fireworks** quest is the **guided** path of The Final Reich. In the **village of Mittelburg**, the team wants to recover the **Hilt** of Emperor Barbarossa's sword, hidden in Dr. Straub's bunker. At the end, you beat the **Panzermörder**, a giant stitched together with Klaus in his chest.

**Before you start:**

- Allow **40 to 55 minutes** solo. Difficulty: 5 out of 10 according to mmmrkennedy.
- Reward: the **Fireworks** achievement (silver) and the first ending cutscene. See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
- Doable solo or in co-op.
- The game helps you: open the scoreboard menu. The **notebook** shows your step and **highlights the objective through walls**.
- If you want to save Klaus later, **do not shoot the orb around the Hilt and do not take the Hilt** until you have prepared everything: see [Full Easter Egg: Dark Reunion](../easter-egg-complet/).

## First steps

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Power, bunker and Salt Mine">
    Turn on the power, open the bunker, then the Salt Mine: everything is in [Turn on the power](../courant/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Charge the Geistkraft device and build the Tesla">
    In the Emperor's Chamber, charge the Geistkraft device. Then escort it to the Laboratory and the Morgue, kill the Brenner and forge the **Tesla Gun**: see [Build the Tesla Gun](../tesla/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The Right Hand of God

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Start the power grid">
    In the **Command Room**, upper platform, behind the Tesla forge, interact with the **Power Grid** machine. In the four columns, **one light** turns on (red, green or blue). **Note the colour of each column, in order.**

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'power_grid.webp'} alt="The Power Grid machine" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'grid_lights.webp'} alt="The lights of the four columns" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the four electric boxes, fast">
    Each box has three choices: **Rot** (red), **Grün** (green), **Blau** (blue). Set them in the order you noted, **within a time limit** (about 45 seconds according to PowerPyx).

    1. **Command Room**, to the right of the Power Grid machine.
    2. **Sewers**, fire trap side of the Pack-a-Punch room.
    3. **Sewers**, at the other end of this room, near the door to Riverside.
    4. **Pub**, near the Tower door, at the back.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'box_command_room.webp'} alt="The Command Room electric box" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'box_sewers_1.webp'} alt="The electric box on the fire trap side of the Sewers" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'box_sewers_2.webp'} alt="The electric box at the end of the Pack-a-Punch room" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'box_pub.webp'} alt="The Pub electric box" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>

    {/* TO CHECK: time limit: PowerPyx ~45 s; mmmrkennedy says only "a time limit". Accelerated Ideas shows the power grid step twice (launch day), mmmrkennedy / PowerPyx / PSLS only once. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Defend the central lightning rod">
    Go to the **Tower**. Interact with the **lever** under the middle: a **lightning rod slowly rises**. A round starts, with endless zombies. **Defend the lever for about 70 seconds**, until the health bar on screen disappears. If it takes too much damage, start again as soon as the lightning rod has gone back down.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'lightning_rod.webp'} alt="The central lightning rod of the Tower" credit={by} source={src} />

    {/* TO CHECK: duration: mmmrkennedy ~70 s, PowerPyx ~70 s, Accelerated Ideas 60 s, an older Accelerated Ideas page 30 s (launch state). */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Defend the other two levers">
    Next, defend the **two levers**, one on each side of the Tower. You have to **activate them at the same time**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'two_levers.webp'} alt="The two levers on each side of the Tower" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the right hand">
    With the three lightning rods raised, go back to the **Emperor's Chamber** and activate the **Right Hand of God**. It is the machine on the **left** as you enter, because it is on the statue's right.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'right_hand_of_god.webp'} alt="The Right Hand of God" credit={by} source={src} />

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
        This step (and the next one) can be cheated with **Frontline**: start the lever, run to the bunker door and, when the lightning rod bar turns red, activate Frontline. All the zombies run at you and the lightning rod finishes in time.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The Zeppelin and the Left Hand of God

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Bring down a Zeppelin generator">
    A **Zeppelin** flies over the village. When it fires at you, **four yellow lights** appear underneath it. Shoot **one** of these lights: it turns red. When it is destroyed, a **generator** drops. Watch where it lands.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'zeppelin_lights.webp'} alt="The four yellow lights under the Zeppelin" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Charge the generator">
    Kill zombies **inside its red circle**. Once charged, it explodes and leaves a **battery** (the Überschnalle).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'generator.webp'} alt="The generator dropped from the Zeppelin" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Carry it to the left hand, three times">
    Bring the battery to the **Left Hand of God**, the machine on the **right** in the Emperor's Chamber. You can put it down and pick it up again whenever you want. **Repeat three times.** The Zeppelin comes back right away. At the third battery, it may **suck up the generator**: shoot its lights again. Once the three batteries are in the left hand, interact with it.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'left_hand_of_god.webp'} alt="The Left Hand of God" credit={by} source={src} />

    {/* TO CHECK: PowerPyx and Accelerated Ideas say a new Zeppelin only comes at the next round (finish the round first); mmmrkennedy says it returns as soon as you give the battery. */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="PowerPyx" source="https://www.powerpyx.com/call-of-duty-ww2-zombies-the-final-reich-easter-egg-guide/">
    You can't shoot while carrying the battery. Solo, **Camouflage** gives you calm to carry it.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## The Voice of God

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Scan the four paintings with the Brenner's head">
    Take the **head of a Brenner** and aim at each **painting**: the purple light shows a **bird** (a raven) and a **Roman numeral** (I to V). Note which bird goes with which numeral.

    - **Pub**, upstairs, near the gramophone.
    - **Courtyard**, in the S-Mine trap area.
    - **Sewers**, to the right of the door to Riverside.
    - **Morgue**, in the spike trap room.

  <Step title="Set the four pillars">

    If it's right, you hear sound effects and an **energy orb** surrounds the Hilt. If it's wrong, a message says the machine is restarting.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The Hilt and the boss

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Shoot the orb and take the Hilt">
    Shoot the **orb** with the **Tesla Gun** (in co-op, shoot together). Then interact with the **Hilt**: the fight begins.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'energy_orb.webp'} alt="The energy orb around the Hilt" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Beat the Panzermörder">
    All players are teleported to a **slightly changed version of the village**. The **Panzermörder** follows you slowly. From time to time it **charges**, swinging its arms: huge damage. The Zeppelin circles above.

    Repeat **three times**:

    1. **Bring down a generator** from the Zeppelin (shoot one of its lights).
    2. **Charge it** by killing zombies near it.
    3. **Draw the Panzermörder** close to the battery and **shoot it** until it is **stunned** and drops to one knee. Take the battery and attach it to it.

    After each battery, the boss leaves and zombies arrive. You don't need to kill them: it comes back after a few minutes. At the third battery, the fight is over.

    {/* TO CHECK: a search summary says each boss hit removes one armour point (not fetched, not used). */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

You earn **Fireworks**.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
    In the boss arena, only the **Type 100**, **M1928** and **SVT** wall weapons are there for ammo: use them during the fight.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="PowerPyx" source="https://www.powerpyx.com/call-of-duty-ww2-zombies-the-final-reich-easter-egg-guide/">
    Solo, clear the round while leaving **a single zombie** before you carry a battery: you carry the battery without a crowd around you.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

The **Slayer** challenge (Big Game) asks you to beat the Panzermörder before round 12: see [Unlock the secret characters](../personnages-secrets/).
