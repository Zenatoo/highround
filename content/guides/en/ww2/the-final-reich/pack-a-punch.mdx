WWII's Pack-a-Punch is called the **Ubersprengen**. On The Final Reich, it is in the **Sewers**, locked in an iron cage. You first need to [Turn on the power](../courant/) and open the way to the Sewers.

Once it's open: **5,000 Jolts** to upgrade a weapon (once per weapon), **4,000 Jolts** to refill the ammo of an already upgraded weapon. More details: [WWII Zombies basics](../systemes-ww2/).

## The steps

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Press the red button in the Sewers">
    Go to the cage: through the Morgue or through the Laufenblitz tunnel. Near the **MP-40** wall weapon, press the **red button**. Three **drain pipes** activate around the map, and the cage rises a little.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'red_button.webp'} alt="The red button near the MP-40 in the Sewers" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the three pipes">
    Use each pipe, **250 Jolts** per slide, in any order you like:

    - **Tower**: in the back left corner, to the left of the **Waffe 28** wall weapon. You need to have opened the Pub and the Tower.
    - **Command Room**: near the **Grease Gun** wall weapon, all the way to the right.
    - **Laboratory**: near the **electric trap**.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pipe_tower.webp'} alt="The Tower pipe" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pipe_command.webp'} alt="The Command Room pipe" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'pipe_lab.webp'} alt="The Laboratory pipe" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Press the button in each small room">
    After each slide, you land in a **small closed room** in the Sewers. Press the **red button** in it.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'red_button_side_room.webp'} alt="The red button of a small Sewers room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use the Ubersprengen">
    At the third button, the cage rises all the way. You can upgrade any non-special weapon.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Pack-a-Punch is required by the **B.A.T. Agent** character challenge ("unlock the Ubersprengen") and by the hard quest: see [Unlock the secret characters](../personnages-secrets/).

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="Game Rant" source="https://gamerant.com/call-of-duty-ww2-zombies-pack-a-punch/">
    Upgrade your weapon late, when it's empty: the 4,000 Jolts of the refill then act as a paid **Max Ammo**.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="Call of Duty" source="https://support.activision.com/call-of-duty--wwii/articles/call-of-duty-wwii-nazi-zombies-mode">
    An upgraded weapon **disappears** if you replace it with a wall weapon. Don't buy a wall weapon out of reflex.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
