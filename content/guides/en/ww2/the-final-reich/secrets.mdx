The Final Reich hides several small secrets outside the main quest: a special PPSh-41, a silent doctor, zombies full of Jolts. The general rules are in [WWII Zombies basics](../systemes-ww2/).

## The Classic, the special PPSh-41

**The Classic** is a **PPSh-41** with a **70-round** magazine, more reserve ammo and a faster rate of fire.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Drop Jolts in the four bowls">
    There are four **bowls**. To put Jolts in them, use the "drop Jolts" button on screen, above the shovel. The bowl takes the Jolts and spits out money.

    - **Morgue**, to the right of the Tesla machine.
    - **Village Square**, in the burnt area, to the left of the SVT-40 wall weapon.
    - **Riverside**, on the ground near sandbags, in front of the M1927 wall weapon building.
    - **Pub**, upstairs, to the right of the Kugelblitz.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Try your luck at the Mystery Box">
    When the four bowls are filled, **The Classic** enters the Mystery Box. It is **not guaranteed** on every pull: see [The Mystery Box and weapons](../boite-mystere/).

## Dr. Straub (Lurking Around achievement)

**Dr. Straub** is a man in a white coat. He appears **silently** somewhere on the map and watches you. He doesn't attack. **Shoot him** to get **Lurking Around**.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Open one of the three doors">
    He doesn't appear until one of these doors is open: **Village Square to Command Room** (the bunker), **Sewers to Morgue** or **Riverside to Courtyard**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Look for him in one of the ten spots">
    Once a door is open, he can appear in one of these spots (even in an area that isn't open yet):

    - **Village Entrance**: in the zombie spawn, to the left of the exit door.
    - **Riverside**: second-floor window, near the weather vane.
    - **Riverside**: third-floor window, near the weather vane.
    - **Laboratory**: in the zombie spawn, behind the Schildblitz.
    - **Laboratory**: to the right of the Schnellblitz, where bodies hang.
    - **Morgue**: behind the switch.
    - **Morgue**: in the "white room", to the right of the armour station.
    - **Morgue**: in the zombie spawn, to the right of the BAR wall weapon.
    - **Morgue**: behind a spawn window, in the spike trap room.
    - **Morgue**: behind the metal grate of the spike trap room.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'dr_straub/dr_straub_example.webp'} alt="Dr. Straub standing, watching the player" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

How he behaves:

- He only appears in **one spot at a time**, chosen **at random**, **out of your sight**.
- He **waits indefinitely**. As soon as you look at him head-on, he **disappears**.
- After he leaves, it takes **5 to 7 minutes** before he appears again.
- Each spot is used **only once per game**.
- He no longer appears once the **final fight** has begun.

{/* TO CHECK: every spawn rule above comes from mmmrkennedy, who says they were confirmed by modding and reading the game's code; no second source. */}

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
    Shoot **as soon as you see him**: a direct look is enough for him to leave, and he doesn't come back to the same spot.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Treasure Zombies

A **Treasure Zombie** is a harmless Bomber with red bombs: it drops **Jolts**. When you see Jolts falling all over the map, it has just spawned. It only appears once **all the doors of the map are open**, then **every 1 h 15**. Killing it can drop a piece of the **Hunter** character: see [Unlock the secret characters](../personnages-secrets/).
