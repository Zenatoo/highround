The Final Reich has **9 achievements** (trophies) of its own, plus **Pressure Cooker**, which belongs to the **Prologue** (the same house as Gröesten Haus). That makes **10 Zombies trophies** for the base game: 2 silver, 8 bronze. Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device. The names are the game's English ones.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Pressure Cooker (bronze)">
    In the **Prologue**, survive to **round 20**. See [The Prologue](../../groesten-haus/prologue/).

    {/* TO CHECK: timing conflict. Gamepressure: you do not have to fight round 20's zombies; PowerPyx body text: pops on reaching round 19; a PowerPyx comment: at the start of round 20. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fireworks (silver)">
    Retrieve the artifact: this is the guided quest. See [Main Easter Egg: Fireworks](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Dark Reunion (silver)">
    Save Klaus: this is the hard quest. Don't shoot the Hilt orb before you're ready. See [Full Easter Egg: Dark Reunion](../easter-egg-complet/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Lightning Handler (bronze)">
    Build a Tesla Gun. See [Build the Tesla Gun](../tesla/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Dark Arts (bronze)">
    Build **all the variants** of the Tesla, in the same game. All the players in the game get it, no matter who built them. See [The four Tesla Gun upgrades](../ameliorations-tesla/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Lurking Around (bronze)">
    Find **Dr. Straub** and shoot him. According to Gamepressure, he appears from round 6 in hard-to-see spots; his spawn rules and his ten spots are in [The Classic, Dr. Straub and other secrets](../secrets/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Red Mist (bronze)">
    Get **10 kills** with **a single Bomber explosion**. Draw a Bomber into the middle of a group and make it explode.

    {/* TO CHECK: no step guide read for Red Mist, Strike!, Undertaker and White Knuckles: only the rules from PlatPursuit and ActuGaming. The advice here is just a restatement of the rule. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Strike! (bronze)">
    Eliminate **10 zombies** with **a Wüstling's charge**. Make a Wüstling charge into a crowd.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Undertaker (bronze)">
    Kill the **Bomber** with the **shovel**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="White Knuckles (bronze)">
    Survive **3 Pest rounds** in the same game **without being hit**. Pest rounds come every 5 rounds (around 5, 10 and 15 according to Gamepressure).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

{/* Names, rules and grades: PlatPursuit PS4 list (EN), ActuGaming (FR names and rules). Dark Reunion/Fireworks grades: silver per PlatPursuit. Xbox and Steam names not checked, expected identical. */}
