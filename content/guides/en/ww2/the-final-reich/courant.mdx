On The Final Reich, the power opens the **bunker**: without it, there is no Mystery Box, Laboratory, Morgue or Pack-a-Punch. It is the first step of the guided quest. Allow a few minutes. The general rules are in [WWII Zombies basics](../systemes-ww2/).

You can follow the objective in the **notebook** (scoreboard menu): the game highlights it through walls.

## Start the generator

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Activate the three gas valves">
    Hold the action button on each valve until the dial is **green and climbs to 60**. If you let go early, it falls back to 0. To find them, follow the pipes that leave the three-light "generator", to the left of the Village Square armour station. It also shows the valves that are already active.

    - **Village Square**, behind the armour station (Geistschild).
    - **Village Square**, to the left of the bunker entrance.
    - **Riverside**, near the Pub entrance.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'valve_armour.webp'} alt="The valve behind the armour station, Village Square" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'valve_bunker.webp'} alt="The valve to the left of the bunker entrance" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'valve_riverside.webp'} alt="The Riverside valve, near the Pub" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Light the Pilot Light">
    In the middle of the **Village Square**, use the **Pilot Light**. Flames shoot out of the hole, along with a few **Pests**. The wooden cover pops off.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pilot_light.webp'} alt="The Pilot Light in the middle of the Village Square" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go down the hole and start the generator">
    Jump into the hole, climb the slope up to the **generator** and hold the action button: the **power is on**. The **Laufenblitz** is right next to it.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'power.webp'} alt="The generator at the end of the slope" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Open the bunker

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Buy the bunker door">
    It is in the Village Square. In the **Command Room**, you can then buy the **Morgue** and **Laboratory** doors.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'bunker_door.webp'} alt="The bunker door" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the two switches within a minute">
    For the **Salt Mine door**, there are two switches. Activate them **within about a minute of each other**, otherwise they reset.

    - **Laboratory**: upstairs, at the very back.
    - **Morgue**: on the right side, near the **STG-44** wall weapon.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'switch_lab.webp'} alt="The Laboratory switch" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'switch_morgue.webp'} alt="The Morgue switch" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>

    {/* TO CHECK: time between the two switches: mmmrkennedy "about 1 minute", PowerPyx (Nov 2017) "within 30 seconds". Final state = mmmrkennedy. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Buy the Salt Mine door">
    **1,500 Jolts**. It releases **3 to 4 Bombers** in the tunnel: be ready. The Salt Mine leads to the **Emperor's Chamber** and the rest of the quest: see [Build the Tesla Gun](../tesla/).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'salt_mine_door.webp'} alt="The Salt Mine door" credit={by} source={src} />
