The **Mystery Box** gives a random weapon for **1,000 Jolts**. It is only available once the **bunker door** is open: see [Turn on the power](../courant/). The general rules are in [WWII Zombies basics](../systemes-ww2/).

## Where the box is

- At the start of every game, the box is in the **Command Room**. After a number of pulls that the game hides, it **moves**.
- According to a fan site, the other spots are the **Courtyard** and the **bridge that leads to the Tower**.

## What it gives

- All the **wall weapons**.
- Weapons that are not in your starting classes, like the **light machine guns (LMG)**.
- The **Fliegerfaust**, a "rocket-launcher revolver".
- The **Jack-in-the-Box**: an explosive that plays a tune, draws the zombies in, then explodes.
- **The Classic**, a special PPSh-41, after the bowls quest: see [The Classic, Dr. Straub and other secrets](../secrets/).

The best weapons are the **machine guns** (MG 15, MG 42, Lewis…): lots of ammo and big damage. According to a fan site, the box also holds the M1A1 Carbine, Gewehr 43, Sten, Lewis, MG 15, Bren, MG 42, GPMG, Karabin, M1903 and Toggle Action.

## Wall weapons

You pay, you get the weapon **with full ammo**. It replaces the weapon in your hands. To refill ammo, you pay **half** the price. The possible starting weapons are the P-08, the 1911, the Machine Pistol (Red 9), the M1A1 Carbine, the Kar98k, the sawed-off shotgun and the M30 Luftwaffe Drilling.
