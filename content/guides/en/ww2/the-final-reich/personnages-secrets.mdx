Besides Marie, Jefferson, Olivia and Drostan, WWII Zombies has **characters to unlock** through **challenges**. You track your progress in the lobby menu: **Soldier**, then **Dossier**, then **Challenges**. The challenges below are those of The Final Reich, listed by mmmrkennedy from the game's code.

{/* TO CHECK: the French names (Chasseur, Montagnard, Survivaliste, Tueur, Élite B.A.T., Agent B.A.T.) come from Kenshin9977's video chapters only. mmmrkennedy warns that these challenges may differ from those found elsewhere online. */}

The challenges often ask you to play **with no Special, no mods and no consumables**. Remove your mods before the game. You can't remove your special ability: don't use it. Auto-revives count as consumables. Armour and Blitz machines are allowed. See [WWII Zombies basics](../systemes-ww2/).

## Hunter: "On the Hunt"

Collect pieces **A to E** of the zombie hunter set. They drop **at random** when you kill **Treasure Zombies**.

- Treasure Zombies only appear once **all the doors** of the map are open, then **every 1 h 15**.
- Each piece has about a **5%** chance to drop per kill. Duplicates are not filtered: you can get a piece you already have.
- So the first piece drops at about 26%, the second at 21%, then 16%, 10% and 5%.

## Mountaineer: "Roughing It"

- Survive **round 20** on The Final Reich **without opening any door**.
- Same, **with no player downed**.
- Survive **round 25** in the **Prologue** (see [The Prologue](../../groesten-haus/prologue/)).
- Same, without going down.
- Same, **without opening the secret room** of the Prologue.

Tip: use **Freefire** with the **MK. II** mod to get temporary upgraded weapons.

<Screenshot src={m + 'mountaineer.webp'} alt="The Mountaineer character" credit={by} source={src} />

## Survivalist: "Survival Guide"

Each challenge is played to **round 30** on The Final Reich:

- **with no player downed**;
- **with no Blitz at all**;
- **without the generator power**: activate the valves for the Pilot Light and buy the **Laufenblitz**; you only leave the hole through the Pilot Light, which throws you out;
- **only each player's starting weapon**: you can upgrade it at Pack-a-Punch;
- **only two Mystery Box pulls** per player (starting weapons allowed but not upgradable). It doesn't matter when you pull, and box consumables like the Jack-in-the-Box are allowed.

## B.A.T. Agent: "On Assignment"

- Kill a **Treasure Zombie**.
- Defeat the **Panzermörder**: see [Main Easter Egg: Fireworks](../easter-egg-principal/).
- Survive **round 25**.
- Unlock the **Ubersprengen**: see [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
- Build the **Tesla Gun before round 6**: see [Build the Tesla Gun](../tesla/). Doable solo with some luck, much easier in co-op.

<Screenshot src={m + 'bat_agent.webp'} alt="The B.A.T. Agent character" credit={by} source={src} />

## B.A.T. Elite: "Off Record"

All these challenges are done while **saving Klaus**: see [Full Easter Egg: Dark Reunion](../easter-egg-complet/).

- **Solo**.
- **Before round 16**. Very hard solo: mmmrkennedy advises going with several players, or following a video by AC OMEGA to the letter.
- In **under 75 minutes**.
- **With no player downed**.
- **With no Special, mods or consumables.**

<Screenshot src={m + 'bat_elite.webp'} alt="The B.A.T. Elite character" credit={by} source={src} />

## Slayer: "Big Game"

All these challenges are done while **beating the Panzermörder**: see [Main Easter Egg: Fireworks](../easter-egg-principal/).

- **Before round 12**.
- **Without upgrading a weapon** at the Ubersprengen.
- **With no Blitz at all**.
- **With no player downed**.
- **With no Special, mods or consumables.**

## Wicht Warden: "Cold Blooded"

Finish the **main quest** of **each of the five maps**:

- The Final Reich: [Full Easter Egg: Dark Reunion](../easter-egg-complet/).
- The Darkest Shore: [Main Easter Egg: Making History](../../the-darkest-shore/easter-egg-principal/).
- The Shadowed Throne: [Main Easter Egg: Stadtjäger Down](../../the-shadowed-throne/easter-egg-principal/).
- The Tortured Path: its three chapters.
- The Frozen Dawn: [Main Easter Egg: Kingfall](../../the-frozen-dawn/easter-egg-principal/).
