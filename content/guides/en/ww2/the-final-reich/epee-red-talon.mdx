The **Red Talon** is a **sword**: The Final Reich's special melee weapon. It kills zombies **in one hit**, even at high rounds. Each kill charges an **area attack** that kills several at once. It replaces the shovel. You can only get it through the hard path and it is needed for [Full Easter Egg: Dark Reunion](../easter-egg-complet/).

Before you start, you need:

- to have done the guided quest **up to the defence of the three lightning rods**: see [Main Easter Egg: Fireworks](../easter-egg-principal/);
- a **Tesla Gun**: see [Build the Tesla Gun](../tesla/);
- the **head of a Brenner**.

## The steps

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Reset the two switches">
    Activate the **Morgue** and **Laboratory** switches again (the same ones as for the Salt Mine door: see [Turn on the power](../courant/)).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Activate the third switch">
    In the **Command Room**, to the right of the Salt Mine door, **another switch** makes **coils** appear.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'switch.webp'} alt="The third switch in the Command Room" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'coils.webp'} alt="The coils" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the coils with the Tesla">
    Hold the Brenner's head and shoot the coils with the **Tesla Gun**: the Command Room lights go out for **a few minutes**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Laboratory safe in the dark">
    In the **Laboratory**, on the left as soon as you enter, an open panel in the wall hides a **safe**. Aim the Brenner's head at it. Then turn the **five dials** by interacting with them, until each one shows a **fingerprint**. The safe opens: take a **half coin**. The red numbers written on it are decoys.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'lab_safe.webp'} alt="The Laboratory safe" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Same thing in the Morgue">
    In the **Morgue**, the safe is in the left wall, just after the entrance. Aim at it, set the dials: you get the **other half**. If the lights come back before you finish, start again at the next round: you keep your progress.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'morgue_safe.webp'} alt="The Morgue safe" credit={by} source={src} />

    {/* TO CHECK: dial colour: Accelerated Ideas "bright blue", PowerPyx "purple", mmmrkennedy "fingerprint". Number of dials (five) from the dossier (Accelerated Ideas / PowerPyx). */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the sword">
    With both halves, go to the foot of the **Barbarossa statue**, in the Emperor's Chamber. Interact with the **hole** in the wall: you receive the **Red Talon**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'sword_pickup_loc.webp'} alt="The wall hole where the Red Talon is taken" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="PowerPyx" source="https://www.powerpyx.com/call-of-duty-ww2-zombies-the-final-reich-easter-egg-guide/">
