In **Call of Duty: WWII**, the Zombies mode is called **Nazi Zombies**. It was made by Sledgehammer Games and came out with the game on **3 November 2017**. It then received four DLC maps (The Darkest Shore, The Shadowed Throne, The Tortured Path and The Frozen Dawn) on top of The Final Reich and the Prologue. The rules below are **the same on every map**. This guide explains them once and for all.

It describes the game **in its final state**, after the 2018 updates. Late 2017 videos sometimes show rules that have changed (for example Camouflage, weaker since update 1.09 in January 2018).

The game's names stay **in English or German**: Jolts, Blitz, Ubersprengen…

## Currency: Jolts

**Jolts** are the game's money. You earn them mostly by killing zombies. You spend them on doors, wall weapons, the Mystery Box, Blitz machines and Pack-a-Punch.

- Kills with the **shovel** or the **throwing knife** earn **more** Jolts than a rifle kill.
- Kills with an **explosive** earn **less**.
- You can **drop Jolts** to give some to a teammate (the button is on screen, above the shovel).

## Before the game: character, role and gear

Before starting a game, you choose:

- **one of the four characters**: Marie Fischer, Jefferson Potts, Olivia Durant or Drostan Hynd;
- **one of the four roles**: **Control**, **Medic**, **Offense** or **Support**. Each role has ready-to-use gear: a starting weapon, an explosive, a special ability and three mods.

From **level 5**, you can make your own class. You earn experience by playing, and your level unlocks weapons, mods, special abilities and attachments. All attachments are open at the last levels.

You can also take **two consumables** (see below). Other characters are unlocked through challenges: see [Unlock the secret characters](../personnages-secrets/).

You start with **a pistol and a few grenades**. The game says the **shovel** is obtained in the Prologue: see [The Prologue](../../groesten-haus/prologue/).

## Special abilities

A special ability (**Special Ability**) charges by killing zombies, then you activate it. There are four, each with its own **mods** (permanent bonuses, like multiplayer Basic Trainings). A mod only works with its ability.

| Ability | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Camouflage** | You turn invisible for a short time; the zombies' eyes glow |
| **Freefire** | **Infinite** necromantic energy ammo for 20 seconds, then your weapon reloads |
| **Frontline** | **All** the zombies in the area chase you, and your weapon does **double damage** |
| **Shellshock** | An energy wave that stuns, knocks down and hurts zombies. They stay stunned until the meter runs out |

{/* TO CHECK: Camouflage nerfed in update 1.09 (23 Jan 2018: meter drains faster), source = comicbook.com patch notes via the Patches section of _shared.md. */}

| Ability | Mods (effect) |
| --- | --- |
| Camouflage | **Field Medic** (revive twice as fast), **Survivalist** (+1 armour), **Exfiltration** (a revived ally is camouflaged for 5 seconds), **Saboteur** (stronger grenades), **Serrated Edge** (bleeding of 3% of max health every 1.5 s for 30 s) |
| Freefire | **Marksmanship** (headshots x3), **Explosives Handler** (2 grenades for you and your team), **Ammo Carrier** (a magazine for nearby teammates), **MK. II** (your weapon temporarily becomes an upgraded version), **Squad Tactics** (+150% to the headshot bonus) |
| Frontline | **Resilient** (health recovery time), **Vicious** (damage bonus at 300%), **Determination** (+2 armour), **Team Effort** (the damage bonus extends to the team), **Punishment** (zombies that hit you die) |
| Shellshock | **Breathing Room** (knockback +200%, radius +150%), **Protector** (+1 armour for allies in the wave), **Fiery Burst** (zombies hit catch fire), **Exploit Weakness** (stunned zombies take x3), **Defibrillate** (downed allies in the wave are revived) |

Other mods exist, for example **Pack Mule** (an extra primary weapon) and **Armored Up** (you start with 3 armour points). Another mod lets you carry **five Blitz** instead of four.

## Explosives and knives

There are five explosives; four go in your gear.

- **Mk 2 Fragmentation**: classic grenade, which can be cooked.
- **Sticky Bomb**: sticks to the target before exploding.
- **Satchel Charge**: stick it on a surface, then set it off remotely.
- **Bouncing Betty**: a mine that jumps into the air before exploding.
- **Jack-in-the-Box**: only found in the Mystery Box. It plays a tune that draws zombies in before exploding.

**Throwing knives** kill a weak zombie. The ones that miss can be picked up.

## Wall weapons, Mystery Box and Pack-a-Punch

- **Wall weapons** (weapon lockers): you pay, you take the weapon with full ammo. It replaces the weapon in your hands. To **refill ammo**, you pay **half** the price again.
- **Mystery Box**: **1,000 Jolts**. It gives a random weapon: wall weapons, light machine guns (LMG), the **Fliegerfaust** (a "rocket-launcher revolver"), the Jack-in-the-Box… It is accessible once the bunker door is open. Details on The Final Reich: [The Mystery Box and weapons](../boite-mystere/).
- **Pack-a-Punch**: its real in-game name is the **Ubersprengen**, the "upgrade station". **5,000 Jolts** to upgrade a weapon, **4,000 Jolts** to refill ammo on an already upgraded weapon. The weapon is renamed, does more damage, has more ammo and a bigger magazine. An upgraded weapon **disappears** if you replace it with a wall weapon. How to open it changes from map to map: on The Final Reich, see [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).

## Blitz and armour

**Blitz** are WWII's perks. They are sold in **Blitz Machines**. Each Blitz is a permanent bonus.

| Item | Effect | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lebenblitz** | Solo: you get back up on your own up to 3 times. In a group: you revive teammates twice as fast | 500 Jolts solo, 1,500 Jolts in a group |
| **Laufenblitz** | 7% faster, infinite sprint | 2,000 Jolts |
| **Faustblitz** | More melee damage | 2,000 Jolts |
| **Schildblitz** | An electric shock when you reload: it stuns and hurts nearby zombies | 2,000 Jolts |
| **Kugelblitz** | Your weapons do **double** damage | 2,000 Jolts |
| **Schnellblitz** | Twice as fast reload | 3,000 Jolts |
| **Armour** (Geistschild) | Each point cancels **one** hit that would have killed you. 3 points at most | 500 Jolts, more and more expensive |

- You carry **four Blitz** at most. A new Blitz replaces the **oldest**. You can give a Blitz back to its machine to remove it.
- Buying a Blitz leaves you **vulnerable for a few seconds**: choose your moment.
- There is **no Jugger-Nog**. Health regenerates on its own.
- According to PC Games, each armour re-buy costs **double** the previous one.

## Power-ups

Power-ups drop from zombies and last a short time. Their in-game names are **German**.

| Power-up | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Totengriff** | Insta-kill: normal zombies die in one hit for a while |
| **Schockmagnet** | More Jolts per kill for a while |
| **Volle Taschen** (or Taschen Voll) | Max Ammo: full ammo and grenades |
| **Überladen** | Fills your special meter, and doubles it for next time |
| **Ausradieren** (or Vernichten) | Nuke: kills all zombies alive. Avoid it outside an emergency, it raises the round for nothing |

## Consumables

A **consumable** is used once (or a few times) during the game. You take **two** before playing. They come from **supply drops**, which you earn by playing. A November 2017 update (1.06) fixed drops that weren't arriving.

- **Totengriff** (Insta-kill), **Taschen Voll** (Max Ammo, for the whole team), **Überladen** (full special meter, for the whole team), **Vernichten** (Nuke).
- **Flamethrower** and **bazooka**.
- **Refund Coupon**: everything costs 25% less for a while.
- **Blitz Machine Coupon**: 50% off a Blitz.
- **Guarantee**: you are sure to get a specific weapon from the Mystery Box.

{/* TO CHECK: consumable list = Gamepressure only (it says the coupon gives "50% discount for upgrading weapons in Blitz Machine", probably meaning Blitz purchases). Rarity, use counts, keys and self-revive consumable come from beta files: left out. */}

A character challenge can forbid consumables: **auto-revives** also count as consumables.

## The Journal

The Final Reich has a quest **journal**. Open the scoreboard menu: you see the current step and the game **highlights the objective through walls**. It doesn't reveal hidden quests.

## Enemies

- **Zombies**: normal.
- **Pest**: thin, fast, with no arms. There are **Pest rounds** every **5 rounds** (5, 10, 15…).
- **Bomber**: two zombies stuck together, the one behind carries a bomb. Shoot the small zombie on its back to make the bomb drop.
- **Wüstling**: a slow brute with a spiked arm. It doesn't move until it has been hit, then charges for big damage. Sticky bombs and long-range headshots work well.
- **Brenner**: a flamethrower sub-boss, with a tank on its back. Its **head** is used in The Final Reich quests.
- **Treasure Zombie**: a harmless zombie that drops Jolts.

Each map has its **own bosses**. The Wüstling has been less frequent since update 1.19 (31 July 2018).

## Updates that matter

- **1.05** (10 November 2017): The Final Reich out-of-map exploit fixed, Klaus's path fixed.
- **1.09** (23 January 2018): Camouflage weakened.
- **1.19** (31 July 2018): fewer Wüstlings, special weapon upgrades more limited by round.
- **1.22** (2 October 2018): the big final Zombies update, mostly for The Frozen Dawn.
