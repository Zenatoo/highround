The **Tesla Gun** is The Final Reich's special weapon. It fires electricity that **jumps from zombie to zombie**: it stuns them and kills several at once. Building it gives the **Lightning Handler** achievement. It is also the "The Weaponsmith" step of the guided quest: see [Main Easter Egg: Fireworks](../easter-egg-principal/).

You need to have opened the **Salt Mine**: see [Turn on the power](../courant/). In co-op, only one player picks up the weapon.

## The steps

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Charge the Geistkraft device in the Emperor's Chamber">
    At the very bottom of the Salt Mine, in front of the Barbarossa statue, use the big **Geistkraft device**. Kill zombies **inside the red circle** on the ground to charge it. When it is charged, it rises into the ceiling.

    {/* TO CHECK: kills needed: PowerPyx says 15-20, all zombie types count; mmmrkennedy gives no number. */}

    <Screenshot src={m + 'hilt.webp'} alt="The Geistkraft device in front of the Barbarossa statue" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn the Command Room crank">
    In the **Command Room**, turn the **crank** of the pyramid-shaped device until it stops.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'crank.webp'} alt="The Command Room crank" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Press the platform button">
    Go up to the **upper platform** and press the **button**. The lights go out and the device leaves on a ceiling rail toward the **Laboratory**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tesla_button.webp'} alt="The upper platform button" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Escort the device in the Laboratory">
    It stops from time to time. To make it go again, kill zombies **inside the red circle** on the ground under it. At the Laboratory generator (near the **Schnellblitz**), it goes inside. Wait about **15 to 20 seconds**: the generator ejects a **Tesla part** (the barrel). Pick it up. Wüstlings come out of their cells here.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'tesla_lab_generator.webp'} alt="The Laboratory generator" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'tesla_part_lab.webp'} alt="The Tesla part in the Laboratory" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Escort it to the Morgue">
    Same thing: follow it, charge it with kills in the red circle. At the Morgue generator, wait about **30 seconds** and pick up the **second part** (the core).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tesla_part_morgue.webp'} alt="The Tesla part in the Morgue" credit={by} source={src} />

    {/* TO CHECK: waiting times. mmmrkennedy: ~15-20 s (Lab) and ~30 s (Morgue); PowerPyx (Nov 2017): 45-60 s each. Final state follows mmmrkennedy. Part names (barrel / core) from PowerPyx and Accelerated Ideas. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill the Brenner">
    When you get back to the Command Room, a **Brenner** appears on the upper platform. Kill it. Keep its **head**: it is used later for [The four Tesla Gun upgrades](../ameliorations-tesla/) and for [The Red Talon sword](../epee-red-talon/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assemble the Tesla">
    On the upper platform, in the centre, put the two parts in the forge. Wait for the assembly to finish, then pick up the **Tesla Gun**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'tesla_build_loc.webp'} alt="Where the Tesla is built" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
    **B.A.T. Agent** character challenge: build the Tesla **before round 6**. It's possible solo with some luck, but much easier in co-op.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Going further

The Tesla turns into four variants: [The four Tesla Gun upgrades](../ameliorations-tesla/). Building them all in the same game gives **Dark Arts**.
