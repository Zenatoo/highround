**Dark Reunion** is the **hard** path of The Final Reich. It is a longer version of [Main Easter Egg: Fireworks](../easter-egg-principal/). After the boss, you **save Klaus**, Marie's brother, with the Hilt of Barbarossa's sword.

**Before you start:**

- Allow **1 h 30 to 1 h 45** solo. Difficulty: 6 out of 10 according to mmmrkennedy.
- Reward: the **Dark Reunion** achievement (silver) and the end of the chapter. See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
- Doable solo or in co-op.
- **Do not shoot the orb around the Hilt and do not take the Hilt** until you have done **everything**: if the boss starts too early, you can no longer save Klaus.
- The big parts can be done **in any order you like**, or at the same time: the guided quest up to the boss, the **four upgraded Teslas**, the **Red Talon sword**, then the record player and the second set of "voices".

## What to prepare

<Checklist>
  <Step title="The guided quest, up to just before the boss">
    Do all of [Main Easter Egg: Fireworks](../easter-egg-principal/): the power, the Tesla, the Right Hand of God, the left hand, the Voice of God. **Stop** when the orb surrounds the Hilt. For the Tesla upgrades, you need to have finished the lightning rod defence.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The four upgraded Teslas">
    Bloodthirst, Reaper, Hurricane and Midnight: see [The four Tesla Gun upgrades](../ameliorations-tesla/). Start early: it takes time.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The Red Talon sword">
    See [The Red Talon sword](../epee-red-talon/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The record player and the second set of voices

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Put the Record in the Pub">
    Take the **Red Talon**. On the **upper floor of the Pub**, put the Record on the **record player** (gramophone). A song plays on a loop.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'record_player.webp'} alt="The Pub record player" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill 25 to 30 zombies with the Red Talon during the song">
    Wait for the song to **start over** (record noise). Then start killing with the **Red Talon**. The record player's arm shows how far along the song is, from the edge toward the centre. If you don't reach the total before the end, the kills reset to zero. Once done, the record player no longer accepts energy, a light flashes green and red and it **clicks**.

    {/* TO CHECK: kill count: mmmrkennedy 25-30; Accelerated Ideas 25-50 depending on players; PowerPyx "until it stops accepting zombie energy". */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Note the four numbers">
    Look at the record player's **light**: count the **green flashes**. There are **four sets** (numbers from 1 to 5); each one is followed by a red flash, and a pause separates the sets. PowerPyx and Accelerated Ideas say to count the sound **clicks** (for example 5, 4, 3, 1): if you can't manage it, turn the music off.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'light_on_player.webp'} alt="The record player light" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'second_set_of_voices_order.webp'} alt="The order of the flash sets" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>

    {/* TO CHECK: mmmrkennedy = count the light flashes; PowerPyx and Accelerated Ideas = count the audio clicks. Both are probably valid. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter them on the Voice of God">
    In the Emperor's Chamber, type the four numbers on the **Voice of God** machine. PowerPyx specifies: start with the **Bloodraven** pillar (the first on the left as you face the machine) and go **clockwise**. If it's right, your character says the noise is getting louder.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The Rabenherz (the stone)

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Shoot the chandelier with the four Teslas">
    Above the Hilt, there is a **chandelier**. Shoot its **outer metal ring** with each of the **four upgraded Teslas**. On every correct shot, the chandelier "shocks" the Hilt. The **order is random**: shoot once with each Tesla to find the first, then the next ones. On the fourth correct shot, you receive the **Rabenherz** and the orb turns **blue**. In co-op, each player shoots with one variant.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'chandelier.webp'} alt="The chandelier above the Hilt" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The Hilt, the boss and Klaus

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Take the Hilt">
    Shoot the **blue** orb with a Tesla, then take the **Hilt**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Beat the Panzermörder (hard version)">
    Same fight as in [Main Easter Egg: Fireworks](../easter-egg-principal/), with three differences: **only one** of the Zeppelin's four lights is on, and that is the one to aim at when it comes back (the game changes it); the boss stays **stunned for a shorter time**; and the three batteries are still required.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Escort Klaus">
    The Hilt **brings Klaus back to life**. He walks slowly to the **Pilot Light trap** in the Village Square. Stay close to him: **infinite ammo** while you are next to him. You don't need to defend him. When he reaches the trap, he shouts story lines and explodes.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

You earn **Dark Reunion** and you progress on the **B.A.T. Elite** character challenges (save Klaus): see [Unlock the secret characters](../personnages-secrets/).
