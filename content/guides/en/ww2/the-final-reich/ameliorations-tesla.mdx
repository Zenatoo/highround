The Tesla Gun (see [Build the Tesla Gun](../tesla/)) has **four variants**. Each one needs a **battery** (the Überschnalle), which you stash, charge with a trap, then transfer into a machine. All four are **required** for [Full Easter Egg: Dark Reunion](../easter-egg-complet/). Getting all four in the **same game** gives the **Dark Arts** achievement to the whole team.

| Variant | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| **Bloodthirst**, red | Stunned zombies **explode** after a few seconds |
| **Reaper**, orange | Turns **one** zombie into a time bomb |
| **Hurricane**, purple | A big, slow **orb** that grows |
| **Midnight**, green | A **small, fast orb** |

## Before you start

Each variant follows the same five steps.

1. Take the **battery** out of its hiding spot.
2. **Charge it** with a trap.
3. Move forward in the guided quest **up to the defence of the three lightning rods**, when the Zeppelin appears: see [Main Easter Egg: Fireworks](../easter-egg-principal/).
4. Bring the battery to the **machine** in the Morgue or the Laboratory that gave you a Tesla part. A slot opens on the left and on the right. Put the battery in, hold the action button, then **defend the machine** until the health bar disappears.
5. Pick up the **new part**, go back to the Command Room forge and use the pedestal to upgrade your Tesla.

In co-op, a variant is only picked up by **one** player: split the four between you.

## Bloodthirst

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Make the spark travel from lamppost to lamppost">
    At **Riverside**, near the **M1928** wall weapon, shoot a lamppost with the Tesla: it crackles. Then shoot the spark with a **bullet weapon** so that it jumps to the next lamppost, quickly, all the way to the **Morgue**. At the last lamppost, a **battery** drops near the spike trap. If you shoot too slowly, the lamppost goes out: finish the round and start again from the first one.

    The order of the lampposts (27 in total):

    - **Riverside**: near the M1928 wall weapon; behind that one; further behind; above the rooster-shaped weather vane; an unlit lamppost behind; at the top of the stairs, after the Pub valve, left side; opposite, toward the spawn.
    - **Village Entrance**: above the M30 wall weapon; above the Lebenblitz.
    - **Village Square**: on the right, just outside the spawn; behind; further behind; next to the "generator" valve with the lights; above and next to the previous one.
    - **Command Room**: just after the bunker door; at the bottom of the left stairs; above the M1 Garand wall weapon; just inside the big room; on the first big lamp above the Salt Mine door; on the second.
    - **Morgue**: on the first big lamp, "above" the stairs; above the next stairs toward the armour station; to the left of the armour station; above the BAR wall weapon; next to the previous one; in the next room, at the top of the stairs; at the bottom of the stairs.

## Reaper

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Have a Wüstling break the metal door">
    In the **Sewers**, to the left of the door to the Pilot Light trap area, there is a **metal door**. Draw a **Wüstling** so that it **charges the door**: it opens and reveals a **battery**. Put it in the charging station next to it.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'reaper/metal_door.webp'} alt="The Sewers metal door" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'reaper/battery_charger.webp'} alt="The charging station near the broken door" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Charge it with the saw trap (the hardest)">
    You need **two charges**. Each charge: kill **two Bombers** in a single use of the **Saw Trap** near the battery. If the Bombers are close enough, the battery glows green. With a single kill, the meter falls back to 0.

    Tip: shoot the **head of the small zombie** carrying the bomb on the Bomber's back, to make the bomb drop and stop it from exploding.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Transfer into the Morgue machine (right slot)">
    **Morgue** machine, **right** slot.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'reaper/right_slot.webp'} alt="The right slot of the Morgue machine" credit={by} source={src} />

    Then upgrade your Tesla at the forge.

</Checklist>

## Hurricane

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Blow up a Bomber against the Laboratory metal door">
    To the left of the **Schildblitz**, in the **Laboratory**, a staircase has a **metal door** in the middle. Draw a **Bomber** and make it explode next to the door: a **battery** drops. Put it in the charging station **inside the electric trap**.

## Midnight

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Reveal the battery with the Brenner's head">
    In the **Courtyard**, to the left of the **Lee Enfield** wall weapon, aim the **head of a Brenner** at the **statue** (aiming down sights): it explodes and hides a **battery**. Shoot it with the Tesla to make it fall, pick it up and put it in the charging station to the right of the statue.
