The Darkest Shore (DLC 1, The Resistance) has **10 trophies** (achievements on Xbox and PC): **7 bronze** and **3 silver**. Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device. The names are the in-game English ones. The difficulty level of the game changes nothing.

{/* TO CHECK: trophy types (bronze/silver) come from PlatPursuit's PS4 list only; Xbox Gamerscore and Steam names not checked. French names from ActuGaming. */}

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Going Up... (bronze)">
    Unlock the Ubersprengen, the Pack-a-Punch. See [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="I Got This (bronze)">
    Survive the first assault on the beach **without using the fixed machine guns**. You can kill the zombies with the shovel.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Own the Fog, Be the Fog (bronze)">
    Kill a **Meuchler** in the fog. The first fog brings none: the first Meuchler arrives around round 7 or 8. You have about 1 minute to 1 min 30 to kill it. Fight it indoors, for example in the U-Boat Pens: you won't be shooting blind. Explosives work well. Its head is its weak point.

    {/* TO CHECK: first Meuchler wave "around 7-8" and the 1-1.5 minute window come from PowerPyx's trophy guide only; mmmrkennedy says every fog except the first spawns one. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Still Lurking (bronze)">
    Find and shoot Doctor Straub. See [The map's secrets](../secrets/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Always on Spine (bronze)">
    Rip out the spine of a zombie of **each type** (zombie, Pest, Wüstling, Meuchler) with a Ripsaw heavy attack. They must be **charged**. The Easter Egg rituals give you Pest, Wüstling and Meuchler spines. See [The Ripsaw and the R.I.P. Saw](../scie-ripsaw/).

    {/* TO CHECK: PowerPyx's trophy guide says Wüstlings and Meuchlers need the upgraded Ripsaw, mmmrkennedy says the base Ripsaw can rip spines. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="The Bakers Dozen Massacre (bronze)">
    Kill **13 zombies** with a single Ranged Ripsaw shot. A straight shot is not enough: you need bounces. Wait for an **Insta-Kill** and shoot in a narrow room full of zombies, like the U-Boat Pens walkway.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Severe Pommeling (bronze)">
    Kill **250 zombies** with the Pommel in a single game. It comes back every 10 to 15 seconds: throw it into big groups.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Black Winds to Darker Lands (silver)">
    Recover the Pommel of Barbarossa during the main quest. See [Main Easter Egg: Making History](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="This Damn Island (silver)">
    Explore the island. According to PowerPyx, you don't need to visit everything: quest steps 1 to 6 unlock it, and the whole group gets it.

    {/* TO CHECK: unlocked by quest steps 1-6 for the whole lobby = PowerPyx only (single source). */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Making History (silver)">
    Escape the island before the bombing: this is the main Easter Egg, final fight included. See [Main Easter Egg: Making History](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="glitch" author="PowerPyx" source="https://www.powerpyx.com/call-of-duty-ww2-dlc-1-the-resistance-trophy-guide-roadmap/">

  </Tip>
