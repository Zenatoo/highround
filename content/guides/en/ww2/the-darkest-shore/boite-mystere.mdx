The Darkest Shore has one **Mystery Box** and a hidden special weapon, **The Classic**. The general rules of the box are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).

## The Mystery Box

One spin costs **1,000 Jolts**.

{/* TO CHECK: where the box sits and whether it moves: no remaining source says. The four-location list was removed. */}

Almost all wall weapons and starting weapons can come out of the box. The **Jack-in-the-Box** (a tactical) is in there too.

## The Classic (special PPSh-41)

It is a faster **PPSh-41** with a **70-round drum magazine** and more reserve ammo. Upgraded at the Pack-a-Punch, it becomes the **Babushka**, with a chrome camo. It is **rare** in the box.

To get it into the box:

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Open the Corpse Gate">
    This is the first step of the main quest: see [Main Easter Egg: Making History](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Call the friendly Bomber">
    Use the **right-hand** machine in the Freezer (behind the Corpse Gate). A friendly Bomber heads to the Artillery Bunker.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Drop 1,000 Jolts on its path">
    It picks up the money. When it has enough, it stops taking any: **The Classic** then joins the box. Nothing is guaranteed: you may not draw it.

    {/* TO CHECK: 1,000 jolts in total = mmmrkennedy ("after 1000 jolts it won't pick up any more"), single source. */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>
