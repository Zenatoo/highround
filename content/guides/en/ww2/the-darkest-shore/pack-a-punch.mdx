The Pack-a-Punch is called the **Ubersprengen**. It is hidden at the top of **Bunker 3**. To raise it, you must pick up **three green batteries** by taking the **minecarts** on the map's three routes.

The Pack-a-Punch costs **5,000 Jolts**. The basic rules are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/). The **Going Up...** achievement unlocks here: see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).

## Before you leave

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Turn on both switches">
    You need both: see [Turn on the power](../courant/). The second one starts the minecarts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Spot the three stations">
    There are three minecart stations: **Strand** (the beach), **U-Boot Hohle** (the U-Boat Pens) and **Graben** (Bunker 1). A **coloured lever** next to the track changes the destination.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The three routes

Do the three routes, **in any order you like**:

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Graben (Bunker 1) to Strand (beach)">
    During the ride, an **Elektroschnalle** prompt appears: press the action button to take the **green battery** lying near the track. You don't need to look at it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Strand (beach) to U-Boot Hohle (U-Boat Pens)">
    Same thing: take the second battery on the way.
  </Step>

  <Step title="U-Boot Hohle (U-Boat Pens) to Graben (Bunker 1)">
    Take the third battery.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

The routes can also be done in the other direction: what matters is that you have taken **all three links**. On the first ride of each round, **four Bombers** (bomb zombies) stand on the rails: shoot them before they explode.

## The Bunker 3 machine

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Go to the small bridge above Bunker 3">
    The machine with **three slots** is at the bottom of a slope, near the **Kugelblitz** machine. One green battery is already inside.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/pap_fuse_box.webp'} alt="The three-slot machine in Bunker 3" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place the three batteries, one by one">
    When everything is in place, the lift goes up and the **Pack-a-Punch** arrives on the bridge.
