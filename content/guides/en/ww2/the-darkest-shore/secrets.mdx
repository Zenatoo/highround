The Darkest Shore hides a few small secrets outside the main quest. There is **no known music Easter Egg** on this map: none of the sources we read mention one. The bigger secrets, like [The Mystery Box and weapons](../boite-mystere/) (The Classic) or [The Ripsaw and the R.I.P. Saw](../scie-ripsaw/), have their own guide.

## Dr. Straub: "Still Lurking"

Doctor **Straub** (a man in a white coat) briefly appears in **unreachable** areas, where zombies spawn. **Shoot him** for the **Still Lurking** achievement. Only the player who shoots gets it.

How he behaves:

- He appears **from the start** of the game, in **one place** at a time, chosen at random, **where nobody is looking**.
- He waits indefinitely. If you **look straight at him**, he leaves, then comes back somewhere else after **3 to 5 seconds**.
- Each place is used only **once** per game.
- He no longer appears once the final fight starts.

## The "HI" beams: 1 free Jolt

In the big **U-Boat Pens** room, look through the **zombie spawn window** in front of the minecart. Above a door, there are **two I-beams**. Shoot the **left** one about **eight times**: it turns, the two beams spell the word "**HI**", and you get **1 Jolt**.

## The final fight Jack-in-the-Box

If you draw a **Jack-in-the-Box** from the Mystery Box, throw it into the **round hole** of the **wooden crate** to the right of the **beached whale**, on the beach. If it works, you hear a thunder noise and the Jack **disappears** instead of exploding. It may take several tries.

At the start of the final fight (see [Main Easter Egg: Making History](../easter-egg-principal/)), during the phase 1 cutscene, the red Meistermeuchler spawns a special **Jack-in-the-Box**. It attracts and kills zombies, then explodes after about **30 seconds** for heavy damage, even to nearby Meistermeuchlers.
