The Darkest Shore takes place on a fog-covered island in the north of Germany. The team (Marie, Drostan, Olivia and Jefferson) follows Doctor Straub's trail there. The island hides a pagan ritual room linked to the goddess **Nerthus**. The quest: recover the **Pommel** of Barbarossa's sword, call in a British bombing by radio, then beat four **Meistermeuchlers** and **escape the island before the bombing**.

**Before you start:**

- Allow **1 h 10 to 1 h 30** in solo. The quest is long and hard (9 out of 10 in solo according to mmmrkennedy): PowerPyx recommends four players.
- It can be done solo or in co-op. Videos prove it (see below).
- The reward: the **Making History** achievement, plus **Black Winds to Darker Lands** on the way to the Pommel. At the end, every player gets **all six Blitz** and sees a cutscene. See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
- Prepare the **power** and the **Ranged Ripsaw**: [Turn on the power](../courant/) and [The Ripsaw and the R.I.P. Saw](../scie-ripsaw/).
- Bring **frag grenades** (or a similar explosive) in your class.
- **Do not take the Jack-in-the-Box** before you have used the Pommel on the radio: the Pommel takes its place as your tactical (see [The map's secrets](../secrets/)).

**Class choice (PowerPyx's advice):** **Shellshock** with the **Exploit Weakness** (triple damage on Meistermeuchlers) and **Sustain Zone** mods is the right choice in a group. The abilities are explained in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).

## Step 1: the Corpse Gate

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Make the hanged man's head fall">
    In **Bunker 3**, a corpse hangs above the Pack-a-Punch stairs. Shoot its **neck** with the **Ranged Ripsaw**: the head falls onto the stairs. Pick it up.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'main_ee/hanging_man.webp'} alt="The hanged corpse in Bunker 3" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Put the head on the headless corpse">
    It is at the back of the **U-Boat Pens**. Kill about **10 zombies** next to it. Straub speaks, the door closes and floor tiles spit fire: this is a **lockdown**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'main_ee/corpse_gate.webp'} alt="The headless corpse, the Corpse Gate" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Close the three gas valves">
    The valves are on each side of the Corpse Gate and to the left of the stairs. The fire under one valve goes out at random (sound of gas shutting off): close that valve then. If you are too slow, the fire comes back: wait. If you play in a group, leave one player outside to revive.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'main_ee/gas_valve.webp'} alt="A gas valve in the U-Boat Pens" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Interact with the corpse">
    It is cut in two and the **Freezer** door opens. A **Brenner** appears: kill it. It drops a **Max Ammo that does not disappear**: keep it for the final fight.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

The quest then splits into **two independent branches**, which you can do in any order: the **war branch** (Bomber, radio, ships) and the **pagan branch** (secret room, statues, rituals). The game's Notebook shows them separately.

## War branch

### Shoot down the planes

Go outside, towards the **Bunkers**. Planes arrive and shoot at you. Use the **anti-aircraft guns** to shoot them down until the message "Shot down the German Fighter Planes". A single plane crashes into the cliff above Schildblitz and leaves its pilot hanging with a radio.

### Escort the Bomber to the cannon

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Call the friendly Bomber">
    Use the **right-hand** machine in the Freezer. A friendly Bomber appears with a battery. You must escort it to the **Artillery Bunker**. You cannot hurt it, but zombies can. It does not have much health.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'main_ee/bomber_spawner.webp'} alt="The Bomber machine on the right in the Freezer" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Charge the cannon">
    Once at the Artillery Bunker, the Bomber puts the battery down and dies. Kill zombies near the battery to charge it: about **5 kills per cannon shot**, and you need **at least two**.

    {/* TO CHECK: kills per shot and cannon capacity: mmmrkennedy about 5 kills per shot, holds 6 shots (single source). */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

If the Bomber dies before it arrives, wait for the next round and start again. If a Bomber has already delivered its battery, you cannot call another. Make sure the planes are shot down before the escort: otherwise they kill it.

### Sink the ships

The ships arrive **one by one**. Use the cannon: the **left panel** sets the **elevation**, the **right** one the **angle**, the **middle button** fires. A help image gives the settings depending on the ship's position. Without it, use the maps on the wall to the right of the cannon: the top one gives the angle and the distance in metres, the bottom one converts the distance into a panel value (+05, -10...). If you miss, a voice guides you ("Aim lower!"). Each shot requires you to **reload** the cannon by killing zombies.

## The three rituals

You need **three rituals**, one per enemy type, in any order and **one at a time**. For each one: rip out a **charged spine** (see [The Ripsaw and the R.I.P. Saw](../scie-ripsaw/)) and give it to the **Monk Head** in the Freezer. When the ritual succeeds, go into the secret room with **all players**: the door closes, a fight starts and you pick up a **head** (Anointed Head). If the friendly enemy dies, you need another spine.

### Meuchler ritual

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Give a Meuchler spine to the Monk Head">
    Meuchlers spawn by themselves every few minutes in the secret room: this is a good way to get the spine. A friendly Meuchler comes out of the Freezer and leaves: the **fog** arrives.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find it and touch it, three times">
    It hides outside, curled up in a ball, and **screams**. Listen for its screams and follow the zombies: they go towards it. Touch it before the zombies kill it or the fog disappears. It then moves elsewhere: **three** times in all. A real Meuchler can also spawn in this fog.

    Possible places: near the whale (beach), on the ledge at the bottom of the Pack-a-Punch stairs, in front of the Pack-a-Punch, near the anti-aircraft gun next to Kugelblitz, to the left of the Lee-Enfield wall weapon (Bunker 1), to the right of the SVT-40 (Bluffs). The list is not necessarily complete.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fight in the secret room">
    All players go in. A Meuchler drops from the ceiling: it has more health than a normal one. Kill it and take the head.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

### Wüstling ritual

<Checklist>

  <Step title="Lead it to the Blitz machines in this order">

Coloured bulbs on its back show the Blitz it has already taken. After Lebenblitz, it goes through the **hole in the wall** next to the machine and disappears.

## Get the Pommel

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Hang the heads">
    Take the **Monk Head** from the Freezer machine. In the secret room, hang it with the three heads on the **four hooks** in the ceiling.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'main_ee/hooks.webp'} alt="The four hooks in the secret room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill the blood pool">
    The door closes. Kill zombies anywhere in the room to fill the hollow in the middle with blood: about **50 to 75 kills**. Zombies spawn endlessly.

    {/* TO CHECK: 50-75 kills = mmmrkennedy only. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the Pommel">
    The animal skull held by the statue explodes: the **Pommel of Barbarossa** appears.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'main_ee/pommel.webp'} alt="The Pommel of Barbarossa" credit={by} source={src} />

    It is a **tactical**: it creates an explosion and an energy bubble. Zombies inside the bubble explode a few seconds later. It comes back by itself every **10 to 15 seconds**.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Patch 1.10 fixed leaving the Pommel room, and patch 1.11 fixed the Pommel's recharge. If the Pommel glitches, a **Max Ammo** often fixes the problem (PowerPyx).

## The final fight: the Meistermeuchlers

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Start the fight">
    Throw the **Pommel** onto the **radio** at the Overlook (the one from the war branch). A prompt appears. **Do not press** before you are ready: take the Jack-in-the-Boxes if you want (see [The map's secrets](../secrets/)). When all players are ready, **everyone presses on the radio at the same time**.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

A cutscene introduces **four Meistermeuchlers**, each with an ability that mimics the players':

- **purple** (Frontline): gives nearby zombies an electric armour; it arrives first and has more health;
- **green** (Camouflage): briefly blinds players it hits;
- **red** (Shellshock): sends out a stun attack, on a timer;
- **yellow** (Freefire): fires a saw blade that bounces off walls.

To **knock out** one of them, shoot until it has no hit markers left: it runs away. If it is too slow, it leaves on its own and another takes its place; the one you did not knock out comes back after the others.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Phase 1: the beach, in the fog">
    Only one Meistermeuchler at a time. When all four are knocked out, the fog lifts. Get in the **minecart** to the next arena: you receive a **Max Ammo** during the ride.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Phase 2: the U-Boat Pens">
    A **Brenner** appears. The exit door is closed and tiles spit fire. **Two** Meistermeuchlers at a time. After the knock-outs, the door opens and the fire stops. You find an **Überladen** power-up at the top of the stairs. Minecart, with a Max Ammo.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Phase 3: the Bunkers area, in the fog">
    You have access to the whole upper area, from the Pack-a-Punch to the Artillery Bunker. **Three** Meistermeuchlers at a time. Minecart, with a Max Ammo.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Phase 4: the beach, without fog">
    All **four** at the same time. This time they **die** instead of running away: focus on just one. When the fourth falls, the ending cutscene starts.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="PowerPyx" source="https://www.powerpyx.com/call-of-duty-ww2-zombies-the-darkest-shore-easter-egg-guide/">
    Use **Shellshock** with **Sustain Zone** and **Exploit Weakness**, two players at most per spot, rotating. When you run out of energy, take an **Überladen** power-up.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## What can go wrong

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="PowerPyx" source="https://www.powerpyx.com/call-of-duty-ww2-zombies-the-darkest-shore-easter-egg-guide/">
    Common mistakes: wrong Blitz order for the Wüstling; too slow on the Meuchler spine (a second one spawns); the friendly Bomber, zombie or Wüstling dying; being locked in a lockdown with no player outside; leaving nobody in the U-Boat Pens during the plane step; the fire in the valve room and the fight arenas.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

In solo, **Lebenblitz** works as your revive. Players have done it solo: a world record of 30 min 25 with consumables (CienkaRentaFX), a run without dying (iSlaymii) and one without consumables (Mikee B Gaming).
