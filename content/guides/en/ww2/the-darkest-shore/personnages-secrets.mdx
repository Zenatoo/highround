The Darkest Shore adds **11 characters** to unlock through challenges, plus two characters that require playing several maps. You track your challenges in the game: **Lobby Menu, Soldier, Dossier, Challenges**.

**To check:** the numbers below come from mmmrkennedy's guide, which says they were read from the game code. The sources also don't say clearly whether you must complete all **three** challenges of a character or just one: do them all.

## Treasure zombies

Several challenges ask for zombie pieces, which are dropped by **Treasure Zombies**: Bombers that don't attack you, carry red bombs and drop Jolts. When one spawns, Jolts fall all over the map. Each piece (A, B, C) has about a **1 in 12 chance** per zombie killed, and you can get a piece you already have.

### Deathraven Mountaineer (Keep Moving)

<Screenshot src={m + 'characters/deathraven_mountaineer.webp'} alt="The Deathraven Mountaineer character" credit={by} source={src} />

All three challenges are done during the U-Boat Pens **fire lockdown** (Corpse Gate step).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Survive 5 rounds during the lockdown">
    This is the Corpse Gate fire pit.
  </Step>

  <Step title="100 kills with the Propeller Trap during the lockdown">
    The propeller trap is on the U-Boat Pens walkway.
  </Step>

  <Step title="10 headshots during the lockdown">
    With a normal bullet weapon (no pistol, no explosive). They don't need to be in a row.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

### The Mountaineer from Ruskeala (Barbarossa)

<Screenshot src={m + 'characters/ruskeala.webp'} alt="The Mountaineer from Ruskeala character" credit={by} source={src} />

All three challenges require getting the **Pommel** in a certain way.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Without taking damage from an Anointed enemy">
    **Anointed** enemies are the glowing ones in the rituals (Pest, Wüstling, Meuchler).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Without a special ability">
    No player uses an ability (Freefire, Shellshock...). Mods and consumables are allowed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Without killing with the Ripsaw shot">
    You can shoot, it is required by the quest, but without hitting or hurting an enemy. Melee is allowed.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

### Bloodraven Survivalist (In Charge)

<Screenshot src={m + 'characters/bloodraven_survivalist.webp'} alt="The Bloodraven Survivalist character" credit={by} source={src} />

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Final fight without being downed">
    Only downs **during** the fight count, for the whole team: a single player downed fails the challenge.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fewer than 10 zombies empowered by the purple Meistermeuchler">
    Focus your fire on it as soon as it appears: if it is knocked out quickly, it runs away and no longer empowers. Kill the zombies you see.
  </Step>

  <Step title="No player stunned by the red Meistermeuchler">
    Knock it out as fast as possible.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

### The Survivalist from Hanoi (The Hunter)

<Screenshot src={m + 'characters/hanoi.webp'} alt="The Survivalist from Hanoi character" credit={by} source={src} />

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Finish the quest without traps">
    See [Main Easter Egg: Making History](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill 3 Meuchlers with traps in one game">
    Meuchlers avoid traps: activate it when they are inside, or stun them in the area. The U-Boat Pens **Propeller Trap** does the most damage. **Shellshock** stuns them.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Survive 5 rounds in a row with only traps">
    The quickest trap to reach is the Propeller Trap. Gather the zombies then use Shellshock with the **Fiery Burst** mod to earn points fast.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

### Bloodraven Slayer (V.I.B.)

<Screenshot src={m + 'characters/bloodraven_slayer.webp'} alt="The Bloodraven Slayer character" credit={by} source={src} />

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Escort the Bomber without it taking damage">
    If it takes any, let it die and try again. If a Bomber has already delivered its battery, you cannot call another.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sink every ship without missing a shot">
    The cannon's help image shows where to shoot: see [Main Easter Egg: Making History](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="10 Bombers in a row killed with headshots in the minecarts">
    Only the tunnel Bombers count. If a Bomber explodes by itself, the streak is not reset: let it explode if you are about to miss.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

### Deathraven Slayer (Tools of the Trade)

<Screenshot src={m + 'characters/deathraven_slayer.webp'} alt="The Deathraven Slayer character" credit={by} source={src} />

<Checklist>
  <Step title="25 rounds in a row with the weapon from your first Mystery Box draw">
    The first weapon drawn counts, even if you don't take it. The count resets if you draw, kill with or hold another weapon at the end of a round. Upgraded versions and attachments count.
  </Step>

  <Step title="25 rounds in a row with the Gewehr 43, the Sten or the GMPG">
    The count starts at the beginning of the game: the Gewehr 43 as a starting weapon is recommended. Don't let the round end while your weapon is in the Pack-a-Punch.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Survive 5 rounds with only the Pommel">
    The Pommel is obtained in the main quest.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The two cross-map characters

### Wicht Warden (Cold Blooded)

You must complete the **main Easter Egg** on **every map**:

- [Full Easter Egg: Dark Reunion](../../the-final-reich/easter-egg-complet/) (The Final Reich, hard version);
- [Main Easter Egg: Making History](../easter-egg-principal/) (this map);
- [Main Easter Egg: Stadtjäger Down](../../the-shadowed-throne/easter-egg-principal/) (The Shadowed Throne);
- The Tortured Path: see [How The Tortured Path works](../../the-tortured-path/chapitres/);
- [Main Easter Egg: Kingfall](../../the-frozen-dawn/easter-egg-principal/) (The Frozen Dawn).

### Rookbane

Reach **level 1000** in Zombies mode.
