In WWII Zombies, perks are called **Blitz machines**. There is **no Jugger-Nog**: **armour** (Geistschild) takes its place. The Darkest Shore has all **six Blitz machines**. The general rules (four-Blitz limit, effects, armour) are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).

You can carry **four Blitz**. If you buy a fifth, it replaces the first one you bought.

## The six machines

Prices come from Activision's official page and apply on every map.

| Blitz | Effect | Price | Where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lebenblitz** | Solo: one automatic revive (3 per game). In co-op: faster revive | 500 Jolts in solo, 1,500 Jolts in co-op | On the beach |
| **Schnellblitz** | Faster reload | 3,000 Jolts | Location not confirmed |
| **Kugelblitz** | More damage | 2,000 Jolts | In Bunker 1, next to an anti-aircraft gun |
| **Laufenblitz** | Faster running, endless sprint | 2,000 Jolts | Outside, in front of the Artillery Bunker, near the grassy arch that leads down |
| **Faustblitz** | More melee damage | 2,000 Jolts | In the Artillery Bunker |
| **Schildblitz** | An electric shock when you start reloading | 2,000 Jolts | In the Bluffs, near the Waffe 28 wall weapon and the Bunker 3 door |

## Armour

Armour is sold at **large red displays** on the map, for example in the **Artillery Bunker** and near the Ripsaw workbench, in the **U-Boat Pens**. Each purchase gives **3 armour points**: one point absorbs one hit that would have downed you. The price starts at **500 Jolts** and goes up with each purchase.

## Blitz and the Easter Egg

Blitz machines are also used in the quest: you must lead a friendly **Wüstling** to four machines in a precise order (Kugelblitz, Faustblitz, Laufenblitz, Lebenblitz). Everything is in [Main Easter Egg: Making History](../easter-egg-principal/). At the end of the quest, every player gets **all six Blitz**.
