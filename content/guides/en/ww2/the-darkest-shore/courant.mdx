The Darkest Shore has **two power switches**. The first turns on the map. The second, higher up, starts the **minecart network**, which you need for the Pack-a-Punch and the Easter Egg. The general rules of the mode (Jolts, Blitz machines) are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).

## The two switches

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Switch 1: in the U-Boat Pens">
    It is in the **U-Boat Pens** area (the submarine shelters). Go down the stairs on your left after the armour stand: the switch is on the wall to the right. It turns on the map's power.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Switch 2: in the Artillery Bunker">
    It is at the top of the map, in the **Artillery Bunker**. It starts the **minecarts**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap/minecart_power.webp'} alt="The minecart switch in the Artillery Bunker" credit={by} source={src} />

## What is the power for?

- You need the power to build the **Ripsaw**: see [The Ripsaw and the R.I.P. Saw](../scie-ripsaw/).
- The second switch (minecarts) is required to [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
- The power is also needed at the start of [Main Easter Egg: Making History](../easter-egg-principal/).

{/* TO CHECK: Player.One says power also opens doors and starts the Blitz machines; no other source says it. */}

Turn on both switches as soon as you can: the minecart tour takes time.
