The **Ripsaw** is The Darkest Shore's special weapon: a **melee saw**. It replaces the shovel. It is upgraded in two stages: the **Ranged Ripsaw** (it throws bouncing blades), then the **R.I.P. Saw** at the Pack-a-Punch.

The three levels:

| Level | Price | What it gives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ripsaw | 2,500 Jolts | Powerful melee, rips out spines |
| Ranged Ripsaw | 500 Jolts | Throws bouncing blades that pass through several zombies |
| R.I.P. Saw | Pack-a-Punch | More blades and more melee damage |

The Ranged Ripsaw is **required** for [Main Easter Egg: Making History](../easter-egg-principal/). Build it early. The power must be on: see [Turn on the power](../courant/).

Since patch 1.19 (July 2018), special weapon upgrades are no longer limited by round: old videos may say otherwise.

## Rip out a spine

With the Ripsaw, you can **rip out an enemy's spine**:

1. Do a **heavy attack**: aim then strike, or run then strike.
2. During the animation, press the action button.

A spine is **charged** when it lights up (a flash of lightning) and a bar appears on screen, near the perks. The spines of Pests, Wüstlings and Meuchlers are almost always charged. Those of normal zombies sometimes are. A spine does not stay charged forever.

<Screenshot src={m + 'ripsaw/ranged_ripsaw/spine_hud.webp'} alt="The spine charge bar on screen" credit={by} source={src} />

## The R.I.P. Saw

You need the Pack-a-Punch ([Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/)), the Ranged Ripsaw and the **Bomber** step of the Easter Egg (to charge the cannon). The goal is to fire the right code with the cannon: a Pack-a-Punch part then drops.

### 1. Find the six dials

Six small broken machines each show **a single dial**. The dial's place on the machine gives its **rank** (far left = first). The **1st and 4th** are a **+ or a -**, the others are **digits**.

### 2. Charge the cannon and enter the code

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Charge the cannon">
    Do the **friendly Bomber** step of the main quest (see [Main Easter Egg: Making History](../easter-egg-principal/)), then kill zombies near the battery it brought. A charging sound means the cannon is ready.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the code">
    The **first three symbols** go on the **left panel** (the **elevation**). The **last three** go on the **right panel** (the **angle**). Press the **middle button** to fire.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'main_ee/cannon_elevation.webp'} alt="The left panel, the elevation" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'main_ee/cannon_angle.webp'} alt="The right panel, the angle" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'main_ee/cannon_fire_button.webp'} alt="The fire button between the panels" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the fuse">
    If the code is right, a **Pack-a-Punch fuse** drops from the cannon onto the concrete pillar. Take it.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'ripsaw/rip_saw_upgrade/fuse.webp'} alt="The fuse on the cannon's concrete pillar" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

### 3. Upgrade the Ripsaw

Go to the Pack-a-Punch and upgrade the Ripsaw. Once upgraded, you can **refill its ammo** at the machine.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="PowerPyx" source="https://www.powerpyx.com/cod-ww2-darkest-shore-how-to-build-upgrade-ripsaw/">
    PowerPyx finds this upgrade **not very useful**: it changes the number of blades and the melee damage, not the power of the blades.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
