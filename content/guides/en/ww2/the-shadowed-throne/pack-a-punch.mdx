On The Shadowed Throne, **there is no power switch**. The Pack-a-Punch (in game: **Ubersprengen**) opens another way: you must first have the Wunderbuss, then charge two electrical panels with it.

{/* TO CHECK: Gameranx-era pages (GamingBolt, VideoGamesBlogger) mention "turn on the power"; mmmrkennedy, Gameranx PaP page and Accelerated Ideas show no power switch. */}

- The Pack-a-Punch costs **5,000 Jolts** per weapon. Refilling the ammo of an already upgraded weapon costs 4,000 Jolts. The general rules are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).
- The Wunderbuss **cannot** be upgraded.
- Finding the Ubersprengen gives the **Das ist gut** achievement. See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).

## Before you start

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Build the Wunderbuss">
    You need it to charge the panels. See [Build the Wunderbuss](../wunderbuss/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bring a throwable explosive">
    You must have a **frag grenade, a Sticky grenade or a Satchel charge** in your equipment. With **S-Mines** or **throwing knives**, you cannot open the panels. The Fliegerfaust rocket launcher also works, but it is very rare in the Mystery Box.

    {/* TO CHECK: Gameranx (PaP page) says Sticky Grenades make it much easier and implies it can be done without; mmmrkennedy says a throwable is required. */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Charge the two panels

Both panels are in the starting building, behind a metal plate that only an explosion can blow off.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Underbelly panel">
    In the **Underbelly** (the starting area), behind bars, to the **right of the door** that leads to the Destroyed Building. Throw your explosive at the metal plate.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'panel_spawn.webp'} alt="The panel behind the bars, in the Underbelly" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Destroyed Building panel">
    In the **Destroyed Building**, on the wall to the **left of the Waffe 28 weapon wall**. Same thing: an explosion on the metal plate.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'panel_waffe.webp'} alt="The panel to the left of the Waffe 28 weapon wall" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot each panel with the Wunderbuss">
    You need about **10 Wunderbuss ammo** per panel. To reload the Wunderbuss, hit a zombie with the suction shot (the aim button): see [Build the Wunderbuss](../wunderbuss/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Watch the lift fall">
    When both panels are charged, the **Destroyed Building lift** falls and reveals the Pack-a-Punch in the shaft.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap.webp'} alt="The Pack-a-Punch in the Destroyed Building lift shaft" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>
