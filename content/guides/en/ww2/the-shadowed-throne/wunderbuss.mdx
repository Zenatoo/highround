The **Wunderbuss** is The Shadowed Throne's wonder weapon. It fires a **beam of electricity**. To reload it, you send a suction shot (the aim button) at a zombie: it steals its energy, and the more zombies near it, the more it fills. In practice, its ammo is almost infinite.

{/* TO CHECK: the description of the Wunderbuss (electric beam, suction recharge) was partly sourced from a Steam guide that was dropped; re-confirm with mmmrkennedy/Gameranx. */}

It is **required**: without it, no Pack-a-Punch, no anchors, no spotlight, and no last phase of the boss. It **cannot** be upgraded. Its quests (radio, flare, Sizzler zombie, battery) are the first part of the Easter Egg: see [Main Easter Egg: Stadtjäger Down](../easter-egg-principal/).

The **Something Wunderful** achievement asks you to kill 50 Gekocht with this weapon in one game. See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).

## Step 1: contact the Russians by radio

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Read the code on the radio">
    On **Main Street**, look at the top of the **radio**. A code is displayed: **two letters, a dash, a digit** (for example "TX-3"). It changes every game.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'radio.webp'} alt="The Main Street radio" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'radio_code.webp'} alt="The code written on top of the radio" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Read the map and the table in the church">
    In the **Church**, to the right of the Mystery Box, two sheets hang on the wall: a **map** with a pin on the name of a region, and a **frequency table** by region. The table never changes, only the pin does.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'papers_church.webp'} alt="The map and the frequency table hanging in the church" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find your two numbers">
    Look up the pin's region in the table. Then take the number that matches the **two letters** of the code, then the one that matches the **digit**.

    Example: code "TX-3", pin on "Barnim". In the Barnim section, TX gives **26.5** and the digit 3 gives **50.1**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'frequencies.webp'} alt="The church frequency table" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the two dials">
    Interact with the radio. The **left** dial takes the first number, the **right** one the second. On a controller, turn the sticks; on PC, move the needle with the side keys (A, D) and the mouse's fire and aim buttons. You have a margin of about 1 to 2 units.

    If it is right, a light turns green, a voice answers, and "Contacted the Russians" appears.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="Gameranx" source="https://gameranx.com/features/id/146531/">
    In solo, Camouflage is handy to set the radio without a zombie sticking to you.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Step 2: call the zeppelin

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Fire the flare">
    In the **Plaza**, to the left of the Museum door, there is a **wooden crate**. Shoot its padlock to open it, then shoot again (or hit it): a flare goes off and attracts Straub's **zeppelin**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'wooden_box.webp'} alt="The Plaza wooden crate" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Step 3: get a Geistbolt

Once it has latched on, the zeppelin fires a **Geistbolt** at a zombie without damaging it. It becomes a **Gekocht** (players say "Sizzler"), red and glowing. It is **about twice as fast**, but has the health of a normal zombie.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Kill a Sizzler in melee">
    A special melee hit kills it and gives you the **Geistbolt**. One appears every few minutes. The spiked bat is ideal.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'sizzlers.webp'} alt="A Sizzler, a red zombie created by the zeppelin" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="mmmrkennedy" source={src}>
    An already wounded zombie can no longer become a Sizzler. Wound them if you want to avoid creating any.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Step 4: find the battery

A **battery** appears in just one of these three places, at random, on a broken weapon wall crate:

- **Museum**, to the left of the Armour stand;
- **Museum**, to the right of the melee machine (Faustblitz);
- **Cabaret**, to the right of the M1927 weapon wall (M1928 in other sources).

## Step 5: build the weapon in the Apartments

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Open the grate">
    Go down to the **Apartments basement**. Put the battery in the grate's **generator**: it opens. Go in and take the battery inside: the grate **closes behind you**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'generator.webp'} alt="The grate generator, in the Apartments basement" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place the parts and take the Wunderbuss">
    Interact with the Wunderbuss on the table to place the Geistbolt and the battery, then interact again to take it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Leave the room">
    Shoot the **generator** with the Wunderbuss until the grate opens.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'wunderbuss.webp'} alt="The Wunderbuss on the basement table" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>
