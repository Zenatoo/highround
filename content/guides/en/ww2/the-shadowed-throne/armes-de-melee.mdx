Three special melee weapons are used in the main Easter Egg: the **Smuggler's Bat**, the **Dancer's Dagger** and the **Nazi Axe**. Each is a stronger version of a melee weapon on the map. You can do them **in any order, and at the same time**. You need the Wunderbuss for almost every step: see [Build the Wunderbuss](../wunderbuss/).

| Weapon | Official name | Version of | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Smuggler's Bat | Nachtmahr | Baseball Bat | Its secondary attack fires a short-range ball of Geistkraft |
| Dancer's Dagger | Perchta | Trench Knife | The heavy attack restores health, or a whole armour piece if your health is full |
| Nazi Axe | Holda | Ice Pick | While sprinting, a charge makes a small explosion that stuns zombies |

{/* TO CHECK: the Holda stun on impact is mmmrkennedy only (single source). */}

Once placed in their stone slots, they charge up and open the hidden courtyard: see [Main Easter Egg: Stadtjäger Down](../easter-egg-principal/). You can keep using them after the quest.

## The Smuggler's Bat

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Read the cash register number">
    In the **Apartments basement**, where you built the Wunderbuss, shoot the **cash register** sitting on a piece of furniture with the Wunderbuss. The drawer opens: a **photo** with **two numbers** is stuck underneath. These are two frequencies.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={sb + 'cash.webp'} alt="The cash register on a basement piece of furniture" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={sb + 'cash_pic.webp'} alt="The photo with the two numbers under the drawer" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Call the smuggler">
    Enter these two numbers on the **Main Street** radio (left then right, as for the Russians). You hear the smuggler: "Contacted the Smuggler".
  </Step>

  <Step title="Look at the ammo type on the table">
    In the same basement room, the **table** shows a type of ammo. It tells you what kind of weapon the smuggler wants: shotgun, pistol or submachine gun, rifle.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={sb + 'ammo_on_table.webp'} alt="The ammo on the basement table" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={sb + 'ammo_combined.webp'} alt="The possible ammo types" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Give him a weapon through the sewer hatch">
    In the **Plaza**, near the Cabaret's back door, shoot the **manhole cover** to open it (a grenade also works, according to other guides). Pass a weapon through the hole by holding the action button. Weapons that work:

    - **Shotgun**: Sawed-off (starting weapon wall) or Combat Shotgun (Museum).
    - **Pistol or submachine gun**: MP40 (Main Street), P08 or 9mm SAP (starting equipment).
    - **Rifle**: FG42 (Church).

    If it is the right class of weapon, "Armed the Smuggler" appears. Otherwise, he says he has no ammo for it. A weapon upgraded at the Pack-a-Punch slightly changes the end of the quest, but only in the story.

    {/* TO CHECK: weapon choice. mmmrkennedy (and Accelerated Ideas, partly): the ammo type on the table decides. Gameranx says it is random, just try guns; Newsweek says the Combat Shotgun worked. */}

    According to other guides, the choice may be random: if the weapon is refused, try another class.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={sb + 'sewer_hole_plaza.webp'} alt="The Plaza manhole cover, near the Cabaret" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait two rounds">
    Let **two full rounds** pass.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pay the smuggler">
    On **Main Street**, shoot the manhole cover in front of the Cabaret (if it does not open, wait one more round). Other guides blow up a Bomber on it. Throw Jolts into the hole as if you were giving them to a teammate, until you see "Paid the Smuggler": you need **1,500 Jolts**.

    {/* TO CHECK: 1,500 Jolts: mmmrkennedy only; other guides give no amount. */}

    <Screenshot src={sb + 'sewer_main_street.webp'} alt="The Main Street manhole cover, in front of the Cabaret" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Break down the wooden door">
    Wait about **45 seconds**, then go back to the Wunderbuss room and hit the **wooden door** three times. A **Wüstling** breaks it down: the smuggler is dead behind it. (If you gave him an upgraded weapon, it is the Wüstling that is dead.) Pick up the **bat** on the ground.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={sb + 'wooden_door.webp'} alt="The wooden door of the Wunderbuss room" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={sb + 'bat.webp'} alt="The Smuggler's Bat on the ground behind the door" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The Dancer's Dagger

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Take the Apartments painting">
    In the **Apartments bedroom**, interact with the big **painting of a woman**: you pick it up ("Found the Dancers Painting").

    <Screenshot src={dd + 'painting.webp'} alt="The big painting of a woman in the Apartments" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Prepare the Cabaret projector">
    At the back of the **Cabaret** stage, there is a **projector**. Above it, a **film reel**: shoot it to make it fall, and it lands in front of the projector. Pick it up, then put the **painting** and the **reel** in the projector.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={dd + 'projector.webp'} alt="The projector at the back of the Cabaret stage" credit={by} source={src} />

  </Step>

  <Step title="Power it with the Wunderbuss">
    Shoot the projector with the Wunderbuss ("Powered the Projector"). A **map of the city** appears on the stage screen, with **one area in green**. This area contains a **clown doll**.

    <Screenshot src={dd + 'clown_map.webp'} alt="The city map on the stage screen, with one area in green" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Count the kills for each clown">
    Go to the green area and kill zombies **near the doll**. Count how many kills it takes for the sparks to stop: from **1 to 9**. A new area then lights up. Do **four dolls**: you get **four digits**. If you miss a digit, the sequence restarts at the first clown after the fourth, so you can recount.

  <Step title="Open the safe">
    Go to the **Apartments safe** and enter the four digits. mmmrkennedy's method: reset the dial with **three full turns to the right** (a turn counts when you pass your first digit). Stop on the **first digit** (going right), turn **left** to the second, **right** to the third, **left** to the fourth. Hold the action button to step away: the safe opens if everything is right. You don't need to land exactly on each digit, as long as you don't move on to the next.

    {/* TO CHECK: safe procedure: mmmrkennedy (3 reset turns), Accelerated Ideas (4 reset turns, some say 1 is enough), Gameranx (1 full reset turn then right/left/right/left). */}

    Depending on the guide, the number of reset turns varies (one to four). If it does not open, start again with more turns.

## The Nazi Axe

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Find two engraved numbers">
    **Radio frequencies** are engraved in just one of these six places (random):

    - **Apartments**: on the lift wall;
    - **Apartments**: on the ceiling of the room next to the Main Street door;
    - **Cabaret**: on a piece of furniture backstage, to the right of the mirrors;
    - **Cabaret**: to the left of the stage screen, on a crate behind a purple dress;
    - **Museum**: on the granite under the M1 Garand weapon wall;
    - **Museum**: at the base of a pillar, at the top of the stairs.

  <Step title="Listen to the Morse on the radio">
    Enter these numbers on the **Main Street** radio. It plays a message in **Morse** that contains **two numbers**. A short beep is a **dot**, a long beep is a **dash**, and a pause separates the digits. The digits are written like this: 1 = · – – – –, 2 = · · – – –, 3 = · · · – –, 4 = · · · · –, 5 = · · · · ·, 6 = – · · · ·, 7 = – – · · ·, 8 = – – – · ·, 9 = – – – – ·, 0 = – – – – –.

    <Screenshot src={ax + 'morse_code.webp'} alt="The Morse alphabet chart" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the magnifying glass">
    In the **Cabaret**, upstairs, pick up the small **magnifying glass** next to the hat, on the sofa to the left of the M1927 weapon wall.

    <Screenshot src={ax + 'mag_glass.webp'} alt="The magnifying glass on the Cabaret sofa" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place the magnifying glass on the church map">
    In the **Church**, interact with the **map** on the wall: the magnifying glass appears and you can move it. Put it on the **coordinates** given by the Morse: the **first number** is the **column** (at the top), the **second** the **row** (on the left). Then leave the map. If it is right, the cabinet to the right of the map opens and contains a **bowl**. The ten possible coordinates are: 8-11, 10-5, 10-8, 11-8, 12-15, 12-19, 18-5, 18-8, 19-21 and 21-14.

    If nothing opens, try every spot on the list.

## The free melee weapons

Four melee weapons are lying around the map, for free. They are also used for **scavenging** (Jolts, ammo or explosives) and for the **Ballistics Be Damned** and **Lumberjack** achievements: see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).

- **Shovel**: in the Underbelly. Hold aim then attack for a thrust that scavenges.
- **Spiked bat (Baseball Bat)**: on Main Street. Hold aim for a long-range hit that sometimes gives Jolts. It is the safest weapon early on.
- **Trench knife**: near the Cabaret backstage door. A thrust that kills in one hit and scavenges.
- **Ice Pick**: in a Museum display case, on the first floor near the stairs. While sprinting, hold attack for a charged hit that kills normal zombies in one hit.

{/* TO CHECK: Gameranx (melee scavenging page, id 146507) calls the Museum weapon "Pickaxe"; mmmrkennedy calls it "Ice Pick". Instakill and bonus rewards: Gameranx and GamingBolt only. */}
