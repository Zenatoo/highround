The Shadowed Throne takes place in **burning Berlin**, in 1945, under attack by the Red Army. The heroes jump from Doctor **Straub**'s zeppelin into the city. The goal of the quest: open a hidden courtyard to take the **Blade** (the third piece of Barbarossa's sword), board the zeppelin, watch Straub die and beat his last creature, the **Stadtjäger** ("city hunter").

**Before you start:**

- Difficulty **8/10** according to mmmrkennedy, **1 h to 1 h 15** in solo.
- The reward is the **Stadtjäger Down** achievement (silver) and the ending cutscene. See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
- There is **no easy and hard version**: a single quest.
- Doable in solo or in co-op. The radio, safe and statue steps are hard with a zombie on your back.
- You need the **Wunderbuss**. Entering the zeppelin is a **point of no return**: prepare well.
- The general rules (Jolts, mods, Blitz) are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).

The quest has two parts that you can do **at the same time**: getting the Blade, and lowering the zeppelin's pod.

## Part 1: get the Blade

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Collect the three special melee weapons">
    Smuggler's Bat, Dancer's Dagger and Nazi Axe, in any order. See [The three special melee weapons](../armes-de-melee/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place them in the stone slots">
    Near the **Church**, to the right of the **big red door**, three stone hollows in a frame wait for the weapons. Place all three (in any order).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'stone_slots.webp'} alt="The three stone slots to the right of the big red door" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Charge them with kills">
    Get kills **near the weapons**. According to mmmrkennedy, you need, in this order: **20 normal zombies**, **10 Sizzlers**, then **10 Pests** (the small zombies). Other guides give the same logic (normal zombies for the dagger, Sizzlers for the axe, Pests for the bat) without giving numbers.

    {/* TO CHECK: 20/10/10 counts and the order: mmmrkennedy only. Accelerated Ideas, Gameranx and Newsweek give the kill types without counts. */}

    When the three weapons are charged, the red door opens onto **Barbarossa's Refuge** (the Hidden Courtyard).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Solve the refuge and take the Blade">
    Everything happens in the courtyard: anchor, statues, ravens, Blade. See [Barbarossa's Refuge and the Blade](../refuge-de-barberousse/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Part 2: lower the pod

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Charge the zeppelin's three anchors">
    Shoot each **anchor** on the ceiling with the Wunderbuss until it turns yellow: about **150 ammo** each. They are:

    - in the **Destroyed Building**, on the ceiling, opposite the Pack-a-Punch;
    - in the **Museum**, on the ceiling;
    - in the **Plaza**, near Kugelblitz.

    **Careful with the Plaza:** this anchor charges either by killing zombies near it, or by shooting it with the Wunderbuss, **but not both**. The first method you use is the one you must finish.

    {/* TO CHECK: 150 ammo (mmmrkennedy) vs about 100 (Gameranx). Number of anchors: mmmrkennedy, Accelerated Ideas = 3 on the map + 1 in the courtyard; Newsweek counts 4; Gameranx says "four" but lists three. */}

    Depending on the guide, there are 3 anchors on the map, plus one in the hidden courtyard (4 in total).

## Get ready: point of no return

Once in the zeppelin, you stay there **until the end of the boss**. Before that:

- Every player must have a **full Wunderbuss**.
- Buy your Blitz and armour, upgrade your weapons and take **Jack-in-the-Boxes** (see [Hangman, hats and other secrets](../secrets/)).
- The fight is long and ammo melts away. mmmrkennedy advises putting an **MP40** (Main Street) or an **FG42** (Church) through the Pack-a-Punch: both have a weapon wall in the arena. Other guides advise a light machine gun.
- The **Dancer's Dagger** is more useful than the Blade for the fight: its heavy attack gives back armour.

## Enter the zeppelin

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get in the pod">
    All players enter the pod on **Main Street**. **All at the same time**, shoot the **cable** at the very top with the Wunderbuss. The pod goes up: "Boarded the Zeppelin".

    <Screenshot src={m + 'cable_drop_pod.webp'} alt="The cable at the top of the drop pod" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Move the energy ball">
    The fight does not start right away. A blue **energy ball** must travel through the zeppelin to open **five doors** (four around Straub's room), then reach Straub's room. Use the **terminals**: each one shows dots linked by wires, and the ball only follows the **green wires**. If the link is missing, try a nearby terminal.

    When the ball goes up the stairs, there are two paths. Take the **upper** path first and open its doors, then come back for the **lower** one, which is the right one: you avoid backtracking. Keep the doors open: they help you kite zombies during the boss.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'terminal.webp'} alt="A terminal to move the energy ball" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'power_ball.webp'} alt="The energy ball in the zeppelin" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Watch Straub die">
    When the ball reaches Straub's room, the round stops and **Straub is killed by his own zombies**. Leave the room: the Stadtjäger drops down and the fight begins.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The fight against the Stadtjäger

The Stadtjäger has **three phases**. Stay away from its blows and its charge. The **Wunderbuss** remains useful. Don't forget to use the arena's weapon walls for ammo.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Phase 1: the orange engine">
    Shoot it to make it angry. When the **engine on its chest** turns orange, it charges you: this is when it takes damage. According to mmmrkennedy, there is no hit indicator: keep shooting the engine. The phase ends when it **injects itself with a Geistbolt**.

    {/* TO CHECK: hit markers on the boss: mmmrkennedy says none in phase 1; Gameranx and Accelerated Ideas say they appear. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Phase 2: the Sizzlers">
    It throws Geistbolts that create **Sizzlers**. You can only hurt it **while it does this**, not while it charges. The phase ends with another injection.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Phase 3: the gas cloud">
    It spits a **gas cloud**. Shoot into the gas (any weapon, the Wunderbuss is recommended) while it stands inside: the gas **becomes electrified** and hurts it. When you have done enough damage, it dies.

    {/* TO CHECK: last phase. mmmrkennedy and Gameranx: gas cloud to electrify. Accelerated Ideas and Newsweek describe electric discs and say only the Wunderbuss works. */}

    Other guides talk about electric discs in the last phase instead: if you see discs, use the Wunderbuss.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go back into the pod">
    Enter the pod you came up in: it closes and takes you back to the street. A few seconds later, the **ending cutscene** plays.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## What can go wrong

- Wrong method on the **Plaza** anchor: you can no longer finish it the other way.
- Wrong radio frequencies: use the table in [Build the Wunderbuss](../wunderbuss/).
- Failed safe: the clowns start over after the fourth, so you can recount.
- You enter the zeppelin unprepared: there is no way back.
- You finish the smuggler step too early: be sure to wait the two rounds.

## The end of the story

Straub is dead and so is his last creature. The heroes are evacuated by Major Rideau, and Barbarossa's sword now has its **hilt, pommel and blade**. The adventure continues with [How The Tortured Path works](../../the-tortured-path/chapitres/).
