Five playable characters unlock through **challenges**. You track your progress in the lobby menu: **Soldier, Dossier, Challenges**.

**To check:** this whole list comes from **a single guide** (mmmrkennedy), who says he read the conditions in the game files. He warns that the challenges may differ from those found elsewhere on the Internet. Don't take them for granted before you have seen them in your menu.

## Necromedic (Fieldwork)

<Screenshot src={m + 'necromedic.webp'} alt="The Necromedic character" credit={by} source={src} />

<Checklist>
  <Step title="The three Zombie Hunter pieces (A, B and C)">
    Each piece is won from a **Treasure Zombie**. It is a **non-hostile Bomber** that carries red bombs and drops Jolts. It can only spawn once **every door on the map is open**, then every **1 h 15**. When you see Jolts falling everywhere, it is there. Each piece has about a **1 in 12 chance** per Treasure Zombie killed, and duplicates are not avoided: you can get a piece you already have.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'treasure_zombie.webp'} alt="A Treasure Zombie" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hands on experience">
    Survive **25 rounds with melee only**, the whole team together in the same area: the Underbelly, the Cabaret, the Church or the Museum. Use the main quest sword or the axe. Progress is lost if a player leaves the room.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Play the field">
    Get **75 kills with each of the three traps** (Propeller, Blitz, Electric) without anyone going down. Kills outside traps don't count, and you don't need all 75 in a row. All three counters reset if a player goes down.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Stalker (Vigilant)

<Screenshot src={m + 'stalker.webp'} alt="The Stalker character" credit={by} source={src} />

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Heavy Machinery">
    Finish the main quest without any player going down. See [Main Easter Egg: Stadtjäger Down](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="I'll be Fine">
    Finish round 30 without taking any damage.
  </Step>

  <Step title="In the shadows">
    Finish round 20 without opening any door.
  </Step>

  <Step title="No shiny tools">
    Finish round 30 without anyone using a special ability, a consumable or a Blitz machine.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Snuff the flame">
    Get **10 headshots in a row on Sizzlers** with a sniper rifle. Use the **Kar98k** from the starting weapon wall. The kills don't have to be consecutive in time. Progress is shared: if a player kills a Sizzler without a headshot, everyone starts over.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Skullbreaker (The Answer)

<Screenshot src={m + 'skullbreaker.webp'} alt="The Skullbreaker character" credit={by} source={src} />

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Go Go Go!">
    Finish round 20 in **under 25 minutes**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hard shutdown">
    Finish the main quest in **under 35 minutes**, final cutscene of about one minute included.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Some ground rules first">
    Finish the main quest without any player using a Blitz machine. You **can** use and buy armour.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Show time">
    All players stay on the **Cabaret stage for 15 rounds in a row**. Take the main quest sword and alternate between the sword and the trap. Progress is lost if a player leaves.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Target Practice">
    Finish round 3 in **under 8 minutes** with **headshots only**. In **solo** only. Body shots that don't kill are allowed, as long as every kill is a headshot.
  </Step>
</Checklist>
