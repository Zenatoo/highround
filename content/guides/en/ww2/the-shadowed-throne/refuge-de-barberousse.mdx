**Barbarossa's Refuge** (the Hidden Courtyard) is the hidden courtyard behind the Church's big red door. It opens when the three special melee weapons are charged: see [The three special melee weapons](../armes-de-melee/) and [Main Easter Egg: Stadtjäger Down](../easter-egg-principal/). You take the **Blade** there, the last piece of Barbarossa's sword.

The **Ammo Conservation** achievement asks you to get the Blade **without firing a single shot**. The **This Belongs in a Museum** achievement asks for 250 kills with the Blade in the Museum. See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).

## Enter the courtyard

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Open the red door">
    Charge the three weapons in their stone slots (20 zombies, 10 Sizzlers, 10 Pests according to mmmrkennedy). The door opens.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Charge the ceiling anchor">
    Shoot the **anchor** on the courtyard ceiling with the Wunderbuss until it is charged (**150 ammo**, it turns yellow). The doors **lock**: every player in the courtyard stays locked in. You only get out after the puzzle.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'anchor_refuge.webp'} alt="The anchor on the hidden courtyard ceiling" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The statues

Four statue walls surround the room. The walls are counted **counter-clockwise**, starting with the one that has **three statues**, to the left of the stairs.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Face every statue forward">
    On each wall, all the statues must look **forward** (towards the big Barbarossa statue in the centre). When you shoot a statue with the Wunderbuss, it turns, **and so do its neighbours**. Another guide talks about interacting with the statue rather than shooting.

    {/* TO CHECK: rotating statues: mmmrkennedy = shoot them; Gameranx = "interact". */}

    <Screenshot src={m + 'first_wall.webp'} alt="One of the four statue walls" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the ravens">
    When a wall is done, a **raven statue** appears in front of the second or third statue: pick it up. You need **four ravens**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'raven_statue.webp'} alt="A raven statue that appeared in front of a finished wall" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

A **solver** exists: [mmmrkennedy's statue solver](https://mmmrkennedyzombiesguides.pages.dev/games/WW2/the_shadowed_throne/the_shadowed_throne_guide) (on his page, under "Statue Puzzle Solver"). You pick the wall, set each statue to the same direction as in game, and it tells you which shots to take.

## The ravens and the Blade

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Place the four ravens">
    Put them on the **Barbarossa statue** in the middle of the room. The position of each raven matters and is not obvious: watch DIG_DUG7's video (raven standing, neck at 90 degrees, leaning forward, screaming), around 14:40 to 15:29. mmmrkennedy's guide also points to a video for this step.

    {/* TO CHECK: raven placement is only shown in videos (DIG_DUG7 AalLzt49WOA 14:40-15:29, a video linked by mmmrkennedy); Accelerated Ideas says the logic is unclear. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the Blade">
    If everything is right, the statue's fake blade shatters and reveals the **real Blade**. Take it.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'blade.webp'} alt="The real Blade, revealed on the statue" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the exit">
    Put the Blade in the **stone slot** at the top of the stairs: the door opens and you can leave.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'blade_slot.webp'} alt="The stone slot at the top of the stairs, for the Blade" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

The Blade is a melee weapon. Its special attack sends out an arc of energy that kills or knocks down enemies.
