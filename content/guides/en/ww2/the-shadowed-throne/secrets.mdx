These secrets are not needed for the main Easter Egg, but they give **free power-ups**, **weapons**, **Jolts** and even a camo.

## The hangman game

On **Main Street**, the **Cabaret** sign hides a hangman game.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Find the controls">
    At the far **left of the sign**, a letter is displayed. On the right, a metal piece with **two arrows** and **three dots**. Shoot an arrow to change the letter, and the **middle dot** to confirm it.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={hm + 'the_cabaret_sign.webp'} alt="The Cabaret sign on Main Street" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={hm + 'letter.webp'} alt="The letter at the far left of the sign" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={hm + 'metal_selector.webp'} alt="The two metal arrows and the three dots" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Guess a word">
    Each wrong letter adds a **limb to the hanged man**, to the left of the letter. When the body is complete, everything resets. There are **seven words** (in capitals): one of them is picked at random.

    <Screenshot src={hm + 'noose.webp'} alt="The hanged man that fills in with each mistake" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

| Word | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| **REAPER** | Insta-Kill (power-up) |
| **WONDER** | Tesla Gun and Ripsaw |
| **FAMISHED** | Max Ammo (power-up) |
| **DAMNATION** | Nuke (power-up) |
| **GLUTTONY** | Double Jolts (power-up) |
| **GEISTKRAFT** | Full Charge, which fills your special gauge (power-up) |
| **THEJACKBOX** | Jack-in-the-Box |

{/* TO CHECK: Accelerated Ideas mentions "free perks" from Hangman, but its own word list and Gameranx / mmmrkennedy give no perk reward. */}

Jack-in-the-Boxes do not come out of the Mystery Box: this is where you win them (see [The Mystery Box and weapons](../boite-mystere/)). They are very useful before the boss: see [Main Easter Egg: Stadtjäger Down](../easter-egg-principal/).

## The five hats

Five hats are hidden on the map. You can only carry **one at a time**. Put them on the **cheetah statue** on the **Cabaret** stage, **in this order**. Each hat has two possible places.

    - **Cabaret**: near the feet of the left golden statue.

## "The Classic" (PPSh-41)

At the far right of the **Cabaret** backstage, three **mirrors**.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="First mirror">
    Throw Jolts in front of the **left mirror**. Lights flash, then **a single light** circles the mirror. Press the action button when it comes back to its starting point (**bottom middle**). If you miss, throw Jolts again to retry right away.

    <Screenshot src={sc + 'mirrors.webp'} alt="The three mirrors at the back of the Cabaret backstage" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Next mirrors">
    Do the same with the two mirrors on the right. Each one is a little **faster** than the previous one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The reward">
    The mirrors spit out banknotes and **The Classic** enters the Mystery Box. You are not sure to draw it. See [The Mystery Box and weapons](../boite-mystere/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The "Thule's Errand" camo

## The record and the woman on the bed

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Make the record fall">
    In the **Apartments**, just after the entrance from Main Street, shoot the **painting**. It falls and leaves a **record**.

    <Screenshot src={sc + 'painting.webp'} alt="The painting to shoot down in the Apartments" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Play the record">
    Put the record on the **record player**, to the left of the Destroyed Building door. You need the Wunderbuss: shoot the record player until the music starts.
