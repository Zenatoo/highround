The Shadowed Throne has **10 trophies** (achievements on Xbox and PC). Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device. The names are the in-game English ones.

The types (bronze, silver) come from the PS4 list. Several are **secret**: they do not show in the game until you earn them.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Stadtjäger Down (silver)">
    Beat the Stadtjäger: this is the main Easter Egg. See [Main Easter Egg: Stadtjäger Down](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Das ist gut (bronze)">
    Find the Ubersprengen, that is, the Pack-a-Punch. See [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ballistics Be Damned (bronze)">
    Reach **round 15** using **only melee weapons**. The free melee weapons are in [The three special melee weapons](../armes-de-melee/).

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="Gameranx" source="https://gameranx.com/features/id/146507/">
        The spiked bat on Main Street is the safest melee weapon early on.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Something Wunderful (bronze)">
    Kill **50 Gekocht** (the Sizzlers) with the special weapon, the Wunderbuss, in a single game. See [Build the Wunderbuss](../wunderbuss/). The zeppelin creates a Sizzler every few minutes: a wounded zombie cannot become a Sizzler, so avoid wounding every zombie in advance.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Putting on a Show (bronze)">
    Keep a **Gekocht alive on the stage for 1 minute**. The stage is the Cabaret one.

    {/* TO CHECK: "the stage" is assumed to be the Cabaret stage (Gameranx and mmmrkennedy mention only that stage). No source explains how to keep the Gekocht alive. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ammo Conservation (silver, secret)">
    Get the **Blade without firing a single shot**. See [Barbarossa's Refuge and the Blade](../refuge-de-barberousse/).

    **To check:** the quest asks you to shoot with the Wunderbuss to charge the anchor and turn the statues. No source says whether this counts as a gunshot.

    {/* TO CHECK: does Wunderbuss fire count as a gunshot for Ammo Conservation? No source says. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Not a Prayer (bronze)">
    After round 20, survive **2 full rounds** without anyone leaving the church (the Church).
  </Step>

  <Step title="This Belongs in a Museum (silver, secret)">
    Kill **250 zombies with the Blade** while you are in the Museum, in a single game.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Equal Opportunity Destroyer (bronze)">
    Collect **Geistkraft** from **every type of zombie** in a single game. The general rules are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).

    {/* TO CHECK: the list of zombie types for this achievement: not in any source. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Lumberjack (bronze)">
    Kill a **Wüstling with every melee weapon** in a single game.

    {/* TO CHECK: whether "each melee weapon" means the free weapons only or also the three special ones and the Blade: no source says. */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>
