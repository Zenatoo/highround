The Shadowed Throne has **6 Blitz machines** (the perks of WWII Zombies) and **Armour stands** for armour. There is no power to turn on: each machine works as soon as you reach its area. You can only keep **4 Blitz** at a time: choose your favourites. The general rules (prices, armour, Jolts) are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).

## The six machines

| Machine | Effect | Where | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Lebenblitz** | Faster revive in co-op, self-revive in solo | Underbelly, right next to the start | 500 Jolts in solo, 1,500 in co-op |
| **Laufenblitz** | Stamina: you run for longer | Main Street, in the middle of the bombed-out street | 2,000 Jolts |
| **Faustblitz** | Melee damage | Museum, on the upper balcony, near the Plaza door | 2,000 Jolts |
| **Kugelblitz** | Weapon damage | Plaza, in the street, opposite the church's small balcony | 2,000 Jolts |
| **Schnellblitz** | Faster reload | Church, on the small balcony, through the central door | 3,000 Jolts (2,000 according to one source) |
| **Schildblitz** | Electric shock when you reload | Cabaret, behind the big stage | 2,000 Jolts |

{/* TO CHECK: Schnellblitz price: Activision Support page says 3,000; Gameranx (April 2018) lists 2,000 for the Church reload perk. Lebenblitz 500 solo / 1,500 co-op: Activision Support + Gameranx. Effects of Faustblitz / Schildblitz are from Gameranx and GamingBolt descriptions; Gameranx calls Schildblitz "electric attack on reload". */}

The machines that play the same role as in older Call of Duty games: Lebenblitz = Quick Revive, Laufenblitz = Stamin-Up, Kugelblitz = Double Tap, Schnellblitz = Speed Cola, Schildblitz = Electric Cherry.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="Gameranx" source="https://gameranx.com/features/id/146521/">
    Take the damage machine (Kugelblitz) first. Reload and stamina matter mostly for the Easter Egg in co-op.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Armour

The **Armour stands** sell armour (the Geistschild). You find them in the **Destroyed Building** and the **Museum**. They are also used in one step of the axe quest: see [The three special melee weapons](../armes-de-melee/).

The starting price is 500 Jolts and goes up with each purchase; armour is limited to 3 points. The details are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="mmmrkennedy" source="https://mmmrkennedyzombiesguides.pages.dev/games/WW2/the_shadowed_throne/the_shadowed_throne_guide">
    The Dancer's Dagger gives back a full armour piece with its heavy attack when your health is full. Handy against the boss.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
