The Shadowed Throne's Mystery Box costs **1,000 Jolts** per try and changes location during the game. The general rules are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/). Here you find where it appears and where to buy wall weapons.

## Where the box appears

- **First position: always the Church.** This is the small church area, behind the Cabaret, the Museum and the Plaza: you have to open quite a few doors to reach it.
- Other positions: the **Museum** (near the three skulls in a display case), the **Cabaret** (under the big stage) and the **Destroyed Building** (opposite the revolver weapon wall).

Special weapons (Wunderbuss, special melee weapons) do not come out of the box: see [Build the Wunderbuss](../wunderbuss/) and [The three special melee weapons](../armes-de-melee/). Neither do the Jack-in-the-Boxes: they come from the hangman game, see [Hangman, hats and other secrets](../secrets/).

## The hidden STG44

A hidden weapon wall sells the **STG44 for 1,500 Jolts**. It is in the **Cabaret**: slip under a low awning of ruins near the stage and look up.

## "The Classic", a special PPSh-41

A mirror quest in the Cabaret adds **The Classic** to the box: a PPSh-41 with a **70-round** magazine, more reserve ammo and a faster rate of fire. You are not sure to draw it. The steps are in [Hangman, hats and other secrets](../secrets/).
