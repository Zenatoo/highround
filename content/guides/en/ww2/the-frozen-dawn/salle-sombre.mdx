The **Dark Room Perkaholic** is a secret quest: a **dark room** gives you **all six perks** at once. A 2018 guide also calls it the **Trial of the Moonraven**. It requires the **Broken Flail**: see [The Flail of Thule](../fleau/).

- You need at least **10,000 Jolts**.
- It also earns the "Light in darkness" challenge of a secret character: see [Unlock the secret characters](../personnages-secrets/).

## The steps

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get the Broken Flail">
    See [The Flail of Thule](../fleau/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Charge the three pressure plates">
    Get kills near each plate until it glows **red**. You don't need to stand on it.

    - **Crash Site**: next to the Thule entrance (the opening plate).
    - **Blood Altar**: near the Kugelblitz machine.
    - **Overlook**: "behind" the shield's blood pool. You have to **teleport with the Flail**.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'perkaholic/glowing_plate.webp'} alt="A pressure plate glowing red" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'perkaholic/plate_double_tap.webp'} alt="The Blood Altar plate near Kugelblitz" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'perkaholic/plate_flail.webp'} alt="The Overlook plate, behind the pool" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the new Transport">
    A new **Thulian Transport** opens in the corridor between the **Overlook** and the **Phylactery**. With at least **10,000 Jolts**, use it. It takes about **6,916 Jolts** from you.

    {/* TO CHECK: cost and trigger conflict. mmmrkennedy (probably final state): need 10,000, the ride takes about 6,916, and it is deterministic. Gameranx 160982 (4 Sep 2018, launch week): ride costs 250 and only randomly sends you to the room ("20+ attempts"). Check in game. */}

    **To check:** according to a launch-week guide (Gameranx), the Transport costs **250 Jolts** and only sends you to the room at random, sometimes after more than 20 tries.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'perkaholic/new_transport.webp'} alt="The new Transport in the Overlook / Phylactery corridor" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the four nails">
    In the **dark room**, find **four metal nails**. Three are on small **stone pedestals**, one is in a **skeleton's skull** near a pedestal. There are 6 to 8 pedestals, only three have a nail.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'perkaholic/nail.webp'} alt="A metal nail in the dark room" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place them on the tall “Blood is Power” pedestal">
    Find the **taller pedestal**: near it, the **Blood is Power** message is displayed. Place the four nails on it and use it. You receive **all six perks** and you are sent out of the room. The new Transport then works like the others.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'perkaholic/taller_pillar.webp'} alt="The taller pedestal, “Blood is Power”" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>
