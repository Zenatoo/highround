## The Classic or The Vintage: the shooting mini-game

This mini-game gives you a **special weapon**, and **only one of the two per game**.

- **The Classic**: PPSh-41, **70-round** magazine, more ammo and faster fire rate.
- **The Vintage**: MG42, **125-round** magazine, more ammo and faster fire rate.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Choose your weapon">
    At the **bottom of the Morgue** (where you take the shield stone), **drop Jolts** in front of an **open corpse container**.

    - **Right**: The Classic. The corpse's eyes turn blue.
    - **Left**: The Vintage. The corpse's eyes turn red.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'vintage_and_classic/corpse_classic.webp'} alt="The right container, for The Classic" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'vintage_and_classic/corpse_vintage.webp'} alt="The left container, for The Vintage" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the right heads">
    After a few seconds, **skulls** light up: they count the rounds left. A head with **eyes of the right colour** appears: shoot it before the lights go out. Repeat for every head of the right colour.

    - **The Classic**: shoot the **red** eyes, never the blue ones.
    - **The Vintage**: shoot the **blue** eyes, never the red ones.

    If you miss or shoot the wrong colour, you fail: drop Jolts again to start over.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'vintage_and_classic/red_eyed.webp'} alt="A head with red eyes" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'vintage_and_classic/blue_eyed.webp'} alt="A head with blue eyes" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Three rounds, then take the weapon">
    Drop Jolts to move on to the next round. After **three rounds**, use the starting container to take the weapon. It then joins the box: see [The Mystery Box and weapons](../boite-mystere/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Zombie Possession

Once per game, you earn **10,000 Jolts**, a **perk** and a **character challenge**.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get the upgraded Flail ready">
    You need the **Talon of Lu'roth**: see [The Flail of Thule](../fleau/). Deactivate the Passage's Pack-a-Punch stone so the Transports don't send you to the Pack-a-Punch.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Possess a zombie in a Transport">
    Get a **zombie or a Pest** into a Thulian Transport with you. While the doors close, make it a **friend** with the Flail.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Watch, then collect your reward">
    When the Transport leaves, you **watch** the zombie. A **Corpse Eater** appears and kills it. You return to your body: 10,000 Jolts, a perk, and the challenge.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Free Double Jolts and Funderbuss

A long **colour** quest. It gives a free **Double Jolts** then the **Funderbuss** (a Pack-a-Punched Blunderbuss).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Prepare your equipment">
    Keep a **frag grenade, a Semtex, a C4 or a throwing knife** in your class, and take the **scythe**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the four candles">
    On the altar at the top of the **Phylactery** stairs, shoot the four lit **candles**. They don't go out, but a raven caws.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'free_double_jolts/lit_candles.webp'} alt="The four candles of the Phylactery altar" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Note the Double Jolts colour">
    A **Double Jolts** appears in the air around the ancient forge and moves. Note its colour: **green, cyan or magenta**. Green and cyan look alike: if you hesitate, try green, then cyan.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'free_double_jolts/power_up_phylactery.webp'} alt="The Double Jolts floating near the forge" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'free_double_jolts/cyan_power_up.webp'} alt="A cyan Double Jolts" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Colour and mix a piece of equipment">
    Combinations: **yellow + blue = green**, **green + blue = cyan**, **red + blue = magenta**.

    Near the candles, throw a piece of equipment to the left or right of the bone structure. It **orbits** and changes colour (white, yellow, red, blue). Pick it up at the right colour: it is fixed. Throw it again, throw a second piece in the same direction, pick up the first one, then the second at its right colour. Then throw them in opposite directions so that they **touch in mid-air**: one takes the new colour, the other explodes. Equipment in the air explodes after 20 to 30 seconds.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'free_double_jolts/throwing_the_equipment.webp'} alt="Throwing the equipment next to the bone structure" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Throw the equipment at the Double Jolts">
    Jump **off the candle platform** (on it, the equipment orbits instead of flying straight), then throw the coloured equipment at the Double Jolts: it disappears. A coloured piece of equipment flies **straight**, with no drop.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Do it again at the Crash Site, then behind Schildblitz">
    At the **Crash Site**, a new Double Jolts floats around the zeppelin: note its colour, make the equipment of that colour in the Phylactery and come back to throw it. The last one is **behind Schildblitz**, in the Phylactery. You then receive a **normal Double Jolts** (time-limited).

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'free_double_jolts/power_up_crash_site.webp'} alt="The Double Jolts around the Crash Site zeppelin" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'free_double_jolts/power_up_electric.webp'} alt="The Double Jolts behind Schildblitz" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="The Overlook throwing game">
    At the **Overlook**, use the **red mist** in front of the armour stand. Four glowing dots form a **target** toward the pyramid: throw a piece of equipment into it. You have an **infinite** piece of equipment that flies straight. A hit target turns green and disappears, another appears: hit **ten**. The targets move farther away. If you miss one, you have to wait **five rounds** to try again.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'free_double_jolts/red_mist.webp'} alt="The red mist in front of the armour stand" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'free_double_jolts/target.webp'} alt="The four dots that form the target" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the Funderbuss">
    After the ten targets, the **Funderbuss** appears at the edge of the Overlook.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'free_double_jolts/funderbuss.webp'} alt="The Funderbuss at the edge of the Overlook" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Two small games

- **Rocket deflect**: take a **Panzerschreck** or an **M1 bazooka** as a consumable, or a **Fliegerfaust** from the box. Fire a rocket at one of the **two front pillars of the big pyramid**, from the Overlook or the Ice Caves: it comes back toward you and can hurt you, like the zombies.

  <Screenshot src={m + 'rocket_deflect/pyramid.webp'} alt="The front pillars of the big pyramid" credit={by} source={src} />

- **Grenade Rain**: start the game with **frag grenades**. In the **Thulian Archives**, throw one into one of the glowing holes in the ceiling (above the pedestal or the Orrery). After a few seconds, grenades fall back down: they hurt the zombies and you.

  <Screenshot src={m + 'grenade_rain/orrery.webp'} alt="The glowing holes in the Orrery ceiling" credit={by} source={src} />
