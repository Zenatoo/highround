The **Thulian Scythe** is a special melee weapon: it hits **twice** in front of you. It is not in the Mystery Box. Once upgraded, it becomes the **Fang of An'heist**. It is one of the four weapons of the Easter Egg: see [Main Easter Egg: Kingfall](../easter-egg-principal/).

- You must have opened Thule: see [Open the way to Thule](../acces-a-thule/).
- The upgrade needs the **Phylactery**, the **Crash Site** and a **Corpse Eater**, not the Pack-a-Punch.
- Since patch 1.19 (31 July 2018), special weapon upgrades are no longer limited by the round number. Old videos still mention a wait.
- The **Deathraven's Chosen** achievement is given by the final trial: see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).

## Get the scythe

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Activate the Phylactery shrine">
    In the **Phylactery** (near the Morgue door), crouch and use the **shrine** stone. A **zone lockdown** starts for **25 to 35 seconds**: kill the zombies that come.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'shrine.webp'} alt="The Phylactery shrine" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the scythe">
    When the lockdown ends, the **Thulian Scythe** appears in front of the shrine. Take it.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'scythe.webp'} alt="The Thulian Scythe in front of the shrine" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Upgrade the scythe: Fang of An'heist

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get a Corpse Eater's spine">
    Kill a **Corpse Eater** (a big white special zombie) with the scythe, then hold the action button to take its **spine**. Two Corpse Eaters appear each time you come back from the Pack-a-Punch: see [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the wire from the wreck">
    In the **Crash Site**, pick up the **wire** on the broken pod of the wreck. It is very discreet: Gameranx places it on the wall opposite the double-barrel shotgun, holding the action button.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'wire.webp'} alt="The wire on the Crash Site wreck" credit={by} source={src} />

    {/* TO CHECK: wire location: mmmrkennedy "broken drop pod"; Gameranx 160396 "wall opposite the double-barrel wall buy". Hard to see. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place the three items on the altar">
    At the top of the **Phylactery** stairs, place the spine, the wire and the scythe on the **altar**. Get about **15 kills** near the scythe until the **Ignited the Forge** message: the energy is no longer absorbed.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'altar.webp'} alt="The altar at the top of the Phylactery stairs" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait two rounds">
    Wait **two full rounds**, then take the scythe back (it doesn't come back as a normal weapon).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take a Transport: the Deathraven trial">
    Use **any Thulian Transport**: you arrive in the **Deathraven trial**, a tutorial for the upgraded weapon (**Brutes** spawn endlessly, then two Thulians: kill everything). You then come back with the **Fang of An'heist**.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The final weapon

{/* TO CHECK: the effects of the Fang of An'heist rest on mmmrkennedy only (single source) now that the Steam guide is dropped. */}

- The **Fang of An'heist** sends a short bolt of lightning in front of you.
- It lets you **cheat death once**: first kill a **charged** Corpse Eater (it glows red) in melee with the scythe. If a hit would be fatal, you survive and a shockwave **stuns** the enemies around you.
