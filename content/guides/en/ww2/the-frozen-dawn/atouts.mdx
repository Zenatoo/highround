In WWII Zombies, perks are **Blitz machines**. How they work, their effects and the four-machine maximum rule are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/). Here: where the ones on The Frozen Dawn are.

## Where the machines are

Four machines are placed in the sources, with these in-game names.

- **Schnellblitz**: in the **Thulian Archives**.
- **Kugelblitz**: in the **Blood Altar**.
- **Laufenblitz**: on the **Overlook**.
- **Schildblitz**: in the big corridor between the **Phylactery** and the **Overlook**, Phylactery side.

mmmrkennedy's guides give their old Call of Duty names (Speed Cola, Double Tap, Stamin-Up, Electric Cherry): they are the same machines, as a reference.

## Armour

An **armour stand** is at the **Overlook** (in the centre) and at the **Morgue**. Armour (Geistschild) can be bought again each time, at a rising price: 3 points at most.

## Prices

The prices given by Activision's official page for the whole Zombies mode:

- Laufenblitz, Faustblitz, Schildblitz, Kugelblitz: **2,000 Jolts**.
- Schnellblitz: **3,000 Jolts**.
- Armour: **500 Jolts**, then more and more expensive.
