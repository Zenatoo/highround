The **Thulian Hammer** is a special melee weapon with two attacks: a hit on one target, or a big sweep (aim button) that hits groups. Upgraded, it becomes the **Fist of Tal'rek**. It is the longest quest of the four weapons. It counts for the Easter Egg: see [Main Easter Egg: Kingfall](../easter-egg-principal/).

- You must have opened Thule: see [Open the way to Thule](../acces-a-thule/).
- The **Stormraven's Chosen** achievement is given by the final trial: see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
- Since patch 1.19, there is no round limit for upgrades.

## Get the hammer

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Bring the battery to the Overlook bowl">
    In the **Crash Site**, pick up the **battery** under the debris, to the left of the M30 Luftwaffe Drilling wall weapon. Bring it to the **bowl** of the **Overlook**, to the right of the Laufenblitz machine, on the blood bridge. Get **15 to 20 kills** near it: it turns purple and no longer absorbs anything.

      <Screenshot src={m + 'bowl.webp'} alt="The bowl to the right of Laufenblitz" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Collect the four stones">
    The four stones are always in the same places.

    - **Thulian Archives**: to the right of the Schnellblitz machine.
    - **Ice Caves**: on the right side of the cliff.
    - **Morgue**: on the pillar behind the armour stand.
    - **Blood Altar**: on the left side of the ledge, across from the Kugelblitz machine.

    <Shots>

      <Screenshot src={m + 'stone_blood.webp'} alt="The Blood Altar stone" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place the stones across from the bowl">
    Place each stone in the slots across from the battery bowl. Each stone stops a **blood fall** and reveals a **rune**. Look at them from the **armour stand**, from left to right. There are **four possible runes**, that look like F, R, S and M.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'stone_slots.webp'} alt="The stone slots across from the bowl" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'runes_bloodfalls.webp'} alt="The runes that appear in the blood falls" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get a kill on each rune circle, in order">
    Around the armour stand, there are four rune circles (one per letter). Get **one kill** on the circle that matches each rune, in order. Example: if the runes are F, F, S, R, get two kills on F, one on S, one on R.

    If you pick the wrong circle or get too many kills, wait a few seconds and start again **from the first rune**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'runes_to_charge.webp'} alt="The rune circles around the armour stand" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make the hammer appear, then solve the pillars">
    Lightning strikes in front of the bowl and the **hammer appears**. Run to it: it teleports. **Four pillars** rise around the Overlook. Each has **four blocks**, with a lightning bolt carved on one side. Shoot the blocks so that **all the bolts face the spot where the hammer appeared** (toward the bowl). The pillars light up when it is right.

    According to Gameranx, a block you hit turns **twice** and its neighbours **once**. mmmrkennedy offers an interactive solver on the page, and TheDrShow a video of solutions (links below). Leave **two slow zombies**, not just one, to have time.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pillars.webp'} alt="The four Overlook pillars" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the hammer, crawl to it, push it to the Overlook">
    The hammer is hidden in an underground area (Gameranx: the Archives, the Phylactery or the Ice Caves). **Go prone** and **crawl** toward it before using the action button. If you approach standing up, it teleports again. When it works, it lies down and slides toward the **Overlook**. If the pillars are set correctly, they stop it: pick it up.

    <Shots>

      <Screenshot src={m + 'hammer_pickup.webp'} alt="The hammer to pick up" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Upgrade the hammer: Fist of Tal'rek

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Carry the electricity from rune to rune">
    With the hammer, **melee** a rune that glows with purple electricity, **fast**, then run to the next one:

    1. **Crash Site**, on the wall to the right of the Transport.
    2. **Ice Caves**, on the wall.
    3. **Morgue**, above the shield's blood pool.
    4. **Phylactery**, above the Morgue door: you must **kill a zombie in the doorway** with the charged hammer.

    If you are too slow, the electricity goes back to the previous rune: start again right away. In solo, only about **one second** is left at the Phylactery: do this step at a high round, when the zombies run.

    </Shots>

    {/* TO CHECK: order Crash Site > Ice Caves > Morgue > Phylactery agrees between mmmrkennedy and Gameranx 160571. A "bug" in mmmrkennedy: putting the hammer away and out again may keep it electrified, skipping the first part of the next step. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Charge an enemy and hit the Blood Altar rune">
    When the Phylactery rune is charged, bring a **Corpse Eater** or a **Wüstling** under it: it gets electrified. Kill it with the hammer, then hit the rune under the Blood Altar's **"contraption"**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'rune_5.webp'} alt="The rune under the Blood Altar contraption" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Solve the three block boards">
    Shoot the contraption's blocks so that **all the lit sides face forward**. When all the blocks are right, they spin and one round is over. There are **three rounds**. A skull block resets the round.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'contraption_blocks.webp'} alt="The Blood Altar contraption blocks" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Follow the invisible path on the Overlook">
    On the **Overlook**, with the hammer in hand, walk on the **invisible path** in front of the armour stand. Keep walking until you are stopped and lifted. You are slowed, but the zombies are not: do it **between two rounds** or throw a **Jack-in-the-Box**.

    {/* TO CHECK: end of the invisible path: mmmrkennedy says toward the pyramid, Gameranx and GamesHedge say toward the volcano (shown by lightning). */}

    <Screenshot src={m + 'invis_path.webp'} alt="The invisible path in front of the armour stand" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Stormraven trial">
    You arrive in the **Stormraven trial**, a tutorial for the upgraded weapon. When it is over, you come back.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The final weapon

{/* TO CHECK: how the Fist of Tal'rek is thrown and what it does to zombies was removed with the Steam guide (single source). */}

- The side hit adds an **electric shockwave**.
- In the final fight, the thrown hammer breaks the zombies' purple shields.
