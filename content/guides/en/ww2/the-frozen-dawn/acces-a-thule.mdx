The Frozen Dawn is the **last WWII Zombies map** (DLC 4, Shadow War). Your plane crashes in Antarctica: you go down into the **lost city of Thule**, a big frozen underground complex on several levels. The rules shared by all maps (Jolts, perks, Pack-a-Punch…) are in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).

**Before you start:**

- You start at the **Crash Site**, the wreck of the zeppelin. The starting wall weapons are there.
- To enter Thule, you must first **open a door with a stone plate**, then pay **750 Jolts**.
- The **The Final Descent** achievement comes with entering the city: see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
- There is no power switch in the sources we read.

## Enter Thule

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Step on the Crash Site stone plate">
    In a dead-end corner, to the **left of where you spawn**, a round stone plate is carved with symbols. Stand on it: it sinks.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'thule/thule_plate.webp'} alt="The stone plate in the Crash Site corner" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill zombies near the plate">
    Killed zombies explode into red energy that feeds the plate. You need **5 or 6**. The door next to it opens.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'thule/open_door.webp'} alt="The open door next to the plate" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go down the ramp and pay 750 Jolts">
    At the end of the ramp, an ice door leads to the **main hall** of Thule. It costs **750 Jolts**.

    {/* TO CHECK: 750 from Gameranx 160251 and One Angry Gamer ("points"); mmmrkennedy gives no price. */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Two other plates of the same kind exist: one in the **Blood Altar** (upper level) and one hidden at the **Overlook**. They are used later (see [The dark room and every perk](../salle-sombre/)). They light up red when you get kills on them.

## The map's areas

The names are the ones used by the guides, in English.

- **Crash Site**: the start, with the wreck.
- **Ice Caves**: the big ice caves. The cliff in the centre is used for the Easter Egg.
- **Passage**: the tunnels, with the Pack-a-Punch stone.
- **Thulian Archives**: the book room, with the book pedestal. The next room, the **Orrery**, is a rotating ball machine.
- **Gearworks**: under the Archives, the gear mechanism.
- **Cypher Room**: hidden room next to the book pedestal.
- **Overlook**: the big balcony with the blood bridge and the pyramid in the distance.
- **Phylactery**: the scythe altar and the stairs.
- **Morgue**: the bodies, the jars and the shooting mini-game.
- **Blood Altar**: the Mystery Box, the big blood pool and a Blitz machine.

## The Thulian Transports

The big metal cylinders are **Thulian Transports**: a paid fast travel. They open up as you unlock the areas. There are some in the Passage, the Crash Site, the Ice Caves, the Overlook and the Blood Altar.

- The **Passage** Transport only works after you press the **grey stone** on the wall opposite. It is all in [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
- Routes mentioned: Passage to Blood Altar, Crash Site to Passage, Blood Altar to Crash Site.
- Prices: the first trip costs **250 Jolts**, then **750** (or **500**, depending on the source) and more.

{/* TO CHECK: Transport prices: One Angry Gamer says 250, 500, 750; Games-Guides and Gameranx 160252 say "250, 750, etc.". */}

Depending on the sources, the second trip costs 500 or 750 Jolts: keep some margin.
