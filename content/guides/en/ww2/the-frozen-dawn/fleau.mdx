The **Broken Flail** is a special melee weapon. When you hold it, it **replaces your grenades** with the **Orb of Lu'Roth**: you throw the orb, then press the button again to **teleport** to where it landed. The orb comes back after a few seconds. Upgraded, it becomes the **Talon of Lu'roth**. It counts for the Easter Egg: see [Main Easter Egg: Kingfall](../easter-egg-principal/).

- You must have opened Thule: see [Open the way to Thule](../acces-a-thule/).
- The **Moonraven's Chosen** achievement is given by the final trial: see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
- The Flail opens secret areas: the Overlook alcove (see [Open the way to Thule](../acces-a-thule/)) and [The dark room and every perk](../salle-sombre/).

    - **Blood Altar**: to the left of the blood pool, when you are near the Mystery Box.

    Place the three gears in the **Gearworks** mechanism, under the Archives. Get about **15 kills** near them: the **Orrery** starts to turn.

  <Step title="Stop the orbs at the right positions">
    In the **Orrery**, stop each orb (action button) at the position shown by the Cypher Room wall. There are only **eight possible positions** for an orb: press when the orb has **passed the previous position**. Start with the **red** orb, the fastest and the easiest to miss. A clear sound confirms a good position, a dull sound a bad one. A wrong orb forces you to **stop the others** to reset everything.

## Upgrade the Flail: Talon of Lu'roth

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Teleport to the room above the Orrery">
    Throw the orb into the **room above the Orrery** and teleport into it. Three **Moonraven stones** are in the corners.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'room_above_orrery.webp'} alt="The room above the Orrery" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'moonraven_stone.webp'} alt="A Moonraven stone" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Charge a stone: a rune appears">
    Get **3 kills** near the "right" stone (the closest to the Combat Shotgun wall weapon). A **rune** appears in the air above it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the constellation and connect the stars">
    A **constellation** appears somewhere on the map. It can only be seen by **aiming with the Flail** ("Flail Vision"). Connect the stars with your sight: the **Beheld the First Message** message is displayed. Go back to the room, charge the middle stone then the last one. Finished constellations stay visible.

    The possible locations:

    - **Crash Site**: above the Thule entrance.
    - **Thulian Archives**: above the Orrery's purple ball.
    - **Overlook**: behind the pool to fill.
    - **Phylactery**: in the window behind Schildblitz.
    - **Blood Altar**: in the air toward the pyramid (to the left when coming from the Phylactery).
    - **Blood Altar**: behind the Mystery Box.

  <Step title="Throw the orb into the Orrery ball">
    When the three constellations are finished, throw the orb into the Orrery's **glowing ball**. You arrive in the **Moonraven trial**, where you teleport over walls. When it is over, you come back.
