The Frozen Dawn has **10 achievements** (trophies on PlayStation), all in the Shadow War pack. They are **bronze** (7) or **silver** (3). Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device. The names are the game's English ones.

**To check:** the types and conditions come from the PlayStation 4 list. The conditions were also read in ActuGaming's French list. Xbox and Steam were not checked.

{/* TO CHECK: names/types from PlatPursuit (PS4), French names and rules from ActuGaming; Xbox/Steam not checked. Several conditions are not explained by any guide we read (see Go Long!, Blood Transfusion, Weapon Lord). */}

<Checklist>
  <Step title="The Final Descent (bronze)">
    Enter the ancient city of Thule. See [Open the way to Thule](../acces-a-thule/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Superior Thulian Technology (bronze)">
    Unlock the ancient Ubersprengen, the map's Pack-a-Punch. See [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Blood Transfusion (silver)">
    Take the Thulian Transport with **every type of zombie**. The Transports are explained in [Unlock the Pack-a-Punch](../pack-a-punch/).

    {/* TO CHECK: no guide explains how to bring each zombie type (Pest, Bomber, Wüstling, Corpse Eater...) into a Transport. Video: DominatorzCrew 2dzUq9cGIAA. */}

    **To check:** how to do it is not explained by the guides we read. DominatorzCrew's video (above) shows it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go Long! (bronze)">
    Cross **instantly** from the Ice Caves to the bridge. The method is not described in the guides we read: watch the videos by DominatorzCrew and AC OMEGA.

    {/* TO CHECK: no text source explains "Bridge" here (Blood Bridge on the Overlook? Flail teleport?). Videos: DominatorzCrew yZ6YkO5twyw, AC OMEGA 0qqZ76D68j0 (not oEmbed-checked, not used in frontmatter). */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Moonraven's Chosen (bronze)">
    Survive the trials of the Moonraven: the flail's Moonraven trial. See [The Flail of Thule](../fleau/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bloodraven's Chosen (bronze)">
    Survive the trials of the Bloodraven: the shield's Bloodraven trial. See [The Shield of Thule](../bouclier/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deathraven's Chosen (bronze)">
    Survive the trials of the Deathraven: the scythe's Deathraven trial. See [The Scythe of Thule](../faux/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Stormraven's Chosen (bronze)">
    Survive the trials of the Stormraven: the hammer's Stormraven trial. See [The Hammer of Thule](../marteau/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Weapon Lord (silver)">
    Kill **100 zombies with each Raven weapon** in the same game. The four weapons are [The Scythe of Thule](../faux/), [The Hammer of Thule](../marteau/), [The Shield of Thule](../bouclier/) and [The Flail of Thule](../fleau/).

    {/* TO CHECK: "each Raven weapon": the four upgraded weapons, or also the Raven's Claw pistol? No guide says. */}

    **To check:** the official text says "each Raven weapon": we assume the four weapons, without knowing whether the pistol counts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kingfall (silver)">
    Put an end to the ancient evil: beat the God King. See [Main Easter Egg: Kingfall](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>
