On The Frozen Dawn, the **Pack-a-Punch** is called the **Ubersprengen**. The machine is **sunk into the blood floor** of a room called **Lost In Blood**. You only get there by travelling with the **Thulian Transports**.

- The **Superior Thulian Technology** achievement is given when you unlock the Ubersprengen: see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
- Upgrading a weapon costs **5,000 Jolts**. {/* TO CHECK: 5,000 from Gameranx 160252 only; Activision support lists 5,000 for the game generally. */}
- Doable solo or in co-op.
- You must have opened Thule: see [Open the way to Thule](../acces-a-thule/).

## Unlock the machine

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Press the stone in the Passage">
    In the **Passage** (the tunnels), facing the Thulian Transport, press the **grey stone carved in red** in the wall. It sinks in and turns **red**. As long as it is red, your trips go through the Pack-a-Punch room.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pap_button.webp'} alt="The stone to press in the Passage wall" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take each of the three Transports once">
    Use **three different Transports**, one at a time. Each trip first sends you to the **Lost In Blood** room (four zombies spawn there), and the machine **rises a little** out of the blood each time.

    - **Passage**: in the tunnels. It sends you to the **Blood Altar**.
    - **Crash Site**: near the crashed plane. It sends you to the **Passage**.
    - **Blood Altar**: under the pressure plate near the Kugelblitz machine. It sends you to the **Crash Site**.

    If a Transport doesn't work, first open the area where you want to go.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'transport_passage.webp'} alt="The Passage Transport, in the tunnels" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'transport_spawn.webp'} alt="The Crash Site Transport, near the plane" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'transport_blood.webp'} alt="The Blood Altar Transport" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Use the machine">
    Once you have taken all three Transports, the machine is available on every trip **as long as the stone is red**. You can leave your weapon in the machine: wait for the timer to end safely, then pick up the weapon. You then continue to your destination.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Good to know

- If the Passage stone is **deactivated**, you travel normally and you don't go to the Pack-a-Punch.
- **Two Corpse Eaters** (big white zombies) appear when you come back from the machine. Another source mentions only one. Kill them carefully: they are also used for the weapon quests (see [The Shield of Thule](../bouclier/)).
- Since **4 September 2018**, each **light machine gun (LMG)** has its own Ubersprengen upgrade (official announcement).
- The trip costs Jolts: see the Transport prices in [Open the way to Thule](../acces-a-thule/).
