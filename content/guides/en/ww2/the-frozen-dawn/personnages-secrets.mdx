Four secret characters are unlocked with **challenges** tied to The Frozen Dawn. A fifth comes from level 1000. Challenge tracking is in the menu: **Lobby → Soldier → Dossier → Challenges**.

**To check:** all the challenges below come from a single guide, mmmrkennedy's, which says it read them in the game's code. The video titles by Invalid, MysteryHQ and Scorpiolo confirm that the characters exist. The exact numbers are not cross-checked.

## Shadowguard (Shadow Hunter)

<Screenshot src={m + 'shadow_guard.webp'} alt="The Shadowguard character" credit={by} source={src} />

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get the scythe without taking damage during the lockdown">
    See [The Scythe of Thule](../faux/). Tip from mmmrkennedy: **Shellshock** with the **Sustain Zone** mod stops the zombies from attacking you.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill 12 zombies while a Corpse Eater eats them, with the Fang of An'heist">
    See [The Scythe of Thule](../faux/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill the Corpse Eaters before they kill the Wüstlings, during the Fang trial">
    The Deathraven trial of [The Scythe of Thule](../faux/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish the main quest in solo before round 25">
    See [Main Easter Egg: Kingfall](../easter-egg-principal/). If another player joins the game, even if they leave again, the challenge is lost.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Survive the death wave (phase 2) and the fire (phase 4) with the Cheat Death">
    Before the fight, kill a **charged** Corpse Eater (it glows red) with the Fang: the gem glows. In phase 2, take the red wave: you survive and the gem goes out. Recharge it with another Corpse Eater, then let the fire hit you in phase 4. See [Main Easter Egg: Kingfall](../easter-egg-principal/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Wayfarer (Wayward Rider)

<Screenshot src={m + 'wayfarer.webp'} alt="The Wayfarer character" credit={by} source={src} />

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get the Raven's Claw">
    See [The Raven's Claw](../griffe-du-corbeau/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go from the Crash Site to the Blood Altar in 1 min 45">
    The timer starts as soon as you enter the Crash Site. You can't fail: if the time runs out, go back to the Crash Site and start again.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Observe the three Flail constellations in under 5 minutes">
    See [The Flail of Thule](../fleau/). The timer starts at the first constellation observed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Die to a Corpse Eater while possessing a zombie">
    This happens by itself during the **Zombie Possession** quest: see [The map's secrets](../secrets/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the four Raven trials in the same game">
    Only the **final trial** of each weapon counts. Another player can do the steps beforehand: you pick up the weapon and do the trial. This challenge is **individual** and **only one player per game** can have it.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Deathbringer (Desert Assassin)

<Screenshot src={m + 'death_bringer.webp'} alt="The Deathbringer character" credit={by} source={src} />

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Survive 15 rounds without opening a door">
    Tip: **Freefire** with the **MK.II** mod to get temporary Pack-a-Punched weapons.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill 5 zombies in the Blood Altar pool with the Roar of San'ket's charged attack">
    See [The Shield of Thule](../bouclier/). The charged attack = melee while running, shield in hand.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Carry a lone Wüstling in a Transport">
    No other zombies or players. You can use the Wüstling from the shield quest. This challenge is temperamental: it sometimes needs two Transports. Finish the round with a Wüstling alive (it doesn't count toward the round total), then transport it when no other zombie has spawned.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Absorb 10,000 damage with the shield without breaking it">
    Let a living zombie hit the shield. When it is about to break, let it recharge and start again. The game counts **uncapped** damage: a Wüstling is faster than a normal zombie.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete the Dark Room quest">
    See [The dark room and every perk](../salle-sombre/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Wicht Warden (Cold Blooded)

It requires the **main quest of every map**, including The Final Reich's **Hardcore** mode.

- The Final Reich, Hardcore.
- The Darkest Shore.
- The Shadowed Throne.
- The Tortured Path.
- The Frozen Dawn: [Main Easter Egg: Kingfall](../easter-egg-principal/).

## Rookbane

You get it by reaching **level 1000** in Zombies.
