**Kingfall** is the main quest of The Frozen Dawn, and the **end of the whole WWII Zombies story**. The four heroes go down into the city of Thule to put an end to the cult of the Ravens. You gather the **four weapons of the Raven Lords**, then face the **God King**.

**Before you start:**

- Allow **1 h 20 to 1 h 35** in solo. Difficulty 8/10 according to mmmrkennedy.
- The reward is the **Kingfall** achievement: see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/). You also get the ending cutscene, **free armour regeneration** for the rest of the game and the right to travel freely between the arena and the map.
- Doable solo or in co-op. The final fight has **one more phase in co-op**.
- The four weapons don't come out of the box: each quest ends with a Trial in another arena. Since patch 1.19 (31 July 2018), there is no round limit for upgrades. Old videos mention a wait.
- Solo speed records are around **19 to 21 minutes** (videos by RandeazyGaming and CienkaRentaFX).

  <Step title="Place the weapons on the Ice Caves cliff">
    On the cliff facing the underground volcano in the **Ice Caves**, place the scythe, the hammer and the flail in **the three pedestals, in any order**. Place the **shield** on the **stone circle** on the ground in front.

## The fight against the God King

The God King attacks with lightning and time bombs. The fight is made of **phases**. Each one ends the same way:

1. Shoot the God King until he moves to the **middle** of the arena. He pulls you in and a message says he is "consuming blood": he hurts you slowly.
2. Shoot the **three pressure plates** of the arena (one in the middle, under him; one on each side of the Transport) until they stop glowing.
3. He falls, stunned, and you can hurt him. A **max ammo** and a **full meter** appear.

<Screenshot src={m + 'god_king_plate.webp'} alt="The pressure plates of the God King's arena" credit={by} source={src} />

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Phase 1">
    Lightning and time bombs. End the phase with the three plates.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Phase 2">
    He flies high and charges an **attack that kills everyone**. Take cover behind the **ice pillars** or block with the **shield**. The scythe's **Cheat Death** works too.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Phase 3">
    Some zombies have a **purple shield** that makes them invincible. Break it by **throwing the hammer**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Phase 4">
    He flies to his throne and throws a **wall of fire**. The player with the **flail** must **teleport behind the fire** to him and damage him to stun him and stop the fire. A player hit by the fire goes down right away.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Phase 5 (co-op)">
    He uses all the previous attacks: death wave, shields, fire.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Final phase">
    He pulls you onto the throne, leaves you at **1 health** and puts you on your knees. A cutscene plays, then you come back to the arena: the God King is sitting on his throne. **Shoot him until he dies.** Otherwise he wipes out the whole team and the game is over.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Once the God King is dead, the ending cutscene plays. Your armour then regenerates non-stop until the end of the game.

### What changed with patch 1.22 (2 October 2018)

Patch 1.22 is the last big Zombies patch. If an older video describes a different fight, it is the video that is out of date.

- The God King is **more aggressive**, but he has **less health**.
- **One more phase** for co-op groups.
- You take **less damage when downed**. More zombies come to help the boss.
- The **visual and audio cues** are clearer and his lines are less frequent. The **weak point** is adjusted.
- The Raven weapons do **more damage** to the God King (the **Raven's Eye** for example). The **Roar of San'ket** hits harder and the **Protection of San'ket** protects from projectiles. See [The Shield of Thule](../bouclier/).
- A crash after a long session in the fight is fixed.

## Solo or co-op

- The hammer's electricity transfer is hard in solo: see [The Hammer of Thule](../marteau/).
- The "Bureau's Pride" character challenge counts **once per game**: see [Unlock the secret characters](../personnages-secrets/).
- No source gives a different step order in solo.

## What can go wrong

- A wrong rune or block: the step resets.
- The hammer teleports if you approach it standing up.
- You lose a trial: redo the step.
- You don't kill the God King after the cutscene: the whole team is wiped out.
