The **Raven's Claw** is an **optional special pistol**. It is not needed for the Easter Egg. It gives a secret character: see [Unlock the secret characters](../personnages-secrets/).

Pack-a-Punched, it becomes the **Raven's Eye**: a headshot kill **gives a bullet back** and summons **ravens** that kill about 2 or 3 zombies. Patch 1.22: it counts for the Quickdraw contract and does more damage to the God King.

A bonus: every raven you shoot makes a **charged Corpse Eater** appear, useful for [The Shield of Thule](../bouclier/).

## Get the pistol

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Touch the golden raven statue">
    On the **Overlook**, near the blood pool, use the **golden raven statue**: it disappears.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'raven_statue.webp'} alt="The golden raven statue near the Overlook pool" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the four ravens, with a pistol">
    **Four ravens** caw somewhere on the map (there are around thirty possible spots). **Shoot each one with a pistol.** With another weapon, the raven reappears elsewhere. Each raven you hit makes a **charged Corpse Eater** appear. When you kill it, a **golden raven statue** appears in the **Blood Altar**, at the bottom of the first ramp from the Kugelblitz machine.

## The raven locations

From mmmrkennedy's list and TheDrShow's video (see above), by area of the map:

- **Crash Site**: on the plane's rear wing; on the Transport; on a metal bar near the shield radio; on the stone pillar of the Machine Pistol wall weapon; above the Thule entrance.
- **Ice Caves**: on a pillar to the right when entering from the Crash Site; on the cliff edge between the middle and right pillars; on the left wall above the pit to the Overlook.
- **Thulian Archives**: above Schnellblitz; in the ceiling corner to the right of Schnellblitz; on the ledge above the Gearworks door; in the ceiling corner above the Combat Shotgun; on the ground in front of the Overlook door.
- **Overlook**: behind the pool; at the top of the pillar above the armour stand; on a pillar to the left of Laufenblitz.
- **Overlook / Phylactery corridor**: in the wall opening between the Transport and Schildblitz; to the left of Schildblitz; on the wall in front of Schildblitz.
- **Phylactery**: on the edge of the big "bowl"; on the wall above and to the right of the FG42; above the Morgue door; on the beam in front of the open part of the wall.
- **Blood Altar**: on the beam of one of the two face statues above the plate; on a pillar to the left of the door to the Phylactery; at the top of the wooden scaffold behind; below on the scaffold; to the right of the Mystery Box spot.
