The **Mystery Box** rule is the same on all maps: see [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/). One spin costs **1,000 Jolts** according to Activision's official page.

{/* TO CHECK: 1,000 Jolts is the game-wide price from the Activision support page; no map source confirms it for The Frozen Dawn. */}

## Where the box is

The box is in the **Blood Altar**. The guides don't say whether it changes location or where else it may appear.

## The weapons

Some special weapons **do not come out** of the box:

- The **four Thule weapons** are made through quests: [The Scythe of Thule](../faux/), [The Hammer of Thule](../marteau/), [The Shield of Thule](../bouclier/) and [The Flail of Thule](../fleau/).
- The **Raven's Claw** pistol is also made through a quest: [The Raven's Claw](../griffe-du-corbeau/).

The **Fliegerfaust** can come out of the box.

## The quest weapons: The Classic and The Vintage

Two special weapons join the box **after** their quest, only one of the two per game.

- **The Classic**: a PPSh-41 with a **70-round** magazine, more ammo and a faster fire rate.
- **The Vintage**: an MG42 with a **125-round** magazine, more ammo and a faster fire rate.

The quest is a shooting mini-game in the Morgue: see [The map's secrets](../secrets/).

## Items that force a draw

Two Activision consumables guarantee a weapon from the box: the **Delisle Guarantee** and the **Blunderbuss Guarantee**. They come out of supply drops. See the rules in [WWII Zombies basics](../../the-final-reich/systemes-ww2/).
