The **Thulian Shield** is a special melee weapon: a **melee hit** sends a short-range shockwave, and **aiming** blocks attacks from the front. It does **not protect your back**. Upgraded, it becomes the **Roar of San'ket** (written "Sang'Ket" in mmmrkennedy's guide). It counts for the Easter Egg: see [Main Easter Egg: Kingfall](../easter-egg-principal/).

- You must have opened Thule: see [Open the way to Thule](../acces-a-thule/).
- The **Bloodraven's Chosen** achievement is given by the final trial: see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
- For the upgrade, you need a **charged Corpse Eater**: the quest of [The Raven's Claw](../griffe-du-corbeau/) makes one appear for every raven shot.

## Get the shield

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Fill the three blood pools">
    Get about **6 kills** in each pool to fill it.

    - **Ice Caves**: to the left of the Type 100 wall weapon.
    - **Morgue**: to the left of the M1928 wall weapon.
    - **Overlook**: this pool is broken until you have placed the **Bloodraven Stone**. Take it at the bottom of the Morgue, near the **body jars**, and place it on the pool.

  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill the Wüstling carrying the shield">
    When the three pools are full, a **Wüstling** comes out of the big **Blood Altar** pool with the shield on its back. Kill it and pick up the **Thulian Shield**.

## Upgrade the shield: Roar of San'ket

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Spot the smoking pools">
    With the shield in hand, look at each pool: only one gives off **red smoke** (visible only with the shield).

    <Screenshot src={m + 'red_smoke.webp'} alt="A pool giving off red smoke" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Kill a charged Corpse Eater in the pool">
    Let a **Corpse Eater** eat **3 or 4 zombies**: it glows red, it is charged. Kill it **while it stands in the pool**: a **pattern** appears in the blood (it changes every game). Do it in all three pools and **remember the order** in which you activated them.

    {/* TO CHECK: "fully charged" count 3-4 zombies: mmmrkennedy; Gameranx 160402 describes steam and an exploding charged Thulian. */}

    <Screenshot src={m + 'pattern.webp'} alt="A pattern in the blood of a pool" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Blow up a Bomber on the metal door">
    In the **Crash Site**, blow up a **Bomber** (it appears from round 8) against the **metal door**: a **radio** and a **speaker** appear.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={m + 'metal_door.webp'} alt="The Crash Site metal door" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'radio.webp'} alt="The radio revealed behind the door" credit={by} source={src} />
      <Screenshot src={m + 'speaker.webp'} alt="The speaker" credit={by} source={src} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place the radio and match the patterns">
    Place the radio and the speaker near the big **Blood Altar** pool. Use the radio to **cycle through the patterns**. When the pattern matches the one of the **first** pool, get a kill in the Blood Altar pool. Repeat with the two other patterns, in order. The seven possible patterns are in the image below.

    {/* TO CHECK: where the radio goes: mmmrkennedy says the Blood Altar pool; Gameranx 160402 and GamesHedge say a ledge over the big blood pool in the Phylactery. */}

    **To check:** according to another guide (Gameranx), the radio goes on a ledge above the big pool of the **Phylactery**.

    <Screenshot src={m + 'frozen_dawn_blood_patterns.webp'} alt="The seven possible patterns in the blood" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go down into the blood: Bloodraven trial">
    When the pool cycles through the patterns very fast, **go down into the blood** with the shield. You are "revived" with **full armour** and without using up a Quick Revive. You arrive in the **Bloodraven trial**. When it is over, you come back.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The final weapon

- Stronger shockwave, sturdier block, and a **charged melee attack** when you run.
- Patch 1.22 (2 October 2018): the **Protection of San'ket** creates a lasting zone that blocks projectiles and **restores your Geistschild**; the Roar of San'ket's melee damage is higher.
