On Awakening, the **Venom** alien weapons are back, and they are crafted like on Mayday: you find a **schematic**, pick up the **parts**, then assemble. The new thing on this map is the **Venom grenades**. The **Venom-X** is back too.

No written source gives the full list of schematic and part locations on this map. The videos at the top of the page show them in game.

{/* Crafting returns on Awakening: Millenium ("retour des compositions"), VideoGamesBlogger ("craft the Venom Grenade after finding the schematic", "where to find Venom-X schematics"). Schematic spots and part lists for Awakening: no text source found. */}

## The Venom grenades

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Find the Venom grenade schematic">
    **To check:** according to a single source, the schematic is in the **starting area**, near one of the lockers. Schematics on this map can also be found in the **lairs**: see [The lairs](../tanieres/).

    {/* TO CHECK: schematic "in the starting area, near one of the lockers" = GamingRacerHD SC7UZjKPaVA description only. Millenium says schematics are found in the lairs. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick up the parts">
    Parts are found by **searching** piles and boxes you can search. Like on Mayday, the type of **Biolum** you pick up decides the **effect** of the grenades.

    {/* TO CHECK: parts in search piles and Biolum deciding the effect = GamingRacerHD description only. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Craft them">
    With the schematic and the parts, you craft a batch of **5 grenades**. They are thrown like C4.

    {/* TO CHECK: 5 grenades per craft and C4-style aiming = GamingRacerHD description only. */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The Venom-X

The **Venom-X** is also crafted with a schematic on Awakening. The videos show at least the normal version (acid) and the **fire** version, and one video shows the **electric (Venom-LX)** version. The principle and the variants are explained in [The Venom-X and its variants](../../mayday/venom-x/), on Mayday.

{/* TO CHECK: Venom-X variants on Awakening: TheSmithPlays b9YqCSnwQDg chapters ("Venom Schematic", "Normal Venom", "Fire Venom"), DZ NIGHT XBl_JLoaBho title ("How to build the VEMON-LX ... in awakening"). Whether the Amethyst (Seeder) variant exists here: unknown. Schematic spot and parts: unknown. */}

## When you can't use them

- The **Earn Your Keep** relic prevents crafting.
- The **Pistols Only** relic prevents Venom weapons.

Relics are explained in [Extinction basics](../../point-of-contact/bases-extinction/).
