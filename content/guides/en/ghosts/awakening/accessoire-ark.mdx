The **Ark** is an attachment fitted at the **end of the barrel**. It replaces the muzzle brake or the suppressor. Your weapon then does **much more damage** and fires glowing bullets, like lasers. It is not a Pack-a-Punch: the weapon stays the same, with an extra attachment.

It even fits weapons that have a built-in suppressor, like the **K7** or the **VKS**.

## Where to find the Ark

There are **4 Arks** per game. Each one sits on a corpse's weapon, at the back of a Rhino or Mammoth **lair**. The lair changes from one game to the next, but the split stays the same:

| Zone | Lairs | Arks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zone 1 | 3 | **1**, in one of the 3 lairs |
| Zone 2 | 3 | **1**, in one of the 3 lairs |
| Zone 3 | 2 | **2**, one in each lair |

{/* 4 per game with this split: PSTHC + Miryu83 forum. VideoGamesBlogger says "one per area, 3 in total", Millenium "a few per game". */}

Be careful: each lair hides an **ambush**, with a Rhino or a Mammoth. Everything is in [The lairs](../tanieres/).

When you buy another weapon, the Ark **moves over by itself** to the new one, even without the Armory upgrade that keeps attachments.

## The Like A Glove achievement

**Like A Glove**: get **5 kills with the Ark** on **each of the 12** compatible weapons, in **a single game**, on Regular or Hardcore.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Set up the Armory">
    Buy the upgrade that **keeps compatible attachments** when you switch weapons: it costs **10 Teeth**. That way, your other attachments follow too.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get the first Ark in zone 1">
    Search the zone 1 lairs until you find the Ark. See [The lairs](../tanieres/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Chain the weapons in the right order">
    Get 5 kills with one weapon, then buy the next one: the Ark moves over to it. Start with the ones that don't accept certain attachments (grip, rapid fire) so you don't lose them:

    1. **VKS**
    2. **Tac 12**
    3. **IA-2** and **MK14 EBR**
    4. **Ripper** and **K7**
    5. **Chain SAW** and **Ameli**
    6. **Maverick** and **Remington R5**
  </Step>

  <Step title="Finish with the USR and the Bulldog">
    Before the end of zone 1, upgrade your **pistol** to the level that lets you carry **two primary weapons**. In zone 2, buy the **USR** and the **Bulldog**, find the **second Ark** in a lair and put it on one of the two. Get 5 kills with each.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="mistake" author="Miryu83" source="http://call-of-duty-zombies.forumgratuit.org/t149-l-arche-trophee-comme-un-gant">

  </Tip>
</Tips>
