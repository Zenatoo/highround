**Intel** items are hidden recordings that tell the story of Awakening. Once found, you can replay them in the **Info** menu of Extinction. Finding them all gives the **Spelunker** achievement.

## The 8 intel items

Awakening has **8**: two come on their own, and you have to find **6**. Each intel item found earns **$500**.

| Intel | How to get it |
| --- | --- |
| **Prologue** | Automatic, as soon as you own the Invasion pack |
| **Guardian Angel** | Phone, random in the small searchable boxes |
| **Dead or Alive** | Phone, random in the small searchable boxes |
| **The Cortex** | Phone, random in the small searchable boxes |
| **Fugitive** | Fixed computer, zone 1 |
| **Defector** | Fixed computer, zone 2 |
| **Executioner** | Fixed computer, final room |
| **Epilogue** | Automatic, when you finish the map |

## The 3 phones

They are found **at random** by searching the small green **boxes**, all over the three zones. They come out fairly easily: search everything you pass.

## The 3 computers

**To check:** these routes come from a single written source (Miryu83). Flapix's and LeRay0409's videos, at the top of the page, show them in game.

{/* TO CHECK: fixed computer spots = Miryu83 forum t148 only (shown in videos t_UQEAKB_jQ, jfBYUQuvHeE, XsJkA2zqmpo). */}

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Fugitive: the right-hand cave, zone 1">
    When you leave the starting area, look **to the left, up high**: there are **two caves**, each with a mushroom above it. Go into the **right-hand** one, the one with **two entrances** (the left one has only one). Climb by jumping from rock to rock. The computer is inside.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Defector: the cave near the MK14 EBR, zone 2">
    At the entrance of zone 2, jump **to the left** and go straight ahead to the **MK14 EBR** lying on the ground. Jump over the rock: a **cave** opens on the right. The computer is inside, on the left.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Executioner: the platform in the final room">
    In the final room, the **Ancestors' hibernation chamber**, go **left** as soon as you enter and climb the rock to reach the **platform**. The computer is there.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## What they tell

Spoiler warning. Samantha Cross knew Archer would come to Ball's Pyramid and was waiting for him there. The two make a deal: she gives him a tour of the **Ark**, he gives her a way off the island. She tells her last memory as a human (gas, broken glass, rain, screams), before waking up in the Ark. When the Cortex is taken, Archer tries to betray her, but she takes control of his body. The epilogue reveals that more than **80%** of Earth is in the aliens' hands and mentions a gigantic Ark where the Ancestors live: the story continues in Exodus.
