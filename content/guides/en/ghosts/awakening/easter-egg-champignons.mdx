Like the golden aliens of Point of Contact and the snowmen of Nightfall, Awakening has its own little Easter Egg: shoot **3 hidden alien eggs** and, for a few minutes, every alien you kill **explodes into mushrooms**. As a bonus, everyone gets **all the class bonuses at the same time**.

- Duration: **between 2 and 3 minutes** depending on the source.
- The bonuses: unlimited Feral Instincts to see aliens through walls, the Weapon Specialist effects, faster reload and weapon swap.
- Doable solo or in co-op.

## Before you shoot

- Shoot with **normal bullets**. The eggs don't react to shots from a weapon fitted with the **Ark**.
- For the zone 1 eggs, don't use **explosive ammo** either.
- The eggs are **not always in the same place**: several spots exist in zones 1 and 2, and even in zone 3. The three spots below are the best known. If an egg isn't there, watch extra bier's video, which shows them all.
- Gravity is low: to see the high eggs, **jump on the big mushrooms**.
