The **Invasion** pack adds **10 achievements** for Awakening (250 G on Xbox; on PlayStation, 7 bronze and 3 silver). Unless stated otherwise, you have to play on **Regular** or **Hardcore**: Casual difficulty doesn't count.

Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Targets Acquired (bronze)">
    Get through the first door. You have to scan the 5 obelisks of zone 1, then destroy them with the Vanguard. See [Map walkthrough](../deroulement/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="A Bridge To Somewhere (bronze)">
    Extend the bridge by scanning the 3 big obelisks of zone 3.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Escaped Awakening (silver)">
    Complete the escape for the first time, after the Cortex.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Twice The Fun (silver)">
    Complete the escape with **two relics** active. You therefore need to be at least **prestige 2**. Relics are explained in [Extinction basics](../../point-of-contact/bases-extinction/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Awakening Completionist (silver)">
    Complete **all the challenges** of the game, then the escape. Miss a single challenge and you have to start over.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Spelunker (bronze)">
    Find all the intel. See [Intel](../renseignements/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Dog Fight (bronze)">
    Kill a **Gargoyle** with the **Vanguard**. The Vanguard has to land the killing blow. Use the phases where you fly the Vanguard in zones 1 and 2.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Egg-stra Awakening! (bronze)">
    Shoot the **alien egg** hidden in each of the 4 multiplayer maps of the Invasion pack, in a **public match**. Then launch Awakening and **scan an obelisk**: you also earn **10,000 XP**.

    | Map | Where the egg is |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Favela** | At the back, bottom right of the map, near the barber: look left through the cemetery grate |
    | **Mutiny** | On the pirate ship in the center: climb onto the middle plank and look through the floor grate |
    | **Pharaoh** | In the top-right room (Federation spawn), blocked by flames: aim at the top-right corner through the fire, with a sniper rifle |
    | **Departed** | In the cemetery (Ghosts spawn): behind the red vase with white stripes, shoot the white trigger, the wall explodes and reveals the egg |

    {/* TO CHECK: egg spots = VideoGamesBlogger only (NoahJ456 and CoD QG videos show them). Public match + scan an obelisk + 10,000 XP: VideoGamesBlogger, PSTHC, Cheat Code Central. The French achievement text says "destroy" an obelisk, the English one "scan". */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Like A Glove (bronze)">
    Get **5 kills with the Ark** on **each of the 12 weapons** that accept it, in a single game. The full plan is in [The Ark attachment](../accessoire-ark/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Well Rounded (bronze)">
    Complete the escape **once with each of the 4 classes** (Weapon Specialist, Tank, Engineer, Medic). The escapes can be done over several games.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The 4-game plan

Since Well Rounded requires an escape with each class, plan **4 games** and fit the other achievements into them:

| Game | Class | Target achievements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Weapon Specialist | Egg-stra Awakening!, Targets Acquired, A Bridge To Somewhere, Escaped Awakening |
| 2 | Medic, with 2 relics | Twice The Fun |
| 3 | Tank | Dog Fight, Like A Glove |
| 4 | Engineer | The last escape for Well Rounded |

For the game with two relics, PSTHC recommends **No Machines** and **Smaller Wallet**.
{/* Plan and relic choice: PSTHC only. */}

On PlayStation, **Spelunker**, **Egg-stra Awakening!** and **Well Rounded** progress together on PS3 and PS4.
