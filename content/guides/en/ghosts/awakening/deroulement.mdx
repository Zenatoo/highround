**Awakening** is episode 3 of Extinction, in the **Invasion** downloadable pack (June 3, 2014 on Xbox, July 3, 2014 on PlayStation and PC). 36 hours after Mayday, your team goes down into the aliens' underground world, beneath the island of **Ball's Pyramid**. The goal: get through several defense barriers, reach the **Ark** of the **Ancestors** and take **the Cortex**, the key to their psychic powers. Then you have to escape.

There is **no boss** on this map: the ending is the Cortex, then the escape. The common rules (classes, skills, cash, Teeth, relics, basic aliens) are in [Extinction basics](../../point-of-contact/bases-extinction/).

## What changes on Awakening

- **Very low gravity**: you jump very high. The big mushrooms work as trampolines to reach high ledges.
- **No more drill on hives**: the drill works as a **scanner**. You place it on **obelisks** instead of hives. Each scan plays like a hive: a wave of aliens, a random team challenge, skill points.
- The **Vanguard**: a small four-rotor drone, armed with grenades, that you fly remotely. It is the only real way to destroy the obelisks.
- The **Ark**, a very powerful weapon attachment: see [The Ark attachment](../accessoire-ark/).
- The hidden **lairs** of Rhinos and Mammoths: see [The lairs](../tanieres/).
- The **Venom weapons** you can craft: see [Venom weapons](../armes-venom/).
- No new firearm: only known weapons, lying on the ground at fixed spots.

## The new aliens

| Alien | What it does | Cash per alien killed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bomber** | Small neon-green flying alien. It hovers, rushes you and **explodes**. Alone it is a minor nuisance, but in a swarm it hurts a lot. | $150 |
| **Gargoyle** | Big flying alien. When it **stops in the air**, it is about to fire a rain of projectiles: take cover. Shoot it until it lands: you can then finish it with the knife. | $750 |
| **Mammoth** | A Rhino that glows **blue** instead of red. It hits harder and can call **three Hunters** at once. It guards the lairs. | $2,000 |

## The map layout

The map is played in **three zones**, then the final room. No source gives the in-game names of the zones, so we call them zone 1, 2 and 3.

| Stage | Objective | What it unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zone 1 | Scan **5 obelisks**, then destroy them with the Vanguard | The first door |
| Zone 2 | Scan **5 obelisks**, then destroy them with the Vanguard | Zone 3 |
| Zone 3 | Scan **3 big obelisks** | The **bridge** to the heart of the Ancestors' complex |
| Final room | Activate the **Cortex** while defending yourself | The escape |
| Escape | Get back to the starting point before the timer runs out | Victory |

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Zone 1: scan the 5 obelisks">
    Carry the scanner to an obelisk and place it. Defend it during the scan, like a hive. Complete the **challenge** shown to earn one more skill point.

    During the scans, watch the sky: **Gargoyles** and **Bombers** attack from above. Also search the small green boxes along the way: locker keys, intel, ammo.
  </Step>

  <Step title="First Vanguard: destroy the obelisks">
    When the 5 obelisks are scanned, go pick up the **Vanguard**: it is marked by a drone icon. Hold the action button to take it, then fly it, aim and fire your grenades at each obelisk. Your normal weapons do very little damage to them.

    Be quick: while you are flying, lots of **Rhinos** and **Mammoths** show up, and your character is left defenseless. In co-op, the others protect you.

    When the obelisks are destroyed, the first door opens: this is the **Targets Acquired** achievement.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="tech" author="BaDaBocK (PSTHC)" source="https://www.psthc.fr/unjeu/call-duty-ghosts-ps3/guide-trophees-dlc3.htm">
        For the Dog Fight achievement, kill a Gargoyle with the Vanguard. The Vanguard has to land the killing blow.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>

    {/* TO CHECK: delay before the Vanguard can be taken again after it is shot down (15 s?) = Fandom snippet only, not used. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Zone 2: 5 obelisks and the second Vanguard">
    Same thing: scan the **5 obelisks** of zone 2, then take the Vanguard again to destroy them.

    Some challenges in this zone ask you to kill aliens **from a higher** or **lower level**: make use of the jumps and the mushrooms.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Zone 3: the 3 big obelisks and the bridge">
    Here there are **3 big obelisks**. You only scan them, with no Vanguard. At the end, the **bridge** extends to the heart of the Ancestors' complex: this is the **A Bridge To Somewhere** achievement.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The Cortex">
    Cross the bridge to the final room. The team activates the **Cortex** and has to defend itself while it charges. There is no timer for this part.

    {/* TO CHECK: how the Cortex phase works exactly (what to hold, how long) = PSTHC only; speedrunners call it "Terminal Defense" (extra bier chapters). */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="The escape">
    As soon as the Cortex is taken, a timer starts: you have **5 minutes** to get back to the **starting point**. Don't stop to kill aliens: run, and quickly revive downed teammates.

    Succeeding gives you **Escaped Awakening**. With two relics active, it also gives you **Twice The Fun**. See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).

    {/* TO CHECK: 5-minute escape timer = PSTHC only (Millenium only says "like Point of Contact"). */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Tips

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="BaDaBocK (PSTHC)" source="https://www.psthc.fr/unjeu/call-duty-ghosts-ps3/guide-trophees-dlc3.htm">
    For equipment, take incendiary or explosive ammo, armor or team boosters, the riot shield as an Equalizer, and a turret, the I.M.S. or the Vulture.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="faster" author="TheRelaxingEnd" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_wraUNDoIck">
    To kill a Mammoth fast: Weapon Specialist, a weapon fitted with the Ark and Armor-Piercing ammo.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="BaDaBocK (PSTHC)" source="https://www.psthc.fr/unjeu/call-duty-ghosts-ps3/guide-trophees-dlc3.htm">
    For the "melee a Gargoyle" challenge, shoot it until it lands, then hit it with the knife.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

The challenges specific to this map: kill flying enemies, kill enemies **while jumping** (easy with the low gravity), knife a Gargoyle, kill enemies from a higher or lower level, kill Bombers. The "kill while prone" challenge comes in two versions, one of which requires **all players** to be prone.

## The story

In the intro, Samantha Cross waits for David Archer in Ball's Pyramid, cuts off his hand and offers him a deal: a guided tour of the Ark in exchange for a way off the island. The rest is revealed in the hidden documents and the ending cutscene: see [Intel](../renseignements/).
