Awakening hides **lairs**: caves at the end of small **side paths**, away from the main route. In each one, a group of aliens waits in **ambush**, with a **Rhino** or a **Mammoth**. In exchange for the danger, they hold the best loot on the map.

<Screenshot src={t + 'taniere.webp'} alt="A Rhino bursts out of a lair in Awakening" credit={by} source={src} />

## How many and where

There are **8 lairs**: **3** in zone 1, **3** in zone 2 and **2** in zone 3.

No written source describes the path to each lair: Jon The Chief's video, at the top of the page, shows them one by one.

{/* 3 / 3 / 2 lairs: PSTHC and Miryu83 forum t149 (both describe the Ark spots by lair). Hidden side paths: Millenium. Exact spots: video only (L5ELSHrKsUE). */}

## What is inside

- One or more **lockers**, opened with a **locker key**. Keys are found by searching boxes all over the map. Lockers give weapons that already have attachments.
- **Schematics** to craft: see [Venom weapons](../armes-venom/).
- The **Ark**, the most powerful weapon attachment on the map: there is one in one of the zone 1 lairs, one in one of the zone 2 lairs, and one in each of the two zone 3 lairs. See [The Ark attachment](../accessoire-ark/).

{/* Lockers, keys, schematics, Ark in lairs: Millenium lairs page. Locker weapons with attachments: game-wide (bases-extinction). Ark split: PSTHC + Miryu83. */}

## Surviving a lair

The **Mammoth** looks like a Rhino, but it glows **blue** instead of red. It hits harder and can call **three Hunters** at once. It gives **$2,000**.
