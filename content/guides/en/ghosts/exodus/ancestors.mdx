The **Ancestors** are the masters of the Cryptids. They have been mentioned since Awakening, but Exodus is the only map where you fight them. They are not single bosses like the Breeder or the Kraken: several Ancestors show up during the game, then three during the final phase.

There is no main Easter Egg quest on Exodus: beating the Ancestors and firing the Medusa is the end of the map. The full walkthrough is in [Map walkthrough](../deroulement/).

## What you need to know

- **Their powers**: they are psychic and telekinetic. They create a gravity field that **deflects bullets** and lets them **levitate**.
- **The shield**: from time to time, an Ancestor wraps itself in a **bubble**. While it is up, bullets do nothing to it: do not waste them.
- **The weak spot**: the **top of the head**. A hit there shows an **orange** hitmarker.
- **Their attack**: a ball of black energy that can **lift a player** into the air. They cannot do anything for a few seconds.
- Around them, the other aliens keep coming, often in large numbers.

## When they arrive

| When | Ancestors |
| --- | --- |
| Second generator of the **gas station** | 1 |
| Second generator of the **parking garage** | 1 |
| Second generators of the **offices** | none |
| **Final phase** (Medusa charge) | 3, one after another |
| If you are slow to fire the Medusa | 2 more |

Each Ancestor killed gives **1 skill point** (except in Hardcore). During the final phase, each Ancestor also starts a **challenge**: kill it in under 3 minutes, do not get hit by an Ancestor for 2 minutes, or deal damage to its weak points.

## Bringing the shield down

- **A charged shot from the NX-1 Disruptor**: charge the weapon fully (10 out of 10) and send the big energy ball at the Ancestor. This is the safest method. See [The NX-1 Disruptor](../nx-1-disruptor/).
- **An NX-1 grenade**, according to one source.
- **Waiting** a few seconds: the shield eventually drops on its own.

## The strategy

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Clear the aliens around it first">
    When an Ancestor arrives, first kill the small aliens that come with it. During a generator, do not forget to defend the generator: if it is destroyed, the game is lost.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bring the shield down">
    One player sends a NX-1 **charged shot** at the bubble.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Aim for the head, all together">
    Right after, a second player sends another charged shot at the **top of the head**. Then everyone fires at the head with their weapons, until the bubble comes back. Repeat.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The final fight and the Medusa

During the final phase, the **Medusa** gauge fills with every alien killed. The three Ancestors arrive one after another. When the gauge is full, press the **launch button**: the Medusa kills **every alien**, Ancestors included, and the game is won.

For the **Timing is Everything** achievement, do not kill the final phase Ancestors: wait for the full gauge, kill just one near the button and fire the Medusa right away. The details are in [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).

The **Hypno Knife** does not turn an Ancestor into an ally: it only gives a hitmarker. That is enough for the hidden **You Wish** achievement.
