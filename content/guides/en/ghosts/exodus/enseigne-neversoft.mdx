Exodus' second tribute to the **Neversoft** studio: a lit sign at the gas station. Shoot its letters in the right order and you unlock a **top secret intel**, the **Always Hard** bonus video.

The first Neversoft secret is in [Star Power Easter Egg: the Neversoft eyes](../yeux-neversoft/).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Go to the gas station">
    From the start, take the **left door** and head to the **gas station**. On the roof of the building, above the **car wash**, there is a red neon sign, almost out. It is near a crater in the street.

    {/* Gas station building roof, red "Carwash" neon: VideoGamesBlogger intel guide, jeuxvideo.com forum (AceRoux: red sign on the shop of the gas station), Cheat Code Central (dim sign above the car wash, near a crater). Millenium calls it the car wash of "the first zone". */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the letters in order: N-E-V-E-R-S-O-F-T">
    Shoot the letters **N, E, V, E, R, S, O, F, T**, in exactly this order. Each time you hit the right letter, it shows up in color at the top of the screen. When the word is complete, **"NEVERSOFT"** appears in the middle of the screen.

    <Screenshot src={s + 'enseigne.webp'} alt="The gas station sign, with the letters NEVERSOFT in color at the top of the screen" credit={by} source={p3} />

    {/* Letter order: Millenium p3, Cheat Code Central, VideoGamesBlogger. Coloured letter on the HUD after each hit: Cheat Code Central (and visible on Millenium's picture). Word on screen: Millenium p3. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Watch the Always Hard video">
    Find the video in Extinction's **intel** menu, at the very bottom of the Exodus list.

    It shows **Joel Jewett**, founder of Neversoft, shooting a **flaming arrow** with a bow at a replica of the studio's emblem, then credits with the team members. It is the studio's farewell, on its last day.

    <Screenshot src={s + 'toujours-dur.webp'} alt="The Exodus intel menu, with the Always Hard video" credit={by} source={p3} />

    {/* Video content: Millenium p3 (Joel Jewett, flaming arrow, staff credits). Last day of the studio: Cheat Code Central, VideoGamesBlogger. Listed last in the Episode 4 Intel menu: Millenium's screenshot. */}  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Good to know

- This intel **does not count** toward the **The Final Chapter** achievement: you need the 7 story intel. See [Intel](../renseignements/).
- Do not mix it up with the **Always Hard** achievement, which requires finishing the map in Hardcore with a relic. See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
