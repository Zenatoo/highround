The **NX-1 Disruptor** is Exodus' special weapon. It fires **blue energy spheres** and **never needs ammo**. It is the best weapon against **Rhinos**, **Mammoths** and above all **Ancestors**, whose shield it brings down. Craft it at the very start of the game.

On Exodus, there is no Venom-X: the NX-1 and the **NX-1 grenades** replace it.

## Crafting the NX-1 Disruptor

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Take the schematic in the parking garage">
    At the start, open the **central door**. When its countdown is over, enter the **parking garage** straight ahead. The NX-1 **schematic** is on a **workbench**, on the **ground floor** of the garage, before the generators.

    {/* Schematic spot: PSTHC (central door, parking in front, workbench), VideoGamesBlogger achievements guide (bottom floor of the parking garage, before the generators), jeuxvideo.com forum tutorial ("straight into the garage"). Millenium p3 and Miryu83 describe the same place in other words. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the 3 components in the red crates">
    Search the long **red crates** all over the map. You need **3 components**, like for the earlier craftable items (Mayday's Venom-X, Awakening's Venom grenades). Search everything you can and follow the edges of the map and of rooms.

    <Screenshot src={x + 'nx1/caisse-rouge.webp'} alt="A red crate to search, with its glowing outline" credit={by} source={p3} />

    {/* 3 components in red crates: Millenium p3, Miryu83, VideoGamesBlogger ("3 red tool boxes"). Search the edges: VideoGamesBlogger Exodus guide. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Craft the weapon">
    With the schematic and the 3 components, craft the NX-1. How schematics and crafting work is explained in [Extinction basics](../../point-of-contact/bases-extinction/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## How it works

- It has **10 charges**. When they run out, wait **a few seconds**: the weapon recharges by itself. Nothing to buy.
- **Single shot**: pull the trigger, and one energy ball is fired per charge.
- **Charged shot**: **hold aim** (LT on Xbox, L2 on PlayStation) to charge, then **fire** (RT / R2). All the charges go out as **one big ball** that explodes on impact, with a wide damage area. For the maximum effect, wait until you reach 10 out of 10.
- A full charged shot **brings down an Ancestor's shield**. See [Beat the Ancestors](../ancestors/).
- It is very powerful: according to one source, **4 shots** are enough to kill a Rhino.

{/* 10 charges, self-recharge: Millenium p3, Accelerated Ideas, Se7enSins (Ws-spobNVgI description). Charged shot = hold aim then fire: Millenium p3 (hold LT/L2, then RT/R2), PSTHC (hold L2), VideoGamesBlogger (aim button fires all 10 shots at once; "charge using LT"). Accelerated Ideas says "hold fire 2-3 s": minority. Shield: Miryu83, Cheat Code Central, VideoGamesBlogger. */}

{/* TO CHECK: "4 shots kill a Rhino" = Millenium p3 only. */}

It also helps with the "do not reload for 90 seconds" challenge: it has no magazine to reload.

## The NX-1 grenades

The **NX-1 grenades** are crafted the same way: a schematic, then **3 components** in the red crates. You get **5 grenades**. They **explode on impact** and create a strong **shockwave**.

{/* Grenades: Millenium p3 (schematic + 3 components, explode on impact, shockwave, 5 grenades), Miryu83 (3 components). */}

**To check:** the grenade schematic is said to be on a workbench, in a **ruined building east of the final zone** (where you defend the Medusa), and a grenade would also bring down an Ancestor's shield. Only one source says so.
