Exodus was made with the **Neversoft** studio (Tony Hawk's Pro Skater, Guitar Hero), which merged with Infinity Ward in 2014. The map pays tribute to it with two secrets. The first: find the **three Neversoft eyes** (the studio's emblem) hidden on the map, and the whole team gets a big bonus, the **Star Power**, just like in Guitar Hero.

The other secret is in [The NEVERSOFT sign and the top secret intel](../enseigne-neversoft/).

**Before you start:**

- There is **one eye per zone**: one in the **starting area**, one at the **gas station** and one in the **central room** of the offices (the room with a generator on the ground floor and one upstairs).
- Each eye has **several possible spots**: it changes place from one game to the next. Check every hiding place in the zone.
- To pick up an eye, **hold the action button** (X on Xbox, Square on PlayStation). A little Tony Hawk's Pro Skater sound plays.
- Works solo or in co-op.

## The reward: Star Power

When the third eye is picked up, the screen electrifies and **the whole team** gets the **Star Power** for about **3 minutes**: the bonuses of all abilities at once, including unlimited **Feral Instincts** and the Weapons Specialist boost. During this time, killed aliens **explode into Neversoft eyes**.
