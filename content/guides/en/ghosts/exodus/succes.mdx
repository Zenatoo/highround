The **Nemesis** DLC adds **10 achievements** (250 G on Xbox) and as many trophies on PlayStation, all tied to Exodus. Only one is hidden: **You Wish**. Tick them off as you go: your progress stays on your device. The names are the ones from the English version of the game.

All the ones that ask you to finish the map or do something during a game only count in **Regular** or **Hardcore** (not Casual). With some luck, two games are enough: one in Regular, one in Hardcore with a relic. Completionist often takes more tries.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Mass Exodus (silver, 30 G)">
    Beat the last wave of Ancestors and fire the Medusa: this is the end of the map. See [Map walkthrough](../deroulement/) and [Beat the Ancestors](../ancestors/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Unstoppable (bronze, 30 G)">
    Same thing, with **at least one relic** active. One relic is enough: pick the one that bothers your team the least.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Always Hard (silver, 30 G)">
    Kill the last Ancestor in **Hardcore** with a **relic** active. This is the hardest: in Hardcore, there are more aliens, lots of Seekers and Bombers, Rhinos and Mammoths come in pairs, and **only challenges** give skill points.

    Do it with 2 or 3 players you know. The **Fragile** relic (less health regeneration) works well if you often place **Armor**. Do not miss any challenge.

    Do not mix it up with the sign's "Always Hard" bonus video: see [The NEVERSOFT sign and the top secret intel](../enseigne-neversoft/).

    {/* Hardcore details, Fragile relic advice, 2-3 players: PSTHC only. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Nemesis Completionist (silver, 30 G)">
    Complete **every challenge** of the game and finish the map. You do not need to open every door: you must complete the challenges of the doors you open, those of the **6 generators** and the **3 final phase challenges**. Play without a relic, with players who communicate. The list of challenges is in [Map walkthrough](../deroulement/).

    You can also earn it by **joining a friend's game** in which they have completed all their challenges, just before they start the final phase (public game with 3 players at most, or an open custom game). Complete the 3 final challenges together.

    It is impossible to get in the same game as **Timing is Everything**: one final challenge asks you to kill an Ancestor in under 3 minutes.

    {/* All from PSTHC. VideoGamesBlogger: restart if you get hard challenges early. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Timing is Everything (bronze, 30 G)">
    Kill the last three Ancestors **within 10 seconds** of the first one. The trick: during the final phase, **do not kill** the Ancestors. Wait for the Medusa gauge to be full, stand near the **launch button**, kill **just one** Ancestor and fire the Medusa right away. It kills the others.

    The final phase then lasts longer: the Ancestors bring in lots of aliens. Keep armor and ammo.

    {/* Trick: PSTHC + VideoGamesBlogger. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Postmaster (bronze, 20 G)">
    Turn on **both generators** of the same zone (gas station, parking garage or offices). You get it automatically by finishing the map.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hat Trick (bronze, 20 G)">
    Kill **3 aliens with a single shot** of the NX-1 Disruptor. Wait for the full charge (10 out of 10) and send the charged shot at a group of **Seekers** or **Scouts**. See [The NX-1 Disruptor](../nx-1-disruptor/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="You Wish (bronze, 20 G, hidden)">
    Hit an Ancestor with a **Hypno Knife**. The knife comes **at random** from the green crates you can search: search everything. When an Ancestor arrives (a zone's second generator, or the final phase), first bring its **shield** down with a charged NX-1 shot, then throw the knife.

    The Ancestor does not become your ally, even with an upgrade: you just see a hitmarker. The achievement still unlocks.

    {/* Hypno Knife in green crates, shield first, hitmarker only: PSTHC, VideoGamesBlogger, Cheat Code Central. Miryu83: a knife thrown at a shielded Ancestor fails, but you can pick it up again before it vanishes (single source, left out). */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="The Final Chapter (bronze, 20 G)">
    Find the **7 story intel**: 4 random smartphones and 3 tablets at fixed spots. See [Intel](../renseignements/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Eggstra Nemesis! (bronze, 20 G)">
    In **multiplayer**, find and shoot the **hidden alien hive** on each of the 4 maps of the Nemesis pack (**Showtime**, **Goldrush**, **Subzero** and **Dynasty**). You need a **public game**: it does not work in a private game. Then, on Exodus, **turn on a generator**.

    On PlayStation, the trophy carries over from one console to the other: if it is unlocked on PS3, turning on a generator on PS4 unlocks it too, and the other way around.

    {/* Egg-Stra XP: PSTHC (public match, then a generator; cross-save), VideoGamesBlogger (map names; it misspells Showtime), MP1st. */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>
