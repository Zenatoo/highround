**Exodus** is episode 4 and the last chapter of the Extinction story, the map of Ghosts' fourth and final DLC, **Nemesis** (August 5, 2014 on Xbox, September 4, 2014 on PlayStation and PC). Three months after Awakening, your team joins the last bastion of human resistance, besieged by the Cryptid army. Your mission: **restore power to the shuttle** so it can carry survivors to a space station, then hold out until the **Medusa** fires, a psionic weapon built around the Cortex.

{/* Dates: Activision blog, Game Informer (Xbox 5 Aug), Charlie Intel, MP1st, PlayStation LifeStyle (4 Sep). GamingBolt alone says 2 Sep in North America. Story: official press text (Worthplaying, Engadget), Millenium p2, VideoGamesBlogger ("3 months after the Ark Event"). */}

Exodus is not like the earlier maps: **no drill and no hives**. You open doors and turn on **generators**, in whatever order you want. It is also the biggest Extinction map.

The rules shared by every map (classes, skills, cash, challenges, relics, Teeth) are in [Extinction basics](../../point-of-contact/bases-extinction/).

**Before you start:**

- **6 generators**, two in each of the three zones: the **gas station**, the **parking garage** and the **offices**.
- You choose your path right from the start: three doors lead out of the starting area.
- You do not need to open every door, but you must turn on all 6 generators to start the final phase.
- The new enemy is the **Ancestor**, a psychic alien protected by a shield. You fight at least **five** of them: see [Beat the Ancestors](../ancestors/).
- Almost every alien from the earlier maps is here: Scouts, Seekers, Scorpions, Rhinos, Phantoms, Gargoyles (the flying aliens), Bombers, Mammoths...
- Craft the **NX-1 Disruptor**, the map's special weapon, early: see [The NX-1 Disruptor](../nx-1-disruptor/).

## The doors and the generators

<Screenshot src={x + 'map/directions.webp'} alt="Exodus' starting area, where several paths begin" credit={by} source={p1} />

**The three starting doors.** Facing the central door:

- The **left door** leads to the **gas station**. Further on, a long **electric fence** also opens for cash.
- The **central door** leads straight to the **parking garage** (a multi-story garage). The NX-1 Disruptor schematic is on the ground floor.
- The **right door** leads to a small hut (an intel is lying there).

{/* Left door -> gas station: Millenium p4 + VideoGamesBlogger intel guide. Central door -> parking garage: PSTHC, VideoGamesBlogger achievements guide, jeuxvideo.com forum tutorial. Right door -> hut: Millenium p5 + VideoGamesBlogger. */}

No reliable source gives the door prices.

**Opening a door** starts a **countdown** and a **challenge**. When the countdown ends, you get **1 skill point**. If you complete the challenge, you get **1 more**.

**Turning on a generator** works like placing the drill on the other maps: you start it, then **defend** it while it charges. The aliens attack it. **If its health drops to zero, the game is lost.** Each generator starts a challenge (+1 point if completed) and gives **1 point** when its charge is done.

{/* Door timer + challenge, generator challenge + end point, generator at 0 = game lost: PSTHC only for the "game lost" part. Generators defended like a drill: PSTHC, Accelerated Ideas. */}

**The second generator** of the gas station and the one of the parking garage each spawn an **Ancestor**. The offices' one does not. Killing the Ancestor gives 1 skill point. If you do not kill it, it drifts away and comes back later.

{/* Ancestor on the 2nd generator of gas station and parking, none in the offices, +1 point: PSTHC, Miryu83 (frqAjpoNXe8). "Drifts away and comes back": Accelerated Ideas only. */}

{/* TO CHECK: Accelerated Ideas alone says an Ancestor that is not killed drifts to the edge of the map and comes back later. */}

Turning on **both generators of the same zone** unlocks the **Postmaster** achievement.

## The recommended path

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Central door: the parking garage and the NX-1">
    Open the **central door** and enter the **parking garage** straight ahead when the countdown is over. Take the **NX-1 Disruptor schematic** from the ground-floor workbench, before the generators. Search the map's **red crates** for its 3 components. Everything is in [The NX-1 Disruptor](../nx-1-disruptor/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Only one generator per zone at first">
    Open the doors and first turn on **just one generator** in the parking garage and one in the gas station. You earn skill points without waking an Ancestor. The offices have no Ancestor: you can turn on both generators there whenever you want. In the offices, one generator is on the ground floor and the other upstairs.

    {/* "Open the doors, then one generator per zone except the offices, to meet the Ancestors with more skill points": PSTHC. Offices layout (one generator downstairs, one upstairs): Millenium p4. */}

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="faster" author="PSTHC (BaDaBocK)" source="https://www.psthc.fr/unjeu/call-duty-ghosts-ps3/guide-trophees-dlc4.htm">
        Keep the second generators of the gas station and the parking garage for last: you will face their Ancestors with more skill points already spent.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="The second generators and their Ancestors">
    Turn on the second generator of the gas station, then the one in the parking garage. An Ancestor appears each time while you defend the generator. Bring its shield down with a **charged NX-1 shot**, then aim for its **head**. See [Beat the Ancestors](../ancestors/).

    Near the end of the game (last doors, last generators, final phase), lots of **Rhinos** and **Mammoths** show up. The NX-1 hurts them badly.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The final phase: charge the Medusa">
    Once the 6 generators are on, you can start the **final phase**. Here, there is **nothing to defend**: it is a zone defense. A **gauge**, at the top left of the screen, charges the **Medusa**. Every alien you kill fills it a little: kill as many aliens as you can.

    **Three Ancestors** arrive one after another. Each one starts a challenge (+1 point if completed) and each Ancestor killed gives 1 point. Plan for a good ten minutes: bring armor, ammo and your NX-1.

    {/* Final zone defence with no device, gauge top left, 3 Ancestors one after another with a challenge each: PSTHC. Kills fill the gauge: Peruviantank (EwoiNlSylQs description) + jeuxvideo.com forum tutorial. "About 12 minutes in a normal run": extra bier (E7rVIN2pT30 description). */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fire the Medusa">
    When the gauge is full, press the **launch button**, next to it. The Medusa kills every alien and the game is **won**: **Mass Exodus** achievement. The ending cutscene (the epilogue) unlocks.

    If you wait too long before firing it, **two more Ancestors** arrive.

    {/* Launch button, Medusa kills every alien = victory: PSTHC. 2 more Ancestors if you wait: Miryu83 (frqAjpoNXe8) + two jeuxvideo.com forum posts (one says "1, 2 or 3"). Epilogue on victory: Millenium p2. */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Skill points and Hardcore

Exodus skill points come from four things: the **doors** (countdown and challenge), the **generators** (challenge and end of charge), the **Ancestors** you kill and the **final phase challenges**.

In **Hardcore**, only **challenges** give points: nothing for countdowns, finished generators or Ancestors killed. You get two to three times fewer points than in Casual or Regular. Do not miss any challenge.

### The map's challenges

The number (X) depends on the number of players. A few challenges that are specific to Exodus or new:

- Kill X enemies in the **circle**, which now **moves**.
- Kill X enemies **on the lower level** or **on the upper level** (on a platform below you or above you).
- Kill X **Bombers** before they self-destruct.
- Do not **reload** for 90 seconds: use traps, turrets, the IMS, Vulture and the NX-1, which needs no ammo.
- Kill the **Leper** in under 30 seconds: it is the only alien that does not rush you. Feral Instincts helps you spot it.
- During the final phase: kill an Ancestor in under 3 minutes, do not get hit by an Ancestor for 2 minutes, and deal X damage to an Ancestor's **weak points** (orange hitmarker on the head).

## Recommended gear

There is no mystery box: weapons sit at fixed spots, at fixed prices (prices not found for Exodus). The **green crates** you can search give the intel and sometimes a **Hypno Knife**. The **red crates** give the components of craftable items.

{/* Green crates: intel phones + Hypno Knife (PSTHC, Cheat Code Central, Miryu83). Red crates: components (Millenium p3, Miryu83, VideoGamesBlogger). */}

**Sticky Flares** can also be crafted on Exodus. According to videos, the map has other schematics (a Tesla trap, Venom grenades), but no written source says where.
