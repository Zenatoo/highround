**Intel** unlocks cutscenes that tell the end of the Extinction story. On Exodus, there are **7** to find for the **The Final Chapter** achievement:

- **4 classified files**: small **smartphones**, hidden **at random** in the crates you can search;
- **3 encrypted files**: **tablets**, always in the **same place**.

On top of that come the **epilogue**, unlocked when you win the game, and a hidden **top secret** intel: see [The NEVERSOFT sign and the top secret intel](../enseigne-neversoft/). These two do not count toward the achievement.

You can find the unlocked videos in Extinction's intel menu.

## The 4 smartphones (random)

Search the **small crates** scattered all over the map: a classified file can come out of them. It drops on the ground with a glowing outline: pick it up. According to one source, the four always come in the same order, whichever crate they come from.

## What they tell (warning, spoilers)

- **Exodus**: an emergency plan calls for restoring an old space station to shelter elite soldiers and scientists.
- **Gargoyles**: the flying aliens are fast, silent, invisible to drones, and rule the skies over cities.
- **Psionic Warfare**: the **Cortex** is the brain of a living Ancestor. It stores energy like a psychic battery and, once released, it fries the Cryptids' brains.
- **Ancestor Combat**: the Ancestors are telekinetic and psychic, with a field that deflects attacks. Archer's experimental weapon could change the game.
- **The Final Option**: the Colorado Ark was only one fortress among dozens. The big cities have fallen and **Godfather** leads what is left of the United States.
- **Caller ID: Unknown**: a message in Archer's voice accuses Samantha Cross of being a double agent. The analysis shows the voice is fake.
- **More Than Human**: Samantha Cross says she is not the same anymore. She can feel the Ancestors' fear.
- **Epilogue**: the rocket takes off, the Medusa destroys the Gargoyle swarms, but Earth is lost. On board, Samantha Cross opens eyes that have turned **red**.
