With Nightfall, Extinction finally tells a story. It comes through the **intel**: recordings and cutscenes you can watch afterward in Extinction's **Intel** menu. There are **15** for this map. Finding them all gives the **Undiscovered Truths** achievement.

Each intel picked up during a game pays **$500**. A counter at the top right of the screen shows how many you have.

## The 15 intel

| Type | Files | How to get them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Automatic (3) | Prologue, Cpt. David Archer, Dr. Samantha Cross | Unlocked as soon as you own the Onslaught DLC |
| Random (6) | 01: Arrival, 02: Origins, 03: Xenobiology, 04: The Obelisk, 05: The Hatchery, 06: Outbreak | In the small Pillage boxes of zones 1 to 3 |
| Computers (5) | 01: Treason, 02: Security Breach, 03: Exfil Denied, 04: Sabotage, 05: The Ark | Always in the same place, see below |
| End of game (1) | Epilogue | Finish Nightfall by killing the Breeder |

{/* 15 intel, names: Telixion chapters (EN), Miryu83 description (FR), Maka91Productions, VideoGamesBlogger, PSTHC ("11 sur la map dont 5 fixes"). $500 each: Miryu83, GamingBolt, VideoGamesBlogger. */}

## The 6 random intel

They come out of the **small Pillage boxes**, at random, **in order** (01, then 02...). Each zone gives one or two. **Only one player** can pick up each file.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="Maka91Productions" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O-GFVHeP0Ds">
    To farm them, play solo: Pillage every box in zone 1, then let yourself die during the first hive and restart the game.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## The 5 computers

<Checklist>
  <Step title="01: Treason (zone 2)">
    After the first fight against the Breeder, enter zone 2 and take the **stairs on the right**. Follow the catwalk (the balcony) to the right until the end: the computer is on the floor, near an **ARX-160**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="02: Security Breach (zone 2)">
    From the entrance of zone 2, go **left**: a container (with a Vector inside) has a **fire trap** next to it. Climb onto the trap, then onto the container. Pull out your **pistol** to run faster, take a run-up and jump toward the nearest **roof**. The computer is on that roof, near a fence.
  </Step>

  <Step title="03: Exfil Denied (zone 3)">
    When you enter the warehouse, a **small booth** is on your left, with wooden crates next to it. Climb onto the crates, then onto the booth: the computer is there, next to a **Vepr**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="04: Sabotage (zone 3)">
    When you enter the warehouse, take the **big ladder** on your right up to the **3rd level**, onto the scaffolding. Follow it to the left and jump over the **broken part**: the computer is on the floor, right after.
  </Step>

  <Step title="05: The Ark (zone 4)">
    In the final fight's arena, cross to the **far left**, behind the fence: the computer is on the floor in a **small booth** (a sort of toll booth). Grab it during the final fight, without wasting time, then go back to shooting the Breeder.

    {/* "Small toll booth across the arena" (Maka91Productions, Cheat Code Central) vs "tout au fond à gauche du grillage, dans un renfoncement" (Miryu83): read as the same spot. */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The achievement

**Undiscovered Truths** unlocks when you have all 15. The longest part is the 6 random files: it sometimes takes several games. On PlayStation, this trophy is **shared between PS3 and PS4**: it unlocks on the second console after the first hive.

{/* TO CHECK: VideoGamesBlogger says the achievement sometimes only pops once all 15 videos have been watched (single source). Cross-save note: PSTHC only. */}

According to one guide, if the achievement doesn't unlock, watch all 15 videos in the Intel menu.
