The **Venom-X** is Nightfall's special weapon: a prototype built with alien technology. It fires a **green acid projectile**, like the Scorpions' one, that leaves a **toxic cloud** on the ground for a while. It is **free**, **every player** can take one, and it **takes no slot**: you keep all your weapons on top of it.

It is mostly the weapon that brings the Breeder to his knees in the final fight.

## Getting it

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Reach the warehouse (zone 3)">
    Destroy the hives of zones 1 and 2, then go through the wall the Breeder breaks at the end of zone 2. See [Map walkthrough](../deroulement/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Destroy the middle hive, on the ground floor">
    In the **middle of the warehouse, on the ground floor**, a hive blocks the entrance to a small weapons room. Place the drill on it and destroy it. Do it **first** in this zone: the Venom-X will help you with the next hives.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Take the weapon from the table">
    Enter the room: the Venom-X sits **on the work tables**. Hold the action button to take it. Every player can take their own.

  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Ammo

The Venom-X only has **4 shots** at the start. To reload it, pick up the small **glowing eggs** that some killed aliens drop.

{/* TO CHECK: which aliens drop Venom-X ammo. Maka91Productions (ao2IVNWCfd0 description) says "dead enemies"; Digital Trends only says "there will be Venom ammo dropped"; Accelerated Ideas says only Scorpions drop it. */}

According to the sources, these eggs drop from any alien, or only from **Scorpions**. Don't waste your shots on the small aliens: save them for the Rhinos and the Breeder.

## Using it

- **Against the Breeder**: one shot can make him **fall to his knees** for 10 to 15 seconds. The whole team takes advantage to shoot his head. See [Beat the Breeder](../breeder/).
- **Against Rhinos** and groups of aliens: the impact hurts, and the acid cloud keeps hurting aliens that walk through it.
- The **Turnabout is Fair Play** achievement requires **50 kills** with the Venom-X in Nightfall. In co-op, ask your teammates to leave you some aliens.

The **Pistols Only** relic prevents you from taking the Venom-X. Chaos mode, added in April 2014, can also drop one in its bonuses. An April 3, 2014 update fixed an invisible explosion when aiming with the Venom-X.
