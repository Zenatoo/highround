Nightfall has **11 achievements** (trophies on PlayStation), worth **250 G** on Xbox. They come with the Onslaught DLC. Check them off as you go: your progress stays on your device. The names are the game's English ones.

On PlayStation, the map's trophies require **Regular or Hardcore** difficulty: a Casual game doesn't count.

{/* TO CHECK: the Xbox texts of the Onslaught achievements don't mention the difficulty (vgtimes, MP1st, Cheat Code Central); the PlayStation texts say "Regular or Hardcore" (PlatPursuit). Casual was added on 22 Apr 2014. */}

You can get almost all of them **in a single game**: only Undiscovered Truths (random intel) and Nightfall Completionist (challenges) often take several tries.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Pushing Ahead: bronze">
    20 G. Enter the **Compound**, zone 2: you must destroy the first 4 hives and drive the Breeder back once. See [Map walkthrough](../deroulement/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Weapon Facility: bronze">
    20 G. Enter the **Facility**, zone 3, after hives 5 to 8.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Survived Nightfall: bronze">
    20 G. Kill the Breeder in the **final arena**. The first fight doesn't count: he runs away. See [Beat the Breeder](../breeder/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Speed Slayer: bronze">
    20 G. Kill the final fight's Breeder in **under 5 minutes**. Everyone enters with the **Venom-X** and its ammo, and you take turns bringing him to his knees. The method is in [Beat the Breeder](../breeder/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turnabout is Fair Play: bronze">
    20 G. Kill **50 aliens** with the Venom-X. Take it as soon as you enter the warehouse and use it on everything that moves. In co-op, ask the others to leave you some aliens. See [Get the Venom-X](../venom-x/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Nightfall Completionist: silver">
    30 G. Complete **all the challenges** of the game (one per hive, plus the one from the first fight against the Breeder: 14 in total), then kill the Breeder.

    - Play with a team that communicates, not with strangers.
    - If the first challenges are hard (precision shooting, kills while prone), **restart the game**: later, with lots of aliens around, these challenges get much harder.
    - Accuracy challenge: one player fires **a single bullet** without missing, then the whole team finishes the wave with the knife. Accuracy stays at 100%.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Throttled Survival: silver">
    30 G. Kill the final fight's Breeder with at least **one relic** active. A relic is activated in the pre-game menu once you have prestiged: see [Extinction basics](../../point-of-contact/bases-extinction/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Phantom Exterminator: bronze">
    20 G. Kill **5 Phantoms** in a single game. There are more than 5 on the map: in co-op, ask the others to leave them to you.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pea Shooter: bronze">
    20 G. Kill a **Rhino** while damaging it **only with the pistol**. A single point of damage from anything else (turret, trap, grenade, another weapon, even from a teammate) cancels the attempt for everyone, and only the player who finishes it gets the achievement.

    The guides recommend doing it on one of the **first Rhinos**, at the end of zone 2. Level up the Weapon Specialist and take armor-piercing ammo. In co-op, take turns.

    {/* First Rhinos: VideoGamesBlogger (3rd drill of Area 2), PSTHC (between Area 2 and 3, "montez les escaliers"). */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Undiscovered Truths: bronze">
    30 G. Find the **15 intel** of Nightfall. See [Intel](../renseignements/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Egg-stra XP!: bronze">
    20 G. It is done in two parts.

    1. In **multiplayer, in a public game**, shoot the alien egg hidden in each of the 4 Onslaught maps:
       - **Ignition**: in the small underground corridor in the middle of the map (entrance through a container), on the wall.
       - **Fog**: in the caves in the center, behind the **waterfall**.
       - **Bayview**: lighthouse side, under the **pier** on the other side of the water. Take a long-range weapon.
       - **Containment**: from the green dumpster in front of the gas station, on the roof of the small building opposite, next to the big green building.
    2. In **Extinction**, destroy a hive with the drill.

    {/* Egg spots: Maka91Productions (3fZujb-8DA4 description), Flapix Z3LavDHfyvo, COD Zombified. Extinction part: Nightfall or Point of Contact per Maka91Productions; the in-game text says "in the Onslaught DLC pack". */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>
