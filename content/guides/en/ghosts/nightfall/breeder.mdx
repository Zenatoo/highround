The **Breeder** is the boss of Nightfall: an alien three stories tall. He doesn't walk, he just **turns** to face his target. You fight him twice: at the end of zone 1, then in the final arena. In between, at the end of zone 2, he only comes to break a wall.

There is no main Easter Egg on Nightfall: killing the Breeder means finishing the map. The full walkthrough is in [Map walkthrough](../deroulement/).

<Screenshot src={n + 'breeder/breeder.webp'} alt="The Breeder facing a player" credit={by} source={p2} />

## His attacks

- **The burst**: he gets into position (the animation is easy to see), then fires hundreds of bullets with his tail. It can kill you **in less than a second**. As soon as the animation starts, hide behind a wall, or lie down if you are up high.
- **The red mist**: he throws a red capsule into the air. It bursts and drops a toxic cloud, like Scorpion acid. Get out of the area.
- **The eggs**: he throws **2 to 4 yellow eggs** that grow, then release Hunters. Shoot them before they hatch.

**His weak spot**: the **red, glowing part of his head**. Aim for it.

## First fight (end of zone 1)

After the 4 hives of zone 1, approach the **yellow marker** on the fence. The fight has a challenge: either kill him in **90 seconds**, or destroy **10 eggs** (fewer in solo).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Set up your position">
    The best spot is **on the roof, near the locker room**. Activate the **fire trap** to cover your back. Two **mounted turrets** can be bought: one near the lockers, the other on the platform at the back. Place your own turret **under the catwalk**: it is safe there from the red mist.

    Your turrets and your I.M.S. must aim at **the small aliens around the team**, not at the Breeder.

    <Shots>
      <Screenshot src={n + 'breeder/turret-roof.webp'} alt="The mounted turret on the roof, facing the Breeder" credit={by} source={p3} />
      <Screenshot src={n + 'breeder/death-machine.webp'} alt="A player shooting the Breeder with the Death Machine" credit={by} source={p3} />
    </Shots>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot the head">
    The Weapon Specialist empties a **machine gun** into the head: the **LSAT** from the control room ($3,000) is perfect. The Maverick with armor-piercing ammo works too. In solo, the upgraded **Death Machine**, hidden behind the obstacle on the left right in front of him, is a good option.

    In a team, one player draws the Breeder's attention while the others shoot.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The egg challenge">
    Call out every egg throw and drop everything to destroy them. Also watch his health: you can make him run away **before** he has thrown enough eggs for the challenge. Don't kill him too fast.
  </Step>

  <Step title="He runs away">
    When he has lost enough health, he **runs away**. Walk where he was and pick up the cash. Zone 2 opens.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="Digital Trends" source="https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/tips-tricks-surviving-call-duty-ghosts-extinction-nightfall/">
    If the red mist is a problem for the team, put one player (ideally the Medic) in the locker room: wounded players will go heal near him.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## End of zone 2: no fight

After the last hive of zone 2, the Breeder comes back, breaks a passage to the warehouse and leaves. **Don't shoot**: save your ammo.

## Final fight (zone 4)

The fight starts when the first player crosses the warehouse's **broken wall**. Before going there, grab the **Venom-X** in the warehouse and keep its ammo: it is the key weapon of the fight. See [Get the Venom-X](../venom-x/).

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Until 50% health: stay up high">
    Same attacks as before. Stay on the **raised platform on the left** of the arena: you only have to lie down during the burst. Aliens keep coming during the whole fight: kill them while staying grouped.

    **Don't spend yet** on turrets or defenses. Save your cash for ammo, armor and heavy weapons (Death Machine, grenade launcher).
  </Step>

  <Step title="At 50%: the wave and the Rhinos">
    The Breeder leaves the arena and a big wave arrives, with **about 3 Rhinos**. This is the time to place your turrets and bring out the Death Machine or the grenade launcher. Stay near the turrets until the Rhinos are dead: the Breeder then comes back.

    {/* Rhinos: 3 (IGN), 3-4 (VideoGamesBlogger), 2-4 (PSTHC). */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Destroy the flowers that heal him">
    In front of the arena's **three rock pillars**, small bulbs with a **flower** grow. If they stay alive, the Breeder eats them to **heal himself**, about once a minute. They often grow back: destroy them as soon as you see them.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Bring him to his knees with the Venom-X">
    One **Venom-X** shot can make him **fall to his knees** for 10 to 15 seconds. Everyone then runs to him and shoots the head and the **glowing orbs** on his body. If you have a strong automatic turret, place it right in front of him, then pick it up when he gets back up.

    Repeat until he dies: the game is won, a cutscene plays and the **Epilogue** intel unlocks.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Killing the Breeder in the final arena gives the **Survived Nightfall** achievement. The first fight doesn't count: he runs away.

## Speed Slayer: kill him in under 5 minutes

The **Speed Slayer** achievement requires killing the final fight's Breeder in **under 5 minutes**.

- All 4 players enter the arena **with the Venom-X** and as much ammo as possible.
- Share cash beforehand (aim + jump to drop a $1,000 crate): in the arena, enemies are rare and pay little.
- Max out the Weapon Specialist and armor-piercing or explosive ammo, and take a machine gun (LSAT or Chain SAW).
- Fire the Venom-X **one after the other** to keep him on his knees, and aim for the head.

{/* TO CHECK: the 5-minute timer starts when the first player crosses the broken wall (VideoGamesBlogger quoting Maka91Productions, single source). */}

According to one guide, the timer starts when the first player crosses the broken wall: get ready beforehand.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="PSTHC" source="https://www.psthc.fr/unjeu/call-duty-ghosts-ps4/guide-trophees-dlc1.htm">
    Solo works too: Weapon Specialist, armor-piercing ammo and armor maxed out, an LSAT or a Chain SAW and full Venom-X ammo. One headshot to bring him to his knees, then empty your machine gun.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

The **Throttled Survival** achievement requires killing him with at least one **relic** active. See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).

Since the April 22, 2014 update, late joiners, spectators and downed players are teleported into the final fight, and a trick that prevented the Breeder from spawning was fixed.
