**Nightfall** is episode 1 of the Extinction story, the map from Ghosts' first DLC, **Onslaught** (January 28, 2014 on Xbox, February 27, 2014 on PlayStation and PC). It takes place **14 weeks after** Point of Contact, in **Alaska**, in a secret research center of the Nightfall program. The scientist **Samantha Cross** has asked you to meet her there: she has left the program and wants to bring it down.

The goal: destroy **13 hives** with the drill, spread over three zones, then kill the map's boss, the **Breeder**. There is **no escape** at the end: the game ends when the Breeder dies.

The rules shared by all maps (classes, skills, cash, drill, challenges, relics) are in [Extinction basics](../../point-of-contact/bases-extinction/).

**Before you start:**

- **4 hives** in zone 1, **4** in zone 2, **4 and then a last one** in zone 3. Within a zone, you choose the order of the hives.
- The Breeder replaces the barrier hives of Point of Contact. You meet him **three times**: a fight at the end of zone 1 (he runs away), a pass at the end of zone 2 (he breaks a wall, no fight), then the final fight in zone 4.
- The difficulty rises faster than on Point of Contact: after two or three hives, the **Scorpions** and the first **Phantom** already show up.
- Each hive has its own challenge, plus one challenge during the first fight against the Breeder: **14 challenges** in total.

## What's new in Nightfall

- The **Phantom**: an alien that looks like a Hunter, with a **blue glow**. It **teleports** short distances toward its target, hits hard, then leaves. With a **Hypno Knife**, turn it into an ally: it is the best pet on the map.
- The **Breeder**, the boss. See [Beat the Breeder](../breeder/).
- The **weapon lockers** and their **keys** (see below).
- The **Venom-X**, a free alien weapon in zone 3. See [Get the Venom-X](../venom-x/).
- The **intel** to find for the story. See [Intel](../renseignements/).

## Weapons and lockers

As on Point of Contact, there is no mystery box: weapons sit at fixed spots, at a fixed price. The confirmed prices:

| Weapon | Where | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Maverick** | Zone 1, near the start | **$1,500** (single source) |
| **LSAT** (machine gun) | Zone 1, the control room behind the rooftop hive | **$3,000** |
| **Chain SAW** (machine gun) | Zone 2, the room behind the middle hive | **$3,000** |

{/* LSAT and Chain SAW at $3,000: Digital Trends + PSTHC. LSAT name: IGN, Accelerated Ideas. Maverick $1,500: Digital Trends only (PSTHC and Accelerated Ideas say to buy it at the start, no price). */}

{/* TO CHECK: ARX-160 (zone 2 balcony, near the Treason laptop), Vector (inside a container, zone 2) and Vepr (Facility, platform near the Exfil Denied laptop): Miryu83's description only, prices nowhere in an allowed source. */}

According to one source, you can also find an **ARX-160** and a **Vector** in zone 2, and a **Vepr** in the warehouse (prices not found).

**The lockers.** In Pillage boxes, you can randomly find a **locker key**. A key opens **one locker of your choice**. Each locker holds a **random free weapon**, already fitted with attachments (often a muzzle brake and a laser sight) and a camo. If you don't like it, leave it to a teammate. The lockers are always in the same places:

- **Zone 1**: 2 lockers in the control room, behind the rooftop hive.
- **Zone 2**: 4 lockers in the room behind the middle hive.
- **Zone 3**: lots of lockers in the warehouse (14 according to one source).

{/* Lockers 2/4/14: Digital Trends only; "a lot of lockers" in the Facility: Accelerated Ideas. Locker key in Pillage + one locker of your choice: Digital Trends, IGN, Millenium. */}

## The game, zone by zone

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Zone 1: destroy the 4 hives">
    A cutscene with Samantha Cross opens the game. You start outside, in the snow.

    The first hive is easy: do it with the knife and pistol to earn cash. One hive sits **on the roof of a building, to the left of the start**: it blocks the **control room** with 2 lockers, Pillage boxes and the LSAT. Do it early, especially if you found a key.

    The zone also has a **fire trap** on that roof and an **electric fence** near another hive (prices not found).

    {/* Rooftop hive + control room: Digital Trends, IGN, Accelerated Ideas. Fire trap on that roof: Digital Trends. Electric fence near a hive: Accelerated Ideas. Trap prices: not found for Nightfall. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Zone 1: first fight against the Breeder">
    After the 4 hives, a **yellow marker** appears on the fence. Approach it: the Breeder comes out and the fight starts. When he has lost enough health, he **runs away**. Walk where he was standing and pick up the cash. The full strategy is in [Beat the Breeder](../breeder/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Zone 2, the compound: destroy 4 hives">
    You enter the **Compound**: **Pushing Ahead** achievement. Scorpions show up often and the first **Rhinos** appear, usually one per hive.

    The hive **in the middle of the zone** blocks a room with **4 lockers** and the **Chain SAW**. The last hive of the zone is a big fight: get your turrets and ammo ready.

    When it is destroyed, the Breeder comes back, but **only to break a passage** to the warehouse. You don't need to fight him: save your ammo.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Zone 3, the warehouse: 4 hives, then the last one up high">
    You enter the **Facility**: **Weapon Facility** achievement. The fights take place in a tight space: turrets are even more useful. Don't stand back to back: a single Scorpion shot would hit everyone.

    Do the **middle hive on the ground floor** first: it blocks the **Venom-X** room. See [Get the Venom-X](../venom-x/).

    After the 4 hives downstairs, a **last hive appears up high** and the room fills with fog. Samantha Cross shows up behind a window, then Captain **David Archer**, head of the program, shoots her and takes her away.

    Go up next to the hive and turn your turrets **toward the hive**: they shoot the aliens climbing up. A Rhino arrives near the end. When this hive is destroyed, a **wall collapses** and opens the exit.

    <Screenshot src={n + 'map/samantha.webp'} alt="Samantha Cross behind the window, in the warehouse" credit={by} source={p4} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Zone 4: the final fight">
    Outside, you see Archer escaping in a vehicle. The **final fight against the Breeder** starts when the first player crosses the broken wall. The last intel computer, **The Ark**, is in this arena.

    When the Breeder dies, the game is won: a cutscene plays and the **Epilogue** intel unlocks. See [Beat the Breeder](../breeder/).

    {/* Fight starts when the first player crosses the broken wall: VideoGamesBlogger (quoting Maka91Productions). Archer escaping in a vehicle: Miryu83, VideoGamesBlogger. */}
  </Step>
</Checklist>

Nightfall was changed by the April 2014 updates: Hardcore difficulty in solo was reworked, Phantom melee damage was lowered in solo, and players who join late are teleported into the final fight. Launch videos may show the earlier state.
