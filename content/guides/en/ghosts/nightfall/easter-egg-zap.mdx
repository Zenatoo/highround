A small Easter Egg just for fun, in the spirit of Point of Contact's [LOL Easter Egg: the teddy bears](../../point-of-contact/easter-egg-lol/). If you shoot four electric poles in the right order, the word **"Zap!!!"** appears and the aliens you kill **explode into snowmen**. It is purely visual: no achievement, no bonus.

It is done in **zone 1**, in solo or co-op.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Spot the 4 poles that spark">
    In zone 1, look at the top of the **electric poles**. On **four** of them, a tank (like a small barrel) fixed at the top gives off **sparks** from time to time. The chosen poles change every game: there are about ten possible spots.

    <Screenshot src={n + 'zap/pole.webp'} alt="An electric pole giving off sparks, seen through the sight" credit={by} source={p5} />

    {/* 4 poles: Millenium, Just Sora (HkfkHkay0hQ: "4 réservoirs", "l'emplacement des 10"), Accelerated Ideas says "4 or 5". */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Note the order of the sparks">
    The four poles don't spark at the same time. Watch them for a while and **note the order** in which they light up. This order also changes every game.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Shoot each tank while it sparks">
    Shoot the tanks **in that order**, each one **at the moment it sparks**. A precise weapon helps: the guides use the **Maverick**, which you can buy near the start.

    {/* Order + timing: Just Sora (shoot in the order they flash), Accelerated Ideas (order they spark), Millenium (shoot while sparking). TO CHECK: whether a specific weapon is required. Accelerated Ideas says to use the Maverick; JokerAlex 21 (wx82Ol-uIAY) has a chapter "Weapon needed"; Millenium's screenshots show the Maverick. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Zap!!!: the aliens turn into snowmen">
    If everything is right, **"Zap!!!"** appears in the middle of the screen. The aliens you kill afterward explode and leave a **snowman head** on the ground.

    {/* TO CHECK: duration. Millenium: until the end of the current wave; Accelerated Ideas: about 5 minutes. */}

    The effect lasts until the end of the wave according to one source, about 5 minutes according to another.

  </Step>
