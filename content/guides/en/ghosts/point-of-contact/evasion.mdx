When the last hive of the cabins is destroyed, a **bomb** appears behind it. Once armed, you have **4 minutes** to get back to the starting point of the map, where the helicopter is waiting for you. The aliens attack nonstop during the whole run.

Succeeding at the escape ends the map and unlocks the Made it Out Alive achievement. Other achievements depend on it: see [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/). To get here, see [Map walkthrough](../deroulement/).

## Prepare for the run

Don't rush to the bomb. The aliens only attack hard once the countdown starts: prepare the way back first.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Place turrets along the way">
    If you have **Sentry Guns**, place them at the entrance of the cabins zone, facing the way back.

    PSTHC recommends a full team setup:

    - **4 turrets** at the very start of the cabins zone, in a half circle;
    - **4 turrets** at the start of the city: 2 near the car parked in front of the van with the machine gun, 1 on the left, and 1 at the back to keep the **2 Rhinos** that spawn there busy.

    If you have a lot of cash, also activate the electric fences and fire traps on the route.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Call the attack chopper">
    If your team has already put **$5,000** into the attack chopper computer, put in the remaining **$1,000** right before arming the bomb: the helicopter will cover your escape. See [Attack chopper, Hypno Knife and other secrets](../secrets/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Equip the whole team">
    Right before arming, place for all 4 players: **Feral Instincts** (the wolf icon), **armor** and **armor-piercing ammo**. **Team Boosters** also give a bit of speed.

    The **Chain SAW** ($3,000, on the barn floor) wreaks havoc, but it slows you down: switch to your pistol to run.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Arm the bomb together">
    All players must press the action button **at the same time** in front of the bomb. The **4-minute** countdown starts.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The run to the helicopter

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Run with your pistol out">
    Take out your **pistol**: you run faster with it, especially if its skill tree is upgraded. Only stop to kill the aliens that block you. Kill the **Rhinos** first.
  </Step>

  <Step title="First meteor: the city entrance">
    At the city entrance, a **meteor** falls between the buildings and blocks the way. Place ammo and armor, and hold on until it **bursts**: the way opens.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Second meteor: the exit toward the motel">
    Run to the exit of the city, toward the motel. A second meteor blocks the road. Same thing: place turrets and a Feral Instincts again if you can, and hold on until it bursts.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Step into the circle of light">
    Rush to the spot of the **first hive**. The helicopter waits above a **circle of blue light**. When all the players still alive are inside it, they board: the match is won.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Avoiding the meteors

The meteors are not mandatory. Two players explain that a **very fast** escape prevents them from appearing: Just Sora escapes in about **80 seconds** without seeing a meteor, and the speedrunner Eric Barnes uses a method that avoids the **second meteor**. Their texts don't detail the method: watch Just Sora's video above.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="GamesRadar" source="https://www.gamesradar.com/call-duty-ghosts-extinction-guide/7/">
    For the Sprinter achievement, you must reach the helicopter with at least **1 min 30** left on the timer: so you have 2 min 30 for the whole run. Prepare everything before arming the bomb.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="PSTHC" source="https://www.psthc.fr/unjeu/call-duty-ghosts-ps3/guide-trophees.htm">
    While you hold off a meteor, keep checking whether it is destroyed: set off again as soon as the way is clear.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
