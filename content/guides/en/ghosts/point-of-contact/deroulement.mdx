**Point of Contact** is the first Extinction map, included in the base game. A helicopter drops you and your team, with the drill, in an American countryside overrun by aliens. You cross **three zones** destroying their hives, then you plant a bomb and run to the helicopter.

There is no boss, no intel and no main Easter Egg on this map. The mode's rules (drill, challenges, skills, cash) are in [Extinction basics](../bases-extinction/).

| Zone | Hives to drill | End of the zone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Motel** | 4 (the first is set) | A barrier hive, destroyed with the helicopter: $3,000 |
| **City** | 3 | A second barrier hive: $3,000 |
| **Cabin** (the cabins) | 5 (the last one lasts 5 minutes) | The bomb, then the escape |

That makes **12 hives** to drill and **2 barrier hives**. Each drilled hive starts a challenge.

## The three zones

<Checklist>
  <Step title="The first hive">
    The helicopter drops you right next to the first hive. Take the drill and place it on it. This hive works as a tutorial: there are few aliens and the challenge is often simple, like getting only melee kills or taking no damage. Your knife and your pistol are enough.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Motel: the other three hives">
    You then choose the order of the next **three hives**. The active ones change from one match to the next.

    Before placing the drill, walk around the zone: search the bags and crates, and spot the weapons on the ground. The **VKS** sniper rifle ($1,500) is on a rock, on the right side of the zone. It is used for the [LOL Easter Egg: the teddy bears](../easter-egg-lol/).

    The zone has two kinds of **traps**: **electric fences** ($500) near the center hive, in the parking lot, and a **fire trap** at the back, near the motel sign ($750 according to one source). Activate them right after placing the drill.

    {/* TO CHECK: fire trap 750 = PSTHC only. Fence 500 = PSTHC + IGN. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="The first barrier hive">
    At the end of the street, a **barrier hive** blocks the way. The drill can't do anything against it: as soon as the fourth hive is destroyed, an **attack chopper** arrives to destroy it. Its health is shown on the left of the screen.

    - **Scorpions** come to spit acid at the helicopter. If it is hit too much, it flies away and the fight lasts much longer. **Kill the Scorpions first**: they always land in the same places, on the rooftops, the rocks and the sign.
    - Pick up the **propane tanks** (the white bottles) and throw them at the hive: they stick to it. Shoot them to make them explode.

    When the hive falls, finish off the remaining aliens. A pile of **$3,000** appears. **Spend your cash before picking it up**: you can't carry more than $6,000. Don't forget to take the drill again, then enter the city.
  </Step>

  <Step title="City: three hives">
    Entering the city unlocks the City Dweller achievement. The hives take longer and the aliens are more varied: Scouts, Scorpions, **Hunters**, **Rhinos** and **Seekers**, which come out of red **meteors** fallen from the sky.

    - A **Rhino** waits on a rooftop at the entrance of the city. You can shoot at it to make it come down: it gives $1,000.
    - A **light machine gun** at **$3,000** is placed on a plank, at the top of a small ladder, near the entrance.
    - The **attack chopper** computer is in a building in the city center. See [Attack chopper, Hypno Knife and other secrets](../secrets/).

    {/* TO CHECK: Rhino on a City rooftop = GamesRadar + gamepressure; IGN says the first Rhino only appears after the City. LMG = M27-IAR? (Military.com names it for PoC, ibxtoycat's weapon list has it; no source ties it to this spot). */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="The second barrier hive">
    This barrier hive is between two buildings, on a wide street. There are a lot of **Scorpions** and they have many places to climb. Place turrets and I.M.S. along the street, and use the electric fences. Don't stay in the acid puddles: they eat away your health and armor.

    When the hive is almost destroyed, **Rhinos** arrive: the whole team deals with them, then goes back to the other aliens. According to IGN, if the fight drags on (more than about 10 minutes), Rhinos keep spawning one by one.

    {/* TO CHECK: hidden timer and Rhino stream after  10 min = IGN only. */}

    A new pile of **$3,000** appears. Take the drill again and go through the alley (another Rhino waits for you there) to reach the cabins.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Cabin: five hives">
    Entering the zone unlocks the Cabin Fever achievement. There are **five hives** and no barrier hive.

    - Start with the **barn** hive, on the first floor: it is the building at the back, facing the bridge you go under, to the right of the entrance. It has an open garage on the right and a **ladder** up to the floor. The **Chain SAW**, a very powerful machine gun, costs **$3,000** there.
    - Two other hives are around the buildings. The third one is always in front of the big building with the **stone wall**, next to a broken fence. Once destroyed, it opens the way to a platform and a large open area.
    - The fourth hive is on the **plateau**, against the cliff.

    {/* TO CHECK: GamesRadar alone puts an attack chopper laptop in the cellar of the stone-wall building (passage along the stone wall). Other sources put the laptop in the City. */}

    According to GamesRadar, an attack chopper computer is also in the cellar of the stone-wall building.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The last hive: 5 minutes">
    The fifth and last hive is the hardest: the drill must hold out for **5 minutes**, against 3 at most for the others. A huge wave arrives, mostly **Scorpions**. Fill up on ammo and armor before placing the drill and set up everything you have.

    When it is destroyed, the **bomb** appears behind it. What comes next is in [The final escape](../evasion/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="Digital Trends" source="https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/call-duty-ghosts-extinction-guide/">
    The best time to explore and buy is just before the last hive of a zone. As soon as the helicopter is announced, go to the barrier hive and place ammo, support and turrets before it starts shooting.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="IGN" source="https://www.ign.com/wikis/call-of-duty-ghosts/Point_of_Contact">
    In the Motel, complete as many challenges as you can to level up a skill tree fast. As a team, split the trees between you: only one player needs to level up ammo or armor.
  </Tip>
</Tips>
