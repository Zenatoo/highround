A small secret of the Motel, the first zone: by shooting the motel sign with a sniper rifle to write **"LOL"**, the aliens you kill turn into **little golden teddy bears** for a while.

It is **purely visual**: no cash, no weapon, no achievement. It works in solo and in co-op, and one player is enough.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Buy the VKS">
    After the first hive, go to the **right side** of the zone, before the motel. The **VKS** sniper rifle is placed on a **rock** (a mound of earth): climb on it to buy it. It costs **$1,500**.

    <Screenshot src={l + 'vks.webp'} alt="The VKS placed on the mound, to buy for $1,500" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go in front of the motel sign">
    Toward the end of the zone, stand facing the lit sign **"GIL'S LODGE MOTEL"**.

    <Screenshot src={l + 'sign.webp'} alt="The GIL'S LODGE MOTEL sign, seen with the VKS" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Turn off the lit letters">
    Some letters of the sign are lit or blinking, in front and behind. Shoot them once to **turn them off**. According to Millenium, these are the **O** then the **L** of the word **LODGE**.

    {/* Pre-step: Miryu83 forum ("lettres allumées devant et derrière le panneau", any weapon) + Millenium (shoot the O then the L of LODGE once to turn off the blinking letter). Exact letters: Millenium only. */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Write LOL on LODGE with the VKS">
    With the **VKS**, shoot the letters of the word **LODGE** in this order, in less than about **5 seconds**:

    1. the **L**;
    2. the **O**;
    3. the **same L** again.

    If it works, the word **LOL** is displayed for a short moment at the top of the screen. If not, start over: it sometimes takes several tries.

    {/* All three letters on LODGE: Miryu83 + Millenium. Accelerated Ideas alone says the first L is the one of the first word. 5-second limit: Miryu83 only. */}

    <Screenshot src={l + 'lol.webp'} alt="The word LOL displayed at the top of the screen" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enjoy the teddy bears">
    {/* TO CHECK: "any kill method works, mortars included" = Accelerated Ideas only. */}

    Every alien you kill now bursts into **little golden teddy bears** that glow. They are harmless. According to one source, every way of killing works, even mortar shots.

    The effect lasts a while: **about 2 minutes** according to one source, "a few dozen seconds" according to another.

    {/* TO CHECK: duration: Miryu83 "2 minutes", Accelerated Ideas "a couple of minutes", Millenium "quelques dizaines de secondes". */}

    <Screenshot src={l + 'golden_aliens.webp'} alt="Killed aliens turned into little golden plush toys" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>
</Checklist>
