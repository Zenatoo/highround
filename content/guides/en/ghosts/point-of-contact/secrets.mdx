Point of Contact has no main Easter Egg, but a few hidden items help a lot. The small visual Easter Egg of the motel has its own guide: [LOL Easter Egg: the teddy bears](../easter-egg-lol/).

## The attack chopper

A **laptop** placed in a building in the **city center** (the second zone), a burned-out one-story building, calls an **attack chopper** that shoots at the aliens for you.

- It costs **$6,000** in total, but is paid in **$1,000** installments, and **any player** can pay. Share the cost across the team.
- According to IGN, it is the same helicopter as the one at the barrier hives: you can't call it during a barrier hive fight.

{/* City laptop: Digital Trends ("burned out second story building in the second area"), IGN ("inside a building in the center of town"), JCbackfire video description ("located in the city"). "Same chopper as the barrier one": IGN only. */}

{/* TO CHECK: GamesRadar puts a laptop in the cellar of the big stone-wall building in the Cabin area; TheCityofZ's video description talks about "laptops" (plural). A second laptop is not confirmed. */}

According to GamesRadar, a computer is also in the **cellar of the big stone-wall building**, in the cabins zone: take the passage along the stone wall, and the computer is against the back wall. GamesRadar advises putting **$5,000** in right away, then the remaining **$1,000** right before arming the bomb, so the helicopter covers the escape. See [The final escape](../evasion/).

## The Hypno Knife

The **Hypno Knife** is a **single-use** throwing knife. Throw it at an alien: it becomes your **ally** and fights the others, like a pet.

- You find it **at random** by searching bags and crates. It is rare, but you can find several in one match.
- Save it for a **strong** alien. An allied **Scorpion** holds up very well against several enemies. Don't waste it on a Scout.
- In the Armory, the **Master Scavenger** upgrade (added with Exodus) makes you find Hypno Knives in searches. See [Extinction basics](../bases-extinction/).

{/* Hypno Knife: Digital Trends (single use, random, several per game, Scorpion), Accelerated Ideas (rare, single use), Millenium (in search spots; Master Scavenger upgrade). */}

## Giving cash to a teammate

Hold **aim** and **jump** for a few seconds: a crate of **$1,000** drops in front of you, with your name on it. A teammate just has to pick it up. Handy to help a player buy a weapon or to pay for the attack chopper.

{/* Military.com ("3 seconds"), Accelerated Ideas, Just Sora video ygqvBhivN8c. */}

## The propane tanks

**Propane tanks** (white bottles) lie around the map. Pick them up and throw them at a **barrier hive**: they stick to it. Shoot them to make them explode and damage the hive. One of the challenges also asks you to kill aliens with these tanks (5 in solo).
