**Extinction** is the co-op mode of Call of Duty: Ghosts, for 1 to 4 players. It is not a Zombies mode: you fight aliens, the **Cryptids**, and there are no rounds. You move forward by destroying their **hives** with a **drill**, zone after zone, until the end of the map.

These rules are **the same on all five Extinction maps**. This guide explains them once and for all. It describes the game **in its final state**, after the last update of August 2014. The 2013 videos sometimes show an older game: no Teeth, only 5 relics, 5 prestiges at most.

## The five maps

| Map | Content | What comes with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Point of Contact** | Base game (November 2013) | The starting map: hives, barrier hives with a helicopter, final escape |
| **Nightfall** | Onslaught pack | Lockers and locker keys, the **Venom-X**, the **Phantom**, a boss (the **Breeder**), intel to find |
| **Mayday** | Devastation pack | Schematics and craftable items, doors to drill, the **Seeder**, a boss (the **Kraken**). Teeth and the Armory arrive the same day, for all players |
| **Awakening** | Invasion pack | The **Ark** attachment, four new Cryptids, a final escape |
| **Exodus** | Nemesis pack | The last episode: defend a rocket launch against the **Ancestors** |

Each map has its own progression guide: [Map walkthrough](../deroulement/) for Point of Contact, then [Map walkthrough](../../nightfall/deroulement/) (Nightfall), [Map walkthrough](../../mayday/deroulement/) (Mayday), [Map walkthrough](../../awakening/deroulement/) (Awakening) and [Map walkthrough](../../exodus/deroulement/) (Exodus).

## Before the match: your loadout

Before starting a match, you choose your **loadout** ("Loadout" in the menu): **one item in each of the 6 categories**. During the match, you deploy these items by paying with your cash, one at a time. None of our sources confirms their price.

| Category (in-game name) | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Class** | Weapon Specialist, Tank, Engineer, Medic |
| **Pistol** | P226, .44 Magnum, M9A1, MP-443 Grach |
| **Ammo** | Standard, Stun, Incendiary, Explosive, Armor-Piercing |
| **Team Support** | Team Explosives, Feral Instincts, Armor, Team Boosters, Random Supplies |
| **Strike Package** | I.M.S., Mortar Strike, Trinity Rocket, Sentry Gun, Vulture (drone) |
| **Equalizer** | Portable Minigun (turret), Grenade Turret, Crowd Control (riot shield), MK32 (grenade launcher), Death Machine |

Ammo and support items are placed on the ground for the whole team: a special ammo crate replaces your bullets until they run out, then your normal bullets come back. **Feral Instincts** lets you see aliens through walls and makes you faster, **Team Boosters** reloads and repairs the drill faster.

{/* All 4 classes by level 10: Digital Trends. Exact ranks (Tank 3, Engineer 5, Medic 10): Millenium unlock list (launch state). */}

You unlock these items by leveling up. All four classes are available at level 10: Weapon Specialist from the start, Tank at level 3, Engineer at 5 and Medic at 10.

### The classes

| Class | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **Weapon Specialist** | More bullet damage (up to +50%), faster handling and reloading, less recoil |
| **Tank** | Up to +100% health and +100% melee damage |
| **Engineer** | Drill armored and repaired faster, more cash per hive, stronger traps that are partly refunded, bigger wallet |
| **Medic** | Revives faster, heals the team, runs faster, immune to Scorpion gas at level 4 |

Each class also has a **class ability**, to buy once and for all in the Armory (below). In a match, press **up on the d-pad twice** to use it, then wait for the cooldown:

- **Engineer**: a stasis field that Cryptids cannot enter;
- **Tank**: draws the aliens in, makes you invincible for a while, and your melee hits stun them;
- **Weapon Specialist**: an adrenaline rush, more damage and full magazines, with a small bonus for the team;
- **Medic**: an area that heals and revives allies.

### The pistols

You always keep your pistol. All four share the same skill tree: at level 4, you can carry **two primary weapons** on top of the pistol; at level 5, you have **two pistols** (akimbo).

## Cash

Currency is shown in dollars ($). You start with **$500**. You can carry at most **$6,000**, or **$8,000** with an Engineer at level 5.

- Every alien you kill gives cash (alien table below). According to Millenium, a critical hit or a melee attack gives **x1.5**, a simple assist **x0.5**.
- You spend your cash on weapons, traps and items from your loadout.
- To **give $1,000** to a teammate: hold aim and jump. A crate drops in front of you, and they just have to pick it up.

## The drill, the hives and the challenges

Your team carries **a single drill**. Place it on a hive: it starts digging and the aliens come to destroy it. Defend it until the hive explodes. The drill can be damaged: hold the action button next to it to **repair** it. **If it is destroyed, the match is lost.**

- The **first hive** of a map is set, then the team chooses the order of the next ones.
- Each hive takes a bit more time than the previous one.
- When you enter a new zone, the aliens only really attack once the drill is placed: take advantage of this to explore and search.
- Each zone ends with a bigger obstacle. On Point of Contact, it is a **barrier hive**, too big for the drill: a helicopter comes to destroy it. The other maps have their own obstacles (a boss, doors to drill...).

### Challenges and skill points

At each drilled hive, the game gives the team a **challenge**: take no damage, get melee kills, kill a certain number of aliens with one type of weapon, keep the drill above 50%... In solo, the numbers are smaller.

Each destroyed hive gives **1 skill point**, and a completed challenge gives **1 more point**.

### Skills

Points are spent in **6 trees**: Class, Pistol, Ammo, Team Support, Strike Package and Equalizer. Each tree restarts at **level 1** every match and goes up to **level 5**. The last levels cost more than one point.

Open the menu with **Back** (Xbox) or **Select** (PlayStation). **The game does not pause**: do it somewhere safe.

## Weapons, searching and traps

- **No mystery box.** Weapons are placed at fixed spots, always at the same price (always more than $1,000). A weapon you can afford is outlined in blue, in yellow if you don't have enough cash.
- Buying a weapon **replaces your primary weapon**, and its attachments disappear with it.
- **Searchable objects** (small bags, crates, boxes) are searched by holding the action button ("Search"). Only one player can search each object. You find ammo, cash, attachments (laser sight, holographic sight, thermal scope, grip, muzzle brake, rapid fire), grenades (Semtex, flares, Trophy) and sometimes a **Hypno Knife**.
- One attachment per slot: taking a new one destroys the old one. Give the ones you don't need to a teammate instead.
- The **traps** on the map (electric fences, fire traps on Point of Contact) are paid for with your cash. The Engineer gets part of it back and, since August 2014, no longer takes damage from his own traps.
- The **Hypno Knife** is a single-use throwing knife: the alien it hits becomes your ally. See [Attack chopper, Hypno Knife and other secrets](../secrets/).

The next maps add more weapons: **locker keys** and pre-equipped weapons (from Nightfall), **Venom-X** alien weapons (Nightfall and Mayday), **craftable items** (from Mayday), the **Ark** attachment (Awakening) and the **NX-1 Disruptor** (Exodus). It is all in each map's guides.

## Teeth and the Armory

**Teeth** are a **permanent** currency. They are used in the **Armory** ("ARMORY: Get Upgrades", in the loadout menu) to buy upgrades that you keep forever, on all maps. They arrived with the April 3, 2014 update, at the same time as Mayday.

You earn a Tooth:

- by finishing a map with at least one active relic;
- by finishing a map with a player who had never finished it;
- the first time you reach levels 2, 6, 11 and 21;
- with each new prestige;
- through the **bonus jackpot** by destroying barrier hives: **one bonus Tooth per day** since August 2014 (at first, up to 3 per week).

You can see your number of Teeth in the loadout menu, or with Back/Select in a match.

**Class abilities** cost **15 Teeth** each. Other upgrades make turrets sturdier, armor-piercing ammo stronger, or give you a locker key from the start. Awakening and Exodus added more. Keeping compatible attachments when you change weapons costs **10 Teeth**.

## Levels, prestige and relics

Extinction has **its own levels**, separate from multiplayer. At level 30, you can go **prestige**: you restart at level 1, but you keep what you unlocked. There were 5 prestiges at launch, then 5 more with each pack: **25 in total**.

Each prestige gives a **relic slot**. A **relic** is a handicap you choose before the match: the game gets harder, but you earn more experience. You can activate as many as you have prestiges.

{/* TO CHECK: "+20% XP per relic, up to +200%" = Military.com only. */}

| Relic | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Take More Damage | The aliens hurt you more |
| Pistols Only | No weapon purchases (no locker, no Venom weapon) |
| Smaller Wallet | You earn and carry less cash |
| Mortal | No class |
| Do Less Damage | You hurt the aliens less |
| Fragile | Health is slow to regenerate, none while sprinting, fall damage |
| Stand Your Ground | You move slower, no reloading while sprinting |
| No Machines | No Strike Package, no Equalizer |
| Limited Ammo | Less ammo in crates and picked-up weapons |
| Earn Your Keep | No Ammo or Team Support, no searching or crafting, cannot take what others place |

The first five have existed since launch, the other five since the June 3, 2014 update. Finishing a map with an active relic earns a Tooth.

## Difficulties and Chaos mode

- **Casual**, **Regular** and **Hardcore**. Casual and Hardcore arrived in April 2014. The achievements tied to map progression require **Regular or Hardcore**.
- **Chaos**: an endless mode added in April 2014. The waves never stop and a **combo counter** goes up with each kill. At certain thresholds, you earn perks and bonuses drop: frozen combo, 30 more seconds, automatic revive, class abilities for 15 seconds, special ammo for the team... It exists on at least Point of Contact, Nightfall and Mayday.

## The aliens

The basic aliens are present on all maps.

| Alien | Good to know | Cash |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scout** | The most common, fast and fragile | $60 |
| **Hunter** | Armored head: it soaks up shots from the front | $200 |
| **Scorpion** | Spits acid from far away, from rooftops and walls; yellow or green marks on its back, its weak point | $200 |
| **Seeker** | Comes out of **meteors** that fall from the sky, red marks, explodes when it reaches you | $100 |
| **Rhino** | Heavily armored, it charges. Keep your distance | $1,000 |
| **Leper** | Only appears during the "find and kill the Leper in 30 seconds" challenge | $60 or $100 |

The packs added more Cryptids: the **Phantom** that teleports and the **Breeder** (Nightfall), the **Seeder** and the **Kraken** (Mayday), the **Bomber**, the **Gargoyle** and the **Mammoth** (Awakening), and the **Ancestors** (Exodus). They are explained in their maps' guides.

## The end of a map

There is **no main Easter Egg** in Extinction. Each map ends with a final objective: an **escape** to reach before the timer runs out (Point of Contact, Awakening), a **boss** (Nightfall, Mayday) or the **Ancestors** (Exodus). For Point of Contact, see [The final escape](../evasion/).
