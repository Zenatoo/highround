Mayday's **intel** are files that unlock story cutscenes. They show **Samantha Cross**, Archer's prisoner on the ship, whose mutation into a Cryptid is progressing. You can watch them again in the Extinction menu, under **Intel**.

<Screenshot src={r + 'samantha.webp'} alt="Samantha Cross as a prisoner, in an intel cutscene" credit={by} source={srcS} />

There are **11 intel** in total. Finding them all unlocks **Inquisitive Mind**, with no difficulty requirement. Each intel you pick up on the map also gives you **$500**. A counter in the top right of the screen tracks your progress.

| Type | How many | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Automatic | 2 | **Prologue** (you get it by owning the DLC) and **Epilogue** (kill the Kraken) |
| Random | 4 | In the **small gray boxes** to search |
| Fixed | 5 | **Laptops** at specific spots |

Older guides mention 3 or 5 random intel: the complete list of files counts 4.

## The 4 random intel

They are called **Terra Incognita**, **Beasts of War**, **Medusa** and **Desperate Allies**. They unlock **in that order**, no matter where you find them.

- Search the **small gray boxes** (hold the action button) in the map's zones. The intel drops on the ground: pick it up.
- Their location changes each game, and they are **rare**.
- Only one player can pick up each intel.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="Maka91Productions" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vKalW3bRf2Y">
    Solo, search everything in the first zone, then let yourself die during the first drill and start over: it's faster to make them drop.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## The 5 laptops

**To check:** these locations come from a single text (Maka91Productions, reused by other sites). The Sleeper Agent one is confirmed by a second guide.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Sleeper Agent (Entrance)">
    In the first zone, drill the **top door**. Shoot the **window**, go outside onto the **pipe** and follow it to the left: the laptop is at the end. Move slowly, it's easy to fall.

    {/* TO CHECK: French name. Miryu83 writes « Espions en sommeil ». Spot also in CheeryChill's Steam guide (door at the top of the ramp, window, pipe). */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Encrypted (Entrance, after the tentacle)">
    On a **balcony**, near the **Tesla Trap** and **Hypno Trap** schematics table, right after the tentacle.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Witchcraft (Cargo Area)">
    In the **middle of the cargo area**, on crates. Jump from crate to crate to climb up.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Mutiny (Top Deck)">
    In the deck's **lab**, at the back of the room, **behind the big round column**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The Guardian (last room)">
    In the last zone before the Kraken, on the **walkway**, on the side **opposite the entrance**.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

To know when you pass through each zone, see [Map walkthrough](../deroulement/). The Epilogue unlocks at the end of [Beat the Kraken](../kraken/).
