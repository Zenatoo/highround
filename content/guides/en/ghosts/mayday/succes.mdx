The Devastation DLC adds **10 achievements** for Mayday, worth **250 G** on Xbox (trophies on PlayStation). Check them off as you go: your progress stays on your device.

Except for **Inquisitive Mind** and **Egg-stra Devastation!**, their text requires **Regular or Hardcore** difficulty: on Casual, they don't unlock.

{/* Names, French names, gamerscore, grades: vgtimes, Cheat Code Central, Platinum Pursuit, psthc (see _shared Achievements [nightfall]). Difficulty wording: VideoGamesBlogger + Maka91Productions descriptions (Egg-stra Devastation! text has no difficulty either). */}

<Checklist>
  <Step title="The Belly of the Beast">
    Bronze, 20 G. Reach the **cargo area** (Cargo Area). See [Map walkthrough](../deroulement/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Come Up For Air">
    Bronze, 30 G. Reach the **Top Deck**, after the transport crate.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Survived Mayday">
    Bronze, 30 G. Kill the **Kraken** for the first time. See [Beat the Kraken](../kraken/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Upping the Ante">
    Silver, 30 G. Kill the Kraken with at least **one relic** active. Relics are explained in [Extinction basics](../../point-of-contact/bases-extinction/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Mayday Completionist">
    Silver, 30 G. Complete **all the challenges** of the game, then kill the Kraken. One failed challenge and this game is lost for it. Don't forget the **tentacle** challenge (hive 3) and, according to one filmed run, the one on a long cargo area door.

    {/* TO CHECK: a challenge on the long Cargo Area door = Eric Barnes gQ0Vd9fHSoI chapters only ("Spend $6,000"). */}

    You can do this one, Upping the Ante and Survived Mayday in the same game.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Inquisitive Mind">
    Bronze, 30 G. Find all **11 intel**. See [Intel](../renseignements/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deforestation">
    Bronze, 20 G. Destroy **15 Seeder turrets** in a single game.

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="tech" author="Video Games Blogger" source="https://www.videogamesblogger.com/2014/04/03/call-of-duty-ghosts-devastation-trophies-guide.htm">
        Take the Weapon Specialist, a fast-firing pistol and explosive ammo once upgraded. Attack the turrets while they are reloading.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Big Game Trapper">
    Bronze, 20 G. Turn a **Rhino** into an ally with a **Hypno Trap**. The Hypno Knife doesn't work on Rhinos. Place the trap in front of an incoming Rhino, or lure it toward the trap with your teammates. The schematic is explained in [Schematics and craftable items](../constructions/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Egg-stra Devastation!">
    Bronze, 20 G. Destroy the 4 hidden eggs on the Devastation multiplayer maps, then a hive on Mayday. See [The map's secrets](../secrets/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="The Architect">
    Bronze, 20 G. Craft **all 5 items**: Sticky Flare, Pipe Bomb, Hypno Trap, Tesla Trap and Venom. See [Schematics and craftable items](../constructions/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>
