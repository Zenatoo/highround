**Mayday** is episode 2 of Extinction, released with the **Devastation** DLC: on April 3, 2014 on Xbox 360 and Xbox One, and on May 8, 2014 on PS3, PS4 and PC. Your team boards an abandoned **Chinese research ship**, adrift in the South Pacific, to find David Archer and Samantha Cross. The ship is overrun by Cryptids.

This guide follows the map from the entrance to the boss. The rules shared by all maps (classes, skill points, cash, drill, challenges, Teeth) are in [Extinction basics](../../point-of-contact/bases-extinction/).

**What's new on Mayday:**

- You also have to **drill doors**, not just hives.
- **Crafting**: you pick up schematics and parts to build items. See [Schematics and craftable items](../constructions/).
- The **Venom-X** is crafted, in four variants. See [The Venom-X and its variants](../venom-x/).
- A new enemy, the **Seeder**, and a giant boss, the **Kraken**.

## The route at a glance

The map is played in one direction, in three zones, then a finale:

| Zone | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Entrance** | Hives 1 and 2, some doors, then hive 3 with the Kraken's **tentacle** |
| **Cargo Area** | Hives 4 to 6 and several doors, then the crate that takes you up to the deck |
| **Top Deck** | Hives 7 to 11, outside then inside the lab |
| Finale | Defend the gas pipes, then the **Kraken** |

In two full runs filmed in 2020 and 2024, there are **11 hives** and **5 drilled doors** before the gas.

## The doors to drill

On Mayday, many doors can't be bought: you set the **drill** on them, like on a hive. Drilling is shorter, but it triggers a **wave of Cryptids** and gives **no skill point**.

Some doors are on the main path. Others are off to the side and hide crafting parts and boxes to search.

{/* TO CHECK: Eric Barnes' 2020 run shows a challenge ("Spend $6,000") on one Cargo Area door, the one his 2024 run calls the "Long Door". Digital Trends says doors give no skill point. */}

A longer-to-drill door in the Cargo Area also seems to give a challenge.

## The Seeder, the new enemy

The **Seeder** ("piégeur" in French guides) looks like a Scorpion with a shell that glows **yellow**. It shoots from a distance and throws pods. Each pod becomes a **plant turret** that shoots you and hurts a lot. It can also climb walls. Its turrets fire fast: don't stay in their line of fire.

## Zone 1: Entrance

<Checklist>
  <Step title="The start">
    The path is straight: there's only one way to go. A pair of **lockers** is there from the start. Their keys are found by searching, at random.

    Go up the stairs on the right: on the toolbox, there are the schematics for the **Sticky Flare** and the **Pipe Bomb**.

    The first two side doors hide parts and boxes to search. Don't force them right away: each door starts a wave without giving a point.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hives 1 and 2, then the door">
    Destroy the **first two hives**, then drill the door that blocks the way.

    The door at the top of the zone leads to the **Sleeper Agent** intel. See [Intel](../renseignements/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hive 3: the tentacle">
    The third hive is in a small room with a raised **walkway**. When you drill, a **giant tentacle** from the Kraken breaks through the hull and strikes. It also spits out Seekers and a **toxic cloud** that hurts and blurs your vision. Keep shooting it: it eventually pulls back and the wave ends.

    Get ready **before** you set the drill:

    - One player buys the room's **fixed turret**.
    - Place your automatic turrets on the **upper walkway**, not on the floor: the tentacle destroys the ones on the floor.
    - Turn on the **fire trap** near the entrance door.

    This tentacle counts as Mayday's "barrier" for the **Teeth** bonus pool, the Armory currency: destroying it can earn you a bonus Tooth (one per day since the August 2014 update).

    {/* Teeth from the tentacle: Attack of the Fanboy (Bonus Pool, April 2014: up to 3 per week). Final state "1 bonus tooth per day": mp1st 4 Aug 2014 update notes (see _shared Game-wide systems). */}

    <Tips>
      <Tip type="tech" author="Digital Trends" source="https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/tips-tricks-surviving-call-duty-ghosts-extinction-mayday/">
        Put one or more turrets on the upper walkway and keep someone ready to buy the fixed turret before you set the drill.
      </Tip>
    </Tips>
  </Step>

  <Step title="After the tentacle: the traps">
    Right after the tentacle, a table holds the schematics for the **Hypno Trap** and the **Tesla Trap**. The **Encrypted** intel is on a balcony nearby.

    {/* TO CHECK: Digital Trends says the next door spawns a Rhino. Single source. */}

    Drill the next door, then go into the cargo area. According to one guide, a **Rhino** is waiting for you behind it.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Zone 2: Cargo Area

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Open the cargo area">
    Entering the cargo area unlocks the **The Belly of the Beast** achievement.

    It's a big open hold, full of crates, with several doors to drill. Drill them, survive the waves and search everything: parts, weapons and schematics, including the one for the **Venom-X**.

    The **Witchcraft** intel is in the middle of the cargo area, on crates.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Hives 4 to 6">
    Destroy the three hives in the cargo area. The order of the hives and doors can change from one game to the next.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get in the crate">
    After the last hive, the game asks you to get into a **transport crate**. When the whole team is inside **with the drill**, you are carried up to the deck automatically.

    You **can't go back** to the entrance or the cargo area. Before going up, search what's left: no Cryptid attacks while you explore.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Zone 3: Top Deck

<Checklist>
  <Step title="The deck">
    Reaching the deck unlocks **Come Up For Air**. Everything here is outside, with a raised platform. The deck also has schematics for the **Pipe Bomb** and the **Venom-X**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The lab and its back door">
    The last hives are in a closed **lab**. The **Mutiny** intel is at the back, behind the big round column.

    **Before destroying the last hive**, drill the lab's **back door**: it hides parts and boxes to search, and you won't be able to open it afterwards.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Finale: the gas, then the Kraken

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Defend the chlorine pipes">
    After the last hive, a button releases **gas** into the next area. You have to defend **4 chlorine pipes** on the wall farthest from the entrance: **2 on the floor** and **2 on the walkway**. Hold on until the countdown ends, about **2 to 3 minutes**.

    If **all 4 pipes** are destroyed, the game is lost.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The last room">
    A door opens onto the walkway. Leave the **drill** behind, you don't need it anymore. Search one last time. The **The Guardian** intel is on the walkway, on the side opposite the entrance.

    **Keep your cash**: don't buy a new weapon, you need all of it for your abilities during the boss.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Jump into the Kraken's arena">
    The fight starts when one player jumps into the arena: the others are teleported next to them. The whole strategy is in [Beat the Kraken](../kraken/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Weapons, lockers and searching

- Weapons are placed at fixed spots, with a fixed price, like on the other maps. No reliable source gives the list of Mayday's prices. The guides' advice: keep your cash and only change weapons if you really need to.
- The DLC's new SMG, the **Ripper**, can be found on the map.
- **Lockers** come back from Nightfall. Keys are found by searching, and the weapons in lockers are random. An Armory upgrade makes you start with a key.
- The **small gray boxes** can give the hidden intel. The **red toolboxes** give crafting parts, but only if you are carrying a schematic.
