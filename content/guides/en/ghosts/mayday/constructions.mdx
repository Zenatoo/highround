Mayday is the first Extinction map with **crafting**. On the map, you find **schematics** for **5 items**. Each item needs its schematic and **three parts**. Once crafted, the item doesn't cost you any cash.

{/* Free crafting: Attack of the Fanboy only ("they don't cost anything for you to make"). */}

## How it works

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Pick up a schematic">
    Schematics are sheets lying on **red workbenches**, two per workbench. They are always in the same places. You can only carry **one schematic at a time**.

    <Screenshot src={c + 'workbench.webp'} alt="A red workbench holding schematics" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search the red toolboxes">
    Parts are in **red toolboxes**, placed at random in each game. A box only opens if you are carrying a schematic, and it gives a part for **that schematic**.

    <Screenshot src={c + 'toolbox.webp'} alt="A red toolbox to search for a part" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="Craft the item">
    With the three parts, open the skills menu (**Back** on Xbox, **Select** on PlayStation) and press **X** (Xbox) or **Square** (PlayStation).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The 5 items

**To check:** the part lists come from a single guide (Attack of the Fanboy), except the Hypno Trap's, which is confirmed by a second one. Schematic locations are confirmed by two guides at most.

| Item | Schematic | Parts | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sticky Flare** | Entrance: at the top of the stairs on the right, on the toolbox | Fuse, TNT, Resin | A flare that sticks and **attracts aliens**. The schematic gives **2**. |
| **Pipe Bomb** | Same spot as the Sticky Flare; another one on the Top Deck | Fuse, TNT, Pipe | A homemade fragmentation grenade. The schematic gives **5**. |
| **Hypno Trap** | Right after the tentacle, on a table near the Encrypted intel balcony | Biolum, Cell Battery, Pressure Plate | A small square trap: Cryptids that walk on it become **your allies**. |
| **Tesla Trap** | Same table as the Hypno Trap | Amolecular Magnet, Wires, a battery | A portable electric trap that electrocutes aliens that come close. |
| **Venom-X** | Cargo Area; another one on the Top Deck | Nucleic Battery, Deactivated Venom-X, Biolum | The alien weapon, in four variants. See [The Venom-X and its variants](../venom-x/). |

## The traps in detail

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Hypno Trap: save it for Rhinos">
    The Hypno Trap works on **all Cryptids**, but it's best to save it for **Rhinos**. Place it **in front of an incoming Rhino**, or lure the Rhino toward the trap with your teammates. It is also the **Big Game Trapper** achievement.

    It is also useful during the Kraken fight. See [Beat the Kraken](../kraken/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tesla Trap: the battery sets the power">
    The battery you find decides the trap's level:

    - **Cell Battery**: basic trap;
    - **Liquid Battery**: medium trap;
    - **Nucleic Battery**: advanced trap.

    According to one guide, it takes about **3 seconds** to recharge between two discharges.

    {/* TO CHECK: battery tiers = Digital Trends only; 3-second recharge and "kills small aliens" = Accelerated Ideas only (cross-check source). */}

    Several Tesla Traps close together link up. With 3 or 4 traps, something special happens: see [The map's secrets](../secrets/).
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="Digital Trends" source="https://www.digitaltrends.com/gaming/tips-tricks-surviving-call-duty-ghosts-extinction-mayday/">
    If you're not going for the achievement, you can skip the Sticky Flare and the Pipe Bomb.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Good to know

- Building **all 5 items** unlocks **The Architect**. See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
- The **Earn Your Keep** relic forbids crafting. See [Extinction basics](../../point-of-contact/bases-extinction/).
