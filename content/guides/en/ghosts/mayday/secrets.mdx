Mayday has **no main Easter Egg quest**: the end of the map is the fight against the Kraken (see [Beat the Kraken](../kraken/)). But two small secrets are worth a look: the hidden eggs of the Devastation DLC, and the Tesla Traps' lightning orb.

## Egg-stra Devastation: the multiplayer eggs

This secret starts **in multiplayer**. Each multiplayer map of the Devastation DLC hides an **alien egg** (a small hive) out of reach. Destroy all 4, then destroy a hive on Mayday: you get a bonus of **10,000 XP** and the **Egg-stra Devastation!** achievement.

**To check:** the egg locations come from a single written source (Cheat Code Central). Flapix's video shows them.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Behemoth">
    Go to the small **rectangular balcony** on the northwest edge of the excavator's walkway. Look west, under the excavator platform, at the west edge of the map: the egg is on one of the railings below. Shoot it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Collision">
    Go to the southeast of the map and stand on the right edge of the **boat**. Look to the right of the **destroyed bridge**: the egg is stuck at the bottom right of the **pillar**, where the waves crash.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Ruins">
    The egg hangs from the roof of a **tower** outside the map's limits. Go up to the **second floor** of the ruins, on the east side, and look south through the window to see the tower.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Unearthed">
    Go to the large **circular structure** in the southwest corner of the map, with a huge **stone pillar** in the middle. Walk around the railing while looking down: the egg is in the **crevice**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Destroy a hive on Mayday">
    Start Mayday and destroy **one hive** with the drill. You then receive the XP bonus and the achievement.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

- You have to play a **public match** for the eggs. Your progress is **saved** after each egg: you can leave the game once the egg is destroyed.
- You must own the Devastation DLC (the 4 multiplayer maps and Mayday).

## The Tesla Traps' lightning orb

A secret to do in **co-op**, with **3 or 4 players**.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Each player crafts a Tesla Trap">
    The schematic and the parts are explained in [Schematics and craftable items](../constructions/).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place the traps together">
    Place all your Tesla Traps **close enough** that they link up, for example in a square.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The orb appears">
    A **blue lightning orb** forms in the middle, then rises and starts to **follow a player**. It kills the Cryptids around it. It keeps following that player **even when the traps are broken**.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## A pet Rhino

A **Hypno Trap** placed in front of a **Rhino** turns it into an ally: it fights for you. This is the **Big Game Trapper** achievement. The Hypno Knife, however, doesn't work on Rhinos. See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
