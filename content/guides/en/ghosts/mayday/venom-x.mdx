The **Venom-X** is Extinction's alien weapon. It first appeared on Nightfall, where you find it ready to use. On Mayday, you have to **craft** it, and it comes in **four variants**: acid, lightning, alien plants or fire. It is added to your weapons: you keep it on top of your primary weapon and your pistol.

<Screenshot src={v + 'venom_model.webp'} alt="The Venom-X" credit={by} source={src} />

Crafting works like for the other items on the map: one schematic, three parts. The principle is explained in [Schematics and craftable items](../constructions/).

## Crafting the Venom

<Checklist>
  <Step title="Get the Venom schematic">
    The schematic is called **Venom**. It is **not** in the entrance: you find it in the **Cargo Area**, and another one on the **Top Deck**. You can only carry one schematic at a time.

    {/* TO CHECK: schematic spots = Digital Trends only (Cargo Area, and "the only schematics you'll find in Top Deck are for the Pipe Bomb and Venom-X"). */}
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the three parts">
    Search the **red toolboxes**. They only open if you are carrying the schematic. You need:

    - a **Nucleic Battery**;
    - a **Deactivated Venom-X**;
    - a **Biolum**, which picks the variant (see below).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Assemble the weapon">
    Open the skills menu (**Back** on Xbox, **Select** on PlayStation), then press **X** (Xbox) or **Square** (PlayStation) to craft. On Xbox, Digital Trends mentions **holding** the button.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## The four Biolums and their Venoms

The Biolum you find in a box is **random**. Its color decides which weapon you get:

| Biolum | Weapon | What it shoots |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Biolum** (neutral, green) | **Venom-X** | acid bombs that deal heavy damage |
| **Amethyst Biolum** (purple) | **Venom-SX** | alien eggs that grow **allied tentacles**, like the Seeders' ones, which shoot at the Cryptids |
| **Blue Biolum** (blue) | **Venom-LX** | **lightning** spheres that electrocute the Cryptids |
| **Orange Biolum** (orange) | **Venom-FX** | **napalm** bombs that burn enemies |

Want a specific color? According to one player, you can **leave** a Biolum you don't like and keep searching until you find the right one.

## Ammo

The Venom doesn't reload like a normal weapon. Some aliens you kill drop an **alien egg**: pick it up to get ammo back. This works for all four variants.

<Screenshot src={v + 'alien_egg.webp'} alt="An alien egg dropped by a Cryptid, to pick up to refill the Venom" credit={by} source={src} />

## Good to know

- With the **Pistols Only** relic, you can't use Venom weapons. With **Earn Your Keep**, you can't craft anything. See [Extinction basics](../../point-of-contact/bases-extinction/).
- Crafting the Venom counts toward the **The Architect** achievement. See [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
