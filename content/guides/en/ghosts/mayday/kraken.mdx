The **Kraken** is Mayday's boss: a gigantic sea Cryptid that guards the colony. It attacks the ship from the sea. Killing it ends the map. Like every Extinction map, Mayday has no main Easter Egg quest: this fight is the end of the game.

To get here (hives, gas, last room), see [Map walkthrough](../deroulement/). Class abilities, cash and relics are explained in [Extinction basics](../../point-of-contact/bases-extinction/).

<Screenshot src={k + 'emerge.webp'} alt="The Kraken rising out of the water in front of the ship" credit={by} source={src} />

**Before you jump:**

- Keep your **cash** for your abilities and team items, not for a new weapon.
- The fight starts as soon as **one** player jumps into the arena: the others are **teleported** next to them. Be ready together.
- The game is lost if the whole team goes down.

## The arena and the turrets

The arena is the ship's deck. In the **4 corners**, there is a **turret control box**. The player who uses it controls a big turret remotely.

- **Green** box: you can use it, the Kraken is on the other side.
- **Red** box: the Kraken is on this side. Run.

While you control the turret, your character is defenseless. A teammate has to **protect them**.

<Tips>
  <Tip type="tech" author="Peruviantank" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8hd0AynNMGs">
    Use the electric turret: it's the most effective one against the Kraken.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## Its attacks

<Checklist>
  <Step title="It sends Cryptids">
    The Kraken brings in waves of enemies: Scouts, Hunters, Seekers, Phantoms and Seeders.

    <Screenshot src={k + 'cryptids.webp'} alt="Cryptids sent by the Kraken onto the deck" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="It sets the deck on fire">
    It sticks its tentacles onto the hull and **heats the whole deck**. Often. The only safe zones are the **4 red corners** (where the turrets are) and the **red circle in the center**, the destroyed hive in the middle of the arena.

    This is also **the best time to shoot**: it is more vulnerable during this attack.

    <Screenshot src={k + 'deck_heat.webp'} alt="The ship's deck glowing red from the Kraken's heat" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="It strikes the center">
    If you are grouped in the center, it strikes with its middle tentacle. It does little damage, but it spawns Cryptids.
  </Step>

  <Step title="It roars">
    Its roar sends out a shockwave that **blurs your vision** for a few seconds.

    <Screenshot src={k + 'blurred.webp'} alt="Blurred vision after the Kraken's roar" credit={by} source={src} />
  </Step>

  <Step title="It cuts the electronics">
    An attack like an **electromagnetic pulse** shuts down everything electronic. Don't count on your automatic turrets: expect to lose them. A well-placed **Tesla Trap** is still a good bodyguard near the turrets.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

## Where to shoot

Aim for the **small glowing red dot on its forehead**, between the eyes: that's where it takes the most damage. When it glows, everything highlighted in red is a critical point.

<Screenshot src={k + 'weak_spot.webp'} alt="The red weak spot on the Kraken's forehead" credit={by} source={src} />

## How the fight goes

The fight is played in **4 rounds**. Each time it loses about a quarter of its health, the Kraken **switches sides** of the ship. Watch the color of the boxes to know where to go.

Around **half its health**, its attacks speed up. This is the time to use your **Hypno Traps** if you have them: the Cryptids that walk on them fight for you.

<Checklist>
  <Step title="In co-op: two teams of two">
    Go to the **side opposite** the Kraken. In each team, one player takes a turret and the other protects them. Stay in the turret's corner when the deck heats up.

    Later, if it gets too hard, everyone falls back to **a single turret** and holds it together. Drop armor and ammo often.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Solo: stay in a corner">
    In the center, Cryptids come from everywhere. Stand in a **turret's corner** instead: you only watch two sides, you are sheltered from the fire, and you can shoot the Kraken between waves.
  </Step>
</Checklist>

<Tips>
  <Tip type="faster" author="TheRelaxingEnd" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FAax_RWi738">
    To kill it fast: fully upgraded portable turrets on top of the fixed turrets, and Sticky Flares to keep busy the Cryptids that attack the players on the turrets.
  </Tip>
  <Tip type="tech" author="joshkeatley" source="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rcLvwTWUtRA">
    Keep an upgraded riot shield on your back: the player on the turret is less exposed.
  </Tip>
</Tips>

## The reward

When the Kraken is dead, the game is won and the **ending cutscene** plays. You also unlock the **Epilogue** intel. See [Intel](../renseignements/).

<Screenshot src={k + 'defeated.webp'} alt="The defeated Kraken" credit={by} source={src} />

The Kraken-related achievements (all on Regular or Hardcore difficulty):

- **Survived Mayday**: kill the Kraken for the first time.
- **Upping the Ante**: kill it with at least one relic active. Finishing a map with a relic also earns you a Tooth.
- **Mayday Completionist**: complete all the challenges of the game, then kill it.

All the details are in [The map's achievements and trophies](../succes/).
